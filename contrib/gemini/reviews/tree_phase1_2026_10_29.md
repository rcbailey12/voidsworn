CROSS-FACTION NOTES

Free-Volley Command Collision (Kassar, Vex, Voice of the Deep, Saro, Vessane): Separated into five distinct spatial geometries-Kassar owns flagship-radius synchronization, Vex owns two-sided broadside line alignment, Voice of the Deep owns armor-melting acid coats and ground pools, Saro owns long-range optical refraction through friendly hulls and straight-line beam piercing, and Vessane owns chain-lightning corona arcs and reactive overwatch triggers.

Extra-Wing Carrier Command Collision (Ashgrove, Reyes, Old Mott, Matriarch, Tamsa): Differentiated by how squadrons interact with hulls-Ashgrove uses sworn escort interception and carrier recall/re-arming, Reyes uses cruiser telemetry painting and synchronized battery follow-ups, Old Mott latches onto enemy hulls to slow/drag them and strip scrap, the Matriarch implants gestating cysts and splits/reabsorbs living swarms, and Tamsa uses squadrons as optical relays and mirror decoys.

Shield-Wall Command Collision (Thale, Maren, Brin, Seveth): Split into four defensive paradigms-Thale builds Grievance counter-fire tokens and bodyguard interposition, Maren couples adjacent shields into a shared formation pool and advances in columns, Brin reflects missed/absorbed energy shots and splits lances through shields, and Seveth projects LOS-blocking shadow zones and reverts hull/section states to prior-round checkpoints.

Full-Burn Mobility Command Collision (Ironmaw, Razormaw, Vell): Contrasted by movement intent-Ironmaw uses speed for front-plate ramming collisions and pass-by boarding pods, Razormaw uses scent-of-blood tracking to wrap around bleeding rear arcs and pursue fleeing prey, and Vell leaves behind holographic afterimages to swap positions and punish missed enemy shots.

Torpedo/Damage-Spike & Hull-Repair Collisions (Draven, Sable, Vorn, Ostrel; Vesh, Mending Throng, Aurex): For the damage-spike Commands, Draven marks a single Quarry for turning torpedoes, Sable plants delayed internal warheads from stealth, Vorn locks down blockade firing zones with reactive overwatch, and Ostrel projects opposite-side shadow copies against isolated ships; for the repair Commands, Vesh over-welds scrap armor and hot-wires guns, the Mending Throng links ships with flesh tendrils and mutates redundant nerve-clusters, and Aurex deploys persistent nanite clouds that can cross over to deconstruct enemy hulls.
