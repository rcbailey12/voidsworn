3. FACING ECONOMY

Fixed-versus-turret numerical payoff (INFERRED from W, since hull mount tables are omitted):

Using turret (2x8 = 16 dmg, r:5, opt:3, acc:74) as the all-arc (T) baseline at range 5:

battery (4x6 = 24 dmg, r:5, opt:3, acc:72) pays +50% raw damage for a fixed mount (F/P/S). That is a healthy tactical tradeoff: missing one turn out of three due to facing breaks even with a turret firing every turn (24 x 2/3 = 16).

hbattery (5x7 = 35 dmg, r:5, opt:3, acc:72) pays +119% raw damage, strongly rewarding broadside positioning.

turbo (2x9 = 18 dmg, r:5, opt:3, acc:74) pays only +12.5% raw damage (+2 dmg) over turret. Even after Hegemony's plasma trait (2x10 = 20 dmg, pierce:.25), if turbo is fixed-arc, +25% raw damage does not compensate for losing all-arc coverage (missing even 1 turn in 4 loses to a turret).

Furthermore, turret's facing is its entire identity: without T (Turret (all arcs)), turret has no reason to exist in W alongside turbo and battery.

Weapons you would ONLY ever take on a turret (INFERRED):

turret (Dorsal turret): Its 16-damage stat line is priced strictly around 360-degree T coverage; on a fixed arc it is dead weight.

Point defense (flak, gsentry, spores at r:2) and tendril (r:1): At range 1-2, small lateral hex steps swing a target across prow/broadside boundaries; short-range defensive guns need wide/turret arcs (T) to screen approaching squadrons or boarding ships.

Weapons you would NEVER take on a turret (or that do not need turret tax) (INFERRED):

Long-range standoff guns (lance at r:7, opt:6; pulsar and ion at r:6, opt:5): At 6-7 hexes, angular velocity per hex moved is small, so a ship can easily keep targets inside a fixed prow (F) or broadside (P/S) cone without paying a turret damage tax.

Cooldown-2 weapons (torp, htorp, startorp, doom with cd:2): Because these weapons fire only once every two turns, the ship has two full movement phases to bring a fixed prow/broadside arc to bear, making turret coverage largely wasted on them.

5. SPECIAL SYSTEMS

Torpedoes (torp, htorp, startorp - delayed hex-lane strike):

Earns complexity? Yes, conceptually, but over-penalized in implementation. Delayed spatial prediction (AIM HEX + TORP_RING 1-2 hex penalty + 3-hex clean dodge + stray-hull body-blocking) is the highest-skill tactical verb in the pack. However, stacking four mitigation layers on one weapon - cd:2, 1-turn travel delay with 3-hex clean dodge, low base accuracy (60-66) with evMult:2, and point defense at impact - made torpedoes so unreliable that the designer had to bolt on a hidden TORP_WARHEAD=1.2 (+20% damage) hotfix ("torpedo admirals fell out of the 50 to 90 band").

What breaks if deleted? Battles lose spatial area-denial, forced movement ("dodge or eat 42+ damage"), and body-blocking (TORP_STRAY=10), collapsing combat into immediate-hit dice checks; Corsair's rockets trait and Ithar's star trait also disappear.

Ion (ion - shield interaction):

Earns complexity? Barely / Under-leveraged. Only a single weapon in the 25-gun table (ion, mounted 4 times, Hegemony only, no faction trait) uses ion:2.5. Meanwhile, 9 other weapons bypass shields directly via pierce (lance, pulsar, gauss, doom, bombs, sporeb at .6; tendril at .5; startorp at .4; torp/htorp at .3; bioplasma at .2; plus Hegemony's own plasma trait at .25).

What breaks if deleted? Only Hegemony loses its dedicated shield-cracker before firing plasma batteries; no other faction interacts with ion. Worse, Hegemony's plasma trait (pierce:.25) partially works at cross-purposes with ion (bypassing shields that ion is trying to strip).

Point Defense (flak, gsentry, spores):

Earns complexity? The system earns its place; the extra weapon rows (gsentry, spores) do not. Because flak is mounted 11 times across all factions (flak 11, spores 2, gsentry 1), it acts as the universal counterplay to both squadrons (vsSq:3) and delayed ordnance ("accuracy, shields, armor and point defense at impact", "point defense can shoot rockets down"). However, gsentry is a literal clone of flak and spores is a rounding error.

What breaks if deleted? Squadrons (guns, bombs, sporeb) and Corsair rockets / torpedoes lose their primary defensive counter, and close-range anti-squadron screening vanishes.

Doom (doom - Deathless 'Corona arc'):

Earns complexity? Yes, at zero system cost. doom (1x40, r:5, opt:4, acc:78, pierce:.6, crit:12, cd:2 + Deathless armorPierce:.5) reuses existing properties (k:'lance', pierce, cd:2, armorPierce) with no bespoke engine rules, delivering the exact same 20 damage/turn average as lance (1x20, cd:1) in a shorter-range (r:5 vs r:7), front-loaded 40-damage alpha strike that pierces both shields (.6) and armor (.5).

What breaks if deleted? Deathless loses its heavy capital alpha-strike gun, leaving the faction with only a 3x6 mid-range gun (gauss) and point defense (gsentry/flak).

5. Questions for the designer

Why do single-faction clone rows (gsentry, spores, turbo, startorp, sporeb) still exist in W alongside FAC_WTRAIT instead of being expressed as faction traits on shared base archetypes (flak, turret/battery, torp, bombs)?

Stakes: Collapsing redundant base rows into W archetypes + WTRAITS would eliminate literal duplicates (gsentry/flak), prevent NOTRAITS simulations from testing dead numbers (startorp's evMult:2), and make every row in W a distinct mechanical role.

In battle.js 531-536, is the intended torpedo dodge penalty [0, 10, 25] (as written in the comment) or [0, 5, 15] (as coded in TORP_RING), and does startorp's r:5 intentionally violate the comment's "Range 6" rule?

Stakes: A 5-to-10-point accuracy swing on moving targets plus an undocumented TORP_WARHEAD=1.2 multiplier determines whether torpedoes are balanced by predictable spatial play or by hidden stat band-aids.

Are fixed-arc weapons (F, P, S) supposed to be balanced against turret inside W (where turbo only gains +2 damage over turret), or by hull mount counts and cp discounts in the ship table?

Stakes: If fixed mounts do not get extra barrels or lower cp cost on hulls, a +12.5% damage bump (turbo vs turret) fails to pay for giving up 360-degree turret coverage.
