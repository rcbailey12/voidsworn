Global Visual Upgrades

Procedural Hull Thermal Scarring: As ships take damage, a secondary dynamic texture layer blends in blackened blast scars, punctured plating, and glowing molten edges around strike points.

Implementation Cost: Low. Material/shader work via an additional UV blend map and dynamic texture stamp.

Volumetric Torpedo Wake Trails: High-value munitions pull thick, lingering fluid ribbon trails through space, making torpedo salvos and intercept windows clear from battle zoom.

Implementation Cost: Low-Medium. Babylon.js ribbon mesh with simple vertex animation or particle instancing.

Boarding Action Breach Siphon: Ships caught in boarding actions display high-intensity breach tethers connecting to the boarding vessel, with violent breach flashes cycling at the victim's hull seams.

Implementation Cost: Low. Animated dynamic line mesh with an emissive point-light attached to the breach node.

Cinematic Flagship Distortion Well: The hex occupied by an admiral's flagship radiates a subtle, continuous localized space-warp ring, ensuring command units are immediately identifiable on a busy board.

Implementation Cost: Medium. Post-processing screen-space distortion shader applied over a defined world-space radius.

Wreckage Ghost-Field Drift: Destroyed hulls do not vanish; they drift slowly along their last vector as scorched, depowered obstacles that cast shadows across combat hexes.

Implementation Cost: Low. Retention of the primary mesh with an unlit scorched material replacement and physics drift.

Part D Admiral Generation

Recommended approach: A structured AI portrait generator utilizing rigid control masks and locked faction-specific prompt syntax. This ensures absolute aesthetic unity across all 24 characters, permits instant regeneration as balance shifts, and costs zero custom manual UI asset modeling.

1. Portrait Style System Specification

Visual Architecture: Sharp, high-contrast digital illustration with heavy rim lighting, low keyfill, and graphic, planar shadows. Portraits are framed from the upper chest up, angled slightly three-quarters toward center, looking deadpan past the camera.

Template Slots:

[FACTION]: Enforces primary materials, racial/mechanical nature, and ambient environment.

[RANK/ROLE]: Dictates command regalia, posture, and optical implants.

[AGE/CYCLE]: Defines physical weathering, shell scuffs, or chassis wear.

[TEMPERAMENT]: Controls eye glow intensity, mouth line, and shadow severity.

[PATRON GLYPH]: Visualized strictly as an etched, recessed metal chest seal or collar brooch.
