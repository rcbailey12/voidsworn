2. DEAD PICKS AND STRICT TAKES

Five Dead Picks (boons a competent player skips):

L_ember ("Pyre of Stars"): Sets chainBoom: 40. At Epic rarity (m=2), basic e_chain already deals Math.round(20 * 2) = 40 damage AND sets targets burning (chainFire: 1). Spending an elite/flagship Legendary reward slot for +0 damage over Epic e_chain (or +20 damage over Common e_chain) with no burn is a trap.

d_jugg ("Juggernaut"): Grants ramPct: 0.5, ramGuard: 1 (+50% ram damage, no recoil). It adds 0% to the ram hull-damage cap (ramCap), whereas basic r_prow adds +35%*m ram damage (+53% at m=1.5, +70% at m=2), +10%*m hull cap, and 50% recoil reduction. Furthermore, d_jugg's ramGuard: 1 is 100% redundant with both ASPECTS.ram and L_rook.

r_mom ("Momentum"): Grants +5%*m damage per hex moved, up to 4 hexes (+20%*m max). Moving a full 4 hexes yields less damage (+20%*m) than sitting still with i_still (+25%*m) or firing with passive weapon boons (h_wings +25%*m, e_war +25%*m, a_lance +35%*m), and only yields +5%*m to +10%*m on normal 1-2 hex combat moves.

s_ev ("Lightning Reflex"): Grants ev: Math.round(6*m) (+6 at m=1, +9 at m=1.5, +12 at m=2). By comparison, r_burn grants +1 speed + +6 evasion at m=1.5 and +2 speed + +10 evasion at m=2, while r_jink grants +15*m evasion (+15 to +30) on a 3-hex move.

e_kindle ("Kindling") at m=1: Grants igniteCh: 0.12*m (12% at m=1) on hull hits only, burning for 6 + 4*m = 10 damage/turn for 2 turns. That is an expected ceiling of 0.12 * 20 = 2.4 damage per hull hit (0 on shield hits), dwarfed by d_inferno (40% ignite, 10 damage/turn for 3 turns = 12.0 expected damage per broadside hit).

Three Strict Takes (always correct when offered):

r_burn ("Afterburners"): Ungated by BOON_NEED, applies to every ship in the fleet, and grants +1 hex speed at m=1, +1 speed and +6 evasion at m=1.5, and +2 speed and +10 evasion at m=2. Fleetwide speed dictates range, arc control, ram reach, and 3-hex movement triggers (r_jink, d_hunter).

a_crit ("Killing Light"): Ungated by BOON_NEED, applies to every weapon in the fleet, and bundles +6%*m crit chance, +0.25*m crit damage multiplier (x1.75 at m=1, x2.0 at m=2), and an unscaled rule-changer (critSys: 1, "every critical hit wrecks a ship system").

r_slip ("Slip the Net"): Ungated by BOON_NEED, independent of m (fx:()=>({fade:1})), and allows every ship in the fleet to "move after firing, up to half the movement they had left", transforming turn-based positioning, arc-dodging, and focus-fire survival at zero rarity penalty.

3. PATRON IDENTITY

Kharon (iron - 'Broadsides, armor and holding the line'): Reads as its patron. Five of seven boons (i_still, i_brace, i_line, i_wall, L_iron) mechanically reward stationary gunlines, ship-to-ship adjacency, and active bracing; only i_broad and i_plate are generic stat percentages.

Vyre (storm - 'Shields, ion fire and lightning'): Split between thematic shield-break cascades and generic stat buffs. s_arc and s_chain capture the lightning/shield-shatter fantasy, while s_cap, s_regen, L_storm, and s_ion are pure numeric scalers. s_ev (+6*m evasion) contradicts the shield theme by avoiding hits altogether and encroaches on Rook.

Crucible (hunger - 'Squadrons, boarding and feeding on the fallen'): Flavorful on-kill scavenging (h_feast, h_brood), but mechanically a closed parasitic island. Six of seven boons are hard-locked to carriers/squadrons in BOON_NEED, while h_board is an isolated boarding stat buff with no supporting boarding boons, duos, or legendaries anywhere in the pool.

Astra (star - 'Lances, precision and critical hits'): Reads as generic sniper buffs plus hard lance gates, with one misplaced flanker boon. a_aim and a_crit are universal gunnery buffs; a_lance, a_mark, and L_star are hard-gated to lances; and a_rear (+25%*m rear-arc damage) is a close-range flanking mechanic that contradicts Astra's long-range precision identity.

Rook (rook - 'Rams, speed and momentum'): Strongest tactical identity on the board, undermined by copy-pasted ram boons. Every boon interacts with hex movement or collision, and r_slip / r_shock change how ships maneuver and collide. However, r_prow, d_jugg, and L_rook sell the exact same ram stat block three times.

Seren (ember - 'Torpedoes, fire and chain explosions'): Three disconnected sub-themes wearing one coat. Torpedo boons (e_war, e_reload, e_spread) have zero mechanical interaction with fire (e_kindle, d_burnswarm, d_inferno) or death explosions/executes (e_chain, L_ember, e_hunt). Ironically, Seren's fire duos trigger off Iron broadsides (d_inferno) and Hunger squadrons (d_burnswarm), not Seren's own torpedoes.

4. SPILLOVER VALUE

Can an off-patron boon ever be the right pick? Yes, in three specific cases - but the pool fails the Q7 ruling outside them:

Where an Off-Patron Pick Wins (The Strongest Case):

Unscaled Rule-Changers (r_slip, i_wall, a_mark, s_chain, e_spread, h_brood): Because all six B4 rule-changers use fx:()=>({...}) with no m multiplier, a hidden rarity penalty on off-patron offers cannot reduce their effect. An Astra lance fleet or Kharon broadside fleet offered off-patron r_slip (fade:1, fire-and-move) or i_wall (wallBrace:1, AoE brace) gains an action-economy upgrade that no third on-patron +20%*m damage buff can match.

Cross-Bucket Multipliers with Unscaled Flags (a_crit, r_burn, e_hunt): Once an Iron fleet holds i_broad (+20%*m) and i_still (+25%*m), further additive damage suffers diminishing returns. Off-patron a_crit at m=1 multiplies existing damage (+6% crit at x1.75) and wrecks a system on every crit (critSys:1, unscaled), while off-patron r_burn at m=1 adds +1 fleetwide speed.

High-Ceiling Duo Bridges (d_inferno, d_hunter, d_bastion): For a multi-hit Iron broadside fleet holding i_broad, drafting a single off-patron Ember boon (e_hunt or e_chain) unlocks d_inferno (40% fire ignition per broadside hit, 10 burn/turn for 3 turns, unscaled by m).

Where Off-Patron Spillover Fails Structurally (The Q7 Defect):

Step-Threshold Cliff (m>=1.5 and m>=2): Six boons lock half their value or a secondary mechanic behind m>=1.5 or m>=2 (h_hatch drops from 2 wings to 1; e_reload drops from 2 turns to 1; r_burn loses all +6/+10 evasion and +1 speed at m=1; i_line, a_long, and e_chain lose their Epic: clauses below m=2). If off-patron spillover rolls at m=1, those six boons are severely nerfed.

Missing Duo Bridges (9 of 15 pairs) and Legendary Gate Delay: Because Legendaries require "two boons from that patron" and 9 of 15 patron pairs have no Duo boon (including 4 of Hunger's 5 off-patron pairings), taking an off-patron pure-% boon in any of those 9 pairs delays Legendary access without offering a Duo bridge.

Not a single boon interacts with map routing, events, credits, repairs, command points (cpCap), captain XP, or refit modules. Because BOON_NEED hides weapon/carrier/shield boons until the fleet already holds that kit, boons cannot even drive speculative ship or module purchases between battles.

Is that share right for the layer between battles? No. Having 45.8% pure stat buffs, 0% map/fleet hooks, and only 22.9% true rule-changers makes the patron signal layer behave like a redundant passive module layer. Between battles is where a roguelite fleet game should shape how the fleet fights and what it shops for next; instead, nearly half the pool just inflates existing numbers.

5. Questions for the designer

How does derive(s) combine identical fx keys when a player holds both a basic/duo boon and its matching Legendary (s_regen + L_storm, r_prow/d_jugg + L_rook, e_chain + L_ember, e_kindle + d_inferno)?

Stakes: If keys overwrite or hit a hard cap (stormRegen: 1, ramGuard: 1, chainBoom: 40, fireDmg: 4), then drafting a patron's Legendary or Duo actively deletes the value of the prerequisite boons required to unlock it.

What values of m can roll on off-patron spillover offers, and how do those rolls interact with the m>=1.5 and m>=2 step thresholds on h_hatch, r_burn, e_reload, i_line, a_long, and e_chain?

Stakes: If off-patron spillover is capped below m=1.5 or m=2, step-threshold boons lose up to half their primary effect or their entire secondary rule, violating the Oct 28, 2026 Q7 ruling.

Why does BOON_NEED hard-block 20 of the 48 boons until the fleet already owns the matching weapon, carrier, or shield kit, rather than allowing at least one speculative build-around offer before a shop or forge node?

Stakes: Hard-gating boons behind current inventory guarantees that boons never influence fleet-purchasing or refit decisions between battles, reducing signals to reactive upgrades for gear the player already holds.
