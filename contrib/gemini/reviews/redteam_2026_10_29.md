Designer Questions (Max 2)

On-patron vs. off-patron selection weight in rollBoonOffer (Spec Fix 1): Once off-patron boons enter the main candidate pool alongside on-patron boons instead of waiting for backfill, what exact relative weight should an on-patron candidate carry against an off-patron candidate (e.g., per-candidate weight multiplier vs. fixed per-slot probability of rolling on-patron)?

Stakes: Because off-patron patrons outnumber the active shrine patron across the run, pooling candidates without an explicit weight ratio will either make shrine offers predominantly off-patron (diluting patron identity) or, if weighted too heavily, recreate the old backfill-only behavior under a new name.

Loss-debrief reveal after an in-battle weakness trigger in nemAfterBattle (Spec Fix 2): If the player triggers (and thus reveals) a Nemesis weakness during a battle they ultimately lose, should the post-battle loss debrief still reveal the second unrevealed weakness, or should the debrief simply acknowledge the weakness discovered in combat?

Stakes: Revealing an additional unrevealed weakness unconditionally on the loss branch means a single lost engagement can reveal 100% of a Nemesis's two-weakness file (one mid-battle + one in debrief), collapsing the multi-encounter intel progression loop on a defeat.
