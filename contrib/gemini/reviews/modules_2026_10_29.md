2. Findings

[BLOCKER] FIT_SLOTS.module collides directly with universal hull slots (data.js 545, 578)

READ: Every hull automatically receives 1 to 3 module slots from command points via "if(h.slots==null) h.slots=cp<=2?1:cp<=4?2:3;". Yet the flagship customization reserve defines "module:'one module slot (flagship only)'" on ship.fit.

INFERRED: These two rules come from incompatible design eras. If ship.fit.module is activated alongside h.slots, a capital flagship (cp >= 5) either gets demoted from 3 module slots to 1, or awkwardly carries 3 standard fleet refit slots plus 1 redundant "flagship module" slot drawing from the same MODULES table.

[BLOCKER] Missing only tags on weapon- and shield-dependent modules (data.js 153-167)

READ: In MODULES, only hangar enforces a hull capability tag ("only:'launch'"). The six other subsystem-dependent modules - capacitor, regen, loaders, crystal, magazine, and flakplus - have no only field, while "Refits can be moved between ships freely".

INFERRED: Unless hardcoded UI checks exist outside data.js, nothing in the data schema stops a player from equipping crystal on a ship without lances, magazine on a ship without torpedoes, or capacitor on an unshielded hull, creating literal 0-effect dead installs.

[FIX] Modules violate the progression constitution by duplicating PERKS and RELICS verbatim (data.js 153-201)

READ: The Chair Census notes a standing rule that "tree skills may not duplicate doctrine effects", while "Where modules sit in that constitution is undefined". In the code, MODULES.auspex ("fx:{aim:10}"), MODULES.cogitator ("fx:{crit:10}"), and MODULES.drive ("fx:{speed:1}") are exact 1:1 clones of PERKS.gunnery ("fx:{aim:10}"), PERKS.killer ("fx:{crit:10}"), and PERKS.helm / RELICS.compass ("fx:{speed:1}"). MODULES.prow (hpPct:.15,armor:.05), MODULES.regen (regenPct:.5), and MODULES.capacitor (shPct:.3) likewise reuse the exact effect keys of PERKS.ironhull (hpPct:.15), PERKS.deflector (regenPct:.4), RELICS.armourweave (armor:.06), and RELICS.lattice (shPct:.15).

INFERRED: Instead of offering mechanical hardware that changes how a ship fights, most modules are interchangeable with captain levels and passive relic buffs, turning ship fitting into anonymous stat stacking.

[FIX] The 1/2/3 CP slot ladder compounds multiplicative modules into a rich-get-richer economy (data.js 153-167, 545)

READ: Hull cost scales linearly with command points ("h.cost=Math.round((60*cp+10)/5)*5"), and slots step up at cp 1-2 (1 slot), cp 3-4 (2 slots), and cp 5+ (3 slots). Nine of the 13 modules grant percentage or per-weapon bonuses (hpPct, shPct, regenPct, hullRegenPct, aim, crit, battShots, lancePct, torpPct) for fixed credit costs between 55 and 90.

INFERRED: Because a cp 5+ hull has both the largest base stat pools/weapon count and the most slots (3), every percentage or all-weapon module yields multiple times more raw combat stats on a capital ship than on a cp 1-2 frigate for the exact same credit price.

[FIX] Action-economy multipliers (hangar, loaders) sit in the same flat price band as minor stat sticks (data.js 153-167)

READ: All 13 modules are priced within a 35-credit spread (cost:55 to cost:90). hangar ("Carriers only: +1 squadron in flight", cost 90) and loaders ("Battery weapons fire +1 shot", cost 90) cost only 5 credits more than damcon ("Repair 4% hull", cost 85) and 15 credits more than cogitator ("+10% critical chance", cost 75).

INFERRED: Adding an entire active squadron or +1 shot to every battery mount increases output by 33% to 100% per wing/mount, whereas +10% crit adds only ~5%-10% expected damage. Compressing module prices into 55-90 credits makes hangar and loaders automatic strict takes on eligible hulls.

[FIX] damcon (85 credits) is mathematically dominated by prow (70 credits) (data.js 154, 164)

READ: damcon costs 85 credits for "Repair 4% hull at the start of each of your turns" ("fx:{hullRegenPct:.04}"), while prow costs 70 credits for "+15% hull, +5% armor" ("fx:{hpPct:.15,armor:.05}").

INFERRED: Because start-of-turn hull repair does nothing while a ship is at full hull or taking damage only to shields, a ship must suffer hull damage and then survive 4 additional turns just for damcon (16% total repair) to match prow's upfront +15% max hull - while never matching prow's +5% armor reduction, costing 15 more credits, and providing zero protection against burst focus fire.

[FIX] Redundant module pairs split single combat jobs into pure math checks (data.js 155, 158-159, 166)

READ: auspex ("+10 aim for every weapon", cost 80) and cogitator ("+10% critical chance", cost 75) both passively buff all-weapon attack rolls. Likewise, capacitor ("+30% shield strength", cost 80) and regen ("+50% shield regeneration", cost 70) both passively buff shield EHP.

INFERRED: Neither pair asks the player to play differently on the tactics grid; one option in each pair is simply mathematically superior for a given hull's base stats or battle length. In particular, cogitator only triggers on hits that already connect, making auspex strictly better for both reliability and expected DPS across almost all accuracy bands.

[FIX] Unrestricted refit movement reduces customization to a pre-battle inventory chore (CHAIR CENSUS)

READ: "Refits can be moved between ships freely (fleet screen text in the campaign code)".

INFERRED: When modules detach and reattach at zero cost between every fight, players do not build specialized ships; they maintain a floating pile of stat tokens that optimal play demands stripping from resting/reserve ships and re-equipping onto the active high-CP lineup before every battle.

[NOTE] Authored HULL_VARIANTS already deliver the true build trade-offs missing from MODULES (data.js 570-571, 579)

READ: i_carrier_a ("A third wing, paid for with lighter shields (22) and one hex of speed") and d_jackal_h ("Both null cannons on turrets and a null spire, 10 less hull") trade hull/shield/speed stats for altered weapon mounts, arcs (F to T), and wing count.

INFERRED: HULL_VARIANTS demonstrates what real ship builds look like in Voidsworn: zero-sum trade-offs that alter firing arcs and tactical roles. By contrast, MODULES contains zero trade-offs and zero arc/utility alterations.

[NOTE] Key name collision between MODULES.cogitator and RELICS.cogitator (data.js 166, 189)

READ: Both MODULES and RELICS define the key cogitator ("Targeting reckoner" in MODULES vs. "Founders' rangefinder" in RELICS).

INFERRED: Reusing identical object keys across equipment tables risks lookup/serialization bugs if ids are ever merged in save state, tooltips, or reward logs. Moreover, RELICS.cogitator ("first attack in every battle hits 95% of the time") is a far better tactical module concept than MODULES.cogitator's flat +10% crit.

3. Explicit checks
1. MODULE IDENTITY

One-line job statements for all 13 entries in MODULES:

prow (Reinforced prow, 70 cr): Increases upfront hull buffer (+15% hull) and reduces incoming hull damage (+5% armor) for brawling ships.

auspex (Scanner array, 80 cr): Raises hit probability across all mounted weapons (+10 aim) regardless of weapon family or range.

drive (Plasma drive, 75 cr): Extends linear hex movement (+1 speed) to control engagement range or close distance.

gyro (Gyro-stabilisers, 60 cr): Improves firing-arc rotation (+1 turn allowance) and passive dodge (+5 evasion).

capacitor (Screen capacitor, 80 cr): Expands peak shield pool (+30% shield strength) to survive front-loaded burst damage.

regen (Shield regenerator, 70 cr): Accelerates per-turn shield recovery (+50% shield regeneration) for prolonged skirmishes.

loaders (Battery loaders, 90 cr): Adds +1 shot to every battery weapon on the hull, multiplying multi-mount battery output.

crystal (Lance focusing crystal, 80 cr): Amplifies single-target lance burst (+25% lance damage) on lance-equipped hulls.

magazine (Torpedo magazine, 85 cr): Increases torpedo cycle rate (reload time -1 turn) and payload (+15% torpedo damage).

flakplus (Flak batteries, 55 cr): Expands point-defense interception envelope (+1 range) and volume (+2 shots) against ordnance/wings.

damcon (Damage control crews, 85 cr): Passively restores 4% hull at the start of each turn to offset slow attrition.

hangar (Hangar expansion, 90 cr): Adds +1 squadron in flight on carrier hulls (only:'launch').

cogitator (Targeting reckoner, 75 cr): Increases critical hit frequency (+10% critical chance) across all weapons.

Duplicates of job at different prices:

Generic All-Weapon DPS Scalars (auspex at 80 cr vs. cogitator at 75 cr): Both exist purely to passively increase expected damage across all weapons without changing firing arcs, cooldowns, or target selection.

Hull Durability Buffers (prow at 70 cr vs. damcon at 85 cr): Both exist solely to increase the total hull damage a ship can absorb before dying - prow via upfront max HP and armor, damcon via slow per-turn healing.

Shield Durability Buffers (capacitor at 80 cr vs. regen at 70 cr): Both exist solely to increase total damage absorbed by shields - capacitor against burst, regen over multi-turn attrition.

2. STRICT TAKES AND DEAD SLOTS

Strict Takes (correct on every ship that can hold them):

On Carriers (only:'launch'): hangar (90 cr, +1 squadron in flight) is an unconditional lock. Defense from the numbers: In HULL_VARIANTS, the Ithar carrier variant i_carrier_a (Ringing aperture) has to sacrifice both shields (sh:22) and 1 hex of speed (sp:3) just to reach 3 wings (mod:{hangar:3,sh:22,sp:3}). That variant proves a base carrier carries 2 wings; therefore, hangar grants a +50% increase in active squadrons (or +33% on i_carrier_a) with zero stat penalty. No passive stat module comes close to +50% action economy.

On Multi-Battery Hulls: loaders (90 cr, battShots:1) is a strict take on any hull with battery weapons. Defense from the numbers: While crystal grants +25% damage and magazine grants +15% damage, +1 shot is a discrete integer increase applied to every battery weapon on the ship (INFERRED from "Battery weapons fire +1 shot"): on a 1-, 2-, or 3-shot battery, +1 shot equals +100%, +50%, or +33% base damage, which then compounds with aim and crit.

Universal (Any Armed Hull): auspex (80 cr, aim:10) is never wrong on any direct-fire ship. Defense from the numbers: Because +10 aim applies "for every weapon", its value multiplies by the number of weapon mounts on the hull. At typical tactical base hit rates of 50%-70% (INFERRED; RELICS.cogitator sets a ceiling of 95%), +10 aim increases actual landed hits by 14.3% to 20.0% across every weapon mount simultaneously while reducing miss variance.

Dead Slots (never worth installing at any price):

damcon (85 cr, hullRegenPct:.04): Never worth its slot over prow (hpPct:.15, armor:.05). Defense from the numbers: Start-of-turn hull repair yields 0% value on Turn 1 (ships start undamaged or absorb initial fire on shields). Even if a ship takes hull damage on Turn 1, it must survive to the start of Turns 2, 3, 4, and 5 (5+ total turns of combat) just to heal 16% hull - barely edging past prow's instant +15% max hull, while still lacking prow's +5% armor reduction on every hit and offering zero burst-survival buffer.

cogitator (75 cr, crit:10): Never worth a slot when auspex (aim:10) and weapon-family modules (loaders, crystal, magazine) exist. Defense from the numbers: Even assuming a 1.5x (+50%) or 2.0x (+100%) critical damage multiplier (INFERRED), +10% crit chance only adds +5% to +10% expected damage on attacks that hit, which is strictly inferior to auspex's +10 aim (+14%-20% expected DPS plus reliability) and crystal's +25% unconditional lance damage.

3. SLOT ECONOMY

The h.slots = cp<=2?1 : cp<=4?2 : 3 ladder makes cp 5+ capital ships (and secondarily cp 3-4 cruisers) the only real build platforms, while locking cp 1-2 frigates out of customization in practice for three structural reasons:

A 1-slot hull cannot form a build: A "build" requires combining at least two synergistic pieces (e.g., capacitor + regen for a shield tank, or auspex + loaders for a battery gunship). At cp 1-2 (1 slot), a frigate can only ever hold a single isolated stat modifier.

Flat module pricing vs. percentage scaling penalizes small hulls: By h.cost=Math.round((60*cp+10)/5)*5, a cp 1 hull costs 70 credits and a cp 2 hull costs 130 credits, whereas a cp 5 hull costs 310 credits. Paying 80 credits for capacitor (+30% shield) or auspex (+10 aim) costs 114% of a cp 1 frigate's hull price for a tiny absolute shield/firepower return, whereas the same 80 credits is only 26% of a cp 5 capital ship's price and scales across a massive shield pool and 3+ weapon mounts.

Upward slot gravity under free movement: Because modules move freely and return far more value per slot on high-CP hulls, the player will always fill the 3 slots on their flagship/capitals and 2 slots on their cruisers first. Frigates only receive leftover modules after the rest of the fleet is completely saturated.

4. FREE REFIT MOVEMENT

What decision remains: Only two decisions remain: (1) during salvage/shopping, whether to strip a captured hull for the refit dictated by its weapons ("strip a captured ship and its weapons decide the refit") or buy a module with credits; and (2) before each battle, how to distribute your pool of owned modules onto the ships deployed for that node.

Build system or pre-battle checklist: It is a pre-battle checklist. When modules have zero commitment to a hull, ships have no persistent build identity; they are blank mannequins that share a communal wardrobe between fights.

Which should it be in Voidsworn: It should be a semi-committed build system with safe hull retirement (INFERRED from Voidsworn's roguelite fleet-tactics loop where hulls are captured, lost, and upgraded). Installing a module into an empty slot should be free, and scrapping/stripping/losing a hull should return its modules to inventory so players are never punished for upgrading hulls. However, detaching a module from an intact ship should either cost a small credit refit fee (e.g., 15-20 credits) or only be permitted at drydock/repair nodes - eliminating tedious pre-battle musical chairs while giving each ship a committed role.

5. THE FLAGSHIP RESERVE

Where FIT_SLOTS and MODULES collide:

Direct slot collision (FIT_SLOTS.module vs. h.slots): FIT_SLOTS defines "module:'one module slot (flagship only)'" (ship.fit.module), while data.js 545 already gives every hull 1 to 3 module slots (h.slots). Those two mechanisms cannot coexist cleanly without either stripping modules from non-flagships or giving the flagship a duplicate 4th module slot in a separate data structure.

Conceptual collision (FIT_SLOTS.pod + trim vs. HULL_VARIANTS): Look at HULL_VARIANTS (i_carrier_a and d_jackal_h): each variant swaps non-spinal weapon mounts (w:[...]) and applies a zero-sum stat trim (mod:{hangar:3,sh:22,sp:3} or mod:{hp:100}). FIT_SLOTS (pod + trim) is essentially a runtime builder for what HULL_VARIANTS already does via authored templates.

What the reserve should borrow and avoid:

Avoid: Drop FIT_SLOTS.module from ship.fit if fleet hulls keep h.slots, OR follow the earlier designer brainstorm ("flagship-only customization, with authored hull variants carrying fleet variety") by removing multi-slot generic MODULES from regular fleet hulls (capping non-flagships at 0 or 1 utility refit) so the flagship is the true multi-slot customization centerpiece.

Borrow: Bring the zero-sum trade-off design of FIT_SLOTS.trim and HULL_VARIANTS into the refit system itself (e.g., overcharged lances that cost shield regen, or heavy prow plating that costs -1 turn allowance), and reserve pure passive percentages (+10 aim, +15% hull, +10% crit) for PERKS and RELICS where they already live.

5. Questions for the designer

Is customization meant to live on the flagship (FIT_SLOTS + HULL_VARIANTS for the fleet) or across every hull in the fleet (h.slots = 1/2/3)?

Stakes: Keeping both gives the flagship two overlapping module systems (h.slots and ship.fit.module) and splits player attention between micro-managing 10+ generic stat tokens across the fleet and customizing their centerpiece ship.

Should the constitutional rule ("tree skills may not duplicate doctrine effects") be extended so MODULES cannot share fx keys with PERKS and RELICS?

Stakes: Until MODULES stops sharing raw stat keys (aim, crit, speed, hpPct, shPct, regenPct) with PERKS and RELICS, refits will read as redundant stat confetti rather than physical hardware that changes how a hull plays.

How can non-weapon modules (prow, drive, gyro, capacitor, regen, damcon) enter the player's inventory if salvage refits are decided strictly by a captured ship's weapons?

Stakes: If weapon-stripping only yields weapon-tagged refits (loaders, crystal, magazine, flakplus, hangar), defensive and mobility modules are either unreachable via capture salvage or require a fallback table not yet aligned with hull roles.
