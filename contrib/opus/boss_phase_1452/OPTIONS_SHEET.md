# Boss second phase: options sheet (issue 1452, run 1)

Stage: options only. Nothing is built. Ryan picks, then run 2 builds it in Godot (R377, R405).

**Short answer.** Every boss gets a real second half. At half health its escorts still flee (the morale beat stays), but
the boss then gets angrier instead of weaker: a banner says "Phase 2", and it starts a faction attack that is always shown on
the board one round before it lands. The final boss of the run also wakes the Hollow Sun itself.

**Fast way to answer:** "Board picks" takes every pick marked below. Or name the letter you want per row.

## What happens today (checked on main, Nov 23)

- The boss fires one Command around round 2 or 3, with a one round warning, and never again.
- At half health its escorts flee, and the boss also loses half its shields and all its repair. The second half is a
  cleanup: you are shooting a weaker ship that no longer does anything new.

## The same for every boss (the frame)

1. **Half health: escorts flee, as today.** That moment stays.
2. **The boss stops getting weaker.** It keeps its shields (no halving). Its repair stays off, so the fight still ends.
3. **A "Phase 2" banner** with the boss's name and what it is about to do, plus a sting in the music.
4. **Every phase 2 attack is warned one round ahead**: the hexes or ship it will hit glow on the board, and the log says
   "strikes next round". You always get one turn to move, brace or shoot the threat. No surprise hits.
5. **It repeats.** The phase 2 attack comes back every second round until the boss dies, so the second half has a rhythm.
6. **Only the boss's death wins a boss fight.** Today the "enemy fleet breaks" line could end the fight the moment the
   escorts flee, before phase 2 ever plays. In a boss fight that line goes away; killing the boss is the win.
7. **Fair edge cases:** a single hit that takes it from above half to dead skips phase 2; a save and reload never
   starts phase 2 twice.

## Per faction: what phase 2 does

Each faction keeps its own feel (RULEBOOK 4.5). The pick is in bold. Picks are spread so no two bosses play the same way.

### Sunward Crusade: Daybreak-class dreadnought

| | Option | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | **Burning Lance** | A straight line of hexes in front of it glows: its spinal cannon is charging. Next round, anything still in the line takes a heavy hit. Step out of the line, or brace and eat it. |
| B | Dawn Pyre | A ring of burning hexes appears around it. Ships that end their turn next to it take fire damage, so you have to fight it from range, where its guns are best. |

Why A: the spinal cannon is already its weapon, the line is the clearest thing to read on the board, and it fits a crusade.

### Meridian Directorate: The Iron Mandate

| | Option | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| A | Registry Broadside | Both of its side arcs glow. Next round, every ship beside it in those arcs is hit. Get in front of it or behind it. |
| **B (pick)** | **Call the Reserve** | Its escorts flee, and it calls a fresh reserve: two small ships, shown as markers at the board edge, arrive next round and every second round after. Kill the boss fast or deal with the line it keeps rebuilding. |

Why B: the Directorate is order and numbers; a boss that refills its line feels like them. It also leaves the "glowing
area" idea to Sunward and the Glasswrights, so the bosses stay different.

### Ashfall Hullbreakers: Warlord's Pyre-Barge

| | Option | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | **Ram and Board** | It marks one of your ships with a target sign. Next round it charges at full speed and tries to board it. Move the marked ship away, put a ship in the way, or brace and fight off the boarders. |
| B | Burn the Barge | It sets its own hull on fire. Each round it gets faster and hits harder, but burns its own hull down. Survive it or finish it fast. |

Why A: boarding is what the Hullbreakers are, and its Command is already called "Burn and board". B is a good backup if
you want a race instead of a chase.

### The Chorus: The Ninefold Maw

| | Option | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | **Brood Burst** | Two egg sacs swell beside it. Next round they hatch into new wings, unless you shoot them first. Shoot the eggs or the Maw: your choice each round. |
| B | Regrowth | It regrows hull every round unless at least two of your ships hit it that round. Spread your fleet's fire or it heals. |

Why A: it gives you a clear target choice every round, and it reads as the Chorus (living, breeding). B adds healing back
into a fight we just took healing out of.

### The Glasswrights: The Unfinished

| | Option | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | **Prism Lattice** | Three glass prisms rise around it. While any prism stands, its shield refills each round. Break the prisms first, then the ship. |
| B | Refraction | Its shield bends your shots: the first hit on it each round is turned back at the ship that fired. |

Why A: a few precise targets fits an elite faction (Glasswrights are elite, not a swarm), and it uses its shield ability.
B overlaps the Feedback Lattice reflect players already have, so it would feel borrowed.

### Eclipse: The Unsetting

| | Option | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | **Restore from the Archive** | The wrecks of escorts you killed earlier glow green. Next round, up to two of them rise as Eclipse ships again. Shoot the glowing wrecks to stop them. |
| B | Blink | Every second round it marks a hex elsewhere on the board and jumps there next round, turning its guns on whoever is closest. |

Why A: rising wrecks are what Eclipse is everywhere else in the game; today the half health break switches that off,
which is backwards for this faction. A shown wreck is also something you can act on.

## The final boss (sector 3): arena twist

This sits on top of the faction pick, whichever faction the last boss is. In sector 3 the Eclipse rival who came back fights
beside that boss (R384), and from run 5 on the new sun lights up over the board (R78, rule 11.6). The twist must sit with
both.

| | Option | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | **The Hollow Sun Wakes** | At the boss's half health, the Hollow Sun's gravity well opens in the middle of the board. Its edge grows one hex every second round, shown a round ahead. Ships close to it get extra speed (a slingshot); ships on the rim take damage and get pulled in. Both sides. |
| B | Solar Flare | Every second round, a flare sweeps one third of the board, shown a round ahead. It hits both sides; shields take most of it. |

Why A: the gravity well already exists in the game (pull, slingshot, tidal damage), so it is the real Hollow Sun, not a
new effect. It turns the map itself into the last enemy and gives a risk choice (slingshot speed against rim damage).

## Board pick in one line

Take the frame plus the bold picks: Sunward lance, Directorate reserve, Hullbreaker boarding charge, Chorus eggs,
Glasswright prisms, Eclipse rising wrecks, and the Hollow Sun waking for the final boss.

## What it costs and how we prove it (run 2)

- Build order: the frame and one boss first (Sunward, the easiest to see), prove it in game with a picture, then the other
  five, then the final twist. About 4 agent runs and 2 review rounds.
- Proof Ryan sees: picture BOSS-1 of the phase 2 banner with the warned hexes on the board, one per faction after that.
- Checks: the Godot rules checks against the saved results (rebaselined only for boss fights, each change explained), a
  test that phase 2 plays before any win line, a one-hit kill skips it, and a reload does not repeat it; then the balance
  sweep keeps Veteran boss win rates inside the guide band. Phase 2 makes bosses harder, so the boss's health may come down
  a little to stay in band.
- No new ship models are needed. Eggs, prisms, reserve ships and markers use existing parts and markers.

## Things to check in run 2 (not yet verified)

  of the enemy fleet the escorts are). Frame point 6 removes the risk either way; run 2 confirms with a sim.
- Shields kept at half health is the Board's call inside the frame. If phase 2 tests too hard, the first lever is boss
  health, not putting the shield cut back.
