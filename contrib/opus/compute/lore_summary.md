# Names and lore: the phone version

For Ryan, Oct 26 2026. Full detail: `lore_bible.md` (factions, looks, naming rules) and `rename_map.md` (every rename with file and line). Nothing is applied until you say so. Picks are marked **PICK**.

## The six factions

| Faction | Who they are | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Voidsworn Crusade** (kept) | Human oath-fleet. Their ancestors burned trying to relight the Hollow Sun; each generation swears to finish it and chalks its dead's names on the flagship. | Black iron, ember orange, spired beacon-tower ships; branded, weathered officers. |
| **Hegemony Navy** (kept) | The big human state. Correct, counted, numbered; losses "within acceptable limits". All the bureaucracy jokes. | Pale grey and steel blue, clean wedge hulls, rows of identical guns. |
| **Ashfall Corsairs** (kept) | Pirates of the Ashfall dens and Rattlehold. Stolen hulls, shares, debts, nicknames, Old Mott. | Rust and blood red, lopsided salvage ships, patchwork armour. |
| **The Chorus** (kept) | A swarm with one appetite that speaks as "we" (a chorus in the play sense; it never sings). Hunger and Memory argue inside it. | Grown, not built: purple-black flesh, ochre chitin, bile-yellow light; no faces. |
| **The Ithar** (kept) | A very old, very few people who sing their ships out of light and glass, and archive everything. No child named in 400 years. Song is theirs alone. | Pearl and gold thread, see-through light sails; long necks, black eyes, gold light under porcelain skin. |
| **The Deathless Court** (was Dynasty) | A court of machine minds waking from sealed vaults to resume its session and "correct" the living. Law and record, polite contempt. | Black stone and green light; minds are broken rings of ceramic plates round a green core, tended by small drones. |
