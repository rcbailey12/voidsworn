the in-game help and the faction names (rename_map_final.md: display names are Sunward Crusade, Ashfall Hullbreakers, the

everywhere; tone (one voice or many); text that tells instead of shows; echoes of other franchises close enough to read as a
copy; choices whose outcomes do not follow from the fiction; places the fiction could explain a mechanic for free.

Write `legs/<model>_lore.md`: up to 12 findings, worst first, each with a quote and its file:line, what is wrong, and a
proposed rewrite or fix. Then up to 5 things that are good and must be kept.
