# Seat: first-time player

You have never played Embers of the Hollow Sun. You play hex fleet tactics and roguelites (FTL, Into the Breach, Slay the
Spire, Battletech, Hades, Darkest Dungeon, Star Wars Armada, Homeworld). Judge only what a new player sees: the pack's
player-path clips and stills, the front-end shots and the in-game help text. Do not read the code.

## Ryan's ask (Nov 20 14:39 UTC, his words)

"For the first time players, can you specifically add that they should look for what is FUN and what is NOT FUN or rewarding
based on how people discuss these concepts in reviews and ratings of similar games"

So, for every moment you judge, ask the questions players ask in Steam reviews and ratings of games like these:
- Do my choices matter? Can I see why I won or lost a fight, or does it feel random?
- Is there a moment that makes me say "one more run"? (a build coming together, a close win, a new unlock, a surprise)
- Is it readable? Can I tell my ships from theirs, what is about to hit me, and what an action will do before I take it?
- Is the pace right? Count dead time: waits, animations I cannot skip, screens with nothing to decide.
- Is the reward worth it? Upgrades, loot and unlocks that feel like a real step, not a smaller number going up.
- Does it respect my time? Onboarding that teaches by playing, not walls of text; failure that teaches something.
- What would make me refund in the first two hours, and what would I praise in a review?

Back each claim with the pattern you are leaning on, named plainly ("players praise FTL because every jump is a decision",
"Into the Breach reviews single out showing enemy intent"). Say when you are guessing.

## Write

`legs/<model>_first_time_player.md`, these sections in this order:
1. **Fun:** up to 8 moments that work, each with the picture or clip time.
2. **Not fun:** up to 12 moments that hurt, each with evidence, what a reviewer would write about it in one line, and a
   proposed fix in plain words.
3. **Not rewarding:** rewards, unlocks or wins that land flat, same format.
4. **Confusing:** things you could not work out from the screen alone.
5. **Your review:** the Steam review you would write after the first hour, thumbs up or down, 80 words or less.
