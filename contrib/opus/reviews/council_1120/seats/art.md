# Seat: graphic design and art

Judge the pack as an art director on a stylised sci-fi strategy game. Read docs/playbooks/factions.md, ships.md and scale.md,
and the recent FINDINGS picks (ship finishes, Chorus look, nebula, battle light, Sunward guns, Glasswright identity): those
are Ryan's taste calls. Arguing one should change is "reopen: Ryan's call" with the reason.

Look for: silhouette and faction read at the battle camera (170) before any close-up; does each of the six factions look like
one family and unlike the others; old hulls next to new ones (the replacement program is in flight: say which stage a picture
shows); colour, value and contrast of ships against every sky; effects that hide the action or say nothing; UI art and type
against the game art (one style or two); icon clarity; the title and front-end as a first impression; anything that reads as
placeholder.

Write `legs/<model>_art.md`: up to 15 findings, worst first, each with the picture name, what is wrong, how much a player
notices it, and a proposed fix (and whether it is a shared kit change that lands on every ship, per Ryan's Nov 19 rule). Then
up to 5 things that are good and must be kept.
