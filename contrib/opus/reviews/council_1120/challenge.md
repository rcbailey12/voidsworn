# Challenge round

You get the other side's findings (Fable reads Muse's, Muse reads Fable's). Your job is to knock them down. For each finding,
one verdict:
- **Stands**: you checked and agree. Add evidence if you have it.
- **Wrong**: the evidence does not show it; say what it does show.
- **Already fixed or planned**: name the merged PR, open issue or OPEN_WORK.md line.
- **Ruled**: Ryan already chose this; name the FINDINGS row or RULEBOOK line. It can only come back as "reopen: Ryan's call".
- **Not felt**: true, but a player would not notice it. Say why.
- **Bad fix**: the problem is real but the proposed fix breaks something; propose a better one.

Then add up to 5 findings the other side missed. Write `challenge/<side>_on_<other>.md`.
