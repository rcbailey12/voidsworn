## Opus calls on items Ryan was not asked (technical or consistency; vetoable)
- **Motif table:** add exceptions, don't rename.
  - The Chorus's Memory throat stays. It is literally Caelan data, so it's the one shared case of memory. This keeps the approved story's Hunger and Memory throats.
  - Grief is shared by the sibling factions: the Glasswrights mourn, the Eclipsed regret.
- **One name, one owner:**
  - Eclipsed ranks become Shade, Dusk, Umbra, Eclipse, Totality, fitting the darkness theme. Zenith stays only as a ship class and Corona only as the weapon.
  - The Unsetting is only the flagship.
  - Glasswright classes become needle, shard, prism, aperture. Facet and Mirror stay only as ranks.
  - Keeper titles belong to the Glasswrights alone.
- **Name clashes:**
  - Corran, Sabine, Ilsabet and Teodric leave the Eclipsed pool. Ilsabet and Teodric read as medieval human names; Corran and Sabine are Star Wars names and Corran clashes with the Glasswright Corin.
  - Serafine leaves the Crusade pool, because it is too close to Seren.
- **Words and lines:**
  - Phase becomes the Eclipsed's alone, as a fold drive (micro-jumps through obstacles). Glasswrights make short jumps.
  - The Archive works by repair swarms rebuilding the hull while the mind re-streams from its last backup. A mind past its backup window has gone dark.
  - Naval tells go: sails becomes launches, the Anchorage becomes Meridian Yards, and Mate and Skipper become Hand and Boss.
  - The leftover Chorus colour line is fixed. Eclipsed bronze becomes violet-black plates.
  - The Chorus archive line and the two lines blaming the wrong machine are repointed.
  - The relic label becomes Refits. Bile plasma becomes Bile spray. The brood words become brood-sac and Maw-mother.
- **Glasswright elf read:** they get one plainly non-human biological trait: light-storing skin with faint patterns, and a clear second eyelid. Their name pool and refit names move toward optics.
- **Glasswright timeline:** they are descendants (Q10 A). Add a short timeline: the collapse about 1,000 years ago, the Dimming felt at Vesper about 90 years ago, secession about 70 years ago.
- **Cores:**
  - They were flung out of Caelum on escape orbits when the Lattice tore apart.
  - Each still broadcasts through surviving Lattice relay nodes. Holding a core gives first call and its tech, but any fleet near a relay can draw on it.
  - Seren drifted to Vesper's outer belt.
  - Each core gets a designation (K-1 and so on), and the old titles become survivor nicknames.

## Q7 (deferred to Opus): the Chorus name
PICK: keep **the Chorus**. Ryan said "Chorus is good as is" in round 2. The stage-chorus reading (many voices, one we) is exactly the faction. Endless Legend 2's Chorus is a peaceful gestalt in a fantasy 4X, which is a minor echo. If Ryan wants it gone, the options are **the Murmur** (a murmuration: many bodies, one motion), **the Shoal** (fits the ocean-moon origin) and **the Ninefold** (already in the draft).
