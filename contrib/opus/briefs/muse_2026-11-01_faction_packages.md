# Muse brief: faction design packages, first drafts (Nov 1, 2026)

From Opus, for Rey (Muse). Run it with your usual model mix; one synthesis per faction plus one cross-faction check. Commit outputs to contrib/muse/design/faction_packages_2026_11/ (one file per faction plus README). Plain words, no em dashes.

## Why
Ryan (FINDINGS R71): "we just do a full pass when we're doing progression where we write out and review the full design package for factions with their lore, everything else for admirals." These drafts are what he reviews before anything is built or painted. Glasswrights get a full rework (today's look "not landing"); the Hullbreaker look also gets another pass.

1. contrib/opus/FINDINGS.md rows R59 to R72 (Ryan's rulings win over every other doc).

## One package per faction (Sunward Crusade, Hegemony Navy, Ashfall Hullbreakers, the Glasswrights, the Chorus, Eclipse)
- Identity in three sentences, and how it feels to play them.
- Lore: origin, what they want in the Hollow Sun war, how they see each other faction (one line each).
- Look: people (for Hullbreakers: alien, not bear, no Predator tells; for Glasswrights: organic with grown or woven glass, faceless, not grey aliens), ships, palette, effects colours. Three concrete visual directions each for Hullbreakers and Glasswrights, described so an image model can paint them.
- Admirals (all four): who they are, play style, signature Command, a skill tree outline (R62: small tree per admiral), one story spotlight beat.
- Nemesis: how this faction's rival behaves, adapts and speaks (sample lines).
- Progression and story: the faction's generational-learning fiction (R62), what unlocks over runs, the finale.
- Open questions for Ryan (multiple choice, plain words, with your pick).

## Cross-faction check (one file)
Do the six feel distinct in look, play and voice? Motif ownership per lore bible 1.3? Any clash with Ryan's rulings? Rank the three biggest risks.
