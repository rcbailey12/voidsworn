## What Ryan saw (first Godot playtest, Nov 1 00:32 to 00:40 CDT, Eclipse, admiral Nekhet, 2 battles won)
His words, lightly trimmed:
- "Painting and ship visuals looked incomplete."
- "Effects looked very weak, especially at a distance and for smaller ships. You almost couldn't tell anything was happening."
- "Ship movement feels way too jerky and fast still. I'm fine slowing things down. It doesn't feel like a space battle right now. It feels rushed and frenzied and sped up."
- "Not sure why the first system or map is immediately the dead star."
- "Hard to tell what's movement squares versus other map hexes."
- "Background asteroids and imagery still needs a serious upgrade."
- "It wasn't always clear what was my next move or what ship I should be moving or what to do when I was done shooting. Or even when I got a reinforcement ship."

What the code shows:
- Movement plays as segments from rest to rest (battle.gd `_move_segments`, ship_view.gd `play_moves`): every turn and every straight run starts and stops dead, at 280 to 430 ms per hex. That is the jerky, rushed feel.
- The dead-star shape can roll in any fight on the storm and graveyard maps (battle.js:52-54, ENCOUNTERS shapes); the Glasswrights' regions (nebula, deep) open on those maps, so a Glasswright sector can open on the dead star.
- The run log (campaign.gd `tel()`) only records battle start and end and reward picks: nothing about turns, hesitation or clicks.
- A 200-battle parity sweep (lane B) ran through the whole playtest and wrote into the same godot.log, wiping the session's error log. Fixed: run_timeout.ps1 now waits while the game is open (see "PC").
