# Where Embers falls short of the four fleet and tactics benchmarks

## 1. Top 10 gaps

**Start with the loss report.** The player can learn what happened to a ship while the fight is running. At the end of a run, the screen records two ships lost but cannot answer which enemy took the ship that mattered. A player who understands that loss has a reason to change their next order, refit or route.

Main reviewed at `dd62d3b1e`, November 24. Ranking weighs how often the player meets the problem, what decision it costs them and what the open work will still leave. Sizes are agent runs. **S** is one, **M** is two to four, **L** is five or more. **OBSERVED** means the named picture or game run shows the stated behavior. **INFERRED** means the current game file supports it and a player run still needs to establish its impact. A dated picture illustrates only the feature named.

## 3. What already holds up

- **The fleets have identities at two distances.** At battle distance the Directorate hull reads as a broad armoured mass beside the tapered Eclipse ship and the ribbed Chorus body. Up close the Prism's pearl skin opens onto coloured glass, with two separate drive tips. Preserve those differences while tuning overlays. [ST2 layout, installed bench][st2] · [I15_11 Prism, installed look proof][prism]
- **The first-target calculation agrees with actual enemy activations in the tested cases.** On this main revision, 16 headless Godot cases passed. In the taunt case x05 switched to x04. An arrived reinforcement, x07, had a valid target. Classic and Alternating, Standard and Veteran all ran. That is a solid base for a player-facing warning. [Threat preview test][threatcheck]
- **Loss already leads somewhere.** The debrief places renown, the next admiral and the ship that finished the run on one page. The loss report in rank 1 can open from the timeline without delaying the next run. [DB1 loss][loss] · [current debrief][ledger]
- **A busy battle can draw within budget.** In the recorded Ship Look V2 test on Ryan's RTX 5080, the second 2160p session in out_gate_on2160/scene_gate.json (build 5736804b) had a 5.29 ms GPU median and a 5.90 ms frame median, with zero late shader compiles after warm-up. That leaves room under the project's 12.5 ms GPU gate in that scene. The first playable frame and round-specific spikes are separate measurements. [Scene gate][gate] · [Board's three-session result][gateout]
