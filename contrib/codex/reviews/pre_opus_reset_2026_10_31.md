## What should survive into the implementation queue

The audits contain substantial correct work. These are the strongest concrete targets from this pass:

- The shipwright two-ship repair bug is real in both sources. Preserve the chosen upgrade target's uid and repair a different eligible ship. Muse's text review explained the re-sort correctly; its later ledger substituted the wrong same-name mechanism.
- The seer Glasswright option is unreachable; haven lacks the ruled free leave option; presence-only flags do not enforce choice-specific requirements. Fix the relevant data and compare stored option keys where the outcome matters.
- Off-core fillers miss favour rarity treatment; eligible legendaries overwrite rolled duos. Fix those mechanisms independently and retain the real distribution tests.
- The approach-column elite guarantee fails on **84 of 4,500** seeded maps. Prefer the existing patch's explicit fallback conversion and retain reachability checks.
- Module fitting can install ineffective refits, and the authoritative equip action trusts the UI. Share one compatibility predicate between offer rendering and mutation, including slots/ability/kind/shields.
- The veteran moving-fire path bypasses the brace decision in JS and the Godot port. Preserve boarding-before-brace behavior rather than blindly applying the first patch block.
- The Nemesis loss branch does not guarantee a debrief weakness reveal; adaptation selection does not exclude counters to revealed weaknesses. Both ruled fixes remain real.
- The base Ascendant speed is 1; the speed-2 ruling and variant trade/disclosure rulings remain implementation work. Do not infer that all power packages or faction bosses must become symmetrical.
