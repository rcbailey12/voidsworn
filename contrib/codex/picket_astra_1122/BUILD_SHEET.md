# Astra Picket · hegemony_H7A

The approved top view sets the long taper, narrow clipped nose and broad aft shoulders. The side view sets a shallow deck rise toward the stern. The build uses nine transverse sections. The lower bevels meet a level sole at every section. ## Major masses and fittings

The frigate is 6.5 game units long. The bow points along +X, port along +Y and the deck along +Z in Blender. Stations below are percentages of the fitted length measured aft from the muzzle.

| Station | Fitting | Construction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 5% | Blunt gun face and twin recessed bores | Open hand-built cheek sleeve around a fixed Navy plasma casemate. Each barrel has a forward laser hard point. |
| 5 to 26% | Forebody | Broad bevels carry the taper into the deck. The forebody ends at the capacitor frame. |
| 33% | Sensor | One low swept Navy receiver, on a clear mounting pad ahead of the dorsal gun. |
| 50% | Boarding doors | A matching pair interrupts the waist light. The boarding origins sit at the doors. |
| 62% | Dorsal barbette | One turning Navy mount with three short picket-laser emitters. The receiver clears its firing arc. |
| 77 to 100% | Drive module | Paired rectangular nozzle pods seat in the stern. Two dorsal louvres cover the feeds. |
| Full belly | Service trunk and heat rejection | Five removable centreline covers carry paired runners. Ten slatted cooler cassettes sit either side. |

## Inside

| Piece | Break line | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bow | Ring at 26% | Feed racks and capacitor banks around the forward passage. |
| Midships | Rings at 26% and 77% | Sensor equipment, control racks and reactor, with a deck through the compartment. |
| Stern | Ring at 77% | Paired drive racks behind the aft frame. |

Each joint has an internal open frame, an external collar and a slight jog in the break path. The two breach origins are on opposite flanks at the joints. Blue emergency strips run along the equipment racks.

## Changed from the picture

Ryan's flat-belly change replaces the rising keel with a continuous maintenance floor. The underside carries removable panels and coolers. The smooth dorsal cover in the beauty becomes a low barbette with the three working emitters specified on the concept sheet. The flange beneath the sensor has clear deck around it.
