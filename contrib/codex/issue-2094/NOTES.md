### How to read the comparison

- **H9A vs H9B:** broad box-nose and parallel sides vs a flare into the stern. Their sides are a flat roof over a thick belt vs a roof that rises toward the aft block.
- **Against H7 Picket:** the dart has a pinched nose and an elliptical taper. H9A carries its width through the nose and belt. H9B widens to a square rear without tapering back in.
- **Against H8 Cordon:** Cordon has a wide forward body and a hard inward step. H9A holds its beam and H9B gains beam going aft. Cordon's side steps down, where H9B rises.
- **Against H10 Dominion:** Dominion has a shoulder bulge and a split stern in overhead outline. Neither Vanguard has either feature. In profile H9A holds a flat plate line and H9B rises well past the low bow, where Dominion loses depth toward the engine end.
- **Against run 3:** both alternatives remove the open central well and the split stern. No pair in the comparison was confusable by eye in the combined top and side views at the printed scale.
