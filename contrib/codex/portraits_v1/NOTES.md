# Admiral portraits

Issue #1897 delivers twelve native 1254 × 1254 PNGs, two per faction. The 1280 × 557 contact sheet places each faction's two portraits in one column, with the admiral id below each image.

Codex's built-in ImageGen produced eleven new portraits in run 1. Sable is the byte-for-byte copy of the approved [issue #1887 render](../portraits_test/NOTES.md), and its original prompt is reproduced below. Neon's run 3 brief at `cc683e37` supplied the faction and admiral details. R439 and R444 put the Eclipse admirals at the hearts of their ships as distinct, removable machine minds.

Run 2 used only built-in ImageGen to redraw four portraits. Ashgrove signals a launch, Maren directs an officer at the shield plot, Anuket is a compact upright scout mind in its mainframe cradle, and Mending is one suspended Node stitching a wound shut. The other eight PNGs are byte-for-byte unchanged.

Run 3 used only built-in ImageGen to redraw Anuket and Mending. Anuket is an exposed, detachable crystal-and-metal mind at the hub of the Eclipse scout mainframe. Mending rises from the healing chamber floor and pulls its torn walls shut with braided sinew arms. The other ten PNGs, including Ashgrove and Maren, are byte-for-byte unchanged. The contact sheet is the run 3 paint proof.

## Review notes

- Grax shows four eyes in one brow row and a split jaw. Tamsa has six back vanes and Saro has four. The Matriarch has nine brood throats.
- The Ashgrove and Maren redraws used their run 1 PNGs as identity and uniform references. The Ashgrove prompt mistakenly says Kara, but the reference and finished portrait are Commodore Sera Ashgrove.
