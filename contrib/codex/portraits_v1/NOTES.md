# Admiral portraits

Issue #1897 delivers twelve native 1254 × 1254 PNGs, two per faction. The 1280 × 527 contact sheet places each faction's two portraits in one column, with the admiral id below each image.

Codex's built-in ImageGen (`image_gen`) produced eleven new portraits. Each began with its own text prompt. The three retouched portraits used their own earlier renders as edit inputs. Sable is the byte-for-byte copy of the approved [issue #1887 render](../portraits_test/NOTES.md), and its original prompt is reproduced below.

Neon's run 3 brief at `cc683e37` supplied the faction and admiral details. R444 puts Nekhet's large ring at the center of the Archive mainframe and Anuket's small spindle at the data junction of a scout's fold-drive spar. Both machines have visibly connected optical paths and releasable mounting hardware.

## Review notes

- Grax shows four eyes in one brow row and a split jaw. Tamsa has six back vanes and Saro has four. The Matriarch has nine brood throats. Mending Throng's light comes from the pool beneath the organ.
- Anuket's etched Eclipse crest has fine radial lines within the disk.
