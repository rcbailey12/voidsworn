## Scope and method

This audit judges whether Opus's approved go forward plan, findings registry and engine test spec actually use and critically review the research corpus produced by Rey/Muse, DeepSeek, Qwen, Gemini and the chair reviewer. Evidence base: the auditor notes supplied in the request. DeepSeek treated those notes as the verified source material and did not re fetch the raw repository files. That means every judgment below is limited to what the notes state. Where the notes say a file exists but give no line, the claim is marked UNVERIFIED rather than inferred. Where a FINDINGS row was not summarized in the notes (most of R1 to R21, F1 to F27, S1 to S7, B-1 to B-16, D1 to D20 and X1 to X11), it is not judged. Those rows are "not assessed" in this audit. Judgment categories: USED means cited or applied correctly, with file and line, commit or clear documentary evidence in the notes. MISSED means corpus material Opus should have used but the plan or spec does not show. CONTRADICTED means Opus says something the corpus or game code says otherwise, with both sides quoted. UNVERIFIED means an Opus claim with no file and line or primary source in the notes. This is research and analysis only. No code was written, nothing was bought and nothing was posted.
