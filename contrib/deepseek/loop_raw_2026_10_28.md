## Overall verdict

**THE LOOP LEAKS** - strongest at run 1; weakest at run 4, the wall before the run-5 milestone, judged as shipped unless I say "as designed."

## Findings, ranked most severe first

1. **BLOCKER - As shipped, the meta-progression hook is bypassed.** OBSERVED: at boot the game "grants all LEGACY items and all admirals" unless `unlockAll` is false. **Judging as shipped:** renown spending and the admiral unlock ladder are not load-bearing; the "one more run" reason collapses toward battle quality alone. This is the single biggest discrepancy between design and shipped state.

2. **FIX - Heat is invisible to most players until they win.** OBSERVED: "There is no difficulty selection before the first win" and "Heat only adds difficulty after a win." With about 17% of runs won, most players in runs 1-5 get no Ascension/Pact-like next question after failure.

3. **FIX - Failure pays, but mostly in currencies.** OBSERVED: "After the run, win or lose, the meta pays out" renown, admiral mastery XP, core sync and stats. INFERRED: unlike Hades' "failure is the progression engine," the brief states no story or failure-specific content payoff; if the payout does not cross an unlock threshold, a loss can feel like a tax. The brief does not give payout sizes.

4. **FIX - Run 5 is win-gated, not run-gated.** OBSERVED: "Heat (unlocked by the first win)" and mastery unlocks are level-based. INFERRED: a player who has not won by run 5 gets no Heat and may still lack the fourth aspect/second Command; run 5 can ask the same question as run 2.

5. **FIX - The run-10 long tail is thin.** OBSERVED: "no post-win content beyond Heat and completion across factions and admirals"; also "no daily seeds, no challenge modes." INFERRED: run 10 asks Heat and completion only; the brief does not cover Heat level count or cap, so depth cannot be judged.

6. **NOTE - Inside-run scaling is legible and protectable.** OBSERVED: "Each node shows its encounter ... and its reward label in advance"; CP, credits, protocols and captures all scale the fleet. INFERRED: this is the strongest planning hook because the player can route toward cores and visible rewards.

7. **NOTE - The Nemesis is a real run-specific hook but back-loaded.** OBSERVED: it "can be permanently killed only in sector 3" and adapts to your build. INFERRED: many failed runs may never collect that payoff, and the brief states no meta payoff for the kill.

8. **NOTE - Permanent loss creates stakes and a likely leak point.** OBSERVED: "deaths cluster at sector bosses and sector transitions." INFERRED: the post-boss loss screen is where the meta payout must sell the next run; if it shows only small currency, the player who liked battles has no new question.

9. **NOTE - Unlock costs may stall early meta.** OBSERVED: "admiral unlocks (cost ladder 0/30/45/80 per faction)" and 12 LEGACY items. INFERRED: without renown payout data, early runs may not buy a meaningful unlock; the brief does not cover exact prices or payouts.

10. **NOTE - The reference loops sharpen the diagnosis.** OBSERVED: Hades treats "failure is the progression engine"; Slay the Spire uses "Ascension levels (a 20-step difficulty ladder)"; FTL has unlockable ships earned by quests. INFERRED: Voidsworn has analogues - Heat, mastery, sync - but gates the strongest long-tail questions behind winning and lacks a stated failure-narrative or long difficulty ladder.

## Checks

**a. THE HOOK.** As built, the hook is: visible route planning, 3-take-1 protocols/refits, core-signal routing, a Nemesis that adapts, and post-run renown/mastery/sync. As shipped, it is decorative because all LEGACY items and admirals are granted, so across-run unlocks do not carry the loop; battle quality alone carries it. As designed, it is partly load-bearing, but thin: failure pays currencies and XP, not a stated new story or mode. The loop does not collapse without the meta as designed, but retention does.

**b. POWER SCALING.** Inside a run, scaling is strong and readable: command points cap growth, credits buy hulls/refits/repairs, protocols stack rule-changing bonuses, prizes and salvage add ships, captains rank up. OBSERVED: node rewards are visible "in advance, including which core's signal it carries." INFERRED: a player can plan around this. Across runs, scaling is legible in principle - renown, mastery, sync, Heat - but as shipped the renown/admiral half is bypassed. As designed, the brief does not give exact prices or payout rates, so pacing cannot be judged. Compared with FTL's scrap tension, Hades' failure engine, and StS's Ascension ladder, Voidsworn's inside-run scaling is the stronger half; its across-run scaling is present but under-gated and under-measured in this brief.

**c. RUN 1 / RUN 5 / RUN 10.**

**Run 1:** the player earns first experience, likely first mastery/sync progress, and - only on a win - Heat. The new question is "can I win and unlock the harder rules?" Failure to land: with 17% wins, most run-1 players lose and get no Heat, so the next question is only "can I buy something with renown?"

**Run 5:** as designed, a winning player should have some mastery levels, possibly alternate fleet/fourth aspect/second Command, some core sync, and Heat. The new question is "can I win under Heat or with a new fleet?" Failure to land: if the player has not won, run 5 is not a milestone; it is another pre-Heat run with the same difficulty question.

**Run 10:** as designed, the player has Heat, faction/admiral completion targets, mastery and sync. The new question is "can I complete factions/admirals and push Heat?" Failure to land: "no post-win content beyond Heat and completion" makes run 10 thin unless the player is self-motivated by completion; the brief does not cover Heat cap or ladder depth.

**d. THE LEAK.** Most likely run 4, at the post-run screen after a sector 2 flagship loss or sector transition. INFERRED assumption: exact run number depends on win rate. By run 4 the novelty is gone; if the player has not won, Heat is still locked, the next mastery unlock may not have landed, and the payout screen shows renown/mastery/sync but no visibly new next-run question. A player who liked the battles can still quit there because the next run looks identical.

**e. FAILURE'S PAYOFF.** A failed Voidsworn run pays renown, admiral mastery XP, core sync, stats, and a Hall of the Fallen memory. That is real progress, but it is mostly currency/XP. Compared with Hades, where failure advances story and permanent upgrades, or FTL, where a failed route still teaches and unlocks ships, Voidsworn's failure payoff is enough only if it crosses a visible unlock threshold or advances a mastery/sync tier. As shipped, the all-unlocked LEGACY/admirals state weakens even that. The brief does not cover payout sizes, so I cannot judge whether the amounts are sufficient.

## THE ONE CHANGE

1. Un-gate Heat from the first win and let it open after the first loss, so every failed run can pose a new next-run question using the existing Heat rules. Cost: rebalancing early difficulty, UI for opt-in Heat, and risk of overwhelming new players; keep it optional and clearly explained.

## PROTECT

1. **Visible routing and reward information** - node encounters, archetypes, modifiers, reward labels and core signals. This is the planning spine.
2. **The 3-take-1 protocol/refit reward chain with skip/reroll and the credits/shipyard spend-or-save tension.** This is the legible inside-run scaling.
3. **Permanent loss and the Nemesis stakes.** Do not soften these to chase retention; they are the identity of the run.

## NOT CHECKED

The brief does not let me judge exact prices, renown payouts, drop rates, Heat level count or cap, battle quality/balance, AI tuning, Nemesis tuning, event-pool quality, narrative quality, whether `unlockAll` is an intentional ship state or debug bypass, platform/monetization, or the precise death distribution beyond "cluster at sector bosses and sector transitions."

## QUESTIONS FOR THE DESIGNER

1. Is the boot `unlockAll` grant a debug bypass or the intended shipped state? If intended, what progression fantasy replaces renown and admiral unlocks?
2. Should Heat/difficulty open after the first loss rather than only the first win, and what first-win rate is intended for run 1?
3. Is "Heat + faction/admiral completion" the intended run-10 long tail, or is a post-win mode expected later?
