Cost: route planning loses a promised fight tier, a small but concrete contract violation in a roguelite map.

## Verdict

Verdict: The 80 percent loss contract is not the wall; the Nemesis sharpening against battered fleets is, and the first run never explains either.

Most important attack: The Nemesis growth cost against battered arrivals, from +14.6pp at full strength to +38.1pp at 40% hull.
