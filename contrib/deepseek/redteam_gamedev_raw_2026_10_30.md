# Game dev red team report: Voidsworn, Oct 30 2026

1. OBSERVED. Critical: the customization layer cannot produce a build because fitting is free, instant, reversible, and duplicate effects stack additively. Evidence: modules synthesis finding 1, "Equip and unequip carry no cost, cooldown, or location restriction"; modules finding 2, "Five modules duplicate five captain perks effect-for-effect"; boons mechanism, "no caps, no overwrites". Cost: optimal play is to refit the whole fleet before every fight, so modules become a pre-battle checklist, and the same lever can be bought through modules, perks, and relics at once. This is the core systems failure: the catalog has distinct jobs, but the structures around it erase commitment.

2. OBSERVED. Critical: the ruled CP principle does not survive the shipped data. Evidence: faction shape finding 3, "all six frigates cost 1 CP and all destroyers 2 CP"; faction shape finding 1, "genEnemyFleet spends CP only; FAC_PRICE is never consulted"; faction shape finding 2, "FAC_PRICE for deathless has no Battleship key". Cost: elite versus swarm is invisible at the ladder bottom, enemy fleets cannot express faction credit asymmetry, and the ruled Eclipse battleship prices below Eclipse's own cruiser. The CP principle is settled, but the data does not implement it.

3. OBSERVED. High: the weapon table still sells paint as choice. Evidence: weapons synthesis finding 1, "flak and gsentry are the same weapon"; finding 2, "turbo and gauss share an expected-damage line"; finding 3, "hbattery strictly dominates battery". Cost: weapon choice and faction differentiation collapse wherever the table, not a trait, is supposed to carry the difference. The arsenal's spine is readable, but a shipping tactics game cannot charge a slot for a literal duplicate and two statistical clones.

4. OBSERVED. High: the carrier and squadron layer is a reskin plus generic fill. Evidence: squadrons synthesis finding 1, "The spore wing is a bomber reskin"; finding 2, "The carrier archetype is one carrier plus generic fill"; finding 5, "Launch occupies the carrier's only ability slot while doing nothing until a wing dies". Cost: the carrier fantasy and wing choices lack mechanical identity. A Chorus signature wing does the bomber's job with a Chorus label, and the carrier's ability slot is dead until a wing dies.

5. OBSERVED. High: the AI verdict "COMPETENT BUT HOLLOW" overstates the competent half for moving shooters. Evidence: AI synthesis finding 1, "the brace decision is bypassed for every moving ship that shoots"; "aiUnit returns before the veteran pre-fire brace decision". Cost: veteran ships that move and fire lose a defensive decision on most activations. This is a verified implementation hole in the battle brain, not a tuning note. I would downgrade the competent half until this is fixed or deliberately kept.

6. OBSERVED. High: AI composition and targeting are hollow around a real core. Evidence: AI synthesis finding 2, "Composition has a readable outline and a random interior"; finding 3, "Targeting is a blended scalar with no readable priority"; finding 4, "The cleverness is veteran-gated". Cost: the player cannot reliably bait, screen, or sacrifice a hull because targeting has no readable priority, and new players meet the hollow AI before the readable one. A shipping tactics game needs composition roles and playable targeting rules, not only a smarter scalar.

7. OBSERVED. Medium-high: module guards are incomplete, so silent no-op fits are possible. Evidence: modules synthesis finding 3, "can be installed on hulls lacking the relevant weapon kind and silently do nothing"; "Only hangar carries an only field". Cost: a player can spend a slot and credits on a module that does nothing, with no warning. The hangar guard proves the pattern exists; it was not applied to every weapon-kind module. This is a trust defect in the fitting screen.

8. OBSERVED. Medium: boon gating and duplicate job collapse remain value defects. Evidence: boons synthesis mechanism 3, "L_storm is absent from the table"; finding 2, "Pyre of Stars is a flat 40 chain explosion"; finding 1, "the ram block is one job sold three times". Cost: a shieldless fleet can be offered a full shield refill, the legendary explosion buys the epic effect a second time at the pool's most expensive slot, and the ram block piles three checkpoints into one build path instead of branching. Additive stacking makes these value defects rather than dead picks, which is worse for slot diversity.

9. OBSERVED. Medium: the data record and port surface carry contradictions and silent deletions. Evidence: weapons synthesis finding 4, "Range 6" against "TORP_RING [0,5,15] and range 5"; finding 5, "startorp's evMult:2 is overwritten"; claims register, "third silent weapon deletion found Oct 30 (b_razor_s drops bioplasma undisclosed)". Cost: the Godot port inherits a design comment that contradicts live constants, dead data carried by the shared-data rule, and a weapon variant that silently drops a weapon. The future build will trust the wrong record unless these are reconciled deliberately.

10. OBSERVED. Medium-low: the repair economy has no binding tension in the model. Evidence: claims register, "repair tension binds in 0.0 percent of 60,000 modeled regroups"; standing constraint, "Repair always beats scrap-and-replace per ship by design". Cost: the ruling is settled, but the intended repair-versus-replace decision never binds. The economy pays for a choice that the model says players will not face. If that is accepted, it should be accepted as a deliberately flat economy, not as a working tension.

## Verdict

Verdict: do not cut over; the systems do not compose into a build-and-battle tactics game, and the single most important attack is that free reversible fitting plus additive duplicate effects removes the build layer before combat.
