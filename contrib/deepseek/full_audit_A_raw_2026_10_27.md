3. New names collision check

Ardent / Ardenti: MINOR ECHO. Warhammer 40k has "Ardentites", a Puritan philosophy within the Inquisition (source: wh40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Ardentites). The echo is close enough to flag. Alternatives: Helion and the Helions, or Ostra and the Ostrani (both already offered at line 44).

Totality: REAL COLLISION. "The Terran Totality" is a human faction in the Starfarer/Fractal Softworks community setting (source: fractalsoftworks.com forum thread "What May Be Safely Written: The Eldest"). The name is used for a space-faring human government. Alternatives: the Occlusion, or the Eclipse (both offered at line 45).

Nadir: MINOR ECHO. "Nadir" is a weapon foundry in Destiny (source: destinypedia.com, "Nadir") and a criminal organisation in Star Wars Roleplay (source: starwarsrp.net/threads/nadir.91742). It is also a common English word. Alternatives: Umbra, or Shade.

Siphoning: CLEAR. The word is generic and no franchise owns it in this exact sense.

Vesper Reach: MINOR ECHO. "Vesta Reach" appears in a Halo fan setting (source: forums.sufficientvelocity.com, "Halo: Acendent"), and "Vesper" is the title of a 2022 science fiction film. The combination is distinct enough. Alternatives: Cinder Reach, or Ashen Reach.

Cortical link: MINOR ECHO. "Cortical stack" is the central implant in Altered Carbon (source: Richard K. Morgan, Altered Carbon, 2002; also shaviro.com summary). "Cortical linkage" is a 40k term (source: test40k.lexicanum.com/wiki/Cortical_linkage). The phrase is close to both. Alternatives: neural port, or sync implant.

Pulse laser: MINOR ECHO. The term is used across many games: EVE Online (source: wiki.egosoft.com, "Pulse Laser"), Starfield (source: creations.bethesda.net), Barotrauma (source: barotraumagame.com). It is generic enough to keep, but it is not distinctive. Alternative: pulse beam.

Meridian Yards: MINOR ECHO. "Meridian Station" is a location in Halo 5 (source: primagames.com mission briefing). "Meridian" is a common place name. Alternative: Aphelion Yards.

Other new proper nouns spotted: "the Unsetting" (line 512) is clear. "The Ninefold Maw" (line 381) is locked. "Vyre" (line 36) is clear. "Kharon" (line 35) echoes the Greek ferryman but is not a franchise collision. "Seren" (line 40) is a Welsh word for star and is clear.

6. Gameplay fit

The Glasswrights cannot brace. Lore line 417 says "They cannot brace". Data.js line 539 says "ithar hulls cannot Brace". This matches.

The Chorus has no shields and hull regen. Lore line 350 says "No shields at all; flesh regrows every round". Data.js line 19 describes the Chorus as "No shields at all, but their flesh knits back together every round". This matches.

The Totality reanimation from the Archive. Lore line 481 says repair swarms rebuild each hull once while minds re-stream from the last Archive backup. Data.js line 25-26 describes the Deathless (now Totality) as having wrecks that "stand back up". This matches.

The Totality Nemesis comeback is sector 3 only. Lore line 481 says "comes back once after he is killed (sector 3 only, your ruling)". This matches R49.

The Hullbreaker Nemesis steals a ship and it returns when he dies. Lore line 280 says "a ship he steals comes back to you when he dies (your ruling)". This matches R49.

The Glasswright Nemesis who flees returns with the same four-ship elite fleet, upgraded. Lore line 417 says "if he flees, he comes back with the same four-ship elite fleet, upgraded". This matches R49.

Plasma is light blue. Lore line 250 says "Plasma lance ... (light blue)". This matches R46.

The Hegemony formation sync around the flagship and ion shield-stripping. Lore line 228 says "Formation around the flagship. Fire syncs near the flagship (the network), ion cannons strip shields". This matches data.js line 498 and the faction description.

No other gameplay contradictions found.
