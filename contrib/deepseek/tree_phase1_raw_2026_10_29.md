### CROSS-FACTION NOTES
- Free-volley Command collision (Vex, Saro, Voice of the Deep, Vessane): separated by payoff axis, not wording. Vex reassigns and compounds volleys like ledger entries; Saro records flaws and pierces shields; Voice shreds armor and spreads acid; Vessane chains consecutive hits and saturates shields.
- Shield-wall Command collision (Thale, Maren, Brin, Seveth): Thale transfers and defers the wall as an oath; Maren pools shields and banks a reprisal; Brin refracts into temporary shields and interference; Seveth trades refill for layered armor and records prevented damage.
- Carrier Command collision (Ashgrove, Reyes, Mott, Matriarch, Tamsa): each owns a different decision. Ashgrove decides who the extra wing guards; Reyes decides squadron interlock and recall; Mott decides which junk persists past the round; the Matriarch decides swarm splitting; Tamsa decides sequencing against already-struck targets.
- Movement/evasion Command collision (Ironmaw, Razormaw, Vell): Ironmaw spends movement to ram and leaves scar trails; Razormaw circles to rear arcs and denies facing; Vell uses decoys and post-move repositioning, with no ram or rear-arc payoff.
- Repair Command collision (Vesh, Mending Throng, Aurex): Vesh donates repair and keeps armor; the Throng clears debuffs and banks a persisting knit; Aurex alone uses stored/buffered repairs and a single conversion of a destroyed ship into continued fighting (no bonus paid for the loss). Also flagged: the delayed-volley motif recurs across several T1s as a decision, but each is scoped differently (withhold one volley, one reload, one repair, or one wing), and every faction's shared Branch B/C tier-1 repeats the reserve/escort and flagship-reroll frame, varied only in faction payoff.
