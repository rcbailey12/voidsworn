1. BLOCKER: Persistent per-weapon damage language is mostly missing or generic, so the R16 bar and fleet readability fail. OBSERVED: "only gauss and bio have their own" persistent marks; "Plasma's persistent trace is the same generic orange-ember scorch"; "Ion runs the identical _beam_burn as lance"; "Flak: NOT IMPLEMENTED"; "Ram routes into _big_hit at 1.5 times scale". INFERRED: at 20 ships, battery, lance, ion, flak, ram and boarding do not leave readable, weapon-specific history, so damage reads as transient flashes over generic scorch. Cost: players cannot learn weapon identity or damage history from hulls, and the cinematic benchmark's "holes with streaming parts" language is not met.

2. BLOCKER: The counted-flash budget does not cover the screen, so the 3 flashes per second hard cap is not a real guarantee. OBSERVED: "the budget certifies a gated subset" while "several of the brightest emitters bypass it"; the review's bar is "WCAG counts flashes on screen". INFERRED: a budget that excludes the brightest emitters cannot certify the accessibility cap or the merged 0-flash reduce mode. Cost: photosensitivity risk and a false compliance claim; the effects pass may ship with a budget that looks green while worst-case salvos violate the cap.

3. HIGH: The Compatibility fallback silently erases every persistent decal mark. OBSERVED: "Compatibility has NO decals in 4.7"; Godot "auto-falls back to Compatibility" since 4.4; the brief says to test or gate via "get_current_rendering_method()". INFERRED: if the fallback ships selectable or auto-triggers, all scorch, hole, corrosion and crater marks vanish while effects still run. Cost: the damage language collapses on lower-end or unsupported GPUs, exactly the audience floor the brief says to design for.

4. HIGH: Fleet-distance readability is not implemented for the marks that need companion cues. OBSERVED: gauss entry hole is "the smallest persistent mark in the game" with no distance-tier cue; torpedo crater "does not exist" at the hit point; flak "never-exposes-structure rule" is inverted because flak chip damage drives stage 3 and 4 breaches; silhouette change is only the stage ladder's edge bite at "a pre-picked anchor". INFERRED: the strongest channels, shape family and silhouette, are absent where the reference says they carry the read. Cost: at 20 ships players see damage but cannot identify source or severity without zooming, which defeats the tactics read.

5. HIGH: Fresh, cooling and scarred states are broken for hit and hole decals, and repair does not reverse them. OBSERVED: scorch emission is "fixed emission 0.7"; hole heat is "a static uniform"; the DECAL shader header "claims cooling its fragment code does not implement"; repair reverses stage geometry but "hit decals are never removed". INFERRED: a repaired section still wears scorch and holes, and a fresh hit does not visibly cool over turns. Cost: the hull contradicts game state, so repair feedback and turn-based damage freshness are unreadable.

6. HIGH: The per-section mark budget and repeat merging are not built; marks can be evicted or bypass trimming. OBSERVED: "One ship-wide array capped at 10" with trim only in add_hit_scorch; "gauss and bio appends bypass the trim"; "No per-section budget, no repeat growth or merging anywhere". INFERRED: repeated hits do not deepen a section's history; they can place twins or evict older marks. Cost: the damage layer cannot show a ship's history at a glance, and the spec's per-section budget is absent.

7. HIGH: Faction effect colours are not brightness-separable from their own glow, and engine colours are worse. OBSERVED: worst weapon against own glow is "Chorus at 1.02:1"; every faction sits between "1.02:1 and 1.40:1"; Hegemony against Eclipse engine colours fall to "14.6 under deuteranopia"; the pale cluster stays within "30 to 61". INFERRED: hue alone carries faction identity in effects, and engine trails can collapse under red-green deficiency. Cost: faction silhouettes and materials lose their effect signature in motion, especially for colour-blind players.

8. MEDIUM: Hull geometry and silhouette artifacts remain, and a material-only fix will not clear them. OBSERVED: V1b reads "pointed from above, blunt and drooping from the side"; the wavy read is "weakest on the hand-kitted Pyre-Barge"; "If A4 fixes only the material, the sag in the outline stays." INFERRED: the hero hull and at least one faction silhouette can ship with an unfair surface and mismatched top and side profiles. Cost: the deferred Pyre-Barge standard and the cinematic close-up benchmark are at risk before the effects pass starts.

9. MEDIUM: The beauty budget collides with the Forward+ clustered light and decal pool. OBSERVED: clustered elements default "512 in view" are a "SHARED budget" with omni, spot, area lights and reflection probes; capital explosions risk "overdraw, not particle count"; "amount_ratio gives NO performance benefit". INFERRED: simultaneous capital deaths with decals, explosion lights, volumetric fog and large soft smoke can exhaust the shared cluster budget or fill rate on the RTX 3060 floor. Cost: the cinematic payoff may be the exact moment frame time spikes, forcing the effects pass to cut the shots the benchmark asks for.

10. MEDIUM: A silent weapon deletion removes effect identity from the data before VFX can audit it. OBSERVED: the claims register records a "third silent weapon deletion" where "b_razor_s drops bioplasma undisclosed". INFERRED: if data is code and a weapon can lose a damage type without disclosure, the VFX mapping and fleet readability for that weapon cannot be trusted from the data file. Cost: a weapon may ship with missing or wrong effects, and the audit cannot tell whether the omission is intentional.

Verdict: The effects pass has strong transients but not a readable damage language; the single most important attack is that the two specs it claims to satisfy, per-weapon persistent marks and an on-screen flash budget, are both bypassed in the shipped Godot fx code.
