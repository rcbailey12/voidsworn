Overall verdict: The text is often strong and faction-aware, but it is not ready as player-facing copy. There are several BLOCKERs in the help text and in hangar wording that teach the player the wrong rules, plus a Deathless Nemesis description that contradicts the data and the checked campaign code. Fix the BLOCKERs first, then the Admiral and Nemesis description mismatches.

Check 1: Name and term consistency against the bible
Verdict: Mostly consistent. Two canonical Chorus weapon names never appear in the checked player-facing paths, one faction blurb uses “carriers” for non-carriers, and a Deathless relic name can leak onto other factions.

1. FIX. Chorus weapon names are collapsed against the bible. OBSERVED: W.bioplasma has base name “Bile spray” and W.spores has base name “Spore drifts”, but FAC_WNAMES.chorus overrides them to “Spine volley” and “Spore cloud”. The bible lists all four names: “Spine volley”, “Spore cloud”, “Bile spray”, “Spore drifts”. As written, a player never sees “Bile spray” or “Spore drifts” from the Chorus hulls. FIX: either use the four names as four distinct weapon entries or remove the unused canonical names from the bible-facing plan.
2. FIX. RELICS.phylactery is named “Eclipse backup frame”. OBSERVED: RELIC_NAMES has a Deathless override “Second Ignition” but no override for the other five factions. If the relic is offered to a non-Deathless fleet, relicName falls back to the Eclipse-specific name. The pack does not include relicUseful or randomRelic, so this is INFERRED from the visible name table. FIX: add neutral or faction-specific names for all factions, or restrict the relic to Deathless.
3. FIX. FACTIONS.hegemony.blurb says “carriers launch fighter screens”. OBSERVED: HULLS.h_vanguard is cls:'Cruiser' with ab:'launch'; HULLS.h_dominion is cls:'Battleship' with ab:'launch'. There is no Hegemony hull in the roster whose class is Carrier. The blurb teaches a class that the data does not use. FIX: say “launch ships” or “Vanguards and Dominions launch fighter screens”.
4. FIX. ABILITIES.blink is named “Phase jump”. OBSERVED: it is used by Glasswright hulls i_wisp, i_blade, i_song, i_carrier. Phase and fold are Eclipse motifs in the bible; Glasswrights own light, glass, reflection, crystal vanes. FIX: rename the ability to a light or glass movement term.
5. NOTE. Internal retired ids remain but are mapped in the checked UI. OBSERVED: HULLS.penitent displays “Vigil-class cruiser”, reliquary displays “Hearth-class carrier”, cathedral displays “Bastion-class battleship”, ascendant displays “Daybreak-class dreadnought”, d_boss has cls:'Tomb-ark' mapped by CLS_NAME to “Archive-ship”, b_boss has cls:'Bio-titan' mapped to “Colossus”, c_boss has cls:'Battle-barge' mapped to “War-barge”. These are not player-facing in the checked paths.
6. NOTE. CLS_NAME maps Ark to “Lattice-ship”. OBSERVED. The bible extract does not give the Ithar flagship a class name; it calls The Unfinished a flagship built around the largest surviving piece of the Lattice. “Lattice-ship” is not contradicted, but it is not bible-confirmed in the pack.

Check 3: Voice consistency per faction
Verdict: Mixed. Ship and weapon names mostly match the bible voice. Several blurbs and one ship name are generic or belong to another faction. Nemesis pronoun handling is inconsistent for female and Chorus Nemeses.

1. FIX. Voidsworn blurb is generic. OBSERVED: “The frontier the Hegemony wrote off, come to stop the Siphoning. Black-iron battleships: heavy armor, lances, and broadsides that hit harder on the move.” It has no dawn, oath, chalk, debt, cold, or brand language. It could carry any black-iron fleet. FIX: bring in the dawn watch, the Roll, or the oath.
2. FIX. Glasswright blurb is generic. OBSERVED: “Few, long-lived masters of light and crystal. Blindingly fast, heavily shielded, fragile once the shields fall.” It is stat copy, not the quiet, precise, mournful Glasswright voice. FIX: use light, reflection, aperture, or what was taken.
3. FIX. Eclipse blurb is close but still generic. OBSERVED: “Starwright minds in machine bodies, stealing starlight to light a new sun. Null weapons cut through shields and armor, and wrecks rebuild themselves.” It has the stolen star and machine body, but not memory, noon, or the grief of the Upload. FIX: add “we remember noon” or the Archive.
4. FIX. SHIP_NAMES includes “Acceptable Loss”. OBSERVED: this list is used beside the Voidsworn-style names “Unyielding Oath”, “Ashen Vigil”, “Debt of Vesper”. “Acceptable Loss” is Hegemony data and optimisation language, not Sunward Crusade oath, fire, cold, or chalk language. FIX: move it to MORE_SHIP_NAMES.hegemony or replace it.
5. FIX. Nemesis pronoun handling leaks masculine pronouns. OBSERVED: nemPanel renders NEM_TRAITS[t].d, NEM_WEAK[w].d, and nemAdaptDesc(a,N.fac) without nemSay. Those descriptions all use “His ships” or “His”. The same panel hardcodes “His fleet is coming: your next fight is his.” nemClockAdd also returns “against his fleet”. For a Chorus Nemesis (g:'n') or a female Deathless or Ithar Nemesis, this is wrong. FIX: pass all these strings through nemSay or make them pronoun-neutral.
6. FIX. ABILITIES.blink uses “Phase jump” for Glasswrights. OBSERVED. This is Eclipse fold language, not Glasswright light or crystal language. FIX: rename to a light or glass movement term.
7. NOTE. Chorus blurb uses third person “their flesh knits back together”. OBSERVED: the bible voice for Chorus is “we”. An external blurb can use third person, but it is off the voice. Consider “our flesh knits back together” if the blurb is meant to be in Chorus voice.
8. NOTE. Many generic events use “captain” and “crew” for Chorus and Eclipse. OBSERVED: mutiny, veterans, pilot, and dock are not restricted by faction. This may be acceptable abstraction, but it is not faction-specific voice.

Check 4: Rules clarity
Verdict: Needs work. Two help BLOCKERs and one hangar wording BLOCKER teach the wrong rules. Several Admiral and protocol descriptions omit numbers or effects.

1. BLOCKER. Help says all factions, admirals, and ships are available from the start. OBSERVED: helpScreen says “every faction, admiral and ship is available from the start.” This contradicts ADMIRALS costs such as malek:{cost:30}, ashgrove:{cost:45}, thale:{cost:80}, and LEGACY unlocks such as u_carrier, u_battleship, and u_dread, which gate shipyard stock. FIX: state that factions are open, but admirals and some hulls unlock with renown.
2. BLOCKER. Help promises a shipyard after each sector flagship. OBSERVED: helpScreen says “After each sector flagship falls, the fleet docks at a shipyard before its next fight.” In afterBattle, the boss branch increments sector, adds command points, repairs 40%, generates the new map, and opens the map screen. It does not open a shipyard. FIX: remove the promise or add the shipyard step.
3. BLOCKER. MODULES.hangar says “Carriers only: +1 squadron in flight.” OBSERVED: MODULES.hangar has only:'launch', not a class check. HULLS.h_vanguard is cls:'Cruiser' with ab:'launch'; HULLS.h_dominion is cls:'Battleship' with ab:'launch'. Both can fit the module under the visible only:'launch' rule. The text teaches that a cruiser or battleship cannot use it when the data allows it. FIX: say “Ships that launch squadrons only” or “Hangar ships only”.
4. FIX. Admiral descriptions omit squadron hull bonuses. OBSERVED: ADMIRALS.ashgrove has fx:{sqPct:.4,hangar:1,sqHp:.25} but text says only “Squadrons deal +40% damage and carriers hold +1 wing.” The same omission appears in reyes (sqHp:.25), mott (sqHp:.25), matriarch (sqHp:.25), and aelith (sqHp:.25). FIX: add “and squadron hull is +25%” or remove sqHp from the data.
5. FIX. ADMIRALS.vaelen text narrows a global aim bonus. OBSERVED: text says “Lances deal +20% damage and gain +10 aim.” The data is fx:{lancePct:.2,aim:10}. In this data model aim is the same global field used by vorn (“+10 aim and +10% critical-hit chance”), so the +10 aim applies to all weapons, not only lances. FIX: either make the text global or change the data to a lance-only aim field.
6. FIX. ADMIRALS.kassar text is ambiguous against its data. OBSERVED: text says “but every gun hits 5% lighter” while the data is dmgPct:-.05. “Hits lighter” can read as accuracy, but the field is damage. FIX: say “but every gun deals 5% less damage”.
7. FIX. Two protocols promise effects that are not in their fx. OBSERVED: BOONS.h_board says “a failed boarding always sabotages a system”, but its fx is only {boardPct:.25*m}. BOONS.h_feast says the carrier “gains a spare wing”, but its fx is only {feast:.05*m}. INFERRED: unless battle.js hardcodes these by boon id, the text promises more than the entry carries. FIX: add fields or remove the promises.
8. FIX. Help omits Legendary protocol rarity. OBSERVED: help says protocols “come in Common, Rare and Epic strength.” RARITY includes a fourth entry, “Legendary”, and BOONS.L_iron etc. are marked leg:1. FIX: add Legendary or say “most protocols come in Common, Rare and Epic”.
9. FIX. Help says three aspects, but mastery can offer four. OBSERVED: help says “you also pick one of three aspects for your flagship.” aspectScreen offers (RUN.mastery||0)>=2?4:3. FIX: say “three, or four with Admiral mastery”.
10. FIX. NEM_ADAPT_FAC.corsair.special name does not match its effect. OBSERVED: name is “Bigger crew”, description is “One more ship joins his fleet.” FIX: rename to “Bigger fleet” or similar.
11. FIX. NEM_ADAPT_FAC.ithar.special has a grammar error. OBSERVED: “His ships are 6 more harder to hit.” FIX: “His ships are 6 harder to hit.”
12. NOTE. EVENTS.beacon seize cargo omits the Nemesis clock. OBSERVED: the option says “+100 credits. Their escort might remember your colours.” The outcome also sets nemClock:1. This may be intentional hidden consequence, but the player is not told the clock moves. Consider “and the Nemesis clock moves on”.
13. NOTE. EVENTS.auction promises a different relic. OBSERVED: option says “Lose a piece of salvage, gain a different piece and 40 credits.” applyOutcome removes payRelic before calling randomRelic, so the same relic can be eligible again if randomRelic only excludes current RUN.relics. INFERRED because randomRelic is not in the pack. FIX: exclude the paid relic explicitly.
14. NOTE. RELICS.cogitator says “Each ship’s first attack in every battle hits 95% of the time.” OBSERVED. “First attack” could mean first weapon, first volley, or first hit. Clarify.

Check 5: Text that contradicts its own numbers
Verdict: Several contradictions. The Deathless Nemesis text is the worst because it contradicts both the behaviour data and the checked campaign code.

1. BLOCKER. Deathless Nemesis text contradicts its data and code. OBSERVED: NEM_FAC.deathless.beh.d says “He never retreats” and “Destroyed early, he simply reassembles; destroyed in the last sector, he rises once more for one final fight.” The behaviour object is {clock:.5,revive:1,strike:'tithe',fleet:'heavy'}. It has no noFlee:1. In nemAfterBattle, a flagship death before sector 3 goes to the else if(flagDead) branch and produces “he escapes, scarred and furious”, not reassembly. FIX: either add noFlee:1 and early reassembly, or rewrite the card to say he escapes before the last sector.
2. FIX. Admiral squadron hull omissions are both clarity and number contradictions. OBSERVED: ADMIRALS.ashgrove, reyes, mott, matriarch, and aelith each carry sqHp:.25 but their descriptions never mention squadron hull. FIX: add the number or remove the field.
3. FIX. ADMIRALS.vaelen contradicts its aim:10 scope. OBSERVED: text says lances gain +10 aim; fx.aim:10 is global. FIX: correct one side.
4. FIX. FACTIONS.hegemony.blurb contradicts hull classes. OBSERVED: “carriers launch fighter screens” while the launch hulls are a Cruiser and a Battleship. FIX: say “launch ships”.
5. FIX. ABILITIES.launch says “Carriers start each battle with their wings in flight.” OBSERVED: h_vanguard and h_dominion have ab:'launch' and are not carriers. This is the same issue as the hangar module. FIX: say “Ships with launch start each battle with their wings in flight.”
6. NOTE. MODULES.flakplus says “Point defense +2 shots and +1 range.” OBSERVED: the only data field is flakPlus:1. The battle code is not in the pack, so I cannot verify whether flakPlus:1 means +2 shots. Mark INFERRED.
