Cross-admiral notes

The forward-deployment chassis recurs across the aggressive admirals (Ironmaw, Draven, Sable, Vell, Umbra, Tamsa, Maren); each is differentiated by what the forward position buys - adjacency for line bonuses, rear-arc access, cover ignore, first-round launch timing, or proximity to the quarry - rather than by a bigger movement number.

Kill-triggered payoffs appear on Sable, Mott, Vesh, Umbra, and Nadir; the trigger is shared but the payoff is deliberately different each time (turn-and-fire, extra wing, section repair, extra shot, full restore with a fire penalty). I kept every trigger on enemy destruction, never on friendly loss.

Rule 6 was the hardest to keep: most admirals' capstones already occupy their obvious tier-3 space, so the clean room was in deployment rows, targeting denial, and tradeoff costs. Where a numeric survived (Vex's split +15%, Saro's escalating +5% per ship, Ostrel's +15% while unobserved), it is compound with a decision and sits at or above the floor.

Vell's and Brin's capstones are so broad that their Branch C tier 3 has little clean space; both lean on tradeoff costs (deception instead of evasion, flagship shields halved) rather than scaled effects. This is the clearest sign that their earlier tiers, not their tier 3, may need a second look.

The pair 12 split is fully justified by the draft: Vell's tier 3 is entirely positional deception and Brin's is entirely shield economy; Vesh's is section repair and scrap conversion. There is no shared mechanic left to carry a pair identity.
