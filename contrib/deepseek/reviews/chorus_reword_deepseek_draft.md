PROPOSED 3.2 (TEXT-ONLY): a wet mass of pulsing sacs and membrane chambers that extrudes fleshy bands, the bands gripping the broken hull and swelling until they fuse into one scarred plug.

PROPOSED 3.3 (VISUAL): a mass of pulsing sacs and membrane chambers that pushes blunt fleshy bands into a broken hull, the bands gripping the edges and fusing without holes or thread lines.
Visual change exactly: remove suture lines, stitch holes, thread, and needle-like cues; show blunt fleshy bands gripping the hull edges and fusing into a scarred mass.
Loss: loses the visual rhythm of sutures, but more clearly avoids the sewing read.
