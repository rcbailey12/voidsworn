CHORUS ADMIRAL REGENERATE BRIEFS (DeepSeek draft)

Label key: OBSERVED means taken from the brief file. PROPOSED means a DeepSeek draft for owner review.

OBSERVED standing rule: Chorus admirals must match their special abilities in some way. Portrait and design reflect Command and doctrine.

OBSERVED faction rules: Chorus is grown flesh, gross and disgusting living tissue. Chorus never uses built things, familiar Earth-animal anatomy (sea creatures included), buildings, or instruments.

1. Matriarch of Nine Throats

OBSERVED ability: Doctrine Brood swarm. Command Spawning surge. Every brood releases an extra swarm even past its limit, then all swarms strike again at once with +50 percent damage this round.

OBSERVED current failure: Matriarch of Nine Throats v2 reads like a spine, which matches neither pipes nor her ability.

Primary brief

Subject (PROPOSED): A tall, asymmetrical Chorus admiral as the central command mass, with nine uneven throat-sacs arranged around her forward membrane like a living command crown. One throat-sac is dilated and visibly birthing an extra swarm bud past the ring limit, while launched swarm buds on short filaments turn together for a second strike. The admiral dominates the frame; the buds read as her retinue, not as separate ships.

Must read as (OBSERVED requirement): An admiral and commander character, not a ship, weapon, or landscape.

Must avoid (OBSERVED): The spine read of v2, plus all standing bans: no Earth-animal anatomy, no constructed things, no instruments, no buildings.

Material and texture notes (PROPOSED): Use wet membrane, pulsing sacs, layered tissue, and suture-like filaments. Living note: seepage at the throat lips and uneven tissue tension make it read as living flesh rather than a built form.

Open question for owner (PROPOSED): How literal should the nine throats be: nine visible birth openings, or a smaller number with the swarm retinue implying the rest?

Alternate direction

The largest sac is mid-spawn, pushing out an extra swarm bud while other buds already sit in a strike wedge. No segmented central column; the silhouette is radial and draped, not spinal.
