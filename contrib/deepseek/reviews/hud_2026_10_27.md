BLOCKER: The text on the right-hand ship card is too small for quick reading. OBSERVED in battle_hud_after.jpg, battle_attack_tip.jpg, 02_move_preview.png, and 03_attack_tip.png. The numbers for hull, shields, crew, movement, and weapon stats (damage and range) are rendered in a tiny font against a dark background. INFERRED that a player cannot parse these critical numbers mid-decision without leaning in, defeating the purpose of having the card open during combat.

BLOCKER: Tooltips obscure the board in the move preview state. OBSERVED in battle_hud_after.jpg and 02_move_preview.png. The "Move here" tooltip appears directly over the ship and the gold movement hexes it is describing. INFERRED that this forces the player to mentally map the path around the UI box, slowing down planning and increasing the chance of a misclick.

FIX: The Admiral's Command tutorial card blocks the battlefield. OBSERVED in battle_hud_after.jpg. The tutorial popup is positioned in the upper center of the screen, covering several hexes. INFERRED that during the first turn, when a player is learning the game, the interface is actively hiding the board state they need to evaluate.

FIX: Threat tinting lacks contrast. OBSERVED in 02_move_preview.png. The red and orange hexes indicating threat are somewhat muddy against the dark starfield and the purple planet backdrop. INFERRED that a player could miss a threatened hex and accidentally move a ship into danger.

FIX: Threat tinting colors are not explained in the move preview state. OBSERVED in 02_move_preview.png. The board shows gold hexes (movement) and red/orange hexes (threat). There is no visible legend on this screen explaining what the red/orange shading means. INFERRED that a new player will not immediately know if red means "enemy threat", "blocked terrain", or something else.

FIX: Firing arc indication is too subtle. OBSERVED in 03_attack_tip.png. The firing arc is indicated by a soft tint over the hexes, but the gradient makes the edge of the arc hard to define. INFERRED that it is difficult to judge exactly which hexes fall inside the arc, making it hard to position for a shot.

NOTE: The selection indicator is clear. OBSERVED in battle_hud_after.jpg and 02_move_preview.png. The green circle around the selected ship is distinct and easy to spot at a glance.

NOTE: Turn state is consistently placed and readable. OBSERVED in all nine images. The "Round 1 Your turn" text in the top left is large and high contrast.

Verdict for Start state (battle_start_after.jpg, battle_start_legend.jpg, 01_start.png): Clear. The board is visible, the help panels are well placed, and the turn state is obvious.
Verdict for Move Preview state (battle_hud_after.jpg, battle_sheet_move_preview.jpg, 02_move_preview.png): Cluttered. The tooltips and tutorial cards obscure the board, and the ship card text is too small to read quickly.
Verdict for Attack Targeting state (battle_attack_tip.jpg, 03_attack_tip.png): Weak. The attack tooltip itself is readable, but the firing arc tinting on the board is too subtle to accurately judge line of sight.
Verdict for Top-down state (04_threat_topdown.png): Adequate. The flat view is readable, but the same text size issues apply to the ship card.

Overall Verdict: The HUD is information-rich but suffers from legibility and occlusion issues. The most critical failure is the tiny text on the ship card, which holds the numbers needed to make tactical decisions. Second is the placement of tooltips and tutorial boxes directly over the board.
