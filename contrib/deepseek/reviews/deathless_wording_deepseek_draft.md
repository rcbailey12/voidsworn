## 1. Contested terms: current usage and wording options

### 1.1 bone

**OBSERVED - Current usage quoted from granted doc:**
- "mineral, slate-and-bone quality"
- "pale bone-white structural ribs"
- "bone #cfd4c8 used structurally, not as paint"
- "bone on the outside"
- "bone prow plate blank and staring"
- "bone-white ceramic computation"
- OBSERVED - Context passage uses "rust and bone" for Corsairs; that is not Deathless usage and is excluded from this term analysis.

**PROPOSED - Metaphorical option:** Treat Deathless bone as colour/structural analogy only; bone-white is a colour name and ribs/spine are shape metaphors.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** Deathless remain machine intelligences with mineral/ceramic structure; the machine-vs-grown-flesh boundary stays clean.

**PROPOSED - Literal option:** Treat bone as actual bone material incorporated into Deathless construction.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** Literal bone reads as grown tissue and blurs the Deathless/Chorus boundary; it also raises an unanswered lore question about bone source/origin, which the granted doc does not supply.

**PROPOSED - Retired-word replacement option:** Retire standalone material noun bone for Deathless; replace with bone-white colour naming or mineral/ceramic structural wording.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** The tomb/bone motif weakens as a material implication but strengthens the unmistakably machine read; no new material or anatomy is introduced.

### 1.2 bone-white

**OBSERVED - Current usage quoted from granted doc:**
- "pale bone-white structural ribs"
- "bone #cfd4c8 used structurally, not as paint"
- "bone-white ceramic computation"

**PROPOSED - Metaphorical option:** Keep bone-white strictly as a colour name for #cfd4c8, not a material claim.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** The Deathless palette keeps its cold tomb tone while the material read stays ceramic/mineral/machine.

**PROPOSED - Literal option:** Treat bone-white as naming actual bone material.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** This converts a palette term into grown-tissue material and blurs the Deathless/Chorus boundary.

**PROPOSED - Retired-word replacement option:** Replace bone-white with ceramic white or pale ceramic for #cfd4c8.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** The machine read becomes clearer, but the established Deathless bone/tomb colour language loses its specific flavour.

### 1.3 ribs

**OBSERVED - Current usage quoted from granted doc:**
- "pale bone-white structural ribs"
- "reads by its ribcage geometry"
- "Exposed longitudinal ribs over a dark core"
- "green light deep in the ribs"
- "ribs half-open like a cage"
- "loses a rib segment"

**PROPOSED - Metaphorical option:** Keep ribs/ribcage as structural shape language for exposed longitudinal machine members over a dark core.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** The silhouette grammar survives, and ribs read as machine geometry rather than anatomy.

**PROPOSED - Literal option:** Treat ribs as actual anatomical bone ribs.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** This introduces grown-tissue anatomy into Deathless and blurs the machine-vs-flesh boundary.

**PROPOSED - Retired-word replacement option:** Replace ribs with longitudinal structural members, cage segments, or exposed structural vanes.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** The machine read is clearer, but the strong ribcage silhouette metaphor and the established damage beat of a lost rib segment weaken.

### 1.4 spine

**OBSERVED - Current usage quoted from granted doc:**
- "sections that read as segments of a spine"
- "Spine segments with visible articulation"
- "Grammar: segmented spine."

**PROPOSED - Metaphorical option:** Keep spine as a structural shape metaphor for segmented machine articulation.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** Ossuary's segmented silhouette survives, and spine reads as machine articulation rather than actual spinal anatomy.

**PROPOSED - Literal option:** Treat spine as actual spinal bone.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** This adds literal grown-tissue anatomy to Deathless and blurs the machine-vs-flesh boundary.

**PROPOSED - Retired-word replacement option:** Replace spine with segmented articulation, articulated segments, or segmented structural keel.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** The machine read is clearer, but the established segmented-spine silhouette grammar loses its evocative shorthand.

### 1.5 face

**OBSERVED - Current usage quoted from granted doc:**
- "face-like prow plates"
- "prow plate like a blank face"
- "bone prow plate blank and staring"
- "sensor light instead of faces"
- OBSERVED - Brief fact: Nekhet has no face; Passage F describes Nekhet as "a monumental broken ring of bone-white ceramic computation around a green core."

**PROPOSED - Metaphorical option:** Keep face only as simile - face-like prow plate - never as a literal Deathless face.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** The blank staring prow survives as an analogy, but the wording still risks implying a humanoid face and sits uneasily with "sensor light instead of faces."

**PROPOSED - Literal option:** Give Deathless literal faces or facial anatomy.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** This conflicts with the no-humanoid-frame requirement, with Nekhet having no face, and with the sensor-light wording; it also pushes Deathless toward organic anatomy.

**PROPOSED - Retired-word replacement option:** Retire face as a Deathless noun; replace with prow plate, front aspect, sensor cluster, or sensor light.
**PROPOSED fiction consequence:** Deathless frontage reads as machine sensor/plate architecture, consistent with Nekhet and with "sensor light instead of faces."

## 2. Recommendations per term

**PROPOSED - bone:** Treat Deathless bone as metaphor only; keep bone-white as colour and structural analogy, and retire standalone bone as a material noun.
**PROPOSED reason:** This preserves the palette and tomb tone while keeping Deathless unmistakably machine and avoiding literal grown-tissue material.

**PROPOSED - bone-white:** Keep bone-white as a colour name for #cfd4c8, not a material claim.
**PROPOSED reason:** The granted doc already uses it as structural trim colour, so it does not need to imply actual bone.

**PROPOSED - ribs:** Keep ribs/ribcage as structural shape metaphor for exposed machine members over a dark core.
**PROPOSED reason:** The ribcage is the established Deathless silhouette grammar and works as machine geometry without literal anatomy.

**PROPOSED - spine:** Keep spine as structural shape metaphor for segmented machine articulation.
**PROPOSED reason:** Ossuary's segmented-spine grammar survives while the material remains machine structure, not spinal bone.

**PROPOSED - face:** Retire face as a Deathless noun; use prow plate, front aspect, sensor cluster, or sensor light.
**PROPOSED reason:** The granted doc already says "sensor light instead of faces," and Nekhet has no face, so face is the term that most directly conflicts with the machine-intelligence ruling.
