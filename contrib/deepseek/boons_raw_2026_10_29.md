## 2. Findings

1. [BLOCKER] The normal patron cores are mostly numbers, not build decisions. READ: Thunder Broadside is +20% damage; Ravenous Wings is +25% damage; Starlight Lances is +35% damage; Incendiary Warheads is +25% damage; Capacitor Surge is +20% shields; Unbent Plating is +8% armor; Lightning Reflex is +6 evasion; Silent Aim is +5 aim. INFERRED: these are the same job - a raw or near-raw combat multiplier - sorted by patron and weapon/stat class. The pool does not consistently make builds; it often buffs numbers.

2. [BLOCKER] The Q7 off-patron question cannot be fully audited from this pack. READ: the ruling says off-patron picks must retain real value and a hidden rarity penalty on off-patron fillers is the defect to avoid; the pack does not include offer-generation, rarity, or weighting code. INFERRED: I can test whether off-patron boons have numeric value, but I cannot verify whether the shipped offer system actually penalizes them. That is the exact defect the commissioner asked about.

3. [FIX] Several jobs duplicate at different strengths. READ: Chain Reaction does 20*m explosion damage; Pyre of Stars does 40 damage on every destroyed enemy within 2 - same explosion job at fixed strength. Ion Lash strips +22% more shield; Ion Lance makes lances strip double shield. Battering Prow, Juggernaut, and Unstoppable all occupy the ram-damage/recoil/cap job. INFERRED: these are not distinct offer identities; they are upgrades or sidegrades of the same job.

4. [FIX] There is at least one concrete offer/need mismatch. READ: Feast on the Fallen requires BOON_NEED 'sq', but its text says its carrier gains a spare wing. INFERRED: a squadron fleet without a carrier could be offered a boon whose carrier-triggered payoff has no carrier target. That is a dead or near-dead offer, and it is exactly the kind of off-patron/filler defect Q7 warns about.

5. [FIX] Many "conditional" boons are still percentage buffs with a trigger, not new decisions. READ: Anchored Volley gives +25% if a ship hasn't moved; Momentum gives +5% per hex moved; Blind Spot gives +25% on rear arc; Funeral Pyre gives +20% below half hull. INFERRED: these change when a number applies, but most do not create a new fleet/map/battle plan; they reward positioning the player already wants.

6. [NOTE] Patron identity itself is mostly coherent. READ: Iron is Broadsides, armor and holding the line; Storm is Shields, ion fire and lightning; Hunger is Squadrons, boarding and feeding on the fallen; Star is Lances, precision and critical hits; Rook is Rams, speed and momentum; Ember is Torpedoes, fire and chain explosions. INFERRED: each slice reads as its patron. The failure is not that patrons are random; it is that each identity is often expressed through generic percentages plus one rule-changing/legendary.

7. [NOTE] The best structural case for off-patron picks is duo unlocking. READ: Fortress is a duo needing iron and storm, and it makes bracing refill every shield facing and block an extra 10%. INFERRED: a storm/shield build can take an off-patron Iron brace boon not only for its own value but to unlock Fortress. That is real off-patron value from the numbers; whether the offer system surfaces it is not in the pack.

8. [NOTE] Legendaries are not part of the normal pool. READ: legendaries are offered at an elite or flagship reward once you hold two boons from that patron, always in the third slot, and never rolls otherwise. INFERRED: the effective normal pool is 42, not 48; legendary duplicates should not be counted as normal offer clutter, but they do duplicate jobs at the top end.

### 2. DEAD PICKS AND STRICT TAKES

I cannot prove literal never-takes from the pack alone, but the closest to dead/offer-defective are:

- Feast on the Fallen - BOON_NEED is 'sq', not 'carrier', but the effect requires its carrier. INFERRED: can be offered without a carrier target.
- Hardened Chitin - +30% squadron hull competes with +25% squadron damage and extra-wing capacity; hull does not improve strike. INFERRED: weaker in most squadron builds.
- Long Silence - at m=1, ignores one hex of long-range penalty; epic only gives lances +1 range. READ/INFERRED: narrow, and no BOON_NEED keeps it out of non-range fleets.
- Evasive Burn - requires moved 3+ hexes; slow fleets get nothing. INFERRED: dead in low-speed fleets.
- Anchored Volley - requires hasn't moved; at m=1 it is only +25% conditional versus Thunder Broadside +20% unconditional, while costing positioning. INFERRED: usually not worth the movement constraint.

Strict takes when offered:

- Unbreakable - Every ship survives its first killing blow each battle at 1 hull; no condition. READ.
- Chain Reaction - any destroyed enemy explodes for 20*m within 2; no BOON_NEED. READ.
- Killing Light - +6*m% critical chance, crits at x1.5+.25*m, and every critical hit wrecks a ship system; no BOON_NEED. READ.

### 3. PATRON IDENTITY

- Iron / Kharon: reads as Iron. Broadside, armor, brace/riposte, formation defense, last stand. But Thunder Broadside and Unbent Plating are generic percentages.
- Storm / Vyre: reads as Storm. Shields, shield regen, ion strip, shield-break lightning, evasion, full shield refill. Capacitor Surge, Storm Mantle, and Lightning Reflex are generic stat buffs inside a coherent shell.
- Hunger / Crucible: reads as Hunger. Extra wings, squadron damage/hull, boarding, feeding on kills, endless swarm. Ravenous Wings and Hardened Chitin are generic squadron stats.
- Star / Astra: reads as Star. Lances, aim, crit, range, rear arc, mark, shield-bypassing lance. Starlight Lances and Silent Aim are generic accuracy/damage.
- Rook / Breaker: reads as Rook. Speed, ram, momentum, evasive burn, ram splash, move-after-fire, unstoppable ram. Afterburners is mostly stat/mobility.
- Ember / Seren: reads as Ember. Torpedoes, reload, fire, chain explosions, finisher/anti-regen, spread pattern. Incendiary Warheads is generic torpedo damage.

Verdict: patrons are not just random sorting labels; each slice has a recognizable silhouette. The problem is that the silhouette is often carried by generic percentages, so the patron label is doing more identity work than the boon mechanics.

### 4. SPILLOVER VALUE

Yes, an off-patron boon can be the right pick from the numbers alone.

Strongest case: assume m=1, current patron Iron, and the fleet is shield/lance-heavy. On-patron Unbent Plating gives +8% armor; off-patron Capacitor Surge gives +20% shields, and off-patron Starlight Lances gives +35% lance damage. If the fleet's defense is shields or its offense is lances, the off-patron numbers beat the on-patron generic armor/broadside numbers. INFERRED: patron fit is not the same as fleet fit.

Stronger structural case: a Storm/shield build can take an off-patron Iron brace boon to unlock Fortress (iron + storm), which refills every shield facing on brace and adds +10% hull block. READ: the duo requirement is in the code. INFERRED: the duo is real off-patron value, not a cosmetic label.

Caveat: the pack does not show offer weights, rarity penalties, or duo offer conditions, so I cannot verify whether the shipped offer system actually lets these picks compete. That is the Q7 defect question, and it remains unproven from this pack.

### 5. NUMBERS VERSUS DECISIONS

Using a strict classification:

- 12/48 (25%) are pure percentage/stat with no condition or new rule: Thunder Broadside, Unbent Plating, Capacitor Surge, Ion Lash, Storm Mantle, Lightning Reflex, Ravenous Wings, Hardened Chitin, Starlight Lances, Silent Aim, Incendiary Warheads, Ion Lance.
- 18/48 (38%) are conditional percentage/trigger effects: they change when a number applies, but mostly not a new plan.
- 18/48 (38%) add a genuine rule/action/board interaction: e.g. Shield Wall, Chain Lightning, Brood Harvest, Marked for Death, Slip the Net, Spread Pattern, Fortress, Unstoppable, Pyre of Stars.

Judgment: the share is not right for the between-battles layer. A quarter of the pool is pure stat filler, and another large band is conditional percentages. Only about 38% reliably changes fleet/map/battle decisions. The layer should make builds; this pool often buffs numbers inside a patron silhouette.

## 5. Questions for the designer

1. What is m exactly, and do multiple copies stack? Stakes: duplicate/domination and strict-take analysis depend on whether +20% and +35% are one-off boons or stackable levels.
2. What is the hidden off-patron rarity penalty, and how does it interact with duo unlocks? Stakes: Q7 compliance turns on whether off-patron picks can compete with on-patron picks and enable duos.
