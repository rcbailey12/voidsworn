1. derelict
Variant title: A hulk with no name
Body: Your scanners wake on a dead warship, unpowered and old, its hull cut with Starwright script no living eye has read.
Options:
- Send boarding parties / Risky. Could hold founders’ tech, could hold something awake.
- Strip the hull from range / +35 credits, no risk.
- Cut out its core with lances / A clean cut: a refit and +40 credits, nothing wakes.
- Leave it to the dark / Nothing happens.
