Cross-pair notes

The reserve chassis (a held unit entering round 2) deliberately serves three pairs differently: pair 3 holds a squadron, pair 4 holds a squadron with +15 aim, and pair 9 holds a ship with +2 movement; each deepens a different faction's tier-1 reserve without repeating it.

The swap/reposition chassis recurs across pairs 2, 7, 8, 10, and 12, kept distinct by what the swap grants (shields, repair, or reach) so no two read as the same skill.

The hardest rule to keep was Rule 6 on pair 12: with surge + restore sharing no idiom, every skill had to both move and patch, and the "reposition and patch" wording is the thinnest fit in the set.

Pair 12 remains the weakest shared tier 2, as flagged in the brief; if any pair should be unshared, it is this one.

Pure numerics were kept at zero (well under the one-in-three cap); every effect carries a decision or condition, so Rule 1's 15 percent floor is never the whole skill.
