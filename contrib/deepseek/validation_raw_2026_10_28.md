# DeepSeek blind validation leg: learning-frame approaches and six faction cycles (Oct 28, 2026)

Chair note: filed verbatim from the model's reply (DeepSeek, DeepThink and Search on). Em dashes and en dashes normalized to hyphens per house rule; wording otherwise unchanged. Evidence pack: the Oct 28 program validation pack (design proposals and lore facts, no code), premises stated as designer constraints. Method notes and screening live outside this file.

1. Proof of reading: I read EVIDENCE PACK title line; THE COMMISSIONER'S QUESTION; PREMISES; THE SYSTEMS AS BUILT; THE APPROACH UNDER REVIEW; THE SIX LEARNING CYCLES; LORE FACTS; WHAT TO JUDGE.

Overall verdict: NOT READY.

F1: SOUND WITH CONDITIONS - debt/archive frame fits L5, but “generation” tempo must be compressed.
F2: NOT READY - telemetry upload and all-fleet doctrine push contradict L7.
F3: SOUND WITH CONDITIONS - fits L4, but “next season/suckled” implies delay.
F4: SOUND WITH CONDITIONS - fits L1, but growth tempo is unspecified.
F5: NOT READY - lagged casting queue breaks immediate run-to-run payoff and A3 honesty.
F6: SOUND WITH CONDITIONS - L2/L7-legal, but collapses with F2 as a distinct mechanism.

2. Findings

• [BLOCKER] F2 contradicts L7. READ: the Registry “ingests every task force's telemetry” and “reissues synchronized doctrine to all fleets at once,” described as “overnight.” L7 forbids instant cross-sector communication except within a fleet battle network or the Eclipse Archive. INFERRED: a remote or destroyed task force cannot deliver telemetry to the Registry overnight, and the Registry cannot push doctrine to all fleets at once without FTL. Fix: make Hegemony learning courier/return-to-yard/light-lag, and doctrine applies only where received.

• [BLOCKER] F5 cannot honor back-to-back run tempo or A3’s immediate debrief. READ: shards are recovered “over years,” and the new run is “the next casting in the queue,” “not the fate of the fleet just played.” INFERRED: when the player starts the next run immediately, the loss just played may not affect that run; the debrief cannot honestly say “the next attempt inherits” this attempt’s lessons. L3 is not violated, but gameplay tempo is. Fix: make the just-played fleet’s shards recoverable by debrief, or explicitly separate immediate progression from lagged casting and stop promising immediate inheritance.

• [FIX] A1’s Memory track resists P4. READ: “patron cores remember the fleets that keep flying their protocols”; sync is “recognizing and trusting the player's line.” INFERRED: that is patron memory/trust, not the faction learning a lesson. Doctrine and Lineage are institutional knowledge; Memory is favor. Fix: define cores as faction institutions/archives encoding protocol lessons, or add a faction-learning layer to sync.

• [FIX] F2 and F6 collapse into the same mechanism under the fiction. READ: F2 has the Registry ingest telemetry and reissue doctrine to all fleets; F6 compiles a “new iteration ... with the prior iteration's combat records.” INFERRED: both are centralized combat-record ingestion producing an updated fleet/doctrine; F6 is essentially the L7-legal version of F2. The set has five distinct mechanisms, not six. Fix: differentiate Hegemony as local flagship-anchor synchronization and Eclipse as Archive versioning, or replace one.

• [FIX] A3’s “specific unlock now affordable” claim will often be false at 80% loss. READ: early death under 10, mid death roughly 45; admiral ladder 0/30/45/80; hull variants 250 total; LEGACY 635. INFERRED: after many losses the next visible sink may be 80, 250, or 635, so the debrief cannot truthfully name an unlock now affordable. The first false note by run4 is likely “every loss bought a named lesson” if the lesson is only a few renown toward a distant sink. Fix: guarantee qualitative named intel/progress (sync tier, mastery threshold, Nemesis read) even when no unlock is affordable.

• [FIX] A4’s run5/run10 claims are not traceable from the stated economy. READ: win ~100, mid ~45, early <10; total sinks 1,815; mastery capstone 50 XP per admiral; sync tier at 25 protocols per core. INFERRED: at 80% loss, by run5 typical cumulative renown is roughly 200-300, which buys a few admirals/hulls/doctrine, not clearly “first doctrine tier complete and one or two lineages deep.” Run10 “full core sync” is aggressive if it means all six cores at 25 protocols (150 protocol picks). Need costs/XP/protocol rates.

• [FIX] Lineage must explicitly make the admiral a line/archetype, not the captain who dies. READ: premise says “every hull you lose stays lost, and so does its captain”; system has “per-admiral XP” across runs. INFERRED: A1’s “admiral's house, line, pack, or line of descent” can reconcile this, but “per-admiral XP” still sounds like the same individual persists. Fix: state admirals are lineages/archetypes or that mastery belongs to the house, not the dead captain.

• [NOTE] A2 does not validate within-run scaling, though P3 requires it. READ: A2 says “Cross-run power mostly widens options” and “only modestly widens margins”; no within-run shape is given. INFERRED: P3’s “upgrading within a run” is left to existing systems and is not judged by this approach. The run1/5/10 trace cannot fully validate P3.

• [NOTE] F1/F3 tempo language needs compression. READ: F1 sends “a new crusade every generation”; F3’s next pack is “suckled” and hunts “next season.” INFERRED: back-to-back runs require treating a run as a generation/season; otherwise the fiction implies waiting. Not fatal, but should be explicit.

3. Explicit checks

a. THE FRAME: Doctrine fits well: standing orders, salvage endowment, staff lessons, price cuts are institutional learning. Lineage fits only if “admiral” is a house/line/archetype; alternate fleets and second Commands as inherited options are good. Memory resists: core sync is patron recognition/trust, not obviously the faction’s learned knowledge. So the frame does not cover all three equally; Memory needs reframing.

b. THE SET: Not genuinely six mechanisms. F2 and F6 collapse into centralized combat-record ingestion producing an updated doctrine/iteration; F6 is the L7-legal version, F2 the L7-breaking version. F1 vs F3 are distinct enough: archive/debt vs oral/trophy/grudge. F4 vs F5 are distinct: genetic adaptation vs lagged perfect replay, though both “correct the killing flaw.” The cycles do imply different relationships to defeat-debt, patch, grudge, evolution, archive replay, versioning-but F2/F6 blur two of them.

c. TEMPO: F1: “generation” implies delay; playable only if run = generation. F2: “overnight” is tempo-fast but lore-illegal; if fixed to courier/years, it becomes tempo-slow. F3: “next season/suckled” implies delay; playable only if pack = next run. F4: no stated wait; evolution can be next brood, but growth tempo is unspecified. F5: explicitly lagged; next run is not the just-played fleet, so it cannot honor immediate debrief payoff. F6: immediate within the Archive for minds already in it; tempo is fine and L2/L7 exception covers it. Weakest cycle: F5, because its fiction deliberately severs the just-played run from the next run-the one link the game tempo and A3 require. F2 is the most severe lore contradiction, but F5 is the weakest as a cycle.

d. THE SHAPE: Run1: bare toolbox, no Heat, no meta options; power comes from within-run choices and player skill only; near-impossible is plausible. Run5: assuming first win around 4-6, cumulative renown is roughly 200-300 if some mid-run deaths occur, lower with early deaths; the player has a few admirals/hulls, some doctrine, some mastery/sync. Power is mostly horizontal options plus modest credits/command points; no single purchase carries the run. First completion around runs 4-6 is plausible, but A2 does not prove it because mastery XP and protocol rates are absent. A4’s “first doctrine tier complete and one or two lineages deep” at run5 is not supported without per-upgrade costs and XP rates. Run10: assuming 2-3 wins over 10 runs, cumulative renown is roughly 500-600; the player has most of one track or several options, Heat climbing, deeper lineage, and core sync. Still not all 1,815 renown sinks are bought, so run10 can still pull-but “full core sync” is aggressive if it means all six cores at 25 protocols; if it means one core fully synced, it is plausible. The shape delivers P2 better than P3/P4; P3’s within-run scaling is not addressed.

e. DEFEAT: At 80% loss, the debrief must name a true qualitative change to the next attempt: a new Command/fleet, a sync tier, a mastery threshold, concrete Nemesis intel, or an actually affordable unlock. The first thing that would feel false by run4 is “every loss bought a named lesson” if repeated early deaths under 10 just inch toward 80/250/635 with no threshold crossed. A run4 loss feels like progress only if it crosses a visible threshold or reveals a specific counter; otherwise “tuition” becomes a euphemism for tax. F5 makes this worse because the next attempt may not inherit the just-played lesson at all.

4. RUN 1 / RUN 5 / RUN 10

Run 1: The player has a bare faction toolbox: base fleet, no Heat, no meaningful cross-run options, little knowledge of the Nemesis or sector checks. Power comes from within-run choices and raw skill. They are chasing survival, a first sector boss, and the first proof that the route exists; a win is near impossible by design.

Run 5: If the first win has just happened or is imminent, the player has roughly 200-300 cumulative renown, a few admirals/hulls, some doctrine, and partial mastery/sync on a favored line. They know more about the Nemesis and sector checks. They are chasing a repeatable completion and the first real horizontal toolbox; Heat may just be unlocking if run4-5 produced a win. A4’s claim of “first doctrine tier complete and one or two lineages deep” is plausible only if costs/XP are tuned low; the pack does not let us verify it.

Run 10: The player has Heat unlocked, a much wider option set, deeper lineage mastery, and substantial core sync; cumulative renown is likely around 500-600, not enough to buy all 1,815 sinks. They are chasing Heat climbs, long-tail lineage depth, remaining doctrine, and full sync on favored cores-not new systems. This can still pull, but “full core sync” across all six cores at 25 protocols each would be a very long tail; one fully synced core is more plausible.

5. Not checked

Individual LEGACY upgrade costs; mastery XP per run; protocol pick frequency; within-run economy/upgrade balance; specific Heat rules; Nemesis adaptation mechanics; actual player-skill distribution; whether the 80% loss rate holds in practice; narrative text quality; patron-core identity beyond the pack; the boot-time grant/shop UI defect is explicitly out of scope.

6. Questions for the designer

Does the Hegemony Registry have any courier/FTL exception for telemetry and doctrine push, or must F2 obey L7 strictly? Stakes: F2 can remain as written only if you grant an exception; otherwise it must be rewritten around return-to-yard or light-lag.

For the Glasswrights, must the next run incorporate the just-played fleet’s shards, or is the lagged queue fixed? Stakes: A3’s debrief honesty and the player’s sense of run-to-run progress depend on this.

What are the per-LEGACY-upgrade costs and the mastery XP/protocol gains per run? Stakes: whether A4’s run5 “first doctrine tier complete and one or two lineages deep” and run10 “full core sync” are achievable under the 80% loss rate or need retuning.
