# DeepSeek independent research leg: wing doctrines and tactics UI readability (Oct 24, 2026)

Filing note: this is DeepSeek's independent research leg on the batch 4 themes (carrier fighter stances, carrier economy, wing veterancy, tactics UI readability, trust rules), run blind to the first leg, with DeepThink ON and Search ON, no files granted, no code written, returned through the browser task. The report arrived condensed from the browser task, and is filed here as returned. The ADOPT/ADAPT/SKIP decisions below are the leg's own. Em dashes in the returned text have been normalized to hyphens; wording is otherwise unchanged. Chair screening is separate. Treat the leg's claims as research leads, not independently verified game documentation, especially where flagged.

## (a) STANCE SYSTEMS

1. Stellaris - combat computers. Source: Space warfare - Stellaris Wiki - https://stellaris.paradoxwikis.com/Space_warfare. Sourced claim: Combat computers set preferred attack range and movement patterns (Charge, Stay at range, Maintain distance, Orbit); similar computers cluster and attack in groups; carrier computers use strike-craft range. Inference: A stance reads mainly through spacing/movement, not a label. Decision: ADAPT - give Screen/Strike/Hunt distinct spatial signatures: Screen holds close and interposes, Strike pushes to weapon range, Hunt ranges ahead. Flag: Community-maintained Paradox wiki, not an official design statement.

2. Homeworld - Aggressive/Evasive/Neutral plus formations. Source: Homeworld Remastered Collection Update 2.0 Notes - https://truesteamachievements.com/n1124/homeworld-remastered-collection-update-20-notes. Sourced claim: Stances/Rules of Engagement are split; Aggressive strike craft gain damage but lose speed and fight closer, Evasive gains speed/acceleration, loses damage, breaks formation on combat entry and fights longer-range, Neutral has no bonuses; formations dictate cohesion/turning. Inference: Stance switching is legible only when formation visibly breaks/tightens. Decision: ADAPT - Screen tightens around carrier/escorts, Strike spreads into attack runs, Hunt loosens and extends forward. Flag: Secondary summary of patch notes; values may vary by version.

3. Starsector - Engage/Regroup. Source: Fighter - Starsector Wiki - https://starsector.wiki.gg/wiki/Fighter. Sourced claim: Engage sends fighters to a target or nearest ship; Regroup returns them to the carrier, makes bombers refuse attack, and increases replacement rate. Inference: A defensive stance is interesting when it trades offence for sustain. Decision: ADAPT - Screen reduces offensive reach but improves interception priority and replacement recovery. Flag: Community wiki.

4. Battlefleet Gothic - special orders. Source: Battlefleet Gothic 1.5 rules (fan-hosted PDF) - https://flogus.free.fr/ ; Italian Wikipedia summary - https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlefleet_Gothic. Sourced claim: Special orders are declared before movement, require a Leadership/Command check, failure can lock further command checks that turn, only one order per ship, and orders impose later-phase penalties. Inference: Pricing a switch prevents free flip-flopping. Decision: ADAPT - charge a modest command-point/cooldown cost, not random failure. Flag: WEAK/UNVERIFIED - fan-hosted rules and secondary encyclopedia summary; official rulebook not directly verified.

5. World of Warships - carrier fighter consumables. Source: Ship:Aircraft - Wargaming Wiki - https://wiki.wargaming.net/en/Ship:Aircraft ; Ship:Aerial Combat - https://wiki.worldofwarships.com/Ship:Aerial_Combat. Sourced claim: Patrol Fighters are placed at a location, auto-engage enemy aircraft within 2.5-3.6 km, last about 60 seconds, and do not follow the ship; Interceptors defend friendly ships and do not spot. Inference: A stance can be an automatic engagement zone. Decision: ADAPT - Screen defines a temporary intercept zone with visible radius/duration. Flag: Official wiki, but these are consumables, not a true persistent stance system.

6. 1942-style intercept bands. Source: 1942 (video game) - Wikipedia - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1942_(video_game). Sourced claim: 1942 is a vertical scrolling shooter with enemy formations; no documented switchable intercept-band stance was found. Inference: This is a weak analogy, not a shipped stance system. Decision: SKIP - do not use as a design precedent. Flag: UNVERIFIABLE as a stance reference; absence of evidence, not proof of absence.

## (b) CARRIER ECONOMY

1. Starsector - fighter replacement rate (FRR). Source: Fighter - Starsector Wiki - https://starsector.wiki.gg/wiki/Fighter ; Fighter Replacement Rate mechanics questions - Fractal Softworks Forum - https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=17050.msg269174#msg269174 ; Fighter Update - Fractal Softworks - https://fractalsoftworks.com/2013/06/07/fighter-update/. Sourced claim: FRR lowers while wings are at/below two-thirds complement, floors at 30%, slowly regenerates otherwise; Regroup slows decay and speeds recovery; combat readiness affects replacement quality and can stop replacement. Inference: A soft floor plus recovery delay makes loss hurt tempo without deleting the carrier. Decision: ADOPT - replacement-rate floor plus recovery timer. Flag: Wiki plus forum; exact numbers are version-specific.

2. World of Warships - plane regeneration/reserves. Source: Ship:Aircraft Carriers - Wargaming Wiki - https://wiki.worldofwarships.com/Ship:Aircraft_Carriers ; Admiral Nakhimov - https://wiki.worldofwarships.com/Ship:Admiral_Nakhimov. Sourced claim: Carriers have plane regeneration times and finite hangar reserves; thin reserves/slow regeneration make losses matter. Inference: Time-gated regeneration plus finite reserve prevents infinite wings while keeping the carrier active. Decision: ADAPT - per-wing reserve count plus regeneration timer; destroyed wings return after several turns at reduced strength, not instantly at full strength. Flag: Official wiki; values vary by ship/patch.

3. Homeworld - RU cost. Source: Homeworld unit guide - https://www.ludo.guide/guide/homeworld-deserts-of-kharak/homeworld-deserts-of-kharak-part-3 ; Homeworld Walkthrough - IGN - https://rc.www.ign.com/articles/2002/09/27/homeworld-walkthrough-1379818. Sourced claim: Basic fighters cost around 65 RU, advanced around 85 RU; carriers can manufacture replacements, so losses consume resources and build time. Inference: Per-wing cost plus build queue makes each loss economically meaningful. Decision: ADAPT - charge resources per replacement wing with a build queue, high enough to change the next few turns but not to make the carrier one-wing-only. Flag: Guide/walkthrough costs, not an official balance document; may vary by game/version.

## (c) WING VETERANCY

1. BattleTech - pilot perk menu. Source: Skills - BattleTech Wiki - https://battletech.fandom.com/wiki/Skills ; BTA3062 Skills and Abilities - https://www.bta3062.com/. Sourced claim: Pilots choose rank-5 and rank-8 perks; some abilities are active with cooldowns and Resolve costs, others passive; choice is visible in a menu. Inference: A small forced menu of visible behaviour perks creates stronger wing identity than hidden bumps. Decision: ADOPT - at most three visible behaviour perks per wing at veteran ranks, with cooldowns/limited uses. Flag: Fan wiki plus mod wiki; secondary.

2. XCOM - soldier perks as forced choice. Source: Training Center - XCOM Wiki - https://xcom.fandom.com/wiki/Training_Center ; Steam discussions - https://steamcommunity.com/app/268500/discussions/. Sourced claim: Soldiers choose one of two abilities per rank from a visible menu; later options allow retraining/cross-class abilities. Inference: Mutually exclusive choices stop every wing eventually getting every perk. Decision: ADAPT - small perk slots, some mutually exclusive. Flag: Fan wiki plus forum; secondary.

3. Zero-K - hidden stat veterancy. Source: Zero-K - Cold Take #15: Experiences With Veterancy - https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/334920/view/4342106498857039209. Sourced claim: Veterancy mainly increases health, reduces reload time and weapon inaccuracy; some players say it does almost nothing except add a medal. Inference: Pure hidden bumps are a readability failure and give no new tactical option. Decision: SKIP - do not model veterancy as hidden accuracy/health/reload modifiers. Flag: Developer/publisher news reflecting a design argument, not neutral documentation.

4. Counterweight to snowballing. Source: Synthesised from BattleTech/XCOM systems above. Sourced claim: BattleTech actives have cooldowns/Resolve costs; XCOM perks are rank-gated and mutually exclusive. Inference: Stop snowballing with slot limits, cooldowns, rising replacement costs and enemy scaling, not by making veterans weak. Decision: ADAPT - cap perk slots, make high-value perks situational/cooldown-limited, increase veteran replacement cost/time. Flag: INFERENCE - no single source states this as a complete solution.

## (d) TACTICS UI READABILITY

1. Into the Breach - hide nothing. Source: Into the Breach dev on UI design: 'Sacrifice cool ideas for the sake of clarity every time' - Game Developer - https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/into-the-breach-dev-on-ui-design-sacrifice-cool-ideas-for-the-sake-of-clarity-every-time ; PCGamesN - https://www.pcgamesn.com/into-the-breach/into-the-breach-ui. Sourced claim: The team cut weapons/firing patterns that could not be conveyed clearly; the UI shows every enemy action before commitment and movement can be undone before an attack is confirmed. Inference: If a stance effect cannot be shown clearly, simplify or cut it. Decision: ADOPT - board-level telegraphs for all stance effects plus undo before committing an attack; do not rely on tooltips for critical information. Flag: Developer interview plus secondary article; strong design source.

2. Fire Emblem - danger overlay/mark-by-type. Source: How to Display Enemy's Incoming Damage in Fire Emblem Engage - Escapist - https://www.escapistmagazine.com/how-to-display-enemys-incoming-damage-in-fire-emblem-engage/ ; Danger Areas - GameFAQs - https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/. Sourced claim: Danger Area toggle highlights enemy reachable/attack tiles in red/purple; individual enemies can be marked to show range persistently. Inference: Layered, optional, toggleable threat plus per-enemy marking is readable; cyan/gold must not be used for threat because they are already reserved. Decision: ADAPT - red/orange for immediate danger, purple/magenta for projected enemy range, white for selection; keep cyan for shields and gold for premium material only. Flag: Secondary guide/forum, not an official UI design document.

3. Homeworld - Sensors Manager. Source: War Stories: How Homeworld brought the third dimension to real-time strategy - Ars Technica - https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2020/04/war-stories-how-homeworld-brought-the-third-dimension-to-real-time-strategy/. Sourced claim: Sensors Manager is a full-screen tactical map for long-range assessment and fleet orders, not just a minimap. Inference: A separate fleet mode helps a 20-ship fleet by removing noise and showing aggregate position/health/stance. Decision: ADOPT - toggleable sensor/fleet view with stance icons, aggregate health and interception radii, not the default board. Flag: Secondary article, not a design spec.

4. Sins of a Solar Empire - fleet flags/icon clarity. Source: Icon Clarity - At a Glance UI Labels - mod.io - https://mod.io/g/sins-of-a-solar-empire-ii/m/icon-clarity ; Sins forums - https://forums.sinsofasolarempire.com/. Sourced claim: Players want a side panel listing every ship, fleet flags that move between systems, and text labels because the base UI can be opaque; the Icon Clarity mod adds concise icon text. Inference: Aggregate flags plus drill-down reduce clutter while preserving detail; a stance glyph on the flag makes behaviour visible without opening a panel. Decision: ADAPT - fleet/wing flags with stance glyphs plus aggregate health, expandable to individual ships. Flag: WEAK - forum plus mod page, secondary.

## (e) TRUST RULES

1. Starsector - AI overriding explicit orders. Source: Fractal Softworks Forum - 'Make Eliminate Actually Eliminate' and ''Defend' messes with the AI' - https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php (generic forum URL only; topic-specific URLs were not supplied by DeepSeek). Sourced claim: Players report ships not promptly obeying 'go here'/'eliminate' orders; AI behaviour can take precedence, and 'Defend' can make ships overly cautious. Inference: Players hate silent override of an explicit order. Decision: ADOPT - explicit move/attack orders override stance; if stance cannot comply, show a clear 'order overridden/conflict' indicator. Flag: Forum posts, anecdotal; strong frustration signal, not a formal study.

2. XCOM - overwatch/reaction-fire unpredictability. Source: XCOM discussion - GameFAQs - https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ ; OpenXcom Forum - https://openxcom.org/forum/. Sourced claim: Players complain soldiers on overwatch do not always shoot in the desired order and reaction fire feels unpredictable. Inference: In turn-based play, predictability beats clever AI after an order is committed. Decision: ADAPT - deterministic stance targeting with exposed priority order, e.g. Screen: torpedoes, fighters, closest ship. Flag: WEAK - forum posts, anecdotal.

3. Pillars of Eternity II - free stance switch in turn-based mode. Source: Fighter Stances - Pillars of Eternity II Wiki - https://pillarsofeternity2.wiki.fextralife.com/Fighter+Stances. Sourced claim: Fighter stances are modal abilities; in turn-based mode switching is a Free Action, in real-time it has a slight cooldown. Inference: Turn-based players accept free once-per-round switching because it does not consume the main action. Decision: ADAPT - one free switch at the start of the fleet's turn; further switches in the same round cost a command point/cooldown. Flag: Fan wiki; secondary.

4. Battlefleet Gothic - priced/risky switching accepted as core rules. Source: Battlefleet Gothic 1.5 rules (fan-hosted PDF) - https://flogus.free.fr/. Sourced claim: Special orders require a Leadership check; failure can prevent further command checks that turn; orders impose later-phase penalties. Inference: A priced switch is accepted when the price is predictable and core to the loop, but random failure feels unfair in tactics. Decision: ADAPT - price with cooldown/command points, not chance to fail. Flag: WEAK - fan-hosted rules.

5. Cadence synthesis. Source: Synthesis of Pillars (free in turn-based), Battlefleet Gothic (priced orders), Starsector/XCOM trust problems. Sourced claim: As in findings 3-4 above. Inference: Free every round risks flip-flopping and devalues the choice; once per battle is too rigid for a 20-ship fleet; the middle path is one free switch per round with further switches priced. Decision: ADAPT - one free stance switch per fleet turn; further switches cost a command point or put the wing on a short cooldown. Flag: INFERENCE - no single source tests this exact cadence.

## (f) ANTI-PATTERNS

1. Infinite replacement. Source: Starsector Wiki - Fighter - https://starsector.wiki.gg/wiki/Fighter ; Fractal Softworks - Fighter Update - https://fractalsoftworks.com/2013/06/07/fighter-update/. Sourced claim: Starsector deliberately limits replacement through FRR decay, a 30% floor, combat readiness and per-deck preparation; wings can be exhausted. Inference: Infinite free replacement removes tension from losing a wing. Decision: SKIP - no infinite instant replacement; use reserves, timers and a soft replacement-rate floor.

2. Hidden stat bumps. Source: Zero-K - Cold Take #15 - https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/334920/view/4342106498857039209 ; Black Desert forum (general hidden-stats complaints) - https://www.naeu.playblackdesert.com/. Sourced claim: Zero-K veterancy is largely invisible stat increases; players object to hidden stats because they remove informed decisions. Inference: Hidden bumps fail readability and trust. Decision: SKIP - do not hide progression in invisible accuracy/health/reload modifiers. Flag: Zero-K source is official-ish; forum complaints are WEAK/anecdotal.

3. Stance as pure stat percentage. Source: Stellaris Wiki - Space warfare - https://stellaris.paradoxwikis.com/Space_warfare ; Homeworld Remastered Update 2.0 Notes - https://truesteamachievements.com/n1124/homeworld-remastered-collection-update-20-notes. Sourced claim: Stellaris computers change preferred range/movement; Homeworld stances change engagement range, formation cohesion and speed/damage tradeoffs - not pure percentage buffs. Inference: A percentage-only stance is a buff, not a stance, and will not read on the board or create tactical decisions. Decision: SKIP - require every stance to change positioning or targeting, not just a stat percentage. Flag: INFERENCE from sourced mechanics; no single source calls this an anti-pattern explicitly.

4. Overlays that hide the board. Source: Into the Breach dev on UI design - Game Developer - https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/into-the-breach-dev-on-ui-design-sacrifice-cool-ideas-for-the-sake-of-clarity-every-time. Sourced claim: The team cut visual ideas that obscured clarity and prioritised a board where every enemy action is legible before commitment. Inference: Full-board opaque overlays destroy readability, especially in 3D where silhouettes/spacing carry stance information. Decision: SKIP - no full-screen opaque danger/stance overlays; use tile/edge tinting, toggles and small world-space icons. Flag: INFERENCE; the interview supports the principle but does not discuss 3D fleet overlays specifically.

## BOTTOM LINE FROM DEEPSEEK

Make Screen/Strike/Hunt primarily positional/targeting-priority changes, not stat percentages; use distinct formation signatures plus stance glyphs on wing flags plus toggleable sensor overlay; use finite reserves plus regeneration timer plus soft replacement floor so losing a wing costs tempo/resources, not the carrier; cap veterancy at three visible behaviour perks with cooldowns/costs and mutually exclusive choices, countered by slot limits and rising replacement cost; explicit orders override stance with a visible conflict indicator, one free switch per round, further switches priced; reserve cyan for shields and gold for premium material, use red/orange for immediate threat, purple/magenta for projected range, white for selection; avoid infinite replacement, hidden stat bumps, percentage-only stances, and opaque full-board overlays.

## CAVEATS FROM DEEPSEEK

DeepSeek itself flagged the 1942 intercept-band claim as unverifiable, Battlefleet Gothic sourcing as fan-hosted/weak, several Starsector/XCOM trust claims as forum-only/anecdotal, and the cadence/counterweight recommendations as synthesis/inference rather than a single tested source. The generic forum URL for Starsector 'Make Eliminate Actually Eliminate' / 'Defend' threads and the generic Battlefleet Gothic PDF host should be re-verified before being cited externally.
