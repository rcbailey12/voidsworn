# Admiral Portraits V2: Per-Faction Brief (Run 2, R439 Approved)

**Issue:** #1879 (Owner: Neon, branch `neon/issue-1879`).
**Status:** Brief updated to Ryan's Nov 23 2026 19:59 UTC rulings (`contrib/opus/FINDINGS.md`, R439). Run 2 produces the 12-portrait test set (`contrib/neon/portraits_v2/test/`, 2 admirals per faction) plus `contrib/neon/portraits_v2/test/contact_sheet.jpg`.

## Summary: Why We Are Starting Fresh

Ryan's Nov 23 2026 ruling (`contrib/opus/FINDINGS.md`, R438) rejected both the PR 1285 candidates and the current `godot/portraits/painted/` set because they were painted admiral by admiral from old anchors with no shared faction logic:
- **Random frost and snow on Sunward Crusade portraits** (`godot/portraits/painted/vex.webp`, `tools/portraits/portrait_specs.py` lines 84-96): an old prompt misread "frost-white markings" and freezing frontier planets as icicles and snow inside heated iron warships that carry massive radiator spires to dump excess reactor heat (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`).
- **Eclipse portraits that cannot fit or act inside an Eclipse ship** (`godot/portraits/painted/nekhet.webp`): drawing an admiral as a vast floating ring-portal or room-sized shrine breaks the lore that Eclipse are separate uploaded minds in mobile machine bodies operating aboard three-deck warships with octagonal hatches and unlit dark decks (`contrib/opus/compute/lore_bible_r8.md` 7.1, 7.4; `docs/ship_pipeline/build_sheets/deathless_E11/feature_table.md`, `inventory.md`).
- **Generic or off-brand settings and cartoon finishes** (`godot/portraits/painted/kassar.webp`, `aelith.webp`, `matriarch.webp`, `mending.webp`, `grax.webp`): generic metal Star Trek consoles behind a Glasswright admiral, a ringed planet with floating hologram scribbles behind Kassar, a two-eyed reptilian snout on Grax instead of a four-eyed split-jaw void hunter, and comic-book outlines on a space hydra and a stitched ball in a sunlit warehouse for the Chorus.

Every claim below cites its source file in this repo. Where the lore is silent, the gap is stated plainly and followed by a labelled **Proposal**.

## Shared Standard Across All 6 Factions

- **Rendering style (Proposal, aligned with R197 ship concept tone):** grounded, realistic painterly concept art with tangible physical surfaces (forged iron, machined alloy, rough hide, translucent silicate flesh, wet veined tissue), dramatic directional light, and soft background depth. No flat cel-shaded comic outlines, no glossy plastic 3D look, and no neon haze. (`docs/playbooks/factions.md` R197; `docs/playbooks/screens.md` section 2.)
- **Format and framing:** 1:1 square (`1024x1024` final), head or focal center in the upper-middle third so square cards and tight UI crops both read cleanly (`tools/portraits/portrait_specs.py` lines 4-5). Clean dark-edged perimeter with **no painted border or frame**: Godot draws frames and Eclipse's restoration marks in code (`godot/portraits/README.md`, R78 Q17).
- **Zero UI noise:** no readable letters, numbers, watermarks, or floating holographic text across faces.
- **No cross-faction bleed:** every faction owns its own motifs, materials, and light colours (`contrib/opus/compute/lore_bible_r8.md` 1.3; `contrib/muse/design/faction_packages_2026_11/cross_faction_check.md`).
- **Live names and ids:** admiral ids and display names follow `godot/campaign/data/campaign_tables.json` (`tables.ADMIRALS`, the live Godot source under R405). Where older lore docs use a slightly different name, the alias is noted in parentheses; no game files are edited in Run 1.

## 1. Sunward Crusade (`voidsworn`)

### 1.1 Who they are (species, body plan, life aboard ship)
- **Species and body plan:** unaugmented frontier humans from the forty-odd dimming systems of the Vesper Reach (`contrib/opus/compute/lore_bible_r8.md` 2.1, 2.5). Zero implants and zero AI (`lore_bible_r8.md` 2.2): defectors and older officers carry plain healed surgical scars at the temple where Hegemony cortical links were cut out (`lore_bible_r8.md` 2.3, 2.5; `contrib/muse/design/faction_packages_2026_11/sunward_crusade.md`). Weathered skin with old healed cold-burn scars from freezing Vesper worlds, oath brands, grey stubble or lined faces, hard tired eyes, and white chalk dust on gloves (`lore_bible_r8.md` 2.5).
- **How a commander fits and works aboard ship:** Sunward warships are symmetric, pressurized, forged black-iron hulls with thick riveted decks, 16 internal frames, manual broadside gun galleries behind armored flank ports, hand-plotted fire-control rooms ("no AI: plotted by hand"), crew dawn-watch berths, a Roll hall where crew names are chalked, and a command deck in the spine block looking out through narrow ember-lit vision slits (`docs/ship_pipeline/build_sheets/voidsworn_S8/README.md`, `feature_table.md`; `voidsworn_S9`/`S10`/`S11` `feature_table.md`). Because their reactors run hot in vacuum, tall spine spires radiate ember-orange heat through dark iron fin slats (`docs/playbooks/factions.md` R331).
- **Approved (R439, proposal 3):** ship interiors are dry, cramped, warm-to-hot near gun galleries and reactors, smelling of hot iron, oil, and chalk dust. "Frost-scarred skin" (`lore_bible_r8.md` 2.5) shows only as old healed cold-weather scarring on the face or hands, never as snow, frost, or ice inside a pressurized warship.

### 1.2 What ties the 4 admirals together as one family
- **Dress:** heavy matte black-iron-toned void-coat with a high collar, layered dark forged shoulder plates with rivet lines (well-kept, never rusted), practical heavy gloves dusted with white chalk (`lore_bible_r8.md` 2.5; `docs/playbooks/factions.md`).
- **Insignia:** the Crusade crest, a half sun (lower half black) over a broken link (`lore_bible_r8.md` 2.9), painted in crisp matte frost-white on the left chest or shoulder plate, alongside simple frost-white rank bars (Proposal: uniform placement across all four so they read as one service).
- **Light and framing:** chest-up half-body framing, slightly low heroic eye level; warm ember-orange key light from manual plotting lamps or gun-deck slits, balanced by a cool neutral-white rim from matte frost-white overhead deck strips (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`; `godot/fx/faction_looks.json`).

### 1.3 Setting (where the portrait is taken)
- Inside a Sunward warship's riveted black-iron working spaces (`voidsworn_S8`/`S10` `feature_table.md`): the spine-block command deck with narrow ember vision slits, the Roll bulkhead lined with hand-chalked crew names, the torpedo bay rails, or the Hearth carrier's ember-lit flight-control gallery.
- **Palette (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`, `godot/fx/faction_looks.json`):** matte black iron, dark worked steel, crisp frost-white painted markings and chalk, bright ember-orange instrument and gallery light.

### 1.4 Must-exclude list
- **No ice, snow, icicles, frozen bulkheads, or frost crystals on clothing or interiors** (directly fixes R438 and `godot/portraits/painted/vex.webp`).
- **No rust** (rust belongs to the Ashfall Hullbreakers; `docs/playbooks/factions.md`, `lore_bible_r8.md` 2.5).
- **No cybernetic implants, glowing temple ports, or holographic blue screens** (those belong to the Meridian Directorate; `lore_bible_r8.md` 2.2).
- **No religious or Warhammer 40k ornament:** no wax seals, parchment purity strips, crosses, statues, stained glass, censer smoke, or cathedral arches (`docs/RULEBOOK.md` 11.2; `lore_bible_r8.md` 1.2, 2.4; `docs/playbooks/factions.md`).
- **No sea-ship cues:** no ship wheels, rope, wooden decks, or brass spyglasses (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`).

### 1.5 The 4 Sunward Crusade admirals
1. **`vex` - Admiral Roderic Vex** (*Line of battle* / *Fire for effect*): Veteran line commander who came up through the manual gun decks, buried two flagships, and chalks the crew roll beside the dorsal airlock before every jump (`sunward_crusade.md`; `godot/campaign/data/campaign_tables.json` `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** weathered, grey-stubbled senior officer standing straight in the riveted black-iron plotting deck beside a chalked crew-name bulkhead, chalk dust on heavy gloves resting on a manual brass-and-iron plotting rail, warm ember light from the plotting table below.
2. **`malek` - Oath-Captain Torben Hark** (*Hunter-killer* / *Hunter's salvo*; live display name in `campaign_tables.json` and `lore_bible_r8.md` 2.10; older draft alias Malek Draven in `sunward_crusade.md`): Former Hegemony defector who cut out his own cortical link with a mess knife and hunts enemy sterns with Cinder torpedoes (`sunward_crusade.md`; `godot/story/data/voices.json`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** lean, hard-eyed officer in a dark void-coat with a visible, plain healed excision scar at his bare temple (zero metal, zero glow), one gloved hand resting on a loaded Cinder torpedo rail inside a dim ember-lit bow tube bay.
3. **`ashgrove` - Commodore Sera Ashgrove** (*Carrier doctrine* / *Scramble all wings*): Deckborn, raised in a Hearth carrier's hangar, youngest commodore in the fleet, wears a wired headset mic to direct her fighter wings (`sunward_crusade.md`; `voices.json`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** younger, alert woman in a lighter flight-bridge void-coat with forged shoulder plates and a rugged wired headset mic at her collar, standing in a Hearth carrier's warm amber-lit flight-control gallery with black-iron fighter launch rails visible through armored glass behind her (dry warm iron, zero frost).
4. **`thale` - High Admiral Oryn Thale** (*Iron doctrine* / *Iron wall*): Oldest living crusader, veteran of three expeditions out of Vesper, steady bulwark commander who pours tea from a battered thermos during alerts and chalks her own name on the Roll (`sunward_crusade.md` uses she/her; `voices.json`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** older, silver-haired, frost-scarred woman with deep-set calm eyes in a heavy senior void-coat with forged iron gorget, standing solidly beside the dark iron Roll bulkhead with a worn metal thermos cup and a stub of white chalk at the edge of the command rail.

## 2. Meridian Directorate (`hegemony`)

### 2.1 Who they are (species, body plan, life aboard ship)
- **Species and body plan:** composed, well-kept, proudly cybernetic core-world humans linked directly to the Registry at Meridian (`lore_bible_r8.md` 3.1, 3.3). Tasteful, flush cybernetics: a neat cortical-link port at the temple with a faint implant-blue indicator, ocular implants with crisp minimal data rings across the iris, and thin metallic subdermal traces along the jaw and hands (`lore_bible_r8.md` 3.3; `contrib/muse/design/faction_packages_2026_11/hegemony_navy.md`).
- **How a commander fits and works aboard ship:** Meridian warships are clean, modular, symmetric pale-grey wedges with white corridors, numbered crew posts, blue emergency light strips, racks of identical equipment, drone bays between stern rails, and a low, flat, recessed **command deck ("the Registry link room")** tucked directly beneath the low sensor fin (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`; `docs/ship_pipeline/build_sheets/hegemony_H10/README.md`, `feature_table.md`; `hegemony_IM5/feature_table.md`). Commanders work calmly at flush, low-profile sensor and sync consoles, standing straight or seated cleanly, with zero clutter.

### 2.2 What ties the 4 admirals together as one family
- **Dress:** immaculate tailored pale-grey high-collared Directorate uniform tunic with crisp steel-blue shoulder piping, flat machined shoulder panels, and fitted dark steel-blue or pale-grey gloves (`lore_bible_r8.md` 3.3; `hegemony_navy.md`).
- **Insignia:** the Directorate crest, a clean wedge inside a ring of five linked nodes (`lore_bible_r8.md` 3.8), worn as a flush brushed-steel clasp at the high collar or left chest, plus clean modular rank bars (Proposal: consistent placement across all four).
- **Cybernetics:** identical Registry hardware family across all four (flush temple cortical-link port with faint blue light, thin subdermal jaw/hand traces, subtle iris readout ring).
- **Light and framing:** poised chest-up framing with clean geometric symmetry, level camera, cool neutral-white architectural overhead light plus soft implant-blue bounce from flush bulkhead strips (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`; `godot/fx/faction_looks.json`).

### 2.3 Setting (where the portrait is taken)
- Inside a Meridian warship's low, flat, modular **command deck / Registry link room** or adjacent flight-ops / sensor-bay deck (`hegemony_H10/feature_table.md`): flush matte pale-grey wall panels with ruler-straight seams, steel-blue trim rails, a single horizontal implant-blue light strip, and clean recessed tactical displays in soft background focus.
- **Palette (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`, `godot/fx/faction_looks.json`):** pale grey, crisp off-white interior panels, steel blue, cool implant-blue indicator light.

### 2.4 Must-exclude list
- **No floating neon hologram grids or scribbles pasted over the face** (fixes `godot/portraits/painted/kassar.webp`; ocular data lives cleanly inside the iris, not hovering like cartoons in mid-air).
- **No giant decorative ringed planet filling a round porthole directly behind the head** (fixes `kassar.webp`; portraits happen inside actual machined Directorate decks).
- **No warm orange/ember light, rust, dirt, torn cloth, or chalk** (Sunward Crusade and Hullbreaker motifs; `docs/playbooks/factions.md`, `lore_bible_r8.md` 1.3).
- **No crude bulky cybernetics, exposed wires, or half-robot skull faces** (implants are sleek, tasteful, and flush; `lore_bible_r8.md` 3.3).
- **No naval conning towers, yacht decks, brass rails, or Imperial/Star Wars peaked caps** (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`; `lore_bible_r8.md` 3.8).

### 2.5 The 4 Meridian Directorate admirals
1. **`kassar` - First Admiral Ilsa Kassar** (*Fire discipline* / *Synchronized volley*): Highest-rated commander in the Registry, formation purist who flies strictly by synchronized clocks while quietly knowing the model also wrote off Vesper (`hegemony_navy.md`; `voices.json`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** cool, razor-composed silver-haired woman standing dead-straight in the central Registry link room, faint blue iris ring and lit temple port, clean horizontal blue fleet-clock light strip aligned across the pale-grey bulkhead behind her.
2. **`vorn` - Commodore Dace Vorn** (*Precision fire* / *Interdiction strike*): Frontier listening-post hunter (Post M-14) who turns instinct into filed data and trims conservative plots down to one razor intercept (`hegemony_navy.md`; `voices.json`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** lean, sharp-eyed officer with a sleek flush precision ocular lens over one eye, angled slightly in a quiet sensor-interdiction station with a single crisp blue intercept vector glowing on a recessed console beside him.
3. **`reyes` - Fleet Marshal Corin Reyes** (*Strike group* / *Launch the strike group*): The navy's polished broadcast-ready carrier marshal who privately switches off his cortical link for the unlogged minute before decisive fights (`hegemony_navy.md`; `voices.json`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** immaculate senior marshal in dress-trimmed pale-grey uniform inside a clean Directorate flight-ops gallery overlooking sleek grey fighter cradles; Proposal: his temple link port is dimmed dark while one gloved hand rests lightly by his collar, quietly echoing the unlogged minute.
4. **`maren` - Line Admiral Oskar Maren** (*Armored column* / *Shield wall order*): Former shield engineer from the cordon squadrons who checks bow deflector gauges himself and cares who gets home to Meridian Yards (`hegemony_navy.md`; `voices.json`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** older, broader-shouldered line officer wearing a heavier armored pale-grey bridge vest over his uniform, practical engineer's hands (thin subdermal lines along the wrists) resting on a steady console showing a clean blue deflector-wall readout on a Dominion command deck.

## 3. Ashfall Hullbreakers (`corsair`)

### 3.1 Who they are (species, body plan, life aboard ship)
- **Species and body plan (locked by R64 and Nov 1 rulings):** huge, heavy-gravity alien void hunters from the Ashfall belt (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.1, 4.3; `contrib/opus/FINDINGS.md` R64; `contrib/muse/design/faction_packages_2026_11/ashfall_hullbreakers.md`). Bear-heavy hunting beast mass: a massive shoulder hump, thick forearms, and heavy clawed hands. Coarse fur **only as a mane** over the hump, neck, and shoulders (in grey, black, or rust-brown); everywhere else, tough dark hide with ridged protective plates running down the back and shoulders (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3). **Face:** bare, short, and blunt, armoured in overlapping dull horn plates grown organically from the hide with dark leathery skin in the creases; **no fur on the face, no snout, and no nose** (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3). **Jaw:** splits vertically down the middle of the chin into two tooth-lined halves that open sideways (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3). **Eyes:** a horizontal row of **four small eyes** across the brow that glint in the dark, with **heat-sensing pits** beside them (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3).
- **How a commander fits and works aboard ship:** Hullbreaker ships are lopsided, heavy-framed scrap vessels welded from scavenged iron and stolen enemy hull plates, built to run cold and dark (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`; `lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3). Ship interiors (`docs/ship_pipeline/build_sheets/corsair_A16/feature_table.md`, `corsair_A17/feature_table.md`) include the aft **Warlord hall** and **bridge** on the Pyre-Barge, the cramped forward **listening room and bridge** behind the wake-sniffer dishes on raiders and cruisers, **boarding kennels** behind the bow grabbers, **loot holds**, and a belly **hangar**. Commanders sit or brace inside heavy welded command harnesses/chairs (`voices.json` Mott; `STORY_BEAT3`), crouched or upright under low riveted girders in dim red light.
- **Posture rule (locked Nov 1 ruling, `ashfall_hullbreakers.md` line 3):** species base is low and wide (Knuckle Stalker), and portrait posture is set by character with the Warlord always upright: **Grax upright in challenge, Old Mott low and wide, Sable low and skulking, Vesh upright over trophies**.

### 3.2 What ties the 4 admirals together as one family
- **Anatomy:** exact same species blueprint on all four (four small eyes in a brow row, heat pits, blunt bare horn-plated face with leather creases, split sideways lower jaw, coarse mane on neck/hump/shoulders only, dark hide, heavy claws).
- **Dress and trophy rule (`FINDINGS.md` R78 Q5; `ashfall_hullbreakers.md` line 3):** heavy dark leather-and-iron void-harness strapped across the massive chest and shoulders, bearing **exactly one hero trophy** (a single recognizable salvaged hull plate or machine fitting taken from another faction, bolted flat). Extra harness clutter stays out.
- **Insignia:** the Hullbreaker crest, a ring of welded scrap teeth around three claw slashes (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.8; `corsair_A16/README.md`), gouged or welded onto a harness plate or background bulkhead.
- **Light and framing:** waist-up or chest-up tight framing that conveys massive heavy-gravity bulk inside cramped iron ship spaces; low-key lighting dominated by dim deep-red running lights and secondary orange work/furnace bounce.

### 3.3 Setting (where the portrait is taken)
- Inside a running-dark Hullbreaker warship (`corsair_A16`/`A17` `feature_table.md`): low welded iron ribs, overlapping mismatched dark-grey and dried-blood-red salvaged bulkhead patches, wake-sniffer scopes, weld sparks, and dim red running lamps (`docs/playbooks/factions.md` R274, R289).
- **Palette (`docs/playbooks/factions.md`, `godot/fx/faction_looks.json`):** matte soot black, dark brick rust, dried-blood red, dark-grey salvaged metal (R274, never chalky white), one faction-coloured trophy accent (e.g. violet Eclipse plate or steel-blue Hegemony plate), dim red running lights and red-orange work glow.

### 3.4 Must-exclude list
- **No reptile/dinosaur snout, no two-eyed face, and no single hinged lizard jaw** (fixes `godot/portraits/painted/grax.webp`, which gave Grax two eyes and a long scaly snout instead of the R64 blunt horn-plated face, four eyes, and sideways-splitting jaw).
- **No bears, wolves, or human bodybuilder torsos** (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3; bulk is an alien beast's hunched shoulder mass).
- **No film Predator tells:** no dreadlocks/quills/cables on the head, no mandible tusks framing the mouth, no mesh netting, no domed helmet (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3).
- **No metal masks, helmets, or riveted metal plates on the face** (face plates are living dull horn; `lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3).
- **No skulls, bones, or hanging chains/cages** (trophies are machines and hull plates bolted flat; `lore_bible_r8.md` 4.3; `docs/playbooks/factions.md` R185 (3); `corsair_A16/README.md` R221).
- **No chalky white hull patches or weird crystals** (`docs/playbooks/factions.md` R274, R197) and **no green ork skin or cartoon pirate props** (`lore_bible_r8.md` 4.2).

1. **`grax` - Warlord Grax Ironmaw** (*Fast assault* / *Full burn*; live display name in `campaign_tables.json` and `lore_bible_r8.md` 4.9; older rename-map draft Grax Steelgrin): Warlord of Rattlehold's biggest pack who won the Pyre-Barge by challenge and kept it through eleven more (`ashfall_hullbreakers.md`; `STORY_BEAT3`). **In the picture:** upright, hunched-forward challenge stance in the Pyre-Barge's dim aft Warlord hall, bristling rust-and-black mane, split jaw slightly open in a fierce challenge snarl, one scorched Hegemony steel-blue cruiser plate bolted across his shoulder harness as his hero trophy, massive clawed hand slamming a heavy redline throttle lever.

## 4. The Glasswrights (`ithar`)

### 4.1 Who they are (species, body plan, life aboard ship)
- **Species and substance (locked by R252 Loom body law; `contrib/opus/FINDINGS.md` R252; `contrib/muse/design/glasswright_species_2026_11/design.md`; visual canon `docs/shots/portraits/glasswright_species/round_4_loom/loom_sheet.jpg`):**
  - The Starwrights who stayed flesh, living 300 to 400 years (`lore_bible_r8.md` 6.1; `design.md` section 6).
  - Made of **one continuous living silicate tissue (lumen-flesh)** ranging seamlessly from pale, soft, hydrated organic flesh to clear optical glass and opaque died-back silver-white fibre at vane edges (`design.md` section 1). Nothing is bolted on or separate; they are organic weavers, never glass statues. Soft pale green-white **stored light** glows from deep inside the upper chest/thorax core; involuntary wavelength shifts act as an emotional blush (amber for grief, cold blue for focus), while they speak normally with their voices (`design.md` section 1, Nov 1 ruling).
  - **The Loom body plan (R252):** tall, narrow, vertical thorax held high (about 1.5 m off the floor) on **four long, straight, jointed support limbs** with broad ridged foot-pads (never bipedal, never a squat low spider; `design.md` section 2; `loom_sheet.jpg`). **Four arms** in two pairs from the upper thorax: upper longer **working arms** (4 fingers + opposed thumb, glass-capped tips) and lower finer **precision arms** (5 slender digits) with **spinneret pores on the inner wrists** that extrude glass thread (`design.md` section 4).
  - **Face (`design.md` section 3; `loom_sheet.jpg` panel 2):** broad, slightly flattened skull on a short thick neck; **two flush, slightly convex deep-green grown lens-eyes** with slow hydrated sliding lids (never faceless, never protruding, never compound); a **heavy segmented translucent brow** (5 to 7 rounded cushions, more in elders); **no nose**; and a **small flush woven-glass reed grille** at the mouth (part of the face, never a mask or pipe).
  - **Sails/vanes (`design.md` section 5):** a shallow layered fan of translucent, silver-edged crystal vanes grown organically out of the upper back behind the shoulders, splitting rainbow fringes at their thin margins. They furl back for bench work and spread to gather light, vent heat, or open in grief toward the dead sun.
- **How a commander fits and works aboard ship:** Glasswright ships are flowing braided **opaque pearl-white ribbons with silver edges** wrapped around **marbled stained-glass mosaic panels** of sapphire blue, emerald green, violet, and golden amber (`docs/playbooks/factions.md` R337; `docs/ship_pipeline/build_sheets/ithar_CD5/README.md`). Inside, `ithar_CD5` and `ithar_GA1` have **two shaped glass decks, 15 open annealed glass frames, three full-length braided light cores, and optical reservoirs** (`ithar_CD5/feature_table.md`, `ithar_GA1/feature_table.md`). Tall vaulted corridors and optical chambers accommodate the high four-legged Loom carriage; Glasswrights anchor their ridged foot-pads to the glass deck and command standing tall (`design.md` section 7).
- **Approved (R439, proposal 4):** Glasswright bridges, observatories, and aperture hangars have **no dark metal console boxes or blinking plastic buttons** (fixing the generic sci-fi bridge in `loom_sheet.jpg` panel 4 and `godot/portraits/painted/aelith.webp`). Instead, commanders work inside tall pearl-white and silver light chambers beside suspended hand-ground aiming mirrors, slender silver-filigree optical rails, braided light-core conduits, and softly glowing stained-mosaic wall ribs.

### 4.2 What ties the 4 admirals together as one family
- **Anatomy:** strict adherence to the R252 Loom sheet across all four (tall vertical thorax, four arms, four support limbs, broad skull, two flush deep-green lens-eyes, segmented brow, flush woven-glass mouth-grille, pale translucent lumen-flesh with fine internal light-veins, chest stored-light glow, back crystal vanes). Variation lives strictly in age/fusion depth, glow warmth, and individual vane plan (`design.md` section 6).
