### Wave 1: Core Tactical Fun: Kill Rubber-Banding, Fix Pacing, Restore Agency & Readability (Highest Fun ROI)
**Goal:** Make every single battle feel crisp, fair, legible, and rewarding before adding or reshaping higher-level content.

#### 1A. Remove Dynamic Rubber-Banding & Move to Honest Fixed Encounter Curves (Fixes G1)
- **Sources:** `fleetPower()` (`godot/rules/rules.gd:1372-1383`), `genEnemyFleet()` (`godot/rules/rules.gd:1631-1644`), `startBattle()` (`godot/rules/rules.gd:1459-1473`), `map_intel.gd` (`godot/ui/campaign/map_intel.gd:21-58`), `tables.json` (`godot/rules/data/tables.json`) (`RAMP`, `BOSS_SCALE`).
- **Recommended Approach (Pick A):**
  - Remove `fleetPower()` budget scaling (`0.75x-1.30x`) from `genEnemyFleet()` and remove `bossFit` (`0.55x-1.0x`) from `startBattle()`.
  - Replace hidden `RAMP.thin` (`0.73/0.67/0.62`) + rubber-band math with an **explicit, fixed sector/column/tier enemy budget table** (calibrated cleanly per Sector 1, 2, 3 and Easy / Standard / Elite / Boss nodes) so repairing ships, buying Mk upgrades, and drafting strong combos strictly make you stronger against the curve.
  - Update `map_intel.gd` tooltips to show **readable encounter strength tier + enemy ship count/archetype** (never `"23% of your fleet's (includes Standard AI -5%)"`).
- **Alternative (Option B):** Keep a tiny one-way floor (`min 0.85x` protection only in Sector 1 Column 1-2 if a player lost half their fleet early), with **strictly 0% upward penalty** (`cap = 1.0`) for upgrades, repairs, boons, relics, or meta unlocks.
- **Fable Sign-Off:** Pick A (pure fixed curve like FTL/Slay the Spire) vs Option B (fixed upward, gentle Sector 1 pity floor)? Target win-rate band on Threat 0 across `playtest-and-balance` sims (e.g., 45-60% for competent play)?
- **Astra Sign-Off:** Rules determinism & saved-results impact: this intentionally changes battle enemy generation and HP baselines, requiring a coordinated re-baseline of `godot/rules/test/` saved results + 30-seed campaign balance sim verification (`playtest-and-balance` skill). Confirm zero save-file breakage for in-progress runs (store fixed encounter budget on node generation).
- **Opus Scope:** 1 Opus run + 1 balance-sim tuning pass (1-2 review rounds).

#### 1B. Battle Pacing, Camera Hygiene & Enemy Tactical Intent (Fixes G3 + G4)
- **Sources:** `battle.gd:64-67,165` (`godot/scenes/battle/battle.gd:64-166`), `_play_batch()` (`godot/scenes/battle/battle.gd:1280-1378`), `_cine_wants()` (`godot/scenes/battle/battle.gd:2187-2210`), `_overlay()` (`godot/scenes/battle/battle.gd:3327-3452`), `aiPickActivation()` (`godot/rules/rules.gd:7266`), `aiUnit()` (`godot/rules/rules.gd:7499`).
- **Recommended Approach (Pick A):**
  1. **Camera stability:** Change default `cine = "key"` so `_cine_wants()` triggers ONLY on **capital/flagship kills, boss Commands, and player-flagged dramatic finishers**, NEVER on routine lances, torpedoes, or novas. Keep `"full"` as an explicit opt-in in Settings for cinematic fans.
  2. **Snappy turn tempo:** Include enemy move time inside the round speedup budget (`battle.gd:1329`), trim default `MOVE_HEX` lead-in/per-hex travel by ~25-30% at 1x speed, and add a **sticky Fast/Skip toggle** (or hold-to-fast-forward that persists across the whole enemy turn instead of resetting at `battle.gd:1370`).
  3. **Enemy Intent Telegraphing:** Pre-select the next activating enemy ship (using a pure deterministic preview call to `aiPickActivation()` + intended primary target/move goal) and render:
     - A crisp **"NEXT" badge/pip** on the next enemy ship to activate.
     - A subtle **hoverable target-intent tether** (or icon: Attack / Ram / Board / Ability) showing who that next enemy wants to hit, plus an optional toggle for all unacted enemies' primary threat.
  4. **Readable Combat Odds & Softer Section Breaks:**
     - Trim hit/crit variance extremes (e.g., damage variance `0.90-1.10` instead of `0.85-1.15`; raise base torpedo accuracy against cruisers/capitals or give torpedoes a clear armor/evasion split so big cooldown shots aren't 24% coin flips).
