## 3. First-Time Player View: FUN vs NOT FUN or Rewarding (Ryan's Ask)

Judged from the player-path stills (`run_seed10`, `run_seed121`, `stills/`, `factions/`) and in-game help/UI, using the patterns players cite in Steam reviews of FTL, Into the Breach, Slay the Spire, Battletech, and Darkest Dungeon.

### What is FUN (5)

1. **Flagship Aspect build hook (`PT25adec3_seed10_t001.jpg`):** Opening a run with a crisp, well-lit 3D cruiser (`VIGILANT ASH`) beside three build-defining roles (`Ram`, `Spear`, `Admiral`) immediately invites theorycrafting. *Player-review pattern:* Slay the Spire and FTL players praise an instant "what kind of run am I building?" choice in the first 30 seconds.
2. **Approach-to-the-star 3D Sector Map (`PT25adec3_seed10_t003.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t023.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed121_t019.jpg`):** Clear branching routes toward the Hollow Sun, visible sector modifiers, Nemesis Hunt Clock, and `I II III` progression make every jump feel like a real risk-reward decision. *Player-review pattern:* FTL reviews single out "every jump is a decision" as the core "one more run" loop.
3. **Deterministic move and firing previews (`PT25adec3_battle_02_2_move_preview.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t007.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t010.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t035.jpg`):** Seeing movement cost, bearing guns, hit percentages, which shield side takes the hit (`prow shield`), and expected shield vs hull damage *before* committing makes tactics feel fair and earned. *Player-review pattern:* Into the Breach and Battletech players constantly praise transparent pre-commit math ("when I lose, I see why").
4. **Tangible refit and captain promotion spikes (`PT25adec3_seed121_t018.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t038.jpg`):** Choosing `Lance focusing crystal (+25% damage)` vs `Battery loaders (+1 shot)` and captain perks (`Steady hand` vs `Rapid cycling`) feels like an actual combat upgrade rather than a tiny `+2%` stat trickle. *Player-review pattern:* Slay the Spire and Hades reviews highlight upgrades you feel on the very next turn.
5. **Readable alien silhouettes and bleak command voice (`PT25adec3_deathless_05_6_enemy_turn.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed121_t040.jpg`):** Eclipse's stark geometric ring ships stand out cleanly at battle range, and Admiral Vex's defeat line (`"Add my name to the wall, under the others"`) after losing a 2/3-round Relay gives a harsh sci-fi weight that lingers after a wipe. *Player-review pattern:* Darkest Dungeon and Battletech players quote memorable voice barks and harsh loss lines in positive reviews.

### What is NOT FUN or Not Rewarding (5)

1. **Feeling blind on Turn 1 and buried in tan rocks (`PT25adec3_battle_01_1_battle_start.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t025.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed121_t023.jpg`):** The enemy fleet leaves the screen with no edge arrows, dark ships vanish into giant pale asteroid piles, and Battle 2's `Relay` hex has no marker or hover tooltip. *Player-review pattern:* Tactics games (Battletech, XCOM) get quick refunds when players lose turns just hunting for where enemies and objectives are. *One-line review:* "Spent two rounds squinting past brown rocks trying to find the enemy and the relay." *Fix:* Add edge markers, a 3D Relay beacon + hover rule, and tone down rock cluster scale/brightness.
2. **Contradictory `Burn and Fire` + chopped combat log (`PT25adec3_seed10_t014.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t018.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed10_t037.jpg`):** Target panel says `"No ready gun can reach it"` right beside `"BURN AND FIRE"`, hovers say `"to the ghost"` and `"now 0"`, and every hit in the battle log cuts off before hull damage (`"7 to prow shields, 1..."`). *Player-review pattern:* Into the Breach / Slay the Spire players lose trust fast when the UI contradicts itself or hides combat outcomes. *One-line review:* "UI told me no gun could reach while flashing Burn and Fire, and the log cuts off every damage number." *Fix:* Compute Target preview from the attack-move hex, clean dev jargon, and wrap/widen log entries.
3. **Fake 3-card Scrap loot and silent hold items (`PT25adec3_seed10_t022.jpg`, `PT25adec3_seed121_t019.jpg`):** Winning Battle 1 and getting three Scrap-only cards (`+36c`, `+25c`, `+25c`) with no explanation of why no refit dropped feels empty; meanwhile, when a refit *does* drop into the hold, the map button (`Fleet and refits`) never reminds you to equip it. *Player-review pattern:* Roguelite players (Slay the Spire, FTL) complain bitterly when post-fight reward screens offer non-choices or bury earned loot. *One-line review:* "Beat my first fight and got offered three flavors of pocket change, then forgot my refit in cargo." *Fix:* Explain kill-cause scrap rules (or auto-award when all wrecks are scrap) and badge `Fleet and refits (N in hold)`.
