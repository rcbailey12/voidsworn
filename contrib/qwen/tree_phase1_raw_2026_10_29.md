CROSS-FACTION NOTES
Free-volley Commands (Vex, Saro, Voice of the Deep, Umbra Vessane, Kassar): all share the "every ship fires at best target in reach" shape. Differentiated by mechanic focus: Vex manipulates arcs, timing, and kill-chains; Saro focuses on range, aim-sharing, and armor penetration; Voice of the Deep applies stacking corrosion and armor stripping; Umbra Vessane iterates on corona arcs and targeting priority; Kassar anchors everything to formation radius and sync.
Carrier/wing Commands (Ashgrove, Reyes, Mott, Matriarch, Tamsa): five admirals share the "extra wing, squadrons strike again +50%" shape. Differentiated by: Ashgrove offers escort splitting and permanent wing persistence (debt idiom); Reyes integrates cruisers and staggers launch timing (Registry idiom); Mott emphasizes volume, kill-chains, and expendable wings that never return (pack idiom); Matriarch spawns new swarms from kills and grows them (song idiom); Tamsa records flight paths, corrects missed attacks, and grants permanent accuracy gains (glass idiom).
Shield-wall Commands (Thale, Maren, Brin, Seveth): four admirals share "shields refill, 40% less hull damage." Differentiated by: Thale converts damage taken into offensive output and offers a reactive trigger (oath idiom); Maren pre-files the wall, purges debuffs, and enables shield transfer between allies (Registry idiom); Brin reflects blocked damage, splits oversized hits across adjacent ships, and hardens shields under impact (glass idiom); Seveth scales reduction with hits taken, stores blocked damage as delayed healing, and restores modules (Archive idiom).
Movement/accuracy Commands (Ironmaw, Razormaw, Vell): three admirals share "+2 movement, +1 turn, -20 enemy accuracy." Differentiated by: Ironmaw rewards closing to adjacency and ramming range (charge idiom); Razormaw rewards flanking, rear-arc positioning, and pursuit chains (hunt-song idiom); Vell rewards evasion, misdirection, and converting dodged attacks into counter-damage (refraction idiom).
Repair Commands (Vesh, Mending Throng, Aurex): three admirals share "repair 25% hull, mend destroyed sections." Differentiated by: Vesh converts scrap into cumulative armor and max-hull gains (pack/scavenger idiom); Mending Throng converts overflow healing into damage and sustains multi-round regen (organic-growth idiom); Aurex stores repaired states as one-time damage shields and iterates each rebuild stronger (Archive idiom).
