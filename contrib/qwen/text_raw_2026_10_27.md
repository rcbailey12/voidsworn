BLOCKER 1. Text contradicts its own numbers: deathless faction blurb uses "starlight" where the bible mandates "stellar energy"
OBSERVED. data.js FACTIONS.deathless.blurb reads: "Starwright minds in machine bodies, stealing starlight to light a new sun."
The lore bible round 8 front matter states explicitly: "Energy, not light. Eclipse siphon stellar energy: arrays built round other stars collect it and push it home through jump relays." Section 7.2 reinforces: "The dark, shadow, the eclipse, warmth they remember and can no longer feel." The word "starlight" is a direct contradiction of a named ruling. The blurb also says "to light a new sun" which leans on light language the bible retired for this faction.
Verdict for this check: one BLOCKER found; all other numbers in descriptions match their data fields.

BLOCKER 2. Name consistency: h_picket_i variant description uses the base weapon name instead of the Hegemony faction name
OBSERVED. data.js HULL_VARIANTS.h_picket_i.d reads: "Turret laser and +6 evasion: screens wherever it points."
The weapon id is 'laser'. FAC_WNAMES.hegemony.laser is 'Plasma cannon'. The bible section 3.8 lists "Plasma cannon" as the Hegemony weapon name. The player sees "Plasma cannon" on the ship card (via wName), but the variant flavour text in the shipyard says "Turret laser", a name no Hegemony ship ever displays. This teaches the player the wrong name for the gun they are buying.
Verdict for this check: one BLOCKER; all other names (hulls, weapons, ranks, classes, ship names, captain pools, relics, modules, admirals, Nemesis pools) match the bible round 8 exactly across all three files.

FIX 1. Rules clarity: five weapon trait descriptions omit the numbers the data carries
OBSERVED. data.js WTRAITS:
(a) las: d is "holds its aim at long range". Effect: falloff halved, +4 accuracy. No number given.
(b) hellfire: d is "sets the target burning". Effect: ignite=1. No damage per turn, no duration.
(c) scrap: d is "an extra shot, wild aim, lucky crits". Effect: +1 shot, -12 accuracy, +3 crit. No numbers.
(d) needle: d is "precise: better aim and more criticals". Effect: +6 accuracy, +8 crit. No numbers.
(e) star: d is "fast: hard to dodge". Effect: evMult=1.2 (20% harder to evade). No number.
INFERRED. A player reading these cannot predict the mechanical effect. Compare with traits that do give numbers: spines ("each hit strips 3% armor (down to 15% less)"), acid ("acid eats 4 hull a turn for 2 turns"), gauss ("ignores half of armor"). The five above should follow the same pattern.

FIX 2. Rules clarity: WING_PERKS.pickets gives no number
OBSERVED. data.js WING_PERKS.pickets.d: "Screening wings shoot down torpedoes more often."
No percentage, no flat bonus, no comparison. A player cannot judge whether this perk is worth one of three slots.

FIX 3. Rules clarity: the "las" trait description does not say what "long range" means
OBSERVED. Same entry as FIX 1(a). "Holds its aim at long range" does not tell the player that range falloff is halved. A player who does not know the falloff mechanic cannot act on this.
INFERRED. This is the same root cause as FIX 1(a) but affects comprehension differently: even if the number were stated, the mechanic (falloff) needs naming.

FIX 4. Voice: deathless blurb uses light-verb phrasing ("to light a new sun")
OBSERVED. Same blurb as BLOCKER 1. The bible section 7.2 assigns Eclipse "the dark, shadow, the eclipse, warmth they remember and can no longer feel" and explicitly separates them from light language, which belongs to the Glasswrights (section 1.3 motif table: "Light, glass, reflections, grief, what was taken" is owned by Glasswrights). "To light a new sun" puts a light verb in Eclipse's mouth. Suggested direction: "building a new sun from stolen stellar energy" or "driving stolen fire into a new sun".

FIX 5. Duplicate ability descriptions across factions with no mechanical difference
OBSERVED. data.js ABILITIES:
(a) evasive.d and phase.d are both "+25 evasion until your next turn and +2 movement now." (cd:3 both).
(b) focus.d and frenzy.d are both "All of this ship's weapons deal +30% damage this turn." (cd:3 both).
INFERRED. These are mechanically identical and textually identical. The player has no way to tell from the text whether the Deathless "Fold skip" differs from the Voidsworn "Evasive burn", or whether Chorus "Blood frenzy" differs from Voidsworn "Concentrated fire". Either the numbers should differ or the text should say they are the same effect under a different name.

FIX 6. Rules clarity: the Chorus trait "spines" caps description is ambiguous
OBSERVED. WTRAITS.spines.d: "each hit strips 3% armor (down to 15% less)".
INFERRED. "Down to 15% less" could mean the target's armor is reduced by at most 15 percentage points, or that armor cannot go below 15% of its original value. The data field is shred:.03. Without seeing the battle code, the player cannot tell which reading is correct. Suggest: "each hit strips 3% of the target's armor, up to a maximum of 15% stripped."
