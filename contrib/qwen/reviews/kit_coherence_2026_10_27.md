FACTION 1: HEGEMONY NAVY (id: hegemony)
Findings, worst first:
FIX. OBSERVED. The ionpulse ability on h_interdictor reads: "Every enemy ship within 4 hexes loses half of each shield facing." Chorus fields five hulls all with sh:0 and rg:0 (b_drone, b_spitter, b_razor, b_brood, b_boss). Eclipse fields four hulls all with sh:0 (d_scythe, d_jackal, d_harvester, d_boss). Against both factions the ability has no legal target for its effect. Its entire text is negated. That is 2 of 6 opposing factions against which a ship's sole active ability does nothing.
FIX. OBSERVED. The ion weapon carries the property ion:2.5 (data.js, W object). The lore bible ruling (OPUS_HANDOFF.md section 1) demotes shield-stripping to "ion weapon flavor, not the identity." As flavor the property is permitted, but against Chorus and Eclipse (both sh:0 across every hull) the flavor effect has no mechanical expression. The weapon still deals 12 damage as a lance-type, so it is not fully dead, but its distinguishing property is inert against a third of the roster. The Hegemony fields ion weapons on h_interdictor, h_vanguard, h_dominion, and h_boss, so the dead zone touches every capital in the line.
NOTE. OBSERVED. The plasma weapon trait reads "a quarter burns through shields" and sets pierce to at least 0.25. Against Chorus and Eclipse there are no shields to pierce. Whether the pierce value still interacts with armor is not specified in data.js. The trait's dmg:+1 still applies, so the weapon is not dead, but the thematic half of the trait ("burns through shields") is inert against those two factions. INFERRED: the +1 damage keeps the trait alive; only the shield language is void.
NOTE. OBSERVED. The recorded definer is "synchronized fire (Kassar doctrine faction-wide) PRIMARY" (OPUS_HANDOFF.md). In data.js lines 1 to 275 I find no faction-wide synchronized fire mechanic. The MOVE_RULES entry for hegemony is retro thrusters (rev:1). Admiral Kassar's personal fx grants battShots:1 and dmgPct:-.05, but that is admiral-scoped, not faction-wide. The faction-wide implementation may live in battle.js, which is not in the pack. I cannot confirm or deny compliance from this data.
NOTE. OBSERVED. The recorded loss is "formation dependence, flagship-anchor version TO START" (OPUS_HANDOFF.md). No anchor-radius or sync-collapse mechanic appears in data.js lines 1 to 275. As above, this may live in battle.js. Not verifiable here.
NOTE. OBSERVED. h_dominion has n:'Dominion dreadnought' but cls:'Battleship'. The display name says dreadnought; the mechanical class says Battleship. The lore bible 3.8 lists the class simply as "dominion" without a type word. Minor naming friction; the player sees "dreadnought" in the name and "Battleship" in the class tag.
NOTE. OBSERVED. Lore bible 3.8 lists "Heavy pulse laser (was Turbolaser; it fires in pulses)" as a weapon. The data entry hturbo has n:'Heavy pulse laser' and the Hegemony rename is also 'Heavy plasma lance'. Wait: FAC_WNAMES hegemony gives hturbo:'Heavy plasma lance'. The base W entry gives hturbo:{n:'Heavy pulse laser'}. The lore says the weapon is "Heavy pulse laser." The faction rename overrides it to "Heavy plasma lance." The lore bible lists both names in the same sentence, so this appears to be base versus faction name. No conflict. Resolved in place; no action needed.
Rulings compliance:
Synchronized fire as definer: not visibly expressed in data.js. See finding 4.
Ion as weapon flavor only: the ion:2.5 property sits on the weapon, not as a faction identity mechanic. Compliant in placement. INFERRED: the ruling is honored in data.js; whether battle.js elevates it further is unknown.
No breach of Chorus no-repair, Glasswrights no-Brace, or Eclipse no-shield rulings applies here (those concern other factions).
Verdict: PASS WITH CONDITIONS. The ionpulse dead zone (finding 1) is the sharpest issue. The data does not breach any of the four listed rulings, but two mechanics are inert against a third of the opposing roster.

CROSS-FACTION FINDINGS
FIX. OBSERVED. Shield-stripping dead zone. Two factions field zero shields across every hull: Chorus (5 hulls) and Eclipse (4 hulls). The ion weapon property (ion:2.5) and the ionpulse ability target shields specifically. Against Chorus and Eclipse these effects are void. This concentrates the dead zone on the Hegemony, whose identity ruling (OPUS_HANDOFF.md) already demotes shield-stripping to "ion weapon flavor." The ionpulse ability, however, is a ship's sole active ability and becomes a blank button against two of six opponents. INFERRED: a fallback (e.g. ion:2.5 converting to hull or system damage against unshielded targets) would preserve the button's function without restoring shield-stripping as the Hegemony definer.
NOTE. OBSERVED. Shield-dependent modules and perks. The modules capacitor (+30% shield strength), regen (+50% shield regeneration), and the perk deflector (+40% shield regeneration) are worthless on Chorus and Eclipse hulls (sh:0, rg:0). data.js does not gate these by faction or shield presence. Whether the UI prevents selection is not in the pack. INFERRED: if the UI does not block them, a player can waste a module slot or perk on a dead effect.
NOTE. INFERRED. No identity blur detected. Each faction's mechanical levers are distinct: voidsworn (broadside-on-move rake), hegemony (retro thrusters, sync fire pending), corsair (sideslip, scrap/rockets), chorus (regen, free pivot, no shields), ithar (evasion-after-move, blink, no brace), deathless (fold drive, reanimate, no shields, armor pierce). No two factions share the same MOVE_RULES key, the same weapon trait set, or the same ability suite.
NOTE. OBSERVED. The bible promises the Chorus was "grown to fight Eclipse" (lore 5.1, 5.4). The acid trait on tendril and sporeb deals damage "through shields and armor." Eclipse has no shields but heavy armor (ar:.2 to .35). Acid bypassing armor is mechanically relevant against Eclipse. INFERRED: the counter relationship is delivered by the acid trait, though there is no explicit bonus damage against Eclipse specifically.

OVERALL VERDICT
PASS WITH CONDITIONS.
No faction's kit contradicts its bible identity in weapons, hulls, or abilities. The four rulings I was asked to check (Hegemony definer, Chorus no repair, Glasswrights no Brace, Eclipse no shields) show no breach in data.js, though three of the four are enforced in code outside the pack and could not be confirmed. The one mechanical finding that warrants a fix before playtest is the ionpulse dead zone (Hegemony finding 1): a ship ability that is a blank button against two of six opponents. The ion weapon's inert shield property against those same two factions is a lesser instance of the same shape. The Eclipse internal key naming (gauss, doom, nekhet, sekhmar, anuket, khepra, phylactery) contradicts the lore bible's explicit avoid list at the source level; this is invisible to the player but should be cleaned up to match the stated design direction. Everything else is NOTE-level: presentation gaps, minor asymmetries, and items awaiting confirmation from code outside the pack.
