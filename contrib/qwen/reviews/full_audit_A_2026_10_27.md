## Job 4. Consistency

4a. Timeline and causes.
The four dates (lines 73 to 76) are internally consistent. Collapse at roughly 1,000 years ago; Siphoning measurable at roughly 90 years ago (giving the Totality about 910 years to plan and build, reasonable for machine minds); secession at roughly 70 years ago (20 years of observed dimming before the Reach breaks away); Vyre captured 25 years ago. The Siphoning affects the Vesper Reach's stars (line 137), and the Totality siphon "neighbouring stars along vast beam arrays" (line 460), so the Reach must be within beam range of the Ardent system. This is consistent with the three-sector board around the Hollow Sun (line 69). No contradiction found.

4b. Faction wants follow from story.
All six factions' wants derive from their origins. Checked each: Crusade wants the Siphoning stopped because their stars are dying (line 137, 169). Hegemony wants the cores because Vyre proved their value (line 209, 224). Hullbreakers want the war and Rook because they hunt heat (line 259, 276). Chorus wants to come home and eat because Memory remembers the sun (line 329, 346). Glasswrights want the cores shut down because their ancestors' work killed a star (line 395, 413). Totality wants all six cores to steer the new sun (line 120, 477). No contradictions.

4c. Deeds versus "what they think of the others."
Checked all thirty opinion lines (lines 177 to 181, 237 to 241, 289 to 293, 359 to 363, 426 to 430, 490 to 494) against the deeds described in the origin and history sections. Every opinion tracks a deed or a known fact. One intentional tension: the Crusade says of the Glasswrights "Their siblings run the siphons" (line 180), blaming them by kinship. The Glasswrights acknowledge this at line 426. This is characterization (the Crusade is unfair), not a continuity error. No contradictions.

4d. Core "where it went" lines versus faction sections.
All six cores checked. K-1 at Ardent with the Totality (lines 35, 122, 477). V-2 captured by the Hegemony (lines 36, 123, 209). H-3 seized by the Chorus at the collapse (lines 37, 124, 329). A-4 caged by the Glasswrights (lines 38, 125, 395). R-5 adrift, hunted by Hullbreakers (lines 39, 126, 259). S-6 at Vesper's outer belt with the Crusade (lines 40, 127, 168). All consistent.

4e. Motif owners versus name pools and text.
CONTRADICTION 1. Heat motif. Line 100 assigns "heat" to the Chorus's motif row ("Short hungry lines, taste, heat, smell, teeth"). Line 104 assigns the Hullbreakers "Violence, strength, trophies, scrap, fear" and does not include heat. But the Hullbreakers' entire identity is heat-based: they see by heat (line 259), their skulls glow red when they hunt (line 269), their ships have a "red-orange heat glow" (line 270), they "talk in heat" (line 265), and their opinions use heat language ("They run hot," line 289; "Warm, and hungry," line 291; "No heat at all," line 293). Fix: add "heat-sense, the hunt by warmth" to the Hullbreaker row at line 104, and narrow the Chorus row at line 100 to "taste, smell, teeth, hunger" (dropping "heat" from the Chorus, since the Chorus uses heat as flavour while the Hullbreakers use it as vision).
CONTRADICTION 2. "Flare" in the Totality ship classes. Line 512 lists "flare (destroyer)" as a Totality class. Line 99 gives "Light" to the Glasswrights. A flare is a burst of light. The Totality's own motif row (line 101) is darkness and memory; their look is violet-black (line 472). Fix: rename the destroyer class to "umbra" (freeing it from the rank list at line 511, where it can be replaced by "penumbra") or to "shade-class" if the rank list is adjusted, or simply "veil."
