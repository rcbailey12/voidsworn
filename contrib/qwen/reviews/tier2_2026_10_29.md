Cross-pair notes
The "once per battle, full recall-and-relaunch" chassis (Pair 4 Branch C T2, option 1) and the "once per battle, all-at-once activation" chassis (Pair 11 Branch B T2, option 1) both serve as ultimate burst buttons; they read differently because Pair 4 resets launched assets while Pair 11 compresses the activation sequence, but playtesting should confirm they do not feel identical at the table.
Pair 12 (Vell + Vesh) is flagged in the brief as the weakest fit, and the shared tier 2 leans hard on the "move then heal" loop to bridge surge and restore. If playtesting shows the two admirals want different things out of tier 2, this pair should be split before tier 3.
Keeping pure numeric effects to Branch C and at or above the 15% floor was the hardest constraint when the natural deepening of a pair's iron or restore identity suggested a small passive modifier; several drafts were reworked into triggered or conditional effects to stay within the one-in-three cap.
The Mending Chain heal in Pair 10 Branch B (5% splash) is below the 15% floor, but it is not a pure numeric effect - it is a positioning trigger that changes adjacency decisions. If the ruling is that any percentage modifier counts, this skill should be reworked.
Pair 6 (Vorn + Sable) and Pair 5 (Draven + Ostrel) both occupy the hunt family but their tier 2 branches diverge: Pair 5 deepens torpedo-specific pursuit while Pair 6 deepens multi-arc concentration and wound-follow-up. This felt correct given the Commands, but the two pairs should be checked together for overlap in feel.
