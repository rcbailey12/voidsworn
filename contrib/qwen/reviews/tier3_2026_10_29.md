Cross-admiral notes
- The "flagship repair/volley doubled" chassis appears across Vesh, Mending Throng, Nadir Aurex, and Seveth. I differentiated by varying what the excess does: Vesh splits it freely, the Throng assigns it to the most wounded, Aurex converts it to a section restore, and Seveth banks it for a later round. The shared shape is deliberate (it is the natural capstone-adjacent flagship skill for restore/iron admirals) but the spend decision differs each time.
- The "+15% damage during the Command" pure-numeric Branch C slot proved hard to avoid for barrage and hunt admirals whose Branch A already owns the timing and targeting decisions. I kept it at exactly the 15 percent floor and limited it to one per admiral, always paired with a decision-changing flagship skill in the other Branch C slot. Twelve of the twenty-four admirals carry one such numeric; the ratio stays under one in three.
- Rule 5 (nothing rewards losing) was hardest to keep for the iron/restore admirals, whose identity is taking hits. I resolved it by anchoring every effect to a state that was true before the damage landed (bracing, layered plates, wall declared in advance) rather than paying out because damage was taken.
- Oskar Maren's tier 3 exposes a softness in his tier 2 pair set: pair 8's "Shoulder to Shoulder" (adjacency refill) and his Branch A "Column Filed Straight" (line-of-three refill) already cover the adjacency-reward space, forcing his tier 3 to reach for the chain-overflow and 2-hex swap to stay distinct. If Opus trims either earlier effect, Maren's tier 3 can be simplified.
- Vell of the Third Mirror's tier 3 is drafted entirely against her scatter/refraction identity, honoring the pair-12 split. Her Branch B T3 second choice ("Every Mirror Casts a False Hull") is close in shape to her Branch A T1 ("Two Images Appear"), but escalates from a flat accuracy loss to a conditional evasion payoff on a would-be hit, which I judged sufficiently distinct; flagging for chair review.
