## Economy audit (Pack D, second run)

Findings: (1) OBSERVED (claimed), runRenown never defined in the provided code, so renown defaults to 0 and the whole Legacy economy is unobtainable (campaign_meta.js:187). NOTE: disproven at screening; see the round-2 screening doc. (2) INFERRED, Master Shipwrights (45 renown, permanent 15% discount) strictly superior to War Chests (20 renown, one-time +75 credits); one Cathedral purchase saves 51 (data.js:247). (3) INFERRED, "never pay for repairs" is mathematically optimal: drydocks (free 60%) and field repairs (free 20%) appear roughly twice per sector per the sim annex, plus the salvage relic's free 8% (campaign_run.js:185). (4) INFERRED, scrap vs repair threshold inverts on heavy damage: a Penitent at 1 HP costs 60 to repair but sells for 70 (campaign_run.js:181). (5) OBSERVED, income scales faster than sinks; S3 col-6 boss pays about 237 while costs never scale, so sector 3 pressure drops (campaign_run.js:237). (6) OBSERVED (claimed), Heat mechanics unwired in the provided code (data.js:376). NOTE: pack artifact; battle.js was not in the pack and does apply Heat. (7) INFERRED, Salvage Rig relic breaks the repair economy: 15 credits plus 8% repair per battle, estimated about 450 credits of value over a 10-battle run (data.js:195). (8) INFERRED, module stripping strictly better than scrapping wrecks (modules 55 to 90 vs scrap 10+9*cp) (campaign_run.js:271). (9) OBSERVED, Scarce uniquely counters the free-repair meta: repair:.75 also nerfs drydock/field percentages while price:1.2 inflates the yard (data.js:373). (10) INFERRED, the Mercenary event is arbitrage: 80 credits for a 130-credit Harrow plus a veteran captain (data.js:285).

Not checked (per the audit): battle.js and combat logic; the runRenown function; human play patterns; patron boon execution math.
