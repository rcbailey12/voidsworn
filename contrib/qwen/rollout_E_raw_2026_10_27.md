Job E1: G4 20-ship bench (commits cc8aeed and 2aac0ca)
E1-1. The stated cause is convincing. OBSERVED.
Evidence: commit 2aac0ca message in context_E.md; g4_gallery_readme.md, "20-ship performance record" section. The record names two mechanisms: (a) every damage rebuild re-launched the ship's death cut, costing seconds of worker time on big hulls with piece meshes uploaded on the main thread, and (b) a death waited on a cut still running. The worst-frame times (4302 ms Forward+ 1080p, 4029 ms Forward+ 4K, 1584 ms Compatibility 1080p) are consistent with a multi-second mesh rebuild and upload blocking the main thread. The averages remain comfortable (253, 143, 281 fps), confirming the stall is an isolated event, not sustained load. The six-flagship 4K comparison (1% low 21.2 ms) versus the 20-ship 4K run (1% low 109.8 ms) confirms the stall scales with ship count, which fits a per-ship rebuild trigger.
E1-2. The fix is sound against that cause. INFERRED.
Evidence: commit 2aac0ca message in context_E.md. The fix has three parts: (1) a ship re-cuts at most every 6 s, which rate-limits the expensive rebuild; (2) the last finished cut is kept and a death breaks up along it, which removes the wait on a running cut; (3) a first cut still running holds the cook-off up to 2.5 s instead of freezing the frame, which converts a hard stall into a bounded visual delay. Each part addresses one of the two named causes. The 6 s throttle is well below the bench's 30 s window and well above the frame budget, so it should not cause visible popping in normal play. The 2.5 s cook-off hold is short enough to feel like a beat of dramatic timing rather than a hang. No logical gap between cause and fix is apparent.

E1-4. NOTE. The 4K 1% low of 9 fps is the same stall at higher resolution. INFERRED.
Evidence: g4_gallery_readme.md bench table. Forward+ 4K shows 1% low 109.8 ms (9 fps) and worst frame 4029 ms, versus 1080p at 66.8 ms (15 fps) and 4302 ms. The worst-frame times are nearly identical (both around 4 s), confirming the same death-cut stall. The 1% low is worse at 4K because the GPU frame budget is tighter (6.99 ms average vs 3.96 ms), so a given CPU stall displaces more frames. The fix targets the CPU-side cause, so it should lift the 4K 1% low as well, but this is not confirmed without r19.
E1-5. NOTE. The Compatibility renderer shows a shorter but still real freeze. OBSERVED.
Evidence: g4_gallery_readme.md bench table. Compatibility 1080p: 1% low 54.6 ms (18 fps), worst frame 1584 ms (1.6 s). The freeze is shorter than Forward+ (1.6 s vs 4.3 s), likely because the Compatibility path has a lighter render pipeline and the mesh upload finishes sooner, but the same code path triggers it. The fix in 2aac0ca is renderer-agnostic (it gates the cut and the cook-off, not the draw calls), so it should apply here too. UNSURE whether the 1.6 s freeze has a second contributing cause specific to the Compatibility renderer; the pack does not break it down further.
E1-6. NOTE. No other likely 20-ship hitch is identified in the pack beyond the death-cut stall. INFERRED.
