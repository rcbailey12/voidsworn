YOUR ONE TASK: a faction asymmetry audit, in two parts.

The owner's benchmark is StarCraft (Zerg, Protoss and Terran are different games wearing one ruleset) and Warhammer 40,000 (Tyranids and Orks do not play like Necrons or Eldar). He wants factions that differ in decisions, not decimals.

PART 1, MEASURE THE SAMENESS. Cite file and line for every claim, quoting the exact line (under 20 words) where the claim is numeric. Mark findings OBSERVED or INFERRED. Maximum 10 findings, most damning first.

PART 2, PROPOSE ASYMMETRY. Give each of the six factions a distinct playstyle identity at the StarCraft bar. For each faction state: its unit-count profile (swarm, standard, elite), its range profile, its shield and survivability model, its movement character, its economy (how it gains and loses resources differently), its win condition and its lose condition, one thing it cannot do that another faction can (a prohibition), and the smallest set of changes that would get it there using mechanics already present in the pack where possible. Anything that needs a new engine system must be flagged ENGINE CHANGE with one sentence on what the system is. End with: the three changes you would make first across all factions, and how you would prove with the game's own simulation data that the factions had actually become different.
