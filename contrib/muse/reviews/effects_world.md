Credit where due: most of the classic leaks are already handled in these files. Weapon effects draw through capped sprite pools (vfx3d fxPool), shield domes, beams, shock rings and torpedo bodies are pooled, scorch decals are capped instances, flash lights are a fixed pool so light count never changes mid-battle, and disposeAllViews (fx3d.js) releases views, trails, hulks, projectiles and the model cache. The findings below are what is left.

## Recommended next step

Fix finding 3: stop forcing computeWorldMatrix(true) in navLights. It is a pure CPU saving on every frame and every device, changes nothing on screen, and the nav sprites are the only consumer of that forced matrix.
