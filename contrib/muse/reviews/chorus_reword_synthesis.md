# Chorus Admiral Reword Synthesis (chair)

Legs: contrib/qwen/reviews/chorus_reword_qwen38max_draft.md (Qwen3.8-Max) and
contrib/deepseek/reviews/chorus_reword_deepseek_draft.md (DeepSeek). Both
legs are PROPOSED draft options only. Reword vs regenerate vs waive remains
undecided for all three admirals. Razormaw is untouched in both legs, per
the formation carve-out.

Chair verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS. The reword options are ready for all
three admirals, and the Mending Choir fix is effectively settled as a text
option. The condition is the per-admiral reword-vs-regenerate call, which
turns on the existing art, not on the wording alone (see the contested
point below).

## What both legs agree on
- All three admirals can be reworded as text-only fixes that keep the
  visuals and keep the grossness. No leg proposes a rule violation in its
  own replacement text.
- Mending Choir is the cleanest text-only fix. Both legs independently
  land on near-identical wording: press the admiral's own growing flesh
  into the breach and let it spread and fuse over the wound (Qwen A,
  DeepSeek 3.1). Two legs converging on the same sentence is the
  strongest signal in either draft.
- The grossness survives in every option. Nothing got sanitized in the
  rewrite; several options (Qwen Mending B's secreted flesh, DeepSeek
  Mending 3.2's tumorous plug) are grosser than the originals.

## Per admiral: chair picks
- Matriarch of Nine Throats. Qwen gives three text-only options; DeepSeek
  gives two text-only plus one VISUAL option. For a text-only pick, the
  chair takes DeepSeek 1.2 (tubes fused at the base into a pulsing mass,
  tipped with weeping openings that slurp and seal), because fusing the
  base attacks the regularity that makes a tube array read as an organ.
  Qwen A (wet membrane tubes, raw throats weeping mucus) is the alternate.
  DeepSeek 1.3 is the regenerate brief if the art needs it: uneven tubes
  of different lengths, one leaking fused base, split openings, and
  explicitly no rack, cabinet, keyboard, or evenly ranked pipe face.
- Voice of the Deep. Qwen keeps the word "bell" in all three options;
  DeepSeek swaps it for "dome" in all three. Same silhouette, different
  word, and the choice is Ryan's, not a detail to slide past. Chair text
  pick is Qwen B (hollow bell of abyssal membrane, rim a ragged lip of
  regrowing flesh shedding strips into the dark water): it keeps the bell
  word, keeps the shape, and is the grossest of the bell options.
  DeepSeek 2.3 is the regenerate brief if the art needs it: remove
  comb-row plates and glassy jelly transparency, replace with opaque wet
  membrane and irregular fleshy ridges, keep the dome silhouette.
- Mending Choir. Adopt the converged press-and-fuse wording (Qwen A /
  DeepSeek 3.1). Qwen C carries a flag both screenings note: "knotting"
  skirts a constructed metaphor, so it is the weakest of the nine Qwen
  options on rule compliance and should not be the pick. DeepSeek 3.3 is
  the regenerate brief if the art needs it: remove suture lines, stitch
  holes, thread, and needle cues; show blunt fleshy bands gripping the
  hull edges and fusing into a scarred mass.

## Contested point, resolved by the chair: hardest to save
Qwen says the Voice is hardest (comb-jelly is the entire visual concept,
not just a word). DeepSeek says the Matriarch is hardest (a regular
vertical tube array reads as a pipe organ even if the text calls it
membrane). Both mechanisms are real, and the disagreement dissolves into
one rule: a reword holds only where the existing art does not embody the
violation, and a regenerate is needed where it does. Neither leg's
hardest-case call is wrong; each names a different way text fails. So the
per-admiral call cannot be made from the drafts alone. It needs the art
check in the recommended next step.

## Conditions
1. Art check per admiral before ruling (see next step). The embodying
   features to look for are named in the legs: Matriarch, a ranked pipe
   face, rack, cabinet, or keyboard; Voice, comb rows or glassy jelly
   transparency; Mending, suture lines, stitch holes, or thread.
2. Voice word choice: "bell" (Qwen options) vs "dome" (DeepSeek options).
   Same silhouette. Ryan picks the word if he adopts a DeepSeek Voice
   option, or takes Qwen B and the question goes away.
3. Qwen Mending C ("knotting") is not recommended as the pick, per its
   own constructed-metaphor flag.

## Recommended next step
Check the three existing portraits (Matriarch v2, Voice of the Deep,
Mending Choir v2) against the embodying-feature lists in condition 1,
then rule per admiral: reword (using the chair picks above), regenerate
(using the DeepSeek VISUAL options as the briefs), or waive. The check is
one look per portrait; the ruling is the only decision this synthesis
cannot make for Ryan.
