## derelict
Title: The silent wreck
Body: A dead warship turns slowly ahead of you, no beacon, no heat. Old Starwright letters crawl along its spine, worn thin by a thousand years of dust.
Options:
- Put crews aboard / Risky. Founders' tech, maybe. Something awake, maybe.
- Cut it apart from a safe distance / +35 credits, no risk.
- Take the core out with lances / A clean cut: founders' tech and salvage, nothing wakes.
- Let it drift / Nothing happens.

## Cross-pool notes
- haven: the only variant that changes the option list. The shipped event's three options are a fight, a 40-credit cost, and a corsair-only recruit, so no always-enabled exit existed; the added fourth option is flagged in place, per the ruling.
- reactor, storm, squall: these shipped events also have no free option, but none of them can start a fight, so the rule 4 exit requirement does not trigger; their option sets are preserved exactly, costs and all. A variant that quietly added a free exit here would change what the node does.
- beacon: the shipped "Seize the cargo" sub-line does not mention the ambush or the Nemesis clock step in its result. The variant keeps the same disclosure level rather than improving it, because spelling out the trap in the variant would make the variant read differently in kind, not just in words; flagged as the pool's least honest sub-line in both versions.
- cache: the shipped "both" option's sub-line ("A refit and the credits") never states the 45 credits its result pays. The variant states the number, matching the sibling option's 45, since leaving it unstated was the only place where a rewrite could make the text more exact without changing the outcome.
- astropath and shrine: both ship with faction-specific alternate titles and bodies (the v blocks). The variants above are written against the base text; the faction alternates would each need the same treatment before a variant set is complete for those two events.
- moon: the bombard option pays captain experience with no fight and no cost, which reads generous next to the purge option's elite battle for salvage; preserved as shipped, since repricing it is a design change, not a variant.
