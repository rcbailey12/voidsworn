## Recommended next step

Fix finding 1: in checkEndRules, make the Intercept escape outcome a loss (or a withdraw) instead of a win once the transports that get through exceed the number the player was required to stop, and do not end the battle while the last transport's fate is still undecided. It is the only finding here that changes who wins a battle, and campaign and chain sims currently record those escapes as cleared nodes.
