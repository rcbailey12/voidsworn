Rey leg of the admirals audit (Oct 27 program item 4, pack pinned to 68b42ce406286eb95ea7df6aaf3f683c1231b51d). Method: the same evidence pack as the three external legs (data.js lines 185 to 275 and 428 to 505, bible 1.3, 1.4 and the faction x.9 subsections), plus direct verification reads of the admiral tables at the pin in the working clone, and the bossmatrix seed-42 results from the item 7 balance pack as the only measured strength evidence available to this audit. Verdict first, findings ranked, OBSERVED unless marked INFERRED.

OVERALL VERDICT: FAIL on identity, PASS on function. All 24 admirals work; the roster's problem is that a visible share of them are not distinct people. Three passive packages repeat verbatim across factions, one Command id carries two different texts, two doctrines (interdiction, ram-and-board) name mechanics the admirals' own kits do not contain, and every carrier admiral hides the same unlisted squadron-hull bonus. None of this touches balance: the item 7 bossmatrix band (55% to 85% across all 24) holds with the duplicates inside it.

1. [FIX] Three passive packages repeat verbatim across factions. OBSERVED: Thale and Maren share fx {hpPct:.15, shPct:.1} at the same cost 80 with the same iron effect text. The carrier package {sqPct:.25, hangar:1, sqHp:.25} with scramble appears three times: Matriarch (cost 0), Aelith (cost 0), Mott (cost 45). Nekhet's passive {hullRegenPct:.04} at cost 0 repeats on Mending at cost 45. Command effect text is shared verbatim within each cmd id across all users, which reads as archetype design; the passives have no such excuse, because the passive line is the admiral's identity next to their name. Different factions, hulls and starting fleets keep these from being balance defects; as roster identity they are the audit's core defect.

2. [FIX] Vorn's doctrine is interdiction; nothing in his kit interdicts. OBSERVED: doc "Interdiction", fx aim/crit, Command hunt with the plain +30% text. No pinning, no movement effect, no range effect. This cuts against Ryan's standing rule that an admiral should match their special ability, and it sits one word away from the bible 3.8 avoid list (the hull beside him was renamed Cordon for the same reason). Three-leg convergent.

3. [FIX] The hunt Command id carries two different texts. OBSERVED: Malek and Sable include the torpedo reload clause; Vorn and Anuket/Ostrel do not; all four share id 'hunt'. If the implementation keys behaviour off the id, one pair's text misdescribes the effect; if it keys off the text, the id is doing nothing. Either way a Hegemony or Eclipse player who captures a torpedo prize cannot tell from the card whether hunt reloads it. Genuine question for Opus, three-leg convergent.

4. [FIX] Grax's doctrine says ram and board; his own kit contains neither. OBSERVED: doc "Ram and board", fx speed/damage, Command surge. Ramming and boarding exist in the Corsair kit around him (the Scrap ram plating prow module, boarding traits in the Nemesis pool), and surge is a plausible closing enabler, so the fleet can do what the doctrine says; the admiral himself contributes nothing to it. Identity defect of the same kind as finding 2, one step softer. Bossmatrix grax reads 75%, mid-band.

5. [FIX] Khepra's Command text is half inert for his own fleet. OBSERVED (chair-added; the external legs circled it without landing it): the iron Command text, shared by all four iron users, reads "All shields refill and your ships take 40% less hull damage this round". Every Eclipse hull has sh:0. For Thale, Maren and Brin the sentence is whole; for Seveth (Khepra) the first clause can never do anything in his own fleet. The shared-text archetype defence fails here because the text makes a faction-specific promise.

6. [FIX] All five carrier admirals hide the same bonus. OBSERVED: ashgrove, reyes, mott, matriarch and aelith all carry sqHp:.25 in fx; no passive text on any of the five mentions squadron hull. A player comparing carrier admirals is comparing incomplete cards, and the omission is uniform enough to look systematic rather than accidental. Three-source convergence (this audit's Argon leg in full, plus the text audit's DeepSeek leg independently).

7. [FIX] Vaelen's aim bonus is global; his text scopes it to lances. OBSERVED: fx aim:10 applies fleet-wide while the passive text describes a lance bonus. The card understates the admiral. Same family as finding 6 (card accuracy), opposite direction. Converges with the text audit's card-accuracy family, which also confirmed the kassar wording issue ("hits 5% lighter" against dmgPct:-.05, a damage effect described as accuracy).

8. [FIX] Chorus Nemesis lines speak as "I". OBSERVED: NEM_FAC.chorus personas resolve into NEM_LINES written in first person singular ("I have watched you. I know where you are thin."), against bible 1.3's rule that the Chorus speaks as "we" in short hungry lines; the patient persona's line also borrows an Eclipse-style immortality claim. Companion defect: behaviour and trait text uses "his" for a g:'n' Chorus. Voice FIX, Argon-originated, quote-backed in its leg.
