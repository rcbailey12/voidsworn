1. Verdict

THE STACK DOES NOT HOLD.

The verdict depends most on HD1, TY4, and MP6. HD1 is the single scaling formula that denominates every battle HUD size, and the pack records no capture judging it at 1440p or 4K. TY4 shows the same scaling idea copied three times with two different clamp ranges, so the port does not agree with itself about how text scales. MP6 records the campaign home screen changing medium from an SVG diagram to a 3D orrery with six constants as its only written design record, so the campaign map's visual language did not survive the move. These three make the stack unreviewable as a system, not merely under-measured item by item.
