## 1. Fixed in this branch

1. BOONS h_board, Boarding Frenzy (data.js). Text claimed "and a failed boarding always sabotages a system." No such rule exists anywhere in the code (a repo-wide search finds the word only in that text; the boon's fx is boardPct only). Fix: the clause is deleted. The text now reads "Boarding parties fight as X% more crew." with the rarity scaling unchanged. Found by DeepSeek (CERTAIN), confirmed by the chair.
2. TIPS select card, the Brace sentence (ui3d.js TIPS and godot/scenes/battle/battle.gd TIPS, same text in both). The card taught that Brace means the ship stays put and cannot move. The shipped rule (ORDERS brace) is that the ship can move first, cannot brace after it has fired, and bracing ends its turn. Fix: the sentence now reads "Or Brace: the ship can move first, then digs in: it takes less hull damage and does not take the +10, but bracing ends its turn, so it cannot move or fire again this turn." One wrinkle: the Onboarding 2 check (contrib/opus/tools/b1_tests.js) pins the old tip's closing phrase "cannot move or fire again this turn" by regex. The corrected text keeps that phrase, now true as a consequence of the turn ending rather than as a ban on moving first, so the check passes unedited (39 ok, 0 FAIL) and no test file is touched in this branch. Found by DeepSeek (CERTAIN), confirmed by the chair against the ORDERS text; Argon's charitable-reading objection was considered and not adopted, because the card's contrast ("stays put") teaches the wrong plan at the movement step.
3. Campaign card c_clock, first sentence (ui3d.js TIPS and godot/ui/campaign/campaign_tips.gd CARDS, same text in both). The card claimed "Every jump moves the clock at the top." Jumps never advance the Nemesis clock: nemClockAdd is called on battle wins (campaign3d.js, nemesis aftermath), on loud event choices (campaign_run.js, the shake event), and on reward choices carrying nemClock (campaign_run.js). Fix: the sentence now reads "The clock at the top fills when you win fights and make loud choices." and the last sentence "Side stops pay, but they cost jumps." became "Side stops pay, but they can fill the clock too." Found by DeepSeek (AMBIGUOUS from the pack, CERTAIN after chair verification).

Propagation: data.js re-copied to godot/rules/test/oracle/data.js and godot/campaign/test/oracle/data.js; ui3d.js re-copied to godot/campaign/test/oracle/ui3d.js; a dated note added to both oracle READMEs; node godot/rules/test/node/export_data.js and node godot/campaign/test/node/export_campaign.js re-run: the campaign tables changed (sha256 bb9eeebebbcab7a7; the teaching cards are exported there), while the rules tables came out byte-identical (sha256 8979ec019ec1f878), because the one data.js fix is a function-valued text the rules export drops as code. The h_board text is a function in data.js and is dropped from the exported tables as code, so no Godot-side copy of that text exists to edit; the sabotage string appears nowhere under godot/.
