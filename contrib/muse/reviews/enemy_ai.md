## Recommended next step

Fix finding 1: gate the in-range term of evalThreatVet (and evalThreat) on the same los() value the after-move branch already computes. It is a few lines, it changes no numbers anywhere else, and it makes cover work for the enemy's positioning the way it already works for its guns, which is the single most visible dumbness in play.
