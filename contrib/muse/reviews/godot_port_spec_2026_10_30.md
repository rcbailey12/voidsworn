Chair note: This spec was commissioned by Ryan on Oct 30, 2026 as the engineering reference for the Godot port: a behavior specification derived from the real code, so the port can be built and checked against it instead of re-reading the whole codebase per question. It is not the earlier "engineering bible" brief (whose premises, Unity, save version 12, eleven pre-existing sections, did not match this project). Everything here is derived fresh from the shipped browser code and the in-progress Godot tree.

## Conventions

- Every factual claim carries a file:line citation to the browser code unless it is explicitly about the Godot tree.
- INFERRED marks a claim reasoned from code structure rather than read directly. OBSERVED, where it appears inside hazard items, marks a claim verified by direct read or execution during this pass (md5 sums, table diffs, validator runs).
- Numbers are the shipped numbers at the pin, including the defects. Where the shipped behavior contradicts a ruling or a comment in its own code, the spec states the shipped behavior first and flags the contradiction.
- A machine-readable export of every data.js table accompanies this spec (contrib/muse/tools/port_export/), generated by executing data.js in Node and validated for counts, id uniqueness, and cross-reference resolution. Its validation report is summarized in the appendix and filed alongside the export in full.

## How to use it

- Port a system by its section: the section states the rule, the order of operations, and the numbers, with the citation for when the port needs the exact source.
- Settle parity disputes against the pin code first, this spec second, and any synthesis document third. The hazard register (last section) documents why: the filed syntheses lag the tree in both directions, listing fixed defects as open and missing live divergences.
- The export is the reconciliation target for the Godot tree's two generated data tables, which currently disagree with each other (hazard H2).

## Scope limits

- Presentation is specified only where it carries a rule (settings semantics, tip gating, zone colors as data). Babylon rendering, Godot fx, audio mixing internals, and the story content are out of scope; sfx.js is covered only through the settings and the one verified mix defect noted in the hazards source material.
- No test was executed against the Godot build for this spec. Godot-side claims are reads and diffs, not runs.
# 10. Battle

Scope: this section specifies battle behavior as implemented in battle.js, with inputs from core.js (derive, owner effects are read in battle.js, seeded RNG), data.js (W, HULLS, ABILITIES, MOVE_RULES, FAC_GATES, WING_STANCES), ui3d.js only where a battle rule lives there, and sim3d.js only to state where the bot driver differs from live play (battle.js:1, core.js:2 to core.js:10, data.js:2, data.js:61, data.js:105, data.js:132, ui3d.js:101, sim3d.js:1).

Notation: a citation after a sentence covers every claim in that sentence. INFERRED marks a conclusion drawn from two or more cited rules, not a line read directly.

## Battle setup and deployment

- The canvas is 30 columns by 22 rows (BW 30, BH 22), and CX is 2, the shift that centers the old 26 wide board (battle.js:2 to battle.js:3).
- Blocking terrain kinds are rock, hulk, planet, and station; nebula gives 15 points harder to hit, debris gives 10 points harder to hit, and storm removes 10 shield from every facing each turn and makes aim 10 points worse (battle.js:6, battle.js:8 to battle.js:14).
- Board shapes are open, corridor, lanes, ring, dense, compact, deadstar, rift, shoals, hourglass, archipelago, and colossus (battle.js:18).
- Arenas are strip (margins top 3, bottom 3, left 1, right 1), square (top 0, bottom 0, left 4, right 4), and round (all margins 0, R 11) (battle.js:263).
- Arena choice: a named cfg.arena wins; otherwise, only for rout, hold, or flagship, not for a boss, not for compact shape, and not for ambush deployment, the weighted ENC_ARENA table is used; all other battles use strip (battle.js:56, battle.js:303 to battle.js:305).
- ENC_ARENA weights are rout: strip 0.3, square 0.35, round 0.35; hold: round 0.6, square 0.4; flagship: square 0.45, round 0.35, strip 0.2 (battle.js:56).
- On a square arena the approach is diagonal with probability 0.6, else cross; on a round arena it is headon with probability 0.4, else cross or pincer with equal probability; cfg.approach, when present, replaces the approach kind but not the rolled angle (battle.js:308 to battle.js:309).
- If the arena is not strip and the shape is corridor, lanes, hourglass, rift, or compact, the shape is re rolled from open, dense, deadstar, shoals, archipelago, colossus, and ring (battle.js:264, battle.js:307).
- startBattle reseeds first: the seed is cfg.seed taken as an unsigned 32 bit integer when present, else a uniform integer in 0 to 4294967295, and rndSeed(seed) is called before any other battle roll (battle.js:295 to battle.js:296, core.js:5 to core.js:6).
- The objective is cfg.obj if it is a key of ENCOUNTERS, else rout; defend forces map station; an open battle for a non boss fleet of at most 2 living campaign ships in rout, hunt, or ambush becomes compact unless NOCOMPACT is set (battle.js:287, battle.js:297 to battle.js:299).
- If the map is not belt, station, planet, storm, or graveyard, and the enemy faction has a FAC_MAP entry, there is a 0.55 chance the map is picked from that entry; lanes or ring on a belt map are re rolled to station, planet, storm, or graveyard (battle.js:57, battle.js:300 to battle.js:301).
- Terrain generation is retried up to 6 times until terrainConnected(T) is true; if all 6 fail, the shape is forced to open and one more terrain is generated with no connectivity check (battle.js:311 to battle.js:312).
- terrainConnected requires a non blocking path from column c0 plus 2 to column c1 minus 2, and a top to bottom path through the central band of columns 10 plus CX to 16 plus CX (battle.js:71 to battle.js:77).
- For a boss battle, after terrain generation each rock is deleted with probability 0.45 (battle.js:313).
- The battle object B starts at round 1, side player, resolve 1 for each side, over false, reviveUsed false, and sector copied from RUN.sector (battle.js:314 to battle.js:315).
- Player units are created, in RUN.fleet order, for every campaign ship with hp above 0; ships at hp 0 or below do not enter the battle (battle.js:317).
- The enemy list is cfg.enemies when supplied, else genEnemyFleet(cfg.fac, sector, cfg.col, cfg.kind, cfg.arch, cfg.mod) (battle.js:318).
- genEnemyFleet budget base is 4.2, 9.5, or 14 for sector 1, 2, or 3, plus col times 0.9, 1, or 1.1 by sector, times RAMP.grow for the sector (1.2, 1.24, 1.26), and times 1.05 for kind elite (battle.js:139 to battle.js:141, battle.js:136).
- For a boss, the faction boss hull is pushed first and the remaining budget is multiplied by RAMP.boss for the sector (0.3, 0.44, 0.53) (battle.js:141 to battle.js:142, battle.js:136).
- Unless NO_CAP is defined, the budget is scaled by fleet power: budget times clamp(pow(max(0.5, fleetPower()) divided by RAMP.refP[sector], RAMP.pw), RAMP.pwMin 0.75, RAMP.pwMax 1.3), with RAMP.pw 0.6 and refP 7, 12, 20 by sector (battle.js:143, battle.js:136, battle.js:123 to battle.js:125).
- Standard AI level multiplies the budget by RAMP.std 0.9; the h_hunted heat multiplies it by 1.15; mod bounty multiplies it by 1.1 (battle.js:144 to battle.js:146).
- Fleet generation stops when budget is below 1, when the list reaches max(4, RAMP.ceil[sector] minus min(7, living player ships), defaulting to 4 when there is no fleet), or after 80 iterations; RAMP.ceil is 10, 14, 20 by sector (battle.js:155 to battle.js:162, battle.js:136).
- Archetype handling: effArch turns carrier into heavy when the faction roster has no launch ability hull; swarm restricts the roster to hulls within 1 cp of the minimum, excluding carriers; heavy pre picks the largest affordable hull of cp at least 2 and restricts the roster; carrier pre picks the largest affordable carrier and removes other carriers from the roster (battle.js:138, battle.js:149 to battle.js:152).
- Enemy scaling in startBattle: sector scale sc is 1 plus 0.12 times (sector minus 1), and sector damage dm is 1 plus 0.08 times (sector minus 1) (battle.js:319).
- A boss hull is scaled by sc times BOSS_SCALE[sector] (0.5, 0.55, 0.7) times bossFit times BOSS_FAC[faction] times 1.2 if h_flag heat is on, and its damage by dm times BOSS_DMG[sector] (0.85, 0.88, 0.95); bossFit is clamp(playerStrength() divided by 6, 9, or 12 by sector, 0.55, 1) (battle.js:320, battle.js:323, battle.js:128 to battle.js:129).
- Non boss generated enemies use thin instead of sc: thin is 1 for a supplied cfg.enemies list, else RAMP.thin[sector] (0.73, 0.675, 0.62) times RAMP.escort 0.78 for a boss battle; their damage multiplier is dm times thin (battle.js:322 to battle.js:323, battle.js:136).
- Elite fights mark the larger half by cp, rounded up, as elite; in a boss fight each non boss enemy also has an independent 0.3 chance to be elite; h_elite makes the first non boss index elite; mod veteran makes the single highest cp index elite (battle.js:321, battle.js:323).
- An elite unit gets maxHp times 1.1 in makeUnit, aim 6, and crit 5 (battle.js:102 to battle.js:103).
- makeUnit for a campaign ship uses derive(ship) for maxHp, shields, regen, armor, speed, turns, evasion, aim, crit, damage, fx, hangar, hull regen, and weapons (battle.js:97 to battle.js:101, core.js:204 to core.js:216).
- derive computes maxHp as hull hp times mark multiplier (1 plus 0.2 per mark above 1) times (1 plus fx.hpPct plus 0.06 per class track level), shield max likewise with fx.shPct, armor capped at 0.6, speed and turns and evasion as base plus fx, damage as 1 plus 0.15 per mark above 1 plus fx.dmgPct plus 0.06 per class track level (core.js:208 to core.js:215).
- makeUnit for a generated unit scales maxHp, shield max, and regen by the scale argument, uses hull armor, speed, turns, and evasion unscaled, aim 0 and crit 0 unless elite, and hull regen as the hull regen field times scale (battle.js:100 to battle.js:104).
- A unit starts facing direction 0 for the player and direction 3 for the enemy, with all four shield facings at shMax, weapons with fired false, cdLeft 0, and off 0, and ability cooldown abCd 0 (battle.js:106 to battle.js:114).
- A non squadron unit gets three sections: prow max round(maxHp times 0.3), mid max round(maxHp times 0.45), eng max round(maxHp times 0.25), each starting at that max times the unit current hull fraction, rounded; it gets crewMax round((12 plus size times 40) times CREW_MULT[faction] times 1.8 if boss), and speed plus 1 (battle.js:117 to battle.js:119).
- CREW_MULT is voidsworn 1.2, hegemony 1, corsair 1.25, chorus 1.1, ithar 0.8, deathless 1 (battle.js:82).
- A campaign ship unit keeps hp equal to min(ship.hp, derived maxHp), and a campaign ship unit created at hp 0 or below is raised to hp 1, although startBattle never creates one from a ship at hp 0 or below (battle.js:109, battle.js:116, battle.js:317).
- Under h_undying heat, every enemy non squadron non boss unit gains reanimate true (battle.js:324).
- Carrier adaptation: if the player has at least 2 carriers, counting a scramble Command admiral or a hive aspect as one extra carrier when at least 1 real carrier exists, and the enemy list was generated (or ADAPT is 1) and NOADAPT is not 1, enemy non boss non squadron ships without a flak kind weapon receive one added point defense weapon (flak, or spores for chorus), turret arc; the count is all candidates at 3 or more carriers, else half the candidates rounded up, chosen by descending cp; if the enemy faction has a squadron type, B.adaptWings is set to 2 at 3 or more carriers, else 1 (battle.js:326 to battle.js:330).
- Intercept battles add 3 transports if the enemy list has at least 3 entries, else 2 transports; a transport uses the cheapest non boss non carrier roster hull, scale sc times 1.3, no weapons, no ability, speed min(speed, 3), and maxHp and hp times 1.5 rounded (battle.js:331 to battle.js:332).
- Deployment (deploy) considers only non squadron units, sorted by descending size, and places them on free hexes (not blocking terrain, not occupied) inside the zone; if the zone has fewer free hexes than units plus 1, it falls back to the whole own half of the board (battle.js:245 to battle.js:256).
- Deployment scoring for each candidate hex is min(distance to the nearest already placed unit, 2) times 100, minus distance to the zone centroid times 4, plus rnd() in 0 to 1; the highest score wins (battle.js:257 to battle.js:260).
- Line deployment zones: player columns L0 plus 4 to L0 plus 6 with row within 5 of the middle row, facing 0; enemy columns R0 minus 7 to R0 minus 5 with row within 5 of the middle row, facing 3, where L0 is ARENA.mL and R0 is BW minus ARENA.mR (battle.js:245 to battle.js:250).
- Flank deployment zones: player columns L0 plus 3 to L0 plus 9 and rows at least T1 minus 7, facing 5; enemy columns R0 minus 10 to R0 minus 4 and rows at most T0 plus 6, facing 2 (battle.js:248).
- Ambush deployment: the player is in columns L0 plus 5 plus CX to L0 plus 9 plus CX within 3 rows of the middle, facing 0; the enemy zone is columns R0 minus 14 minus CX to R0 minus 6 and rows at most T0 plus 2 or at least T1 minus 3, with facing computed toward board center (battle.js:249).
- deployMode is ambush for the ambush encounter, line for defend, hunt, intercept, and break, and otherwise cfg.dep or line (battle.js:314).
- Break deployment overrides the zones: player columns 8 plus CX to 10 plus CX within 4 rows of the middle; the main enemy body in columns 16 plus CX to 20 plus CX within 6 rows; every third enemy ship by descending size deploys in columns c0 to c0 plus 3 within 5 rows (battle.js:333 to battle.js:335).
- Hunt deployment: player columns 5 plus CX to 8 plus CX within 5 rows; the single highest cp, then highest maxHp, enemy ship in columns 12 plus CX to 14 plus CX within 3 rows; the rest use normal deployment (battle.js:336 to battle.js:337).
- On square and round arenas approachDeploy overrides normal deployment: deployment points are placed at radius ext times 0.68 (0.74 for diagonal) from the arena centroid in the approach direction, zones grow from radius 2.4 hex widths in steps of 0.6 until they hold at least n plus 2 free hexes; a pincer splits the enemy into alternating halves at plus and minus 0.95 radians; a cross deploys the enemy at a right angle; after deployment every non squadron unit faces the centroid of the opposing side (battle.js:266 to battle.js:278).
- Intercept transports deploy last, in rows at most r0 plus 1 and columns 13 plus CX to 18 plus CX (battle.js:342).
- For a boss battle in line mode, the boss deploys in columns BW minus ARENA.mR minus 10 to BW minus ARENA.mR minus 8 within 5 rows of the middle, and its escorts use normal deployment (battle.js:340).
- clearSpawns runs after deployment unless NOCLEAR is 1: for each side, rock and hulk are deleted within radius 2 of each player ship (radius 1 for enemy ships), and along the hex line toward the enemy centroid for the first 6 steps for the player (4 for the enemy), with radius 1 for the first 3 player steps and radius 0 otherwise; the count removed is stored in B.spawnCleared (battle.js:289 to battle.js:293).
- deployWings runs after clearSpawns unless NOPRELAUNCH is 1 (battle.js:343 to battle.js:344).
- After deployment, B.cp0 records sideCP for both sides, B.usage is zeroed, setupObjective(cfg.obj) runs, setupFactionRules() runs, then applyNemesis and applyVerb run when cfg supplies them, and finally startSideTurn(player) runs (battle.js:345 to battle.js:351).
- startBattle ends by resetting every living enemy unit intercepts to 2 if it has any flak kind weapon, else 0; this overwrites the value set by the player turn start because startSideTurn(player) does not touch enemy units (battle.js:351 to battle.js:352, battle.js:365 to battle.js:385).
- A mid battle event is attached by the caller, not by startBattle; rollBattleEvent returns null for a boss, returns null with probability 0.55 when not forced, and otherwise returns an event with warnR 2 and r equal to ri(3, 4) (battle.js:26, campaign3d.js:59 to campaign3d.js:60).
- The three battle events are reinf_enemy, reinf_ally, and flare (battle.js:22 to battle.js:25).
- eventTick runs at player turn start when round is above 1: the warning is logged once at warnR; at round r the event fires once; a reinforcement spawns a cheapHull unit for its side at edgeHex, with enemy scale 1 plus 0.12 times (sector minus 1) and damage 1 plus 0.08 times (sector minus 1), facing 0 for the player and 3 for the enemy, marked reinf, and its cp is added to B.cp0 for its side; a friendly reinforcement is named with the prefix Garrison and returns after the battle (INFERRED from the event text and the absence of a ship record on the unit) (battle.js:27 to battle.js:38, battle.js:366).
- A flare sets, in startSideTurn, every unit of the side whose turn is starting to shields 0,0,0,0, but only when the battle round equals the event round and the event is done (battle.js:391).

## Activation flow and round structure

- There are two turn structures. Classic: one side acts with all of its ships, then the other side acts. Alternating: B.alt is true, one player ship activates, then one or more enemy ships activate, until no ship on either side is left unactivated, then a new round starts (ui3d.js:6 to ui3d.js:9, ui3d.js:550, ui3d.js:567 to ui3d.js:592, ui3d.js:595 to ui3d.js:601).
- SETUP.alt defaults to true, and prepBattle copies SETUP.alt into B.alt, sets activated false on every unit, and, in alternating mode only, calls startSideTurn(enemy) once at battle setup to prime enemy per turn state (ui3d.js:11, ui3d.js:49 to ui3d.js:51).
- INFERRED: because startBattle already called startSideTurn(player) and prepBattle then calls startSideTurn(enemy), an alternating battle begins with both sides having received one turn start tick before the first activation (battle.js:351, ui3d.js:51).
- In alternating mode a player unit may be used only if it is not activated and either no unit is active or it is the active unit; squadrons are exempt from the active unit restriction (ui3d.js:8).
- endActivation marks the active unit activated, or, when the player manually ends with a selected unactivated non squadron ship and no active unit, marks that ship activated and logs that it holds position; then aiStep runs (ui3d.js:567 to ui3d.js:575).
- aiStep: if any enemy ships are pending, all unactivated enemy squadrons act, plus either the single best enemy ship chosen by aiPickActivation (when player ships are still pending) or all pending enemy ships (when none are); then control returns to the player; if both sides have no pending ships, nextRound runs; if only the player has none, aiStep recurses (ui3d.js:576 to ui3d.js:592).
- nextRound increments B.round, clears activated on every unit, clears activeUnit, calls roundStartBoth, then runs the player swarm phase and then the enemy swarm phase, each under swarmGuard (ui3d.js:595 to ui3d.js:601).
- In classic mode endTurn requires side player, not busy, and not over; it sets side enemy, awaits turnStart(enemy), runs aiTurn under a 90 second stall guard, then increments B.round, sets side player, awaits turnStart(player), and runs the player swarm phase (ui3d.js:555 to ui3d.js:565).
- turnStart(side) is the shared entry: it calls startSideTurn(side), awaits the tick death promise if any, then, if the battle is not over, resolves that side torpedoes (battle.js:853).
- roundStartBoth is the alternating round entry: it calls startSideTurn(player) and then startSideTurn(enemy) unless the battle ended, awaits both death promises, then resolves player torpedoes and then enemy torpedoes, stopping early if B changed or the battle is over (battle.js:855).
- startSideTurn sequencing, in order: (1) if side is player and round is above 1, eventTick then objectiveTick with a shared deaths list; (2) if side is enemy, aiRefreshFocus in an empty try/catch, then bossCmdTick in an empty try/catch; (3) escortsBreak and nemBreak for both sides; (4) if side is enemy, nemFleeCheck, and, when round is at least 2 and a Nemesis is present, nemTrip(slow); (5) if side is enemy, aiStance for every living enemy non squadron unit; (6) gainResolve(side, 1); (7) legionTurn(side) collects rising Deathless ships; (8) the per unit reset and tick loop; (9) the flare shield zeroing; (10) weapons of risen ships are marked fired; (11) if any tick killed a ship, checkEnd runs; the function returns a promise that settles the queued tick deaths, or null when there were none (battle.js:365 to battle.js:395).
- The empty try/catch at step (2) is exact: try aiRefreshFocus, catch with an empty block, then try bossCmdTick, catch with an empty block, both on one line, so an exception in either is silently discarded and the other still runs (battle.js:368).
- Per unit reset in startSideTurn, for each unit of that side that is not dead: braced, coverBy, postFire, lockOn, focus, fullAhead, and noTurn are cleared; postMp is set to 0; pivotUsed is cleared; evBuff is set to 0; order is cleared; ramBoost is set to 0 (battle.js:375).
- Movement and turns reset: mp becomes max(1, speed minus st.engine), and turnsLeft becomes turns (battle.js:376).
- Weapon reset: fired becomes false for every weapon, cdLeft decreases by 1 if above 0, and off decreases by 1 if above 0; abCd decreases by 1 if above 0 (battle.js:377 to battle.js:378).
- Shield regen: if st.shieldOff is above 0 it decreases by 1 and no regen happens; otherwise, if shMax is above 0, each facing not locked by regenLock gains regen times B.condRegen, capped at shMax; then ithChargeTurn runs and regenLock is cleared (battle.js:379 to battle.js:380).
- Hull regen: if hullRegen is set, hp is below maxHp, and the unit is not marked pyre, hp gains hullRegen capped at maxHp (battle.js:381).
- Acid tick: if st.acid is above 0 and the unit is not a squadron, st.acid decreases by 1 and hp loses 4, ignoring shields and armor; at hp 0 or below the unit dies with killCause melt, queued into the deaths list (battle.js:382).
- Fire tick: if st.fire is above 0 and the unit is not dead, damage is st.fireDmg or 6, st.fire decreases by 1, hp loses that damage; at hp 0 or below the unit dies with killCause dark, queued; when st.fire reaches 0, st.fireDmg is reset to 0 (battle.js:383).
- hexMoved is reset to 0 for every unit of the side at its turn start, after the acid and fire ticks and before the storm terrain effect (battle.js:384).
- Storm regen effect: if fx.stormRegen is set, shMax is above 0, and shields are not off, every facing gains round(shMax times fx.stormRegen), capped at shMax (battle.js:384).
- intercepts reset: 2 if the unit has any flak kind weapon, else 0 (battle.js:385).
- boarded, rammed, emerg, and repel are cleared at turn start (battle.js:386).
- Storm terrain: a non squadron unit standing in storm terrain with shMax above 0 loses 10 from every shield facing, floored at 0 (battle.js:387).
- A unit with crewMax set and crew at 0 or below gets mp 0, turnsLeft 0, and every weapon marked fired (battle.js:388).
- The gravity well tick (wellTick) runs last in the loop for each unit (battle.js:389).
- Resolve: gainResolve adds to B.resolve for the side, capped at 5; resolve starts at 1 per side; other gains are 1 when a section is destroyed by an attacker, 1 when a kill is made by a non squadron killer, and 2 for a capture (battle.js:314, battle.js:360, battle.js:738, battle.js:946, battle.js:912).
- No rule in battle.js spends resolve. INFERRED: resolve is a meter that only accumulates in this file (battle.js:360).
- A ship activation in live play is not time limited by battle.js; long actions are wrapped by stallGuard in ui3d.js and sim wrappers, which increments B.epoch on a stall so the abandoned action can no longer commit (battle.js:1057 to battle.js:1065, ui3d.js:584, ui3d.js:595).

## Movement, facing, and arcs

- Hexes use axial coordinates with DIRS in facing order 0 to 5, and hdist is the axial distance (abs(dq) plus abs(dr) plus abs(dq plus dr)) divided by 2 (core.js:22 to core.js:24).
- Facing is an integer 0 to 5; the relative angle of a target is its bearing minus facing times 60 degrees, normalized to minus 180 to 180 (core.js:26 to core.js:27).
- Arcs: T is always in arc; F requires absolute relative angle at most 60.5; R requires at least 119.5; S requires relative angle from 29.5 to 150.5; P requires relative angle from minus 150.5 to minus 29.5 (core.js:28 to core.js:35).
- Shield quadrant from the target point of view: absolute relative angle below 50 is quadrant 0 (prow), above 130 is quadrant 2 (rear), otherwise quadrant 1 (starboard) for a positive angle and quadrant 3 (port) for a negative angle; QUAD_NAME is prow, starboard, rear, port (core.js:37 to core.js:38).
- Movement points available for a reach computation are postFireMp(u): normally u.mp, but after the unit has fired, min(u.mp, u.postMp) (battle.js:466).
- computeReach is a Dijkstra search over states (q, r, facing, turns used) starting at the unit position with cost 0; states at cost at least the movement budget are not expanded; only states that canEnd (on board, not blocking terrain, and empty or the unit itself) are returned as destinations (battle.js:409 to battle.js:445).
- A squadron moves to any of the 6 neighboring hexes at cost 1 per hex, keeping its facing value in the state (which is set to the loop direction), subject to passable: on board, not blocking terrain, and not occupied by an enemy when the mover is not a squadron; a squadron may pass through an enemy occupied hex (battle.js:401 to battle.js:402, battle.js:417 to battle.js:418).
- A non squadron ship has four step options from each state: forward (direction equal to facing) cost 1; forward left and forward right slips (facing plus 1 and facing minus 1 modulo 6) at cost MOVE slip; and reverse (facing plus 3) at cost MOVE rev; the ship facing does not change on any step (battle.js:419 to battle.js:428).
- MOVE_BASE is slip 2, rev 2, over 1, evadeAt 3, evade 5, still 10, rake 0.1 (battle.js:406).
- MOVE_RULES overrides by faction: voidsworn rake 0.4; hegemony rev 1; corsair slip 1; chorus pivot 1; ithar evadeAt 2 and evade 18; deathless over 0; moveRules merges MOVE_BASE with the faction table for non squadron units only, so squadrons always use MOVE_BASE (data.js:105 to data.js:111, battle.js:407).
- A thruster step (slip or reverse) taken as the first step of a move (state cost 0) costs min(its normal cost, the whole movement budget), so a ship whose budget is below the step cost can still make exactly that one hop (battle.js:421 to battle.js:423).
- Crossing: if the destination terrain blocks and is rock or hulk, or the destination is occupied by an enemy, the step is an over step and costs an additional MOVE over; station and planet terrain can never be crossed; a friendly occupied hex is not an over step and is charged the normal step cost, but canEnd still forbids ending there (battle.js:423 to battle.js:428, battle.js:408).
- After a unit has fired (postFire), over steps are forbidden entirely (battle.js:410, battle.js:425).
- Rotation in computeReach: if the unit is not a squadron, noTurn is false, and turns used are below turnsLeft, it may rotate by plus or minus 1 facing at the same hex; the rotation cost is 0 for the first rotation when the faction has pivot and pivotUsed is false, else 1, and the rotation is allowed when cost plus rotation cost is at most the movement budget, or the rotation is free (battle.js:430 to battle.js:433).
- Destination selection: for each destination hex the cheapest state is kept, with fewer turns used breaking a cost tie (battle.js:443 to battle.js:445).
- moveUnit commits the whole path at once on commit: position becomes the last non rotation step, facing becomes the facing of the last path element, mp decreases by the state cost, postMp decreases by the state cost if postFire, turnsLeft decreases by turns used, pivotUsed is set if any turn was used by a pivot faction, moved is set true, and hexMoved increases by the number of non rotation path steps (battle.js:447 to battle.js:452).
- objAfterMove runs after a committed move, and also after a blink and after a ram run up is committed by its own code path (battle.js:457 to battle.js:462, battle.js:975 to battle.js:977).
- rotateUnit is a separate in place rotation: it requires canRotate (alive, not squadron, not noTurn, turnsLeft above 0, mp above 0 or a free pivot available, not braced); a free pivot consumes pivotUsed and no mp, otherwise it costs 1 mp and 1 turnsLeft; facing commits only after the animation and the live check (battle.js:468 to battle.js:469).
- Firing after moving: the first non intercept shot of an activation by a non squadron unit sets postFire true and sets postMp to floor(mp divided by 2) if the unit can fade, else 0; canFade is faction ithar or fx.fade set (battle.js:761, battle.js:465).
- INFERRED: for a non ithar unit without fx.fade, firing any weapon ends all further movement except rotation, because postFireMp becomes min(mp, 0) equals 0 while canRotate does not consult postFire (battle.js:465 to battle.js:468).
- hexMoved has two uses. Use one, target motion: motionHit returns plus MOVE still (default 10) when the target hexMoved is 0, minus MOVE evade (default 5) when hexMoved is at least MOVE evadeAt (default 3), and 0 otherwise; it returns 0 for squadrons, for braced targets, and when hexMoved is null (battle.js:632, battle.js:406).
- Use two, attacker momentum: rakeBonus, momPct, momCrit, and stillPct all read the attacker hexMoved (see the firing section); stateView adds the path length of a candidate move to hexMoved so AI and preview evaluations price the destination as if the move were already made (battle.js:633, battle.js:675, battle.js:708, battle.js:1223).
- hexMoved accumulates over every moveUnit call in the turn and is only reset at that unit own side turn start, so in alternating play it measures movement since the start of that side last turn start, not since the unit last activation (INFERRED from the reset site and the absence of any other reset) (battle.js:384, battle.js:451).
- The gravity well (deadstar shape only, at TERRAIN_META.deadstar) has WELL_RIM 3, WELL_PULL 5, WELL_ASSIST 6, WELL_DMG 7 (battle.js:280, battle.js:314).
- wellTick, per unit at its turn start: within WELL_ASSIST the unit gains 1 mp and gravAssist true; within WELL_RIM it instead takes 7 hull damage, ignoring shields and armor, and dies with killCause tidal at hp 0 or below; otherwise within WELL_PULL it is dragged one hex to the first DIRS neighbor that is exactly one hex closer to the well, on board, not blocking, and unoccupied, if any (battle.js:281 to battle.js:285).
- Terrain movement effects: only blocking kinds stop movement or a move ending; nebula, debris, and storm never block (battle.js:6, battle.js:401 to battle.js:408).
- Line of sight: los walks hexLine between the two hexes and is blocked if any intermediate hex is blocking terrain or is in SOLID (a cut hex filled by a set piece); the endpoints never block (battle.js:626 to battle.js:627, core.js:43 to core.js:44).

## Firing and hit resolution

- weaponReady is fired false, cdLeft 0, and off 0 (battle.js:628).
- inReach requires distance at least 1 and at most weapon range r, the target in the weapon arc, and line of sight (battle.js:629).
- canFire is false when the attacker is dead, the target is dead, both are on the same side, the attacker is braced, or the weapon kind is flak; it is also false when the weapon is not ready; otherwise it is inReach (battle.js:630).
- Flak rule, stated exactly: a flak kind weapon can never be fired by canFire, for any ship, any target, any range; flak only ever fires through fireWeapon with opts.intercept true, which skips the canFire check entirely (battle.js:630, battle.js:754 to battle.js:758).
- The only intercept call site in battle.js is the squadron gauntlet inside fireWeapon: when a squadron attacks a non squadron target, every living defender of the target side that is not a squadron, has intercepts above 0, and is not braced, fires its first flak weapon with off equal to 0 whose range covers the attacking squadron, once, and loses 1 intercept (battle.js:764 to battle.js:771).
- A second flak mount adds nothing in that gauntlet: the defender fires once per attacking volley, using findIndex, the first mount with off equal to 0, and the intercept budget is per ship (2 per turn with any flak mount), not per mount (battle.js:766 to battle.js:770, battle.js:385).
- INFERRED exception: a second flak mount is a spare only in the narrow case where the first mount is offline (off above 0) in the gauntlet search, or off is exactly above 0 in rocketDefense, because both searches scan the weapon list for the first usable flak weapon; two mounts never produce two intercept volleys from one ship against one squadron attack (battle.js:767, battle.js:789).
- rocketDefense is the second point defense rule: against a weapon with the rockets trait (corsair torp and htorp after the rockets trait is applied) firing at a non squadron target, each living non squadron defender of the target side with intercepts above 0, not braced, holding a flak weapon with off not above 0 whose range covers the target hex, rolls rnd() below 0.55 for each currently hitting shot in order, turning that shot into a miss and spending 1 intercept per shot stopped; it runs at fire time for direct fire and at impact for a travelling torpedo (battle.js:789 to battle.js:791, data.js:49 to data.js:52, battle.js:783, battle.js:844).
- A screenshot of weapon base stats is in data.js W: for example torp has shots 1, dmg 42, r 6, opt 6, acc 64, fall 0, pierce 0.3, crit 15, cd 2, evMult 2; flak has shots 3, dmg 3, r 2, opt 2, acc 80, fall 10, vsSq 3, vsCap 0.6; lance has shots 1, dmg 20, r 7, opt 6, acc 82, fall 6, pierce 0.6, crit 10 (data.js:2 to data.js:27).
- mkWeapon copies W, applies fx modifiers (battery shots, flak shots and range and opt, torp cooldown floor 1, prow range for F arc, lance range), and nameWeapons renames by faction and applies the faction weapon trait unless NOTRAITS is set (core.js:197 to core.js:203, data.js:40 to data.js:58).
- fireWeapon order of operations: (1) reject if the unit is not in this battle; (2) reject by canFire unless intercept; (3) snapshot fired, cdLeft, sureUsed, postFire, postMp for a stall rollback; (4) unless intercept, mark the weapon fired, set cdLeft to weapon cd if any, and increment sureUsed for a player non squadron attacker; (5) record usage telemetry; (6) set postFire as described in the movement section; (7) if the weapon travels as a torpedo, delegate to launchTorp and stop; (8) run the squadron gauntlet; (9) compute the shield quadrant, hit chance, and one hit roll per shot; (10) run rocketDefense; (11) animate; (12) if the action is no longer live, roll back the snapshot (non intercept only) and return false; (13) mark Hegemony sync; (14) call landVolley (battle.js:754 to battle.js:786).
- Each shot is an independent roll: shots[i] is rnd() times 100 below hitChance (battle.js:779 to battle.js:780).
- hitChance formula, in order: start with weapon acc plus attacker aim; add 15 if the attacker has lockOn; subtract 15 if the attacker has fullAhead; subtract over times weapon fall, where over is max(0, distance minus weapon opt minus fx.longAim); subtract min(45, target ev plus target evBuff) times weapon evMult (default 1); subtract 15 if the target is a squadron and the weapon kind is not flak and not sq; subtract 15 if the target stands in nebula; subtract 10 if the target stands in debris; subtract 10 if the attacker stands in storm; subtract 10 if attacker crew is below 50 percent of crewMax, and another 10 if below 25 percent; subtract target fx.burnEv if the target hexMoved is at least 3; add motionHit(target); add SYNC_AIM 6 if syncOn; subtract 10 if the attacker is a Nemesis unit with the brawl weakness and distance is at most 2; add 10 if the target is a squadron in hunt stance; subtract target fx.lineEv if a living same side non squadron ally is adjacent to the target; add 10 if the target is this side marked target for this round (battle.js:634 to battle.js:653).
- Two sure hit overrides replace the formula result: a lance fired by a unit with fx.lanceSure, or any first attack of the battle by a player non squadron unit when runFx().firstSure is set and sureUsed is 0, returns 95, or 75 when the target side has an active surge Command; otherwise the result is clamp(round(c), 5, 95), and an active surge Command on the target side then returns max(5, that value minus 20) (battle.js:650 to battle.js:653).
- INFERRED: the sure hit test reads sureUsed from before the current shot, because fireWeapon restores the snapshot value before computing hitChance and restores the incremented value afterwards (battle.js:777).
- dmgMult order: start with attacker dmg; add fx.broadPct for P or S arc weapons; add fx.lancePct for lance, fx.torpPct for torp; add sqPct for a squadron attacker; add fx.rearPct when rear; add 0.25 when rear against a Nemesis with the flank weakness; add 0.2 for the Nemesis duel pair; add 0.12 for a Nemesis zeal trait attacker at distance 1; for a squadron attacker against a non squadron target add 0.2 in hunt stance against a target below half hull and 0.15 if the parent has the killers wing perk; add fx.huntPct when the target is below half hull; add 0.3 if the attacker has focus; add max(fx.stillPct when the attacker has not moved and the weapon is P arc, S arc, or battery kind, syncDmg when syncOn); add min(4, hexMoved) times fx.momPct; add fx.prowPct for F arc weapons; add rakeBonus; multiply weapon dmg by that total; multiply by vsSq against a squadron target or vsCap against a capital target (defaults 1); multiply by 1 plus 0.15 times bloodied stacks for a bloodied non squadron attacker; multiply by the hunt Command factor if active for the attacker side; by the scramble Command factor for a squadron attacker if active; and by B.volleyMul when a barrage Command volley is running for that side (battle.js:661 to battle.js:683).
- rakeBonus: 0 unless the attacker is not a squadron, the weapon arc is P or S, and attacker hexMoved is at least 2; then it is moveRules(attacker).rake, which is 0.4 for voidsworn and the MOVE_BASE default 0.1 for every other faction, because no other MOVE_RULES entry sets rake (battle.js:633, battle.js:406, data.js:105 to data.js:111).
- landVolley order: a called shot adds 15 crit to the attacker for the duration of the volley and sets the target called section, then each shot in order: stop if the target is dead; a miss increments misses; a hit increments hits, computes per shot damage as dmgMult (or the torpedo per function) times (0.85 plus rnd() times 0.3), calls applyHit, and accumulates shield, hull, and crits; a crit may trigger systemDamage (see the damage section); after the loop the called shot bonuses are removed (battle.js:793 to battle.js:802).
- After the shots: Nemesis weakness trips are checked; shield break effects (chain lightning and arc short) fire when shield damage was dealt and the struck facing is at 0.5 or below and it was above 0.5 before the volley; the log line is written; Chorus bite, lance marking, riposte, and the kill check follow in that order (battle.js:803 to battle.js:822).
- Riposte: if the volley was not a torpedo and not an intercept, scored hits, the target was braced, both are alive, they are on opposite sides, and the target ownerFx has riposte, the attacker takes riposte hull damage directly (no shields, armor, or section damage) and dies through killUnit if that takes it to 0 or below (battle.js:820).
- If target hp is at 0 or below after the volley, killCause is set from killCauseOf(weapon, target) and killUnit(target, attacker or the torpedo by unit) is awaited (battle.js:821 to battle.js:822).
- Torpedoes: a torp kind weapon fired by a non squadron unit does not resolve at fire time unless TORP_INSTANT is 1; torpTravels is the test (battle.js:537).
- launchTorp spends the gun as a normal shot, then stores a salvo object with a sequential id, side, attacker id, a shallow copy of the weapon, target id, aim hex equal to the target hex at launch, from hex, launch round, and the called section if any; the damage multipliers are frozen at launch as base equal to dmgMult(attacker, weapon, target, rear false) times TORP_WARHEAD 1.2, plus rearAdd equal to the rear multiplier minus the base multiplier, times TORP_WARHEAD (battle.js:825 to battle.js:832, battle.js:536).
- TORP_RING is defined at battle.js:536 as [0, 5, 15]: the to hit penalty at impact when the original target is 0, 1, or 2 hexes from the aim hex; TORP_STRAY is 10, and TORP_WARHEAD is 1.2 (battle.js:536).
- resolveTorps(side) at a side turn start removes and resolves, in launch order, every salvo of that side whose launch round is below the current round, stopping if B changed or the battle is over, and then calls checkEnd (battle.js:850 to battle.js:851).
- torpImpact target selection: if the original target is alive, hostile, and within 2 hexes of the aim hex, it is the target, with penalty TORP_RING[distance], except a weapon with trait star uses [0, 0, 10]; else the nearest living non squadron enemy of the salvo side within 0 hexes of the aim hex (within 1 hex if the original target is already dead) is a stray target with penalty TORP_STRAY 10, ties broken by distance then by id order; if there is no target, a spread pattern boon (fx.spread on the ghost) may still splash every non squadron ship of the opposing side within 1 hex of the aim hex for base times fx.spread damage through applyHit, else the salvo runs wide (battle.js:834 to battle.js:841).
- torpImpact hit chance is clamp(hitChance(ghost, weapon, target) minus penalty plus 15 if the target is a Nemesis with the torp weakness, 5, 95), rolled once per shot; rocketDefense and then screenTorps thin the hitting shots; landVolley is called with torp true, with called only when the target is the original target, and with a per function returning the frozen base (plus rearAdd when rear) when the target is the original target (battle.js:842 to battle.js:849).
- The torpedo ghost is a shallow copy of the attacker at launch with sureUsed set to the pre shot value; its dealt damage is credited back to the real attacker after impact if the attacker still exists (battle.js:538, battle.js:846 to battle.js:849).
- INFERRED: a torpedo fired in round N can only strike at the firing side turn start of a round above N, so a battle that ends in round N never resolves it, because resolveTorps is only called from turnStart and roundStartBoth and checkEnd does not resolve torpedoes (battle.js:850 to battle.js:855, battle.js:1007 to battle.js:1029).

## Damage pipeline (shields, armor, sections, crits, statuses)

- applyHit is the single damage entry for weapon hits, nova shells, station guns, and torpedo spread splashes (battle.js:697, battle.js:973, battle.js:1200 to battle.js:1205, battle.js:840).
- applyHit order: (1) record the struck facing in target shHit for Ithar prism charge; (2) if the weapon has ion, the attacker faction is hegemony, and the target has shields, lock that facing regen for one turn start (regenLock); (3) compute pierce (1 for a lance fired with fx.lanceSure, else weapon pierce or 0) and ion (ionVs); (4) shieldable damage is dmg times (1 minus pierce), through damage is the remainder; (5) absorbed is min(shieldable, facing shield divided by ion), the facing shield falls by absorbed times ion, floored at 0; (6) armor is max(0, target armor minus target shredded) times (1 minus weapon armorPierce), and hull damage before multipliers is (through plus remaining shieldable) times (1 minus armor, or 1 minus armor times 0.5 when rear); (7) multiply by braceMul(target), then by cmdTaken(target) times guardMul(target); (8) crit roll; (9) round; (10) ignite and trait effects; (11) subtract from hp, credit attacker dealt, and call damageSection (battle.js:697 to battle.js:719).
- ionOf is (weapon ion or 1) plus owner fx.ionPlus, plus fx.ionLance for a lance; ionVs multiplies that by 1.5 when the target is a Nemesis with the ion weakness and the weapon has ion or is a lance (battle.js:660, battle.js:602).
- Crit: only when pre crit hull damage is above 0.5, one roll rnd() times 100 below weapon crit (or 0) plus attacker crit plus 10 if attacker lockOn plus 15 if rear plus fx.momCrit when attacker hexMoved is at least 3; on a crit hull damage is multiplied by 1.5 plus fx.critMul (battle.js:708).
- Crit system damage is decided in landVolley, not applyHit: on the first crit of a volley, if the shot was called, or the attacker ownerFx has critSys, or a further rnd() roll is below 0.4, systemDamage(target, called) runs once for that volley (battle.js:797 to battle.js:800).
- systemDamage picks from engine, shields, weapon, fire, mapped from a forced section (engines or eng to engine, shields or mid to shields, weapons or prow to weapon); a shields result on a shieldless target and a weapon result on a weaponless target become fire; effects: engine adds 1 to st.engine capped at speed minus 1; shields sets st.shieldOff to at least 2 unless it is already 99 or above; weapon sets one weapon off to at least 2 (a forced weapon hit picks the highest dmg times shots weapon with off equal to 0 and sets off 2; an unforced hit picks a random weapon with off below 99); otherwise fire sets st.fire to 2 (battle.js:740 to battle.js:752).
- Ignite from fx: when hull damage is above 0 and the target is a living non squadron, chance ch is fx.igniteCh plus fx.broadIgnite for a P or S arc shot plus fx.sqIgnite for a squadron attacker; on rnd() below ch the target gets st.fire at least 2 plus fx.fireLong, and st.fireDmg at least 6 plus fx.fireDmg (battle.js:710 to battle.js:711).
- Weapon trait effects in applyHit, when hull damage is above 0 and the target is a living non squadron: weapon ignite sets st.fire at least 2 and st.fireDmg at least 6; weapon shred adds to target shredded, capped at 0.15 total; weapon acid sets st.acid to exactly 2 (battle.js:712 to battle.js:714).
- fx.pyre on the attacker owner marks a target below half hull as pyre, which blocks its hull regen, Chorus regrow, and both revival paths that check pyre (battle.js:715, battle.js:381, battle.js:507, battle.js:937).
- Sections: SEC_OF_QUAD maps quadrant 0 to prow, 1 to mid, 2 to eng, 3 to mid, so port and starboard hits both damage midships (battle.js:720).
- damageSection does nothing for a target without sections, a section that does not exist, or damage at 0 or below; crew loss is round(dmg divided by maxHp times crewMax times 0.35), floored at 0 crew, and it is applied even when the section is already broken or at 0 hp; otherwise the section loses dmg hp, floored at 0, and breaks only on the transition to 0 while not already broken (battle.js:729 to battle.js:732).
- A broken prow sets every F arc weapon off to 999; a broken mid sets every P and S arc weapon off to 999, and, if the ship has shields, sets st.shieldOff to 999 and all four shield facings to 0; a broken eng saves turns and evasion in secSave, sets st.engine to speed minus 1 (floored at 0), caps turns at 1, and sets evasion to 0; the attacker side gains 1 resolve on any break (battle.js:733 to battle.js:738).
- Because off only decreases by 1 per own turn start, an off value of 999 is effectively permanent for the rest of a battle unless repairSection restores it (INFERRED) (battle.js:377, battle.js:722 to battle.js:728).
- repairSection: an unbroken section gains round(max times frac) hp capped at max and returns false; a broken section is unbroken, set to at least 1 hp or round(max times frac), prow restores F arc weapons with off at least 99 to off 0, mid restores P and S arc weapons likewise and clears st.shieldOff if it is at least 99, eng clears st.engine and restores saved turns and evasion (battle.js:722 to battle.js:728).
- braceMul: a braced target takes max(0.15, 1 minus 0.4 minus target fx.bracePlus) of hull damage; a target with coverBy from a living braced covering ship takes the same factor using the covering ship bracePlus; otherwise 1 (battle.js:1051).
- guardMul: for a non squadron target with at least one living same side non squadron ally at distance exactly 1, hull damage is multiplied by (1 minus min(0.5, target fx.lineGuard)) when lineGuard is set, and by (1 minus the largest ally fx.guardAura) when any ally has one (battle.js:658 to battle.js:659).
- cmdTaken: a target whose side has an active iron Command this round takes 1 minus cmdPow(iron) of hull damage, minus a further 0.1 if it has no shields (battle.js:1092).
- expectedVs (preview and AI) mirrors the pipeline: it computes hit, raw damage, the shield absorption using the same ion and pierce, hull through armor with rear armor halving, then applies braceMul, cmdTaken, and guardMul, but it does not roll crits, ignite, shred, acid, or section breaks (battle.js:685 to battle.js:695).
- Statuses summary: st.fire (turns left, with st.fireDmg), st.acid (turns left, flat 4 per tick), st.shieldOff (turns left, 999 when the generator section is destroyed), st.engine (movement penalty, subtracted from speed when mp is reset), shredded (armor reduction, cap 0.15), pyre (boolean), regenLock and shHit (per facing arrays used by Hegemony ion and Ithar prism charge) (battle.js:372 to battle.js:388, battle.js:697 to battle.js:714, battle.js:500 to battle.js:501).
- Ram damage is not applyHit: ramEstimate base is (14 plus 46 times attacker size) times 1.2 for a RAM_FAC faction (voidsworn, corsair, chorus) else 1, times run factor 0.75 for a run of at most 1, 1 for a run of 2, 1.15 for a longer run; raw is base times (1 plus fx.ramPct) times ramBoost times 1.2 unless the struck quadrant is prow, times (1 minus target armor times 0.5); soak is min(raw times 0.5, target facing shield); damage is round(min(raw minus soak, target maxHp times (0.4 plus min(0.3, fx.ramCap)))); recoil is round(base times 0.35 for a RAM_FAC faction else 0.5, times clamp(target size divided by attacker size, 0.5, 1.6), times (1 minus attacker armor times 0.5), times (1 minus min(1, fx.ramGuard))) (battle.js:84, battle.js:868 to battle.js:870).
- ramUnit commits the run up, sets mp 0 and turnsLeft 0 and rammed true, subtracts soak times 1.5 from the target facing shield, applies damage to the target with damageSection on the struck quadrant section, applies recoil to the attacker with damageSection on prow, rolls rnd() below 0.35 for an extra systemDamage on the target section, applies fx.ramSplash damage to every living ship of the target side adjacent to the target, and then kills either ship through killUnit if its hp is at 0 or below, with killCause big, then checkEnd (battle.js:871 to battle.js:884).
- Boarding damage is crew only, except for sabotage systemDamage: see the abilities section for the full boarding procedure (battle.js:901 to battle.js:917).
- Nova damage is applyHit with a synthetic weapon (pierce 0.3, crit 5) for 40 times attacker dmg at the center hex and 20 times attacker dmg at distance 1, times 1.5 against squadrons (battle.js:1199 to battle.js:1212).
- Station guns in a defend battle use applyHit with a synthetic weapon (crit 0, pierce 0.2) for a flat 12 damage, with no hit roll (battle.js:970 to battle.js:974).
- Direct hull damage that bypasses applyHit entirely: fire and acid ticks, well tidal damage, riposte, ram damage and recoil, ram splash, chain boom, and the arc short shield break effect (battle.js:378 to battle.js:379, battle.js:282, battle.js:820, battle.js:878 to battle.js:882, battle.js:951 to battle.js:952, battle.js:806 to battle.js:807).

## Death, revival, and capture order

- killUnit(target, by) is the only death procedure; its first step, before any survival check, is to record killedBy once, using the parent carrier name when the killer is a squadron (battle.js:933).
- If the target is already dead, killUnit returns at once (battle.js:934).
- Survival and revival checks run in this exact order, and each one that succeeds returns without setting dead: (1) lastStand, (2) phylactery revive, (3) the Deathless legion budget test, which is computed before the random reanimate roll but does not itself roll, (4) the random reanimate roll, which is skipped entirely when the legion test passed (battle.js:935 to battle.js:938).
- (1) Last stand: if target fx.lastStand is set and usedLastStand is false, usedLastStand becomes true and hp is set to exactly 1, regardless of how far below 0 hp had fallen; fx.lastStand comes from a perk, a relic run effect carrier, or the legendary boon L_iron, which grants it to every ship (battle.js:935, data.js:182, data.js:683).
- (2) Phylactery: if the target side is player, the target is not a squadron, runFx().revive is set, and B.reviveUsed is false, then B.reviveUsed becomes true and hp is set to round(maxHp times 0.25); runFx().revive comes from the relic whose id is phylactery, with run effect revive 1, named Eclipse backup frame in the base table (battle.js:936, core.js:196, data.js:193).
- INFERRED: the phylactery check has no pyre, boss, flagship, or transport exclusion, so a pyre marked player capital ship can still be saved by it, unlike the legion and random reanimate paths below (battle.js:936).
- (3) Legion test: legionCan(target) is true only when the target is not a squadron, its faction is deathless, its hull template has reanimate, it has not already reanimated this battle, it is not pyre, it is not a boss, not the player flagship (pflag), not an objective flagship, and not a transport, and its side legion counter has used below max (battle.js:513).
- The legion budget is set in setupFactionRules: for each side, count non squadron deathless units whose template has reanimate (transports are not excluded from this count); max is 2 when the count is at least 6, 1 when the count is at least 1, else 0; a Nemesis undying trait or legion adaptation adds 1 to the enemy max, and the waken faction verb adds 1 to the player max (battle.js:487 to battle.js:490, battle.js:569, battle.js:607).
- Note the asymmetry: the count that sets the budget does not exclude bosses, flagships, or transports, but legionCan excludes all three from using it (battle.js:489, battle.js:513).
- (4) Random reanimate: only when the legion test failed, target.reanimate is true, target.reanimated is false, target is not pyre, and the target is not a deathless hull with template reanimate, one roll rnd() below 0.2 for an enemy or below 0.35 otherwise (the player and any non enemy side) succeeds; on success reanimated becomes true and hp is set to round(maxHp times 0.3) (battle.js:938).
- INFERRED: because a deathless template reanimate hull is excluded from the random roll, a Deathless ship that fails legionCan only because the budget is spent, or because it is a boss, flagship, or pyre, gets no second revival chance at all in killUnit (battle.js:937 to battle.js:938).
- Death commit: after all four checks fail, dead becomes true and hp is set to 0 (battle.js:939).
- If the legion test passed, the death is a deferred rise: reanimated becomes true, riseAt is set to B.round plus 1, the side legion used count increases by 1, and the unit stays dead until legionTurn of its side at a later turn start (battle.js:940, battle.js:520).
- legionRise places the ship back on its own hex if that hex is on board, not blocking, and unoccupied, else on the nearest free hex within distance 2 (searching board hexes in board order and keeping the strictly smallest distance, so the first hex at the best distance wins); if no hex qualifies, riseAt is cleared and the ship stays dead permanently for this battle (battle.js:514 to battle.js:518).

- Avenge recipient filter in code checks only isSq and hp below maxHp; it does not check transport, but no player transport units are created by any code path in battle.js, so in practice the recipients are player capital ships (INFERRED) (battle.js:943, battle.js:331 to battle.js:332).

- Bloodied: if the killer side is player, the dead unit is not a squadron, and runFx().bloodied is set, the killer (or its parent carrier, for a squadron killer) gains one bloodied stack, capped at 3, worth plus 15 percent damage per stack in dmgMult (battle.js:944, battle.js:680).

- killCauseOf assigns presentation and salvage causes only: gauss trait, gauss, or doom kinds are flay; acid or spines traits, bioplasma, tendril, or sporeb kinds are melt; lance or beam kinds, or a plasma trait weapon whose name matches lance, are cut; torp, htorp, startorp, or bombs kinds are big; battery family kinds are dark with probability derived from a hash of the target id and the round (3 in 10), not from the rules RNG; everything else is blast (battle.js:925 to battle.js:932).

- Capture happens only through boarding, never through damage: see boardUnit in the abilities section; a captured ship changes side in place, is marked captured and activated, and its weapons are marked fired (battle.js:901 to battle.js:914).

- INFERRED: because no save of RUN happens inside battle.js, quitting or reloading mid battle loses all mid battle hull changes; the campaign only ever sees the finishBattle write back (battle.js:295 to battle.js:358, campaign3d.js:66 to campaign3d.js:103).

## Abilities, orders, and Admiral's Command

- A ship has at most one ability, hull template ab, with cooldown from ABILITIES, reduced by fx.cdMod, floored at 1: abilityCd is max(1, ABILITIES[ab].cd minus fx.cdMod) (battle.js:106, battle.js:1052, data.js:132 to data.js:147).

- useAbility sets abCd to abilityCd at the end for every ability except that launch also passes through the same final assignment, and nova sets it before its blast resolves and returns early; a nova whose animation stalls returns false without spending the cooldown; a blink whose animation stalls still spends the cooldown (battle.js:1179 to battle.js:1216).
- Ability effects, evasive: evBuff plus 25 and mp plus 2; phase: evBuff plus 25 and mp plus 2 (identical numbers) (battle.js:1181 to battle.js:1184).
- reload: every torp weapon of the unit has cdLeft set to 0 and fired set to false (battle.js:1182).
- focus and frenzy: focus becomes true, worth plus 0.3 in dmgMult for the rest of the turn (it is cleared at the unit next turn start) (battle.js:1183, battle.js:1185, battle.js:671, battle.js:375).
- overburn: mp plus 3 and ramBoost 1.5, which multiplies ram raw damage until the next turn start clears ramBoost to 0 (battle.js:1186, battle.js:869, battle.js:375).
- regrow: heal min(missing hp, round(maxHp times 0.25)) (battle.js:1187).
- reclaim: heal the most damaged (lowest hp fraction) living same side non squadron ship within 3, which may be the user itself, by min(missing hp, round(its maxHp times 0.2)) (battle.js:1188).
- ionpulse: every living enemy non squadron ship within 4 has every shield facing halved with floor (battle.js:1189).
- blink: the target hex must be in blinkHexes (on board, distance 1 to 3, not blocking terrain, unoccupied); the unit position changes instantly before the animation; objAfterMove runs after (battle.js:1173, battle.js:1190).
- surge: all four shield facings are set to shMax, and st.shieldOff is set to 0 unless it is at least 99 (battle.js:1191).
- launch: see the squadrons section (battle.js:1192 to battle.js:1196).
- nova: targets are board hexes at distance 3 to 9 in the F arc; the blast hits every living unit of both sides within 1 hex of the chosen hex, center 40 and ring 20, as specified in the damage section (battle.js:1220, battle.js:1199 to battle.js:1212).
- Orders: canOrder is false when the unit is dead, already has an order, or is braced; full requires not moved and not a squadron; lock always passes that test; helm requires not a squadron and not noTurn; brace requires not a squadron, the faction gate does not forbid brace, and no weapon has fired except that flak weapons are ignored in that test (battle.js:1031 to battle.js:1037).
- FAC_GATES gives ithar noBrace, so Ithar hulls can never brace, and corsair noKind lance, which is a fleet building gate, not an order gate (data.js:541, battle.js:1036).
- giveOrder effects: brace sets braced true, mp 0, every weapon fired true, runs braceCover, and, with fx.bastion and shields, refills all facings to full; full sets fullAhead true (minus 15 accuracy in hitChance) and noTurn true, and adds ceil(mp times 0.5) mp; lock sets lockOn true (plus 15 accuracy, plus 10 crit) and mp 0; helm adds 1 turnsLeft (battle.js:1039 to battle.js:1047, battle.js:636, battle.js:708).
- Defect (not in the brief): the ORDERS table in data.js defines only brace, but canOrder and giveOrder accept full, lock, and helm; giveOrder ends by reading ORDERS[o].n for its log line, so a call with o equal to full, lock, or helm would throw a TypeError after applying the effect, and no UI order button for those three ids exists in the files read (data.js:148 to data.js:150, battle.js:1031 to battle.js:1047).
- braceCover: a bracing voidsworn ship, or any ship with fx.wallBrace, covers adjacent same side non squadron allies: without wallBrace only allies with the same facing, and only the single most damaged by hp fraction; with wallBrace every adjacent ally regardless of facing; covered ships get coverBy set to the bracing ship id, cleared at their own turn start (battle.js:1049 to battle.js:1050, battle.js:375).
- Ram procedure: ramInfo requires a non squadron, non braced, alive unit that has not rammed, with crew above 0 if it has crew, with at least 1 movement (min(mp, max(1, speed minus st.engine))), and an enemy non squadron ship straight ahead in the facing direction with at least 1 clear hex of run up and no blocking terrain or board edge in between; any other ship in the lane, a friendly ship, a squadron, or an adjacent enemy (0 clear hexes) makes it null (battle.js:857 to battle.js:863).
- Boarding eligibility (boardTargets): the attacker must be a non squadron, non braced, not yet boarded, alive ship with crewMax set and crew at least max(4, crewMax times 0.4), and no weapon of the attacker may have fired; targets are living ships of the other side at distance exactly 1 that are not squadrons (battle.js:886 to battle.js:887).
- boardUnit sets boarded true and repel true on the attacker, mp 0, and every attacker weapon fired true; then attacker crew strength a is crew times BOARD_MULT[faction] times (1 plus fx.boardPct) times (0.75 plus rnd() times 0.5), and defender strength d is target crew times BOARD_MULT[target faction] times (2.25 if the target has repel, else 1.5) times (0.75 plus rnd() times 0.5); defenders killed is round(a times 0.38); boarders lost is min(attacker crew minus 1, round(d times 0.3)); both crews are reduced, attacker crew floored at 1 (battle.js:901 to battle.js:906, battle.js:83).
- Capture result: if target crew falls to 0 or below and the target is a player ship or a boss, there is no capture: target crew is restored to max(2, round(crewMax times 0.15)) and two systemDamage results are applied as sabotage (battle.js:908 to battle.js:909).
- If target crew falls to 0 or below otherwise, the capture succeeds: an anchor target loses sync first, then the target side becomes the attacker side, crew is set to max(3, round(crewMax times 0.4)), captured and activated become true, mp and turnsLeft become 0, braced becomes false, every weapon is marked fired, the attacker side gains 2 resolve, onCapture and checkEnd run (battle.js:910 to battle.js:913).
- If the target crew stays above 0, the boarding is a raid: with probability 0.5, or always when the attacker has fx.boardPct, one systemDamage sabotage result is applied and reported (battle.js:915 to battle.js:916).
- captureChance (the displayed odds) is the exact probability of the capture roll: with s equal to attacker crew times BOARD_MULT times (1 plus fx.boardPct) times 0.38, and x equal to (target crew minus 0.5) divided by s, the chance is round(100 times clamp((1.25 minus x) divided by 0.5, 0, 1)); it is 0 against a boss target (battle.js:891).
- INFERRED mismatch: captureChance prices the attacker roll only and treats capture as round(crew times mult times r times 0.38) reaching target crew, but boardUnit also rolls the defender multiplier (0.75 plus rnd() times 0.5) for the loss number only, so the displayed chance is exact for the capture test as coded, since the defender roll never affects whether crew reaches 0; the defender repel factor likewise does not enter the capture test, only the boarder losses (battle.js:891, battle.js:904 to battle.js:906).
- Admiral's Command: commandFor(player) returns RUN.cmdOverride if set, else the admiral record cmd, else COMMANDS[admiral id], else COMMANDS.vex; the four fallback COMMANDS are barrage, hunt, scramble, and iron (battle.js:1067 to battle.js:1070, battle.js:1089).
- canCommand requires a battle, not over, side player, a command record, round at least 1, and B.cmdUsed false: one Command per battle, available from round 1 (battle.js:1091).
- cmdTier is clamp(round minus 1 plus 1 if any living player unit has fx.cmdTier plus 1 if any player non squadron non transport unit is dead minus 1 if h_cmd heat is on, 0, 2) (battle.js:1073).
- CMD_POW by tier 0, 1, 2: barrage 0, 0.25, 0.5; hunt 0.3, 0.45, 0.6; scramble 0.5, 0.75, 1; iron 0.4, 0.5, 0.6; restore 0.25, 0.35, 0.45; surge 2, 3, 4 (battle.js:1072).
- useCommand sets B.cmdUsed, stores B.cmd with id, side, round, and tier, and runs runCommand (battle.js:1093 to battle.js:1095).
- runCommand barrage: B.volleyMul is set to 1 plus cmdPow for the side; each living non squadron ship of the side, in alive order, has all weapons temporarily unfired and cooldowns cleared, fires each weapon once at the best target by expectedVs (hull plus shield times 0.3, plus 25 for a kill), and then has its original fired and cdLeft values and its postFire and postMp restored; volleyMul is cleared afterwards even on an exception (battle.js:1097 to battle.js:1099).
- runCommand hunt: every torp weapon of every living ship of the side has cdLeft 0 and fired false; the damage bonus is not stored on the weapons but applied in dmgMult through cmdActive(side, hunt) for the rest of the round (battle.js:1100, battle.js:680).
- runCommand iron: every living ship of the side with shMax above 0 has all facings refilled to shMax; the damage reduction is applied through cmdTaken for the rest of the round (battle.js:1101, battle.js:1092).

- runCommand surge: every living non squadron ship gains cmdPow movement points and 1 extra turn, or 2 at tier 2; the accuracy penalty against that side is applied inside hitChance through cmdActive(target side, surge) (battle.js:1103, battle.js:650 to battle.js:653).
- runCommand scramble: each living launch ability ship launches 1 extra wing (2 at tier 2) into its launch hexes sorted by distance to the nearest enemy, even beyond the hangar limit and without spending spare; then every squadron of the side is reset (activated false, mp equal to speed, weapons unfired and cooldowns cleared) and swarmPhase(side) runs at once; the damage bonus applies through dmgMult for squadron attackers while the Command round matches (battle.js:1104 to battle.js:1105, battle.js:680).
- Boss Command: BOSS_CMD maps faction to a command id (hegemony barrage, corsair surge, chorus restore, ithar iron, deathless restore, voidsworn hunt); bossCmdTick, called at enemy turn start, schedules it when round is at least 2 or the boss is below 60 percent maxHp, setting B.cmdENext to round plus 1 and announcing it; bossCmdFire, called at the start of each enemy ship activation in aiUnit, fires it once at tier 1 (stored in B.cmdE) when round reaches cmdENext (battle.js:1109, battle.js:1111 to battle.js:1114, battle.js:1351).
- INFERRED: because bossCmdTick is called inside the empty try/catch in startSideTurn, a failure inside bossCmdTick can never prevent the enemy turn from starting (battle.js:368, battle.js:1111).

## Squadrons and wings

- Squadron hulls are templates with the sq field: fighter (hp 18, speed 6, evasion 35, guns kind sq), bomber (hp 28, speed 5, evasion 25, bombs kind sq), and spore (chorus, hp 26, speed 5, evasion 25, sporeb kind sq); squadrons have no sections and no crew (data.js:69 to data.js:71, battle.js:117 to battle.js:119).
- A squadron unit isSq is true, sqKind is the template sq value, parent is the carrier unit id, and sqPct is inherited from the carrier fx.sqPct (battle.js:111 to battle.js:114).
- makeWing creates the wing at a given hex with the carrier facing, names it from the wing hull name and the carrier name (player) or the last word of the carrier template name (enemy), applies wing veteran perks (aces aim plus 10, jinkers evasion plus 8, armored maxHp and hp times 1.25 rounded), and, for a player carrier, sums the fleet fx list to read sqHp (battle.js:1147 to battle.js:1150).
- deployWings: every living non squadron unit with launch ability or fx.hive launches min(hangar, sector) wings for an enemy boss, else hangar wings; spare is set to max(0, sector minus 1) for an enemy boss, 99 when fx.endless is set, else hangar plus 1 if the carrier has the reserve wing perk; launch hexes are sorted by distance to the nearest enemy (battle.js:1152 to battle.js:1156).
- Adapt wings: when B.adaptWings is set, that many fighter wings are launched from the biggest living enemy non squadron non transport hosts, cycling through the host list (battle.js:1157).
- wingKind: a faction squadron type that is not fighter is always used; for a fighter faction, an enemy carrier always fields fighters; a player carrier fields bombers when the enemy has no squadron and no carrier, else it alternates bomber, fighter by launch index, and for a manual launch it returns bomber when bombers are at most fighters in count, else fighter (battle.js:1159).
- launchHexes are the 6 neighboring hexes that are on board, not blocking terrain, and unoccupied (battle.js:1178).
- A launch ability use consumes 1 spare when spare is not null, creates the wing at the chosen or a picked launch hex, and sets the new wing mp 0 and every weapon fired, so a freshly launched wing cannot act until a later swarm phase resets it through its activation (battle.js:1192 to battle.js:1196, battle.js:1146).
- SQ_LEASH is 8 hexes from a living parent carrier; a strike order adds 2, a hunt stance adds WING_HUNT_LEASH 4, and a screen stance instead ties the wing to within WING_SCREEN_R plus 1 (3) of the carrier when guarding, or within 3 of an explicit guard target (battle.js:1117, battle.js:612, battle.js:1127 to battle.js:1135).
- sqWants: a bomber wants only non squadron targets; a fighter wants squadron targets when the target side has any living squadron, and otherwise wants any enemy target (battle.js:1120).
- Wing orders: a player wing may hold a wingOrder of kind strike (a chosen enemy) or guard (a friendly ship); the order is dropped when the target is dead or on the same side; a strike order multiplies that target value by 3 and adds 60, and lets the wing plan a move toward the target when it cannot yet fire; a guard order doubles and adds 20 to the value of targets within 3 of the guarded ship (battle.js:1126 to battle.js:1140).
- Wing stances are per carrier, not per wing: strike (default), screen, hunt, stored on the carrier as stance, changeable at most once per round per carrier through canStance, and only for a launch ability or hive carrier (data.js:506 to data.js:509, battle.js:613 to data.js:615).
- Screen stance effects: wings guard their carrier, targets that are squadrons are worth double, and screening wings within WING_SCREEN_R 2 of a torpedo target shoot down torpedo shots at impact: for each such wing, at most one hitting shot per salvo is removed with probability WING_SCREEN_TORP 0.45, or 0.65 when the parent has the pickets perk, and each removed shot counts into res.pd (battle.js:612, battle.js:622 to battle.js:624, battle.js:1131 to battle.js:1138).
- Hunt stance effects: leash plus 4, value times 1.6 against a non squadron target below half hull, damage plus 0.2 against such a target in dmgMult, and attackers gain 10 accuracy against a hunting wing in hitChance (battle.js:1127 to data.js:1138, battle.js:668, battle.js:646).
- aiStance for an enemy (or bot) carrier at its side turn start: screen when an enemy torpedo salvo is in flight or enemy squadrons outnumber own squadrons by more than 1, else hunt when any enemy capital ship is below half hull, else strike; it uses setStance, so it is also limited to one change per round (battle.js:617 to battle.js:619, battle.js:369).
- sqPlan evaluates the stay state and every computeReach state, keeps only states inside the leash, and for every ready weapon and wanted target that inReach from that state computes value as sqValue minus state cost times 0.01, with the order and stance modifiers above; sqValue is expected hull plus shield times 0.3, plus 25 for a kill, times 1.5 when a bomber evaluates a squadron, times 1.3 when an enemy wing evaluates the veteran AI focus, plus 6 when the target is near the defend station or an intercept transport, plus 10 times the target missing hull fraction (battle.js:1121 to battle.js:1140).
- If no attack plan exists, a guarding wing moves toward its guard target, a strike ordered wing moves toward its target by pathField distance, any other wing moves toward its nearest wanted target by pathField distance, and a wing stranded outside the leash flies toward its carrier (battle.js:1139 to battle.js:1143).
- sqAct moves the wing to the planned state (unless it is the stay state) and then fires every weapon that canFire at the planned target, in weapon order (battle.js:1144 to battle.js:1145).
- swarmPhase(side) acts, in alive order, every squadron of that side that is not activated, wraps each in a 15 second stallGuard, and marks it activated afterwards (battle.js:1146).
- In live alternating play, player squadrons act in the round start swarm phases (player then enemy) inside nextRound, and enemy squadrons act inside aiStep before the enemy capital ships; in classic play, the player swarm phase runs at the end of endTurn after the player turn start, and enemy squadrons act inside aiTurn, which processes squadrons first in speed order (ui3d.js:598 to ui3d.js:601, ui3d.js:581, ui3d.js:564, battle.js:1311 to battle.js:1315).
- A squadron attacking a capital ship triggers the flak gauntlet described in the firing section before its own shots are rolled, and the squadron may die there, in which case fireWeapon returns true with no shots fired (battle.js:763 to battle.js:772).
- A squadron death gives its parent no kill credit transfer, but a parent with fx.feast gains a spare wing and hull, and a squadron killer resolves chorusFeed and Brood Tithe through its parent (battle.js:947 to battle.js:948, battle.js:503, battle.js:942).
- ownerFx for a squadron returns its parent fx, so squadron damage uses the carrier boons and refits (battle.js:656).

## Objectives and battle end

- The eight encounter types and their stated goals are rout, ambush, defend, hunt, intercept, break, hold, and flagship, defined in ENCOUNTERS with ENC_ROUNDS 6 (battle.js:19, battle.js:58 to battle.js:66).
- setupObjective builds B.obj with type, score 0, and escore 0, and then adjusts: a flagship objective with fewer than 3 enemy capital ships becomes rout if some enemy is a boss hull, else hunt; a defend objective with no station in B.map becomes hold (battle.js:957 to battle.js:959).
- Hunt and flagship both mark the enemy capital ship with the highest cp, then highest maxHp, as flagship (u.flagship true); hunt also sets o.rounds to ENC_ROUNDS 6 (battle.js:960, battle.js:965).
- Defend sets o.hex to the station hex from B.map, o.hp and o.hpMax to 5, and o.rounds to 6 (battle.js:961).
- Intercept sets o.total to the number of transports, o.need to max(1, total minus 1), o.exitR to the board maximum row, and kill and esc to 0; with no transports it becomes rout (battle.js:962).
- Break sets o.need to max(1, ceil(player capital ships divided by 2)) and o.exitC to the board maximum column minus 1, with out 0 (battle.js:963).
- Hold picks the free, non blocking, unoccupied board hex nearest the board center as o.hex, and deletes blocking terrain on every hex within 1 of it (battle.js:966 to battle.js:967).
- objZone classifies a hex as exit when its column is at least o.exitC in a break battle, texit when its row is at least o.exitR in an intercept battle, and guard when its distance to the station hex is exactly 2 in a defend battle; ZONE_INFO in ui3d.js gives the player facing text for all three zones (battle.js:969, ui3d.js:101).
- objAfterMove: in a break battle a player ship ending its move on an exit hex is marked exited, dead, and fled and checkEnd runs; in an intercept battle a transport ending its move on a texit hex is marked dead and fled and checkEnd runs (battle.js:975 to battle.js:977).
- objectiveTick runs only at player turn start when round is above 1, as part of startSideTurn (battle.js:366).
- Defend tick: each enemy capital ship within 2 of the station removes 1 from o.hp, floored at 0, then, if o.hp is above 0 and round is at most o.rounds, stationFire runs, then checkEnd (battle.js:998 to battle.js:1000).
- stationFire targets the 2 nearest enemy capital ships within 5 of the station, in distance order, for a flat applyHit of 12 with pierce 0.2 and no hit roll, and queues a death when one reaches hp 0 (battle.js:970 to battle.js:974).
- Hunt tick: when round is above o.rounds, the enemy flagship, if alive, is marked dead and fled, o.escaped is set true, and checkEnd runs (battle.js:1001).
- Hold tick: at player turn start, if a living non squadron player ship is within 1 of the relay and no living non squadron enemy ship is, score increases by 1; the mirror case increases escore by 1; if both are present the relay is contested and neither score changes; then checkEnd (battle.js:1002 to battle.js:1003).
- checkEnd is the single owner of battle end: it records whether B.over was already set, runs checkEndRules, and, on the first transition to over, rises player legion ships after a win and schedules the end flow once (battle.js:1007 to battle.js:1008).
- checkEndRules order: (1) if all enemy capital ships are gone, over is win; (2) break: if exited count reaches need, win, else if exited is above 0 and remaining player capital ships plus exited is below need, loss with withdraw true; (3) if all player capital ships are gone, loss; (4) intercept scoring; (5) defend scoring; (6) hunt scoring; (7) flagship scoring; (8) hold scoring; (9) the rout rule (battle.js:1009 to battle.js:1029).
- Intercept scoring in checkEndRules: kill is the number of transports dead and not fled, esc is the number fled; kill at least need is a win with routed true; esc above total minus need is also a win, but with partial true (battle.js:1016 to battle.js:1019).
- Note the intercept partial rule exactly: it is a win for the player even though the transports got through, flagged partial, because esc above total minus need means fewer than need transports remain destroyable (battle.js:1017 to battle.js:1019).
- Defend scoring: o.hp at 0 or below is a loss with withdraw true; round above o.rounds is a win (battle.js:1020).
- Hunt scoring: when the objective flagship is dead, it is a win; with escaped true it is flagged partial, otherwise routed (battle.js:1021).
- Flagship scoring: when no living enemy unit has the flagship flag, it is a win with routed true (battle.js:1022).
- Hold scoring: score at least 3 is a win; escore at least 3 is a loss with withdraw true (battle.js:1023).
- Rout rule: it is skipped entirely when B.cp0 is missing, or the objective is intercept or hunt; the player wins with routed true when the enemy boss is dead in a boss battle, or when sideCP(enemy) is at most B.cp0.enemy times 0.35, unless B.nemNoRout is set (a Voidsworn or Deathless Nemesis) (battle.js:1024 to battle.js:1029).
- sideCP sums template cp over living non squadron units of a side, so a captured ship counts for its new side and a fled or dead ship counts for neither (battle.js:956).
- Because the first checkEndRules test is enemy capital ships equal to zero, any objective can also be won by destroying or capturing the whole enemy capital fleet, except that transports are capital ships for caps() purposes (they are not squadrons), so an intercept battle is not won until the transports are also gone or fled (battle.js:91 to battle.js:92, battle.js:1010).
- INFERRED: fled enemy ships are dead, so they count toward the rout threshold and toward the empty fleet test, which is why escortsBreak and nemBreak can end a battle without a kill (battle.js:363, battle.js:599, battle.js:1010).
- There is no round limit for rout, ambush, break, intercept, or flagship battles in checkEndRules; only defend and hunt read a round count, and hold ends only on score (battle.js:1009 to battle.js:1029).
- The sim drivers impose their own 30 round cap and report timeout when B.over is still unset, which is a harness limit, not a battle rule (sim3d.js:72, sim3d.js:118).

## AI decision flow

- The enemy AI entry points are aiTurn (classic) and aiUnit (one unit, used by both modes); aiUnit also serves enemy squadrons by delegating to sqAct (battle.js:1311 to battle.js:1316, battle.js:1349 to battle.js:1350).
- aiTurn order: first every living enemy squadron in an order sorted by squadron first, then descending speed, then each non squadron unit once, in an order that is the fixed list for standard AI or aiPickActivation order for veteran AI (battle.js:1311 to battle.js:1316).
- aiVet is true when AI_LEVEL is undefined (the battle.js default assumption) or equals veteran; the campaign sets AI_LEVEL to standard for a fresh profile, and sim3d.js sets AI_LEVEL from the AI environment variable with default veteran (battle.js:1241, campaign3d.js:3, sim3d.js:40).
- aiUnit sequence: (1) if the unit is a squadron, sqAct and return; (2) bossCmdFire if a boss Command is due; (3) abort if the battle is over, the unit is dead, or its side is not enemy; (4) aiAbilities in pre mode; (5) tacticRam unless the unit is a runner (a transport, or the hunt flagship); (6) movement planning and moveUnit, with the mid fire volley described below; (7) nova when applicable; (8) tacticBoard; (9) the veteran pre volley brace test; (10) aiAbilities in aim mode; (11) aiVolley; (12) aiAfterFire (a lined up ram, then a fade move, then aiAbilities in escape mode); (13) a final brace if nothing fired, the unit is below half hull, and incoming threat is above 10; the unit is then marked activated by the caller or by aiTurn flow (battle.js:1349 to battle.js:1401).
- NOMIDFIRE defect, stated exactly: at battle.js:1385 the mid fire condition reads process.env.NOMIDFIRE, in the expression that also requires animPath to be a function and the process object to exist; a repository wide search of the browser JS files finds NOMIDFIRE only at that one read site and no assignment or definition anywhere (battle.js:1385).
- Because NOMIDFIRE is only ever read, it is undefined in normal play, so the inner conjunction is false, its negation is true, and mid fire is enabled by default, including in the browser, where there is no process object and the conjunction is therefore also false; only a node or sim process with the environment variable NOMIDFIRE set to a non empty string disables mid fire, and the string 0 counts as non empty and therefore disables it (INFERRED from the three part condition) (battle.js:1385, battle.js:20).
- Mid fire mechanics: when midFire is true, moveUnit is given an onLate callback that fires about two thirds through the animation; the callback does nothing if boardTargets is non empty (boarding takes the activation instead), otherwise it starts aiAbilities in aim mode followed by aiVolley, and that promise is awaited after the move; if a volley promise exists, aiUnit returns through the volley path after aiAfterFire and the fallback brace, and never reaches the nova, board, or second volley code below (battle.js:447 to battle.js:462, battle.js:1385 to battle.js:1389).
- Movement planning: for every computeReach state, the AI builds a stateView, computes offense (evalOffense, or evalOffenseVet for veteran), threat (evalThreat, or evalThreatVet for veteran), a faction profile, and a score, and keeps the highest scoring state (battle.js:1361 to battle.js:1383).
- Score formula, in order: score is offense minus threat times tw; veteran only: minus 25 when threat is at least the unit hp (the death cliff); veteran only: range discipline from the faction profile; veteran only: plus ramSetup damage times 0.35 for a RAM_FAC faction; veteran only: flagship escort spacing terms in a flagship battle; when offense is 0 or below, minus max(0, pathField distance minus the smallest weapon opt) times 4; minus state cost times 0.05; minus torpThreat times 0.9; minus syncPull times 2 for veteran or 1.2 for standard, doubled again for a Nemesis line flagship escorts case (u.nem and B.nemLine); minus hex distance to the relay times 1.6 in a hold battle for standard AI only; plus aiObjScore; plus aiWellScore (battle.js:1368 to battle.js:1382).
- tw (threat weight) is 0.15 for a squadron, 0.3 for standard AI, 0.65 for a flagship under veteran AI, and otherwise aiHurtW times the faction profile tw under veteran AI; nemProf may multiply tw and override the preferred range (battle.js:1370, battle.js:588).
- aiHurtW is a smooth curve: 0.32 when healthy, rising to 0.6 times the profile hurt weight when crippled, with hysteresis: below 30 percent hull the unit is marked aiHurt, and the mark clears only above 45 percent hull (battle.js:1343).
- FACTION_AI profiles (tw, hurt, range, rw): voidsworn 1, 1, 0, 0; hegemony 1.15, 1.3, range 3, 1.2; corsair 0.8, 1.4, range minus 2, 1.2; chorus 0.6, 0.7, range minus 1, 1.8; ithar 1.3, 1.6, range 2, 1.2; deathless 0.55, 0.6, range minus 3, 0.8; a positive range penalizes being closer than that range, a negative range penalizes being farther than its absolute value, in both cases times rw per hex (battle.js:1233 to battle.js:1239, battle.js:1371).
- evalOffense sums, per ready weapon, the best single target value, where value is expected hull plus shield times 0.3, plus 15 plus target maxHp times 0.1 for a kill, halved against a squadron when the weapon has no vsSq (battle.js:1224 to battle.js:1230).
- evalOffenseVet additionally multiplies value by 1.3 against the fleet focus and by 1.1 for a rear shot (battle.js:1265 to battle.js:1267).
- evalThreat sums, per enemy ship within 9 hexes, per weapon that is not on a long cooldown or offline and is aiGunLive, in range and in arc, expected hull plus shield times 0.2 (battle.js:1275 to battle.js:1279).
- evalThreatVet extends the range to 13, and adds a 0.2 weighted term for weapons that could bear after the enemy moves one step and turns, when line of sight is clear (battle.js:1244 to battle.js:1246).
- aiGunLive excludes flak weapons always, and, in alternating play, excludes weapons of a ship that has already fired or is braced (battle.js:1248).
- The fleet focus (veteran only): aiFocusVal prices a player capital ship as ((cp times 12) plus 20 if flagship or player flagship) divided by max(20, effective hp), where effective hp is hp divided by max(0.15, braceMul times guardMul times cmdTaken) plus total shields times 0.3, scaled by 1 when at least 2 enemy shooters can reach it, 0.8 when exactly 1 can, and 0.4 when none can (battle.js:1254 to battle.js:1260).
- aiRefreshFocus keeps the current focus unless it is dead, off side, or no longer in this battle, or another target scores above 1.25 times its value; it is called at enemy turn start (inside the empty try/catch), in aiPickActivation, and once per aiUnit activation (battle.js:1262 to battle.js:1264, battle.js:368, battle.js:1360).
- For standard AI, aiRefreshFocus sets B.aiFocus to null and returns null, so there is no focus, no focus bonus, and no sticky target (battle.js:1262).
- aiPickActivation (veteran): for each candidate, value is the best offense over all 6 facings, plus half the best reachable weapon damage (times 1.3 against the focus), plus a quarter of evalThreat against the unit, plus 10 when the unit is below 30 percent hull; the highest value acts next; standard AI, or a list shorter than 2, returns the first entry (battle.js:1269 to battle.js:1273).
- aiVolley loops once per weapon at most: it collects ready non flak weapons not yet tried, picks a primary with aiPickPrimary, orders the guns with aiGunOrder and fires the first, then repeats so the primary is re picked before every gun (battle.js:1292 to battle.js:1309).
- aiPickPrimary values each player target as (min(expected hull, target hp) plus expected shield times 0.3) times 0.6 for a squadron or 1.1 otherwise, plus 25 plus target maxHp times 0.15 for a kill, plus 10 against the focus, plus 15 times the target missing hull fraction, plus nemTargetBonus; target hysteresis keeps the previous activation primary when its value is at least AI_STICK 0.85 of the best (battle.js:1285 to battle.js:1289, battle.js:1284).
- aiGunOrder sorts ready weapons by descending shield share of expected raw damage (rounded to quarters), then by descending dmg times shots, so shield strippers fire first (battle.js:1290 to battle.js:1291).
- In aiVolley, a Bulwark taunt overrides the primary: a living player non squadron ship with fx.taunt whose value covers the attacker must be fired on when the chosen gun can fire at it; otherwise a veteran AI fires at the primary, except that a heavy gun that would overkill the primary by a factor of 2 or more is redirected to the best other target worth more than 3 when a lighter ready gun can also kill the primary; standard AI fires each gun at its own best target by min(hull, hp) plus shield times 0.3, plus 20 for a kill, times 1.1 against capital ships (battle.js:1297 to battle.js:1307).
- A gun fires in aiVolley only when its best value is above 0.5 (battle.js:1308).
- aiAbilities pre mode, in order: launch when able (into the launch hex nearest the enemy); surge when the shield facing toward the nearest enemy is below half of shMax and that enemy is within 8; evasive or phase when any enemy is within 5; reload when an enemy capital ship is within 6 plus speed; regrow below 60 percent hull; reclaim when an ally within 3 is below 60 percent hull; ionpulse when enemy shields within 4 sum to at least 30; overburn when the nearest enemy is farther than speed plus 1; blink when below 35 percent hull and threat is above 30 percent of hp, or when no ready weapon can reach any enemy (battle.js:1325 to battle.js:1338).
- aiAbilities aim mode re runs only focus and frenzy (when a ready weapon can fire at some enemy) and reload (when a spent torp weapon now has a target in reach); escape mode re runs only blink, below 35 percent hull with threat above 30 percent of hp (battle.js:1325 to battle.js:1338).
- tacticRam requires a run up of at least 2 path hexes; it rams when the estimate damage is at least the target hp, or at least 30 percent of target hp for a RAM_FAC faction, or 45 percent otherwise (battle.js:918 to battle.js:919).
- tacticBoard boards only a target whose capture chance is at least 70, choosing the highest value of 100 plus target maxHp divided by 10 (battle.js:920 to battle.js:921).
- fadeMove (ithar, or fx.fade): after firing, when postFireMp is at least 1 and current threat plus torpThreat is at least 5, the unit moves to the reachable state with the lowest threat plus torpThreat, but only when that value is at most 0.75 of the current value (battle.js:1345 to battle.js:1347).
- Brace decisions by the AI are made in exactly three places: (a) pre move is not one of them; the brace decision the brief calls pre move is in fact inside aiUnit after the volley path returns, and in the veteran check before the aim abilities: when aiVet, the unit is not a boss, brace is orderable, its own offense is below 0.35 of the threat against it, threat is above 45 percent of its hp, and it is below 70 percent maxHp, it braces and ends its activation; (b) after a volley that fired nothing, or after the mid fire volley path, when the unit is below half hull and evalThreat is above 10, it braces; (c) the same test repeats at the end of aiUnit (battle.js:1390 to battle.js:1400).
- Correction to the brief wording: there is no brace decision inside evalOffense, and no brace decision before movement in aiUnit; evalOffense and evalOffenseVet only return numbers, and the first brace test in aiUnit is the veteran test at battle.js:1393, which is after movement, after the nova attempt, and after the boarding attempt (battle.js:1224 to battle.js:1230, battle.js:1385 to battle.js:1395).
- aiObjScore adds objective pull to a candidate position: transport units score minus 14 per row short of the exit row; a hunt flagship scores up to 27 for distance from the nearest player capital ship; defend scores minus 3 per hex beyond 2 from the station, times veteran urgency; break scores an interception of the leading player ship (veteran) or a flat pull for standard; intercept escorts (veteran) score toward their transports; hold (veteran) pulls only the two nearest enemy ships toward the relay (battle.js:978 to battle.js:993).
- aiWellScore penalizes a candidate position by 45 within the well rim and by 6 within the pull radius (battle.js:286).

- The sim bot fires weapons in list order at the best target per gun by expected hull plus shield times 0.3 plus 20 for a kill, whereas the live AI re picks a primary per gun and orders guns by shield share (sim3d.js:55 to sim3d.js:59, sim3d.js:107 to sim3d.js:110, battle.js:1292 to battle.js:1309).

## Random number generation in battle

- All rules randomness in battle.js goes through rnd(), ri(a, b), pick(array), and shuffle(array) from core.js; cosmetic effects may use Math.random and never feed a rule (core.js:2 to core.js:10).
- rnd is mulberry32: the state is advanced by adding 0x6D2B79F5, then mixed with two imul rounds and xorshifts, and the result is an unsigned 32 bit integer divided by 4294967296, giving a value in 0 to below 1 (core.js:5 to core.js:7).
- rndSeed(s) sets the state to s taken as an unsigned 32 bit integer (core.js:5).
- The initial state at load is seeded from Math.random, so anything rolled before startBattle (sector generation, node seeds) is not reproducible from a battle seed (core.js:4, core.js:9 to core.js:10).
- startBattle reseeds with cfg.seed before terrain generation, deployment, fleet generation, and every combat roll, so a seed plus the same sequence of player decisions replays the same battle; the campaign derives cfg.seed from the node seed stored in the saved run, with a separate salt for event fights, and also seeds the pre battle rolls from that node seed (battle.js:296, campaign3d.js:50 to campaign3d.js:55).
- ri(a, b) is a plus floor(rnd() times (b minus a plus 1)), inclusive at both ends; pick is array[floor(rnd() times length)]; shuffle is Fisher Yates from the end using rnd() (core.js:7 to core.js:9).
- Draw order inside a battle is therefore significant and is fixed by the code paths above: setup rolls (terrain, fleet, deployment) come first, then, per activation, ability rolls, movement planning (which rolls nothing except through expected value functions, which roll nothing), hit rolls per shot in weapon order, per shot damage variance rolls in landVolley, crit rolls inside applyHit, ignite rolls, system damage picks, boarding rolls, and torpedo impact rolls at the next turn start (battle.js:296 to battle.js:358, battle.js:779 to battle.js:800, battle.js:904 to battle.js:906, battle.js:842 to battle.js:845).
- INFERRED: expectedVs, hitChance, dmgMult, evalOffense, evalThreat, and aiFocusVal contain no rnd() call, so AI planning and previews consume no draws; the only planning side effect on the draw sequence is through the actions the AI then takes (battle.js:634 to battle.js:695, battle.js:1224 to battle.js:1230, battle.js:1275 to battle.js:1279).
- Exceptions that read the environment instead of the seeded RNG: BOSS_SCALE, BOSS_DMG, RAMP, TORP_INSTANT, NOMIDFIRE, NOCLEAR, NOPRELAUNCH, NOADAPT, ADAPT, NOTRAITS, and NOCOMPACT are all read through process_env or direct process access, and none of them is part of a saved game (battle.js:20, battle.js:131 to battle.js:137, battle.js:289, battle.js:344, battle.js:537, battle.js:1385, data.js:55).
- killCauseOf deliberately avoids a rules draw for its battery family dark result, using a hash of the target id and the round instead, so death presentation cannot shift the draw sequence (battle.js:931).
- newId uses Date.now and a counter, not rnd(), so unit ids, and therefore id order tie breaks in torpImpact and aiObjScore, are not reproducible from the battle seed (core.js:19, battle.js:838, battle.js:988).

- Fields that newRun does not set literally (for example seenEvents, condsUsed, classTrack, reserve, ransoms, verbUsed) are supplied lazily by readers or by normalizeRun on load; runDefaults lists the full expected field set (core.js:66, core.js:68). INFERRED: a fresh in-memory RUN therefore lacks some fields until first use, and code that reads them defensively (for example genSector reading RUN.condsUsed with a fallback) is load bearing.

## 2. Sector generation and its guarantees

- genSector(s) is the only sector generator. It takes the sector number s (1 to 3 in a normal run) and returns an object with fac, cols, edges, cond, region, and biome (core.js:251, core.js:296).
- Sector faction: if RUN.foes exists, the sector faction is RUN.foes[s minus 1], so sector 1 uses foes[0], sector 2 uses foes[1], and sector 3 uses foes[2] (core.js:252). Only if RUN.foes is missing does it pick from SECTOR_POOL[s minus 1]: sector 1 pool corsair or hegemony, sector 2 pool chorus, ithar, or hegemony, sector 3 pool deathless, chorus, or ithar (core.js:252, data.js:130).
- The remaining foes entries are used elsewhere: foes[3] is the Nemesis faction source and foes[3] and foes[4] are the rival raider factions for ordinary battle nodes (core.js:249, campaign_run.js nemesis creation in campaign_run.js:243 to 248).
- Grid shape: COLS is 7. Column 0 and column 6 contain exactly 1 node each. Columns 1 to 5 contain ri(2, 4) nodes each, that is 2, 3, or 4 nodes (core.js:253 to 255).
- Node ids are the string col, a hyphen, and the row index within the column; each node records col, row, n (the column size), y set to (row plus 0.5) divided by n, type, and done false (core.js:263).
- Initial type assignment, before any guarantee pass: column 0 is battle in sector 1 and shipyard in sectors 2 and 3; column 6 is boss; column 5 is a uniform pick of shipyard, drydock, or field; columns 1 to 4 roll rnd() with battle below 0.48, elite below 0.61, event below 0.78, shipyard below 0.90, and drydock otherwise; in column 1 an elite or drydock roll is converted to battle (core.js:259 to 262).
- Pass order matters. genSector applies its fixups in exactly this order: (1) elite guarantee, (2) middle shipyard and rest stop checks, (3) budgets loop, (4) field conversion, (5) encounter assignment, (6) node reward assignment, (7) special encounter guarantee and neighbour dedup, (8) edge building, (9) archetype and node modifier assignment, (10) sector condition, (11) region and biome (core.js:268 to 295). Later passes can overwrite earlier guarantee results where noted below.
- Elite guarantee (pass 1): the target column is cols[COLS minus 3], that is column 4, the column before the rest stop column. If no node in column 4 is elite, the first battle node in column order is converted to elite, or if there is no battle node, the first event node is converted to elite (core.js:267 to 268).
- Elite guarantee defect: if column 4 rolled only rest nodes (shipyard and drydock, the only other types possible at this stage besides battle, elite, and event), both find calls return undefined, the if(b) guard fails, and the pass does nothing (core.js:268, core.js:262). The guarantee is therefore not absolute. INFERRED: in that case the later budgets loop will still force at least one battle into column 4 (see below), but it will not force an elite, so a sector can legally have no elite in column 4.
- Middle shipyard check (pass 2a): if column 3 has no shipyard, one uniformly picked node in column 3 is set to shipyard, overwriting whatever type it had, including battle, elite, event, or drydock (core.js:269 to 270).
- Rest stop check (pass 2b): if column 5 has no drydock and no shipyard, node 0 of column 5 is set to drydock, overwriting a field node if the column rolled all field (core.js:271, core.js:261).
- Middle shipyard caveat: the budgets loop runs after pass 2 and can convert rest nodes in column 3 to battle (see below). INFERRED: if column 3 ends pass 2 with more than one rest node, the excess rest conversion pops rest nodes in column order and converts the last ones to battle, so the shipyard forced in pass 2a can itself be converted away if it is the last rest node in column order. The middle shipyard is therefore also not absolute.
- Budgets loop (pass 3): the loop runs for c2 from 1 while c2 is below COLS minus 2, that is columns 1, 2, 3, and 4. Column 0, column 5 (the rest column), and column 6 (boss) are excluded (core.js:272 to 276).
- Budgets loop step A, drydock removal: every drydock node in the column is converted to battle with probability 0.5, otherwise to event (core.js:274). The inner condition c2 below COLS minus 2 is always true inside this loop and is redundant (core.js:274).
- Budgets loop step B, rest scarcity: while the column has more than 1 node of type shipyard or drydock, the last such node in column order is popped from the rest list and converted to battle, and the rest list is recomputed (core.js:275). After this step a budgets column has at most 1 rest node.
- Budgets loop step C, fight guarantee: if the column has no battle and no elite node, candidates are the nodes whose type is neither shipyard nor drydock; one picked candidate is converted to battle, and if there are no candidates (the column is all rest nodes after step B, which in practice means a single rest node column is impossible here because columns 1 to 4 have 2 to 4 nodes, so no candidates means every node is a rest node and step B has already reduced rests to 1, leaving the other nodes as candidates unless the column size is 1) the last node of the column is converted to battle instead (core.js:276). INFERRED: the fallback branch (cand empty) is reachable only if every node in the column is shipyard or drydock after step B has run; step B leaves at most 1 rest node, so for column sizes 2 to 4 the fallback is effectively dead code, and the fight guarantee normally succeeds through the candidate pick.
- Field conversion (pass 4): one column index c3 is rolled with ri(2, COLS minus 3), that is ri(2, 4), so only columns 2, 3, or 4 can be targeted; columns 0, 1, 5, and 6 can never receive this conversion (core.js:277).
- Field conversion candidates: nodes of type event, plus nodes of type battle but only if the column contains more than 1 fight (battle or elite). If the candidate list is empty, no conversion happens and the sector may have no field node from this pass (core.js:277). If a candidate exists, one uniformly picked candidate becomes type field (core.js:277).
- Field nodes therefore have exactly two sources: the column 5 initial roll (pick of shipyard, drydock, field per node) and the pass 4 conversion in columns 2 to 4 (core.js:261, core.js:277). A sector can have zero field nodes. INFERRED from both rules combined.
- Encounter assignment (pass 5): every battle and elite node in every column gets rollEncounter(node type, node col) merged into it, which sets its encounter and shape fields (core.js:278). Boss nodes do not get an encounter here.
- Node reward assignment (pass 6): if RUN exists, every battle, elite, and boss node gets n.rw from rollNodeReward(node type) (core.js:279). The reward rules are in section 6.
- Special encounter guarantee (pass 7): for each column c2 from 1 to COLS minus 2 inclusive in the loop condition (c2 below COLS minus 1, that is columns 1 to 5), the fight nodes (battle or elite) are collected; if there is at least one fight and none of them has an encounter in ENC_SPECIAL, one picked fight has its enc replaced by a picked ENC_SPECIAL entry and its shape replaced by a picked shape from that encounter (core.js:281).
- Neighbour dedup (pass 7, second half): within the same column fight list in column order, if two adjacent fights share the same enc, the later one has its enc replaced by a picked entry from ENC_POOL excluding its current enc, and its shape is re-picked from the new encounter shapes (core.js:282). Note this compares only adjacent entries in the filtered fight list, not adjacent rows.
- Edge building (pass 8): for each adjacent column pair, each node in the left column gets an edge to the nearest node in the right column by absolute y difference; with probability 0.45 it also gets an edge to the second nearest node, but only if that node y difference is below 0.5 (core.js:283 to 288). A reverse pass then guarantees every node in the right column has at least one incoming edge, adding an edge from its nearest left column node if it had none (core.js:288).
- Resulting graph property: every node except column 6 nodes has at least one outgoing edge, and every node except column 0 nodes has at least one incoming edge (core.js:286 to 288). Movement is forward only, one column at a time (section 3).
- Archetype and modifier assignment (pass 9): for each battle and elite node, arch is swarm with probability 0.25, heavy with cumulative probability 0.47, carrier with cumulative probability 0.62, otherwise null; mod is bounty if col is at least 2 and a second roll is below 0.14, otherwise veteran if col is at least 2, the node is a battle (not elite), and that roll is below 0.26, otherwise null (core.js:291). NODE_MODS defines bounty as plus 50 percent credits and 10 percent more enemy ships, and veteran as an elite captain on their largest ship (data.js:598 to 600).
- Sector condition (pass 10): one condition is picked uniformly from SECTOR_CONDS keys excluding any key already in RUN.condsUsed; if all keys are used, the full key list is used again. The picked key is appended to RUN.condsUsed, so a condition never repeats within a run until the table is exhausted (core.js:293). SECTOR_CONDS has 5 entries: ion, dark, wreck, war, scarce (data.js:602 to 607).
- Region and biome (pass 11): region is pickRegion(fac) and every node gets n.map from regionMap(region, col, COLS) and fitEncounter(n); biome is rollBiome(region). Both are wrapped in try and catch and become null on failure (core.js:294 to 295).
- genSector does not assign node seeds, node stock, or event ids. Battle seeds are assigned lazily when the map is first shown (section 3), shipyard stock lazily on first shipyard entry (section 7), and events lazily on event node entry (section 4).

## 3. Map and node flow

- The map screen is mapScreen in campaign_run.js. Entering it sets the global battle object B to null, so any battle state is discarded by showing the map (campaign_run.js:25 to 26).
- Map backfill for older saves, in order: any undone battle or elite node without an encounter gets rollEncounter merged in; if the map has no region, a region and biome are picked and every undone node gets a region map and fitEncounter, and the run is saved; ensureNemesis and ensurePatrons run; any undone battle, elite, or boss node without rw gets rollNodeReward; if nodeSeeds assigned any new seed, the run is saved (campaign_run.js:31 to 34).
- Node seeds: nodeSeeds in campaign3d.js assigns a random 32 bit seed to every undone node of type battle, elite, boss, or event whose seed is null, and returns how many were assigned (campaign3d.js:46). Seeds are therefore assigned at first map display, not in genSector.
- Available nodes: if RUN.pos is null (start of a sector), available nodes are exactly the nodes of column 0. Otherwise available nodes are exactly the targets of edges whose source is RUN.pos (core.js:344 to 347). There is no backward movement, no skipping a column, and no staying in place.
- Only available nodes are clickable on the map; availability is computed from availableNodes and rendered as the avail state (campaign_run.js:26, campaign_run.js:39).
- ACT.enterNode is the single entry point for node resolution. It first rejects any id not in availableNodes, then sets RUN.pos and RUN.curNode to the id and logs the node type and column (campaign_run.js:89 to 92).
- Dispatch by node type in enterNode: battle, elite, and boss go to battle (with the Nemesis and ambush overrides in section 5); event goes to eventScreen; shipyard goes to shipyardScreen; drydock goes to drydockScreen; field goes to fieldRepairs (campaign_run.js:93 to 102).
- completeNode marks the node pointed to by RUN.curNode as done, clears RUN.curNode to null, and saves the run (campaign_run.js:104). If RUN.curNode is null or the id is not found, completeNode only clears and saves (campaign_run.js:104, core.js:349).
- When each node type completes: shipyard completes only on Leave (ACT.leaveYard), not on entry, so the player can enter, browse, use the fleet screen, and return without consuming the node until they leave (campaign_run.js:195). Drydock applies its repair, then completes the node and shows the map under its modal (campaign_run.js:206 to 208). Field repairs applies its repair, completes the node, and shows the map (campaign3d.js:41). Events complete inside ACT.eventChoice after a non-fight choice, or through the battle and reward path for a fight choice (campaign_run.js:380 to 385). Battles complete inside rewardFlow (wins), inside finishBattle retreat handling, or not at all on a loss because the run ends (campaign_run.js:417, campaign3d.js:102 to 104).
- Node types and their map descriptions are fixed in NODE_INFO: battle, elite, shipyard, drydock, field, event, boss (campaign_run.js:2 to 3). There is no node type for a rest node separate from shipyard, drydock, and field; the rest stop concept in genSector is those three types.
- Faction verb panel: the map also hosts the once per sector faction verb button, whose availability and cost rules are part of node flow because its free unlocks come from event flags. verbFor returns FAC_VERBS for the run faction (campaign_run.js:329, data.js:498 to 504). See section 4 for flag writing and section 9 for verb costs.
- verbCanUse: the verb is usable only if RUN.verbUsed is below RUN.sector (so once per sector, tracked as verbUsed set to the current sector number on use), or the verb is free via an event flag; if not free, its credit cost must be payable, its module cost requires a non-empty inventory, and its XP cost requires the flagship captain to have at least that much XP (campaign_run.js:331 to 332, campaign_run.js:335 to 337).
- useVerb order: pay the cost first (credits deducted, first inventory module consumed, or flagship captain XP reduced but not below the XP floor of the captain current rank), then set verbUsed to the current sector, increment RUN.flags for that verb, then apply the verb effect; next-battle verbs are stored in RUN.verbNext instead of applying now (campaign_run.js:335 to 340).

## 4. Events (including the free-exit rule as implemented)

- Event nodes call eventScreen with the node, but the node itself does not determine which event appears. nextEvent picks the event uniformly from EVENTS excluding events barred for the run faction by their not list and excluding the fallback event, and excluding any event id already in RUN.seenEvents; the picked id is appended to RUN.seenEvents before the event is shown (campaign_run.js:99, campaign_run.js:229 to 231).
- No event repeats within a run. When the unseen pool is empty, nextEvent returns the event whose id equals EVENT_FALLBACK, which is quiet, and still appends it to seenEvents, so quiet can be shown repeatedly after exhaustion (campaign_run.js:230 to 231, data.js:422, data.js:418 to 420).
- Faction barred events: beacon is barred for hegemony, seer for ithar, moon and shrine restrictions and others are declared per event in the not field; examples: shrine barred for chorus and deathless, tomb barred for deathless (it has a deathless twin tombhome barred for all other factions), cache barred for corsair, deserters barred for hegemony (data.js:280, data.js:293, data.js:299, data.js:302, data.js:306, data.js:309, data.js:368, data.js:371).
- Faction variants: if an event has a v table and v for the run faction exists, its title and body override the base title and body in the shown event; options are not overridden by v (campaign_run.js:233 to 234).
- The shipped EVENTS table contains 33 events: 11 in the initial array (derelict, beacon, astropath, merc, storm, seer, wright, moon, shrine, deserters, cache) plus 22 added by the later EVENTS.push (tithe, pilot, reactor, squall, broker, bait, spawn, lament, tomb, tombhome, mutiny, convoy, battlefield, scout, defector, dock, well, auction, saboteur, veterans, haven, quiet) (data.js:275 to 311, data.js:334 to 420).
- Blue options: additional options spliced into the first 11 events and options inside the pushed events can carry a need requirement. They render in blue with the requirement text whether or not the requirement is met, and they are disabled when not met (campaign_run.js:236 to 237, data.js:322 to 333).
- needMet is the requirement test. It returns true for no need. Otherwise all present fields must hold: fac equals the run faction (or is a list containing it), kind is present in fleetKit, carrier is present in fleetKit, shields is present in fleetKit, relic is either 1 meaning any relic held or a specific relic id held, mod means the inventory is non-empty, cred means RUN.credits is at least nd.cred, vet means some captain rank is at least nd.vet, flag means RUN.flags for that flag is not null, intel means the count of revealed Nemesis weaknesses is at least nd.intel (campaign_run.js:218 to 224).
- Note the cred need and the credit cost are different mechanisms: need.cred is only a threshold test in needMet; r.cost is the amount actually deducted by applyOutcome (campaign_run.js:218 to 224, campaign_run.js:351).
- eventOptOk, the free-exit rule as implemented: function eventOptOk(o) reads cost as o.r and o.r.cost only when o.r is an object; it returns true exactly when (there is no cost or RUN.credits is at least cost) AND needMet(o.need) is true (campaign_run.js:232).
- Consequences of that definition, each verified against campaign_run.js:232: (a) an option with r as a string (derelictBoard) has no readable cost, so cost is undefined and the cost test passes; (b) an option with r.cost of 0 is falsy, so the cost test passes regardless of credits, the 0 credit case: a 0 cost option is enabled even at 0 credits and deducts nothing distinguishable; (c) damage costs (dmgAll, dmgOne, dmgBig), module costs (payMod), XP costs (payXp), relic costs (payRelic), and Nemesis clock costs (nemClock) are not read by eventOptOk at all, so an option whose only price is hull, a refit, XP, a relic, or clock is enabled whenever its need is met, including when the player cannot meaningfully pay (for example payRelic with zero relics still enables, and applyOutcome then does nothing for that part); (d) a disabled option cannot be clicked in the UI and ACT.eventChoice also re-checks eventOptOk and returns early (campaign_run.js:236 to 237, campaign_run.js:380 to 381).
- Always-enabled free exit definition used for this spec: an option that eventOptOk enables for every fleet state (no cost, no need) and whose outcome applies no damage, no resource loss, and no clock gain. INFERRED as a definition; the underlying option data are cited per event below.
- Events with NO always-enabled free exit, 7 of 33, verified option by option:
- haven (data.js:414 to 417): options are raid (r.fight corsair, which starts an elite battle and does not resolve the event as an exit), tribute (cost 40, nemClock minus 1), and recruit (need fac corsair). There is no leave option at all. haven is therefore a fight-start or pay or faction-gated event, not a free exit event.
- storm (data.js:290 to 292, plus the spliced blue option at data.js:323): options are punch through (dmgAll 0.1), angle shields (need shields, XP gain), and chart around (cost 40). Every universally available option damages the fleet or costs credits.
- reactor (data.js:343 to 347): options are vent (dmgBig 0.2 on the largest ship), rebuild (need mod, payMod), repair drones (need fac deathless, free outcome), and buy parts (cost 45). Only deathless fleets have a free exit here.
- squall (data.js:348 to 351): options are ride it out (dmgAll 0.08), shields up (need shields, XP gain), and go around (cost 35). Same pay-or-suffer shape as storm.
- mutiny (data.js:374 to 377): options are pay a bonus (cost 50), make an example (payXp 20 from the flagship captain), and senior captain talks (need vet 2, that is a captain of rank index 2 or better, with an XP gain). No option is both need free and cost free.
- scout (data.js:386 to 389): options are hunt it down (dmgAll 0.05, nemClock minus 1, intel), torpedo it (need kind torp, no damage), and let it watch (nemClock plus 1). The ignore option is enabled for everyone but advances the Nemesis clock, so it is suffer, not a free exit.
- saboteur (data.js:406 to 409): options are search (dmgOne 0.1, intel), you know his face (need flag defector), and ignore (nemClock plus 2). Same shape as scout, with a larger clock penalty.
- The other 26 events each have at least one option that is enabled in all states and applies no loss, typically an explicit leave, ignore, decline, refuse, or move on option with r as an empty object, or a free beneficial option such as deserters take them in, cache crack it open, defector interrogate, convoy escort, or quiet rest or drill (data.js:276 to 420, passim; each such option has no cost field and no need field, so eventOptOk at campaign_run.js:232 returns true).
- Choosing an option: ACT.eventChoice rejects a missing option or a failed eventOptOk, writes event flags, then either starts a fight or applies the outcome, completes the node, shows the map, and shows the outcome lines in a modal (campaign_run.js:380 to 385).
- Fight options: if o.r.fight is set, the modal closes and battleScreen is called with kind elite, fac set to o.r.fight, col set to the event node col plus 1, node set to the event node id, and eventRelic true. The event node is NOT completed at this point; completion happens through the battle reward path (campaign_run.js:382).
- Event flags: eventFlags sets RUN.flags[event id] to the option key k, or to the option index as a string if there is no k, and then adds each fl counter in the option fl table into RUN.flags (campaign_run.js:240). Later systems read these flags: faction verb free unlocks read RUN.flags for the verb free key, and the saboteur face option reads the defector flag through need.flag (campaign_run.js:330, data.js:408, data.js:498 to 504).
- applyOutcome is the single outcome interpreter, called with o.r (campaign_run.js:347). Its order of operations is fixed: the string derelictBoard case first (60 percent module plus 40 credits, otherwise a random ship takes 25 percent of its maxHp in damage with a floor of 1 hp remaining and 20 credits gained), then in order: cost deduction, cred gain, xp to all captains, xpLow to the lowest XP captain, loseMod, relic gain (or 80 credits if no relic remains), dmgOne, dmgAll, ship gain, upgradeOne, healAll, gamble, mod gain, ambush flag, payMod (consumes the first inventory module), payXp (flagship captain XP reduced but not below current rank floor), payRelic (see below), repairOne, dmgBig, xpTop, modId gain, shipRaw gain (or 40 credits if CP does not allow it), wingPerk, nemIntel, nemClock (campaign_run.js:347 to 378).
- applyOutcome damage floors: dmgOne, dmgAll, and dmgBig all clamp the damaged ship hp to at least 1, so event damage alone never destroys a ship (campaign_run.js:357 to 370).
- applyOutcome ship gains check CP: for r.ship the hull is first mapped through equivHull into the run faction, and if fleet CP plus the hull CP exceeds cpCap the ship is not added and the option cost is refunded by adding r.cost or 0 back; for r.shipRaw there is no equivHull mapping and the fallback is a flat 40 credits (campaign_run.js:359, campaign_run.js:373).
- payRelic at campaign_run.js:368: if RUN.relics is non-empty, it removes the MOST RECENTLY acquired relic, implemented as splice(RUN.relics.length minus 1, 1), that is the last array element, then calls changeFleet with an empty mutation to re-base hp after the relic fx change, and reports that relic name. If no relic is held, payRelic does nothing at all (no fallback cost, no message) (campaign_run.js:368).
- Event outcomes do not call completeNode themselves; the caller in ACT.eventChoice does, after applyOutcome returns (campaign_run.js:383 to 384).

## 5. From node to battle (battle configuration)

- For a battle, elite, or boss node, enterNode computes kind as the node type and fac as nodeFaction(n), then applies two overrides in order (campaign_run.js:93 to 97).
- Nemesis override (first): if nemNodeIs(n) is true, the battle is replaced by the Nemesis fight: battleScreen is called with kind elite, fac set to RUN.nem.fac, the node col and id, nemesis set to nemBattleCfg(), and arch set to the Nemesis faction behaviour fleet archetype if that archetype exists (campaign_run.js:95). nemNodeIs is true only for battle or elite nodes (never boss, shipyard, event, drydock, or field), only while the Nemesis is alive and not yet met this sector, and only if the strike flag is set or the node column is at least map cols length minus 3, that is column 4 or later in a 7 column map (campaign_run.js:283).
- Ambush override (second, only if not a Nemesis node): if RUN.ambush is true and kind is battle (not elite, not boss), kind becomes elite, fac becomes hegemony unless the run faction is hegemony, in which case fac becomes the sector map faction, RUN.ambush is cleared to false, and a toast explains the ambush (campaign_run.js:96). RUN.ambush is set by event outcomes with r.ambush (campaign_run.js:364).
- Normal call: battleScreen is called with exactly kind, fac, col (the node column), and node (the node id) (campaign_run.js:97).
- nodeFaction: for non-battle nodes, and for any node when RUN.foes is missing, the faction is the sector map faction. For battle nodes with foes present, a deterministic hash of the node id plus the sector number is computed; if hash modulo 5 is 0, the faction is RUN.foes[3 plus ((hash shifted right 3) modulo 2)], that is foes[3] or foes[4], otherwise the map faction (core.js:248 to 249). INFERRED: this is the about 1 in 5 rival raider rule stated in the code comment at core.js:248.
- battleScreen itself is in campaign3d.js. Its campaign relevant steps, in order: it resolves the node seed (assigning one if needed, using the node seed directly, or the node seed XOR 0x27d4eb2f for eventRelic fights) and reseeds the rules RNG from seed XOR 0x5bd1e995 before any pre-battle roll, so a reload replays the same fight (campaign3d.js:47 to 50).
- Reinforcement at battle entry: if the fleet has 1 or fewer ships and RUN.reinf is below the current sector, a free warden hull (mapped only as the literal id warden, not through equivHull) is added, RUN.reinf is set to the current sector, and a toast and telemetry event fire. This can happen at most once per sector (campaign3d.js:51).
- Encounter resolution in battleScreen: if the node has no encounter and kind is not boss, rollEncounter is assigned to the node and its region map and fitEncounter are applied if missing. The battle objective and shape come from bossEncounter for boss kind, else from the node encounter if present, else from a fresh rollEncounter(cfg kind, cfg col or 1) (campaign3d.js:52 to 53).
- The final battle config merges defaults under cfg, so cfg fields win: map defaults to the node region map (or the boss faction home map for bosses), deployment defaults to line for bosses and a weighted pick of line, line, or flank otherwise, objective and shape from the encounter resolution, arch and mod taken from the node for non-boss fights and forced null for bosses (campaign3d.js:53).
- The pending faction verb (RUN.verbNext) is copied into the battle config as verb if the config does not already have one (campaign3d.js:54).
- Event fights differ from node fights in three ways, all from campaign_run.js:382 and campaign3d.js:50: kind is always elite, col is the event node col plus 1 (which feeds battle credit and difficulty formulas that read col), and eventRelic is true, which changes only the seed salt in battleScreen.
- What the campaign layer hands to a battle and therefore what a port must preserve: kind, fac, col, node id, seed, map, deployment, objective (enc), shape, arch, mod, verb, nemesis config when present, and eventRelic when present (campaign_run.js:95 to 97, campaign_run.js:382, campaign3d.js:47 to 54). Enemy fleet construction, budgets, and in-battle rules are outside this section.

## 6. Reward flow after battle

- Battle exit is finishBattle in campaign3d.js. It runs once per battle (guarded by B.finished), converts a loss with a withdraw option and survivors into a retreat, builds the summary object, settles ship hp, XP, losses, prizes, and wrecks, and then branches: loss or empty fleet goes to endRun(false), retreat goes to completeNode plus map, win goes to rewardFlow (campaign3d.js:66 to 68, campaign3d.js:80 to 104).
- Ship hp settlement on battle exit: each surviving player ship hp is set to its in-battle hp clamped between 1 and derived maxHp. On a retreat each such ship then loses a further 10 percent of its in-battle maxHp, floored at 1 hp. On a win each ship gains battle XP (see section 12) (campaign3d.js:84 to 86).
- Post-win patch (battle exit, before rewardFlow): on a win, every ship in RUN.fleet heals 15 percent of its derived maxHp, capped at maxHp (campaign3d.js:88). This heal is NOT multiplied by repairMult (see section 7).
- Pyrrhic win: if the result is a win and the fleet is empty, a free cheap hull for the run faction is added and the flagship is re-designated before the loss check, so the run continues (campaign3d.js:101).
- rewardFlow is only called for wins (campaign3d.js:104). Its order of operations is: (1) increment run stats, (2) compute and bank battle credits, (3) apply the salvage relic effect if present, (4) resolve the node reward kind, (5) build the saved reward chain state and complete the node, (6) show the victory modal with the chain entry button (campaign_run.js:396 to 419).
- Stats step: RUN.stats.battles increments by 1 for every rewarded battle; elites also increment RUN.stats.elites and bosses also increment RUN.stats.bosses (campaign_run.js:397 to 398). Note battles here counts wins that reach rewardFlow, because rewardFlow is win only. INFERRED from campaign3d.js:104.
- Battle credits formula: credits equal round((25 plus 12 times sector plus 3 times cfg.col or 0) times kindMult times (1 plus runFx credPct plus sector condition cred or 0)), where kindMult is 3 for boss, 1.6 for elite, and 1 otherwise. If cfg.mod is bounty, credits are then multiplied by 1.5 and rounded again. If summary.partial is true (objective escaped), credits are halved and rounded (campaign_run.js:399 to 401).
- runFx credPct sources that feed that formula: relic ledger gives credPct 0.25, and the Legacy unlock salvagers gives credPct plus 0.2, summed in runFx (core.js:196, data.js:194, data.js:251). Sector condition cred values: wreck 0.15, war 0.2, scarce 0.25, others 0 (data.js:602 to 607).
- Salvage relic step: if runFx has salvage (the Scrap rig relic), credits gain a flat plus 15 and every ship heals 8 percent of its derived maxHp, capped at maxHp (campaign_run.js:402, data.js:197). This heal is NOT multiplied by repairMult.
- Node reward resolution: the node is found from cfg.node; its rw is used if present. If the node is missing or has no rw, a fallback rw is built: for kind battle a boon reward from a picked patron with no minimum rarity, for any other kind a boon reward from a picked patron with min 1 (campaign_run.js:405).
- Node reward kinds from rollNodeReward (assigned in genSector): elite and boss always return a boon reward with a picked patron and min 1. Other battle nodes roll rnd(): boon with a picked patron below 0.62, mod below 0.78, cred below 0.90, otherwise repair (core.js:191).
- Immediate node reward effects in rewardFlow: cred kind adds 35 plus 10 times sector credits immediately. Repair kind heals every ship by 25 percent of derived maxHp times repairMult, capped at maxHp. Boon and mod kinds have no immediate effect; they are resolved in the pick stage of the chain (campaign_run.js:407 to 408).
- Relic choice pool is fixed at rewardFlow time: for elite or boss, a shuffled list of relic ids not already held and passing relicUseful, truncated to 3 for boss or 2 for elite; for ordinary battles, an empty list (campaign_run.js:410). relicUseful excludes only the lattice relic for a shieldless fleet (core.js:239).
- Wrecks are fixed at rewardFlow time: summary.wrecks (up to 3 enemy wrecks chosen in finishBattle by highest CP) each get salvageYield attached (campaign3d.js:95, campaign_run.js:413). Salvage rules are in section 8.
- Prizes are fixed at rewardFlow time: summary.prizes filtered to entries whose tpl exists in HULLS (campaign_run.js:416). Prize rules are in section 8.
- completeNode timing inside rewardFlow (key rule): RUN.reward is constructed with stage set to prize if there are prizes, else salvage if there are wrecks, else pick, and completeNode() is called on the same expression line, BEFORE any reward pick is made and before the victory modal is shown (campaign_run.js:414 to 417). Consequences: the node cannot be replayed for double pay after a reload, the battle credits and immediate node effects above are already banked at that point, and a reload resumes only the unmade picks.
- The victory modal shows kills, rounds, banked credits, lost ships, the immediate node effect line if any, flagship transfer notice if ensureFlagship moved the flag during rewardFlow, and XP lines, then one primary button whose target depends on the chain stage: prizeStep if prizes exist, salvageStep if wrecks exist, rewardNext for a boon or mod pick, otherwise rewardNext as Continue (campaign_run.js:405 to 406, campaign_run.js:418 to 420).
- Chain stage order after the victory modal is strictly: prize, then salvage, then pick (boon or mod), then relic, then promo (promotions), then afterBattle (campaign_run.js:417, campaign_run.js:452, campaign_run.js:480 to 485, campaign_run.js:508 to 523).
- Pick stage, boon branch: rewardNext calls boonStep for rw kind boon. boonStep uses the saved RUN.reward.boonOffer if present, otherwise rolls rollBoonOffer(patron, min) once and saves it into RUN.reward before showing it (campaign_run.js:481 to 482, campaign_run.js:488).
- rollBoonOffer rules: the pool is the patron non-legendary boons not already held, shuffled, with not-useful boons (per BOON_NEED against fleetKit) separated as junk and excluded from the first slice; the first 3 useful boons are offered, each with rarity from rollRarity(min, sector, patron). A qualifying duo boon can replace the third slot or be appended: duos require the duo to include this patron, the player to already hold a boon from the other duo patron, and the duo to be useful; insertion happens if the offer has fewer than 3 entries or with probability 0.45. If the offer still has fewer than 3, it is backfilled first from other in-range patrons useful boons (with rarity rolled WITHOUT the patron argument, so without that patron favour bonus; this is the shipped off-patron fill behaviour), then from this patron junk pool. Finally, legendaryFor can overwrite the third slot or append the patron legendary at rarity index 3 (core.js:181 to 189).
- rollRarity formula: rare threshold is 0.24 plus 0.06 times (sector minus 1) plus 0.12 if the patron favour tier is at least 1; epic threshold is 0.06 plus 0.03 times (sector minus 1) plus 0.03 if favour tier is at least 1. A roll below epic gives rarity 2, below epic plus rare gives rarity 1, otherwise 0. If favour tier is at least 3 the result is raised to at least 1. The result is then raised to at least minR (core.js:177).
- Boon reroll and decline (pick stage only): decline pays 30 plus 10 times sector credits and proceeds to relic. Reroll costs 25 plus 15 times RUN.boonRerolls, is allowed once per reward chain (RUN.reward.rerolled), requires credits to cover the cost, increments the run reroll counter, and re-rolls the offer up to 4 extra times while the new offer ids are all contained in the old offer ids (campaign_run.js:494 to 503).
- Taking a boon: the boon is pushed as an object with id and rarity r, inside changeFleet; favour is then increased for the boon patron (or both duo patrons) by 1 plus r each; then the chain proceeds to relic (campaign_run.js:504 to 507). Favour details are in section 11.
- Empty boon offer: if rollBoonOffer returns an empty list, the player gets a flat 60 credits and the chain proceeds to relic (campaign_run.js:489).
- Pick stage, mod branch: rewardNext for rw kind mod offers 3 modules from a shuffled modulePool, using the saved RUN.reward.modOffer if present and saving the rolled offer before showing it if not. Taking one pushes it to inventory and proceeds to relic (campaign_run.js:483 to 486). For rw kinds other than boon and mod (cred and repair, already paid immediately), rewardNext goes straight to relic (campaign_run.js:481 to 485).
- Relic stage: relicStep first normalises the saved stage to relic (clearing saved boon and mod offers) and saves, then if the fixed relic choice list is non-empty it shows a pick 1 modal and waits; taking a relic pushes it inside changeFleet, clears the saved relic list, and proceeds to promotions. If the list is empty it proceeds directly to promotions (campaign_run.js:508 to 518).
- Promotions stage: promotionsStep sets the saved stage to promo, clears saved relics, and saves; then if any captain has pending promotions it opens promoteModal for the first such ship and loops back to promotionsStep after each pick, otherwise it calls afterBattle (campaign_run.js:519 to 523). Promotion mechanics are in section 12.
- resumeReward (reload recovery): Continue calls resumeReward if RUN.reward exists. It restores the window reward state from the saved reward (kind, node, rw, relics, wrecks, boonOffer, modOffer), then dispatches by saved stage: prize with prizes present goes to prizeStep; salvage with wrecks present goes to salvageStep; pick goes to rewardNext; relic goes to relicStep; ANY other stage value falls through to promotionsStep and returns true (campaign_run.js:525 to 529, campaign_meta.js:42 to 43).
- resumeReward defect for unrecognised stages: there is no validation of R.stage in resumeReward itself, so an unrecognised stage string behaves exactly like promo (promotions, then afterBattle). A second, earlier guard exists on load: repairRunIds in core.js rewrites a saved reward whose stage is not one of prize, salvage, pick, relic, promo to promo before resumeReward can see it (core.js reward repair block in repairRunIds). The net shipped behaviour for an unrecognised saved stage id is therefore forced to promo in both layers: load repair forces promo, and even without it resumeReward would fall through to promotionsStep (campaign_run.js:529).
- afterBattle (chain exit): afterBattle clears RUN.reward to null and calls completeNode again (a no-op for the already completed battle node, because RUN.curNode was already cleared by the first completeNode, unless a new curNode was set in between), then branches on kind (campaign_run.js:530 to 532).
- afterBattle, boss branch: if sector is at least 3, endRun(true) is called (victory). Otherwise sector increments by 1, cpCap increases by 3, a new sector map is generated with genSector(new sector), RUN.pos resets to null, the Nemesis adapts once (nemAdapt), every ship heals 40 percent of derived maxHp times repairMult (the transit patch, capped at maxHp), the run is saved, the map is shown, and a sector intro modal is displayed (campaign_run.js:533 to 540).
- afterBattle, non-boss branch: the map is shown and nothing else changes (campaign_run.js:542 to 543).
- Retreat branch (no rewardFlow): a retreat completes the node, shows the map, and pays no battle credits, no node reward, no relic, no promotions chain, and no XP (campaign3d.js:85, campaign3d.js:103). Loss branch: endRun(false) with no rewards (campaign3d.js:102).

## 7. Regroup: repairs, shipyard, refits, decommission

- Regroup is not a single screen in this build. Between battles the player regroups through some combination of: a shipyard node, a drydock node, a field node, the fleet screen (refits and promotions, reachable from the map and from a shipyard), event and reward heals, and the automatic heals on battle win and on sector transit (campaign_run.js:89 to 104, campaign_run.js:127 to 137). Sections below give each mechanism its exact numbers.
- Shipyard entry does not complete the node (section 3). Leaving does (campaign_run.js:195).
- Shipyard stock is per node and persistent for that node: shipyardStock returns n.stock if it exists, otherwise it builds and stores it, so re-entering the same shipyard in the same sector shows the same stock minus sold modules (campaign_run.js:156 to 164).
- Stock hulls: the base list is warden, harrow, penitent, plus reliquary if META.unlocked.u_carrier, plus cathedral if META.unlocked.u_battleship, plus ascendant if META.unlocked.u_dread. Each is mapped through equivHull into the run faction and deduplicated, then 3 are picked by shuffle and sorted by CP ascending. Unlocked hull variants whose base is a stocked hull are inserted immediately after that base hull, so a yard can show more than 3 hull cards (campaign_run.js:157 to 163).
- Stock modules: 3 modules from a shuffled modulePool, stored with a sold list. modulePool is kind-fit filtered (see the guard defect below) (campaign_run.js:163, core.js:234).
- Shipyard prices all pass through priceMult: priceMult equals (0.85 if META.unlocked.wrights else 1) times (1.25 if Heat h_coffers is on else 1) times (sector condition price or 1). The only sector condition with a price value is scarce at 1.2 (core.js:227, data.js:607, data.js:613).
- Hull purchase: cost is round(HULLS cost times priceMult). The buy button is disabled unless credits cover the cost, fleet CP plus hull CP is at most cpCap, and the hull is not a unique hull already in the fleet. ACT.buyHull re-checks credits and CP, deducts, creates the ship with newShip (a fresh rank 0 captain unless a reserve captain is waiting, see section 12), and appends it to the fleet (campaign_run.js:169 to 171, campaign_run.js:196).
- Module purchase: cost is round(MODULES cost times priceMult). A stocked module can be bought once; buying marks its stock index sold and pushes the module to inventory (campaign_run.js:172 to 173, campaign_run.js:197).
- Paid repair, per ship: missing hull is derived maxHp minus hp. The price is ceil(missing divided by 2 times priceMult), displayed as "Repair (missing) for (price)", and paying sets that ship hp to full derived maxHp (not a partial repair). The button is disabled at zero missing or insufficient credits (campaign_run.js:174 to 176, campaign_run.js:198 in ACT.repair; the handler sets hp to derive max).
- Paid repair, whole fleet: total missing is summed over the fleet, price is ceil(total missing divided by 2 times priceMult), and paying sets every ship to full (campaign_run.js:179, campaign_run.js:199 in ACT.repairAll).
- Paid repair does NOT pass through repairMult at any point. Both repair price formulas use priceMult only (campaign_run.js:174, campaign_run.js:179). This is the paid side of the Argon claim verified below.
- Mark upgrade at a shipyard: a ship below Mk 3 can be upgraded for mkCost, which is round((40 times hull CP times current Mk plus 30) times priceMult). Paying increments Mk inside changeShip, so hp is re-based by the maxHp delta and clamped (campaign_run.js:177, campaign_run.js:200 in ACT.upgradeMk, core.js:230, core.js:224). Mk effects in derive: maxHp and shield values scale by 1 plus 0.2 per Mk above 1, and damage scales by 1 plus 0.15 per Mk above 1 (core.js:208 to 213).
- Class refits at a shipyard: for each distinct hull class in the current fleet, one track button is shown. Track level is RUN.classTrack for that class, capped at CLASS_TRACK_MAX 3. Cost is round((60 plus 50 times current level) times priceMult). Buying increments the track inside changeFleet. The effect in derive is plus 6 percent hull and plus 6 percent damage per track level for every ship of that class, owned now or later (campaign_run.js:188 to 189, core.js:220 to 223, core.js:208 to 213).
- Decommission (sell a ship) at a shipyard: the button is disabled when the fleet has 1 ship. Its label is computed at shipyard render time as sell equals round(HULLS[s.tpl].cost times 0.35) (campaign_run.js:174, campaign_run.js:178).
- Decommission label defect (NaN): the label formula at campaign_run.js:174 reads HULLS[s.tpl].cost directly with no fallback. For a hull with no cost field, cost is undefined, undefined times 0.35 is NaN, and round(NaN) is NaN, so the button reads "Decommission (+NaN)". Which hulls lack cost: data.js gives explicit cost only to the 6 voidsworn player hulls; all other roster hulls get a computed cost in the FAC_PRICE loop at data.js:543 to 546, but only if they are in a faction roster list; boss hulls, squadron hulls, and any hull not in a roster and without explicit cost keep cost undefined (data.js:59 to 104 for explicit costs, data.js:543 to 546 for the computed fill). INFERRED: a captured or event gained hull of a non-roster type is the realistic path to seeing the NaN label.
- Decommission actual payout differs from the label: the confirmation modal and ACT.sellConfirm both use prizeScrap(s.tpl), which is round(shipValue(tpl) times 0.35), and shipValue falls back to 40 times hull CP when cost is not a finite number, so the actual payout is never NaN (campaign_run.js:201 to 203, core.js:193 to 194). The defect is therefore label only: the amount actually credited is the fallback based amount, not NaN and not the label value.
- Decommission effects on confirm: the ship mods are pushed back into inventory, prizeScrap credits are added, the ship is spliced out of the fleet, and the shipyard re-renders. The captain is not added to fallen or reserve; the modal text says the captain retires (campaign_run.js:203). Note decommission does not call ensureFlagship or changeFleet, so if the decommissioned ship was the flagship, flagUid dangles until the next ensureFlagship call (for example on the map reward path or Continue). INFERRED from the absence of such a call in ACT.sellConfirm at campaign_run.js:203 and the repair logic in core.js:195.
- Refit management (fleet screen): the fleet screen lists inventory modules with, for each module, the list of ships it can be fitted to. Fitted modules can be removed back to inventory at no cost, and fitting is also free; both go through changeShip so hp re-bases (campaign_run.js:127 to 142).
- Module install guard defect at campaign_run.js:128: the compatible ship list for an inventory module is filtered by exactly two conditions: the ship has a free slot (s.mods.length below HULLS slots), and (the module has no only field OR the ship hull ability equals MODULES[m].only). It keys on ability alone. There is no weapon-kind check and no MOD_NEED check in this filter (campaign_run.js:128).
- The only module with an only field is hangar, with only launch, so the guard at campaign_run.js:128 in practice only restricts hangar expansions to carriers (data.js:166 in MODULES, data.js:259 for MOD_NEED).
- Where kind-fit IS checked: MOD_NEED kind-fit is enforced at pool generation time, not at install time. modulePool filters out any module whose MOD_NEED kind is absent from fleetKit, unless fewer than 3 modules would remain, in which case the full module list is returned (core.js:233 to 234, data.js:259). modulePool feeds shipyard stock, randomModule (event gains, salvage yields, boon-adjacent module gains), and the reward mod offer (campaign_run.js:163, core.js:235, campaign_run.js:483). MOD_NEED mapping: loaders needs batt, crystal needs lance, magazine needs torp, flakplus needs flak, capacitor needs shields, regen needs shields, hangar needs carrier (data.js:259).
- Consequence of the guard defect: a kind-mismatched module can still reach a ship through any path that bypasses modulePool or after the fleet changes. Concrete shipped paths: a module gained while its kind was present stays in inventory after the last ship with that kind is lost or decommissioned, and campaign_run.js:128 will then happily fit it to any ship with a free slot; the alternate fleet plan pushes the hangar module directly onto a carrier without any pool check (core.js:332); and ACT.equip itself re-checks nothing beyond the fleetScreen filter, it just pushes the module (campaign_run.js:140 to 141). INFERRED as behaviour composition of those cited lines. The module fx then applies through derive regardless of whether the ship has the relevant weapons (core.js:207), so for example a crystal on a lanceless ship applies its fx object to derive, where lancePct simply has no lance weapon to scale.
- Drydock node: on entry it immediately heals each ship by ceil(missing times 0.6 times repairMult), where missing is derived maxHp minus hp, with no cap check beyond adding to current hp (in practice derive based maxHp bounds it because missing is computed from the same max), then completes the node and shows the map with a summary modal (campaign_run.js:206 to 208). Note the drydock heal is a fraction of MISSING hull, while field, transit, and reward repair heals are fractions of MAX hull.
- Field node: on entry every ship heals round(maxHp times 0.2 times repairMult), capped at maxHp, then the node completes (campaign3d.js:41, campaign_run.js:2 to 3 for the node description).
- Free healing stack, complete enumeration of no-cost hull repair sources at the campaign layer, with repairMult treatment for each:
- 1. Post-win patch: 15 percent of maxHp to every ship, in finishBattle on a win. Does NOT use repairMult (campaign3d.js:88).
- 2. Transit patch: 40 percent of maxHp to every ship, in afterBattle on a boss win that advances the sector. Uses repairMult (campaign_run.js:536).
- 3. Field node: 20 percent of maxHp to every ship. Uses repairMult (campaign3d.js:41).
- 4. Drydock node: 60 percent of missing hull to every ship. Uses repairMult (campaign_run.js:207).
- 5. Node reward repair: 25 percent of maxHp to every ship, when the won node rw kind is repair. Uses repairMult (campaign_run.js:408).
- 6. Salvage relic (Scrap rig) battle reward: 8 percent of maxHp to every ship plus 15 credits, in rewardFlow when runFx.salvage is present. Does NOT use repairMult (campaign_run.js:402, data.js:197).
- 7. Event healAll outcomes: healAll fraction of maxHp to every ship (shrine offer 0.3, shrine relic offering 1.0, spawn feed 0.3 for chorus, quiet rest 0.1). Does NOT use repairMult (campaign_run.js:361, data.js:302 to 304, data.js:360 to 363, data.js:418 to 420).
- 8. Event gamble (shrine): 50 percent chance of 30 percent of maxHp to every ship, otherwise nothing. Does NOT use repairMult (campaign_run.js:362).
- 9. Event single ship heals: upgradeOne fully repairs the most damaged ship (and upgrades its Mk), repairOne fully repairs the most damaged ship (or the second most damaged when upgradeOne is also present). Full repairs, so repairMult is not applied and would have no effect on the amount (campaign_run.js:360, campaign_run.js:369).
- 10. Faction verb gorge (chorus): 20 percent of maxHp to every ship, or 30 percent if the spawn feed flag is set. It costs a module from the hold unless free conditions apply, so it is not strictly no-cost; it is listed here because it is not a paid credit repair. Does NOT use repairMult (campaign_run.js:340, data.js:502).
- 11. Nemesis raid warning aside: no heal. Not a source.
- repairMult definition: repairMult equals (0.5 if Heat h_repair is on else 1) times (sector condition repair or 1). The only sector condition with a repair value is scarce at 0.75, so repairMult is one of 1, 0.75, 0.5, or 0.375 in normal play (core.js:229, data.js:607, data.js:614).
- Verification of the Argon leg claim (repairMult scales FREE healing at four call sites while the paid shipyard price never passes through it): CONFIRMED in its narrow form and REFUTED in its broad form. Narrow form true: repairMult has exactly 4 call sites in the campaign layer, and all 4 are free heals: field node (campaign3d.js:41), drydock (campaign_run.js:207), node reward repair (campaign_run.js:408), and transit patch (campaign_run.js:536); a grep of the shipped JS finds no other repairMult call sites. The paid shipyard repair price uses priceMult only, never repairMult (campaign_run.js:174, campaign_run.js:179, core.js:227, core.js:229). Broad form false if read as all free healing scales: the post-win patch (campaign3d.js:88), the salvage relic heal (campaign_run.js:402), all event healAll and gamble heals (campaign_run.js:361 to 362), and verb gorge (campaign_run.js:340) are free heals that do NOT pass through repairMult, so Heat h_repair and the scarce condition repair factor leave them at full strength.
- Practical consequence for a port: h_repair (Short supplies, "every repair restores half as much", data.js:614) and scarce repair 0.75 must be applied only at those 4 call sites to match shipped behaviour, not globally. INFERRED as a porting instruction from the call site list above.

## 8. Capture outcomes

- Captures are created in battle (boarding) and settled in two places: finishBattle builds summary.prizes, and the reward chain prize stage lets the player dispose of each prize (campaign3d.js:89 to 91, campaign_run.js:452 to 470).
- Prize eligibility in finishBattle: a unit counts as a prize only if it was captured, is on the player side at battle end, is not dead or has exited, is not a ship unit, is not a squadron, and its hull exists and is not a boss hull and the unit is not flagged boss, so a sector flagship can never become a prize (campaign3d.js:89).
- Full fleet auto-scrap in finishBattle: if fleet CP plus the prize hull CP exceeds cpCap at battle end, the prize never reaches the reward screen; it is immediately scrapped for prizeScrap(tpl) credits, recorded in summary.scrapped, and the total is toasted after rewardFlow (campaign3d.js:90, core.js:194). Note this test uses the fleet CP at battle end, before any prize is added.
- Prize record fields: tpl, name, hpf (remaining hull fraction clamped between 0.05 and 1), fac, weapon kinds list, ability, regen flag, shMax, and cp (campaign3d.js:91).
- Corsair capture bonus: if the run faction is corsair and there is at least one prize, the FIRST prize also yields an immediate bonus before the reward screen: a module from that prize salvage yield (or a random module if the yield has none) is pushed to inventory and 20 credits are added. This is in addition to the normal prize disposition of the same ship (campaign3d.js:93).
- Prize disposition screen (prizeStep and prizePlan): all prizes of the battle are shown on ONE screen, each with three choices (Add, Strip, Ransom), a computed default per prize, and one Confirm for the whole set. Only player changed picks are stored in RUN.reward.prizeSel, so a reload rebuilds the same screen and recomputes the same defaults (campaign_run.js:447 to 461).
- Values: shipValue(tpl) is HULLS cost if finite, otherwise 40 times hull CP (core.js:193). Ransom equals round(shipValue times 0.55 times (0.6 plus 0.4 times hpf) times max(0.4, 0.85 to the power of ransoms already taken)). Strip equals a module (from the prize salvage yield, or a deterministic fallback pick from modulePool indexed by shipValue and CP) plus round(shipValue times 0.15) credits. Scrap for a full fleet (above) equals round(shipValue times 0.35) (campaign_run.js:440 to 443, core.js:194).
- Ransom decay is per run, not per battle: the exponent uses RUN.ransoms, which increments by 1 for each ransomed prize at confirm time and is never reset inside a run, with a floor factor of 0.4 (campaign_run.js:440, campaign_run.js:468).
- Default choice per prize, computed in prizePlan in battle order: Add if the prize fits (CP room remaining after earlier Adds in this same plan, and no faction gate) and hpf is at least 0.4; otherwise Strip if strip value (strip credits plus half the module shop price, or strip credits alone if no module) is at least the ransom value, otherwise Ransom (campaign_run.js:447 to 451).
- Faction gate on Add: prizePlan computes room as CP room AND not factionGate(run fac, tpl). factionGate returns a block only for factions with FAC_GATES entries: corsair cannot field any hull carrying a lance kind weapon; ithar has no prize gate (its gate is noBrace, which factionGate does not test) (campaign_run.js:448, data.js:541 to 542). A gated prize can only be stripped or ransomed; its Add button is disabled with the gate text (campaign_run.js:460).
- Confirm (prizeConfirm) processes prizes in plan order: Add creates a new ship of that tpl, keeps the prize name, and sets hp to the hpf fraction of its derived maxHp clamped to at least 1 (its captain is a fresh captain from newShip, not the enemy captain); Strip pushes the strip module if any and adds strip credits; Ransom adds ransom credits and increments RUN.ransoms. The saved prize list is then cleared and the chain advances to salvage (campaign_run.js:464 to 470).
- A player pick of Add that no longer fits when the plan is recomputed is silently downgraded in prizePlan to the default logic result (strip or ransom by value) (campaign_run.js:450).
- There is no upkeep, fee, or later cost for a kept prize (campaign_run.js:464 to 470, by absence of any charge in the confirm path).
- Salvage (wrecks) is a separate system from prizes: finishBattle selects up to 3 enemy wrecks (dead, not fled, not squadrons, not ship units, not captured), sorted by hull CP descending (campaign3d.js:95).
- salvageYield for a wreck: a module is offered only if the wreck is intact (its kill cause has no scrap flag in KILL_CAUSES) and a weapon-matched module exists in modulePool; the match priority is the first applicable of crystal (lance), magazine (torp kinds), loaders (battery kinds), flakplus (flak), hangar (launch ability), cogitator (gauss, needle, or shard), damcon (regen), capacitor (sh at least 20), auspex (turret or autocannon). Scrap credits equal round((10 plus 9 times wreck CP) times (1 if intact else 1.3)) (campaign_run.js:427 to 432).
- Tow option: a wreck can be towed only if the run faction is corsair, wreck CP is at most 2, the wreck is intact, its kill cause is not dark, its hull exists, there is no faction gate, and CP room exists at yield time (campaign_run.js:433 in salvageYield tow computation).
- Salvage screen rule: the player works exactly ONE wreck. takeSalvage applies the chosen yield for the clicked wreck (module to inventory, or scrap credits, or tow: the wreck hull joins the fleet at 35 percent of maxHp with its wreck name and a fresh captain), then clears ALL wrecks and advances the chain to the pick stage, discarding the other wrecks (campaign_run.js:471 to 480).
- Tow CP re-check at take time: if CP no longer allows the tow when clicked, the wreck is scrapped for its scrap credits instead (campaign_run.js:477).

## 9. Economy: credit sources and sinks

- Credit balance is RUN.credits, a single integer pool per run. It starts at 120 plus War Chest bonuses (core.js:324 to 326). There is no second currency inside a run; renown is the meta currency (section 10).
- Credit sources, battle: the rewardFlow battle credit formula in section 6 (base 25 plus 12 per sector plus 3 per node column, times kind multiplier, times credit percent bonuses, bounty and partial adjustments) (campaign_run.js:399 to 401).
- Credit sources, node reward: a cred node reward pays 35 plus 10 per sector on a win (campaign_run.js:407).
- Credit sources, salvage relic: plus 15 flat per rewarded battle while the Scrap rig relic is held (campaign_run.js:402).
- Credit sources, prizes and wrecks: ransom, strip, and full fleet scrap values in section 8; wreck scrap values in section 8 (campaign_run.js:440 to 443, campaign_run.js:427 to 432).
- Credit sources, decommission: prizeScrap of the decommissioned hull (section 7) (core.js:194, campaign_run.js:203).

- Battle XP award (win only): in finishBattle, each surviving player ship gains XP equal to min(60, round(8 plus dealt divided by 4 plus kills times 12)), where dealt and kills are that ship in-battle totals. No battle XP is awarded on a loss or a retreat (campaign3d.js:86).
- Event and verb XP awards go through the same addXp (so the relic xpPct scaler applies): event xp gives the amount to every captain, xpLow to the lowest XP captain, xpTop to the highest XP captain (campaign_run.js:353 to 354, campaign_run.js:371).

The offer is generated once and stored on cap.offer: a shuffle of perk ids the captain does not already have, excluding the wing perk unless the ship hull ability is launch, truncated to 2 (campaign_run.js:144 to 149).
- Taking a perk: the perk is pushed to cap.perks inside changeShip, pending decrements by 1, cap.offer is cleared to null (so the next pending promotion generates a fresh offer), the modal closes, and if pending is still above 0 promoteModal runs again for the same ship (campaign_run.js:151 to 153).
- Promotion timing: pending promotions are collected during the reward chain promotions stage (one ship at a time, first ship in fleet order with pending above 0, looping until none remain), and can also be opened at any time from the fleet screen via the promote button on a ship card (campaign_run.js:519 to 523, campaign_run.js:122, campaign_run.js:143).

## 13. Run end and debrief

- A run ends in exactly three ways: victory (sector 3 boss beaten, through afterBattle calling endRun(true)), defeat (finishBattle loss branch or empty fleet calling endRun(false)), and abandonment (starting a new run over a saved run, calling endRun(false, true)) (campaign_run.js:534, campaign3d.js:102, campaign_meta.js:56).
- Defeat by retreat is impossible: a retreat completes the node and returns to the map, and a loss that the battle layer converts to a withdraw-with-survivors result becomes a retreat in finishBattle before the loss branch is tested (campaign3d.js:68, campaign3d.js:102 to 103).
- endRun order of operations, in exact sequence: (1) update META.stats (best sector, wins, wonFacs), (2) append RUN.fallen to META.fallen truncated to the last 80, (3) log the run_end telemetry event, (4) compute and bank renown with the Heat multiplier, (5) compute and bank admiral mastery, (6) update heatBest on a Heat victory, (7) store RUN.carry with renown gained, mastery gained, mastery levels before and after, and Heat level, (8) set RUN.over true, clear SAVED_RUN, and persist, (9) build and show the debrief screen, (10) set the global RUN to null (campaign_meta.js:188 to 215).

- Renown banked at endRun is the only renown income in the game, and it is banked for defeats and abandonments as well as victories, using the runRenown formula and Heat multiplier in section 10 (campaign_meta.js:194, campaign3d.js:30). An abandoned run therefore still pays out for the battles already won in it. INFERRED from campaign_meta.js:56 calling the same endRun with no formula change.
- Mastery banked at endRun uses the mxGain formula in section 11, for defeats and abandonments as well (campaign_meta.js:195).
- Favour is NOT banked at endRun; it was already written incrementally at each boon take (section 11) (campaign_run.js:505, campaign_meta.js:188 to 198 by absence).
- Debrief screen content: a victory, abandoned, or lost title (victory titles are per faction in WIN_TITLE), a subtitle, the carried-forward panel (renown gained and new total with Heat annotation, mastery gained with level-up notice or progress to next level), the run recap tiles, the standout ship, the surviving fleet list, the captains lost list if any, and either a victory summary (win count and factions won with) or a best sector line, plus on victory a suggested next faction panel (campaign_meta.js:178 to 213).

- Standout ship: among surviving ships with hp above 0, the ship with the highest score, where score is in-battle career dealt plus kills times 40, is marked as standout if its score is above 0. The surviving fleet list is sorted by the same score (campaign_meta.js:182 to 185). Career dealt, kills, and battles are accumulated per ship in finishBattle (campaign3d.js:81).

- After the debrief, the only exits are a new run (with or without the suggested faction) or the title screen; RUN is null, so no map, fleet, or reward state remains reachable (campaign_meta.js:213 to 214).
- What persists after a run ends, complete list: META.renown, META.mastery, META.favour (written during the run), META.variants (including any arc variant granted during the run), META.unlocked and META.admirals (unchanged by endRun), META.stats, META.fallen, META.heatBest, and META.opts. ## 6. The fx pool: sumFx and derive (stacking semantics)

- sumFx(list) is the only aggregator for the derive fx pool: it creates an empty object, skips falsy entries, and for every key in each fx object adds the numeric value to the running total (core.js:171). There is no cap, no floor, no deduplication, no per-key maximum, and no ordering effect, because addition is the only operation (core.js:171).
- derive(ship) builds the pool in this exact list order: fleetFxList() first, then the ship module fx in ship.mods order, then the ship captain perk fx in cap.perks order, then the aspect fx array if applicable (core.js:207). Order does not change totals, per the line above, except where a later derive formula reads a summed key (core.js:171, core.js:207).
- fleetFxList() order: for each relic in RUN.relics, the relic fx object if RELICS[r] exists, otherwise unknownId is called and nothing is pushed; then the admiral fx object if ADMIRALS[RUN.admiral] exists; then for each boon in RUN.boons, BOONS[b.id].fx(RARITY[b.r or 0].m) if the boon exists (core.js:172). Fleet fx therefore apply identically to every ship in the fleet (core.js:172, core.js:207).
- Contributor verified 1, relic fx: only relics with an fx field contribute. Those are lattice {shPct:0.15}, spore {hullRegen:3}, compass {speed:1}, coils {cdMod:1}, armourweave {armor:0.06} (data.js:188). Relics with a run field instead do not enter this pool; see section 8 (data.js:188, core.js:196).
- Contributor verified 2, admiral fx: ADMIRALS[RUN.admiral].fx, one object per admiral, 24 admirals in the export (core.js:172). Admiral fx keys in the export are: aim, armor, battShots, broadPct, crit, dmgPct, ev, hangar, hpPct, hullRegenPct, lancePct, rearPct, regenPct, shPct, speed, sqHp, sqPct, torpCd.
- Contributor verified 3, boons: BOONS[b.id].fx evaluated at RARITY[b.r].m, where RARITY multipliers are Common 1, Rare 1.5, Epic 2, Legendary 1 (core.js:172, data.js:620). - Derive formulas, hull: maxHp equals round(t.hp times mm times (1 plus (fx.hpPct or 0) plus 0.06 times tr)) (core.js:209). hpPct is uncapped, so stacked hpPct above 1 more than doubles hull before Mark (core.js:171, core.js:209).
- Derive formulas, shields: shMax equals round(t.sh times mm times (1 plus (fx.shPct or 0))) (core.js:210). regen equals round(t.rg times mm times (1 plus (fx.regenPct or 0))) (core.js:210). Neither is capped in derive (core.js:210).
- Derive formulas, armor: armor equals min(0.6, t.ar plus (fx.armor or 0)) (core.js:211). This is the only summed stat with a cap inside derive. INFERRED: armor contributions above the cap are lost, not banked.
- Derive formulas, movement and evasion: speed equals t.sp plus (fx.speed or 0), turns equals t.tn plus (fx.turns or 0), ev equals t.ev plus (fx.ev or 0), none capped in derive (core.js:211).
- Derive formulas, aim and crit: aim equals fx.aim or 0 and crit equals fx.crit or 0; the hull template contributes neither (core.js:212). Both are uncapped in derive (core.js:212).
- Derive formulas, damage: dmg equals 1 plus 0.15 times (mk minus 1) plus (fx.dmgPct or 0) plus 0.06 times tr (core.js:212). dmgPct can be negative; the admiral kassar has dmgPct minus 0.05 in the export, and sumFx adds it as a negative (core.js:171).
- Derive formulas, hangar: if t.hangar is truthy, hangar equals t.hangar plus (fx.hangar or 0) plus (fx.hive or 0); if t.hangar is falsy, hangar equals (fx.hive or 0) only (core.js:213). So a hangar fx on a non-carrier hull does nothing in derive, while a hive fx creates a hangar of 1 on any hull (core.js:213).
- Derive formulas, hull regen: hullRegen equals (fx.hullRegen or 0) plus round((fx.hullRegenPct or 0) times maxHp) plus round((t.regen or 0) times mm) (core.js:214). hullRegenPct is therefore evaluated against the already computed maxHp, after hpPct and Mark (core.js:209, core.js:214).
- Derive formulas, weapons: weapons are built by mkWeapon for each template weapon with the summed fx (core.js:215). mkWeapon applies: battShots adds to shots for battery kind (core.js:197); flakPlus adds 2 times flakPlus shots and flakPlus range and opt for flak kind (core.js:198); torpCd reduces torpedo cd to max(1, cd minus torpCd) (core.js:199); prowRange adds range and opt for arc F weapons (core.js:200); lanceRange adds range and opt for lance kind (core.js:201).
- Keys summed but not read by derive itself: sumFx preserves every key, including keys derive never reads directly, such as broadPct, lancePct, torpPct, sqPct, sqHp, cdMod, lastStand, rearPct, huntPct, bracePlus, longAim, prowPct, ramPct, and all boon rule keys (core.js:171, core.js:212). Those keys ride on the returned fx object (core.js:212) and are read by battle code through u.fx or ownerFx, for example abilityCd reads fx.cdMod (battle.js:1052), braceMul reads fx.bracePlus (battle.js:1051), and dmgMult reads fx.broadPct, fx.lancePct, fx.torpPct, fx.rearPct, fx.huntPct, fx.stillPct, fx.momPct, fx.prowPct (battle.js:663 to 676).
- Duplicate boons cannot occur through offers because hasBoon filters owned ids (core.js:182), but the same fx key from different sources stacks without limit subject only to the derive caps above. Example: speed from compass (1), grax admiral (1), a rook boon, a drive module (1), and a helm perk (1) would all add (core.js:171 to 172, core.js:207). ## 7. Boons: offer generation and resolution

- Tables: PATRONS has 6 patrons: iron, storm, hunger, star, rook, ember (data.js:621). BOONS has 48 entries in the export: 36 single patron non-legendary (6 per patron), 6 duo, and 6 legendary, one legendary per patron (data.js:629, data.js:676). RARITY is Common m 1, Rare m 1.5, Epic m 2, Legendary m 1 (data.js:620).
- Run patrons: ensurePatrons sets RUN.patrons to 4 of the 6 patrons, shuffled, if empty, and ensures RUN.boons is an array (core.js:174). newRun sets the same 4 patron draw directly (core.js:325).
- Node reward assignment: rollNodeReward(type) picks a random patron from RUN.patrons for the boon patron (core.js:190). Elite and boss nodes always return {k:boon, p, min:1} (core.js:190). Other fight nodes return boon with probability 0.62, mod below 0.78, cred below 0.90, and repair otherwise, each with the same random patron pre-picked but only used for boon (core.js:190).
- Offer entry point: boonStep(p, minR) uses RUN.reward.boonOffer if present, otherwise rollBoonOffer(p, minR), and stores a fresh offer into RUN.reward so a reload reuses it (campaign_run.js:488).
- rollBoonOffer signature and setup: rollBoonOffer(p, minR) calls ensurePatrons, reads sector s, have as boonPatrons() (the set of single patron ids among owned boons; duo boons contribute nothing because BOONS[id].p is undefined for them), and kit as fleetKit() (core.js:181, core.js:176).
- Usefulness filter: boonUseful(id, kit) returns true if BOON_NEED[id] is undefined, otherwise whether kit has that key (core.js:180). BOON_NEED is defined at data.js:689. fleetKit derives torp, lance, batt, flak, broad, still, shields, carrier, and sq from the current fleet (core.js:179).
- Main pool: pool is all BOONS ids where BOONS[id].p equals p, the boon is not legendary (leg falsy), and the player does not already own it, then shuffled (core.js:182). The pool is split into junk (not boonUseful) held aside and the useful remainder, which becomes the working pool (core.js:182).
- Main draw: out is the first 3 useful pool ids, each as {id, r: rollRarity(minR, s, p)} (core.js:184). Note the patron argument p is passed, so these rolls get the patron favour bonus (core.js:184, core.js:177).
- Rarity roll: rollRarity(minR, s, p) computes favour tier ft from p, or 0 if p is falsy (core.js:177). rare probability is 0.24 plus 0.06 times (s minus 1) plus 0.12 if ft is 1 or more; epic probability is 0.06 plus 0.03 times (s minus 1) plus 0.03 if ft is 1 or more (core.js:177). One rnd draw x gives Epic (r 2) if x is below epic, else Rare (r 1) if x is below epic plus rare, else Common (r 0) (core.js:177). If ft is 3 or more, r is raised to at least 1 (core.js:177). The result is max(minR or 0, r) (core.js:177). Favour tiers are at favour 0, 4, 12, 25 (data.js:590), computed by favourTier (core.js:304).
- Elite and boss floor: because elite and boss node rewards set min 1 (core.js:190), every main pool, backfill, and junk pick at those nodes is at least Rare, through the max in rollRarity (core.js:177). Duo and legendary picks are exceptions; see below.
- Duo eligibility: duos are all BOONS ids where b.duo exists, the player does not own it, boonUseful passes, b.duo includes p, every duo patron q satisfies have.has(q) or q equals p, and at least one duo patron other than p is in have (core.js:183). In plain terms: the player must already hold a boon from the other patron, and the other patron need not be in RUN.patrons (core.js:183, core.js:176).
- Duo insertion: if duos is non-empty and (out has fewer than 3 entries or rnd() is below 0.45), one random duo is chosen and inserted as {id, r:0} (core.js:185). If out already has 3 entries it replaces out[2]; otherwise it is pushed (core.js:185).
- Duo rarity defect free note: duo picks always enter at r 0, Common, and never roll rarity. The object is constructed literally as {id:pick(duos), r:0} with no rollRarity call (core.js:185). Their fx functions ignore the multiplier in data (duo fx are functions of no argument returning fixed objects, data.js:629), so the Common label does not reduce their effect, but favour gain on taking a duo uses r, so a duo grants only 1 favour per patron; see resolution below (campaign_run.js:505).
- Backfill path, trigger: backfill runs only if out still has fewer than 3 entries after the main draw and duo insertion (core.js:186).
- Backfill path, source pool: extra is all BOONS ids where the boon has a single patron, is not legendary, that patron is not p, that patron is in RUN.patrons, the player does not own it, boonUseful passes, and it is not already in out, shuffled (core.js:186). Off-patron picks therefore enter only as backfill when the offer is short, never as part of the main draw (core.js:182, core.js:186).
- Backfill rarity, THE Q7 DEFECT AS SHIPPED: backfill picks are pushed as {id:extra.pop(), r:rollRarity(minR, s)} with no third patron argument (core.js:186). rollRarity with p undefined uses favour tier 0 (core.js:177). - Offer size: if the main pool, duos, extra, and junk together cannot supply 3 distinct unowned boons, out is returned shorter than 3 (core.js:186 to 188). boonStep handles an empty offer by granting 60 credits instead and moving to relicStep (campaign_run.js:489).
- Legendary insertion: after backfill, legendaryFor(p, minR) is evaluated (core.js:187). If it returns an id, it overwrites out[2] when out has 3 or more entries, otherwise it is pushed, always as {id:L, r:3} (core.js:187). r 3 is Legendary, whose multiplier is 1 (data.js:620).

- Gift boon at run start: newRun finds the first patron in RUN.patrons with favourTier 2 or more (core.js:337). ## Save system (browser build)

### Storage layout and envelope

- The local save lives in localStorage under the key `voidsworn.v1` (core.js:48, STORE_KEY). The key name says v1 but the payload version is SAVE_VERSION 2 (core.js:49). The port must not read the key name as the version.
- The envelope is a single JSON object `{meta: META, run: SAVED_RUN}`, written by Persist.saveLocal (core.js:118). A run of `null` means no run in progress. The cloud copy uses the same envelope shape, with the run replaced by `{none: true}` when absent (core.js:145 to 152, Persist.push).
- Three side keys exist: `voidsworn.v1.bak` (one-save-behind backup, core.js:119 to 121), `voidsworn.v1.repaired` (write-once raw copy of a save that needed id repairs, core.js:116 to 117), and `voidsworn.tel.v1` (a telemetry mirror, campaign3d.js:15).
- META shape (defaultMeta, core.js:50): `{v, renown, unlocked, admirals (starts {vex: true}), fallen, stats {runs, best, wins, ...}, opts, tel, updated, mastery, favour, variants, heatBest}` plus `runAt` once a run write has happened (core.js:155) and the one-shot migration flags `backfilled` and `wonFix41` (core.js:52 to 61).
- RUN shape (runDefaults, core.js:66): 34 fields including fac, foes, admiral, credits, cpCap (starts 10), sector, relics, inventory, fleet, pos, stats, fallen, boons, patrons, aspect, flagUid, heat, reserve, seenEvents, curNode, nem, and flags. normalizeRun (core.js:68) fills any missing field from runDefaults and repairs fleet captain objects. A ship object carries uid, tpl, name, mk, hp, mods, cap {fac, name, xp, rank, perks, pending}, and optionally fit (reserved, schema only: shipFit at core.js:170 returns ship.fit or null and nothing applies it yet).

### Sanity gates

- saneSave (core.js:104) accepts an envelope only if meta passes saneMeta (core.js:102: the named containers are plain objects, tel and fallen are arrays, renown is null or a number) and the run passes saneRun (core.js:103: null, or {none}, or an object with a fleet array and a map with a cols array).
- Load order in Persist.loadLocal (core.js:112 to 117):
  1. Parse `voidsworn.v1`. A JSON parse failure yields null.
  2. If the parsed value fails saneSave, try `.bak`; if the backup passes saneSave it replaces the main value and sets Persist.recovered.
  3. If the value still fails saneSave, keep whichever half is sound: a run failing saneRun is nulled, a meta failing saneMeta is nulled.
  4. META is migrateMeta over defaults; SAVED_RUN is migrateRun over the run (a {none} run becomes null).
- The `.repaired` copy is written only on two paths: the migrated run carries `_repairs` (id repairs happened), or migrateMeta/migrateRun throws. It is write-once: an existing `.repaired` is never overwritten (core.js:117).
- Defect (verified by the Argon QA leg and the chair, Oct 30, 2026): a save that fails JSON.parse, or parses with an insane half and no good `.bak`, never gets a `.repaired` copy, although the comment at core.js:117 claims a failed load "is kept raw, never silently lost". On those paths the damaged string survives only until the next saveLocal overwrites the main key.
- Defect (same source): saveLocal rotates the previous main value into `.bak` when it merely parses as JSON (core.js:120); it does not call saneSave. A malformed-but-parseable main save therefore overwrites a good `.bak` on the next write.

### Migration and repair

- migrateMeta (core.js:52 to 61): fills missing fields from defaultMeta, coerces stats numbers, upgrades v<2 (ensures opts and tel, sets v=2), and runs two one-shot backfills guarded by flags:
  - `backfilled`: replays the telemetry log to credit mastery (battle_end wins: boss 5, elite 2, other 1; victorious run_end: 10), favour (boon_pick: 1 + rarity per patron), and a capped renown credit (min(60, accumulated); the accumulator is battles x2 + elites x4 + bosses x15 + kills/4 + (sector-1) x5 per run_end).
  - `wonFix41`: rebuilds stats.wonFacs from run_start/run_end pairs because early builds logged run_end without a faction.
- migrateRun (core.js:63 to 64): defaults fac to voidsworn, upgrades v<2 (v=2, stats and fallen defaults), then normalizeRun.
- repairRunIds (core.js:76 to 100) is the id-integrity pass. It checks, in order: faction (reset to voidsworn), admiral (reset to a same-faction admiral or vex), foes (nulled if any entry is not a faction), relics, inventory (module ids), heat, boons (by .id), patrons, aspect, giftBoon; then per ship: hull tpl (the ship is DROPPED if its tpl is not in HULLS), captain perks, fitted mods, name and hp coercion (hp not a positive number becomes 1). It also validates a pending reward object (stage forced into the known set, prizes filtered to known hulls, relic and mod offers filtered, wrecks sanitized), nulls flagUid if the flagship is gone, and resets NaN credits to 0. Every repair is appended to run._repairs (last 20 kept) and dropped ships are counted in run._shipsDropped. Unknown ids are reported once each via unknownId (core.js:106), which also logs a telemetry event.
- Consequence the port must preserve or consciously change: repairs are silent deletions. A renamed hull id deletes the ship, its captain, its perks and its fitted modules, with no compensation (Argon QA leg, finding 5).

### Write paths and stamping

- Persist.save (core.js:155) is the only stamping writer: it sets META.updated = now, and META.runAt = now unless called with {meta: true} (purchases and problem reports stamp meta only). It then saveLocal + push.
- Telemetry, error and stall paths call saveLocal directly and stamp nothing (core.js:154 comment; battle.js:1061; campaign3d.js:5), so diagnostic writes can never win a cloud conflict.
- saveLocal failures (storage full) set Persist.failed, toast once, and rely on the cloud write (core.js:118 to 121).

### Cloud reconciliation

- Persist.connect (core.js:122 to 144) uses the host environment's db and user services (window.claude.use) and reads `data/users/<uid>/save`. Outside that host it returns silently and the game is local-only.
- If the cloud envelope fails saneSave, the local save is kept and pushed over the cloud copy when META.updated is set (core.js:131). There is no half-salvage on the cloud path, unlike loadLocal.
- Otherwise meta and run resolve on separate stamps (core.js:136 to 141): takeMeta if cloud meta.updated > local META.updated; takeRun if cloud runAt (falling back to updated) > local runAt. Taken halves are migrated, mergeLocalTel runs, and the merged result is written back locally. If either local half is newer, the local envelope is pushed.
- Persist.push (core.js:145 to 152) is serialized with a writing flag and a pending flag; a push requested mid-write replays once after completion.
- Boot order (main3d.js:11 to 14): Persist.loadLocal runs first, options are applied, mergeLocalTel and titleScreen run, the session telemetry event fires, then Persist.connect reconciles; onPersistLoaded re-renders the title if a cloud run arrives after the title is up.

### What is NOT saved

- Battle state is never persisted. The B object exists only in memory; hull damage from a battle is written back to RUN.fleet only in finishBattle (see the battle sections). Reloading mid-battle restarts the fight from the pre-battle fleet at no cost (Argon QA leg, finding 7; the pagehide handler at main3d.js:20 only logs battle_abandon and saveLocal). INFERRED: the Godot port inherits this shape unless it deliberately adds battle snapshots.

## Telemetry (browser build)

- tel(ev, d) is defined at campaign3d.js:16. Each event is `{t: Date.now(), ev, sector: RUN ? RUN.sector : 0, build: BUILD_ID or 'dev', ...d}`, appended to META.tel and mirrored to localStorage key `voidsworn.tel.v1` (campaign3d.js:15 to 17).
- The log is a ring: META.tel is capped at the last 600 events (campaign3d.js:16). mergeLocalTel (campaign3d.js:18) merges the localStorage mirror into META.tel on load, deduplicating on (t, ev) pairs, re-sorted by t and re-capped at 600.
- Event names emitted by the browser build at the pin (31 distinct names, counted from tel call sites): session, run_start, run_end, battle_start, battle_end, battle_abandon, command, node, shop (4 call sites), reward_pick, prize_pick, salvage_pick, boon_pick, boon_decline, aspect_pick, arc_unlock, variant_buy, field_repairs, reinforced, nem_signature, unknown_id, error, action_error, stall, busy_watchdog, problem_report, perf_prof, battle_warm, hull_wait, scene_heal, wing_order, hulls.
- Known gaps against the filed telemetry spec (contrib/muse/reviews/telemetry_spec_2026_10_29.md): no nemesis_meeting, nemesis_adaptation, weakness_revealed, regroup_open, regroup_choice, xp_grant, level_up, tree_pick, or boon_offer events exist in either build at the pin (Rey engineer leg, finding 5, from a grep of the Godot campaign's 18 call sites; the browser list above confirms the same gaps). The spec's clash-audit join also has a known flaw: it joins nemesis_adaptation to the same run's weakness_revealed events, but the Q5 loss-debrief reveal carries weaknesses across runs, so the join needs the run lineage, not the single run (Argon engineer leg, finding 8).
- The Godot campaign keeps its own tel_log inside its save file (campaign.gd:370, the save_text envelope carries "tel"), capped at 600 entries (campaign.gd:155 to 156 per the Rey engineer leg). Port decision needed: one telemetry store per build is current behavior; the spec's play-data questions (regroup tension, Nemesis snowball, generational payoff) are not answerable from either store as shipped.

## Settings and options

- Player options live in META.opts (a free-form object in the save envelope, core.js:50) and are applied once at boot (main3d.js:11). Known keys and boot effects:
  - fxSet / fxReduced: reduced-flash mode. If fxSet is set it wins; else if G.mobile, fxReduced is forced false at boot; else an explicit fxReduced is honored (main3d.js:11). This is the patch pack's G.mobile item: G.mobile is computed at scene3d.js:4 from `pointer: coarse` plus a minimum window dimension under 900, so a touch-capable desktop in a small window is classified mobile, gets quality forced to medium (unless opts.quality set) and reduced-flash forced off when no explicit setting exists.
  - shakeRed: screen-shake reduction; defaults to fxReduced behavior for old saves.
  - quality: graphics quality override (ultra default; medium when G.mobile and no override).
  - sfxOn, sfxVol: sound toggles (sfx.js consumes these; the in-battle settings panel also exposes a sound button).
  - cine / cineMode: cut-in camera mode ('key' default, 'off' disables); follow: action camera follows activations (default true); alt: SETUP.alt variant; fast: SPEED = .45; taa: temporal anti-aliasing on.
  - ai: AI_LEVEL override; with no override and at least one recorded run, AI_LEVEL becomes 'veteran' at boot (main3d.js:11). The veteran ratchet is a boot default, not a stored difficulty choice.
  - tips / tipsOff: tutorial tip visibility. tipsOff is set by the Hide tips action; there is no in-battle control that clears it (ui3d.js references tipsOff exactly twice: the gate and the setter; Argon uiux leg, finding 2).
  - unlockAll: if anything other than false, boot grants every LEGACY unlock and every admiral into META (main3d.js:11). Because META is what gets saved, the grant persists into the profile and into any future imported save (DeepSeek and Argon QA legs). At the pin this is the shipped default state of the browser build, not a debug flag someone left on by accident in one place: it is inline in the boot sequence.
- saveOpts writes go through Persist.saveLocal only in the battle settings path (Argon QA leg, finding 6): settings changed in battle do not stamp META.updated and do not push to cloud until some other save stamps. INFERRED from the call sites; the campaign settings path was not separately traced.
- The in-battle settings panel (#setpanel, ui3d.js:62 to 64) exposes 18 buttons including developer items (Copy report: "Copy your recent play data to paste to Claude"; Report a problem: "Save a snapshot of the game state for debugging"; Diagnostics) and phone copy ("Medium: tuned for phones", "(phones)" log toggle) on the PC-only build. Verified in the clone by the chair, Oct 30, 2026.

## Port hazards (numbered, for the cutover checklist)

### H1. The parity oracles have already diverged (verified by md5 at the pin)

- Three distinct copies of battle.js exist: live (md5 904e6dee...), godot/rules/test/oracle (a161e192...), godot/campaign/test/oracle (655081cc...). Three distinct copies of data.js: live (bb00d51c...), rules oracle (5fbcf894...), campaign oracle (82dca494...). core.js is identical in all three places (4d1744ff...). The two oracle copies also differ from each other, so the rules parity suite and the campaign parity suite certify against different snapshots of the game. No automated hash check compares the copies (both oracle READMEs document a manual re-copy; DeepSeek engineer leg, finding 5). Until a hash compare runs in both parity runners, every green parity result needs a footnote naming its oracle.

### H2. The two generated Godot data tables disagree with each other (verified by diff at the pin)

- godot/rules/data/tables.json (121 tables incl. harvested constants) and godot/campaign/data/campaign_tables.json share 121 tables; 6 shared tables differ with real value differences, not formatting: HULLS (12 entries, all Chorus regen values: b_drone 4 vs 5, b_spitter 5 vs 6, b_razor 7 vs 9), MOVE_RULES (2: Crusade rake 0.4 vs 0.3, Glasswright evade 18 vs 14), ADMIRALS (10: e.g. malek rearPct 0.5 vs 0.2, ashgrove sqPct 0.4 vs 0.25, kassar start fleet h_picket vs h_interdictor), HULL_VARIANTS (6: e.g. vs_penitent_l mounts a battery turret vs a flak turret; h_interdictor_e mod hp vs sh), FAC_RULES (1: Chorus feed cap 12% vs 15%), CHORUS_BITE (0.08 vs 0.12).
- On every sampled entry the RULES file matches data.js and battle.js at the pin (regen at data.js hull entries; rake 0.4 at MOVE_RULES; FEED_CAP .12 at battle.js:474; CHORUS_BITE .08 at battle.js:510), and the CAMPAIGN file is one balance pass behind. Both files predate the hull display renames in data.js at the pin (both say "Penitent-class cruiser" and "Reliquary-class carrier" where data.js says "Vigil-class cruiser" and "Hearth-class carrier"). INFERRED: the tables were generated at different times from different game states and never reconciled; a decode-aware regeneration from one pinned source (the export accompanying this spec) closes it.
- Both generated files faithfully reproduce the data.js defects (flak identical to gsentry, d_boss without reanimate) and neither contains d_meridian. Parity against a frozen oracle preserves known defects as correct behavior (Argon engineer leg, finding 5); the findings-to-tests ledger (8 PASS, 40 FAIL at the pin) is the defect list that parity cannot see.
- The float encoding in both files is a "#d:<ieee hex>" string for non-integer doubles (the _enc scheme visible at campaign.gd:365 to 370). Any external diff tooling must decode it before comparing, or every float reads as a difference.

### H3. The importer contradiction (R33)

- campaign.gd:201: "a Godot profile never had a browser log (R33: old saves are not migrated)"; campaign.gd:354: "browser saves are not carried over, R33". A grep of godot/ finds no importer code (Rey engineer leg, finding 4). Against that, the wave 5 importer fixtures (26 files, contrib/muse/tools/importer_fixtures/ on main) exist precisely to pin an importer's behavior, and the saves audit rates the missing importer a BLOCKER.
- Two further facts whichever way the decision goes: the browser build has no export path (its Copy report emits a diagnostic summary, not a save), so a desktop build cannot reach browser localStorage without a transport that does not exist yet (Argon engineer leg, finding 2); and any imported browser META carries the boot unlockAll grant baked in (see Settings), so a verbatim import lands fully unlocked (Argon QA leg, finding 2).
- This is a product decision wearing an engineering costume (Rey leg's phrasing). The spec takes no side; it records that code, fixtures, and the audit register currently tell three different stories, and cutover planning cannot be honest until they tell one.

### H4. Godot save write: the pack claim is stale, the pin code is atomic

- The Argon and DeepSeek engineer legs describe the Godot write as "copy to .bak, then overwrite in place" (non-atomic). At the pin, campaign.gd:382 to 384 writes both the backup and the main file through _write_atomic (temp file, flush, rename), with the comment "Atomic: the new save is written whole to a temp file". The Rey engineer leg read the pin code and reported this correctly (its finding 2 lists four such already-fixed items the filed syntheses still carry as open work: the atomic write, the jsm.gd scratch buffer, the battle_api mutex wrappers, and the warning classes). Lesson recorded for the port: synthesis documents lag the tree; this spec cites code, not syntheses, wherever they conflict.

### H5. Threaded-shell parity blindness: mitigations present at the pin, live risk stands

- The pack-based legs report that step() was the only locked battle_api method, threat_map() cleared shared eval caches from a worker thread, and jsm.gd:15 kept one process-wide static scratch buffer. At the pin, jsm.gd:19 documents "a fresh 8-byte buffer per call, never a shared static one", and the Rey leg reports battle_api.gd:17 and 24 to 25 wrapping every public function in a recursive mutex, with godot/rules/test/thread_test.gd present. The single-threaded golden suites still cannot see live-play races by construction (all three engineer legs agree on the construction, whatever the mitigations). The port's own thread test is the only net; this spec cannot verify it passes, only that it exists (INFERRED from the Rey leg's read).

### H6. Terrain state IS in the golden hash

- The pack carried a "document divergence" over whether the canonical golden hash covers CUT and SOLID module state. golden.js:145 builds the hashed state with cut and solid arrays included (Rey engineer leg, finding 3, code read). The exclusion claim is false at the pin; any pre-cutover task premised on it is phantom work.

### H7. Clean checkout cannot build the shippable game

- godot/gen/ is gitignored and absent; godot/render/build_assets.gd:59 and 477 to 478 fall back to engine-test inputs under contrib/opus/enginetest (outside the port tree); godot/render/ship_view.gd:95 falls back silently to a plain BoxMesh when hull assets are missing (Rey engineer leg, finding 6; DeepSeek engineer leg, finding 2). The Oct 26 music blocker from the same review round is stale in the other direction: godot/music/ at the pin holds music.json, the track files, and CREDITS.md (24 tracks per the audio legs), so file-existence claims in either direction need re-checking against the pin before they drive work.

### H8. Hand-copied constants with no comparison check

- FAC3D, AI_HULLS, CLS_LEN, FINISH, BIOMES, ROCKS and PLANETS-class constants are hand-copied into godot/render (build_assets.gd, hull_table.gd, ship_view.gd) and godot/scenes/battle (battle.gd, lineup.gd) with no automated comparison against data.js or battle.js (Argon engineer leg, finding 10). H1 and H2 are this failure mode already realized in the test and data layers.

### H9. The C# decision is undocumented

- The engine port plan chose C# for the rules and campaign state, with GDScript as a fallback if a spike rejected C#. The shipped port is GDScript throughout (rules.gd:1 to 15 documents managing the choice via jsm.gd numeric emulation and the warning configuration). No document in the tree records the spike outcome or an acceptance of the residual risk (all three engineer legs). The mitigations are real; the decision record is missing.

### H10. The findings ledger is not wired into the cutover gate

- The 48 findings-tests (8 PASS, 40 FAIL at the pin, contrib/muse/tools/findings_tests/ on main) are the honest browser-side defect checklist, and the Godot runners (run_parity.sh, run_sweep.sh) know nothing about them (Rey engineer leg, finding 8). The 41 testable-but-unwritten ledger rows mostly need a campaign-file loader the harness lacks (game_loader.js loads only data.js, core.js, battle.js). Unless the ledger is named in the cutover criteria, cutover can be declared on parity evidence while 40 reproduced defects ride along.
