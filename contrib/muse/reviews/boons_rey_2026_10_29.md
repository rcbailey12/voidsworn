## Verdict: DUPLICATES WITH PAINT, with the mechanism now fully priced

The chair concurs with DeepSeek and Argon. What this leg adds is the three code answers the pack never contained, and they change the severity of two filed findings.

## The three code answers

1. AGGREGATION IS PURELY ADDITIVE. sumFx (core.js) folds every fx object (relics, the admiral, then each boon at its rolled m) into one object by adding numeric keys: o[k]=(o[k]||0)+fx[k]. No caps, no overwriting, no per-key special cases at aggregation time. Consequences for the filed findings:
   - Argon's L_ember finding resolves DOWN. Eye of the Storm aside, Pyre of Stars (flat chainBoom:40) stacks additively on Chain Reaction: an epic Chain Reaction (m=2) already gives chainBoom 40 plus chainFire, and holding the legendary takes the boom to 80. The legendary is not a no-op duplicate; it is the same effect sold a second time at the legendary slot, priced as a flat +40. That is a value and feel defect, not a dead pick.
   - Argon's L_storm qualification resolves the same way: Eye of the Storm (stormRegen:1) stacks on Storm Mantle's .18*m, and the application site (battle.js:384) clamps each facing at shMax, so the combined regen simply refills to full; the Mantle's contribution is wasted only in the turns where the legendary alone would already cap the facing. Additive with an application clamp, not an overwrite.
   - The ram block (r_prow / d_jugg / L_rook) stacks additively across all three keys too, so epic Prow ramPct 0.70 plus the legendary's 0.50 is a combined 1.20 before any application clamp. Whether a clamp exists at the ram resolution site is a one-read check for Opus; the aggregation layer imposes none.

2. THE M ROLL IS A RARITY ROLL, AND ITS DISTRIBUTION IS NOW EXACT. RARITY (data.js:620): Common m=1, Rare m=1.5, Epic m=2, Legendary m=1 (legendary fx are flat and ignore m). rollRarity (core.js): epic chance .06 + .03 per sector past 1, rare chance .24 + .06 per sector past 1; holding the patron's favour at tier 1 or better adds +.12 rare and +.03 epic, and favour tier 3 or better floors the roll at Rare. At sector 1 with no favour: 70% Common, 24% Rare, 6% Epic. Duo boons are always offered at r:0 (m=1). DeepSeek's m assumption, marked INFERRED in its leg, is confirmed in shape and now has numbers.

3. THE SHIPPED HALF OF THE OCT 28 SPILLOVER RULING (Q7) IS VIOLATED IN CODE, EXACTLY AS RULED AGAINST. rollBoonOffer (core.js) builds offers in this order: the patron's own useful pool first (three slots); a duo may take the third slot (45% when the pool is full, otherwise appended); off-patron boons from the run's other patrons backfill ONLY when the on-patron useful pool runs short; gated-out "junk" backfills last. Two shipped facts follow. First, off-patron picks structurally cannot compete head-to-head with a full on-patron pool; they appear only when the patron has nothing useful left to offer. Second, the backfill roll calls rollRarity WITHOUT the patron argument, so off-patron picks roll with no favour bonus: lower rare and epic chances and no tier-3 Rare floor. That is precisely the hidden rarity tax Ryan's Oct 28 ruling named as the defect ("off-patron picks must retain real value"). The ruling is not implemented at this pin; the tax is in the shipped offer code, not just in the numbers. This is the boons item's most consequential finding, and neither filed leg could see it because the pack omitted the offer code (chair pack defect, owned in the DeepSeek screening).

## Filed findings the chair confirms, refines, or downgrades

- BOON_NEED has produced one verified defect of each kind, confirmed by direct read of the table (data.js:689). Wrong gate: h_feast is gated on 'sq', but fleetKit derives 'sq' from any ship's derived hangar being above zero, which is also what the feast payoff needs (a wing parent with fx.feast), so the gate is closer to right than DeepSeek's framing; downgrade to a NOTE. Missing gate: L_storm is absent from BOON_NEED entirely (L_hunger and L_star are present), and legendaryFor checks boonUseful, which passes any id with no table entry. A fleet with no shields that holds two ungated storm boons can be offered Eye of the Storm, a full shield refill for ships with no shields. Argon's catch stands at full strength.
- h_board's sabotage promise: the fx returns boardPct only in the sliced data; whether boarding resolution implements the promised sabotage elsewhere remains an Opus check, as Argon left it.
- Argon's a_long dead-tier claim remains WRONG (chair arithmetic in its screening) and is not carried.
- Duo coverage (Argon): 6 duos over 15 patron pairs, Iron in 3, Hunger in 1. With the offer code now read, the chair adds: duos also only surface when the player already holds boons from both patrons, and they enter at m=1 always, so the duo layer is both structurally rare and numerically flat. That strengthens the filed point.

## What the chair would protect

The patron silhouettes read correctly in both filed legs, and the offer system's bones (useful-first gating, duo bridging, legendary earned by holding two of a patron's boons at elite and flagship rewards) are a sound skeleton. The defects are in the values and the spillover path, not the architecture.
