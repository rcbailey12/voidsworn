# Outside check: PR 525 (issue 521, kill moment and nose-first movement)

Refs #256. PR head b580280e (opus/issue-521). Checked against the issue 521 brief, the PR's own report and measurements.json, and movement_spec.md as amended. Council: blind Rey leg, DeepSeek, Qwen, Flash, Grok. All legs read the complete 18-file diff, the brief and the blind leg.

## Verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS (paper conditions only, nothing blocking)

This PR is the complete form of what its brief asks: the report, measurements.json and the five named strips are in the PR, all five carried items from the issue 505 report are answered with numbers, both new headless checks are listed in godot_checks.txt, and the code is presentation only. The council was unanimous, and unusually aligned: every leg independently reached the same two paper conditions.

## Conditions

1. **State the battle pace of the measured kill hold releases.** The hold is assembled from two clocks: core_in is read off the fx clock (death_core_at minus fx.now), the 0.48 s beat and KILL_AFTER 0.35 are real seconds, the release runs on a SceneTree timer, and the event loop waits on game_t. At the capture pace the arithmetic reproduces the report exactly (cruiser flash 1.67, release 2.48; capital 2.28, release 3.10), and Qwen's reading bounds the risk: the timer is the authoritative release (it clears kill_hold_until and exits the wait loop), so the visual timing should hold at any pace. But the fallback hold is pace-scaled (1.6 * speed) while the new hold is not scaled at all, the report never states the pace its releases were measured at, and no check exercises _kill_hold at a non-default, non-hurried pace. One sentence in the report naming the captures' pace closes this; anything more is the Board's call.

2. **Record the slide pace's whole-move scope in movement_spec.md section 1a.** SLIDE_PACE is chosen once per move from the last glide step's thrusters flag, which _nose_first sets from whether any leg burned. A mixed route (a two-hex retro back-off, then a burn; or a run ending in a dogleg sidestep) therefore flies its thruster legs at burn pace. Code and spec agree to the letter (the spec ties the pace to "a move flown only on thrusters"); the visible consequence is worth the one line, or the code change if that is not the intent.

3. **Correct the "real rules routes" wording** (DeepSeek, adopted). nose_first_check builds its nine routes as hand-written step dictionaries in the rules' format (its _path function) and flies them through the real _glide_steps and play_glide. It never calls the rules engine (battle.api.reach). The routes are faithful representatives, and the check's assertions are the right ones, but the report and spec call them "real rules routes", which overclaims what the check does. Reword, or have the check obtain its routes from the rules.

## Recorded, not conditions

- **The dreadnought's long diagonal now runs into MOVE_CAP** (7.00 s for four sidesteps, the report's own disclosure): the cap compresses the move, so the biggest hull's per-hex pace moves toward the cruiser's, against the grain of the weight the spec wants. The check flies with the same cap and its assertions hold. A look trade for Ryan on the M521 dreadnought strip, not a defect.
- **Enemy move time rose and is honestly reported** (carried item 3): s24 5.9 to 6.5 s on the measuring machine, s23 16.0 to 20.4, s26 10.5 to 12.6, with the machine caveat stated. That is the cost of the added turns and slower slides; the report's "For Ryan" section already puts it, and the two-hex back-off staying on retro jets, in front of him as Board picks.
- **The beat's 0.48 is written twice**: a literal in battle.gd's _kill_hold and DEATH_SLOW_S in fx.gd. Equal today; they can drift. One line of defence would be battle referencing the fx constant.
- **movement_spec 1a's "at most 0.2 of a hex"** for a hex centre's distance from the flown line is the common zigzag's figure; LEG_TOL and LEG_PULL together allow about 0.22 in the general case (DeepSeek). The check's enforceable bound is 0.45 hex from each centre, and it passes.
- **Flagship coverage of the beat rests on fx_ship's death_big** (R73, class not size), which is outside this diff; kill_slowmo_check exercises Cruiser, Dreadnought and Frigate hulls. Nothing in the PR contradicts flagship coverage; it is asserted by the report, not proved by the check.
- **A click consumed by a HUD control cannot reach _unhandled_input** and so cannot skip the hold (DeepSeek, INFERRED). The letterbox covers the screen during the hold and any key still skips, so the practical exposure is small.
- **The byte-identical seeded logs rest on the report's md5 prefixes**, with the raw dumps in the PC capture folder (opus521). DeepSeek asked for the dumps to be cited in the repo; the chair records the claim's basis instead: the diff's scope is verifiably presentation-only, which every leg confirmed, and the prefixes plus the folder are the house pattern for capture evidence. The report's claim that measurements.json holds "every number below" is slightly overbroad (the effect-clock 0.35 to 0.45 line is not in the file).
- **One leg claim screened out at chair.** DeepSeek's finding 13 (first half) held that a non-big cined death gets _kill_hold called twice, the second call discarding the first. The gfx branch of _play_death ends in an unconditional return, so the second call runs only on the gfx == null path; Qwen's finding 21 reads the same code correctly. Not adopted.

## What the checks prove, and what they cannot

kill_slowmo_check proves at the fx level: core flash times (cruiser 1.66, capital 2.28), the beat starting at the flash, no core and no beat for a frigate, no beat while kill_slowmo is off (the reduce-motion path), and the skip function ending or pre-empting the beat. nose_first_check proves, on nine representative routes across three classes flown through the production glide: exact end hex and facing, every route hex passed within 0.45 hex spacings, the nose within 35 degrees of course above half peak speed on runs of two or more (the back-off and the dogleg tail excepted by design), no roll, no heading jump over 3.5 degrees a frame, and a one-hex slide at least 1.3x a one-hex burn. Neither check exercises the battle-level hold, the kill camera (_kill_frame, a Board review change in this PR that frames the dying ship alone), the input path as an InputEvent, or non-default paces; those rest on the PC captures and the report.
