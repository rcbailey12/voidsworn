Legs: blind Rey first, then DeepSeek, Qwen, Grok and Flash (R158 roster; ChatGPT not waited for, Argon blocked). Chair: Rey.

## Pass 2 (graphics and effects)

P1, every explosion is one tiny smoke puff: HOLDS WITH A CHANGE. The bake lines and the fill measurements hold (independently re-measured: about 52 of 256 and about 92 of 512, bracketing the auditor's and Board's figures; all threshold-dependent). Attack the prescribed cure, not the facts. A 256-cell grid is roughly 64 times the cells of the 64 grid per frame, times 36 to 64 frames times several seeds and kinds; the audit never estimates wall-clock bake time, and bake cost, not GPU room, is the real driver. Comparable stylised games (FTL, Homeworld's effect layer, Battlefleet Gothic) lean on painted or procedural flipbooks with authored colour ramps, because raw simulation reads as generic gas at any resolution; the audit's own heat-channel proposal is already hand-authoring on top of the sim. The better route is hybrid: a moderate grid (about 128) for the smoke body as a shape source, authored hot cores, ramps and spark overlays composited on top, and the saved budget spent on per-event source shapes and seeds, which is where the sameness complaint actually lives. The fire itself is settled (R88, restated in R158): flash and snuff out, short hot core; the Board's amendment is the operative version and is not attacked.

P2, biggest moments have no weight: HOLDS. On-screen facts verified; cause honestly marked inference, and the Board owns its part (run 4 was told to shrink the puff). One framing note: the API.md:116 quote is selective. The full line describes gas jets, a tear, a split at 1.38 s, secondary explosions and cooling coals, so "no aftermath" is strongest for torpedo hits and cruiser deaths and weakest for capital deaths, where the spec already describes a longer sequence that does not read on screen. Guard for pass 3: fix the three-beat ladder's sizes in pixels at the battle camera and its durations in seconds, or the shrink ratchet runs in reverse.

P3, ship paint is one flat colour: HOLDS WITH A CHANGE. Facts hold (bakemat4.py:4, :21; the dict's other channels do not change the single base hull albedo claim). On the Board's question, what breaks on the sides the concept does not show: single-camera projection smears at grazing angles, the far side, belly and top get no projection and fall to sampled fill, which must invent the palette for half the ship and will lack the concept's directional light, and the seam between projected and filled regions will show unless blended. Any geometry change reopens the projection. So the paint job must be judged on far-side and underside stills, not only from the concept side, or the sheets repeat the one-camera trap P13 catches in the outline gate. Separately, the free texture sources' licence lines are unchecked (the auditor says so); pass 3 must check each texture as used, since CC0 and CC-BY carry different duties.

P4, at the battle camera ships vanish or burn out: HOLDS. Verified against the captures and the camera and light lines (camera_rig.gd:9, look.gd:19 and :52, bakemat4.py:22). The standing 36-ship sheet should include storm map lighting alongside the darkest and brightest skies; the worst reading case in the captures is ships inside rings on a storm map.

P5, thin ruler lines: HOLDS. The streak calls read as quoted; which call draws which line is the auditor's medium-sure part and is marked as such.

P6, shields: no resting state, one balloon: HOLDS WITH A CHANGE. Facts hold (facings summed at fx_ship.gd:752; one SphereMesh built at fx_ship.gd:387-421; bubble from the box at fx_hull.gd:569). Two precisions: the :752 sum sits in regen detection, not drawing; and on a break the code does pass a direction to the shader (fx_ship.gd:464-465), so "the whole bubble" is a visual claim, Board-confirmed from sh_01. On the Board's cost question: the audit requires an R116 cost report for P10's fog route but not for this rebuild, and that is an omission. The steady-state cost is bounded by the resting-state rule, not by 20 full bubbles, but the hit-state cost is unmeasured; the job must carry a gate-scene cost report (gate 12.5 ms; current readings 6.0 ms at 1080p and 5.45 ms GPU at 4K per R118). Design precision: "split into the four facings" is a data requirement, not necessarily four meshes; one hull-hugging surface carrying facing data avoids multiplying shield draw calls when several facings are lit at once. Sequencing after the recipe can emit the shield surface is right.

P7, shells, bolts and beams are plain shapes: HOLDS. Code facts verified (fx_weapons.gd:216, fx_shaders.gd:291). Record note: the Board opened one of the six images named, and its verdict header ("checked against the repo and the images") overstates that depth; the verdict is still right on the code. Same header point applies to P12.

P8, looks tuned as numbers, judged from wrong cameras: HOLDS. The missing bake scripts are confirmed absent at the pin (both return 404) and are the Board's own scope miss, already being restored. P9, death puffs placed off the hull: HOLDS WITH A CHANGE. The arithmetic checks (0.42 times 0.85 is the 0.36 offset), but the offset line applies to the vent kind specifically (fx_death.gd:179 tests the kind). The real gap is wider: record_spawn is never called from fx_death at all, so no death puff of any kind is covered by the stray-puff check. Widen the small job to all death kinds, not only the vent offset.

P10, nebula and storm hexes are flat puffs: HOLDS WITH A CHANGE. Direction is right and R90 allows fog volumes at the top tier. But volumetric fog cost is full-screen, not per-hex; on a multi-patch map at 4K it could break the very gate the finding cites, so the gate measurement comes before the look work, not after. The audit also skips the cheap version of its own finding: world_living.gd already samples one continuous 3D noise field in world space, so hex-sized, denser billboards from the existing system are a costed fallback worth pricing alongside the fog route.

Record note: the 90.1 percent figure is correct at the cited branch commit 8f50c07; the later branch head reads 90.0, which is drift, not an audit error.

P14, three factions need their own materials: PARTLY CONFIRMED HOLDS. Timing with the R138 concept redo is right; paint and material designed once, not twice.

P15, shield surfaces read as line art: HOLDS. Follows P6, as the Board has it (fx_shaders.gd:859, API.md:320 both verified).

## Misses inside the audits' own scope (five)

M1. Unreproduced numbers carry verdicts and will size pass 3 jobs. Several quantitative claims are the auditor's run outputs or full-tree counts that neither the Board nor this check reproduced: pass 1 F2's repo totals, F4's 46 failures, F7's fixture counts beyond the corroborated 191, F10's script counts, F11's 63 shader sites, F12's read counts, F3's drift counts. Every line citation attached to them resolves, so the verdicts are probably right, but the load-bearing ones (F12's site count, F2's totals) should be re-measured before they become job sizes.

M2. The Board's fire citation does not resolve at either audit pin. Issue 337 and R157's programme cite the fire ruling as R158; the FINDINGS copy at both pins ends at R155, and the copy this check started from had no R158 either. R158 does exist on live main as of this check (line 185, recorded the same evening, substance matching R88), so this is a timing and pinning hole, not a wrong ruling: anyone re-checking the citation from the audit pins will fail to find it. Pass 3 should cite R88 alongside it.

M3. Cross-twin save compatibility is F1's untested neighbour. #335 repairs the pick key inside each twin; nothing in the audit or the repair checks a campaign saved under one twin's storage being read by the other, the same hand-off class as the original defect, in the one place the audit did not look.

M4. Pass 2's evidence base is not contained in its pin. The 43 captures do not exist at 70e87f2 (the contents listing 404s; they were added to main after the pin, in the audit's own PR, and are on main now). A reader checking out the pin cannot see the pictures the findings rest on. Pass 1 met the stricter standard (everything checkable at da01a7a); pass 2 should have pinned the captures with the code, or said plainly that the images live outside the pin.

M5. The F3 repair does not name export_campaign.js. godot/campaign/test/node/export_campaign.js:1 exports from the pinned oracle files; the Board's pick deletes those copies, so this script's input changes or breaks, and neither the audit's repair list nor the Board's verdict names it as a file that must be updated.
