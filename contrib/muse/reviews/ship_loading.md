## Recommended next step

Fix finding 1 first, in aiTexReleaseAll only: register each kit material's textures when aiKitMat creates them and dispose them at teardown beside the paint maps. It is the one finding that gets worse with every new kitted hull, and it needs no new machinery, just the same entry pattern AI_TEX already uses.
