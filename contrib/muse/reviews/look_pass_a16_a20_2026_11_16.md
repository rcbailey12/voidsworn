# Look pass: A16 to A20 after the 726 repaint (PR 878)

Rey, Nov 16, 2026, at Ryan's request. This is an addendum to the ship look audit of Nov 16, 2026 (issue 881). Evidence: the run 4 paint pictures on main at 2a5399eb (paint_full composites, six views per ship, per-ship detail pictures, A20 in the game at camera 170), read against the accepted concepts and the audit's pre-repaint record. Run 5 of issue 726 was code only; the paint is run 4's, R252/R274 stack, all five ships installed.

## Verdict

The repaint is mechanically what was promised and the Board's grey flag is accurate but incomplete. Two audit flags close on this evidence. The plate scale finding stands. Ryan ruled on both open items the same evening; both rulings are recorded below for the Board.

## Closures

1. A18 engine glow (audit kind K2/K3, "no engine glow reads in the proof"): CLOSED. The dedicated engines picture shows every bell lit, hot core in the centre, per-bell hot spots and soot. The glow did not read in the earlier composite because that camera faces the bow quarter and the bells point away; a proof framing artefact, not a paint miss. Note for the record: the bells burn unevenly by design, each bell's glow painted separately, and brightness follows bell size (two large flank bells, one small centre bell). The asymmetry is visible in every stern view and is authored, not a bake error.
2. A20 hangar cheek (audit kind K4, "the proof picture does not show the feature"): CLOSED. The R283 before and after sheet shows the faceted cheek replacing the flat wall beside the port bay, and the painted six views confirm the facets under paint beside both bays. The stern view also shows five lit engines.

## Standing finding

Plate character overridden at paint (audit kind K1) STANDS after the repaint. A16's patchwork is big and uneven; A17 to A20 wear a finer, more even tiling than their concepts. Clearest on A20: the top view is a dense grid of small tiles where the concept carries broad slabs, and at the in-game camera 170 the deck reads as fine texture rather than bold patchwork. A18 and A19 show the same finer grain. The audit's fix is unchanged: per-ship plate character (size, irregularity, contrast) named in the sheet as a stack input, and the 875 plate measure run on painted output. The next Hullbreaker paint round should be judged on plate scale explicitly.

## Rulings by Ryan, Nov 16, 2026

1. Brighter grade: YES. The builds compress the concepts' palette (near-white pale plates read mid-grey, rust desaturates to brown-red, violet shrinks to small muted plates) uniformly across all five ships, including the approved control A16. Ryan ruled for the brighter concept register. This is a family grade target change (audit kind K2): per-ship colour targets taken from the accepted concept pictures, checked in the proof comparison. It is Board work, not a defect in run 4.
2. A18 dish: REMOVE. A18 carries a large dish amidships that its accepted concept does not carry there. Ryan ruled it removed. Geometry work for the Board; no repaint could have answered it.

## Noted, no action

Not checked in this pass: in-game motion and damage states, and A16 to A19 at camera 170 (only A20's sheet carries an in-game camera 170 picture). Bench light is not battle light; the grade ruling above is stated on the same bench composites the Board used.
