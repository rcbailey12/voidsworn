# Red team audit synthesis: audio seat (Oct 30, 2026)

Chair synthesis of the audio persona legs. Prongs in hand: blind Rey, DeepSeek, Argon (3 of 4). Qwen prong: PENDING FOLD-IN (daily cap at send; folds in as an addendum when it lands). Pin: main head b3a3b802. This seat had no claims register to attack (no prior audio verdict exists, itself a finding), so the register below is built from the legs' targets: the shipped sound surface, the Godot audio state, and the program's open music credits question.

## Seat verdict

NOT READY on sound. The SFX engine is genuinely well built (buses with reverb sends, a compressor and brickwall limiter, voice stealing that fades instead of cutting, salvo gain compensation, positional pan, a round-robin picker that never repeats a take). Everything wrong is coverage and routing: the shipped game has no music at all, the volume slider does not control the reverb it ships with, the most-heard sounds have the least variation, and the campaign layer rests on one ambient loop. The Godot tree holds the cure for most of it, integrated and wired, which makes the browser build's silence a cutover-distance problem, not a talent problem.

## Register: disposition

1. **The shipped browser build has no music whatsoever: CONFIRMED, 3 of 3, direct-verified.** The Rey leg grepped "music" across every browser .js and .html file: no hits. The only continuous sound on the campaign layer is a synthesized ambient bed plus one looping recorded roomtone at gain .032. Meanwhile 24 finished, licensed, loudness-levelled tracks sit in godot/music/tracks/, and the Godot music system is genuinely integrated (player instantiated at main.gd:91-95; battle start calls play_battle at battle.gd:502; intensity fed live combat stats at battle.gd:642; victory and defeat stings wired at battle.gd:1687-1689; a calm-to-intense handover band with slow smoothing in music_player.gd). The feel benchmark is cinematic; the playable build meets it in silence. This persists until cutover by the freeze, which is a sequencing fact, not an oversight anyone can patch around.
2. **The music credits question: RESOLVED on data, OPEN on presentation, 3 of 3 on the gap.** This closes the program's long-blocked item with evidence. The Rey leg verified godot/music/CREDITS.md against the tree: the 24 credited titles match the 24 track files exactly, and music.json carries each track's attribution line. DeepSeek finding 1 called the question unclosable, but that was pack-relative (CREDITS.md contents were not in its pack); the direct verification supersedes it. What does not exist is any surface that shows the credits: grep for attribution and CREDITS across the Godot .gd and .tscn files returns nothing, and the browser build has no credits screen either. Eight of the 24 tracks are CC BY 3.0 or 4.0 (map_drone, calm_tactical, calm_darktimes, hot_heat, fac_voidsworn, sting_victory, sting_champion, sting_lose), licences that require attribution as a condition of use. The music README calls the stored attribution line "ready for a credits screen," which is an admission the screen does not exist. Data work: done. Compliance exposure: live until a credits surface ships, and it must ship before any public release.
3. **The sfx.js reverb send bypasses the master gain: CONFIRMED by the chair in the clone (Argon finding 1).** sfx.js line 35 routes the convolver return into the highpass filter downstream of the master gain; line 36 taps each bus's reverb send before the master; setVol (line 22) and setOn (line 24) adjust only this.master.gain. Consequences, verified by the routing: at volume zero the dry signal is silent while reverb tails still sound at full send level, and mute leaves an unattenuated tail for the 400 ms suspend delay. A shipped defect with a small fix class (route the return through the master, or scale sends with it). Note the freeze: this is a browser-build defect, and whether it earns a break-fix slot or rides to the Godot port (where the graph should simply be built correctly) is a sequencing call; the defect itself is not in dispute.
4. **Faction coverage gap in the music listing (DeepSeek finding 4, Argon finding 3): LARGELY A NAMING ARTIFACT, screened out, with one surviving flag.** Both legs read the track names fac_corsair, fac_deathless, fac_ithar, fac_voidsworn as outside the faction list. Those are the code ids: corsair is Ashfall Hullbreakers, deathless is Eclipse, ithar is Glasswrights, voidsworn is the Sunward Crusade. All six factions have a fac_ track. The same slip touches Argon finding 3's claim about stale identifiers in the lance and torp cue dispatch: the dispatch keys on code ids because everything does. What survives from Argon finding 3 is one concrete observation: fac_corsair.ogg is 227,608 bytes against 2.2 to 3.7 MB for the other faction tracks. Whether the Hullbreakers theme is a short loop by design or an unfinished stub is unverified by any prong; it is flagged for a duration and content check, not carried as a defect.
5. **Variation poverty where it is heard most: CONFIRMED, 3 of 3 in substance.** sfx_data.js holds 27 recorded keys; 16 have exactly one variant, including every UI clip, yourTurn, enemyTurn, victory, impactChord, whoosh, launch, and scrape. UI cue pitch jitter is 2 percent. The house broadside cannon has four offline-rendered synth takes and two boomLow clips behind it, recycled across fleets of up to 20 ships. The Godot tree already fixes the recorded side (hullHit has six variants, laserLarge five). Argon finding 7 adds the synth side: cues render offline at 32 kHz (a 16 kHz ceiling on all synthesized material, Rey finding 10 concurs) into 3 to 4 baked takes, so multi-burst weapons cycle the same band-limited renders thousands of times across a multi-generation campaign. Ear fatigue lands first on the UI layer and the main gun, the two sounds with the thinnest material.
6. **The campaign layer has one acoustic room (Rey finding 4).** The recorded ambient is a single variant, looped from a random offset over a thinner synth bed. Node choices, events, shops, regroups, and the Nemesis clock all play over that one roomtone, for hours of campaign time. The Godot set answers this on paper with six map tracks; in the shipped build the sector-to-sector contrast the rest of the game builds has no audio component.
7. **Volley drop logic at fleet scale (Argon finding 5, leg-reported with quoted code).** SFX.play staggers overlapping calls to the same cue by gap and silently returns null when the staggered start exceeds the cue's defer window. gun_sq (gap .05, defer .08) starts dropping after two staggered calls; gun_shell (defer .14) after three to four simultaneous shots. At the ruled 20-ship typical battle, multi-turret salvos and squadron strikes routinely lose reports or lag up to 140 ms behind the visual flash. Not chair re-verified; the code is quoted in the leg and the arithmetic is plain. This is the audio half of the VFX seat's flash-budget problem: the biggest moments are where both systems quietly thin out.
8. **Missing cues for the game's signature events (Argon finding 6).** There are no dedicated cues for boarding, brace, or Nemesis adaptation; banner() triggers ui_boss only on the exact string "Sector flagship." And the outcome players hear on roughly 80 percent of runs (sting_loss) is offline synthesis, while the rare win gets a recorded sample (sting_win). The central hook of the game, an adapting rival inside a generational loss loop, has no sonic identity.
9. **Cue triggers are keyed to display strings (Rey finding 8).** banner() decides which sting plays by matching banner text and color literals ("Your turn", "#ff8a6a", "Sector flagship"). Any rename or VFX color tweak silently removes or misassigns the sound, with no error anywhere. The Godot SFX port (sfx.gd, cues.json, whose existence the Rey leg verified by direct read) is the chance to key cues to events instead of captions. Argon finding 9's stronger claim, that the Godot audio tree shows no runtime SFX graph porting sfx.js's salvo, pan, and composite logic, is NOT established: the leg worked from a truncated listing, and the Rey leg's direct read found the port files. Whether the port faithfully carries the salvo gain scaling, salvo tails, and composite destruction layers (boom_l plus bass_drop plus delayed wreck) is unverified by either prong and is carried as a verification task.
10. **Mix architecture notes (Argon finding 8, Rey findings 7 and 9).** The UI bus shares the master compressor and limiter with explosions, so heavy detonations pump UI clicks and warnings down with them; duck() attenuates only the ambient bus and cancels active holds unconditionally, so a minor duck during a win or loss sting releases the sting's own 3-second duck early. The voice cap still reads the retired mobile flag (maxVoices: G.mobile?20:34), so a misclassified desktop (the drift verdict's coarse-pointer case) gets the 20-voice budget in the densest broadsides; the phone target was dropped and the game is PC only. And the browser build gives the player one volume knob for all sound; the Godot build adds a music slider but SFX sub-mixes remain one knob there too.

## Ranked actions: Opus lane (technical)

1. Build a credits surface in the Godot build (title screen or settings) that renders the music.json attribution lines, before any public release. The data is ready; this is presentation only, and it is the seat's only compliance item.
2. Fix the reverb routing in the Godot audio graph by construction (return through master, or sends scaled by master volume). Decide whether the browser build's identical defect earns a freeze exception as a break fix or is documented as known and ported correctly; do not leave it ambiguous.
3. Rework the gap/defer drop logic for fleet scale: raise or remove the defer cliff for salvo-class cues, or coalesce simultaneous identical cues into one louder composite instead of dropping them past the second or third call.
4. Add the missing signature cues: boarding, brace, and Nemesis adaptation first; a recorded or richer defeat sting second, since the loss is the game's most-heard ending.
5. Key Godot cues to events, not display strings (complete the sfx.gd/cues.json direction), and verify the port carries sfx.js's salvo gain scaling, salvo tails, positional pan and attenuation, and composite destruction layers; write the check as a test, not a read.
6. Variant pass on the thinnest material: UI clips and the broadside cannon first; render synth cues at 44.1 or 48 kHz in the Godot pipeline instead of 32 kHz.
7. Give the campaign layer more than one room: the six Godot map tracks solve this at cutover; if the browser build remains the playable truth for long, a second ambient variant is the cheapest relief.
8. Check fac_corsair.ogg's duration and content (the 227 KB flag from item 4); replace it if it is a stub.
9. Drop the retired mobile branch from the voice budget (PC only: 34), and add per-layer volume (music, weapons, UI, ambience) to the Godot settings while the audio graph is being built anyway.
