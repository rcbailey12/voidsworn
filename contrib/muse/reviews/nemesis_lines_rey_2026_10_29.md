## Notes

- NEM_FAC lists all six factions (voidsworn, hegemony, corsair, chorus, ithar, deathless), so the debrief pools cover all six under their display names: Sunward Crusade, Hegemony, Ashfall Hullbreakers, the Chorus, Glasswrights, Eclipse. None omitted.
- NEM_FAC gives fem and masc name lists for several factions, so the Nemesis's gender varies by roll. The debrief lines therefore avoid gendered pronouns for the Nemesis throughout, and the spoken lines are all first-person, so no line depends on the Nemesis's gender.
- Every loss-reveal and win-reveal line was checked against all seven weakness names (Thin stern plating, Brittle shield emitters, Poor point defense, Green boarding crews, Long-gun doctrine, A proud flagship, Worn engines) and reads sensibly with each name in the [WEAKNESS] slot, including the two that are phrases rather than parts (Long-gun doctrine, A proud flagship).
- The again lines rely on the pack's stated rule that an adaptation may not directly counter a revealed weakness; they claim a counter to the winning method ({beat}), never to a weakness, which is all the tables support.
- The gloat flagship line assumes the flagship was destroyed in the previous meeting, which is the shipped trigger's own framing; no new fact is introduced.
- One judgment call, flagged rather than hidden: the Eclipse loss-reveal line about a recovered backup leans on the deathless behaviour text in NEM_FAC (backups, reassembly), which is in the tables; it is written as a partial, partly legible fragment to keep the defeat register's uncertainty.
