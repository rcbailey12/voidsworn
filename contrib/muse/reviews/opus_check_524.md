# Outside check: PR 524 (issue 461, hits upgraded)

Refs #256. PR head e2c34aba9885174087b12963f316f39d0159ecbf (opus/issue-461), base main 7d96357956. Checked against the issue 461 brief and the chaired issue 352 table (synthesis_352.md). Council: blind Rey leg, DeepSeek, Qwen (re-run, see the council note), Flash, Grok. All legs read the complete 14-file diff, the brief and the 352 table.

## Verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS

The build is real: a recipe per weapon family, sizing by gun and target, matter that outlives flame, wounds that stay, neighbour light, and all four carried items are in the code, with an honest headless check wired into both CI lists. Two conditions block merge; the rest are cheap.

## Blocking conditions

1. **The PR carries none of its done-when evidence.** The brief's Done when is written in captures and numbers: the ladder plainly bigger at each step in real captures at the battle camera, the flash counter's refusal count in the 20-ship storm gate, frame intervals before and after, texture memory, the report at contrib/opus/reviews/hits_report.md, and the named strips plus filmed fight under docs/shots/look_bench/hits/. None of it is in the 14 files. The playbook and two code comments cite the report as the place the costs live; the file does not exist in this PR. All five legs found this. Land the report and the shots, or have the Board record on the issue why they follow in a separate PR. The storm-gate numbers matter more than usual here because of condition 2's companion finding: _burst now books every flash at px / BURST_PX_K (about 1.54x the diameter, about 2.37x the area) on the bench finding that the baked flash blooms past its floor. Only the new hull-hit paths pre-scale and book at their ladder size. Every other caller (_crit at 40, _bio_splat at 28, the shield beam pulses at 32, the retained _impact_old path) books about 2.37x more area than before this PR, and a macro hit books twice (the staged flash, and _shell_hit's extra burst), while a lance burn books at full lance size about every 0.18 s. Whether any of that refuses something a player would miss is exactly what the missing report exists to answer.

2. **A torpedo on a shield is drawn and booked off its ladder size, and is refused every time at cruiser scale and above.** _shield_impact computes the ladder row correctly (110 px flash, 156 px ripple, scaled by gun and target, rockets at 0.65) but passes the flash size to _burst unscaled, where the hull paths pass ladder * BURST_PX_K. So it books at about 169 px (area about 22,500 px2), over the counter's 15,606 px2 limit on its own, and draws about half again bigger than its row. This is the failure the PR's own ladder comment says the 140 px hull torpedo was chosen to avoid. Four legs found it independently (DeepSeek, Flash, Grok, blind Rey); Qwen's leg missed it. hit_recipe_check never fires a torpedo at a shield (all its volleys land on hulls; the shield row enters only as a constant in _ladder), so the path is untested. Fix: pass spx * BURST_PX_K like the hull paths, and add a shield torpedo to the check's looks.

## Cheap conditions

3. **Reconcile the playbooks.** deaths_and_hits.md now contradicts itself on ion: the new family list and family_of put ion in the lance row (blue-white burn, arcs, no molten spray, and _beam_burn builds exactly that), while the faction paragraph on the same page still says ion fire is a macro hit. effects.md still gives the worked example "a torpedo on a hull flashes 154 px for 0.16 s" and claims each ladder step is about 1.4x, while this PR builds 140 and the top steps run 1.79x (lance to torpedo) and 1.27x (shield to hull). The brief requires the playbooks to carry the build's rules back; these lines were left stale.

4. **Write down the needle exception.** family_of puts needle in lance and _beam_burn crawls it like a beam at half reach, but ladder_px sizes it from the energy row (40/46/52, not 70/74/78). Probably right for a light beam; neither the ladder comment nor the playbook records it, so a reader checking code against playbook finds an unexplained mismatch. One line in each place.

5. **Disclose the lance cut's limits in the playbook, or lift them.** Carried item (b) is built, but with two limits the playbook does not state: fracture_plan clamps the cut position to the middle band (lx clamped to 0.3 to 0.7, so a beam that kills near the bow or stern does not cut on its own line), and _death_inner runs the recut only for big hulls of non-Chorus factions killed by a cut. The playbook states the clean-cut rule without limits. (DeepSeek finding; verified in the diff at chair.)

## Recorded, not conditions

- **The 140 px torpedo is a reasoned, disclosed deviation from the ruled table, not a violation.** The 352 table says 154; the ladder comment and the playbook both say plainly why 140 was built (a lone 154 is over the counter's area limit and spent the budget deaths need, seen in bench round 2). Because the table is Ryan's ruling and the playbook has already been edited to match the code, the Board should put the 154 to 140 change to Ryan at the next hits look as a table amendment.
- **Order of the ladder.** The brief's Done-when lists torpedo on a hull before torpedo on a shield in a bigger-at-each-step sequence; the 352 table (ruling Q3) deliberately sets the shield flash smaller (110) with the ripple (156) as the biggest body of any hit. The chair records: the 352 table governs; the build follows it.
- **Weight overlap across families.** The ladder rows are per-family weight ladders (energy 40/46/52, shell 44/56/64, lance 70/74/78 at weights 1/2/3). The canonical steps (las w1 40, macro w2 56, lance w3 78) order correctly and the check proves those; cross-weight pairs (a heavy las at 52 against a light macro at 44) are not ordered by construction. Noted from DeepSeek; whether such pairings occur is a weapon-data question outside this diff.
- **Capital-scaled torpedoes exceed the single-flash limit by design** (140 * 1.2 * 1.2 = 201.6 px) and will be softened by the counter; the ladder comment says so ("still makes it bigger (and counted)"). The storm-gate report under condition 1 should say whether a player would miss it.
- **_shell_hit passes a 1.14 factor for weight-3 macro** into _staged_hit, on top of the gun band. Undocumented in the table; a tuning choice inside R187 latitude (Qwen finding).
- **Reduce Flashing's soft twin uses the flat maxf(life, 0.4) floor**, not the 352 table's per-row stretched lives (0.40 to 0.65). Pre-existing behaviour, and the brief froze Reduce Flashing; recorded so the difference is a known one (DeepSeek and Qwen).
- **Two leg claims screened out at chair.** DeepSeek's worry that _heat_spot might hold full heat past the 0.25 s clause is refuted by the kind 21 shader at the PR head (add = heatRamp(1.0 - t) * I * (1.0 - t): the spot cools in colour and intensity over its whole life). DeepSeek's unverified flag on the fx_ship.gd comment "four factions set one" resolves true: faction_looks.json at the PR head sets an accent for exactly four factions (voidsworn, corsair, ithar, deathless). The same stale wording the PR fixed in fx_ship.gd ("none in use yet") still stands in faction_look.gd's own header comment for the accent field; worth the same one-line fix when someone is next in that file.
- **Flash's wound-cap arithmetic** (three sections at 8 each exactly equal the 24 global cap) is the designed behaviour of a per-section cap, not a defect; noted only.
- Minor: look_moments.json loses its trailing newline; the look bench's e_engine_close row sets engines on every ship in the scene, not only the attacker its label names (DeepSeek); scene_gate's flash row trusts the log entry shape (DeepSeek).

## What the check proves, and what it cannot

hit_recipe_check.gd proves, on box ships, headless: the ladder's ordering and gun/target scaling from the sizing rule itself; each family's recipe element types (shell debris and sparks, energy sparks without debris, lance heat points along the crawl, torpedo gas and debris, ion without molten spray); no flash past 0.70 s and sparks under a second; outward directions; a torpedo light reach of at least 30 (asserted at 30, weaker than the 36 built); Reduce Flashing booking and counting nothing; the lance plan's geometry at one test point; the flicker strings; the cap constants. It cannot prove anything visual at the battle camera, the storm gate, the ring's absence, the bow piece at 6.0 s, close-camera flicker as a phenomenon, or costs. Carried items (a), (c) and the visual half of (d) rest on code reading, not on the check.
