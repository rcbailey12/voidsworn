1. Verdict

THE STACKS HOLD ONLY WITH REPLACEMENTS.

The three inventory items my verdict most depends on are E1, A12, and S15.

E1, the effects 60 fps at 4K target, is the unmeasured performance premise under the effects tier rules. If it is not re-derived with an effects-on and effects-off benchmark, many other effects entries are calibrated to a fiction.

A12, the glide movement rule, is the clearest animation case where a feel ruling with no parameters stands in for a movement specification. The animation stack does not fail because its numbers are wrong. It fails because several rules cannot be checked against anything a builder or reviewer can measure.

S15, the listen pass that has never run, is the sound stack's central gate. The sound stack has two verified defects and many pre-listening numbers. Until the owner listen pass executes, the sound quality gate is an event that has not happened.

The stacks do not need to be discarded wholesale. Many items are measured baselines, safety rules, or owner direction. But the assumed numeric gates and absolute feel rules need replacement by the named tests, captures, or listen passes in the pack.
