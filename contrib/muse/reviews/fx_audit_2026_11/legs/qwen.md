VERDICT
THE STACKS HOLD ONLY WITH REPLACEMENTS.
The three stacks together cannot stand as written. Of 76 inventory items, 55 are ASSUMED, 3 are ANCHORED, 8 are MEASURED, and 5 are DERIVED. The worked example in section 1.2 established the owner's ruling: assumed and anchored numeric gates are ripped out, not re-derived one by one; a constraint earns its way back only by proving itself in builds. Applied here, the majority of binding numbers and rules in all three stacks fail that test. However, the stacks do not simply collapse: the flash regime (E6, DERIVED from accessibility guidance) protects player safety, the licence gate (S1) protects legal standing, and several MEASURED items (E2, E14, E23, E26, A2, A5, S5, S9, S10, S14, S15, S16) are genuine baselines. The replacements needed are not new numbers but measurements, tests, and owner judgements that the pack itself specifies but that have not run.
The three items this verdict most depends on:
E1. The 60 fps at 4K target is the root performance gate for the entire effects stack. It is ASSUMED (section 3, E1): no frame budget allocation, no minimum spec decision, no measured effects cost stands behind it. Every tier rule, every emitter cap, every budget split in the effects inventory is calibrated to this number. If it is fiction, the calibration is fiction. If it is easily held, effects are being underbuilt against the AAA objective. It is the single number whose replacement (by the issue 207 benchmark with effects isolation) would convert the most downstream assumptions into arithmetic.
A3. The movement speed ruling ("about twice as slow") is the core feel gate for the animation stack. It is ASSUMED (section 4, A3): not tied to a stated baseline speed, a timing measurement, or a capture. No follow-up measurement verifies the shipped result. Three later rulings (R55, R64, R76) re-rule movement on top of it, which is the pattern an untestable specification produces. The animation stack has zero DERIVED items and zero ANCHORED items; nothing was ever computed from anything else. A3 is the ruling the entire stack's feel hangs on, and it has no number.
S15. The listen pass has never run. This is MEASURED as an absence (section 5, S15): verified in two documents written by the process itself. Every number in the sound stack (S6, S7, S11, S18) is a pre-listening number. The stack's quality gate is an event that has not happened. The battle sound synthesis states that nobody in its process listened to any audio file, including the shipped cues. The entire domain's judgements are pending a single pass. No replacement number can substitute for the owner hearing the artefact.
