# Effects implementation review, synthesis, 2026-10-26

Chair synthesis of four blind inputs on godot/fx/ as built, judged against spec A (contrib/muse/reviews/damage_marks_reference.md) and spec B (the counted-flash rule in contrib/muse/reviews/a5_effects_sheets_synthesis.md, section 8), plus the fx API doc's own claims. Inputs: DeepSeek leg (contrib/deepseek/reviews/fx_review_deepseek_2026_10_26.md), Qwen leg (contrib/qwen/reviews/fx_review_qwen_2026_10_26.md), Argon leg (contrib/gemini/reviews/fx_review_gemini_2026_10_26.md), Rey audit (contrib/muse/reviews/fx_implementation_review_rey.md). All four worked from the same evidence pack (chair-built from the fetched godot/fx sources); code claims below carry the file:line references the legs and pack agree on. Visual verdicts (how any of this reads in motion) stay with Ryan's playtest; this review judges code against spec.

Overall verdict: PARTIAL, leaning MISS on the specs as written. The transient effects and the staged-damage ladder are strong, and the zoom tier's breach geometry exceeds spec A. But the two things the specs actually specify are mostly not built: per-weapon persistent marks (only gauss and bio have their own, and both lack parts of their spec behaviour), and a flash budget that covers what is on screen (the budget certifies a gated subset while several of the brightest emitters bypass it).

## A note on verdict labels

The four inputs agree on nearly every material fact and disagree on some labels (torpedo PARTIAL in three legs, MISSES in DeepSeek; flash coverage PARTIAL in Qwen and Argon, MISSES in DeepSeek and Rey; counted-flash definition split two and two). The chair adopts one label per item below and names the spread where it exists. The chair's bar for coverage: WCAG counts flashes on screen, not calls into a budget function, so a budget the brightest emitters bypass does not meet the spec it claims to implement.

## A. Per-weapon marks (chair verdicts)

1. Battery (including plasma): PARTIAL, all four legs. Light looks (auto, las, scatter, prism) leave no persistent mark at all (fx_weapons_gd.txt:590, 634-635). Medium and heavy hits place individual generic scorch decals into one ship-wide array instead of baking into a per-section scorch layer (spec A entry 10). Plasma's persistent trace is the same generic orange-ember scorch; its identity (slag sprites, drip streaks) is transient only, about 2 to 3 s (fx_weapons_gd.txt:622-626). Whether the transient weapon colour in faction data is the light blue Ryan ruled on Oct 26 is UNSURE from the fx files; the persistent mark has no plasma identity either way, and spec A's droplet fan and splash crater are absent.
2. Lance: PARTIAL, all four legs. The transient molten trench is the best beam treatment in the set; the persistent trace is one round generic scorch decal (fx_weapons_gd.txt:728-729). Spec A's lit slot (the only line-shaped mark, lengthening on repeats, possible far-side exit) does not exist.
3. Ion: MISSES SPEC, all four legs. Ion runs the identical _beam_burn as lance and leaves the identical scorch (fx_weapons_gd.txt:697-730). The branching tree, the most settled mark in the whole reference, is absent. Ion's only distinct extra is a transient shield wash when the target has a shield material.
4. Torpedo: PARTIAL (DeepSeek: MISSES; facts agreed). The transient is the heaviest in the game; the persistent trace is the largest generic scorch decal, 2.6 times scale (fx_weapons_gd.txt:820-821). Spec A's blast crater at the hit point, denting the outline, does not exist; crater-class geometry appears only when a section crosses stage 3 or 4 HP thresholds, at a pre-picked anchor, triggered by any damage source (fx_ship_gd.txt:466-476, 574-586).
5. Flak: NOT IMPLEMENTED, all four legs in substance. Flak sits in the light set and leaves no decal (fx_weapons_gd.txt:590). Spec A's stippled pockmark patch is absent, and flak chip damage still drives stage 3 and 4 structural breaches, the opposite of the spec's never-exposes-structure rule. (Chorus spores route to bio and leave an acid hole; that is the bio mark, not a flak mark.)
6. Bio: PARTIAL, all four legs. A real family mark: corrosion hole decal at the hit point with a green fringe tint (fx_weapons_gd.txt:747-751). Spec A's spread, visible stop, and scar transition do not exist; the decal is created at full size with fixed uniforms and never changes.
7. Gauss: PARTIAL, all four legs; closest to spec of any family. Real entry and exit hole decals with parallax cavity (fx_weapons_gd.txt:766-786). Spec A's distance-tier companion cue (pale stress ring or petaled collar) is absent, and the entry hole is the smallest persistent mark in the game, so gauss history is invisible at fleet distance.
8. Squadron weapons: MEETS SPEC, all four legs. Transient only, inside spec B's spark-ticks allowance. Two quirks worth a line each: squadron bombs and sporeb leave medium decals identical to ship-mounted weapons (Argon), and squadron ships as targets never show stage damage (fx_ship_gd.txt:481-482, Rey; scored under the distance tier).
9. Ram: MISSES SPEC, all four legs in substance. Ram routes into _big_hit at 1.5 times scale and leaves the same glowing scorch as a torpedo (fx_weapons_gd.txt:605-606, 793-821). Spec A's unlit crumple breaking the outline, directional scrapes, and a matching smaller crumple on the ramming ship are all absent; the only silhouette change in the build is the stage ladder's edge bite, which any damage source triggers at a pre-picked anchor.
10. Boarding: MISSES SPEC, all four legs in substance. The tendril routes to _bio_splat; the lash flag's only effect is slightly stronger shake (fx_weapons_gd.txt:745). The trace is the bio acid hole at a random surface sample. Spec A's gash at a boarding point, live venting and interior light, and patch-plate scar are absent from godot/fx. UNSURE whether the rules or UI layer draws any boarding visual; none of the four inputs found one in fx.
11. Cross-cutting behaviour: PARTIAL overall, with split sub-verdicts.
    - Fresh/cooling/scarred evolution: MISSES SPEC for hit and hole decals (fixed emission 0.7 on scorch, fx_death_gd.txt:141; hole decal heat is a static uniform, and the DECAL shader's own header comment claims cooling its fragment code does not implement, fx_shaders_gd.txt:737, 765-833). MEETS for stage breach interiors, which cool properly by age (fx_shaders_gd.txt:874-876). All cooling runs on clock seconds; nothing is turn-keyed.
    - Caps and repeats: MISSES SPEC. One ship-wide array capped at 10, trimmed only inside add_hit_scorch (fx_ship_gd.txt:816-826); gauss and bio appends bypass the trim and can be evicted by later scorch calls. No per-section budget, no repeat growth or merging anywhere.
    - Repair reversal: PARTIAL. Stage geometry reverses on repair; hit decals are never removed, so a repaired section still wears its scorch and holes, against spec A's rule that hull language and game state must not disagree.
    - Shield-only-hit rule: MEETS SPEC, all four legs (fx_weapons_gd.txt:140, 591-593). Pure shield hits leave no hull mark.
    - Renderer caveat: under the Compatibility renderer, scorch and hole decals draw nothing at all (fx_death_gd.txt:132-135, 155-158). If that renderer ships as a selectable path, every persistent-mark verdict above weakens on it.

## B. The counted-flash rule (chair verdicts)

1. Definition: PARTIAL (split two and two across the legs). flash_ok takes only a timestamp; it measures no brightness, size, contrast, or emitter identity (fx_gd.txt:315-334). Spec B counts per-emitter dark-bright-dark pairs above a moderate contrast step and exempts dim sparks below it. The chair scores PARTIAL because a consistent counting mechanism exists and is honestly applied to its gated set; the contrast step and per-emitter terms are simply not in it.
2. Cap and counting window: PARTIAL. The cap value itself MEETS SPEC and holds for every gated caller: FLASH_CAP is 3 (fx_gd.txt:29). The window is two-sided (a recorded flash counts against T within 1.0 s on either side, fx_gd.txt:319-323), where spec B and the API doc describe a trailing rolling second. Because volleys pre-schedule future impact times, a later-scheduled flash can refuse an earlier one. Conservative, never over the cap, but not the specified window.
3. Merge window: MISSES SPEC, and this is the item the four inputs settle. Three positions sit within a page of each other: spec B says 0.33 s; the comment directly above flash_ok says flashes inside 0.33 s merge (fx_gd.txt:314); the constant is 0.12 s (fx_gd.txt:28) and the API doc follows the constant (fx_api_md.txt:128). Argon's salvo arithmetic closes the question of which value the design intends: salvo staggers run 0.08 s (macro) to 0.22 s (auto), so at 0.12 s a 4-shot macro salvo consumes 2 counted flashes and a 3-shot gauss salvo consumes all 3, while at 0.33 s both merge into one salvo event, which is what spec B's merged-per-salvo rule describes. Chair recommendation: set FLASH_WINDOW to 0.33 and correct the API doc to match, or, if Opus prefers 0.12 as a deliberate design change, amend spec B and the code comment so the three documents agree. The defect is the disagreement; the evidence points to 0.33 as the intent.
4. Coverage: MISSES SPEC (Qwen and Argon: PARTIAL; same facts). Gated: pop() callers, gauss muzzle, beam bloom, flak cores, the death sequence's first blast sprite. Ungated, all bright: battery and flak muzzle cores (intensity 11 core checks only reduce_flashing, fx_weapons_gd.txt:317-327; auto 6.0, las 7.0, flak 3.0 kind 0 sprites with no gate at all), beam charge and contact sprites (9.0 sustained contact, fx_weapons_gd.txt:701), beam ribbons and flares, the pooled event-light system (peaks to 14.0, fx_gd.txt:349-410), the torpedo fireball volume and its sympathetic second pop, shield ripple shaders, and the death flame train after the first gated blast. The flash_log the tests check certifies only the gated subset, so the API doc's WCAG claim is not currently supported by the mechanism.
5. Over-budget behaviour: PARTIAL, all four legs. The sprite side steps down as specified (flak loses its core and keeps smoke and sparks; refused pops draw a longer, dimmer sprite plus a ring). The light side does not: every pop() caller also fires fx.light unconditionally, and fx.light attenuates only under reduce_flashing, not when flash_ok refuses (Argon; fx_gd.txt:349-353).
6. Reduce-flashing mode: PARTIAL, all four legs. For the gated set the mode is complete: zero counted flashes, calmed shader flicker, softened decay lights, halved shake. Outside the gated set it leaks: auto, las and flak muzzle sprites emit raw with no reduce check; beam contact sprites, _big_hit's two-stage pops and jump_out sprites do not check the mode; sustain-shape event lights keep an ~8 Hz flicker term (fx_gd.txt:372). This answers the accessibility review's open question: reduce mode zeroes the log, not the screen.

## C. The two-tier read

Distance tier: PARTIAL, all four legs. How hurt and which section is worst read well through the stage ladder. What has been hitting the ship does not read at all once transients end. The spec's glow and emitter ration (at most 2 glowing marks and 1 emitter per ship) has no counterpart in code; Argon quantifies the overshoot (up to 10 glowing decals, up to 4 stage scorch decals per section, 3 particle batches per damaged section). Stage geometry sits at pre-picked anchors biased upward, not at hit locations. Squadron ships are a blind spot: their stages are forced to 0,0,0, so a half-dead squadron ship reads pristine (fx_ship_gd.txt:481-482).

Zoom tier: PARTIAL (Rey: MEETS, scoped to the breach geometry). The chair splits the difference on scope: the stage 3 and 4 breach interiors, cut geometry with peeled plates, and the gauss and bio parallax cavities meet or exceed spec A's detail layer. But 7 of the 9 weapon families have no spec zoom mark at all, so a close inspection of most hits finds a generic flat scorch texture. Both statements are true; the verdict follows the wider scope.

Hull sections: the build has 3 sections (engine, mid, prow; fx_hull_gd.txt:8-10) against spec A's assumed 4 to 8. The budget maths adapts cleanly (3 per section is 9 per ship, close to the existing cap of 10 if the pool were partitioned and battery scorches excluded). Granularity is the cost: one section spans the full width of a third of the ship, so stage holes cannot distinguish port from starboard. Qwen's fix proposing 3 to 4+ hull sections is a structural option for Opus to price, not an fx fix, and the chair does not rank it below.

## Ranked fixes (chair order)

1. Gate or calm the ungated bright emitters, and attenuate fx.light when flash_ok refuses: battery and flak muzzle cores, auto/las/flak kind 0 sprites, beam charge and contact sprites, _big_hit's secondary pops and fireball, the death flame train, and the event-light pool. Repairs B4, B5, B6. This is the only fix that makes the WCAG claim true.
2. Settle the merge window at 0.33 s in the constant and the API doc (fx_gd.txt:28; fx_api_md.txt:128), matching spec B and the code's own comment. One-line change plus a doc fix. Repairs B3.
3. Make the counting window trailing (fx_gd.txt:319-323). Repairs B2.
4. Partition _hit_decals into per-section signature slots (3 per section), bake battery scorches into the section scorch layer, grow the existing mark on repeat family hits, and clear a section's decals when repair lowers its stage. Repairs A1, A11, and the eviction bug.
5. Build the missing signature marks, in spec-confidence order: ion branching tree first (most settled in the reference, currently zero), lance slot, gauss stress ring or collar, flak pockmark patch, plasma splash with the light blue core Ryan ruled (shape carries identity), boarding gash at a socket with a patch-plate scar stage. Repairs A3, A2, A7, A5, A1, A10.
6. Tie craters and silhouette change to torpedo and ram at the actual hit point: torpedo cuts its crater on impact, ram cuts an unlit crumple on the target and a smaller one on the rammer's bow, and the stage ladder's edge bite stops standing in for both. Repairs A4, A9, C distance.
7. Decal cooling: record a birth time at creation and decay heat in the DECAL shader, as its header already claims; ideally step it by turns, per spec A's freshness rule. Repairs A11, A6.
8. Enforce the fleet-distance ration: cap glowing marks and active emitters per ship at distance, and let squadron ships show at least the scorch stages. Repairs C distance.
9. Add a contrast step to flash_ok so small dim pops below a set intensity and size do not spend the budget. Repairs B1.
10. Decide the Compatibility renderer path: if it ships, persistent marks need a fallback there or the marks spec applies only to the Forward+ build. Flagged, not ranked as a code fix.
