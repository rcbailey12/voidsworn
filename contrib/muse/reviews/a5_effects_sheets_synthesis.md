## 1. The spine both legs agree on (adopt)

- The R16 bar (Ryan): damage reads as holes with streaming parts, fire, and other damage states, not orange lines. Weapons and engine trails must feel more realistic and cinematic, up to BattleTech, Sins of a Solar Empire, and Rogue Trader. Both legs build every sheet to that bar.
- Shields keep faction colours (R3). The cyan-only shield rule is retired for 3D shields; interface shield bars stay cyan. Shielded factions: Voidsworn, Hegemony, Corsair, Ithar. Chorus and Deathless take hull hits, no shield. Corsair shields flicker and are patchy. Corsair engines sputter and cough soot (SPEC).
- SPEC effects grammar: weapons read by rate, trail, count, smoke, and timing, not only colour; volleys ripple (desynced), never fire as one blob; impact flash, debris, and shake scale with weapon weight and the big guns stay special; damage is staged on the hull with venting, sparks, and fire at the breach, and parts that come off leave torn edges with squibs, never a clean swap; destroyed ships leave a drifting wreck with fires and debris, not a vanishing act; one key light, fixed exposure, effects add light, they do not change exposure.
- Engine-neutral: no Babylon-only or Godot-only implementation code. The engine call (Godot 4.7 or Babylon 9.30) is still pending. Effects are authored scene-linear, glow before tone mapping.
- Accessibility: hard cap 3 flashes per second, plus a reduce-flashing option. See section 8 for the one genuine definition difference and the chair's merged recommendation.
- Seven weapon kinds only: battery, flak, lance, torp, bio, gauss, sq. No eighth kind in either leg.
- Sound cues in both legs are design descriptions (words only), not claims about existing game audio.

### 2.2 SPEC effect loop pins (centres for the testing ranges)

Per ship, 4-second loop staggered by 0.5 s. Engines: hot core at each nozzle (white-yellow centre, faction-tinted edge), turbulent plume about 1.5 ship-widths long, long fading exhaust trail about 60 units, thinning and cooling as it fades, slightly wavering. Broadside macro-cannons at 0.0 s: four guns ripple-fire 80 ms apart, big muzzle blast (flash, flame tongue, smoke ring lingering about 2 s), bright shell tracer at 160 units per second toward a point 70 units off the port side, impact flash, sparks, small debris spray, faction tint on flash and tracer. Lance at 1.5 s: thick beam from the bow to a point 80 units ahead, 0.7 s, white-hot core, faction-coloured glow, charge-up flare 0.25 s before, heat shimmer, bright impact flare with spark fountain. Shield or hull hit at 2.4 s: shielded factions get a partial shield bubble rippling from the hit point in faction shield colour, fading over 0.6 s (Corsair flickers, patchy); Chorus and Deathless get a hull hit with flash, sparks, debris burst. Hull breach (persistent, from the start): one breach on the upper port flank, about 1/10 ship length across, dark scorched torn hole with jagged glowing-hot edges, venting gas jet, small debris tumbling out, licking fire, intermittent spark showers, small secondary explosion every few seconds. Destruction event (scene-wide, once per 12 s): a seventh ship, Hegemony hull at 0.6 scale above the formation, explodes with blinding flash, expanding shockwave ring, rolling fireball with dark smoke, 3 to 5 large hull chunks with burning edges and squibs, spray of small debris and embers, burning wreck chunk drifting on; it reappears whole 2 s before the next event (fade in).

## 3. Per-weapon sheets (merged)

Weight note, read first: the legs scale impact on different axes, and both are proposals. Qwen scales by weapon weight (light, medium, heavy per kind). DeepSeek scales by target class (light, medium, heavy, capital) and labels that a proposal. The evidence pack defines neither axis. This synthesis keeps both: each sheet below gives Qwen's weapon-weight behaviour and DeepSeek's target-class table where it adds information, and both are labelled PROPOSAL for Opus to adopt, replace, or combine. Do not present either axis as game data.

### 3.1 Battery (broadside macro-cannon, Rogue Trader style)

Fire: four-gun ripple, SPEC pin 80 ms apart (testing range 70 to 90 ms, DeepSeek; Qwen salvo ripple 0.06 to 0.12 s brackets the pin). Wind-up: mechanical (breech, elevation, hydraulic twitch), per-gun 0.05 to 0.15 s, full broadside 0.20 to 0.50 s (DeepSeek compromise; Qwen 0.05 to 0.15 s for medium and heavy, none for light, consistent). Muzzle: layered, not a single sprite (Qwen): additive flash 0.05 to 0.12 s, flame tongue, smoke ring drifting 1.5 to 2.5 s, 2 to 6 ember sparks, momentary point light. DeepSeek adds flipbook variation so consecutive flashes do not repeat identically. Travel: visible shell tracer, bright core, short hot streak, SPEC pin 160 units per second (range 140 to 180), impact point SPEC pin 70 units (range 60 to 80), flight roughly 0.35 to 0.55 s. No coloured line drawn across the screen. Impact on shields: partial bubble ripple in defender faction shield colour, fade SPEC pin 0.6 s (range 0.5 to 0.7), ripple radius 8 to 16 units (DeepSeek), Qwen ring pulse 0.15 to 0.4 s, 4 to 12 ricochet sparks, no decal. Impact on hull: Qwen by weapon weight (light spark burst and tiny scorch, medium spark plus 2 to 5 chips and dent ring, heavy debris spray 5 to 12 fragments, dust plume, fire lick 0.3 to 0.8 s, scorched-plus-dented decal with exposed metal, first tier where R16 applies directly). DeepSeek by target class (light scorch stage 1, medium dent stage 1 to 2, heavy stage 2 to 3 with small breach possible on repeated hits, capital stage 3 with breach likely after several hits). Both agree: a single battery shell does not jump straight to breach on a heavy or capital ship. Sound: layered mechanical report (body thump, breech clank, boom tail, shell whoosh), four beats 70 to 90 ms apart, not one merged bang. Faction: tint on muzzle flash edge, tracer core, impact flash per the table; smoke stays neutral dark grey with faint faction tint in the first 0.3 to 0.5 s (DeepSeek). Qwen's neutral white tracer core for distance identification is adopted as a readability rule: tint lives in the outer glow, not the core.

### 3.2 Flak (point-defence burst gun, proximity-fuzed shells)

Fire: no wind-up in Qwen (panicky, immediate); DeepSeek allows 0.10 to 0.30 s tracking and spin-up. Synthesis: keep wind-up at the short end (0 to 0.30 s) and let Opus test whether zero reads better. Muzzle: small, fast, dirty, 1 to 2 frames, soot puff 0.3 to 0.6 s. Count: DeepSeek 6 to 12 shells per burst, 2 to 4 bursts, 3 to 6 barrels ripple 40 to 80 ms; Qwen burst 4 to 10 shells at 0.04 to 0.1 s ripple, heavy flak 3 to 6 larger shells at 0.08 to 0.15 s. Adopt the overlap: burst 4 to 12 shells, ripple 0.04 to 0.1 s. Travel: dim tracer, heavy smoke trail, faster than battery (Qwen relative 1.2 to 1.5 times battery light; DeepSeek absolute 180 to 260 units per second). Both are proposals in different units; Opus converts once a battery baseline is locked. Impact is mostly airburst, not direct hit (both legs): spherical burst 0.2 to 0.5 s, bright core pop, 8 to 20 shrapnel sparks (Qwen) or 10 to 30 fragments (DeepSeek), smoke ball hanging 2 to 6 s with working band 2 to 4 s so the field does not fill with fog. Fuse: proximity 20 to 40 units (DeepSeek) or 1 to 4 m of a craft or torp (Qwen, different unit, same idea), backup time fuse 1.0 to 2.5 s. Direct shield hits: tiny faction bloom, no ripple ring, reads as harmless. Direct hull hits: pitting only, never past scorched by itself (Qwen) or stage 1, massed fire to stage 2 (DeepSeek). Kill confirmation: one secondary fireball 0.3 to 0.8 s with 3 to 8 debris chunks, must read clearly (Rogue Trader torp-destruction readability pass is the comparable). Sound: rapid chatter, crack, papery tear, distant thumps. Faction: neutral white-yellow burst core for readability, smoke tinted with faction secondary at very low saturation (Qwen); tracer tips in faction weapon colour (DeepSeek). Both can hold: core neutral, tips and smoke tinted.

### 3.3 Lance (sustained beam, Sins style, the R16 showcase)

Fire: charge-up flare SPEC pin 0.25 s before fire. Testing range: DeepSeek compromise 0.20 to 0.40 s, lower half preferred. Qwen's range (0.3 to 0.8 s medium and heavy, 0.05 to 0.15 s light snap-charge) does NOT include the pin and is recorded as a proposal that runs long against SPEC; Opus tests from the pin outward, not from Qwen's range. Emitter flare in faction weapon colour with white-hot centre, heat shimmer, rising hum. No chemical muzzle flash. Beam: one per mount, multiple mounts staggered 150 to 300 ms (DeepSeek); heavy lance is one thick beam, not several thin ones (Qwen). SPEC pin: bow to 80 units ahead, 0.7 s duration (range 0.6 to 0.8 s), white-hot core, faction glow shell, rolling internal noise, heat shimmer along the length, afterglow 0.3 to 0.6 s cooling white to faction colour to transparent. Hitscan read, or fast travelling beam 300 to 600 units per second as a readability alternative (DeepSeek); Qwen allows 5 to 10 times battery speed for the same reason. Impact on shields: wide stressed area, bright core 2 to 4 frames in defender shield colour, ripple 0.5 to 0.8 s, smoother than battery; Corsair flickers 3 to 5 times with brief patchy hole (DeepSeek); Qwen adds sustained contact glow with slow rings one per 0.4 to 1.0 s and 10 to 30 concurrent tangential sparks. Adopt both: initial touch bloom counts as the one flash, then sustained glow and slow rings. Impact on hull (the R16 showcase, both legs agree on the mechanism): molten white-hot contact spot with dark red cooling ring, continuous spark and droplet spray, widening molten trench with emissive edges cooling white to orange to dark char over 1.5 to 4 s (Qwen), transitioning scorched to dented to exposed structure to breach as damage accumulates; streaming parts 1 to 4 per second of sustained fire (cables, gas jets, peeling panels); fire plumes on crossing to breach. DeepSeek target-class table: light burn line stage 2, medium torn edges stage 2 to 3, heavy glowing torn edges stage 3, capital stage 3 to 4 with breach likely. Sound: rising electrical hum, sharp crack at fire, sustained sizzle with crackle, hull-resonance drone on heavy, power-down chirp and steam hiss at end. Faction: glow shell in firing faction weapon colour, white core for all; Qwen's three beam characters (clean surgical, rough overcharged, exotic with secondary sheath) are proposals for Opus to map to factions, not leg claims.

### 3.4 Torp (slow heavy guided ordnance, the dread weapon)

Fire: tube open and vent sequence 0.40 to 0.80 s (DeepSeek) or 0.4 to 1.2 s (Qwen); adopt 0.4 to 1.2 s as the testing range. Cold-launch feel: gas shove, vapour ring, dim flash 0.1 to 0.2 s, lots of gas (Qwen); tube flash 1 to 2 frames, smoke puff 1 to 2 s (DeepSeek). Count: 1 to 2 per launch staggered 200 to 400 ms (DeepSeek) or 1 to 4 at 0.3 to 0.8 s ripple (Qwen); adopt 1 to 4 with stagger wide enough that each torp stays individually trackable, since Qwen's trackability requirement is the point. Telegraph (K2, UNSURE pending scheduling, DeepSeek only): two-stage warning, 700 to 900 ms total ramp to white, K2 pin about 800 ms, first stage dim faction pulse, second stage ramps to white and holds 100 to 200 ms before launch, plus an interception call if the target has point defence. Qwen does not cover this item (pack gap); its proximity warning tick and mount warning light are audio and mount cues, not the K2 telegraph. Travel: full mesh torpedo, readable as a physical object flak can target (both legs). Speed: slow, DeepSeek 40 to 80 units per second, Qwen 0.15 to 0.3 of battery light with 0.3 to 0.8 s boost phase; both proposals, convert once battery baseline locks. Trail: the signature, long persistent plume ribbon 3 to 8 s (Qwen) or smoky exhaust 30 to 60 units, life 1.5 to 3 s, wobbling (DeepSeek); adopt Qwen's longer ribbon as the testing range for trackability, with DeepSeek's wobble, and let Opus test whether 3 to 8 s fills the field. Plume uses the firing faction's engine-trail style so incoming torps attribute to a faction at a glance (Qwen). Audio: launch whoosh and motor rumble, rising whistle audible 1.0 to 2.0 s before impact as the audio half of the telegraph (DeepSeek), low warbling drive tone with doppler and proximity tick (Qwen). Impact on shields: largest single shield flash, 1 flash, 0.2 to 0.5 s, full-surface surge, 2 to 4 ripple rings over 0.5 to 1.2 s, 30 to 80 sparks, shockwave ring (Qwen); DeepSeek adds 0.6 to 1.0 s ripple and shield wash 0.2 to 0.4 s. Intercepted short of the shield: secondary airburst fireball 0.5 to 1.2 s, 4 to 10 debris chunks. Impact on hull: all torp weights are heavy events (Qwen). Light: fireball 0.8 to 1.5 s, 8 to 20 chunks, jump to dented, usually exposed structure or breach on small ships. Medium: fireball 1.2 to 2.5 s, two-stage explosion with sympathetic pop 0.2 to 0.5 s later, 15 to 35 chunks including 2 to 5 large hulks, breach decal with glowing interior. Heavy: fireball 2.0 to 4.0 s, shockwave ring, ember rain, 25 to 50 pieces, guaranteed breach crater with interior structure visible, crater fire 3 to 10 s, brief camera shake even at distance. DeepSeek target-class table agrees on direction (light often destroyed, medium breach likely, heavy stage 3 to 4, capital stage 4 with secondaries) with a shockwave ring 0.5 to 1.0 s, fireball 0.8 to 1.5 s, dark smoke 2 to 4 s. Torp craters keep a fire-and-smoke loop until repaired or death and are the primary carriers of the breach stage. Sound: crunch, blast, rolling debris rattle, deep groan and pressure-pop on heavy breach. Faction: body silhouette differs by faction role (stubby, long and slim, organic-curved, Qwen proposal), drive plume in launching faction engine style, warhead core neutral white-yellow with faction tint in the outer fireball edge, shield impacts in defender colour.

### 3.5 Bio (living ordnance, the weird one)

Both legs agree bio must not read as a recoloured battery shell. Fire: organic wind-up, DeepSeek 0.30 to 0.70 s (default 0.30 to 0.45 s), Qwen 0.2 to 0.6 s with peristaltic pulse and visible inhale; adopt 0.2 to 0.7 s. No bright muzzle flash: wet bioluminescent puff in faction weapon colour, 2 to 4 frames, droplet spray (both legs). Count: 1 to 3 globs staggered 100 to 200 ms (DeepSeek), Qwen adds 20 to 40 percent random jitter on ripple 0.1 to 0.3 s so it never feels mechanical; adopt the jitter. Travel: wobbling glob with faint internal glow, dripping particulate trail, no hard-edged ribbon (the opposite of torp plumes, so the two never confuse, Qwen). Speed: DeepSeek 60 to 120 units per second, Qwen 0.4 to 0.7 of battery light (heavy organism 0.25 to 0.4); convert once battery baseline locks. Impact on shields: glob splashes, clings 0.2 to 0.5 s, slides off, dull bloom 0.1 to 0.2 s, single soft ring, mottled smear fading 0.5 to 1.5 s, no decal. Impact on hull: lingering corrosion, not a single impact (both legs). Light: acid splat, corrosion patch growing 0.5 to 1.5 s, stage 1 to 2 over 1 to 3 s. Medium: corrosion pit to dented equivalent, tendrils anchor and retract over 1 to 3 s, stage 2 over 2 to 4 s. Heavy organism: clinging mass crawling and dissolving 0.8 to 2.0 s, acid sprays, 8 to 20 organic chunks, breach hole with organic growth fringe persisting 4 to 10 s then charring off (Qwen); DeepSeek adds corrosion continuing to spread 2 to 6 s with bubbles and fumes, bio-tinted wreck smoke 2 to 5 s. Ladder skin: Qwen's corrosion and growth variant (etch decals instead of scorch, organic fringe on breaches, dripping instead of sparking) is adopted as the bio decal family, same stage logic. Sound: wet slap, gurgle, hiss and bubbling, short organic shriek cut off by the crunch on heavy. Faction: bio fits Chorus most naturally (both legs, PROPOSAL and UNSURE, not a ruling); any faction fielding bio uses the same behaviour family with palette swaps (organism body in faction secondary, acid glow in faction accent, Qwen). Chorus spore tints, Deathless and Chorus engine puff descriptions, and the bio-warhead torp variant are DeepSeek proposals tagged UNSURE; keep those tags.

### 3.6 Gauss (hyper-velocity kinetic, the railgun sniper)

Both legs agree on the contrast: quiet travel, loud arrival, counterplay in the wind-up, not in flight. Fire: longest wind-up in Qwen (0.5 to 1.5 s standard, 1.0 to 2.0 s heavy) with sequenced capacitor banks breech to muzzle, magnetic shimmer, growing whine, optional alignment line in the last 20 to 30 percent of charge; DeepSeek gives 0.10 to 0.30 s with 2 to 4 coil glow stages. This is a genuine range difference, not a units gap. Synthesis position: start from DeepSeek's short coil sequence for light gauss and Qwen's long sequence for heavy gauss, and let Opus test, because the legs disagree on whether gauss is a fast heavy weapon or a slow siege gun. Do not average them into one number. Muzzle: single hard flash 1 to 3 frames, blue-white with faction edge, shockwave distortion ring, recoil shove larger than any battery (Qwen: whole mount or nose dips 0.1 to 0.3 s). One slug, no ripple; the wind-up IS the ripple (Qwen). Travel: near-invisible thin streak, visible 0.05 to 0.15 s, wake 0.2 to 0.5 s dissipating back to front, faint sonic-boom distortion ring 0.3 to 0.6 s (Qwen); DeepSeek 400 to 800 units per second or near hitscan with visible streak, crossing in under 0.2 s, streak 10 to 30 units fading 0.1 to 0.2 s. Qwen relative speed 2 to 4 times battery light is consistent with DeepSeek's absolute range once a battery baseline exists. Impact on shields: sharp puncture read, tight fast rings 2 to 3 in 0.2 to 0.5 s, narrow forward-and-back spray 15 to 40 sparks, brief afterglow hole closing 0.3 to 0.8 s (Qwen); DeepSeek adds tight ripple 0.3 to 0.5 s with high-frequency flicker. Impact on hull is penetration, the R16 bar at its purest (Qwen): light clean punched hole with larger blown-out exit crater and 6 to 15 fragments if overpenetration applies (design rule, UNSURE); medium through-and-through with interior light pass, 12 to 30 chunks, atmosphere jets from both holes 2 to 6 s, jump to exposed structure; heavy structural event with peeled-back entry crater, guaranteed breach, 20 to 45 pieces including 3 to 6 large sections, interior fires along the slug path, heavy shake, 1 to 3 s groan, and direct wreck trigger on small ships. DeepSeek target-class table agrees on direction (light entry hole stage 2, medium spall stage 2 to 3, heavy stage 3 with breach possible, capital stage 3 with breach possible after multiple hits) and adds the sonic boom arriving 0.2 to 0.5 s after the visual crack. Signature detail (Qwen): gauss holes glow from residual heat, cooling white to red to black over 2 to 5 s, debris leaves brief hot contrails. Sound: coil hum, thunderclap crack sharper and dryer than battery, zip or tear in flight, puncture ping under heavy thud on shield, metallic ring-through on hull, tense silence beat 0.05 to 0.15 s before the shot (Qwen). Faction: charge colour in firing faction accent, slug wake neutral white core with faint accent sheath on heavy only, hot hole edge trim in faction colour (Qwen); DeepSeek faction table for flash edges applies.

### 3.7 SQ (strike craft, launched small craft)

Both legs agree SQ is a swarm read, not a single ship, and must not pollute fleet-scale readability. Launch: bay doors 0.4 to 1.0 s with interior lights, catapult charge glow, slow activity lights (Qwen); craft leave in a stream 1 to 4 per bay cycle at 0.2 to 0.5 s ripple, squadrons 4 to 12 per launch event (Qwen, balance value UNSURE); DeepSeek gives 2 to 6 craft per run, 1 to 3 bolts per craft, ripple 20 to 60 ms, full run 6 to 18 bolts over 0.3 to 0.8 s, deliberately out of phase. Adopt both: launch stream per Qwen, firing run per DeepSeek. Count discipline (Qwen, adopt): at fleet scale, cap concurrent visible craft effects with LOD reduction by distance; thresholds are screen-tuned guesses. Travel: tiny persistent engine glow in parent faction style, no trail ribbon at fleet zoom, short 0.2 to 0.5 s vapour trail at close zoom (Qwen); craft speed 100 to 200 units per second on a strafing run (DeepSeek); loose formations with 1 to 3 m separation jitter (Qwen). Craft weapons are mini-scale derivatives: miniature battery tracers at 60 to 80 percent brightness, no decals at fleet zoom, small spark impacts (Qwen); bolt speed 200 to 350 units per second, trail 10 to 25 units fading 0.1 to 0.3 s (DeepSeek); craft torps if a squadron type carries them are miniature torps with thin plume, impact scaled to light torp minus one step (Qwen, UNSURE). Impact on shields: tiny faction pinpricks 0.05 to 0.1 s, no ripple rings below heavy craft ordnance (Qwen); DeepSeek adds many small ripples 0.2 to 0.3 s, with a massed run treated as one flash event if bolts land within 0.2 s. Impact on hull: spark ticks and pinhole scorch only; squadrons advance the ladder by accumulation, never by single hits; sustained runs reach stage 2 at most (both legs). Craft death (happens often, both legs): one small fireball 0.3 to 0.7 s, 3 to 8 debris sparks, smoke wisp 0.5 to 1.5 s, one flash per death, merged and dimmed by the flash budget when many die in the same second (Qwen). Sound: bay rumble, catapult thump per craft, engine flare chirps, close-zoom mosquito buzz bed tracking craft count, small pop with metallic tinkle on death, distant deaths as soft ticks. Faction: craft silhouette differs by faction role (angular, insectile, sleek, Qwen, primary distance read); miniature engine glow and tracer sheath in faction accent and secondary (Qwen); bolt tips in faction weapon colour (DeepSeek); shield pinpricks in defender colour.

## 4. Engine trails per faction (merged)

Adopt DeepSeek's six-faction table and SPEC shape as the base, and Qwen's four-layer structure as the build method. Base shape (SPEC): hot core at each nozzle (white-yellow centre, faction-tinted edge), turbulent plume about 1.5 ship-widths long (range 1.2 to 1.8), long fading exhaust trail about 60 units (range 50 to 70), thinning and cooling from faction colour to dark grey or transparent, slight waver 2 to 5 units, fade 1.5 to 3.0 s. Nozzle core flicker 8 to 15 Hz (DeepSeek). Four layers (Qwen): nozzle glow (steady, brightness scales with throttle), core plume (bright inner cone, roughly 1 to 3 nozzle diameters wide, length scaling with mass and throttle, 2 to 10 ship-lengths as a screen-tuned guess that must be reconciled with the SPEC 1.5-width pin: use the SPEC pin for the test scene and Qwen's range as the in-game scaling proposal), outer plume and haze (wider, dimmer, softer cone with rolling noise, faint shock-diamond banding for realistic roles), wake (long very faint ribbon or particle wake, lifetime 1 to 4 s, so fleet movements leave readable tracks; torps borrow this layer). Pitfall to avoid (Qwen, from Kerbal developer notes): particle spawn tick-rate causing disconnected spottiness at high speed; fix by spawning along the swept segment or using a stretched ribbon. Faction reads (DeepSeek table, engine column): Voidsworn ember orange steady with bright flares and warm smoke; Hegemony clean pale blue, tight plume, minimal smoke, very steady; Corsair sooty orange, sputters and coughs dark smoke, uneven 4 to 10 Hz, visible pulses (the explicit SPEC exception); Chorus yellow-green soft plume with spore-like puffs (PROPOSAL, UNSURE); Ithar mint green smooth and cool, very little smoke; Deathless green guttering and cold with occasional ember pops (PROPOSAL, UNSURE). Qwen archetype mapping (chair suggestion only): industrial burn fits Corsair, clean ion fits Hegemony, exotic drive fits Chorus; Opus decides the full mapping. Shared rules (both legs): engines are always the OWNER's faction colour, since they identify ships at a glance; strike craft use miniature single-layer versions; damaged ships (ladder stage 3 and beyond) get sputtering, asymmetric, smokier trails with intermittent misfire coughs, plume length 60 to 80 percent of normal, slow modulation 0.5 to 1.5 Hz under 20 percent brightness step, never above 3 Hz under any damage state; engine brightness is a throttle and damage signal.

## 5. Staged damage ladder (merged, numbering DeepSeek 0 to 5)

Qwen numbers scorched as stage 1 through wreck as stage 5; DeepSeek numbers intact as stage 0 through wreck as stage 5. Same ladder minus the intact row in Qwen. Use 0 to 5 with intact explicit. Every stage reads as physical damage with streaming parts and fire, never flat orange paint. Thresholds are balance values, UNSURE; sizes and durations below are screen-tuned guesses.

- Stage 0, intact: clean hull, paint and panels readable, engine trail only.
- Stage 1, scorched: matte black scorch decal 0.1 to 0.4 ship units across, irregular edge, paint darkening without peeling, no glow or faint residual warmth fading 2 to 5 s (Qwen), thin smoke wisp 1 to 3 s, occasional ember 0.1 to 0.3 s (DeepSeek). Streaming: none or at most 1 wispy smoke trail per major hit zone, 3 to 8 s, looping while the stage persists (Qwen). Audio: faint cooling-metal ticking every 2 to 6 s. Bio variant: etch corrosion decal instead of scorch.
- Stage 2, dented: dent normal-map decals and bent panel mesh swaps, plate seams split with thin dark gaps, scorch coverage roughly 1.5 to 2 times stage 1 (Qwen); dent depth 0.05 to 0.15 ship units, scorch 0.2 to 0.5, small paint peel patches (DeepSeek). Streaming: 1 to 3 per zone (fluttering panel strip, sparking wire bursts every 1 to 3 s as sub-flash flicker, thin steam or gas jets). Audio: sporadic creaks and pops every 3 to 8 s. Engine trails gain slight asymmetry if engine zones are near damage.
- Stage 3, exposed structure: hull plating gone in patches revealing interior structure (girder mesh, conduit runs, dull machinery) with scorching on the exposed frame (Qwen, asset cost noted, method UNSURE); exposed patch 0.3 to 0.7 ship units across, torn metal edges, dark recess 0.05 to 0.1 deep (DeepSeek). First persistent fire stage: 1 to 3 small fire plumes per zone, layered fire technique (flame core, licks, embers, smoke), anchored to structural points, slow amplitude flicker not full on-off flashes. Streaming: 3 to 6 per zone (hanging sparking cables, slower gas vents, debris shed 1 piece per 2 to 5 s). Audio: continuous low fire roar plus intermittent sparks and groans. Engine: one or more nozzles sputter, plume length 60 to 80 percent, gains smoke.
- Stage 4, hull breach: actual holes, not decals. SPEC pin: about 1/10 ship length across, jagged glowing-hot edges, dark recess, venting gas jet, debris tumbling out, licking fire, intermittent spark showers, secondary explosion every few seconds (SPEC) or every 3 to 8 s (DeepSeek). Qwen adds: open craters with peeled glowing-edged plating, visible interior volume with fire and machinery, atmosphere jets (white vapour cones) along the local normal bending slightly with rotation, 4 to 8 streaming parts per breach (jets looping 3 to 15 s or permanent while the stage holds, debris bursts every 1 to 4 s, large torn panels with smoke trails, whipping cables), 2 to 6 fire plumes with flame tongues stretching in the jets, pressure hiss bed, structural groans every 2 to 6 s, unstable engine trails oscillating 0.5 to 1.5 Hz with misfire puffs and possible stub plume. Death warning at the low-hull end of this stage (Qwen proposal): klaxon-light pulse on the hull at 1 per second or slower plus an audio alarm layer. Bio variant: organic fringe on breaches, dripping instead of sparking.
- Stage 5, wreck: ship stops fighting, all weapons dark, multiple breaches, slow tumble if the game allows it, heavy interior glow through every hole (Qwen). Continuous debris shedding, 4 to 10 fire plumes, atmosphere jets everywhere, internal secondary explosions one per 1 to 3 s (Qwen) with small fireballs 0.3 to 0.8 s counted against the flash budget. Audio: groaning hull bed, distant internal booms, fire hiss. This stage should look mournful and cinematic, the payoff of the whole ladder.

## 6. Ship death and wreck (merged, two contexts kept separate)

Do not merge the SPEC test-scene destruction event with in-game death. Both are needed. Test-scene event (SPEC, DeepSeek-centred): scene-wide, once per 12 s (range 10 to 14 s), seventh ship at 0.6 scale above the formation, blinding flash lighting nearby hulls 0.1 to 0.3 s, shockwave ring expanding 10 to 20 units to 40 to 80 units over 0.5 to 1.5 s, rolling fireball 0.8 to 1.5 s with white-yellow core and faction-tinted edges, dark smoke 2 to 5 s, 3 to 5 large hull chunks (own mesh pieces or prepared chunk meshes) tumbling 5 to 20 units per second with burning edges and 2 to 6 squibs per chunk along the break line, 20 to 50 small debris pieces and 30 to 80 embers living 1 to 4 s, one burning wreck chunk drifting 5 to 15 units per second with fires 5 to 15 s, wreck fading over 12 to 20 s, ship reappearing whole 1.5 to 2.5 s before the next event with 0.5 to 1.0 s fade-in. In-game death sequence (Qwen, with DeepSeek hitstop and shake ranges): kill moment held beat 0.1 to 0.3 s with lights and engines stuttering (one brightness dip, not a flash); cook-off phase 0.8 to 2.5 s with 3 to 8 internal explosions rippling along the hull (each fireball 0.3 to 0.8 s, budget-managed), engines flaring once then dying, trails collapsing; break-up into 4 to 10 major wreck sections with slow tumble, each trailing fire, smoke, and debris, hot glowing cross-sections cooling over 5 to 15 s; optional final blast for heavy ships, one large fireball 1.5 to 3.5 s with shockwave ring and ember field, 1 big flash, budgeted; wreck field persisting 10 to 30 s (or until battle ends), slowly dimming, still venting small jets and occasional sparks. Audio for both: low boom, tearing-metal layer, fading fire bed, then near-silence with debris ticks as the emotional beat; timeline-driven sequence asset per ship class (Qwen, from the Rogue Trader audio approach). Camera and feedback (Qwen proposals): on player-ship kill, brief slow-motion 50 to 80 percent speed for 0.3 to 0.8 s and one shake pulse; on losing a player ship, desaturate the wreck slightly so it reads as gone. Hitstop and shake during death use the section 2.3 testing ranges (hitstop 7 to 12 frames for capital death, shake 0.15 to 0.30 s positional with trauma model, 0.35 to 0.45 s only for the largest explosions). No forced camera by default (DeepSeek: batch 7 readability rules are background only; any camera treatment must be optional and off by default).

## 8. Accessibility (merged, with the chair recommendation stated)

Agreement: hard cap 3 flashes per second anywhere on screen, plus a reduce-flashing option, plus a worst-case hardware test before A5 sign-off (every weapon kind plus a mixed salvo: heavy gauss, heavy torp volley, mass SQ deaths, flak screen). Basis: WCAG 2.3.2, international photosensitive epilepsy guidelines, Xbox Accessibility Guideline 118, Game Accessibility Guidelines flicker item. See sources.

Chair recommendation (not leg agreement, for Opus to test): count a flash as one brightness transition pair (dark-bright-dark) on a given emitter ABOVE a moderate contrast step (Qwen's definition, with DeepSeek's bloom-threshold idea as the step). Small dim sparks below that step do not count. Count globally across all effects with a rolling one-second counter, cap 3. Merge concurrent flashes inside the same 0.33 s window (Qwen proposal) into one counted event, and scale per-shot flash brightness down as salvo density rises. Per-effect application (both legs, merged): battery muzzle and impact flashes count per shell, merged per salvo; flak muzzle flashes are individually below the step and airburst pops inside 0.33 s merge, with a full-screen flak war capped at 3 counted bursts per second globally and over-budget bursts rendering as smoke and sparks without the bright core pop; lance has no counted flashes while sustained (steady glow, slow rings 1 to 2.5 per second, breathing modulation 1.2 to 3.3 Hz under 20 percent, initial touch bloom counts as 1); torp launch is dim (no counted flash), impact is 1 regardless of weight, secondary pops dimmed or merged within 0.33 s; bio has no counted flashes by design (splats and sizzles, organism pulse 1 to 2 per second under 20 percent); gauss charge is step-glows (not counted), firing flash 1, impact flash 1; SQ death is 1 per craft, mass deaths merged and dimmed; ladder stage 2 spark bursts and stage 4 secondaries are sub-flash flicker or merged; stage 3 and 4 fire uses amplitude flicker only; ship death cook-off plus final blast total no more than 3 per rolling second even for the largest ship; engine misfire and damaged sputter stay slow (0.5 to 1.5 Hz) and under 20 percent, never counted, never above 3 Hz. Reduce-flashing mode (merged): 0 counted flashes (Qwen), achieved with DeepSeek's replacements: bright core pops become expanding rings plus particle bursts plus a gentle brightness lift under 20 percent with no rapid fall-off; muzzle flashes become smoke-forward (gas cone and ember sparks, additive core dropped); impact blooms lengthen and soften over 0.3 to 0.8 s with no peak; fire and explosion flicker smoothed to slow sine breathing; camera shake from effects halved (Qwen, linkage to independent shake settings is a design call, UNSURE); shield ripples and grid pulses keep full contrast unless a separate reduce-motion setting also applies; the option is honoured identically in all scenes including death and heavy gauss, defaults to off, plus a first-run photosensitivity notice (notice wording is a separate task, not effects-owned). DeepSeek's alternative reduce mode (1 per second, 40 to 70 percent reduction, fades lengthened 1.5x to 2x, shake and hitstop reduced or disabled) is recorded as the fallback if the 0-flash mode proves unreadable in testing.
