Q2. Item 18
Yes, the hole is closed for the three named paths. The helper retires only when the card is currently showing (tip_current == id) or the player has dismissed it (tip_dismissed.has(id)). An earned card that has not yet shown satisfies neither, so next_ship, end_ship and activation_complete leave it alive, and _tutorial will show it at the next hand-over because it is neither retired nor dismissed. Normal retirement still works: ending a ship while the card is current retires it, and any hand-over call after a dismiss retires it. The pack establishes that the only "fired" retire sites are the three converted ones, the remaining direct _tip_retire calls target select, target and close, and tip_dismissed is cleared per battle. No path that still retires "fired" unseen exists in the pack.
Two residual nuances, neither the reported hole. First, "seen" is proxied by current-or-dismissed: a card that showed and was then superseded without a dismiss is never retired and can show again; whether that can happen depends on tip queue behavior the pack does not show. Second, dismiss alone does not retire (the test asserts this), so if the battle ends before any later hand-over call, the card reappears next battle. Both are defensible readings of "not now" versus "never again", but they are behavior changes worth a deliberate decision.
