# Tree validation synthesis: skill tree and leveling proposal v1 (Oct 28, 2026)

Chair synthesis of the Oct 28 battle-layer program, tree validation item. The document under review was the chair's own draft (tree_proposal_v1.md), written to give Ryan's ruled direction a concrete shape: admirals as early style choices the player levels up, with skill choices in a small tree per admiral, scaling at runs 5, 10, 15, and beyond (designer rulings Q2 and Q8, Oct 28, 2026). Legs: Qwen (SOUND WITH CONDITIONS), Argon (NOT READY), DeepSeek (NOT READY). Filed legs: contrib/qwen/reviews/tree_validation_2026_10_28.md, contrib/gemini/reviews/tree_validation_argon_high_2026_10_28.md, contrib/deepseek/reviews/tree_validation_2026_10_28.md. The validated revision is filed alongside as skill_tree_proposal_final_2026_10_28.md; this synthesis records what the validation found and what the revision changed, so Opus can see the reasoning rather than only the result.

## Verdict

**THE SPINE IS SOUND; THE DOCUMENT WAS NOT READY. Both verdicts were correct about different objects.** All three legs' traces endorse the spine: the four built mastery perks as a shared trunk, choice branches hung on it, a capstone that evolves the Command, shadow XP for the bench, respec priced in renown. All three legs also found the draft document contradicting itself in at least four places, on arithmetic the chair owned and conceded in screening as each leg landed. The final proposal fixes the document. It does not re-litigate the spine, which was the designer's ruling to begin with.

## Conceded defects (chair-owned, fixed in the final proposal)

1. **Budget arithmetic (all three legs, BLOCKER).** The skeleton yields 8 unique skills per admiral under the draft's own sharing rules (tier 3: 6, capstone: 2), which is 192 across 24 admirals, not the "about 5, about 120" the draft claimed.
2. **Branch A cannot be shared (Argon BLOCKER; DeepSeek and Qwen from other angles).** Branch A deepens the admiral's own Command, and its skills are written against that Command (Kassar's are synchronized-fire skills). Sharing them faction-wide or pair-wide contradicts the standing rule that an admiral's design must match their Command. Branch A is unique at all tiers in the final proposal; Branches B and C keep the sharing tiers. The honest total budget is restated in the final proposal (288 unique plus 72 shared-set skills, 360 authored texts, with a phased build order).
3. **Picks per level incoherence (Argon and DeepSeek BLOCKER).** The draft's one-pick-per-level language collided with its 18-slot skeleton and its own run traces. The final proposal states the rule once: one pick per tree level (levels 5, 6, 7), each pick choosing a branch and one of its two skills at that branch's next tier; capstone at level 8, one of two authored evolutions. Three branch picks plus a capstone is the whole build, which is the BattleTech weight the design wants.
4. **Renown horizon (Argon and DeepSeek BLOCKER; Qwen from the respec side).** The draft deleted the 930-renown admiral ladder in one section and quoted the full 1,815 sinks as a 30-plus run horizon in another. Post-redesign sinks are 885 (LEGACY 635 plus variants 250). The final proposal states 885, and answers the follow-on question (what renown does after run 15) instead of acknowledging it: respec becomes the standing sink, priced per pick with escalation.
5. **Loss XP unspecified (all three legs had to invent a number: Qwen 8 to 12 flat, DeepSeek 8 flat, Argon about 4 early and 19 mid proportional).** The final proposal states the rule: a lost run pays 10 XP to the flown admiral, inside Qwen's band, and re-traces runs 5, 10, and 15 at the ruled loss rate. The traces land: first tree pick around run 5, third pick around run 10, capstone around run 15 for a focused favorite.
6. **Worked-example breaches (various legs, chair-concurred).** Reinforced Bridge broke the draft's own Rule 1 floor (fixed: +20 percent, Branch C, under a precisely restated Rule 2). Registry Priority spent the core sync track from a lineage tree (removed from the example). Replacement Pool paid out on escort loss, the closest genuine Rule 5 case (rewritten as shipyard availability, loss or no loss). Screen Doctrine read as doctrine economy (rewritten as a deployment formation). Heir Apparent was a meta lever sitting in a battle tree (relocated to the catch-up system, where it belongs). Ceremonial Broadside needed a stated base (rewritten as an evasion-ignoring first volley).
7. **Capstone wording (Argon).** The draft supported both "two evolutions" and "one hand-authored capstone" readings. Final: two authored evolutions per admiral, choose one; 48 authored capstones.
8. **Trunk collision (Argon).** The trunk's second Command option versus Branch A and capstones authored to the primary Command. Final proposal states the rule: Branch A and the capstone author against the primary Command only.

## Splits the chair resolved, with reasoning

- **Heat (the parked Q3).** Qwen endorses Heat paying bonus admiral XP outright ("the correct answer"). Argon dissents on cap exhaustion (bonus XP paid to a capped favorite is wasted). DeepSeek proposes splitting the payout between flown XP and faction shadow XP. The three compose: the final proposal pays Heat bonus XP to the flown admiral, routes overflow at the level cap into the line's shadow XP, and pays a smaller share into the faction shadow pool generally. This remains a chair synthesis offered as the candidate answer to Q3, which the designer parked explicitly as dependent on the redesign; it is presented for his ruling with the redesign, not as a ruling itself.
- **Rule 5 and Martyr Protocol.** Argon and DeepSeek grade it a violation (pays out when the flagship falls); Qwen grades it borderline. Chair position, kept: converting a catastrophe into two more rounds of fighting is not a reward for losing, and the skill stays with a wording guard. Replacement Pool was the genuine case and was rewritten (item 6).
- **Post-cap scaling (DeepSeek Finding 9, Argon renown horizon).** A level 8 cap per admiral, times 24 admirals, plus doctrine, was the draft's implicit answer and it is not enough on its own once the renown horizon shrinks to 885. The final proposal adds capstone mastery: XP past level 8 buys up to three mastery levels on the chosen evolution, so a favorite line keeps deepening past run 15 while breadth (the other 23 lines) remains the wide game.

## What did not survive

Nothing structural. The trunk, the three-branch shape, the one-of-two gates, shadow XP at 25 percent, renown-priced respec, and the run 5/10/15 cadence all stand, now with numbers that agree with each other. The draft's "about 120 unique skills" claim and its 1,815-sink horizon are retired as chair errors.

## Not checked

Balance of any individual skill (the example tree is a shape proof, not a tuned list; the bossmatrix admiral band will move when trees land and needs a sim pass then); the Rule 4 duplication audit against LEGACY in full (the final proposal includes the 12-item LEGACY list so Opus can run it mechanically); Godot-side implementation cost.
