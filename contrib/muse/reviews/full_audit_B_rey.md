## Job 1: G1 rules port

The headline record is real and unusually detailed. The parity README (contrib_opus_enginetest_results_g1_parity_2026-10-27_README.md) and the test README (godot/rules/test/README.md, 10,591 bytes in the pack listing) agree on the method: data tables are exported from data.js, core.js and battle.js by node/export_data.js and locked by SHA-256; the PRNG check compares 10,000 draws from each of seeds 1001, 7 and 4242424242; the maths check covers 316,090 inputs with exact 64-bit double comparison and no tolerance; battle records hold the whole canonical state, the battle log, a SHA-256 of the state and the PRNG state at every listed action point. That is a strong design. A shared misunderstanding between the two sides is unlikely on final state, because the record includes the log and the PRNG state, not only a hash.

The scenario spread is also credible. godot/rules/test/README.md maps s1 to s47 onto features: carriers and squadrons, all 36 boons, every fight type, Classic mode, stream B steps 1 to 6, Nemesis fights for all six factions in s35 to s40, the config type case in s41, and the compact field case in s47 that a coverage pass found after s20's note claimed it wrongly. The running sweep record in the parity README totals 1,500 different random battles and 273,265 records with 0 differences, and the per-seed counts shown (200, 200, 200, 200, 200, 200 and 300 across seeds 1 to 7, with the cloud seed 4 run a repeat of the PC seed 4 run) add up to that 1,500.

Problems:

- FIX: The DONE claim is not tied to the pack's current tree. The parity README's PC record is at 61c57fa with 43 of 43 battles for stream B steps 4 and 5; steps 5b and 6 and s47 are covered only by later cloud checks at 02f23da and 48638ef (47 of 47). The evidence pack HEAD is 172f019, which is neither commit. HANDOFF section 0 (in evidence_pack_B.md) says G1 is DONE and Godot matches JS at d8b47c6 plus steps 5, 5b and 6, but on the files given, the owner's own PC has no recorded 47 of 47 run and no run names 172f019. This is a traceability gap, not a parity failure.
- FIX: "Nothing NOT PORTED" is broader than the record's own qualification. FINDINGS G-12 (in evidence_pack_B.md) adds that only non-rule tooltip helpers and G3-owned encounter rolls remain JS-only. Tooltip helpers are fairly called non-rule, but encounter rolls are game rules by any plain reading; they are simply owned by another stream. The claim should carry the qualification wherever it is repeated.
- NOTE: Two checks are narrower than the headline suggests. Threat overlay parity is 3 scenarios (s4, s19, s23) and the thread test is 2 scenarios (s4, s23), per the parity README tables. Both pass, but neither is a blanket check across fight types.
- NOTE: Timing has already missed once. The older PC run at 2b91ea4 recorded a Godot worst think of 0.585 s on s4 against the about 0.5 s bar; the README attributes it to a busy PC, and the later PC run at 61c57fa is inside the bar (worst 0.406 s on s23). Timing is report only in godot/rules/test/README.md, so a loaded machine can still produce a slow activation without failing any gate.
- UNSURE: Whether any JS rules area lacks a Godot counterpart cannot be settled from the listings. The pack listing for godot/rules/ shows API.md (21,699 bytes), battle_api.gd (44,820), rules.gd (209,559), jsm.gd (30,367), data/ and test/, but no file by file mapping from battle.js, core.js and data.js to the port, and no godot/campaign/ listing at all. The test README says rules.gd keeps the JS order and names, which is consistent with a complete port, but the pack does not prove coverage of core.js or campaign-side rules.

## Job 2: G2 whole game

The flow claim mostly holds together. godot/main/FEATURES.md (10,493 bytes in the pack listing) marks the one-program flow, title screen, settings, saves, the battle screen and sound as done, and HANDOFF section 0 says title to final boss, the Windows build and a smart bot are built. The campaign screens can be played through the G2 fallback drawing even where G3's own drawing is not the one in use, so a testable whole game is plausible on this evidence.

Problems:

- FIX: The bot record does not meet the sprint's own bar. The plan (evidence_pack_B.md, section 4) sets the goal as a bot that has played many full runs with 0 errors. HANDOFF section 0 reports 100 smart runs with 0 hangs, then 36 runs across all six factions with only 34 clean. The pack gives no cause for the 2 unclean runs, no error class, and no log path. Until those 2 are explained, the 0 errors claim is unproven, and an unclean run is exactly the kind of fault the owner could meet in his first session.
- FIX: The pace claim mixes a mean with a worst case. HANDOFF section 0 says every battle's worst round is under 15 s. godot/main/FEATURES.md supports the fix with a mean: a 20-ship sector 3 fight measured 30.1 s mean enemy time per round before the Oct 27 pace fix and 13.4 s mean after. A 13.4 s mean does not prove a worst round under 15 s, and the pack contains no max figure for that fight, although FEATURES.md says every battle report carries a pace mean and max. The claim may be true; the evidence shown is the wrong statistic.
- FIX: The Windows build is not smoke tested on the evidence given. godot/main/FEATURES.md marks the build done on the PC and the double-click start done (tools/pc/play_godot.bat), and in the same row says "exe smoke test pending". HANDOFF section 0 lists the Windows build as built. A built exe that has never been run as an exe is the single most likely point of failure for the owner's first click, so the pending smoke test should be closed before the sprint is called done.
- NOTE: The movement approach is sound where the strips show it. In s9_dreadnought_strip.jpg and s25_corsair_big_strip.jpg the drive glow stays at the stern on the hull axis while hulls swing through a turn, and the trails curve behind the stern, which is what a main drive should look like. Neither strip shows a sidestep, and no RCS puff is clearly readable at the turn points at this size, so the RCS and sidestep half of R54 is NOT CHECKED from the strips. HANDOFF section 0 lists thruster-true movement as IN PROGRESS, so this is an evidence gap only.
- NOTE: The Before and After rows in both movement strips are near identical at each timestamp (0.30 s, 0.80 s, 1.30 s, 2.00 s in s9; 0.30 s to 1.90 s in s25). If the strips are meant to prove the R54 change, they do not show it; the s25 After row shows slightly different settle positions late in the move, and that is all.
