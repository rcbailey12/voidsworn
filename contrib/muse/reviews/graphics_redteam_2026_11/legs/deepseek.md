1. Verdict

THE STACK HOLDS ONLY WITH REPLACEMENTS.

The verdict depends most on C3, C45, and C54. C3 is the only binding performance law and has no chosen number yet; if its first measurement becomes the gate by default it repeats the C1 pattern with better scenery. C54 is a triangle-denominated detail share that survived the Nov 6 correction even though ship triangle caps were retired for the measured reason that triangles do not predict GPU time.
