# Blind Rey leg (writer): red team of the Voidsworn graphical standards

Sole evidence: PACK.md. Every entry is marked OBSERVED (present in
the pack, pack location cited), INFERRED (reasoning from pack
contents, basis stated), or NOT IN PACK (a fact the pack does not
hold). Costs are stated only in the pack's agent-term units: Codex
runs, Opus runs, one-time script builds, per-hull unattended runs,
owner review rounds.

## 1. Verdict

THE STACK HOLDS ONLY WITH REPLACEMENTS.

The three constraints this verdict most depends on: C3, C54, C60.

Reasoning. OBSERVED (pack 3, inventory header): of 65 inventoried
constraints, 4 are MEASURED, 11 DERIVED, 6 ANCHORED, and 44
ASSUMED. Assumption alone is not the failure: the paint standard
exists because unmeasured paint failed five rounds in a row (pack
1.3), and its instrument demonstrably discriminates, since the
hand-built V3 scores 7 of 9 clean cells and every AI hull scores 0
of 9 (pack 2.3). The measured anchors that exist are real and are
correctly fenced per faction (C21, C25, C29). What does not hold
is the denomination and completeness layer around that working
core. OBSERVED (pack 3, C3): the stack's only binding performance
law, the scene gate, carries no number yet, and its number is to
be set from a benchmark that has not run, which makes it a single
point of failure for every performance judgement in the stack.
OBSERVED (pack 3, C54 and C60): two live rules are still
denominated in triangles under a correction whose own stated
logic is that triangles do not predict GPU time in the measured
series (pack 2.2); both survived because they were rulings, not
because their unit was re-argued. OBSERVED (pack 3, C18, C19,
C42, C45): several assumed perceptual numbers gate sign-off today,
each with a priced settlement that has not been run. A stack
whose core instrument works, whose performance law is unnumbered,
and whose residue contradicts its own governing correction holds
only with the replacements priced in part 5.

Chair hypotheses, tested rather than repeated. H1: confirmed in
substance; the assumed numbers that gate sign-off today are C18,
C19, C42, and C45, and the only measured performance law (C3) has
no number (pack 3). H2: confirmed; the residue is visible in the
texts of C54 and C60 themselves (pack 3). H3: confirmed; C46 is
arithmetic on the assumed C45, C26 is an equality with the assumed
C18, and C16 and C37 are sound derivations rooted in the
self-calibrated blur of C17 (pack 3). H4: confirmed; C34 against
C35 and C39 against C40 are parallel rules at job seams that no
single document reconciles (pack 3). H5: confirmed as an open
defect; the radius versus sigma mismatch is in the record (pack
3, C17), but its effect on the round 6 sheets is NOT IN PACK
until the priced script check runs. H6: confirmed; nearly every
settlement in part 5 rides a sheet, run, or job already scheduled
(the C18 ladder build, the C16 capture comparison, issue 169,
issue 198, issue 199, the issue 207 benchmark).
