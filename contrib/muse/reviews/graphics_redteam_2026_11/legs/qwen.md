VERDICT
THE STACK HOLDS ONLY WITH REPLACEMENTS.
The three constraints this verdict most depends on: C3, C18, C45.
C3 (the scene gate) is the sole binding performance law after the Nov 6, 2026 correction, but it carries no number. Every performance holding in this report is conditional on the issue 207 mixed-fleet benchmark, which has not run. Until it runs, the stack has no binding performance law.
C18 (S3 neighbour value step, 12 luma levels) gates paint sign-off, feeds D7's region-breaking logic, and prices the first expensive ladder in this report. It is labelled INFERRED, its generator is unknown, and no step-ladder measurement backs it. The number most cited as a quality gate is the least measured.
C45 (D9 noise gap, 4 units) is the detail standard's most load-bearing perceptual claim. It defines the density ceiling (C46), anchors the cluster composition (C48) and the close spacing (C50, C51). It is the single most expensive measurement in the pack and the one most likely to break a build.
The stack holds only with replacements because its architecture (per-faction references, the blur test, the nine-cell sweep) is sound but its numbers are conventions. The ceiling synthesis, the paint standard, and the detail standard all carry numbers labelled INFERRED or stated without measurement. The corrections are not a new stack but a priced set of replacements for numbers that cannot be defended as measured.
