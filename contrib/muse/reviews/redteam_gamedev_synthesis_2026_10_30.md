# Red team audit synthesis: game dev seat (Oct 30, 2026)

Chair synthesis of the game dev persona legs. Prongs in hand: blind Rey, DeepSeek, Argon (3 of 4). Qwen prong: PENDING FOLD-IN (daily cap at send; folds in as an addendum when it lands). Pin: main head b3a3b802; code checks against the local clone at the pin state.

## Seat verdict

NOT COMPOSED (Rey), do not cut over (DeepSeek), SYSTEMS FRACTURED AT TURN AND TABLE BOUNDARIES (Argon). Three independent seats converge: the parts are well made, the composition layer is missing. Every system folds into one uncapped additive pool, fitting carries no commitment, and the two wave 5 deliverables that were cleared to land (the Eclipse battleship entry and patch pack section 1) are both mechanically broken against the live code. The game dev seat's claims register mostly survives; its two newest clearances do not.

1. **Weapons DUPLICATES WITH PAINT: UPHELD, 3 of 3 prongs in hand, with one refinement.** All three legs confirm flak and gsentry are the same weapon in every field (data.js:4 and data.js:22; k:'flak' on both). The hbattery dominance sub-claim is REFINED by Argon finding 5 and chair spot-check of the HULLS rows: battery and hbattery never share a hull (penitent vs cathedral and ascendant), so the pair is hull-tier separation, not a fitting choice the player can get wrong. The refinement does not rescue the claim's spirit: Rey finding 5 shows turbo and gauss sit on the identical expected-damage line (13.32) with gauss adding pierce for free, so the table still sells statistical clones as choices.
2. **Squadrons DUPLICATES WITH PAINT: UPHELD, 3 of 3.** Argon finding 9 reads the tables directly: spore (hp26, sp5, ev25) is a numerical clone of bomber (hp28, sp5, ev25) firing a near-identical weapon, with no distinct delivery rule. DeepSeek finding 4 and Rey finding 10 concur. Argon adds carrier value inversions the original synthesis did not have: the player carrier reliquary (cp4) is strictly worse than c_hulk (cp4) on hull, armor, and direct fire, and h_dominion (cp6) spends its battleship ability slot on launch for hangar:1.
3. **Modules MOSTLY DISTINCT JOBS / NO BUILD LAYER: UPHELD and sharpened, 3 of 3.** The seat now has the structural cause, not just the symptom. Rey finding 2: sumFx (core.js:171) folds fleet, module, perk, and aspect effects with plain addition, no caps, no overwrites, no exclusivity, and derive() (core.js:207) pours every system through that one fold. DeepSeek finding 1 adds the second half: fitting is free, instant, and reversible, so modules are a pre-battle checklist. Argon finding 7 confirms both and adds that MODULES.cogitator collides on a key name with RELICS.cogitator inside the same pool. The design layer has a no-duplication constitution; the runtime has no mechanism that could detect a violation.
4. **Boons DUPLICATES WITH PAINT: UPHELD, 3 of 3 in substance.** Argon findings 7 and 8 confirm the ram trio and the chain-explosion pair stack the same keys linearly, and that BOON_NEED still omits L_storm, so shieldless fleets (Chorus, Eclipse; all hulls sh:0) can be offered a shield refill. DeepSeek finding 8 concurs.
5. **AI COMPETENT BUT HOLLOW: REFINED, competent half downgraded, 3 of 3.** DeepSeek finding 5 states it plainly: the brace bypass means aiUnit returns before the veteran pre-fire brace decision, so moving shooters lose a defensive decision on most activations; that is an implementation hole, not a tuning note. DeepSeek finding 6 keeps the hollow half (composition is a readable outline with a random interior; targeting is a blended scalar with no readable priority). Argon finding 10 adds the onboarding consequence, carried to the Ryan lane below.
6. **Faction shape SHAPES PARTIAL: UPHELD, 3 of 3.** All six frigates cost cp1 and all six destroyers cp2 (Rey finding 7, DeepSeek finding 2, Argon finding 8). CP therefore carries no faction signal where battles are actually fought early, and at cp1 d_scythe (hp56, armor .2) and i_wisp (hp30, armor 0) show CP does not encode hull power at the bottom either. genEnemyFleet spends CP only and never consults FAC_PRICE (all three legs), so the ruled CP principle (Eclipse stronger and more expensive, Chorus the opposite, across stats, price, and CP) is implemented on the player-facing side only. Argon finding 8 adds a mechanism the faction shape synthesis did not have: RAMP.ceil converts any Chorus CP discount into heavier hulls rather than larger swarms, so even a correct CP table would not express the swarm on the enemy side.
7. **Eclipse battleship entry ENTRY SOUND (wave 5): OVERTURNED. Chair-verified facts are in the next section.
8. **Patch pack section 1 (veteran brace fix) as written: OVERTURNED; remedy agreed.** The pack's diff evaluates evalOffense from the pre-move position, so a damaged veteran starting out of range or with fixed-arc guns pointed away scores myOff 0, moves into the kill zone along its chosen path, then braces without firing and never checks boardTargets (Argon finding 2, from inReach at battle.js). ## Chair-verified facts (checked against the clone at the pin)

1. flak (data.js:4) and gsentry (data.js:22) are field-identical and both carry k:'flak'. canFire returns false for k==='flak', so flak-kind mounts never fire offensively. intercepts is a boolean some() at battle.js:352: any flak-kind mount grants 2, a second mount grants nothing.
2. d_meridian as filed mounts gauss, gauss, doom, gsentry, flak. Its offensive battery is identical to the cp4 d_harvester (data.js:100) and to the cp12 d_boss (data.js:101). Its second point-defense mount is functionally void. Its ability, phase, is already carried by d_scythe and d_jackal (data.js:98-99), the frigate and destroyer. The hull does not yet exist in data.js at all (Rey finding 1).
3. d_boss has no reanimate field, while d_scythe, d_jackal, and d_harvester all carry reanimate:true. The Eclipse flagship lacks the faction's signature mechanic.
4. The ascendant versus cathedral inversion is real (Argon finding 5, chair spot-checked): ascendant (cp8, sp1, hbattery P/S, turret, flak, cost 520, unique) against cathedral (cp5, sp2, hbattery P/S, lance, flak, cost 340). The dreadnought pays 3 more CP and 180 more credits for half the speed and a weaker forward gun.

## New findings, ranked

1. **Turn-boundary state is broken in three places (Argon findings 3, 4, 6; leg-reported with quoted code, not chair re-verified).** One property, u.hexMoved, serves two opposite contracts (motionHit reads it as last-turn stillness on the target; rakeBonus reads it as this-turn movement on the attacker), and startSideTurn zeroes it for both sides at round start, so evade and rake math silently misread at every boundary. rakeBonus is dead for three of six factions outright (Chorus, Glasswrights, Eclipse have zero P or S mounts anywhere) and unreachable at base stats for the sp1 dreadnoughts that carry the biggest broadsides. Inside startSideTurn itself: stormRegen leaks through a shieldOff that was decremented five lines earlier, shield regen caps before the storm drain so a full shield in a storm loses the regen and the 10, the storm tick lacks a dead check, and risen Eclipse hulls are locked out of brace because w.fired=true is set on all their weapons and canOrder('brace') requires no weapon fired.
2. **Phased activation is priced like a UI change and is actually a rules rewrite (Rey finding 3).** The shipped model is per-unit and interleaved (onLate mid-move fire, brace legality closing once any weapon has fired). A side-wide move beat then fire beat changes when brace, boarding, abilities, and torpedo resolution are legal, and the parity oracle mirrors the old model line for line, so the Godot tests will fight the redesign test by test. If this is scheduled as presentation work inside the port, it arrives as an unbudgeted rules rewrite in the middle of the migration whose whole safety case is parity.
3. **Turn start is a fifteen-subsystem pipeline with silent failure built in (Rey finding 4).** startSideTurn (battle.js:365 onward) sequences event ticks, objective ticks, focus refresh, boss ticks, escort breaks, Nemesis checks, wing stances, resolve gain, reassembly, shield and hull regen, status ticks, terrain, and the gravity well. On the enemy entry, aiRefreshFocus and bossCmdTick run inside empty try/catch blocks (battle.js:368): a throw there fails forever with no signal. The real ruleset is the order of operations in this one function, and nobody can state it in full.
4. **Defect knowledge is compounding faster than fixes land (Rey finding 8, DeepSeek finding 9).** NOMIDFIRE, the flag gating the brace bug path, is read once at battle.js:1385 (plus two Godot oracle mirrors) and set nowhere. The findings-to-tests ledger stands at 8 PASS and 40 FAIL at the pin, every failure a correct regression test against an unfixed defect. Unless each patch spec is carried into the Godot port as behavior with its acceptance check, the port inherits all 40 knowingly.
5. **Dead and contradictory records will be ported as truth (Rey finding 9, DeepSeek finding 9, Argon finding 5).** TORP_RING is [0,5,15] at battle.js:536 while the torpedo comment promises 10 and 25 at range 6 and data.js still lists torp and htorp at r:6 against startorp at r:5. ARC_NAME defines a Rear arc (data.js:29) under the standing rule that guns never face backward; no mount uses it. startorp carries evMult:2 that the Ithar trait overwrites in play. Each item is trivial alone; as a class they are the port's requirements documents, because parity is enforced against mirrors of these exact files. Cheapest deletion window is before the oracle freezes them.
6. **The Chorus has a stat block where the other factions have systems (Rey finding 10).** Eclipse reanimation is a real state machine in the turn code; the Ithar shield charge has its own turn-start step. The Chorus, whose fiction is grown fleets that feed, has regen numbers and a bomber-reskin signature wing, and no feed or grow mechanic anywhere in the evidence. Combined with finding 6 of the claims register (enemy generation cannot express faction design), it is the one faction a player cannot describe after a run.

## Ranked actions: Opus lane (technical)

1. Rework the battleship entry before it lands in data: a real offensive step-up over the zenith, one point-defense mount not two, an ability that is not the frigate's phase. Answer the d_boss reanimate question in the same pass; the default is consistency with the rest of the Eclipse line.
2. Rewrite patch pack section 1 to run the brace decision inside onLate, after the boardTargets early return, suppressing tacticBoard on a brace call (chair addendum and Argon leg agree). Keep the pack's acceptance check.
3. Split u.hexMoved into last-turn and this-turn properties and re-audit every reader (motionHit, rakeBonus, r_slip). Fix the four startSideTurn sequencing defects in the same pass: stormRegen shieldOff leak, cap-before-drain on storm, the missing dead check, and the risen-hull brace lockout (risen ships need a cannot-fire flag that is not w.fired).
4. Give the effect fold a composition mechanism before the 216 tree texts land in it: caps, overwrites, or exclusivity in sumFx, plus the missing compatibility gates (weapon-kind module guards beyond hangar, BOON_NEED entry for L_storm). The tree inherits the duplication verdict on arrival otherwise.
5. Decide rakeBonus's fate as a data problem: either give the three F/T-only factions a broadside path or scope the rule to the factions that can use it, and fix the sp1 dreadnought reachability. Do not leave a core combat rule dead for half the roster.
6. Purge the dead record before the oracle freezes: TORP_RING comment and torp range drift, the unused Rear arc, startorp evMult. Reconcile each against live behavior and delete or correct in the shared data file so both builds inherit the fix.
7. Carry the 40 failing regression tests into the port as a behavior checklist with the patch pack's acceptance checks attached; a defect not on that list is a defect the port re-creates by accident.
8. Sequence phased activation as a rules change with its own parity plan, before or after the port, but not disguised as presentation work inside it.

## Ranked actions: Ryan lane (human direction)

1. **The Chorus needs a system or an admitted exception.** Story: every other faction has a mechanic you can name (reanimation, shield charge, synchronized fire); the Chorus has regen numbers and a reskinned wing. Stakes: it is the swarm faction, the one new players are most likely to try first for the fantasy, and right now the fantasy is not in the game. Cost: a feed or grow mechanic is real design and code work in the Godot build; the alternative is accepting the Chorus as a stat faction and letting its fiction carry what its mechanics do not. Full treatment in the final synthesis.
2. **Veteran AI gating works against the learning loop.** Story: fresh profiles start on Standard AI with focus fire, the death-cliff check, and veteran bracing off (Argon finding 10), while the game's fiction says each generation learns from the last. Stakes: run 1 is near-impossible by your ruling, so new players lose their first run to stat and economy walls while fighting an AI that does not use the tactics they are supposed to learn to counter; when Veteran unlocks, the rules change under them. Cost: ungating or softening the gate is a small change; the question is whether the lobotomized first opponent is a deliberate on-ramp you want to keep.
