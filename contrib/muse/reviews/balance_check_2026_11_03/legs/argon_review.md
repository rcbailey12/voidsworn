Risks this measurement cannot see

Teaching gate and single-run resets masking progression power (PR #63, PR #83, teaching gate): Because the runner calls defaultMeta() before every run and does not enable the R69 playtest or everything-unlocked switch, cross-run shadow XP (shadowPool and heirs in PR #83 and PR #95) never accrues, and gated progression content cannot appear. Even for in-run XP, The Glasswrights recorded the exact same win rate (68.0%) and sector death split (5/10/1) across deterministic seeds 1 to 50 as in v2, meaning PR #63's trunk perks (+320 lines in data.js, +41 in core.js) were either completely blocked by the teaching gate inside a single fresh run or never altered a single battle outcome under the baseline bot policy.

Asymmetric multi-run scaling once Glasswright progression unlocks (PR #63, PR #83): Only The Glasswrights have a lineage tree so far (PR #63), and they already sit at 68.0% in fresh-profile runs--4 points below Eclipse (72.0%) and 10 points above Ashfall Hullbreakers (58.0%). In persistent player profiles where the teaching gate opens and shadow XP (PR #83) carries across runs into heirs, Glasswright win rates could climb past Eclipse and push the true faction spread past the 15-point trigger while the fresh-profile runner continues to report a 14-point spread.

Admiral-level skew hiding behind n=50 faction averages (Admiral splits, PR #38): Dividing 50 runs per faction across admirals leaves roughly 12 to 13 runs per admiral, where wide internal splits persist: Sunward Crusade spans 66.7 points from Thale (100.0% over 12 runs) to Ashgrove (33.3% over 12 runs), and Ashfall Hullbreakers spans 44.8 points from Vesh (83.3%) to Sable (38.5%). Because five of six factions showed zero shift in win rate or sector deaths after PR #38 (sumFx effect caps in core.js), the caps are not binding in standard bot battles, leaving open the risk that sumFx caps only clip specific outlier kits or human-stacked synergies while leaving 33%-38% win-rate admirals untouched.

Untraced battle timeouts and potential cap-induced stalemates (4 battle timeouts, PR #38): The 300-run execution logged 4 battle timeouts alongside 0 driver errors. The summary table does not expose whether those 4 timeouts existed in v2, which factions or sectors produced them, or whether PR #38's sumFx caps contributed to stalemates by capping offensive scaling against defensive hulls.

Baseline bot policy blindness to effect caps and trunk perks (PR #38, PR #63): The standing cautions note that absolute win rates are policy-sensitive under the single baseline bot policy. A bot that does not draft around sumFx caps (PR #38) or actively path into Glasswright lineage perks (PR #63) will under-report the ceiling of both changes compared to human play.
