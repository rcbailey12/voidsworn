# Chair correction to the assembled Nemesis lines (Oct 30, 2026)

This note amends `nemesis_lines_assembled_2026_10_29.md`, already on main. It exists because the Argon narrative leg of the Oct 30, 2026 red team audit (finding 2) charged that the assembly spliced incompatible perspectives inside the loss-reveal debrief pool and reintroduced a motif the Oct 27 text audit had ordered removed. The chair verified the charge line by line against the assembly and the three filed drafts. It holds, with one precision: the splice is exactly one line, and its root cause is a keying mismatch the chair missed in screening, not a defect in the draft itself.

## Withdrawal 1: the Chorus loss-reveal line keyed to the wrong side

Withdrawn: The Chorus [Qwen]: "Tissue samples recovered from the retreating mass suggest a vulnerability to [WEAKNESS], though the organism was already adapting."

Two defects in one line. First, the debrief pools in the assembly are keyed by the PLAYER's faction: each line is the player's own faction voicing what a lost fight taught it. Qwen's draft keyed its debrief pools by the NEMESIS's faction (its own closing note says the pool labels were read as Nemesis factions), so both of its Chorus loss-reveal lines describe the enemy's biology. Assembled into a player-keyed pool, the line only reads correctly when the player is facing a Chorus Nemesis, and it is incoherent when the player IS the Chorus facing anyone else. Second, even on its own keying it fails: this is a LOSS debrief, the Nemesis won the fight, and the line describes it as "retreating."

The chair's screening note on Qwen's draft called its loss-reveal pool the strongest of the three drafts and never checked the keying. That is a chair miss, recorded here rather than in the leg's file, because the leg did nothing wrong: it followed its own stated reading of the pack.

Replacement, from Rey's filed draft (its second Chorus loss-reveal line, unused in the assembly): The Chorus [Rey]: "Growth remembers what the battle tried to hide, though the memory is young and could be wrong: [WEAKNESS]." Player-keyed, pronoun-clean, uncertainty register intact, checked against all seven weakness names on the same basis as the rest of the pool.

## Withdrawal 2: the Hullbreakers loss-reveal line that smells

Withdrawn: Ashfall Hullbreakers [Rey]: "The pack sniffed over what was left of the field and came back with a scent, faint and maybe imagined: [WEAKNESS]."

Lore bible r8 gives the Hullbreakers trail and sound, and the Oct 27 text audit (Batch 10) ordered a Hullbreaker tracker-smelling instance removed on exactly that ground. The blind Rey leg wrote the motif back in and the chair assembled it without catching the collision. Chair miss, second instance in the same pool.

Replacement, from the same filed draft (its second Hullbreakers loss-reveal line, unused in the assembly): Ashfall Hullbreakers [Rey]: "Old scars on the taken trophies hint at it, nothing stronger than a hint: [WEAKNESS]."

## Flagged, not withdrawn: first-person Chorus Nemesis meet lines

The leg also charged that the meet pool's first-person register repeats the Oct 27 Batch 10 defect for Chorus Nemesis lines. The meet pool is keyed by persona and written in the Nemesis's own voice, which is first person by the shipped pool's design; a Chorus Nemesis rolling a singular persona line ("I eat whatever is nearest") does sit against the Chorus's collective idiom. The chair flags this rather than withdrawing lines: the fix is a drafting decision (plural-voice variants for Chorus-rolled Nemeses, or an explicit acceptance), and it belongs to Opus. No meet line changes in this correction.

## Resulting mix

Loss-reveal is now Rey 8, Qwen 4 (was Rey 7, Qwen 5). All other pools unchanged. Totals: 41 lines, Rey 26, Qwen 12, DeepSeek 3 (was Rey 25, Qwen 13, DeepSeek 3).

## Standing lesson

When a content pack keys a pool, the keying belongs in the leg prompt in so many words, and the chair checks each draft's declared keying against the pack before assembling from it. Qwen's draft declared its keying openly in its own notes; the failure was the chair's, in screening and in assembly both.
