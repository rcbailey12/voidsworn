# Skill tree tier 3 (Branch B and C) assembled set (savings pass wave 4, Oct 29, 2026)

Chair assembly from the three filed prongs: the blind Rey draft (primary text), the Qwen draft, and the DeepSeek draft, all filed verbatim under contrib/muse/reviews/, contrib/qwen/reviews/, and contrib/deepseek/reviews/. Method matches the tier 2 assembly: Rey text is the base, swaps are marked per admiral with the source leg, and nothing is silently edited. The one wording fix is marked where it occurs. The Argon prong is still outstanding; if it lands stronger picks, they arrive as an addendum for Opus, not a rewrite.

Scope: Branch B tier 3 and Branch C tier 3 for all 24 admirals, two options per branch, 96 skills. Pair 12 stays split, per the four-source recommendation carried out of tier 2: Vell and Vesh are assembled against their own identities only.

Excluded as written, before any quality judgment: DeepSeek's Vorn Branch C first option (it invokes "Total Interdiction," a mechanic that exists nowhere in the pack) and DeepSeek's Reyes Branch B second option (it sends wings to "the filed target," which is Vorn's mechanic in Reyes's mouth). Both stay in the filed DeepSeek draft as record; neither is assembled.

## Sunward Crusade

### Admiral Roderic Vex
Branch B T3: The Ledger Reassigns - when the Command is declared, you may assign each ship its own named target, and any ship whose target is destroyed before it fires may choose a new target instead of losing its volley. || Paid in Advance - before the volley, you may hold one ship out of it entirely; every other ship may then choose any target in range, ignoring the best-target rule.
Branch C T3: The Flagship's Signature - once per battle, the flagship fires its volley after every enemy has moved, and may split it between two targets in the same arc at full damage each. || The Admiral's Own Guns - the flagship's volleys deal +20% damage for the whole battle.

### Oath-Captain Malek Draven
Branch B T3: Second Mark Entered - during the salvo round, when the marked prey is destroyed, you may immediately mark a new prey, and the salvo's bonus applies to it for the rest of the round. || Broadside Answers the Salvo - during the salvo round, a ship with no torpedoes left may fire its guns with the salvo's damage bonus instead.
Branch C T3: The Flagship's Patience - if the flagship holds its torpedoes through the salvo round, its first torpedo next round deals double damage. || The Flagship Hunts Alone - once per battle, the flagship may fire all of its torpedoes at one target outside its firing arc, at half damage.

### Commodore Sera Ashgrove
Branch B T3: Escort Becomes the Strike - an extra wing assigned as escort may abandon its ship when that ship is fired on and strike the attacker at full effect instead of intercepting. || Wings Fly the Gaps - extra wings may strike targets behind the enemy line, passing over screening ships without being screened themselves.

### High Admiral Oryn Thale
Branch B T3: Iron over Iron - while Iron wall is active, a ship may give its damage reduction to an adjacent friendly ship whose shields are already gone, and take that ship's hits at full effect itself. || The Wall Advances in Step - during Iron wall, at round end all of your ships may move 1 hex in the same direction, keeping their formation and the damage reduction.
Branch C T3: The Flagship Is the Wall - once per battle, the flagship may extend its own damage reduction to every friendly ship within 2 hexes for one round. || The Admiral Stands in the Ledger - while the flagship's shields are above half, it takes 20% less hull damage.

## Assembly notes

Swaps (8 of 96 slots; everything else is the Rey draft as filed):
- Kassar Branch B, second option: Qwen's Iron Procedure Under Fire replaces Rey's The Net Closes in Order. Sync measured at the moment of firing removes a real Hegemony friction the Rey option (choosing fire order) only worked around.
- Reyes Branch C, first option: Qwen's The Flagship Publishes the Strike replaces Rey's The Flagship Flies the First File. Striking last at any range with a kill-triggered second strike is the richer flagship decision.
- Maren Branch B, first option: Qwen's The Column Files Its Own Repair replaces Rey's The Column Wheels. The refill chain is the column mechanic Maren's earlier tiers set up; the wheel rotation is in reserve.
- Maren Branch C, second option: DeepSeek's The Flagship Refills the Last Ship replaces Rey's Maren's Own Plating. A flagship that empties its own shields to refill the weakest ship is a decision; the plating was a flat number.
- Ironmaw Branch B, second option: Qwen's Burn Past the Blood replaces Rey's The Run Does Not Stop for Wrecks. Ramming through a target continues Ironmaw's ram identity; wreck hexes are terrain trivia by comparison.
- Mott Branch B, second option: Qwen's Wings Come Back for More replaces Rey's Launched off Anything Flat. A recall and relaunch gives every wing a second decision per Command.
- Vell Branch B, first option: Qwen's Refraction Deepens to Invisibility replaces Rey's Mirrors Left Behind. The Rey draft's own notes flag that Vell's sets lean on false images in both branches; this swap gives Branch B one non-image option. His Branch C pair stays image-based by design.
- Saro Branch C, second option: DeepSeek's The Flagship Counts the Light replaces Rey's Long Focus, Longer Still. The assembled version pays for fleet-wide focus fire, which is Saro's decision; the Rey option was a flat flagship number.

Rule audit of the assembled set:
- Pure numerics: exactly one unconditioned pure numeric in 96 (Vex, The Admiral's Own Guns, Branch C, +20%, above the 15% floor). The remaining numeric-flavored Branch C options are all conditional or compound (Kassar's sync count, Thale's shield state, Vessane's fire-before-move, Voice's and Ostrel's scaling stacks, Saro's per-ship count), so the one-in-three cap holds with a wide margin and every pure or conditional numeric sits on Branch C.
- Rule 5: the two banked-patience skills (Draven's Flagship's Patience, Sable's Smiling Knife Waits) pay only for patience declared in advance, never for a loss taken. Seveth's Twice-Restored refill is a catastrophe conversion anchored to the wall declared before the damage, the pattern the design rules allow.
- Rule 6: no assembled skill restates a capstone at lower power. Where a tier 3 touches a capstone's ground (walls, volleys, surges), it moves sideways into timing, allocation, or target reassignment, per the Rey draft's discipline, which the chair kept.
- Qwen's draft carried its "+15% damage during the Command" Branch C pattern on twelve admirals. None of those twelve texts is assembled; the pattern is noted here so Opus can see it was a deliberate exclusion, not an oversight.

Reserve picks (strong texts not assembled, in case Opus wants alternates): Rey's The Net Closes in Order (Kassar), Rey's The Flagship Flies the First File (Reyes), Rey's The Column Wheels (Maren), Rey's Launched off Anything Flat (Mott), Qwen's Unbroken, Unpaid, Unyielding (Thale), and Qwen's Weld the Teeth Back On (Vesh; not assembled because its name collides with Rey's assembled Weld the Teeth First for the same admiral, and the mechanic can be renamed if Opus prefers it to The Pack Shares Its Scars).

Flags for Opus (unchanged from earlier tiers, restated once): Ironmaw's Branch A still proposes a boarding action the current rules do not have; the Phase 1 assembly flag stands and this tier does not assume boarding exists. Whether any tree pick may touch fleet composition remains the open design question recorded in the Phase 1 assembly.
