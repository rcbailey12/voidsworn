# Skill tree tier 2 shared sets, ASSEMBLED (savings pass wave 4, Oct 29, 2026)

Status: assembled from the three prongs in hand (blind Rey draft, DeepSeek draft, Qwen draft, all filed verbatim under contrib/ for Oct 29, 2026). The Argon draft, when it lands, files as a fourth verbatim prong; stronger Argon picks are an addendum source for Opus, not a reason to re-open this assembly. The pairing table is the chair proposal in wave4_brief.md, flagged there for Opus to amend.

Method: the Rey draft is the primary text source (the most rule-disciplined of the three and the only one whose every skill hooks the Command declaration, which is what a shared tier 2 deepening wants). Swaps to Qwen mechanics are noted per pair. DeepSeek's draft contributed no assembled text: its two chassis (reserve entry in three pairs, swap/reposition in five) were the convergence the Phase 1 assembly diversified away from, and its flat +10 register sits below this set's. Assembly fixes applied: no assembled skill is a pure numeric below the 15 percent floor; the two numerics that survive sit on Branch C at or above the floor; no skill pays out on a friendly ship being damaged or destroyed.

## Pair 1: Admiral Roderic Vex + Saro of Long Focus (barrage)
Branch B T2: The Held Volley - when you declare the Command, you may hold the volley instead of firing it; it fires at the start of the next round, at targets chosen then. || Converging Arcs - after the first friendly ship fires at a target this round, every other friendly ship with a volley that can reach that target may shift its firing arc by one hex-face before shooting.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Names the Target - when you declare the Command, the flagship may name one enemy in range of at least two of your ships; every ship that can reach the named enemy fires its volley at it instead of its best target. || The Flagship Fires Last - the flagship's volley resolves after every other ship's volley, and the flagship may choose any target in range rather than its best target.
Provenance: Rey draft, with Branch B's second choice swapped to Qwen's arc-shift mechanic (a targeting decision neither other draft had). Rey's Volley in Two Hands was dropped as close kin to The Held Volley.

## Pair 2: First Admiral Ilsa Kassar + Umbra Vessane, Third-Housed (barrage)
Branch B T2: Volley on the Counterstroke - you may declare the Command and hold it as a threat; the volley is released immediately after the first enemy ship fires in the next round, each ship at its best target then. || Corrected Solution - once per round, after a friendly ship misses every shot in its volley, one other friendly ship may immediately fire a single volley at the same target at +15 aim.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Corrects the Record - when you declare the Command, the flagship may exchange targets with one other friendly ship; each fires its volley at the other's best target. || The Flagship Covers the Gap - the flagship may fire its volley at an enemy no other ship targeted with the Command volley, even if that enemy is not its best target.
Provenance: Rey draft, with Branch B's second choice swapped to Qwen's correction volley (fire discipline for the Hegemony, iteration language for Eclipse, one text serving both). Rey's Ships That Could Not Fire, Close is held as a reserve pick for Opus.

## Pair 3: Commodore Sera Ashgrove + Tamsa of the Clear Aperture (scramble)
Branch B T2: Extra Wings, Held Aloft - when you declare the Command, you may keep the extra wings aboard their carriers; they launch at the start of the next round and strike then with the Command's strike bonus. || Wing Rotation - once per round, one carrier may recall a wing and immediately relaunch it at a different target without waiting for the next round.
Branch C T2: The Flagship's Wing Strikes First - the flagship's extra wing strikes before every other squadron in the Command, and it may strike any enemy that any friendly squadron could reach. || The Flagship Recalls and Relaunches - when you declare the Command, the flagship may recall one of its squadrons already in flight; it launches again with the Command and may strike a new target.
Provenance: Rey draft, with Branch B's second choice swapped to Qwen's rotation. DeepSeek's reserve-entry version of this pair was not used (the reserve frame is a tier-1 chassis, already diversified in Phase 1).

## Pair 4: Commodore Corin Reyes + The Matriarch of Nine Throats (scramble)
Branch B T2: Roles for the Second Wave - when you declare the Command, assign each extra wing a role before it flies: strike, and it strikes as normal; or screen, and it covers one named friendly ship, taking the first squadron strike aimed at that ship this round. || The Second Strike Chooses Again - squadrons striking again under the Command may choose new targets instead of repeating the target of their first strike.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Names the Wings' Target - when you declare the Command, the flagship names one enemy; every extra wing that can reach it must strike it. || First Voice, Loudest - the flagship's squadrons deal +20% damage on their Command strike.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout. Qwen's Recovery Cycle (replacement wing on a wing's destruction) was not assembled: it pays out on a loss event where the banked-conversion shape is safer, and is recorded here for Opus to judge.

## Pair 5: Oath-Captain Malek Draven + Ostrel of the Long Copy (hunt)
Branch B T2: Quarry Marked - at battle start, name one enemy ship as the quarry; friendly ships may fire torpedoes at the quarry at any point during their movement, not only at the end. || Torpedoes Held Keep Their Teeth - a ship may hold its reloaded torpedoes through the Command round; torpedoes held this way keep the Command's damage bonus when they are fired next round.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Holds the Trail - once per battle, after enemy movement resolves, the flagship redirects all friendly torpedoes fired this round to a single new target of your choice. || The Killing Shot, Copied - if the flagship destroys an enemy ship in the Command round, choose one other friendly ship; it may fire one torpedo immediately at a target in range.
Provenance: Qwen's two standout hunt mechanics take Branch B's first choice and Branch C's first choice; Rey's banked-torpedo text and kill-copy complete the set. Rey's Salvo, Split was dropped as close kin to pair 6's held strike.

## Pair 6: Commodore Dace Vorn + Sable the Smiling Knife (hunt)
Branch B T2: The Strike, Held for the Answer - you may declare the Command and hold its round; the damage bonus applies in the following round instead, beginning before the enemy acts. || One Lane, Every Gun - when you declare the Command, name one enemy; ships that fire at the named enemy keep the damage bonus, and ships that fire at any other target do not gain it this round.
Branch C T2: The Flagship's Bonus, Banked - if the flagship does not fire during the Command round, its damage bonus carries to its first shot next round. || The Flagship Calls the Kill - when you declare the Command, the flagship names one enemy; if your fleet destroys it this round, the flagship may immediately fire one weapon at a new target.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout (the patient-hunter identity; the other drafts' wound-chasing set for this pair read as pair 5 with different names).

## Pair 7: High Admiral Oryn Thale + Seveth, Twice-Restored (iron)
Branch B T2: Wall Raised on Their Turn - the Command may be declared at the start of the enemy's turn instead of your own, its refill and reduction covering the enemy's fire. || A Clean Wall Refills Twice - ships that take no hull damage during the Command round refill their shields again at the start of the next round.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Gives Its Refill Away - when you declare the Command, the flagship may pass its shield refill to one other ship whose shields are empty; that ship refills twice, and the flagship not at all. || The Flagship Keeps the Wall - the flagship's damage reduction from the Command lasts through the following round as well.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout. Qwen's shield transfer between adjacent ships is held as a reserve mechanic for Opus (strong, but a second redistribution chassis in the same branch pair as pair 8's was one too many).

## Pair 8: Line Admiral Oskar Maren + Brin of the Fourth Lens (iron)
Branch B T2: Shoulder to Shoulder - during the Command round, a ship that ends its movement adjacent to another friendly ship refills its shields again at round end. || Braced at First Light - ships that do not move during the Command round begin the next round braced.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Steps to the Gap - before the Command resolves, the flagship may move up to 2 hexes, so the wall forms around the place the line most needs it. || The Flagship's Refill, Kept in Reserve - the flagship does not refill when you declare the Command; its refill is stored and may be spent at the start of any later round.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout. Qwen's shield-value swap between adjacent ships is held as a reserve mechanic for Opus.

## Pair 9: Warlord Grax Ironmaw + Razormaw (surge)
Branch B T2: Burn, Fire, Burn - during the Command round, each ship may split its movement: move part of it, fire, then move the rest. || The Kill Opens the Way - during the Command round, when a friendly ship destroys an enemy, one other friendly ship may move up to 2 hexes immediately.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Burns Furthest - during the Command, the flagship gains +3 movement instead of +2. || In the Flagship's Wake - ships that end their Command movement in a hex the flagship passed through this round keep the Command's shelter from enemy fire into the next round.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout. Split movement during the Command round is this set's answer to what mid-move fire should feel like once phased activation lands; flagged for Opus to reconcile with the activation model rather than assume.

## Pair 10: The Mending Throng + Ninth Frame (restore)
Branch B T2: Growth Where It Is Needed - when you declare the Command, name one ship; it repairs twice as much, and every other ship repairs half as much. || Sections Before Hull - when you declare the Command, choose the fleet's mending: as normal, or damaged sections are restored first and hull repair is halved.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Banks Its Mending - the flagship repairs nothing when you declare the Command; it may spend its repair at the start of any later round. || The Flagship Gives Its Growth Away - the flagship may give its repair to one other ship, which repairs its own share and the flagship's share as well; the flagship repairs nothing.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout (allocation is the pair's decision; both other drafts reached for repeatable percentage repairs, which the screenings flagged as the balance surface to avoid). Qwen's Triage Verse (forgo a volley to repair) is held as a reserve pick for Opus.

## Pair 11: Voice of the Deep + Old Mott (burst release)
Branch B T2: Release in Two Waves - when you declare the Command, split your fleet in two: one part bursts now, and the other part bursts at the start of the next round with the same effect. || One Name for the Whole Burst - when you declare the Command, name one enemy; every ship and wing in the burst that can reach it must strike it, and the rest keep their own targets.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Withholds Its Burst - the flagship may hold only its own part of the Command (its volley, or its extra wing and strike) and release it at any point later in the round. || The Flagship Lends Its Burst - another friendly ship or carrier performs the flagship's part of the Command in its place: one ship fires a second volley, or one carrier launches a second extra wing that strikes with the burst.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout. Qwen's two activation-system reaches for this pair (simultaneous activation; a double action at the cost of the next round) were NOT assembled: phased activation is the ruled activation shape (Oct 29), so both are flagged here for Opus as design questions, not texts. Qwen's Flagship Unleashes (a volley split across every enemy in range) is held as a reserve pick.

## Pair 12: Vell of the Third Mirror + Vesh the Collector (reposition and patch)
Branch B T2: Re-form After the Craft - after the Command resolves, each ship may move up to 2 hexes, settling the fleet's shape once the burn or the mending is done. || Facing, Set as One - after the Command resolves, every ship may change its facing freely, turning the fleet to meet what comes next.
Branch C T2: The Flagship Delays Its Own Craft - the flagship may hold its own Command effect (its movement, or its repair) and spend it at the start of the next round instead. || The Flagship Steps First - before the Command resolves, the flagship may move up to 2 hexes and turn freely, taking the place the fleet's craft is about to need.
Provenance: Rey draft throughout, as the least-bad shared set. RECOMMENDATION, four sources in agreement (the chair brief, the Rey leg, the DeepSeek leg, the Qwen leg): split this pair before tier 3 and draft Vell and Vesh per admiral there. Surge and restore share no mechanic; this set is built on the one thing both Commands do, changing the fleet's state in an instant, and it reads as a bridge, not an identity.
