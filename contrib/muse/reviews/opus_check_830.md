## The Board's four points

(a) Faction and space craft read at the battle camera. HF1D reads clean: a pale grey machined dart with flecks of the Hegemony blue line, the cassette at the prow; a Hegemony fighter and a space craft at native size. KB1CC reads Hullbreaker now (broad rust-red block, red-orange drives, the chunkiest of the three); its narrowness fails only against the KB1C-C render, which is the comparison Ryan made at R273, not against its faction. CS1BA2 does not read as Chorus at game size: at native pixels it is a smooth olive spindle with one pale bone line, and at 3x the gill banding, amber valleys and pores still do not register. The battle camera sheet's caption claims each craft reads apart by outline and colour at game size; for CS1BA2 the picture does not support the caption.

(b) What would make CS1BA2 read as Chorus is outline work, not paint. The council converged, with Qwen and ChatGPT the most specific: the two gill bladders proud of the flank line so the silhouette carries paired organs; the gill divisions broadened until they change the profile, not six fine bands; the stern orifice cluster given a silhouette (raised rims or a scalloped stern) instead of a smooth cap; the bone ridge and bow fork strengthened so the one surviving cue is structure, not a line. The Chorus has no paint stack yet and the other two craft read unpainted by outline, so a paint promise cannot close this. ChatGPT's acceptance test is adopted as the re-check standard: the revised craft, unpainted, at the same native battle camera beside the other two, distinguishable as a living craft without the caption or the enlarged panel.

(c) The sporeb reading stands as a Board reading. ships.md rule 11 as amended lets a T-arc weapon sit fixed on a launcher for a bomber-role craft, extending the bombs precedent to the acid sacs, and the text labels itself the Board's reading, not Ryan's words. The strains the council named: the sacs sit on the flanks rather than pointing down the long axis like bomb tubes (Qwen), "launcher" is stretched to cover a grown organ (DeepSeek, Grok), and the pack does not establish whether the game turns bomber-role craft onto their targets when firing, which is the fiction the fixed mount rests on (ChatGPT). None of the six legs would reject the reading; five of six, the chair included, want it put to Ryan for ratification at his next issue 793 look rather than left to settle silently, because it interprets his R241 for a weapon kind he has not ruled on.

(d) data.js is the wrong record, unanimously. factions.md is the law for look (ships.md section 1): dark olive-brown hide, ochre bone and chitin, bile yellow-green light from inside, and a Never line banning red or magenta flesh, which plum #57364c leans against. C10 and C11 built olive, this build is olive, the concept's plum is an image artefact of the kind R204 says to fix in the sheet, and data.js's own glow field (#d6d24a, bile) already agrees with factions.md. Only the hull field is stale.
