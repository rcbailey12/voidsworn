# Event variants: assembled set (savings pass wave 4, item 3, Oct 29, 2026)

Chair assembly from the three filed prongs: the blind Rey draft, the DeepSeek draft, and the Qwen draft, all filed under contrib/muse/reviews/, contrib/deepseek/reviews/, and contrib/qwen/reviews/. One whole variant per event (title, body, and options stay together; nothing is spliced within an event), source marked per event. The Argon prong is still outstanding; if it lands stronger variants they arrive as an addendum for Opus, not a rewrite.

Every assembled variant preserves the shipped event's option order, gates, results, flags, and stated numbers (chair-verified for all three drafts: option counts match the shipped table for all 33 events). The one structural change in the set is haven's added free exit, per Ryan's Oct 28 ruling.

## Assembly notes

Mix: Rey 12, Qwen 11, DeepSeek 10, spread deliberately so no single draft's voice dominates the pool a player will meet across runs.

Per-event calls worth recording:
- beacon goes to DeepSeek on a correctness-adjacent ground: its seize sub states the ambush and the clock step outright, where the shipped sub and the other two drafts only hint ("might remember your colours"). Under the Oct 27 audit's repair standard, the explicit disclosure is the better variant text. The Rey draft's own notes flag the shipped sub as the pool's least honest in both versions; the assembly sides with disclosure.
- haven goes to Rey: its body is a true variant (DeepSeek's haven body is the shipped body nearly word for word, which defeats the purpose of a variant), and its in-place note states the ruling's reasoning most precisely.
- moon goes to DeepSeek partly because Qwen's body carries a preserved typo (lowercase "the Chorus" opening the sentence) in its filed copy; the assembly does not edit filed text, it selects.
- cache goes to Rey because its second option's sub states the 45 credits the shipped sub leaves unstated; the only place in the set where a variant is more exact than the original without changing an outcome.

Settled across all three drafts, carried to Opus as one shared flag: astropath and shrine ship with faction-specific alternate texts (the v blocks), and all three legs drafted base variants only. A complete variant set for those two events needs the faction alternates treated the same way.

Also settled across drafts: reactor, storm, and squall have no free option and need none, since none of them can start a fight; their option sets are preserved exactly in every assembled variant.
