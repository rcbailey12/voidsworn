## Cross-admiral notes
- The allocation chassis (move a shared Command resource toward one ship) recurs in Vesh, the Mending Throng, Aurex, and pair 10's tier 2 line, but tier 3 differentiates it by direction and timing: Vesh allocates by section type across the fleet, the Throng allocates by hull threshold, and Aurex allocates by when in the round the rebuild happens.
- The banked flagship benefit appears for Draven (a held torpedo at double damage) and Sable (a held rear-arc shot at +30%), differentiated by weapon and arc: both pay only for patience declared in advance, never for a loss taken.
- Rule 6 was the hardest to keep. Most capstones already contain the strongest version of each admiral's trick (walls that last two rounds, volleys that fire twice, surges that repeat), so tier 3 had to grow sideways into timing, allocation, and target reassignment rather than magnitude, and several first drafts were discarded for restating a capstone at lower power.
- Ironmaw's tier 3 deliberately uses rams only. His Branch A proposes a boarding action the current rules do not have (flagged for Opus in the Phase 1 assembly), and this draft does not assume it exists.
- Vell and Vesh were drafted against their own identities only, per the pair 12 split. Vell's sets lean on false images in both branches, which is also his capstones' ground; if any admiral's tier 3 exposes crowding in his earlier tiers, it is Vell.
