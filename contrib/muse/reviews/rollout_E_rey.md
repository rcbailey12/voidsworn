## Job E1: the G4 20-ship bench (cc8aeed, 2aac0ca)

The r18 record (g4_gallery_readme.md, 20-ship performance record; commit cc8aeed): Forward+ 1080p average 3.96 ms (253 fps), 1% low 66.8 ms (15 fps), worst frame 4302 ms. Forward+ 4K average 6.99 ms (143 fps), 1% low 109.8 ms (9 fps), worst frame 4029 ms. Compatibility 1080p average 3.56 ms (281 fps), 1% low 54.6 ms (18 fps), worst frame 1584 ms. The bench is a seeded running battle with random volleys and three deaths over 30 s on Ryan's RTX 5080.

Finding E1.1 (FIX): there is no post-fix re-bench anywhere in the pack. The README says the fix landed in 2aac0ca and "the r19 run re-measures"; context_E.md (HANDOFF section 0, G4 row) lists the re-bench under Still to do: "the fix landed in 2aac0ca, but a re-bench is needed to confirm." So the claim that the 4.3 s freeze is fixed rests on the fix description in the 2aac0ca commit message alone. No after numbers (average, 1% low, worst frame, count of frames over 33 ms) exist in the evidence. The bench now listing every frame over 33 ms is a good change, but it has not yet produced a record a reviewer can check.

Finding E1.2 (NOTE): the stated cause is convincing as far as the pack lets me check it. A worst frame of about 4 s at both Forward+ sizes and a smaller 1.6 s worst on Compatibility fits a stall tied to deaths and damage rebuilds on big hulls (seconds of worker time, piece meshes uploaded on the main thread), and a 1% low far below the average fits the same cause repeating on every damage rebuild in a running battle, not only on the three deaths. The fix is aimed at that cause in the right three places: re-cut at most every 6 s bounds the re-launches, keeping the last finished cut removes the wait at a death, and holding the cook-off up to 2.5 s when a first cut is still running trades a delayed break-up for a frozen frame.

Finding E1.3 (NOTE, UNSURE): two residual risks the pack cannot settle. First, a death now breaks along the last finished cut, which can be up to 6 s stale against the ship's current damage state; that is a visual compromise, not a stall, and reads as an acceptable trade. Second, the pack does not say the piece-mesh uploads themselves moved off the main thread in 2aac0ca; if a finished cut still uploads on the main thread, smaller hitches at cut completion remain possible. UNSURE from the text evidence; the r19 re-bench would settle both.

Finding E1.4 (FIX): even if the freeze fix works, the rest of the r18 record is not comfortable at 20 ships. The 4K 1% low is 9 fps (109.8 ms) against a 143 fps average, and Compatibility, the shipped safe mode (findings_extract.md R49: play_godot_safe.bat ships to players), records a 1% low of 18 fps and a 1.6 s worst frame. For comparison the README's six-flagship 4K bench has a 1% low of 21.2 ms, so the lows degrade sharply between 6 and 20 ships. Whatever r19 shows about the death freeze, the 4K and Compatibility 1% lows at fleet size 20 need their own explanation or their own fix before the return test feels smooth.
