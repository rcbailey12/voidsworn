## Cross-pair notes
- The flagship-resolves-last chassis appears twice with different jobs: in Pair 1 it buys the flagship a free choice of target, and in Pair 2 it buys coverage of the one enemy nobody else shot at. Both were kept because the two barrage pairs needed different flagship decisions, but a chair that wants the chassis used once should cut Pair 2's version first.
- Pair 4's Roles for the Second Wave deliberately deepens the Chorus tier-1 verse idea (strike or screen) by moving it from ships at battle start to the Command's extra wings at declaration; it is an extension of that system, not a restatement of the tier-1 text.
- Rule 2 was the easiest rule to keep: only two skills in the whole draft are pure numeric effects, both on Branch C (Pair 4's flagship squadron damage, Pair 9's flagship movement). Almost everything else is a timing, targeting, positioning, or redistribution choice.
- Rule 5 shaped several near-misses: a number of skills trigger on friendly kills (Pairs 5, 6, and 9), which pay for winning an exchange, but no skill in the draft triggers on a friendly ship being damaged, destroyed, or forced back, and none pays out for retreating.
- Pair 12 is the pair whose shared tier 2 I least believe in. Surge and restore share no mechanic, so its sets are built on the only thing both Commands do, changing the whole fleet's state in an instant, and every skill governs what the fleet does with the moment just after. If any pair should be un-shared and drafted per admiral instead, it is this one.
