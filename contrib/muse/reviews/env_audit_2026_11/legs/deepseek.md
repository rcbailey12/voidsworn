Part 1 Verdict

THE STACK DOES NOT HOLD.

The environment stack is 70 ASSUMED items out of 74, several are asserted into build failure, and multiple adopted documents disagree with each other or with shipped code. Under the Nov 6 rulings, assumed and anchored numeric gates stop binding. The stack therefore cannot stand as a binding standards set.

The three items my verdict most depends on:

1. AS1, the 0.05 rad backdrop rock angular cap. It is an owner-adopted strictness preference asserted in code, it caps every backdrop rock, and AS8 shows it sizes the mountain field rather than the mountain range doing so. If the R88 hollow-asteroid read has a numeric root, this is the candidate.

2. AS14, the debris tumble cap. Two adopted specifications disagree by about 2 to 3 times for small rocks, and the code obeys the lower one. This is the clearest proof that builders and reviewers can work from different settled documents.

3. BD18, the quality tier table. It has no measured per-tier cost, yet NB12, NB13, AS3, BD13, and PL13 hang real content decisions on it. This is the load-bearing unmeasured structure under the stack.
