# Red team audit synthesis: VFX seat (Oct 30, 2026)

Chair synthesis of the vfx persona legs. Prongs in hand: blind Rey, DeepSeek, Argon (3 of 4). Qwen prong: PENDING FOLD-IN (daily cap at send; folds in as an addendum when it lands). Pin: main head b3a3b802.

## Seat verdict

The transient craft is real (rippled volleys, twelve distinct effect families, death staging that performs) and the written visual laws are not: the 3-flash hard cap exists in no code path in either engine, per-weapon persistent marks are one generic scorch texture in the build players see, and living ships have no staged damage state at all. Worse, two of the failures are not code bugs but spec collisions: the wreck spec schedules enough ambient explosions to exhaust the flash budget by itself, and the A5 seven-weapon-kind lock aliased four of Spec A's ten mark signatures into wrong effects before a line of fx code was written.

## Claims register: disposition

1. **FX implementation review "PARTIAL, leaning MISS" with breach geometry that "exceeds Spec A": OVERTURNED to structural MISS (Argon finding 1, corroborated 3 of 3).** A spec rule violated is not a spec exceeded. Spec A rules that flak never exposes structure in the mark language and that only ram crumple and torpedo crater alter a hull's outline; in the Godot code, flak chip damage drives stage 3 and 4 structural breaches, and crater geometry triggers from any damage source at pre-picked anchors rather than impact points (fx_ship_gd.txt:466-476, 574-586). Of ten Spec A mark entries, the two that score MEETS SPEC (squadron weapons, shield-only hits) do so by drawing nothing. Four families score MISSES SPEC or NOT IMPLEMENTED (ion, flak, ram, boarding), and the light looks leave no persistent mark at all. The review's softer verdict is superseded.
2. **Per-weapon persistent marks: CONFIRMED missing, 3 of 3, in both engines.** Godot: only gauss and bio have their own marks; plasma's persistent trace is the generic orange-ember scorch; ion runs the identical _beam_burn as lance; flak is NOT IMPLEMENTED; ram routes into _big_hit at 1.5x scale; boarding routes to _bio_splat. Browser (the build players see): one generic scorch texture (fx3d.js:335-340), placed per-family by probability (flak at probability 0, wfx3d.js:284), capped at 28 per ship with the oldest silently disposed. The R43/R16 promise ("a ship's history reads at a glance"; damage as "holes with streaming parts, fire, and other damage states, not orange lines") is unmet where it is played.
3. **The 3-flash cap: CONFIRMED unenforced in both engines, and the spec itself is over budget.** Browser: flash() at fx3d.js:293 fires unconditionally; there is no counter anywhere; reduced mode only shrinks and dims. A capital death stacks a reactor sprite, two pooled flashes, a fireball, up to two delayed extra fireballs, a 45 percent chance of a second delayed flash, a light flash, 160 sparks, and the xl tier's fullscreen flash (fx3d.js:473-479, wfx3d.js:87-97) at the game's showcase moment. Godot: the budget certifies a gated subset while the brightest emitters bypass it. Argon finding 5 adds the spec-side failure: A5's Stage 5 wreck schedule (internal explosions every 1 to 3 s) and the dying-hull cook-off (3 to 8 explosions in 0.8 to 2.5 s) can consume the entire 3-per-second budget from background wrecks alone, so even a perfect gate forces a choice between dark guns and a violated cap. The gate must count flashes on screen, and the wreck schedule must be re-budgeted against it; fixing only the code leaves the spec non-compliant.
4. **Plasma's ruled color: VIOLATED at impact (Rey finding 9).** The Oct 26 ruling (plasma is light blue) is honored in flight (electric-blue bolt, additive overlap kept low so it stays blue, wfx3d.js:215-217) and reversed at consequence: orange splash (1,.55,.16), orange hull glow, orange light flash, and the generic black scorch as the persistent trace. The one weapon color ruled on personally reads as generic fire at the moment it matters.
5. **Living-ship damage ladder: ABSENT in the playable build (Rey finding 3).** A ship at 95 percent hull and a ship at 5 percent hull differ only in scorch-plane count: no HP-driven states, no dents, no exposed structure, no holes, no smoke or venting on damaged living ships (streaming exists only on dying hulks). A knocked-out section's "burning breach" is three large copies of the same scorch sprite. The ladder's middle, where ships spend their fighting lives, is missing; only death performs.
6. **Cooling and repair: BROKEN in the Godot fx build (Argon finding 3, DeepSeek finding 5).** Hit decals hold fixed emission 0.7 forever (the DECAL shader's header claims cooling its fragment code does not implement), so a hit from five turns ago glows as brightly as this turn's, defeating Spec A's "fresh means this turn" read. Stage breach cooling runs on wall-clock seconds, not turns, so breach heat fades while the player pauses to think, in a turn-based game whose ruled UI is phased activation. Repair reverses stage geometry but never removes hit decals: a fully repaired ship still wears its scorch and holes, so hull language and game state disagree, against Spec A's own rule and against the repair ruling's feedback loop.
7. **Forward+ clustered budget: the brief never budgets its headline feature (all three prongs, arithmetic agreed).** The 512 clustered-element pool is shared by decals, omni and spot lights, and reflection probes. The Godot fx build caps decals at 10 per ship ship-wide; 60 ships at that cap is 600 decals before one explosion light, shield light, or probe, and Spec A budgets 8 to 20 decals per ship. Gauss and bio appends bypass the trim entirely (fx_ship_gd.txt:816-826), so the cap is not even a cap. No document reconciles pool size, fleet size, and mark budget. The effects pass will hit this ceiling mid-build and resolve it by quietly shrinking the mark language unless the budget is set first.
8. **Compatibility fallback: erases the entire damage language (DeepSeek finding 3, Argon finding 4).** Compatibility in Godot 4.7 has no decals; the engine auto-falls back to it when the RenderingDevice is unsupported. On the audience floor the brief designs for, every scorch, hole, corrosion, and crater mark vanishes while effects still run. The brief's own mitigation (test or gate via get_current_rendering_method()) is not yet a decision on file. This is the same silent-degradation pattern the engineer seat found across the port: a fallback that runs, looks plausible, and removes a headline feature without telling anyone.
9. **Palette safety: the "distinguishable on paper" verdict is REFINED to a narrow truth.** It holds for the six primaries in flat sRGB. It does not hold for weapon against own glow (1.02:1 to 1.40:1 for all six factions; hue only, never brightness), not for engine colors under deuteranopia (Hegemony against Eclipse at 14.6), and not in-engine, where scene-linear bloom before tone mapping pushes the pale cluster (Hegemony, Glasswrights, Eclipse, within 30 to 61 RGB of each other) toward white cores. Two untested pairings matter most: the palette audit never tested the shield or shield_edge fields, and Eclipse's violet palette (#DDC4FF, #DDBCFF) sits beside the violet enemy IFF ring (FOE_C, .62,.22,1) that fx3d.js chose on the claim that violet is furthest from all faction shield colors. Separately, the shipped browser table (FAC3D, ships3d.js:2-8) has never been through the check at all, and it has its own pale cluster, including Eclipse's shield green (.18,1,.36) against the friendly IFF green (.42,1,.55).
10. **Cinematic cut-ins: shipped default contradicts the recorded council position (Rey finding 6).** The A5 synthesis records "no forced camera by default," camera treatment "optional and off by default." The build defaults cut-ins to key-moments mode (main3d.js:11); cineWants fires for every command activation and, on desktop, for routine lance, torp, and nova shots, and while a cut-in plays the letterbox hides the HUD, plates, grid, and board overlay. The Godot brief's director layer plans the shot stack without revisiting the default, so the migration inherits the argument. Aligning the default with the recorded position is an Opus-lane fix; if the default is meant to change, that is a Ryan call, flagged in the final synthesis as a note rather than a decision item because the recorded position already exists.
11. **Hull geometry: the wavy read is geometry-first and its decisive test is still a proposal (all three prongs).** Concept hulls are the default path (aiHulls on unless settings disable it). The diagnosis leans geometry-first on silhouette evidence (the H8 underside outline sags), warns that a material-only fix leaves the sag, names the flat-color re-render that would settle it, and that test is still unrun. V1b's bow fix left its two profiles disagreeing (pointed from above, blunt drooping snout from the side). The Pyre-Barge, whose sign-off Ryan withheld pending a much stronger graphical upgrade, ships in the primary path as a "round 10 bolt-on B pass over the round 8 B set" (ships3d.js:707 comment), and the procedural fallback (buildGeneric returns buildWedge) makes any hull that misses both pipelines read as a Hegemony wedge. Paint and effects work continues to land on hulls whose fairness is unproven.
12. **Performance calibration: the PERFCAP problem, third seat to find it.** PERFCAP is Babylon-only code (it cannot run in Godot at all), defaults are authored at ultra on the strongest GPU in the room (desktop quality defaults to ultra with no adaptation; PERFCAP is opt-in via URL flag and gates nothing), and the brief's plan calibrates the Godot budget on Ryan's RTX 5080 against its own RTX 3060 floor. The producer and engineer syntheses carry the same finding; it is recorded here because the effects pass is where the cost lands.
13. **Process findings carried, not re-litigated.** Argon finding 6: A5's "seven weapon kinds only" lock is the upstream cause of four missing mark signatures; the spec layer aliased ion, plasma, ram, and boarding before implementation began. Argon finding 8: A5's wreck multiplication (4 to 10 sections per kill, plumes persisting to battle end) collides with Spec A's anti-fog rule ("reserve persistent smoke for the worst ships or 20 ships become a fog bank"); at 8 to 12 kills that is 32 to 120 fire-and-smoke emitters over the hex board. Argon finding 9: A5 section 7 leaves tonemapper, rim light, hitstop, shake, and anticipation open (with a 20x spread on gauss wind-up timing) while faction_looks.json already hardcodes HDR values against those undecided parameters. DeepSeek finding 10: the b_razor_s silent bioplasma deletion removes effect identity at the data layer (owned by the gamedev and QA seats; carried here because VFX mapping cannot be trusted from data.js until the data validators gate it).

## Ranked actions: Opus lane (technical)

1. Re-budget the flash system end to end: one on-screen flash counter in both engines (or in the port, with the browser defect logged under the freeze), and re-budget the A5 wreck and cook-off schedules so background wrecks cannot consume the combat flash budget. Until both halves land, the accessibility cap is a claim, not a feature.
2. Set the clustered-element budget before the effects pass: reconcile the 512 pool against fleet size and the Spec A per-ship mark budget, enforce the decal cap on every append path (gauss and bio included), and decide the Compatibility fallback explicitly (gate it, or accept the decal loss in writing).
3. Implement the missing mark signatures (ion tree, flak, ram crumple, boarding) and the per-section budget with repeat merging; fix the stage ladder so flak chip damage cannot drive structural breaches and craters land at impact points, not pre-picked anchors.
4. Make marks turn-aware: fresh marks glow this turn and cool by turns, not wall-clock seconds, and repair removes or scars hit decals so hull language and game state agree after a repair.
5. Fix plasma's impact palette to the ruled light blue (splash, hull glow, light flash, persistent trace).
6. Run the palette check on the shipped FAC3D table and on the shield and shield_edge fields, including the Eclipse violet against FOE_C pairing, under bloom conditions, not only flat sRGB.
7. Build the living-ship damage ladder's middle stages in the port (HP-driven dents, exposed structure, venting) so damage reads on hulls, not only on HUD bars.
8. Run the flat-color geometry test on the hull set before further material work; give the Pyre-Barge a bespoke hero read or record why the bolt-on pass is the intended final form; make the procedural fallback faction-neutral instead of a Hegemony wedge.
9. Align the cut-in default with the recorded council position (off by default), and lock A5 section 7's open parameters (tonemapper, rim light, timing spreads) before authoring more HDR values against them.
