Rey leg of the battle HUD readability audit (Oct 27 program item 6, pack pinned to 68b42ce406286eb95ea7df6aaf3f683c1231b51d). Method: firsthand read of all nine pack images (three Godot G2 capture batches: a259c36, b7ca8c1, fa8106d; all Godot, no exe build in the pack). This leg settles the disputes the external legs left open and states only what the images show. Findings cite filenames. Verdict first.

OVERALL VERDICT: NOT READY (readability). The battle screen's core loop is legible: your fleet, your ships, your orders. Its awareness layer is not: in the start state and the threat view the enemy the narration keeps announcing is nowhere on screen, the one overlay meant to explain the board's colours never opens, and the tutorial card both covers the approach lane and describes a button state that is not on screen. State verdicts: Start FAIL, Move FAIL, Attack PASS WITH FIXES, Top-down FAIL.

SETTLED: THE (G) QUESTION

Argon's finding 06 claims Launch squadron and Board both display (G) in battle_hud_after.jpg, battle_attack_tip.jpg and battle_sheet_move_preview.jpg. Read directly, all three images show Launch squadron as "(0)" and Board as "(G)". There is no hotkey collision in any image in the pack. The claim does not survive. Two genuine residues stand from the same finding: Concentrated fire (03_attack_tip.png) and the Wings and wing-stance buttons (battle_hud_after.jpg, 03, 04) carry no hotkey labels at all, and those gaps are real inconsistent coverage, not collisions.

FINDINGS

1. [FIX, top] The enemy is not visible in the start or threat states. battle_hud_after.jpg and battle_start_after.jpg: the friendly carrier cluster sits centre frame; the narration reports "Contact: Hegemony Navy, 9 ships on the auspex"; no enemy ship or plate is in frame. 01_start.png: enemy plates ("Interdictor destroyer", "Hegemony picket") bleed in at the very top edge, clipped by the top bar and half-hidden behind the tutorial card, their ships off-frame. 03_attack_tip.png: the hovered destroyer's plate is clipped behind the top bar while its tooltip renders top left. 04_threat_topdown.png, the view that exists to answer "where is the threat": three friendly ships, no enemy unit anywhere, while the narration reports 4 ships. Camera framing may share the blame with the views themselves; the player-facing fact is the same across four images in three batches.

2. [FIX] Nameplates merge in the carrier cluster. battle_hud_after.jpg, battle_start_after.jpg, battle_start_legend.jpg, battle_attack_tip.jpg, battle_sheet_move_preview.jpg: bomber wing plates overprint into single garbled strings ("Bomber wing (Nine Lives)er wing (Nine Lives)"), and the Nine Lives plate is itself covered by the tutorial card's Hide tips button in two of them. In the densest, most common formation in the game, individual wings cannot be told apart by their labels.

3. [FIX] The Engagement legend never explains anything. All nine images: collapsed to a word, three unlabeled swatches and a caret. This includes battle_start_legend.jpg, a capture whose filename indicates it was taken to show the legend. Meanwhile the board uses at least four unexplained colour systems: gold reachable hexes, purple hexes (battle_hud_after.jpg, 01_start.png), the tan and maroon sector washes (02_move_preview.png, 03_attack_tip.png), and the blue squadron rings against green selection rings. Qwen read the 03 sectors as firing arcs; whether they are arcs or threat washes, they run to the board edge well past the card's stated ranges (5 and 7) with no key, so the same overlay is doing unexplained work either way.

4. [FIX] The tutorial card contradicts the screen it sits on. All nine images carry the Admiral's Command card saying the Command "glows gold in the top bar". In every image the Command button is dark (dark navy in the JPG batches, dark with gold text in the PNG batch) while Pass and End turn are the gold buttons. The card also sits over the approach lane in the start states and over the carrier cluster's plates. Its content is a first battle's most-read text; it is currently teaching a cue that points at the wrong button.

5. [FIX] Move preview answers the wrong half of the question. battle_sheet_move_preview.jpg and 02_move_preview.png give cost and guns-bearing ("3 movement. 0 of 2 guns bear on an enemy from here.") and nothing about incoming threat, the turn budget the move consumes (02: the card says "Turns 2" while the hover sits at "Movement 4 of 4"), or the path. In 02 the tooltip also covers the selected ship's own nameplate, clipping it to "Mar". The destination arrow in battle_sheet_move_preview.jpg renders in front of Gutter Queen while Nine Lives is the selected ship.

6. [FIX] The right column cannot hold a boon ship's card. battle_hud_after.jpg and battle_attack_tip.jpg: the Nine Lives card fills the column with a scrollbar, the help paragraph is crushed to one clipped line or absent, and the dark red warning text ("Has not moved: enemies get +10 to hit") is clipped by the scrollbar at the card's right edge. The same warning text is the lowest-contrast text on the card in 02 and 03 as well (dark red on navy). In the PNG batch the help moves to the top right when nothing is selected (01_start.png), so its position also changes with selection state.

7. [NOTE] Toggle and stance states are invisible. Threat (R) and Top-down (T) render identically to Menu and Speed in every image, including 04_threat_topdown.png where top-down is presumably active. Wing stance reads as prose ("Wing stance: Strike. Default.") above three identical unhighlighted Wings buttons. A player cannot tell which view they are in or which stance their wings hold from the controls themselves.

8. [NOTE] Help text is duplicated and drifts between captures. The full hotkey paragraph at the foot of the right card and the one-line bottom strip ("Right-drag orbit ... Enter end activation") appear together in most images. Across batches the wording drifts too: "Your activation (3 ready)" and Pass in the PNG batch against "Your turn" and End turn in the JPG batches; blackletter Round header and sentence-case panel headers in b7ca8c1 against clean serif and uppercase headers in a259c36 and fa8106d. Restated per the chair's provenance correction: this is cross-capture drift between G2 builds, not two products; the pack does not say which batch is current, so no leg can grade the drift's direction.

9. [NOTE] The ship card's three section bars invite a shield misread. The Prow, Midships, Engines bars sit directly above prose listing four shield facings (battle_hud_after.jpg, 03_attack_tip.png, 04_threat_topdown.png). The chair had the same first impression as the Argon leg: they read as shield bars. Layout clarity, not wrong data.

10. [NOTE] Edge placement wastes the frame. In 01_start.png the player's own Oathkeeper is half cut off at the bottom edge; in battle_start_legend.jpg most of the board is empty dark hexes while the formation is compressed under the panels. Minor against findings 1 to 6.

WHAT READS WELL: the fleet panel is clean and scannable, weapon cards carry range and behaviour in plain words, the attack tooltip's content (hull, armour, evasion, per-weapon range math, "No ready weapon can reach this target") is exactly the right information, and gold for the active player's affordances is consistent everywhere except the Command button the tutorial describes.

NOT CHECKED: any state past round 1 (damage states, wrecks, the Nemesis banner); the expanded legend's contents (it never appears expanded); animation or hover behaviour; resolution scaling below the capture size.
