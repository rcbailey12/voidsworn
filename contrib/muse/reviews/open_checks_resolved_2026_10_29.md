## 2. h_board sabotage: implemented, but not as the text promises (boons item, ANSWERED with a twist)

The boon text (data.js:646) promises that "a failed boarding always sabotages a system," and its fx emits only boardPct. Boarding resolution (boardUnit, battle.js:901) has three outcomes. On a capture, no sabotage. On the bridge-holds outcome (target crew reaches zero but the hull cannot be captured), two systemDamage calls fire unconditionally, for any attacker, boon or not. On the partial outcome (the boarding resolves without capture), sabotage is a coin flip: a system is damaged when rnd() < .5 OR the attacker holds any boardPct. So sabotage itself happens on two of the three branches; what the boon adds exists only on the partial branch, where it converts a 50 percent chance into a certainty. On the bridge-holds branch the guarantee is already universal without the boon, and on a capture there is no sabotage for anyone. Verdict: the description overpromises in scope, not in existence. The defect is wording: "a failed boarding" should read as the partial outcome the boon actually governs. One-line text fix candidate for Opus. (Refinement, Oct 29, 2026, Argon red-team leg: the alternative shape once floated here, carrying the sabotage guarantee into the capture branch, is withdrawn. A capture succeeds and the ship becomes the attacker's prize, so sabotaging it would damage the capturer's own new hull. The wording fix is the whole fix.)

Correction (Oct 29, 2026, after the DeepSeek red-team leg, commit 1da30590): the original filing of this item said the promise "is kept only on the partial-outcome branch," which reads as sabotage occurring only there. That phrasing was wrong, and the paragraph above replaces it: sabotage occurs on the bridge-holds branch unconditionally; the partial branch is only where the boon's own contribution lives. The mechanism analysis and the verdict are unchanged. The same leg correctly noted that several answers in this document rest on full-repo reads beyond the evidence pack's slices (the mount separation in item 4, the install consequence in item 3, the launch count in item 5); those are chair reads at the pin, marked here as beyond-pack rather than pack-proven, and the leg's pack-bound CANNOT DECIDE verdicts on them are a scoping fact, not a refutation.

## 3. Weapon-kind modules: an install guard exists and checks ability, not weapon kind (modules item, CORRECTED)

The MODULES table carries exactly one `only` field (hangar, only:'launch', data.js:165). This item's first filing claimed no fit guard exists anywhere; that was WRONG, and the Argon red-team leg's fork (either a guard exists or the only field is dead data) forced the right search. A guard exists at campaign_run.js:128: the fit screen offers a module only for ships where the module has no `only`, or the hull's ABILITY equals the `only` value. So hangar fits only launch-ability hulls, enforced. The same search confirms the surviving core of the original finding: the guard keys on ability alone, no weapon-kind check exists anywhere, so a crystal, magazine, capacitor, regen, loaders, or flakplus module (none of which carry an `only`) can be fitted to a hull with no matching weapon kind and silently does nothing. Corrected fix shape: the enforcement point is campaign_run.js:128; extend it with a weapon-kind compatibility test, or give the weapon-kind modules an equivalent guard field the same line can read. The pattern and the guard both exist; the kind dimension is what is missing. (Correction filed Oct 29, 2026; the section heading's original "no fit guard exists" claim is superseded by this paragraph.)

## 4. battery versus hbattery: dominance is table-real, mount-separated (weapons item, CLOSED)

On the table, hbattery strictly dominates battery (shots 5 vs 4, dmg 7 vs 6, crit 6 vs 5, identical range, optimal, accuracy, falloff; data.js:5-6), as DeepSeek said. Mounts separate them in practice: plain battery appears on the penitent cruiser (cp3) and its variants only, while hbattery appears only on the cathedral battleship (cp5) and the ascendant dreadnought (cp8). Weapon cost is not priced per mount; hull cost derives from CP by formula (round((60cp+10)/5)*5) with explicit overrides, so the dominance never arbitrages: no hull offers the player a choice between the two. Verdict: a table-level wart, not an in-game defect. If a future hull ever mounts both, it becomes a real one; a data comment or a checkpack rule (no hull mounts both battery and hbattery) is the cheap guard.

## 5. Hive wing sources: the ceiling is higher than the launch count (squadrons item, ANSWERED)

canStance (battle.js:614) accepts a unit whose ability is launch OR whose folded fx carries hive. The hive fx comes from boons and the hive aspect, and core.js:214 folds fx.hive into a hull's derived hangar even when the base hull has none (hangar becomes fx.hive when the template has no hangar at all). So a ship with no launch ability can become a wing source through boon or aspect, and the wing-source count is not capped by the launch hulls; mechanically, boon-granted sources get stance control today, intended or not (a design read for Opus).

Correction (Oct 29, 2026, Argon red-team leg, verified by chair re-read): this item's first filing claimed battle.js:326-327 "already treats a hive flagship as a second carrier for enemy-response purposes, so the generation side knows about non-launch sources." That was a misread. At battle.js:326-327 the base count nCar0 counts only hulls whose TEMPLATE has a hangar, and the doctrine bonus (a carrier admiral or the hive aspect) adds its +1 only when nCar0 is already at least 1. A fleet whose wings come solely from the hive aspect or hive boons on non-carrier hulls therefore counts as ZERO carriers for the enemy point-defense adaptation, which triggers only at nCar >= 2: the generation side is blind to wing fleets with no true carrier, the exact opposite of the filed claim. The corrected consequence is sharper than the original: a pure-hive fleet fields wings the enemy never adapts to.

## 6. The ceiling fill loop can strand budget (AI item, MEASURED BY READING)

genEnemyFleet's fill loop (battle.js:156) runs while budget >= 1 AND the list is under the ceiling AND a guard counter lasts. Argon's stranding claim was marked INFERRED in its leg; the loop's structure confirms the mechanism: because hull CP costs are integers of 1 or more and the loop exits on the ceiling as well as the budget, any fleet that hits the ceiling with budget left over strands the remainder by construction. The CP sims commissioned alongside this pass measure the magnitude per faction and sector; this item records only that the mechanism is real, not how much it costs.
