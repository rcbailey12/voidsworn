# First-time player leg: DeepSeek

Model gate: DeepThink on, Search off, verified at both ends. Chat:
https://chat.deepseek.com/a/chat/s/e2a5f9d7-dd99-4f2b-9cd8-a75bdc21ea78
Evidence note: 14 of the 15 pack pictures reached the model;
11_firing.jpg would not attach (file grant rejected), and the model
was told it was missing. Its one item that leans on the gap (an
"abrupt jump" from picture 14 to the defeat in 15) rests on a wrong
premise: 14 and 15 are from two different runs, not a sequence. The
chair rests nothing on that item.

## 1. Fun

- 03_flagship_pick: Pattern: Slay the Spire / FTL starting loadouts.
  Choosing between Ram, Spear, or Admiral aspects immediately defines
  my run. I can see exactly how my starting strategy will differ.
- 06_move_preview: Pattern: Into the Breach shows enemy intent.
  Showing "0 of 3 guns bear on an enemy from here" before I commit to
  a move is excellent. It respects my time by preventing blind
  misclicks.
- 07_enemy_clicked: Pattern: Battletech's armor/damage readouts.
  Hovering over an enemy to see armor, evasion, and specific blocking
  (asteroids) is exactly what a tactics player wants to see before
  firing.
- 08_tip_card_legend: Pattern: Hades' contextual tutorials.
  Explaining terrain modifiers (Nebula, Debris) right on the
  battlefield, with an option to "Turn off all tips," respects both
  new and veteran players.
- 12_victory_salvage: Pattern: FTL's post-battle scrap screen. Losing
  the Cold Reckoning hurts, but the "Lifepod" mechanic (where a
  wounded captain will take over your next hull) gives a strong
  narrative hook. It makes losses feel like part of the story, not
  just a game over.
- 13_promotions: Pattern: XCOM's level-up perks. "Steady hand" vs.
  "Rapid cycling" is a real choice. It makes me feel like my
  surviving captains are becoming veterans with specific roles.

## 2. Not fun

- 05_battle_start: The UI is extremely dense. I have to parse
  shields, hull, three weapon slots, and P/S/F arcs while also
  looking at the board. Reviewer line: "My eyes glaze over looking
  at the bottom menu." Fix: Simplify the weapon UI; hide the math
  until I hover over the enemy.
- 09_enemy_turn and 14_battle2_under_fire: The ships are tiny, dark,
  and easily blend into the asteroid/nebula background. Reviewer
  line: "I literally can't tell where the enemies are half the
  time." Fix: Add strong color-coding (bright blue vs. bright red
  outlines) or increase the ship scale slightly.
- 04_sector_map: The node-based map is a spiderweb of white lines on
  a black background. Reviewer line: "It's impossible to tell which
  path leads where until you hover." Fix: Highlight the available
  forward paths in a distinct color.
- 02_credits: The Credits screen is just a panel slapped over the
  title screen. Reviewer line: "Sloppy UI layering." Fix: Make it a
  dedicated screen or dim the background entirely.

## 3. Not rewarding

- 12_victory_salvage: The rewards are just credits and scrap.
  Compared to FTL (where you get new weapons) or Hades (where you
  get boons), getting a flat "+36 credits" feels like a spreadsheet
  action, not a triumphant reward. Reviewer line: "The loot after a
  win feels deeply dry." Fix: Drop a physical weapon module or a
  named crew member onto the salvage screen, not just abstract
  numbers.
- 15_defeat: The defeat screen just says "The line is broken." There
  is no mention of meta-progression, unlocking a new ship, or
  retaining a scrap currency. Reviewer line: "I died, and it feels
  like I wasted 45 minutes." Fix: Even a simple "You earned 50 Legacy
  Points" text here would trigger the 'one more run' feeling.

## 4. Confusing

- 05_battle_start: The weapon UI shows letters "P", "S", and "F". I
  assume Port, Starboard, and Forward, but it is not labeled. If I
  don't know that, I don't know how to angle my ship.
- 05_battle_start: The hex grid has blue lines in some areas, and
  orange lines in others. I am guessing blue is my territory and
  orange is the enemy's, but it isn't explained.
- 07_enemy_clicked: The text says "Blocked by the asteroid at 9.10".
  I have no idea where coordinate 9.10 is on the board. A visual
  highlight line showing why the shot is blocked would be clearer
  than coordinates.

## 5. Your review

Thumbs up (with caveats). "Embers of the Hollow Sun is a dense,
crunchy hex-tactics roguelite. The 'Aspect' system and move previews
(showing firing arcs) are excellent, very Into the Breach. The
lifepod mechanic after losing a ship is a brilliant narrative touch.
However, the ships get lost in the busy backgrounds, and the UI is
overwhelming at first. It's a steep learning curve, but if you love
FTL or Battletech, there's a great run hiding here."
