# First-time player leg: Flash seat, run on Gemini Hydrogen

Seat disclosure: the brief names the Flash seat; the Gemini model
picker no longer offers a Flash label, and this leg ran on Hydrogen,
the everyday-help model, selected in the picker. Chat:
https://gemini.google.com/app/1a554bfcce50a85b
Evidence note: 14 of the 15 pack pictures reached the model;
10_pause_menu.jpg would not attach (file grant rejected twice), and
the model stated plainly that nothing from that screen is included
or assumed. Gemini caps attachments at 10 per message, so the set
went in two messages. Chair note: its build-stamp item rests on the
pack's capture stamp, not the game; flagged below and not counted
against the game in the synthesis.

## 1. Fun

- 03_flagship_pick: the aspect choice (Ram, Spear, Admiral)
  immediately sets a run identity. Pattern: Slay the Spire and FTL
  ship layouts, where starting choices genuinely bend how you
  position and fight from turn one.
- 04_sector_map: a node graph with a ticking hunt clock for a named
  nemesis gives every jump stakes. Pattern: FTL, where every jump
  balances reward against a pursuing threat.
- 05_battle_start and 06_move_preview: directional firing arcs
  projected onto the hex grid make positioning tactile. Pattern:
  Star Wars Armada and Battletech broadsides.
- 06_move_preview: the ghost preview with the gun-bearing count.
  Pattern: Into the Breach's clear action previews.
- 07_enemy_clicked and 11_firing: exact combat maths and cover
  blockers in tooltips. Pattern: Battletech and Into the Breach
  players value transparent maths over hidden dice.
- 05_battle_start: the enemy-intent warning ("focusing its fire on
  this ship") makes defensive actions feel smart, not lucky.
- 14_battle2_under_fire: varied objectives beyond deathmatch (hold
  the relay) force ships into contested space. Pattern: Into the
  Breach's positional tension.
- 15_defeat: wrecks become physical battlefield hazards mid-fight.
  Tactical reviewers love emergent terrain.

## 2. Not fun

- 01_title and 02_credits: the title blurb promises permadeath
  ("every hull you lose stays lost, and so does its captain"),
  which 12_victory_salvage contradicts when Lucan Ivers survives in
  a lifepod. Reviewer line: "The game breaks its own permadeath
  rule on the very first battle." Fix: reword the title text so
  captains can sometimes escape in lifepods, wounded, matching the
  mechanic.
- 05_battle_start and 06_move_preview: round 1 opens with all
  enemies out of range, an empty approach turn. Fix: tighten
  deployment so fast ships or long-range lances can threaten on
  round 1.
- 07_enemy_clicked and 08_tip_card_legend: the "No ready gun can
  reach it" banner sits centre-screen and stays stuck after the
  tooltip closes. Fix: state it once, in the target card only.
- 07_enemy_clicked and 09_enemy_turn: asteroids blend into the
  backdrop yet silently block shots. Fix: distinct hex borders or
  rim light on blocking asteroids, and highlight the exact
  obstructing rock when aiming.
- 08_tip_card_legend: two stacked panels cover the right third of
  the screen over the enemy flank. Fix: anchor tips to a compact
  bar, dismiss one card at a time.
- 09_enemy_turn: during the enemy turn the bottom HUD still shows
  my flagship's weapon panel, and ships are tiny against dark
  space. Fix: show the acting enemy ship's card and pan to its move.
- 11_firing: firing zooms in and throws a dark tooltip directly over
  the beam and target, cutting off the bottom HUD. Fix: offset the
  combat tooltip to the side of the target hex.
- 14_battle2_under_fire: enemy and player ships look nearly
  identical in silhouette and colour at tactical zoom. Fix: distinct
  hull paint, clearer team-coloured nameplates, high-contrast
  markers.
- 15_defeat: zero breakdown of why I lost, though the objective
  stood at hold the relay 2/3 when the fleet was wiped. Fix: a
  concise recap (final blow, damage per ship, how close the win
  was).

## 3. Not rewarding

- 12_victory_salvage: winning a 7-round battle and losing a ship
  pays +44 credits and forces a pick of only one of three near
  identical "Scrap it" options, no captured hull, module or
  protocol. Fix: guarantee one tangible non-credit choice, or scrap
  all wrecks automatically and spend the choice on a real reward.
- 12_victory_salvage and 13_promotions: the victory screen says
  "promoted to Commander", then the promotions screen labels him
  "Captain", and the perks are flat stat nudges. Fix: align the
  rank title, and pair stat perks with a signature active order or
  a visible change.
- 13_promotions: a real equipment drop ("battery loaders stripped
  from the Hullbreaker raider") is buried in the bottom ticker.
  Fix: present salvaged modules with an icon, a stat comparison,
  and an "Equip now" prompt.

## 4. Confusing

- 03 vs 05: the flagship is Vigilant Ash in the pick and map, but
  battle 1 flies Resolve of the Reach, Dusk and Oathkeeper, and
  salvage returns to Vigilant Ash's fleet; nothing in the battle
  HUD says which mode or run this is. (Chair note: this is the
  evidence set mixing runs; the underlying ask, show run context in
  the battle HUD, is recorded in the synthesis.)
- 05 and 14: weapon arc letters P, S, F and T are never defined on
  screen.
- 07 and 11: the tooltip's shield pool and "this side catches"
  numbers vs the HUD's single shield bar; how one pool splits
  across facings is opaque.
- 03 and 04: Cores, Signals and Protocols are named but never
  explained.
- 14 and 15: a new Heat meter and the relay counter appear with no
  on-screen explanation.
- 14 and 15: red patches on the ship damage diagram, with no
  tooltip saying what they mean.

## 5. Review

Thumbs down (for now). "Embers of the Hollow Sun nails the Star
Wars: Armada and Into the Breach tactical itch: directional firing
arcs, move previews with gun-bearing counts, and transparent damage
math feel genuinely smart. Right now, though, early runs stumble
over empty opening turns, hard-to-read ships and asteroids, and
flat post-battle rewards where salvaging '+25 credits' barely
registers. Clean up the UI clutter and make loot and promotions
feel punchy, and this easily flips to a recommendation."
