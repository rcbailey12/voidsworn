Battles

Wreckage and debris in battles: look at the battle backdrop and wreckage fields (the floating debris around a fight). Good: debris looks like recognisable pieces of ships. Bad: debris still looks like plain rocks or generic blocks.

Ship deaths: in any battle where a ship breaks apart or dies, move close to the wreckage. Good: broken pieces look solid inside, and deaths feel big and real in space. Bad: any broken piece still looks hollow or empty inside.

Stations

Station shapes: on the campaign map, go to the station screens at their map nodes, and look at any view with a station. Good: stations have visibly more structure than the plain shapes of the last build. Bad: stations still read as a few plain blocks stuck together.

Station paint: same station views. Good: plates read as metal, light and dark steps separate the parts, no plastic look. Bad: bright toy-like panels, or paint so dark and flat that the new shapes disappear.

Note: Do not judge stations as finished. More surface detail (more bumps and features on the outside) is ordered but not in this build.
