# Playtest checklist for the next build

Start: On your PC, open Downloads\voidsworn_test and double-click play_godot.bat. If it will not start, use play_godot_safe.bat instead. This is the build made after the last one you played. Start a run the usual way: title screen, pick an admiral and faction, then play from the campaign map.

## In battles

1. Look at: A ship that breaks apart or dies. Move in close on the pieces.
   Good: The broken pieces look solid inside, like real broken ship parts all the way through.
   Bad: Any broken piece that looks hollow or empty inside, like a thin shell.

2. Look at: The moment a ship dies.
   Good: Deaths feel big and loud, and they fit being out in space.
   Bad: A death that feels small, flat or quiet for a ship that size.

3. Look at: The bits of wreckage spread around the fight.
   Good: The wreckage looks like pieces of real ships that broke up.
   Bad: Wreckage that still looks like plain rocks or plain blocks.

## At the stations

Note: The stations are not finished yet. They have new shapes and new paint in this build, but a later pass will add much more surface detail. Judge the shapes and paint below, not the final level of detail.

4. Look at: Each station, the fleet station and the refinery, on its station screen and in any view that shows it.
   Good: Each station has clearly more structure than the plain shapes in the last build, and nothing looks taken away.
   Bad: A station that still reads as a few plain blocks stuck together.

5. Look at: The paint on both stations, up close and from farther back.
   Good: The plates read as metal, the lighter and darker shades keep the parts separate, and there is no plastic look.
   Bad: Bright panels that look like toy plastic, or paint so dark and flat that the new shapes disappear.

## Screenshots to take

- One close shot of a broken ship, showing the inside of a broken piece.
- One shot of a ship death or the wreckage left right after one.
- One shot of a wreckage spread during a fight.
- One shot of the fleet station.
- One shot of the refinery.
- One shot of anything that looks wrong, hollow, plain or toy-like, wherever you find it.

# Fidelity note

- The pack says deaths are louder and more space-real, but it names no specific sound or moment to listen for, so checklist item 2 can only ask for the overall feel of a death, not a named effect.
- The pack does not name a specific battle, admiral, faction or map stop that guarantees a ship death, a wreckage spread or a station view. The checklist therefore says any battle and each station in any view that shows it, rather than sending the owner to a named place the pack does not support.
- The pack gives the run flow only in broad terms (title screen, pick an admiral and faction, campaign map, battles from map nodes, station and shipyard screens at their map nodes). I have not added menu names or steps beyond that.
- The build time in the pack, about 20 minutes to make the build, is build-side information, not something the owner can check while playing, so it is not in the checklist.
- Nothing in the pack supported adding checks for the changes already present in the last build, such as automatic squadrons or the earlier space-real deaths. Those are left out of the checklist on purpose, as the pack says they are not new.
