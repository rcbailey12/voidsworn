VOIDSWORN PLAYTEST CHECKLIST - NEXT BUILD AFTER g2_pt_d03ac22
Board issue 202, owner: muse
HOW TO START
The build lands in Downloads\voidsworn_test on your PC. Double-click
play_godot.bat to launch. If it will not start, try play_godot_safe.bat
instead. You will see the title screen, then pick an admiral and faction,
then the campaign map. Battles come from map nodes; stations and the
shipyard sit at their own map nodes. The Nemesis shows on the campaign
map and you meet it in battle. This build is made from current main and
takes about 20 minutes to produce.
WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE LAST BUILD YOU PLAYED
Four things you can see. Two in battles, two at stations. Nothing else
new is visible. The automatic squadrons, the first round of space-real
deaths, the flipbook weapon hits, the campaign map fixes, and the admiral
balance changes were already in your last build. They are not new.
--- BATTLES ---
CHECK 1: Broken ship pieces now have solid insides; deaths are louder
and feel more real in space.
Where: any battle where a ship breaks apart or dies. Get close to the
wreckage. Use the closest camera view you can on the debris.
Good: broken hull pieces look solid inside, like torn metal with depth.
Deaths feel big and loud.
Bad: any broken piece that still looks like an empty shell or a hollow
box. A death that feels quiet or small.
CHECK 2: Wreckage fields are built from real ship pieces, not generic
chunks.
Where: battle backdrops and the floating debris fields during fights.
Look at what floats around and behind the fighting ships.
Good: the debris reads as recognisable bits of ships. Panels, struts,
hull fragments. You can tell a ship broke to make this mess.
Bad: debris that looks like plain rocks, grey blobs, or generic blocks
with no ship character.
--- STATIONS ---
CHECK 3: Both stations (the fleet station and the refinery) have new
shapes added to them.
Where: go to the station nodes on the campaign map. Open the station
screen and any view where a station sits in frame.
Good: stations have visibly more structure than the plain shapes you saw
last time. New additions on the hull, extra forms. Nothing has been moved
or taken away from the old shape; only added to.
Bad: stations that still read as a few plain blocks stuck together with
no extra structure.
CHECK 4: Both stations have been repainted so the plates read as metal.
Where: same station views as Check 3.
Good: the panels look like metal plates. Different parts of the station
separate clearly by shade and value. No toy look.
Bad: bright white or tan panels that look like plastic. Or paint so dark
and flat that you cannot tell the new shapes apart.
--- SCREENSHOTS TO TAKE ---
One broken ship piece, close up, showing whether the inside is solid.
One full death moment in a battle (the explosion or break-apart).
One wreckage field during a fight, showing what the debris looks like.
The fleet station, full view.
The refinery, full view.
Anything that looks wrong or off to you. Grab it and note what screen.
That is the whole visible change set. Four checks. If a station or a
battle looks the same as last time in ways not listed here, that is
expected: only these four things changed.
