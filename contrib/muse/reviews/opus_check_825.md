## Verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS

## The Board's questions

The mirror option: leave it exactly as it is. It lives in shared paint_stack.py, defaults off, and a stack opts in through its LAYERS table; only the Eclipse sets it, because evenness is the Eclipse's law (R243, R256). Extending it would fight two factions' written identities (the Hullbreakers are lopsided on purpose, R240 and the KB1CC sheet; the Chorus is symmetric where a body would be and ragged where scarred), and the machined fleets have no ruling and no measured complaint. Moving it into the Eclipse stack would fork the paint code for no gain. If a future ruling asks for evenness elsewhere, the switch is already there.

The thruster angles: do not fix now. kit.py's thruster helper builds its frame from normal.orthogonal(), which does not mirror, so the four attitude thrusters sit at unmatched angles side to side. The visible cost is a few pixels at the flanks of a ship Ryan passed at R272; no beauty read has flagged it. The real cost is the fixture: kit.py is hashed into the A16 fixture, so any edit re-strikes the fixture, re-opens the flagship's build verification, and changes thruster orientation on every ship built afterwards. The panel's agreed trigger: fix it piggybacked on the next kit.py change that already forces a re-strike, or if a beauty read ever calls the thrusters out. The agreed fix shape: derive the frame from an up hint projected into the surface, as the same file's _surface_part already does, with ChatGPT's caveat recorded (reflection reverses handedness, so the fix gets tested on mirrored thruster pairs, A16 rebuilt and eyeballed before its fixture is deliberately re-struck).

R256 itself: satisfied, unanimously. Five causes fixed at five levels, measured by the PR's own mirror_check study (painted mean difference 13.0 to 2.8 of 255, pixels over 24 from 11.3% to 0.8%, corona light with no twin 30.6% to 6.6%), and the ruling landed in ships.md item 7 with the figures framed as reported, not a bar. On the residual 6.6%: the majority read it as explained by the unmirrored geometry under the paint; ChatGPT's narrower point is the accurate record and the chair adopts it: the study measures rendered image difference, which also captures occlusion, antialiasing and lighting, so the residual is a documented residual consistent with the two leftovers, not proven to come entirely from them, and not a paint failure either way. The blockout corona figure (1.3% with no twin) is the cleaner evidence that the paint sources of asymmetry are gone.
