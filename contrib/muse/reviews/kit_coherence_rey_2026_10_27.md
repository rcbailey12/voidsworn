# Faction kit coherence audit, Rey leg (chair)

Leg: Rey, Oct 27, 2026 (Oct 27 program item 3). Pinned commit: 68b42ce406286eb95ea7df6aaf3f683c1231b51d.
Basis: the same blind pack the external legs read (data.js lines 1 to 275, lore bible round 8 faction chapters 2 to 7, the October 2026 asymmetry rulings excerpts), plus chair checks of the full tree at the same pin (battle.js, campaign_run.js, core.js), which the pack slice did not cover. Where a chair check changes a severity an external leg assigned, this leg uses the corrected severity and says so. Claims are marked OBSERVED (cited entry or line) or INFERRED (reasoning from it). This leg is an observation set for Opus to confirm, per the standing role split.

## Overall verdict: PASS WITH CONDITIONS

No BLOCKER. One approved ruling is not coded anywhere in the browser campaign (finding 1), five further FIXes, four NOTEs. Every faction is playable and testable as it stands. The pack alone makes three rulings look missing; the full tree resolves two of them (the Glasswright Brace prohibition is coded and enforced, Hegemony synchronized fire lives in battle.js mechanics) and the third, the Voidsworn carrier gate, was never adopted in the form the price ladder drafts assumed (the synthesis marks it adoptable with a condition, and bible 2.7 says carriers kept), so the Hearth carrier in the roster is not a breach on this record.

## Ranked findings

1. FIX, Chorus. The approved Chorus shipyard repair prohibition is not coded in the browser campaign. OBSERVED: the price ladder synthesis records "Chorus no shipyard repair (approved, NEEDS CODE, scope across shipyard, Drydock, and Field repairs OPUS TO CONFIRM)". FAC_GATES (data.js:541) carries only the corsair lance kind gate and the ithar noBrace flag; there is no chorus entry. ACT.repair (campaign_run.js:198) and ACT.repairAll (campaign_run.js:199) restore hull for credits with no faction check, the drydock screen (campaign_run.js:206) is likewise unconditional, and the field repairs node is defined for any fleet (campaign_run.js:3). Non-Crusade runs exist in this code (RUN.fac drives prize and name logic), so the gap is reachable, not theoretical. INFERRED: a Chorus fleet can repair at a shipyard exactly like any other fleet, so the approved asymmetry does not exist in play. Scope care: in-battle regen, the regrow ability and the Regrowth tide Command are the faction's designed identity, not breaches; the missing piece is only the campaign repair gate. This is the audit's most concrete new defect, and no external leg could see it from the pack. The Godot campaign port was not checked (see Not checked).

2. FIX, Hegemony. The ion pulse ability is fully dead against Chorus and Eclipse. OBSERVED: ABILITIES.ionpulse (data.js) reads "Every enemy ship within 4 hexes loses half of each shield facing", and every Chorus hull (b_drone to b_boss) and every Eclipse hull (d_scythe to d_boss) in HULLS has sh:0. The ability sits on h_interdictor, the Cordon destroyer, and nowhere else. INFERRED: in two of six matchups the destroyer's ability slot does nothing at all. The wider ion family degrades rather than dies (W.ion keeps its base damage when its ion:2.5 has no shield to strip, and the plasma trait keeps its +1 damage when its pierce finds no shield), so the total dead zone is the ability, not the faction's guns. Convergent: all three external legs, and this leg agrees it is the top weapon-side FIX.

3. FIX, Eclipse. The faction entry still renders in Necron green with tomb styling. OBSERVED: FACTIONS.deathless (data.js:126) sets trim '#1fbf4a', glow '#3dff6a', style 'tomb'. Bible 7.2 bans green glow and every tomb tell; bible 7.3 specifies dark violet-black plates, a white-gold eclipse corona and violet-white inner glow. The same entry's blurb still says Eclipse is "stealing starlight", where bible 7.1 has it siphoning stellar energy (and Ryan ruled the wording change on Oct 27). INFERRED: unlike the internal keys (gauss, doom, phylactery), which are invisible and rate a NOTE only, the trim, glow and style fields are player-visible rendering data, so the shipped Eclipse still wears the exact colours round 8 removed. Argon catch, chair-confirmed at HEAD.

4. FIX, Hegemony. The guns named "Plasma lance" are not lances, and lance buffs land on the ion cannon instead. OBSERVED: FAC_WNAMES.hegemony (data.js:36) names turbo "Plasma lance" and hturbo "Heavy plasma lance", but both weapons are k:'battery' (data.js:11 and 12). MODULES.crystal (fx lancePct) and PERKS.lanceadept key off the lance kind, which in the Hegemony set is only W.ion, the Ion cannon. The battery loaders module is what actually buffs the two "lances". INFERRED: a player reading names will buy lance buffs for the wrong guns and never see why they did nothing. Either the names or the kind needs to move; that choice is Opus's. Argon catch, chair-confirmed at HEAD. Related text lag, NOTE level: the Hegemony blurb still leads with ion shield-stripping and fighter screens and never mentions formation or synchronized fire, so the faction's shop-window text teaches the pre-ruling identity.

5. FIX, Ashfall Hullbreakers. Boarding is half of the faction's promised close-combat kit and has no expression in the kit itself. OBSERVED: bible 4.5 promises "rams and boarding" and 4.3 puts boarding claws at the bow. The corsair kit delivers the ram half (ABILITIES.overburn, +50% ram damage, on c_raider and c_marauder) and nothing for boarding: the only boarding-named weapon in the game, W.tendril "Boarding tendrils", belongs to the Chorus. Boarding exists as a system (the campaign capture flow and the help text both use it), and Grax's doctrine is literally "Ram and board". INFERRED: the doctrine name promises a verb the faction's own ships express no better than anyone else's. Worded as a kit expression gap, not a missing mechanic. Agrees with DeepSeek's FIX.

6. FIX, Eclipse (small, text level). Seveth's Command refills shields his fleet does not have. OBSERVED: ADMIRALS.khepra (data.js:236) has cmd 'Sunshade wall' with the text "All shields refill and your ships take 40% less hull damage this round", while every Eclipse hull has sh:0. The other three admirals sharing the 'iron' command template (Thale, Maren, Brin) all lead shielded fleets, so the copied text works for them and is half dead only for Seveth. INFERRED: every Eclipse run under Seveth shows a Command description whose first clause can never do anything. Per-faction command text already exists per admiral, so this is a one-line wording fix. Residue of Argon 6.1, at corrected severity.

7. NOTE, cross-faction. Duplicate ability entries under faction-flavoured names. OBSERVED: ABILITIES.evasive and ABILITIES.phase have identical description text and effect (+25 evasion, +2 movement, cd 3); ABILITIES.focus and ABILITIES.frenzy are likewise identical (+30% damage this turn, cd 3); and ab:'nova' is shared by three faction bosses (ascendant, h_boss, d_boss). INFERRED: if the fx pass differentiates these per faction, the duplication is cosmetic; from data alone the six factions carry fewer distinct abilities than the roster suggests. Convergent across all three external legs.

8. NOTE, Eclipse. The Unsetting does not reanimate, and nothing in the record says that is deliberate. OBSERVED: d_scythe, d_jackal and d_harvester all carry reanimate:true in HULLS; d_boss does not. Bible 7.5 says the fallen come back, with repair swarms rebuilding each hull once. INFERRED: excluding the flagship may be sound boss tuning, but it is currently an undocumented exception to the faction's signature rule; one line in the record either way closes it. Convergent: DeepSeek and Qwen.

9. NOTE, Sunward Crusade. Three small coherence gaps, none functional. OBSERVED: (a) MOVE_RULES.voidsworn makes port and starboard guns hit 40% harder after moving, but warden, harrow and reliquary mount no P or S weapons, and Malek's starting fleet (harrow, harrow, warden, warden) contains no broadside gun at all, so that fleet cannot touch its own faction drive rule until it buys a Vigil or a Bastion. (b) FACTIONS.voidsworn has no sq field while the other five factions specify one; battle.js:1159 (wingKind) falls back to 'fighter' when the field is absent and then mixes wing kinds by need, so Hearth launches work, and the omission is data hygiene rather than a bug. (c) MOD_NAMES and RELIC_NAMES (data.js) carry entries for the other five factions but none for voidsworn, so Crusade refits and salvage show the generic names every other faction gets flavour for. Items (b) and (c) converge with Qwen and Argon; item (a) is Argon's catch, confirmed from the pack.

10. NOTE, cross-faction. The dead-module worry is already handled on the offer path; perks are the open end. OBSERVED: MOD_NEED (data.js:259) plus the Chorus and Eclipse MOD_NAMES entries (a shield capacitor named "Membrane bloom" for a shieldless faction, and so on) look like dead picks, but core.js:234 (modulePool) filters module offers through core.js:179 (fleetKit), which only sets the shields flag when a fleet hull actually has shields, and core.js:239 (relicUseful) separately blocks the shield relic for a shieldless fleet. The code comment at core.js:233 states the intent outright. INFERRED: a pure Chorus or Eclipse fleet is never offered shield modules, so the faction names for gated-out modules are inert table completeness (a mixed fleet holding a captured shielded hull could still use them, which is a reason to keep the names). This downgrades Argon 4.2 and 6.1 from BLOCKER and answers Qwen's open question from the pack. What this leg did not trace is the captain perk path: whether PERKS.deflector (shield regeneration) or PERKS.stubborn (Brace bonus) are filtered when offered to captains of shieldless or noBrace fleets. See Not checked.

## Verified passes (chair checks, for the record)

- Glasswrights, no Brace: PASS. FAC_GATES.ithar.noBrace (data.js:541) and canOrder enforces it (battle.js:1036). The Glasswright kit otherwise matches bible 6.5 (needle trait on shard and turret for precision and crits, star trait on the star torpedo, blink for the short jumps). DeepSeek and Argon both tagged this as a breach because the gate sits outside the pack's line slice.
- Hegemony, synchronized fire and flagship anchor: PRESENT. setupFactionRules runs at battle start (battle.js:348, comment "B3: Hegemony anchors"), and a grep of battle.js finds the sync and anchor mechanics throughout (51 and 18 hits respectively). The definer is mechanics-side, exactly where the OPUS_HANDOFF anchor definition left it. Presence verified; the anchor radius and sync strength as tuning were not assessed.
- Hullbreakers, no lances: PASS. FAC_GATES.corsair.noKind (data.js:541) with the factionGate helper (data.js:542), applied at prize time (campaign_run.js:448), so a Hullbreaker fleet cannot field a lance-kind hull even by capture.
- Eclipse, no shields: PASS as a roster fact. Every deathless hull is sh:0, rg:0, and reanimate on the line hulls matches bible 7.5.
- Chorus versus Eclipse counter: DELIVERED. FAC_WTRAIT.chorus gives tendril and sporeb the acid trait (damage through shields and armour) and bioplasma the spines trait (armour shred), which is the bible 5.5 answer to Eclipse's heavy armour. Agrees with Qwen's positive confirmation.
