## The Board's five points, plus R218

1. **Every lime action still reachable: HOLDS.** The orders builder (ledger.gd, the _orders function from line 636) lists the orders the ship's data marks usable, Ram X and Board G among them, each with its key and a click hit; Turn carries Q and E; the Command row and End ship are built in; Next ship appears in the orders line when the card offers it and Tab works regardless; guns arm by click or keys 1 to 6; the fleet list and the full log (L) are sheet elements with hits. The one behaviour change is disclosed and deliberate under R177: an order that cannot be given now is hidden rather than greyed, and its key still answers with the reason (ledger.gd line 635 comment; the report's moved table). Nothing the lime screen offered is gone. One gap in the proof, noted not conditioned: the readability test asserts hit areas for the action box, guns, Turn, Next ship, Command, End ship and fleet lines, but never for Ram or Board in its every-order moment; they run through the same keyed() path as Brace, so this is a test-coverage note for the follow-up, not a reachability doubt.
2. **Glacier only for shield readouts, 3D shields keep faction colours: HOLDS.** In ledger.gd every GLACIER use sits inside the shield plan (arcs and figures), and a ui_spec guard fails the build if a non-comment GLACIER line appears outside that function. The hardcoded #92b8e8 elsewhere is shield content only: the shield figures in hover tips (ledger.gd lines 511 and 607) and the "shield" float in battle.gd. hud.gd's BB_MAP translates the old shield cyan to Glacier in text the battle still writes (line 360); the translation is blunt (any old cyan becomes Glacier), but the old cyan meant shields everywhere it was used, and battle.gd itself is guarded against writing it. The 3D half holds by construction: the PR touches no fx file; its scene changes are the ember selection ring (ship_view.gd), the ember hover ring and bone threat wash (board.gd), and camera_rig.gd's screen_shift. The shield bubbles' faction colours are fx code this PR never opens. The 1080 picture agrees (a mint ring on the player's ship, violet enemy markers, no Glacier in space).
3. **Text floor, 17 px at 1080 and 4.5 to 1 over the brightest board: HOLDS, with one method caveat recorded.** The evidence is a real test, not an assertion: ledger_readability_test.gd (in godot_checks.txt, so CI runs it) builds the actual HUD, feeds it through hud.gd's setters, and measures what ledger.gd lays out, which is what it draws. Across seven moments (your turn, enemy turn, prompt, 20 ships, six guns with every order and the longest names, all at once, no ship up) the smallest word is 17 px and the lowest contrast 5.25 to 1, the contrast taken against the sheet's own darkening composited over pure white, the brightest board possible. The chair's own read of the full-size picture agrees: nothing clipped, nothing cramped, the smallest lines legible. The caveat (Qwen's catch, adopted as a note): the test composites the veil over white in sRGB numbers; if the viewport blends the overlay in linear space, the same construction gives a brighter backdrop and TEXT3's margin shrinks below the floor. The game's boards are dark space and nebulae, the pictures show wide margins in practice, and the pure-white construction is already a stress case no real board reaches, so the point holds; but the "over the brightest board possible" wording in the report should either name the sRGB assumption or the test should composite the way the renderer blends. The test also measures sheet items only; the restyled menu, end card, tips and log panel are not in its scope (their sizes in hud.gd are 17 px and up, unchanged in kind by this PR).
4. **No code path left drawing lime: PARTLY HOLDS.** Drawn lime: none. The sheet, the board rings, the banners, floats and every bbcode colour in battle.gd are ember, bone, Glacier or the text strengths; ui_spec guards fail on the lime hex in ledger.gd and battle.gd; the pictures show none. Code paths: the old shell still exists and runs (condition 4). "Built and filled but never shown" satisfies the ruling as a player experiences it and as R191 intends (lime retired from the screen), and it does not satisfy the Board's literal wording. The check records both halves and puts the acceptance to the Board rather than deciding it silently.
5. **The third capture and the enemy turn view.** The capture is the defeat card (condition 2). The view it meant to prove does exist: ledger.gd's turn line has an enemy branch ("Enemy turn", the faction and the acting ship's name, a thin progress line), the last three log lines draw in every state (they are visible even in the defeat picture), and the readability test's enemy-turn moment asserts the sheet reads "Enemy turn" and "Hegemony Navy: Iron Warden" with no FIRE box.
