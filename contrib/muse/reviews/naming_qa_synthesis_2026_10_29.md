# Naming QA synthesis, Oct 29, 2026

Question (Ryan): the packs and chair documents were built while the game's data layer still carried pre-rename display names (Ithar, Deathless, Corsair, Turbolaser, the old admiral names). Did that produce incorrect reads, incorrect data, or incorrect results, in the council's work or in what Opus reads?

Verdict: NO WRONG READS AND NO WRONG RESULTS FROM THE NAMING LAYER. The naming layer is dirty; the data layer underneath it is clean apart from three minor pack defects listed below (none of them caused by name matching, and none of them changed a filed conclusion) and the roster census error documented earlier. One stale document is fixed in the same push as this synthesis.

Method: three legs, run Oct 29, 2026 evening. (A) A classification sweep of every old-name hit in chair analytical prose and pack material: 2,048 hits. (B) A pull-integrity diff: the ground-truth tables extracted programmatically from data.js at pin 264f441d and compared field by field against the Oct 28 battle packs, the Oct 28 progression packs, all ten Oct 29 packs, and the savings-pass packs. (C) An Opus-facing check of OPUS_HANDOFF.md, Opus's verification files, the rename application pack, and the audit syntheses for conclusions resting on name matching across the two naming layers: 18 candidates. Leg reports are held as chair working papers (naming_classification.md, pull_integrity.md, opus_facing_names.md in the QA working folder); every decisive figure is reproduced here.

## Findings

1. Classification (leg A). Of 2,048 hits: 863 are ID-USE (a data id, code key, file name, or quoted code; safe by construction), 1,061 are DISPLAY-ONLY (the right entity under an old label, with no claim depending on the name string), and 124 are SUBSTANTIVE-CANDIDATE hits on 66 unique lines. Adjudicated one by one: none of the 124 is a wrong entity read. Every candidate is a string-subject claim inside a document whose job was comparing the two naming layers (the lore collision checks, the rollout name verification, the rename application pack, the retired word sweep), or a rename-versus-mechanic comparison verified against the data tables in the same pass. No line in chair analytical prose treats two names for one entity as two entities, and no count, comparison, or result changes when an old label is swapped for the approved one.

2. Pull integrity (leg B). No admiral was pulled into the wrong faction, no doctrine or Command was copied from the wrong admiral, no FAC_PRICE row was keyed to the wrong faction, and no weapon row was mismatched to its id, in any pack checked. Verified exact against data.js: all 24 admiral rows in the four savings tree packs (one exception, finding 4 D1); the full FAC_PRICE table in five packs; the weapons pack's 25-weapon mount census; the factionshape pack's CP ladders for all six factions; the squadrons pack's 8 launch hulls and hangar sizes; and the renown totals (LEGACY 635, admiral ladder 930, variants 250, sinks 1,815).

3. Opus-facing (leg C). 18 candidates: 0 ERROR, 2 STALE, 16 SAFE. The sharpest traps came back clean: Pulse laser and Plasma lance were handled as one weapon id with a faction display override (data.js:11 and :36), exactly right in the weapons synthesis; the Nadir triple use (admiral name, rank title, dropped Nemesis pool entry) was separated properly in the rename application pack; and the one real name-confusion product, DeepSeek's "Terran Totality" claim in the full audit, lived entirely inside that external leg and is carried as REJECTED in both the synthesis and Opus's verification, so it never became a conclusion. The 2 STALE hits are both in OPUS_HANDOFF.md (pre-r8 Chorus names presented as current, plus old faction labels throughout its ruling sections); fixed by the names addendum added in this push. No conclusion in the handoff was at risk: its mechanics are code-anchored.

4. Three DATA defects in pack text (chair wording, none caused by name matching, none changing a filed conclusion):
   - D1. tree_pack_full.txt (savings pass) stated Kassar's doctrine as "Synchronized fire" and extended her Command with a formation mechanic. data.js:217 gives her doctrine as "Fire discipline" (batteries fire one extra shot per volley, every gun hits 5% lighter) and a plain free-volley Command. The pack substituted the ruled design framing (Hegemony synchronized fire under a flagship anchor) for her data fields. Fixed in the pack the same evening, with the two layers labeled; Kassar is not re-drafted by any leg, and the brief's worked example remains the proposal's design framing, which Opus should reconcile against the data doctrine when the tree is implemented.
   - D2. The Oct 29 economy pack head calls ransom "55 percent of scrap value". The code computes 55 percent of ship value (shop price); scrap is the separate 35 percent figure, so the pack sentence understates the implied credit value by nearly a factor of three. The Oct 28 chair_verifications states the formula correctly, and the economy synthesis's figures were traced from code, so no filed number changes.
   - D3. The Oct 28 roster pack's judging question says "the 8-point dreadnoughts and the 12-point boss hulls". data.js has exactly one 8 CP dreadnought (ascendant) and boss CPs of 8, 10, 11, 12, 12, 12, so the framing misprices the Corsair and Glasswright bosses. Question framing only; the legs worked from the pack's hull tables and the roster synthesis used the real CP values.

5. Census error spread. The previously documented roster census error (five base hulls per faction, 45 hulls total; actual ladders 5/4/4/4/4/3 and 48 ship hulls) appears beyond the roster pack in exactly two places: roster_leg_prompt.md ("the five base hulls' jobs" per faction) and the Oct 28 variety pack's chair sheet ("45 hulls", in a sentence whose other totals all verify). Both were already corrected in their syntheses. The Oct 29 factionshape pack carries the corrected counts; the savings packs make no hull-count claims.

6. Residual risks, neither a misread. (a) The built artifact voidsworn-3d.html and the portrait tooling still carry pre-rename text; already recorded as a stale-build defect with a rebuild fix in the B lane. (b) The superseded split tree packs used Ithar and Corsair as primary headings, and those labels propagated into the split drafts; those drafts were quarantined and not filed, and the corrected full pack (tree_pack_full.txt) supersedes them. (c) The one near-miss pattern in the legs, a model using Grax Steelgrin from a pre-ruling pin, was caught and annotated in that leg's screening at the time.
