## Conditions

2. **The R247 scale bar is not met, and the check that claims it cannot fail.** review.py line 357 computes the station's height as the tallest loaded ship's extent plus exactly 1.0 unit (height_scale from (max_ship_height + 1.0) / initial height), giving 52.129 against 51.129 for the tallest of 30 loaded hulls, with a plan span of 51.59 x 37.54. check.py lines 49-50 then assert height > max_ship_height, which the fit guarantees by construction; the PASS in review_checks.json proves only that the fit ran. R247 rules that a station "dwarfs every ship class": a 2 percent margin over the largest ship, produced by a +1.0 rule, is parity with the flagship, not dwarfing, and the fit is vertical-only (factor 1.506117), stretching the design half again taller than drawn. The sheet itself asks for the ruling ("Vertical fit factor 1.506117 is provisional and must be judged against the accepted concept"; "The resulting proportions require a Board call before further building"). Condition: before production, the station's size gets a stated scale decision (the R247/R249 scale table, issue 710) under which it dwarfs every ship class, the +1.0 fit rule is replaced by that decision, and the "taller than ships" check tests the decision rather than the fit's own output. DeepSeek and Qwen graded this BLOCKING for the R247 question; the chair records it as the head condition because this delivery is the sheet that asks the question, and production is already gated on the Board's answer.

3. **R247's required evidence is not in the PR.** R247: "Each terrain or station PR shows the battle camera with a ship of each class beside it." The PR contains no image files at all (16 files, none an image); the camera-170 sheet and the low compare are linked from the PR body as paths on the PC, and the low compare shows one cruiser, not a ship of each class. Condition: the sheets are committed with the review docs, and the compare shows every class (frigate through flagship) beside the station at the battle camera, before the Board is asked to judge the scale.

4. **The remaining Done when stays tracked on issue 373.** All of it is declared in the delivery, none of it is hidden, and the check records it so the list survives: production paint stack (stack.py is preview materials and schematic AO today); recipe/export support (station_T2.json itself notes the ship export contract does not yet support stations, and install is false); the full fit review and the tethered arm's swept-volume check (the 49-pose sample shows zero intersections, and the sheet says plainly that discrete samples do not prove a continuous sweep); production seam paths, torn piece export and a destruction capture proving the modelled insides (55,092 inside triangles; Qwen re-added the feature table and the sum is exact); the station-map scene gate at 1080p and 4K (R116); and Ryan's look before any swap.
