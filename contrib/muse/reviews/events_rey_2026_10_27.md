Rey leg of the events audit (Oct 27 program item 5, pack pinned to 68b42ce406286eb95ea7df6aaf3f683c1231b51d). Method: the same evidence pack as the three external legs (data.js lines 275 to 505, battle.js lines 1 to 120), plus direct reads of the campaign outcome machinery in campaign_run.js and campaign3d.js in the working clone at the same HEAD, so the mechanism claims below are code-verified rather than inferred from the data tables alone. Verdict first, findings ranked, OBSERVED throughout unless marked INFERRED.

OVERALL VERDICT: FAIL at the option layer, PASS at the battle-event layer. The campaign event tables contain one option that can never fire, one ambush that is never disclosed, and one systemic flag bug that makes five follow-on unlocks fire on the wrong condition. None of these breaks a run; all of them teach the player that event text does not mean what it says, which is the worst habit a choice system can train.

1. [FIX] The seer event contains an option that can never be chosen. OBSERVED: the seer event header at data.js:293 excludes Ithar (not:['ithar']). A splice at data.js:327 adds an option gated need:{fac:'ithar'} (the glass-song answer, r:{relic:1}). The event can never be offered to the one faction the option requires, so the option is dead data and the relic reward behind it is unreachable. The fix is one line either way (drop the exclusion or drop the splice); which way is a design call, because the exclusion may be deliberate (the seer may be meant to refuse Glasswrights) and the splice the leftover.

2. [FIX] Event flags record the choice made, but every consumer tests only that the event happened. OBSERVED: eventFlags (campaign_run.js:240) stores the chosen option's key or index string in RUN.flags[ev.id]. needMet (campaign_run.js:223) and verbFree (campaign_run.js:330) test only that the stored value is non-null. Consequences, all verified against the tables: the Hegemony drill verb goes free after ANY defector resolution, including selling the defector back; the Ithar attune verb goes free after any lament resolution; the Voidsworn vigil goes free after any shrine resolution, including Leave; the Eclipse waken goes free after any tombhome resolution, including stripping the yard for parts; and the saboteur face option (data.js:408, need:{flag:'defector'}) opens after any defector outcome, including the ransom its own text contradicts ("The defector you questioned named him."). The data already stores which option was picked, so the repair is a comparison change in the two consumers, not new state. This is the audit's most systemic finding: five separate text promises broken by one shared mechanism.

3. [FIX] The beacon event hides both of its consequences. OBSERVED: the base option at data.js:282 grants cred:100 and ambush:true with the text "Their escort might remember your colours", which discloses neither. The splice at data.js:325 adds fl={greed:1} and r.nemClock=1 on top, also undisclosed. The comparable bait event says "ambush" out loud in its own option text, so the disclosure standard exists inside the same table and beacon does not meet it. A player who takes the 100 credits learns about the ambush and the Nemesis clock advance only by suffering them.

4. [FIX] Repair effects in events are gated by faction inconsistently. OBSERVED: the Chorus no-repair ruling is scoped to shipyard and drydock repair (kit audit, from the primary record); regrowth and feeding are designed Chorus identity, which is why the spawn option (data.js:363, healAll:.3, text "regrows") is not itself a violation. The inconsistency is in the generic events: wright, dock and quiet carry repair effects with no chorus gate at all, quiet is the EVENT_FALLBACK every faction can draw, and the shrine event DOES exclude chorus and deathless, proving the design gates repair events by faction where it intends to. One shared healAll key also serves both generic repair (quiet rest, data.js:419, healAll:.1, whose text says "repairs", and which Chorus can take) and Chorus regrowth, so the ruling is not distinguishable in the data. Either wright, dock and quiet take the shrine treatment, or the shrine gate is the anomaly; and if Opus wants the ruling machine-checkable, regrowth needs its own key.

5. [FIX] Haven is the only fight-start event with no free exit. OBSERVED: haven (data.js:414-417) offers raid (a fight), tribute (40 credits, disabled below 40 via eventOptOk) and recruit (corsair only). Moon has a free bombard option; tomb has strip and leave. A non-corsair player under 40 credits has exactly one enabled choice, and it is a fight. The fight is telegraphed in the option text, so this is a design outlier rather than a hidden trap, but it is the only event in the table that can force a battle on a player who did nothing to choose one.

6. [NOTE] A clean core of faction-gated choices is genuinely solved, and the wider pattern is a lesser version of the same shape. OBSERVED: the clean set, where the faction option is strictly better and free: merc oath, veterans oath, reactor metal (data.js:346), well sails (data.js:400), broker debt (data.js:353-354, corsair takes nemIntel free against a 60-credit buy), defector enlist (data.js:391-393, hegemony gains xpLow:50 plus nemIntel against nemIntel alone), lament join. Equipment-gated blue options (flak, torp, shields, lance) are the intended blue-option pattern. Two cautions before calling the wider set solved: several events pay in currencies the simple comparison ignores (fight loot from moon; the greed and loud flags feed the Corsair verb payout), and one price inversion sits inside the pattern: wright grants upgradeOne free while dock charges 100 credits for the same effect. Carry the clean set as working design; the pattern beyond it is a tuning NOTE, not a defect list.

7. [NOTE] Tombhome's wake option is double-gated and the prize is small. OBSERVED: data.js:371-372 applies a redundant faction gate on top of the event's own gating for the wake option. The prize is d_scythe (data.js:98), the Eclipse mote frigate, a small hull, against a 60-credit strip alternative on the same modal. Harmless redundancy, thin reward; both worth a line in a cleanup pass, neither worth its own fix.

8. [NOTE] The loud counter is written but, in the checked files, never read. OBSERVED: eventFlags accumulates fl counters including loud; the Nemesis clock advances via r.nemClock (campaign_run.js:376) and via wins (campaign3d.js:74). No consumer in campaign_run.js or campaign3d.js advances the clock from the loud counter, while the NEM_CLOCK comment promises the clock responds to "every loud choice". No secret advance exists on bait, tomb or haven, and no double increment exists on tithe or convoy; the gap is between the comment and the code, not between two code paths. Either wire the counter or correct the comment.

9. [NOTE] Derelict's cut option promises salvage and pays something else. OBSERVED: the option text promises salvage; the outcome grants mod:1 plus cred:40. Since the rename, salvage is a defined term with its own step ("Strip the wrecks") and its own UI lines, so a text that spends the word on a module and credits is now wrong in a way it was not before the rename tightened the vocabulary.

BEVENTS VERDICT: PASS. OBSERVED: the battle-event layer is the healthy half of this audit. The one candidate defect resolves on a full read: the flare round does work, because battle.js:391 polls for it at round start and zeroes shields (u.sh=[0,0,0,0]) for shielded units; the pack slice ended at battle.js line 120, before that code, which is why two legs could not see it. The boss exclusion in rollBattleEvent reads as intentional, and the Hegemony Nemesis reinforcement is signalled inside eventTick rather than forced at roll time. The shield adaptation picks for Chorus and Eclipse Nemeses (NEM_WEAK.ion, the shields adaptation) converge with the kit audit's ion dead zone and belong to that fix family, not to BEVENTS itself.
