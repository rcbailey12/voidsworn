2. HINGES

CM1 (the 170-unit camera radius, ANCHORED). Named a hinge by all three writer legs. One Babylon-port default (camera_rig.gd r and home_r both 170.0) denominates the px-per-unit readability math of two adopted standards (7.7 px/unit at 1080p) and appears a second time as the floor in the JS overview framing formula (CM9). If the owner's played radius differs, every readability figure in both standards is off by that ratio. The Flash leg's challenge strengthens the point from a different angle (a lead, not evidence): pixel-per-unit standards on a perspective camera vary center to edge, so the ruled (radius, FOV) pair, not radius alone, is the correct denominator. Settlement: section 6, round S-E.

DS7 with DS14 (the capital freeze, ASSUMED). In Godot physics mode, capital break pieces are frozen rigid bodies (fx_death.gd: freeze = death_big and not organic) while frigate pieces tumble freely, inverting the spectacle order that DS14's whole constant set (3 cuts, longer drift, bigger shake, slowmo) buys. DeepSeek hinged on DS14, Qwen and the blind Rey leg on DS7; the chair treats them as one hinge because DS14's paired capture is the settlement for both. The Flash leg challenges the accident framing (lead only): the freeze may be a deliberate safeguard against Jolt penetration jitter on large colliders, and naive unfreezing could rubber-band. The chair adopts that challenge as a settlement amendment, not a finding: the paired capture carries THREE capital variants (frozen as shipped, unfrozen in Jolt, authored visual-kinematic drift with no solver), so the capture answers the spectacle question and the jitter question in the same round.

PF7 (no explosion impulse on bystanders, ASSUMED by absence). Named a hinge by DeepSeek and Qwen. The largest visual event in the game imparts no physical shove to nearby live ships; if that absence is a large share of R88's "sudden and jerky" complaint, choreography fixes alone cannot close it. It is also the physical half of owner question 2 (section 8), which merges it with DS10's boon-gated chain damage and DS11's absent fire spread: three separate silences that are one design decision.

PF9 (the gravity well ghost). Named a hinge by the blind Rey leg. A settled ruling (Dead Star background only, no gravity mechanics) with live code against it: the frozen JS implements the full well (WELL_RIM 3, WELL_PULL 5, WELL_ASSIST 6, WELL_DMG 7, killCause tidal), the Godot fx hook (fx.well, tidal velocity bonus) is verified, and the Godot rules state could not be verified from the files read. No leg proposed reinstating gravity play, and this synthesis does not either. Settlement: one Opus run states and fixes the code state (round S-F).
