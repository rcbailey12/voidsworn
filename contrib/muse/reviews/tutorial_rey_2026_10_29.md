## Notes

- c_nodes: the pack lists the node kinds but does not describe what rest nodes or events do mechanically, so I kept the glosses to kind-level ("choices", "safe stops") and did not invent rest effects or event outcomes.
- c_relic: "one-per-fleet" is the pack's wording. I read it as one of each piece per fleet (no duplicates); if it instead means a cap on total pieces carried, the text needs one word changed.
- c_heat: the pack states Heat pays the flown admiral first and feeds the faction's shadow pool. I phrased the split as overflow going to the pool; the pack does not state the exact split, so the overflow wording is a guess at the mechanism's shape.
- c_prize: the ransom decline is stated in the pack ("owners pay less for each ship you sell back"). I did not invent a rate or a floor for it.
- c_shipyard: the repair-below-replacement rule is stated in the pack's rules, so I put it in the shipyard tip where a player would act on it; repair pricing is described without numbers, as the pack gives none.
- Everything else tracks the pack's specified content with no added numbers. The only number in the set (35% hull for a towed ship) is the pack's own.
