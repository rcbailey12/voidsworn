Job 5 of the Nov 1, 2026 four-job commission (contrib/opus/briefs/muse_2026-11-01b_four_jobs.md). Question: the verified coverage work confirms silent gaps at boarding, brace, and Nemesis adaptation, and the feel pass staged ship deaths over time while their sound still fires in one instant. Deliver a cue list for every battle moment the feel pass added or changed, with free licensed sources (CC0 or CC BY only, rule R40), plus a mixing note.

## Headline decisions

Deaths get shape in sound: three new stage cues (power-down, vent, crack) plus re-timed existing layers for the split and core flash, a death run and cooling chunk for small ships, and a near-silent vent for hulks. The three verified silent events (boarding, brace, Nemesis adaptation) each get one cue dispatched from the branch that already exists. Dense salvos stop losing cue starts by fixing defer windows in the cue data, with a voice-count test to prove it. Nobody has heard the incumbent Hullbreaker track (its file is absent from the snapshot), so the track work is an audition list, not a replacement.
