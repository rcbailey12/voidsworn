1. Headline

The 46-point full-run win-rate spread from Sunward Crusade at 34.0 percent to The Chorus at 80.0 percent is driven by an intersection of mid-run combat sustain, weapon-to-defense mismatches in Sector 2 and Sector 3, and extreme within-faction starting fleet imbalances. Conversely, Sunward Crusade and Hegemony Navy underperform because three of the four Crusade admirals and two of the four Hegemony admirals depend on low-accuracy torpedoes ( evMult 2 ), zero-pierce broadsides diminished by the step 13 enemy brace fix, or shield-dependent ion mounts that lose their multiplier against the shieldless Chorus and Eclipse fleets populating the later sectors.
