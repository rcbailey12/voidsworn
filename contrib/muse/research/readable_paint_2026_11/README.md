# Readable ship paint standard (issue 188)

Research for board issue #188, filed under R103 after the owner rejected the Pyre-Barge round 5 paint as a cluttered, unreadable mix. Four legs: a blind Rey leg written from the evidence pack only, DeepSeek and Qwen writer legs, and an Argon review leg. The chair synthesis merges them under FINDINGS R96, R97 and R103.

## Files

- `synthesis.md`: the chair synthesis. Section 1 explains why round 5 read as cluttered (value structure, zone size, noise frequency versus the camera, melted geometry). Section 2 is the standard itself, clauses S1 to S12, every hull is judged by it. Section 3 specifies the blurred greyscale battle crop test with pass and fail criteria and the round 5 V1 fail and round 4 V3 near-pass examples. Section 4 gives per-faction notes for all six factions. Section 5 covers shipped-game practice with sourcing tiers stated. Section 6 holds five owner questions with chair picks.
- `legs/rey_blind.md`: the blind Rey writer leg.
- `legs/deepseek.md`: the DeepSeek writer leg (raw copy; its Chorus palette error and invented Hegemony gold trim are flagged in its filing header and corrected in the synthesis).
- `legs/qwen.md`: the Qwen writer leg (raw copy; its section 5 citations failed screening, see the synthesis screening notes).
- `legs/argon_review.md`: the Argon review leg, with the chair's screening header.

## Headline

Round 5 passed its colour measurements and failed the eye, because readability lives in patch size, value steps and shapes, not colour totals. The standard's core numbers: at most three base tones plus a recess tone; no tone zone smaller than 2 units (30 px at 4K, 15 px at 1080p) at the battle camera; value steps of at least 12 luma levels between neighbours and 25 at major breaks; grain either invisible at battle distance (contrast at or under 8 levels) or structural (features at least 1 unit), with the visible middle band banned; trim accent at or under 2% of the battle crop; median luma judged against each faction's own reference. The blurred greyscale test is the gate, and round 6 (#187) is the first job that runs it.
