## Audio sourcing report

### 1. Headline

Approach: pack-led for broad SFX coverage (Kenney Impact/Sci-Fi/Interface, rubberduck CC0 packs, Little Robot CC BY 3.0), with per-file picks only for faction-specific or niche sounds (flesh, glass, deep explosions). Music is mostly KEEP: the incumbent set is already CC0/CC BY and fits R37 except fac_corsair, which is flagged for a stronger audition. No files were downloaded; all links were checked against live source pages where possible. Verified combined total: about 7.52 GB with the Sonniss GDC 2026 bundle, or about 49.6 MB if Sonniss is excluded. Kenney pack sizes are not stated on their pack pages, so they are marked UNVERIFIED and excluded from the total.

### 6. Risk register

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| No listening test – all picks are pack/file-level, not auditioned | High – a pack may not contain a suitable file for a cue | Audition each cue before locking it in |
| Kenney pack sizes are UNVERIFIED | Medium – total size may be larger than stated | Verify sizes after download; exclude Kenney from the verified total |
| Sonniss bundle is 7.47 GB | High – bundle is too large to be a sole SFX source | Use Sonniss only for boom_l/boom_xl, or exclude it and use Kenney/rubberduck |
