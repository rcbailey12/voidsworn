1. **Filename collision on `shot_01.ogg` and `shot_02.ogg`:** `gun_flak` and `torp_rocket` list `shot_01.ogg` at 0.459s and `shot_02.ogg` at 0.374s, whereas `gun_tracer` lists `shot_01.ogg` at 0.752s and `shot_02.ogg` at 0.325s. If these identically named takes come from different packs into a shared directory, one pair will overwrite the other or load the wrong audio during the listen pass unless the filenames are namespaced first.
2. **Unconverted `.wav` extensions in cue definitions:** Although the intake notes state all takes were converted to mono Ogg, `gun_las`, `lance`, and `engine` still reference `.wav` files (`Laser_01.wav` through `Laser_07.wav` and `SpaceShip_Engine_Medium_Loop_00.wav`). This mismatch could cause missing-file errors or silent layers during the listen pass if the loader expects `.ogg` extensions on disk.
3. **Identical sets and gains across opposite gameplay roles:** `lance_ion` and `shield` use the exact same `forceField_000/001/002.ogg` takes at the same 0.3 gain, while `lance_choir` shares `impactBell_heavy_002/003.ogg` with `ui_round` and `gun_needle` shares all three `impactGlass_light` takes with `patter_shield`. Playing the exact same clips for offensive weapon fire, defensive shield states, and UI round markers will mislead the owner's ear about which event just triggered unless differentiated by pitch or filtering.
4. **Impact recordings used as weapon fire layers:** Weapon cues `gun_rail`, `gun_needle`, `torp_star`, and `torp_heavy` rely on `impactMetal`, `impactGlass`, and `impactPlate` files, with `torp_heavy` sharing `impactPlate_heavy_000.ogg` and `002.ogg` directly with `hit_rail` at nearly identical gains (0.45 vs. 0.5). On the listen pass, firing these weapons may sound like the player's own hull is taking damage rather than dealing it, and any later mix change to the impact set will change both weapon and hit cues at once.
5. **Steady 5.0s loops used as one-shot falls on `death_run`:** Because `death_run` replaces its synth layer with three 5.0s `spaceEngineSmall` loops as one-shots with no wind-down recording and no listed trim or fade, the cue will play as a steady engine bed that cuts off abruptly at the end of its round-robin rather than falling away. Applying a pitch-drop and fade-out envelope in the playback engine would prevent this from sounding like a missing or truncated death cue during the owner's listen pass.
6. **Mixed families and loop lengths in `engine`:** Replacing the `engine` bed with a single round-robin set that mixes a 2.123s Little Robot loop (`SpaceShip_Engine_Medium_Loop_00.wav`) and two 5.0s Kenney loops (`spaceEngineLow_000.ogg` and `spaceEngine_000.ogg`) as its only layer risks jarring timbral, pitch, and seam shifts whenever the variant switches. The listen pass should specifically test loop transitions to see if `spaceEngineLow_000.ogg` causes an abrupt bass drop compared to the other two takes.
7. **Extreme duration variance within single round-robin sets:** `puff` combines a 2.638s take (`sfx100v2_air_01.ogg`) with a 0.285s take (`sfx100v2_air_03.ogg`), `ui_round` pairs 0.654s–0.697s heavy bells with a 0.123s `bong_001.ogg`, and `gun_las` ranges from 0.933s (`Laser_01.wav`) up to 2.0s (`Laser_05.wav`, which is also used in `lance`). Because `[replace]` cues have no backing synth layer, rolling a take that is five to nine times shorter than its siblings can mislead the reviewer into thinking a voice has cut out prematurely, while the 2.0s `gun_las` take blurs the length distinction against `lance`.
8. **Potential missing attack transient on trimmed `torp` loops:** Trimming the 5.0s `thrusterFire_000.ogg` and `001.ogg` loops down to 1.4s with a tail fade controls duration, but raw loop files typically lack an initial ignition transient and can click if cut mid-waveform. During the listen pass, verify that the start of the trim does not pop and that the untouched incumbent layer supplies enough launch punch.
9. **Layering asymmetry between `[add]` and `[replace]` cues:** Because `[add]` cues keep their shared incumbent sets and stack new layers at gains up to 0.5 (`gun_rail`, `hit`, `hit_plasma`, `hit_rail`), they risk loudness jumps, phase cancellation, or masking that hides whether an artifact is in the new take or the incumbent layer. Conversely, `[replace]` and `[variant_replace]` cues (`hit_glass`, `hit_wet`, `board_grapple:chorus`, `death_crack:ithar`) clear their prior layers entirely, so they may sound noticeably thinner or quieter beside dual-layer `[add]` cues unless auditioned both solo and in context.
10. **Heavy reuse of slime and debris fallbacks across adjacent events:** With Freesound picks replaced by pack fallbacks, `slime_01/03/04/09/10.ogg` are shared across `gun_glob`, `torp_sac`, `hit_wet`, and `board_grapple:chorus`, while `bfh1_breaking_01.ogg` is used in both `wreck` (gain 0.25) and `death_crack` (gain 0.4). When a wet projectile fires and immediately triggers `hit_wet`, or when `death_crack` transitions into `wreck`, back-to-back playback of the exact same raw file will cause audible comb-filtering or repetition unless randomized pitch offsets are active.
