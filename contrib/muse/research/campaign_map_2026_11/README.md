# Campaign map research, 2026-11

Job 6 of the Nov 1, 2026 four-job commission (contrib/opus/briefs/muse_2026-11-01b_four_jobs.md). Question: the owner wants the campaign map at a high-quality Sins of a Solar Empire 2 star-system standard (R51), and the G3 stream has since built the 3D system, stops, lanes, and threat diamonds. Research how Sins 2, Stellaris, Homeworld, and Endless Space 2 present their strategic maps, then design the next step for the three-sector run map, reusing the F4 battle world assets.

## Files

- synthesis.md: the chair synthesis. The merged design (shared sector sky bake, F4 Hollow Sun, battle-asset stops, four-state curved lanes, zoom-tiered information, a held scan view, rebuilt panels and text, in-world cores and Nemesis ring, inward sector spiral), mock layouts per sector, a 9-step build order with Low fallbacks, and three owner calls.
- legs/rey_leg.md, legs/deepseek_leg.md, legs/qwen_leg.md, legs/argon_leg.md: the four review legs, verbatim. Screening notes are in the synthesis, section 6: Qwen researched the wrong Homeworld and linked the wrong Sins 2 app id, and Argon states two specifics (a planet class list, threat diamond colours) the evidence pack never gave it.

## Headline decisions

This is a presentation pass on a built system, not a second rebuild. Map and battles share one sky bake per sector and the same Hollow Sun. Information comes in two tiers by zoom, with exact numbers near and bands far, plus a held scan view that flattens the scene to symbols on demand. Panels and text, the least rebuilt part of the owner's cheap list, get one family in the battle register, leader-line pills instead of floating labels, and a slim bottom ribbon instead of sidebars. The Nemesis clock and the cores move into the world itself, as a ring around the sun's shadow and points on its disc rim.
