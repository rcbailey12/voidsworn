# Job 10: backdrops, planets, stations, debris and scale

Verdict: GO. The R76 backdrop complaints all trace to code level causes with bounded fixes: backdrop rocks have no angular cap (a 630 long mountain at 900 away reads about twice the planet's width), the planet bake double encodes its gamma and washes pale areas into white blotches, the play area planet shows a 22.5 high dome over a 3 hex footprint, the station is an ungroomed AI mesh, and debris uses low detail meshes where blockers use full detail ones. The chair plan caps every backdrop rock at 0.05 rad so the planet is always at least 4.4 times wider than any single rock, fixes the bake in place, sinks the play area planet to an 11 high horizon, rebuilds the station as a Blender kitbash from the game's own ship parts, and upgrades debris to blocker quality rocks plus ship derived wreckage.

The picks: improve the procedural planet bake (not free licence textures); flat 0.05 rad angular cap with range spans up to 0.15 rad; play area planet sunk, footprint unchanged; station at the shipped 46 long sizing, one kit, variant ready; debris counts unchanged, detail and tumble fixed. Four owner questions sit at the end of the synthesis (planet route, cap strictness, station variants, planet detail step), chair pick marked on each.

How to read this folder: synthesis.md is the chair document (reference study, scale plan, planet diagnosis and pick, station and debris plan, build order sized as Codex tasks, Blender jobs, screening, owner questions, Not checked).
