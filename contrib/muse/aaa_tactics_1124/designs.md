# AAA design research: faction command sets and a deployment step

Issue 2060, run 1. Owner: muse. Chaired by Rey from five legs: blind Rey, DeepSeek (DeepThink on, Search off), Qwen (Qwen3.8-Max), Gemini on Hydrogen (the Flash seat), and Grok (seat empty, bot wall on the cloud browser, nothing submitted; record in legs/leg_grok.md). Leg files sit beside this doc in legs/. Research and recommendations only (R451): no pictures. Every number is a tuning starting point. Holds: shields are ONE pool and no shield facings appear anywhere (R450); the once-per-battle charge-and-hold mechanic stays (CMD_HOLD: full power from round 3, losing a ship charges a level); per-admiral riders (CMD_RIDERS) stay exactly as they are. Sources: godot/rules/data/tables.json (COMMANDS, CMD_POW, CMD_RIDERS, BOSS_CMD, ADMIRALS, FAC_RULES, FACTION_AI), PR 2038 (the balance pass that landed every faction within about 3 points of the mean at both difficulties), issue 2033 (called shots, in build).

## The structure the chair adopted, and the two discipline rules

Three of four seated legs (DeepSeek, Qwen, and Gemini's design half) proposed the same skeleton: keep four commands per faction, one per admiral, each anchored to the template it replaces, with the faction's battle rule supplying a gate or a bonus. The blind leg proposed one signature command per faction, and Gemini's own objection to the brief argued for that signature structure on build-cost grounds. The chair adopts the anchored four, for the reason Qwen and DeepSeek both gave: every command still maps to a known template budget, so the PR 2038 balance stays auditable command by command. A signature structure would be cheaper to build (six effects, not twenty-four) and the design doc records it as the fallback if the build seats balk at twenty-four; the per-faction sections below are written so each faction's four can collapse to its first command plus riders without redesign.

Two discipline rules from the blind leg apply to every set:

1. No command may be dead on its own faction's hulls. Today Eclipse's Khepra holds iron, whose shield refill does nothing on shieldless Eclipse hulls. Every set below was checked against its faction's hull facts (Chorus and Eclipse: no shields; Glasswrights: cannot Brace).
2. A faction bonus never pays out without the positioning or timing cost that earns it. Gemini's Sunward draft let a free volley count as having moved 2 hexes, collecting the Line of battle bonus without moving; the chair rejects that shape wherever it appears (Gemini itself flagged the double-dip as its biggest risk). Bonuses trigger on the real condition or not at all.

## Sunward Crusade (voidsworn). Theme: the line fires as one and does not break.

- Vex, Broadside Order (was barrage, Fire for effect). Every ship fires a free volley with its flank batteries at the best target in reach. A shooter that actually moved at least 2 hexes this round gets the Line of battle side-gun bonus on the volley, as it would on a normal shot. Tiers: T1 volley, T2 volley +10 percent damage, T3 volley +20 percent and battery shots in the volley cannot miss (Vex's rider, promoted to the tier 3 baseline for him; his rider then steps up, a rider-tuning note for the build seat). Reuses: barrage, the existing moved-2 check. New rule: none. All four legs converged on this command in some form.
- Malek, Lance Pounce (was hunt, Hunter's salvo). Torpedoes and lances reload at once; lance and battery damage +15/25/35 percent this round; ships that moved 2 or more hexes keep their line bonus on those shots. Reuses: hunt, Line of battle. New rule: none.
- Ashgrove, Escort Wings (was scramble, Scramble all wings). Scramble unchanged in effect: extra wing where there is room, squadrons strike again at +50 percent. The faction note is presentational and tactical, not mechanical: Sunward wings screen a line that braces, and the command text says so. Two legs proposed giving wings a damage-reduction aura over nearby friendlies (Gemini's Brace cover, Qwen's interceptor screen); both need a new squadron aura in the damage pipeline. The chair leaves the aura OUT of the set and lists it under flagged options below.
- Thale, Iron Litany (was iron, Iron wall). Iron as today (shields refill, 40 percent less hull damage), plus at T3 Brace covers two adjacent allies facing the same way instead of one (Qwen; a parameter change to the existing adjacency check, not a new rule).

Balance: every command maps to its template budget; the two bonuses (line volley, Brace extension) are gated on movement and formation the faction already pays for. Sunward was the lowest faction on Veteran before PR 2038 (31.7 percent) and now sits at 43.8, so this set deliberately starts neutral, not generous.

## Meridian Directorate (hegemony). Theme: the link makes every gun a battery.

- Kassar, Sync Volley (was barrage, Synchronized volley). Every ship fires a free volley; volley shots from synced ships (within 3 hexes of the command ship) carry the Synchronized fire aim and damage bonus, as a normal synced shot would. Tiers: the barrage tier curve, with the sync bonus applying from T2 and +10 percent crit on synced volley shots at T3 (Qwen; flagged for the balance re-check, since sync aim plus damage plus crit is the set's known spike risk). Reuses: barrage, the sync checks. New rule: none.
- Vorn, Ion Cascade (was hunt, Interdiction strike). Torpedoes reload, +30 percent damage as hunt; ion weapons this round strip 50 percent more shield (DeepSeek's double-strip, softened; if the strip amount is not already a parameter, that parameterization is the only build note). Reuses: hunt, ion strip. New rule: none beyond the parameter note.
- Reyes, Relay Strike (was scramble, Launch the strike group). Extra wing where there is room, squadrons strike again at +50 percent; a squadron strike this round gains the sync damage bonus if its parent ship is synced. Reuses: scramble, the sync check on the parent. New rule: none (the sync check already runs per ship; wings read their parent's state). Two flashier options were set aside: drone wings extending the sync aura (Gemini; new mobile aura anchor) and a called-shot accuracy debuff on synced ships (DeepSeek; touches issue 2033's surface for a small effect). Both are listed under flagged options.
- Maren, Sync Shield (was iron, Shield wall order). Iron as today; at T3, synced ships take an additional 10 percent less hull damage while the command ship lives (Qwen). If the command ship is gone, the command is plain iron, which is the faction's existing tension stated in the command text. Reuses: iron, sync range check, one alive check. New rule: none.

Balance: the whole set is worth its templates when the fleet stays in the net and degrades to plain templates when the net breaks, which is Meridian's identity as a risk. Meridian sat mid-table at both difficulties in PR 2038 (40.0 Veteran, 71.7 Standard), so a conditional set that pays only for formation is the right shape.

## Ashfall Hullbreakers (corsair). Theme: close the gap before they can breathe.

- Grax, Rocket Dash (was surge, Full burn). Surge as today (+2 movement, +1 turn, enemy fire loses 20 accuracy); boarding attempts this round get +25 percent crew strength (Qwen); at T3 each ship may also fire one free rocket salvo at the end of its move (DeepSeek). The salvo's timing (end of move, point defense thins it as normal) is a small new timing rule, flagged. Reuses: surge, boarding strength, rocket salvos.
- Sable, Rocket Storm (was hunt, Knife in the dark). Rocket pods reload on the torpedo reload system, +30 percent damage as hunt; at T2 rocket salvos this round cannot be thinned by point defense (Qwen). The chair drops Qwen's T3 (+1 rocket range) on Qwen's own risk note: PD immunity plus the damage tier is already two multipliers on the faction's main gun. T3 instead raises the damage tier to hunt's top value. Reuses: hunt, the point-defense step with a skip flag. New rule: none.
- Mott, Swarm Dive (was scramble, Release the swarm). Extra wing, squadrons strike again at +50 percent; fighter squadrons this round gain +1 hex of movement before their strike run (+2 at T3). Whether squadrons already have a pre-strike move step decides if this is a parameter or a small new rule; flagged as possibly-new, small (Qwen). Gemini's alternative (wings make boarding attacks) is a larger new rule and is not in the set; it is listed under flagged options.
- Vesh, Salvage Restore (was restore, Patch and weld). Restore as today (repair, patch sections); a ship that killed or boarded an enemy this round repairs an extra 5 percent (DeepSeek). The conditional trigger is a flag on an existing event, not a new system. Fits Vesh's scrap identity: the repair is worth more in the brawl he flies into. Reuses: restore, kill and boarding events. New rule: none beyond the trigger flag.

Balance: surge, hunt, scramble and restore budgets, each paid for in the faction's own currency, which is range. Ashfall cannot field lances and starts light on shields; every bonus above requires being close, adjacent, or arrived. Ashfall post-pass: 41.7 Veteran, 69.6 Standard, no headroom argument for more.

## The Chorus (chorus). Theme: every wound feeds the swarm; every kill feeds the ship.

No shields exist here, so no shield effect appears anywhere in this set (all four legs held this line).

- Matriarch, Brood Surge (was scramble, Spawning surge). Extra swarm where there is room, swarms strike again at +50 percent; swarm hits this round heal their parent brood 3/4/5 percent hull by tier. This is Matriarch's existing rider promoted to the faction baseline for this command (DeepSeek and Gemini both landed here). Reuses: scramble, Feed, the rider's own numbers. New rule: none.
- Razormaw, Predator Lunge (was surge, Blood frenzy). Surge as today; this round, a Chorus ship that destroys an enemy regrows 10 percent hull at once, and at T3 nearby allies regrow too (Qwen; the Feed rule's own kill geometry). Gemini's alternative paid for a bigger damage tier with self-damage on a kill-less round; the chair rejects the self-damage cost as a new mechanic the set does not need. Reuses: surge, Feed kill trigger. New rule: none.
- Mending, Flesh Knit (was restore, Regrowth tide). The Chorus repair is denominated in regrowth, not in a flat percent: every ship immediately gains two rounds of its normal Feed regrowth (three at T3) and patches damaged sections; destroyed sections patch at T3 only. The blind leg's argument, adopted: a flat 25 percent of max hull is worth more on Chorus's big bio hulls than on anyone else's ships, while regrowth ticks scale with the rate the faction already lives on, so the command is worth restore's budget without being worth more than restore. Gemini's added hull-damage reduction is left out (it stacks with everything else Chorus does to survive; DeepSeek names Chorus out-sustaining the band as the set's biggest risk). Reuses: Feed regrowth, restore's patch. New rule: none.
- Voice, Feeding Volley (was barrage, Spitting volley). Free volley as barrage; kills made in the volley trigger Feed for the killer and nearby allies exactly as a normal kill does; at T3 the killer regrows an extra 5 percent (DeepSeek). Qwen's lifesteal-on-damage version needs a heal-on-damage hook that may not exist; Feed on kill already exists, so the chair takes the kill trigger. Reuses: barrage, Feed. New rule: none.

Balance: Chorus was the strongest Veteran faction pre-pass (45.4) and sits at 45.0 now. This set adds no new healing source; it only lets the existing Feed fire on the command's kills and ticks. The sustain risk is real and named: if the re-check shows Chorus climbing, the first knob is Flesh Knit's tick count, not the faction rule.

## The Glasswrights (ithar). Theme: be somewhere else before the hit lands.

No command references Brace; the faction cannot use it (all four legs held this line).

- Aelith, Prism Scatter (was scramble, Starling flight). Extra wing, squadrons strike again at +50 percent; this round, ships may slip away after firing at no movement cost, and at T3 gain +1 movement after the slip (Qwen). Reuses: scramble, the slip-away mechanic with its cost waived for the round. New rule: none.
- Vaelen, Refracted Beam (was barrage, Burning glass). Free beam volley as barrage; a ship firing while its own shield is full deals +15/25/40 percent beam damage by tier (Gemini's condition, the blind leg's full-shield clause in the same shape). The bonus dies the moment the shield takes a hit, which is the faction's whole game in one line. The chair passes on Qwen's shield pierce (it steps on Meridian's ion identity, Qwen's own note) and on DeepSeek's split shielded/unshielded table (two tables where one condition does the work). Reuses: barrage, a shield-full check. New rule: none.
- Thorne, Glass Slip (was surge, Scatter the light). Surge as today; this round, Prism charge triggers even for ships that were hit since their last turn (Qwen; the untouched condition gets a one-round bypass flag). At T3, +1 extra movement. Reuses: surge, the Prism charge condition check. New rule: none beyond the bypass flag.
- Mirelle, Prism Overcharge (was iron, Prism ward). Iron's refill, with iron's hull-damage reduction kept; a ship whose shield was already full at round start takes an additional 10 percent less hull damage this round (Qwen's Crystal Aegis condition). Gemini's version (refill plus evasion) is close; the chair takes the full-shield condition over flat evasion because it rewards the prism play the faction is already making. Reuses: iron, a round-start shield state check. New rule: none if round-start shield state is tracked; flagged as a build check.

Balance: Glasswrights post-pass sit at 40.8 Veteran and 69.6 Standard. Their set pays for defense in the only currency they have (an unbroken shield) and for damage in the same currency, so an aggressive opponent taxes both halves at once. The kite risk (Glass Slip movement plus free Prism charge) is named by Qwen and is the first thing the re-check should watch against the slowest factions.

## Eclipse (deathless). Theme: the Archive remembers; death is a filing error.

- Khepra, Void Plate (was iron, Sunshade wall). The shield refill is deleted, not replaced with a no-op: all ships take 40 percent less hull damage this round (iron's surviving half) and armor counts as 20 percent higher for the round (Qwen). Gemini's crit and system-damage immunity is not adopted; immunity to called shots' forced system damage would fight issue 2033 head-on. DeepSeek's minimum (reduction only, identical to today's effective iron) is the fallback if the armor term misbehaves. Reuses: iron's reduction, a temporary armor modifier. New rule: none.
- Sekhmar, Null Salvo (was barrage, Noon volley). Free volley as barrage; null weapons in the volley ignore 20 percent of target armor at T2 and 30 percent at T3 (Qwen's caps; Gemini proposed up to 100 percent, which the chair rejects as deleting a defensive stat for a round). Combined with called shots, system damage from this volley caps at one system disable per target per round (Gemini's Q3 cap, adopted in the called-shots section). Reuses: barrage, the armor step in the damage pipeline. New rule: none.
- Anuket, Fold Hunt (was hunt, Out of the shadow). Hunt as today (torpedoes reload, +30 percent damage); the command text states what the faction already knows, that fold movement this round passes through ships, rocks and wrecks at no extra cost, so the damage window can be spent from angles other factions cannot take. Qwen's fold-through damage (damage for passing through an enemy hex) is a new rule the chair does not adopt; the fold is positioning, not a weapon. Reuses: hunt, fold drives as they stand. New rule: none.
- Nekhet, Archive Recall (was restore, Release the repair swarms). T1: restore as today. T2: in addition, one eligible fallen ship (never the flagship, the rule's own terms) is recalled by the Archive NOW, reassembling this round at the rule's own 35 percent hull instead of at next round's start. T3: a second eligible ship returns at 20 percent hull. This is the chaired set's one deliberate new rule, flagged in full: NEW RULE (timing). Archive restore normally resolves at the next round's start; this command resolves one restore immediately (and a weaker second one at T3). Every number in it (35 percent, flagship exclusion, eligibility) is the existing rule's. Three legs reached for this slot independently (the blind leg's early recall, Gemini's Archive Surge, Qwen's death-save variant), which is why the chair spends the set's new-rule budget here and nowhere else. Gemini and the blind leg both name the same least-sure point, the T3 second ship, and the chair agrees: if anything in this doc gets cut in review, cut the T3 second ship first. Conservative fallback, if the Board rejects the timing rule: Nekhet keeps restore exactly as today (DeepSeek's set), and Eclipse's set still stands, because Void Plate alone fixes the iron misfit.

Balance: Eclipse was the weakest Veteran faction pre-pass (29.2) and the strongest on Standard post-pass (74.6), the widest split on the table, so its command set is the one to re-check at both difficulties before anything else in this doc. The recall's value is tempo, not hull: the ship returns at the same 35 percent it would have had next round, one round early, once per battle.

## Flagged options the chair did not adopt (kept for the Board's record)

- Wing screen aura (Sunward): squadrons reduce damage to nearby friendlies (Gemini, Qwen). New squadron aura in the damage pipeline. Thematic, small, and the first candidate if a second new rule is ever budgeted.
- Designated mark (Meridian): one marked enemy, synced ships volley at it and share one called section (blind leg, Gemini's Networked Volley in part). Needs a designated-target state in the target bar. Strongest identity option in the whole council; the chair held it back only because Sync Volley gets most of the feel with zero new state.
- Drone sync relays (Meridian, Gemini): wings extend the sync aura. New mobile aura anchor.
- Wing boarding attacks (Ashfall, Gemini): squadrons board instead of striking. New squadron action type.
- Chorus persistent section resistance (Qwen): repaired sections gain lasting damage resistance. Moderate new rule, and it stacks with Feed in exactly the direction DeepSeek warned about.
- Eclipse death-save (Qwen): a ship at 0 hull survives at 1 and enters the Archive pipeline. Moderate new rule; the chair preferred the recall, which uses the Archive's front door instead of intercepting destruction.

## Deployment step: the pick

All four seated legs picked B, place-in-zone. The chair adopts it, in this exact shape:

- Before round 1, the player places each of their ships anywhere inside a deployment zone on their side of the board, 2 to 3 hexes deep (Qwen and Gemini both land there; zone depth is a tuning value with its own pass).
- The enemy fleet is already placed and visible (Gemini's asymmetric form). The player is counter-deploying, and the design should admit that instead of pretending otherwise.
- Auto-place is the default button, using a sane faction-aware layout, and the game remembers the player's last layout per admiral and offers it as one click (blind leg and Qwen converge on the saved layout; without it, the chair would pick C, because an unremembered placement step in a 10 to 20 battle run becomes a tax).
- Enemy placement uses per-faction templates keyed to the range preference the AI already carries in FACTION_AI (Meridian +3, Glasswrights +2, Chorus -1, Ashfall -2, Eclipse -3), with three or four hand-checked templates per faction rather than one heuristic (Qwen's exploitation warning).
- Placement serializes into the battle record. The checks suite replays saved battles, so a placement that is not in the record cannot be replayed; this serialization is the real build cost, ahead of the placement UI itself (blind leg). Estimated build cost across the council's estimates: the placement phase and UI in one build run, enemy templates in the same or a second, fixture and replay work in a third. Formation pick (C) was estimated cheaper by Gemini and dearer by DeepSeek; the disagreement is recorded, and C remains the fallback if the fixture work grows.

Scoring summary (chair's consolidation of four legs): player value: A low, B high, C medium. AI handling: A none, B templates keyed to existing FACTION_AI preferences, C simplest of the active options. Teaching load: A none, B one screen with a default that plays itself, C lowest active load but the formations become a solved menu. Build cost: A zero, B the highest of the three once replay serialization is counted, C in between.

Strongest argument against B, stated by every leg in some form and adopted by the chair as the design constraint: it adds 15 to 30 seconds before every battle in a roguelite, and a player who always takes the saved layout is clicking through a ceremony. That is why auto-place plus memory is not a nice-to-have in this pick; it is the pick. Second argument, DeepSeek's: a counter-deploying player gains a round 1 alpha the current balance never priced. The chair's answer is sequencing, below.

Sequencing (adopting DeepSeek's objection as the plan): ship the command sets and the called-shot rulings first, re-check the PR 2038 window, then land deployment in its own balance window with its own re-check. Three systems that all move win rates should never land in one pass, because the re-check could not say which one moved the number.

## Called shots (issue 2033): the rulings these sets need

1. Free volleys honor called shots that were already set. A command volley (Broadside Order, Sync Volley, Feeding Volley, Refracted Beam, Null Salvo) routes a ship's shots to the section that ship had already called on that target, with the +15 crit and forced system damage, and pays no second trade-off (the trade-off was paid when the priority was set). A ship with no called section on its target fires the volley on default routing with no crit bonus. This merges Qwen's honor rule with DeepSeek's condition: commands never create a called shot, they only respect one that exists. The blind leg's Meridian fleet-called section applies only if the Board adopts the designated mark option above; under Sync Volley as chaired, per-ship settings stand.
2. Torpedoes and rockets do not take called shots. They get command damage bonuses (Lance Pounce's window, Rocket Storm's PD immunity) but no section routing, no +15 crit, no forced system damage. Qwen and the blind leg agree; DeepSeek's hunt-torpedo honoring is the recorded dissent. The line is weapon class: guns and beams aim at sections, torpedoes and rockets are area weapons thinned by point defense, and a torpedo that picks an engine block blurs the one distinction the weapon table works to keep.
3. Squadron strikes never take called shots (all four legs). Wings hit the hull pool.
4. One system disable per target per round from a single command volley (Gemini). Null Salvo plus a called section is the case this exists for: armor pierce and forced system damage may combine, but one volley may not strip two systems off one ship.
5. Restore and Flesh Knit patch a section destroyed by a called shot, but do not clear that round's forced system damage (DeepSeek). Patching is structural; the system stays offline until the normal repair rules clear it.

## Picks for Ryan

1. The Eclipse timing rule (Archive Recall): the chaired set spends its one new-rule budget on resolving an Archive restore immediately, once, at the rule's own numbers. Approve it, approve it without the T3 second ship, or take the conservative fallback (Nekhet keeps restore; the set still stands).
2. Deployment sequencing: commands and called shots first with a balance re-check, deployment after in its own window (the chair's recommendation, from DeepSeek's objection). The alternative is all three in one pass with a bigger re-check.
3. Torpedoes and called shots: the chair rules no (ruling 2 above), with DeepSeek dissenting. If you want torpedoes to honor a set called section, that is a one-line change to the ruling and a real buff to hunt-slot admirals, Malek and Sable first among them.

## Council record

- Blind Rey: one signature command per faction with riders as the variation; Chorus repair in regrowth ticks (adopted); Eclipse early Archive recall (adopted as the set's one new rule); deployment B with auto-place and remembered layout as load-bearing conditions; objection: signature structure thins admiral choice within a faction if riders read as fine print.
- DeepSeek: four commands per faction, template budgets gated by the faction rule; deployment B, with the counter-deployment warning the chair answered with sequencing; called shots honored only if set before the command fired (adopted into ruling 1); objection: the brief bundles three balance-moving systems, so ship commands and called shots first and defer deployment (adopted as the sequencing plan).
- Qwen: four commands anchored to template slots with conditional bonuses; the fullest new-rule accounting (seven flags, three moderate); deployment B with a saved layout; objection: commands are once-per-battle highlight moments while battle rules are continuous, so a command can reward the rule but cannot carry faction identity by itself; multi-round command effects would fix that and break the budget. Recorded as the honest limit of this whole design: these sets make the command button speak the faction's language for one round; the identity still lives in the battle rule.
- Gemini on Hydrogen: twenty-four bespoke commands, several of which the chair mined (full-shield beam bonus, swarm heal baseline, the system-damage cap); deployment B asymmetric with the enemy visible; its own objection argued for the signature structure its design half voted against, and the chair notes the split because it is really an argument about build budget, which the build seats should settle with the fallback in hand.
- Grok: seat empty, bot wall, nothing submitted (legs/leg_grok.md).
