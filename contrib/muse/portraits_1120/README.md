# Admiral portraits, run 1 (issue 1253)

Delivery for issue 1253: paintings for the 11 admirals who had crests only
and repaints for the 3 outdated Ithar admirals, 14 in total. Candidates,
the chaired pick, and a 720 px labelled contact sheet per faction
(before, candidate A, candidate B). No game files change: the Board swaps
the picks into godot/portraits/index.json after Ryan picks, keeping each
old picture as an alt.

## Method

Anchor paintings served as palette, material and finish references only;
composition is fresh per admiral (R68: own setting, pose and lighting
within the faction palette). Two divergent candidates per admiral, chair
picks one, the other ships in the same folder as the alt candidate.
Square, oil-painterly R68 finish, subject large in frame. Chorus and
Deathless are single-subject organism and housing portraits, not busts.
Ithar follow the Loom body law (R252): four support limbs, four arms,
crystal sail vanes on the back only, six vanes. Mirror-pair pose rule
across the two human factions: no pose repeats across Malek/Vorn,
Ashgrove/Reyes, Thale/Maren.
