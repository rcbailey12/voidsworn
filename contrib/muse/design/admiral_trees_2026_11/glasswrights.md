# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: The Glasswrights

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Step 2 of the progression plan (issue 19) builds its machinery from this file, seeded by Saro of Long Focus worked tree. This file is complete and final for Phase 1.

The Glasswrights

#### Tamsa of the Clear Aperture - Command: Starling flight

- Branch A name: Recorded Flight
- T1: Flight Paths in the Glass - an extra wing that strikes the target its carrier last fired at gains +15% damage || Cast Twice in Light - one carrier extra wing strikes, is recorded, and strikes the same target again at half damage if that target has not moved
- T2: The Replay Corrects - an extra wing's strike again against a target already hit this round gains +10% damage || Starlings in Formation - extra wings that strike the same target as another extra wing gain +10 aim
- T3: Flaw in the Flight Removed - extra wings ignore the flak of one chosen enemy ship for this Command || Every Lens Aloft - a carrier that held still this round launches one additional extra wing, and that wing strikes this round at the full strike again bonus
- Capstone: Flight Cast Perfectly - during Starling flight, wings striking the same target ignore its flak, its shields stop only the first wing hit, and wing hits after the first deal +25% damage to its most damaged section || The Forge Casts Ahead - after the Command, one extra wing per carrier strikes the same target again at the start of next round, at +15% damage
- Changes: T3 second slot: the draft recorded strike into next round restated the second capstone at lower power; the replacement launches the additional wing this round instead. Capstone first slot: the draft name used a memory word owned by Eclipse, and its three strike chassis duplicated the second capstone extra strike; the replacement combines same target wing attacks into one point defence ignoring strike (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair).

#### Saro of Long Focus - Command: Burning glass

- Branch A name: Long Focus
- T1: Focus Drawn Long - volley shots at targets beyond half the weapon's range gain +20% damage || One Lens, One Target - choose one of your ships: it fires its whole volley at a single target you name, and its shots gain +25% damage against that target
- T2: Light Arrives Together - during the volley, a shot at a target already hit by one of your other ships this round gains +15% damage || The Recorded Angle - before the volley, each ship may turn once to bring its best lance to bear
- T3: Burning the Flaw Out - volley hits on one target stack; the third and later hits on that target ignore its shields || Long Light - lance weapons in the volley gain +2 hexes of range for any ship that has not turned this round
- Capstone: Sun Through One Lens - Burning glass may concentrate every ship's volley on one target; its shields stop only the first hit, and no shot suffers range falloff || The Flaw, Corrected Forever - after Burning glass, your lances keep +15% damage against the target the volley hit most, for the rest of the battle
- Changes: T3 second slot: a no turn condition was added so the range gain is a piloting decision, not an unconditional bonus (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair).

#### Vell of the Third Mirror - Command: Scatter the light

- Branch A name: Refraction
- T1: Two Images Appear - during the scatter, the first enemy shot at each of your ships each round is aimed at a false image and loses a further 20 accuracy || Angles Recorded - a ship that ends its scatter movement facing a different direction than it started gains +10 evasion until next round
- T2: The Mirror Steps Aside - during the scatter, a ship may dive under one enemy-held hex at no extra cost || Light Split Three Ways - during the scatter, a ship may move its full remaining movement after it fires
- T3: Refraction Deepens - during the scatter, a ship that spends all its movement cannot be targeted by weapons fired from beyond range 6 until next round || Every Mirror Turns - at the end of scatter movement, every ship may face any direction at no cost
- Capstone: The Fleet Is Elsewhere - the first time an enemy fires at your fleet this battle, every ship gains +2 movement and may turn once for free || A Hundred False Hulls - in the scatter round, the first two enemy attacks are wasted on false images and automatically miss their targets
- Changes: Capstone second slot: restated wasted volleys as wasted attacks in the attack and miss terms the battle code uses, scoped to the scatter round.

#### Brin of the Fourth Lens - Command: Prism ward

- Branch A name: Prism Recasting
- T1: Light Bent to the Weak Side - during the ward, the friendly ship with the lowest shields takes 20% less hull damage until the start of next round || Prisms Face the Fire - a ship facing its nearest enemy gains shields above its maximum equal to 25% of its maximum shields during the ward
- T2: Recharge While Lit - during the ward, shields recharge a second time at round end, at the prism recharge rate || The Lens Rotates - during the ward, a ship that changes its facing gains +10 evasion until its next turn
- T3: Flaw in the Glass Corrected - while the ward is active, the first ship that has a shield facing emptied refills that facing to 15%, once per battle || Hard Light - during the ward, a ship with shield left on the struck facing takes no section damage from its first hit
- Capstone: Ward of Clear Glass - during Prism ward, an enemy ship that fires at a warded ship loses 20 accuracy on its next shot, once per enemy ship || Every Facet Lit - during the ward, each warded ship gains temporary hull equal to 20% of its maximum shields
- Changes: Capstone first slot: the draft lasted two rounds with a second refill, the same chassis as the Thale and Maren capstones; the replacement is a reflected accuracy penalty during the ward, with no duration extension.

- Branch B T1 (shared): Deployment Read from the Shards - after seeing the enemy deployment, reposition one of your ships anywhere in your deployment zone || Casting Corrected Before Battle - after deployment, swap the positions of any two of your ships
- Branch C T1 (shared): The Flagship Lens - once per round, the flagship may focus one enemy; your lance shots against it gain +10 aim || First Light Recorded - the flagship records the first enemy ship that fires each battle; your fleet gains +10 evasion against that ship for the battle
- Changes: none.
