# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: The Glasswrights

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Step 2 of the progression plan (issue 19) builds its machinery from this file, seeded by Saro of Long Focus worked tree. This file is complete and final for Phase 1.

The Glasswrights

#### Tamsa of the Clear Aperture - Command: Starling flight

- Branch A name: Recorded Flight
- T1: Flight Paths in the Glass - record each extra wing strike path before launching; a wing that follows its recorded path exactly strikes at +15% damage || Cast Twice in Light - one carrier extra wing strikes, is recorded, and strikes the same target again at half damage if that target has not moved
- T2: The Replay Corrects - after the first strike resolves, each extra wing may be retargeted freely for its strike again, using what the glass recorded || Starlings in Formation - extra wings that strike in the same order as their carriers are arranged gain +10 aim
- T3: Flaw in the Flight Removed - extra wings ignore the point defence of one chosen enemy ship for this Command || Every Lens Aloft - a carrier that held still this round launches one additional extra wing, and that wing strikes this round at the full strike again bonus
- Capstone: Flight Cast Perfectly - during Starling flight, all wings striking the same target combine their attacks into a single simultaneous strike that ignores point defence and deals +25% damage to the target's most damaged section || The Forge Casts Ahead - after the Command, one extra wing per carrier stays aloft and repeats its recorded strike at the start of next round
- Changes: T3 second slot: the draft recorded strike into next round restated the second capstone at lower power; the replacement launches the additional wing this round instead. Capstone first slot: the draft name used a memory word owned by Eclipse, and its three strike chassis duplicated the second capstone extra strike; the replacement combines same target wing attacks into one point defence ignoring strike (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair).

#### Saro of Long Focus - Command: Burning glass

- Branch A name: Long Focus
- T1: Focus Drawn Long - volley shots at targets beyond half the firing ship's range gain +20% damage || One Lens, One Target - a ship may fire its volley as a single shot at +25% damage instead of firing every gun
- T2: Light Arrives Together - you may hold the volley until all your ships have moved, and fire lance shots in any order you choose || The Recorded Angle - before the volley, each ship may turn once to bring its best lance to bear
- T3: Burning the Flaw Out - volley hits on one target stack; the third and later hits on that target ignore its shields || Long Light - lance weapons in the volley gain +2 hexes of range for any ship that has not turned this round
- Capstone: Sun Through One Lens - Burning glass may concentrate every ship's volley on one target, resolving as a single combined strike that ignores range penalties || The Flaw, Corrected Forever - after Burning glass, your lances keep +15% damage against the target the volley hit most, for the rest of the battle
- Changes: T3 second slot: a no turn condition was added so the range gain is a piloting decision, not an unconditional bonus (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair).

#### Vell of the Third Mirror - Command: Scatter the light

- Branch A name: Refraction
- T1: Two Images Appear - during the scatter, the first enemy shot at each of your ships each round is aimed at a false image and loses a further 20 accuracy || Angles Recorded - a ship that ends its scatter movement facing a different direction than it started gains +10 evasion until next round
- T2: The Mirror Steps Aside - during the scatter, a ship may pass through one enemy-held hex without stopping || Light Split Three Ways - during the scatter, a ship may split its movement, half before it fires and half after
- T3: Refraction Deepens - during the scatter, a ship that moves its full distance cannot be targeted by weapons fired from beyond range 6 until next round || Every Mirror Turns - at the end of scatter movement, every ship may face any direction at no cost
- Capstone: The Fleet Is Elsewhere - Scatter the light may be declared after the first enemy ship fires; your ships move immediately, interrupting the enemy turn || A Hundred False Hulls - in the scatter round, the first two enemy attacks are wasted on false images and automatically miss their targets
- Changes: Capstone second slot: restated wasted volleys as wasted attacks in the attack and miss terms the battle code uses, scoped to the scatter round.

#### Brin of the Fourth Lens - Command: Prism ward

- Branch A name: Prism Recasting
- T1: Light Bent to the Weak Side - you may give one ship's shield refill to the friendly ship with the lowest shields instead || Prisms Face the Fire - a ship facing its nearest enemy refills +25% more shield during the ward
- T2: Recharge While Lit - during the ward, shields recharge a second time at round end, at the prism recharge rate || The Lens Rotates - during the ward, a ship may change its facing after moving without losing the damage reduction
- T3: Flaw in the Glass Corrected - while the ward is active, the first ship whose shields fall to zero refills to 15%, once per battle || Hard Light - during the ward, a ship remaining shields also block the first section damage it would take
- Capstone: Ward of Clear Glass - during Prism ward, an enemy ship that fires at a warded ship loses 20 accuracy on its next shot, once per enemy ship || Every Facet Lit - during the ward, shield strength refilled beyond a ship's maximum becomes temporary hull of hard light, up to +20%
- Changes: Capstone first slot: the draft lasted two rounds with a second refill, the same chassis as the Thale and Maren capstones; the replacement is a reflected accuracy penalty during the ward, with no duration extension.

- Branch B T1 (shared): Deployment Read from the Shards - after seeing the enemy deployment, reposition one of your ships anywhere in your deployment zone || Casting Corrected Before Battle - after deployment, swap the positions of any two of your ships
- Branch C T1 (shared): The Flagship Lens - once per round, the flagship may focus one enemy; your lance shots against it gain +10 aim || First Light Recorded - the flagship records the first enemy ship that fires each battle; your fleet gains +10 evasion against that ship for the battle
- Changes: none.
