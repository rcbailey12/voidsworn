# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: Sunward Crusade

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Sunward Crusade

#### Admiral Roderic Vex - Command: Fire for effect

- Branch A name: The Inherited Line
- T1: Ledger of Arcs - a ship firing its volley from a broadside arc gains +15% damage || Held in the Account - you may hold one ship volley when the Command is declared and fire it after that ship moves this round
- T2: Named in the Ledger - before the volley, name one enemy; every ship may aim its volley at the named enemy even if another target is closer || Inherited Aim - a ship that has not moved this round fires its volley at +15% damage
- T3: Every Gun Sworn - each ship chooses its own volley target freely, ignoring the best target rule, and ships that choose the same target gain +10 aim against it || Debt Comes Due - you may delay the whole volley to the start of next round; when it fires, every ship gains +20% damage
- Capstone: The Grand Ledger - once per battle, when Fire for effect is used, choose one enemy; every ship that can reach it fires at it, and its shields stop only the first hit || The Last Account - after Fire for effect resolves, each ship that destroyed its target with the free volley fires one weapon again at a new target
- Changes: none.

#### Oath-Captain Malek Draven - Command: Hunter's salvo

- Branch A name: The Hunter Ledger
- T1: Prey Entered by Name - when the salvo begins, mark one enemy; torpedoes fired at the mark this round gain +15% damage || Salvo in Two Hands - you may hold the damage bonus for up to half your ships and spend it next round instead of this one
- T2: Torpedoes Run First - torpedoes fired during the salvo round strike at once instead of travelling || Rear Arc Reckoning - during the salvo round, rear arc hits from any weapon gain +15% damage
- T3: The Debt Hunts - during the salvo round, a ship that starts its activation closer to its target than any other friendly ship treats side arc hits on that target as rear arc hits || Two Salvos Sworn - the salvo damage bonus also covers the first torpedo each ship fires next round
- Capstone: Salvo Without End - during the salvo round, each ship may fire one torpedo a second time at half damage || The Named Prey Falls - when the salvo is declared, choose one enemy; every torpedo in the fleet may retarget to it, and it takes +25% damage from all hits this round
- Changes: Branch name: the apostrophe in the draft branch name was removed for the naming lint; mechanics unchanged. T3 first slot: the draft reloaded one torpedo tube on a rear arc hit, restating the first capstone reload at single tube scale; the replacement converts side arc hits to rear arc hits for the closest ship (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair).

#### Commodore Sera Ashgrove - Command: Scramble all wings

- Branch A name: Wings on the Ledger
- T1: Escort Sworn to a Name - an extra wing may be assigned as escort to one named friendly ship instead of striking, intercepting fire aimed at it this round || First Wave Owes the Second - choose one carrier; its extra wing strikes at +20% damage
- T2: The Account Splits - an extra wing that strikes a different target than its carrier's other wings gains +15% damage || Held in Reserve - you may keep one extra wing unlaunched; it launches free at the start of next round and strikes then
- T3: Every Generation Aloft - a carrier that has not moved this round launches one additional extra wing || Wings Settle the Debt - an extra wing striking the same target as its carrier's guns gains +30% damage, once per carrier
- Capstone: The Roll of Wings - during Scramble all wings, every squadron strike ignites the struck ship and reduces the struck ship's flak by 1 for the rest of the battle || Oath of Unbroken Wings - during Scramble all wings, extra wings may form an escort wall across the fleet, granting every friendly ship within 2 hexes of a carrier a screen refill equal to 25% of its maximum shields, and intercepting the first torpedo fired at each escorted ship this round
- Changes: Both capstones: the draft names used a sky word outside the faction idiom, the first duplicated the Tamsa extra strike chassis across the Wings family, and the second overlapped the T2 reserve slot; the replacements are an ignition and point defence suppression capstone and a carrier escort screen capstone (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair). The ignition and point defence effects are stated in the pack vocabulary; Opus to confirm the exact keys at build.

#### High Admiral Oryn Thale - Command: Iron wall

- Branch A name: The Iron Account
- T1: Hold and Be Counted - a ship that does not move during Iron wall keeps the damage reduction for one extra round || Screens Where the Debt Falls - you may shift the wall's protection: one chosen ship takes 20 points more hull damage reduction, and one other ship takes 20 points less
- T2: Wall Declared Early - if Iron wall is declared before any of your ships has moved or fired this round, its damage reduction is 10 points stronger this round || Oath of Stillness - during Iron wall, a ship that holds its position refills its shields again at half strength at round end
- T3: Unpaid and Unbroken - during Iron wall, ships below half hull take 20 points more hull damage reduction than the wall normally gives || The Wall Answers - while Iron wall is active, a ship whose shield facing is emptied by a hit fires its smallest weapon at the attacker once that shot has resolved, once per ship per battle
- Capstone: Iron Testament - Iron wall lasts two rounds, and shields refill again at half strength at the start of the second round || The Wall That Moves - while Iron wall is active, your ships gain +1 movement
- Changes: T1 second slot: renamed from shields to screens to match Crusade vocabulary, mechanic unchanged (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair). Capstone second slot: the draft name used a sea word barred by the lint; renamed, mechanic unchanged.

- Branch B T1 (shared): Sworn Deployment - after seeing the enemy deployment, place one of your ships anywhere in your deployment zone || Debts in Reserve - hold one ship off the field; it enters at full strength at the start of round 2 at any edge of your deployment zone
- Branch C T1 (shared): Flagship Keeps the Ledger - once per battle, the flagship may order one other ship that has already fired to fire one weapon again at half damage || Standard of the First Crusade - once per round, one ship within 2 hexes of the flagship may reroll a missed shot
- Changes: none.
