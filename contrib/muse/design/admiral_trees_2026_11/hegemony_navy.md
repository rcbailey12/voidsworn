# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: Hegemony Navy

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Hegemony Navy

#### First Admiral Ilsa Kassar - Command: Synchronized broadside

- Branch A name: Synchrony
- T1: Wide Net - synchronized fire radius +1 hex || Second Volley - the first synced target each round may be fired on by one additional synced ship out of arc
- T2: Shared Solution - each synced ship fires with the best aim modifier any synced ship has || Cold Drill - a synced ship that has not moved this round fires at +10 aim
- T3: Rolling Fire - once per round, when a synced ship kills an enemy, another synced ship that has not yet fired this round may fire one weapon at once at a new target || Iron Tempo - the first time the sync anchor is destroyed or captured, the largest remaining Hegemony ship becomes the new anchor for the rest of the battle
- Capstone: Living Relay - the flagship projects sync from its position at double radius || One Voice - once per battle, every synced ship fires at one named target, and its shields stop only the first hit
- Changes: none.

#### Commodore Dace Vorn - Command: Interdiction strike

- Branch A name: Filed Interdiction
- T1: Target Filed in Triplicate - when the strike begins, file one enemy as the interdicted target; your hits on it gain +10 crit chance || Strike by the Book - during the strike round, a ship gains +15% damage if its speed is at least the speed of the last ship that fired this round
- T2: Cross-Referenced Fire - during the strike round, the second and later hits on the same target gain +15% damage || Filed Before Movement - during Interdiction strike, a ship that fires before it has moved this round gains +10 crit chance
- T3: Total Interdiction - during Interdiction strike, any target hit by three or more of your ships takes +30% damage from every hit that round || Standard Seven Breach - during the strike round, a critical hit also shuts down the struck section's weapons for one round
- Capstone: Interdiction, Permanent File - any target hit during Interdiction strike takes the strike damage bonus again from all your fire next round || One Order, Every Gun - during Interdiction strike, every ship may fire one weapon a second time at half damage
- Changes: none.

#### Fleet Marshal Corin Reyes - Command: Launch the strike group

- Branch A name: Strike Group Procedure
- T1: Cruisers Filed as Carriers - during the launch, choose one ship without a hangar; it launches one wing this Command || Launch Order Published - publish the strike order before launching; the first wing to strike gains +20% damage
- T2: Second Wave on Schedule - you may hold all extra wings and launch them at the start of next round instead; they strike then at normal damage, and the Command's strike bonus does not carry over || Procedure Rehearsed - every extra wing's strike again this Command gains +10 aim
- T3: Full Group Assembled - if every launch ship launches an extra wing, each launch ship launches one more, but all extra wings must strike the single filed target || Registry of Escorts - extra wings may instead form a screen over your fleet, cutting enemy wing damage by 25% this round
- Capstone: One Target, Every Wing Filed - designate one enemy before the launch; all extra wings must strike it, and it takes +35% damage from all wing hits this round || Standing Strike Group - after the Command, one extra wing per launch ship stays on patrol; the first time an enemy ship moves within its launch ship's range each round, it strikes that enemy at once
- Changes: Capstone first slot: the draft returned all wings for a second strike at half damage, a second use chassis the Wings family already carries elsewhere; the replacement is a concentration decision, all extra wings on one filed target at +35% damage (Qwen leg fix, adopted by the chair).


#### Line Admiral Oskar Maren - Command: Shield wall order

- Branch A name: Column Standard
- T1: Column Filed Straight - during the wall, ships that end their movement in a straight line of three or more friendly ships gain shields above their maximum equal to 25% of their maximum shields || Overflow by Regulation - during the wall, a ship that is at full shields gains +10 evasion until its next turn
- T2: Wall at the Bell - while the wall is active, the first hit against your fleet each round deals no hull damage || Standard Rotation - during the wall, once per ship, a ship may swap hexes with an adjacent friendly ship
- T3: The Column Endures - while the wall is active, the first ship each round to have a shield facing emptied immediately refills that facing to 25% || Filed in Armor - during the wall, a ship that has not fired yet when it is hit takes 15 points less hull damage
- Capstone: The Standard Covers All - while Shield wall order is active, every ship takes a further 20% less hull damage || The Flagship Holds the Standard - while the wall is active, the flagship counts as part of any friendly line, and ships in a line with the flagship gain shields above their maximum equal to 10% of their maximum shields
- Changes: Capstone first slot: the draft two round duration and refill chassis repeated Thale and Brin; the replacement extends refill coverage to ships adjacent to the column. Capstone second slot: the draft transferred half the flagship hull damage to another ship, a cross ship damage allocation the pack vocabulary does not contain, and its name used a sea word; the replacement makes the flagship join every friendly line for column effects.

- Branch B T1 (shared): Formation Filed Before Battle - ships that end their movement next to two friendly ships gain +10 aim that round || Drill Revision One - once per battle, after the enemy has moved, reposition one of your ships up to 2 hexes
- Branch C T1 (shared): Flagship Registry Beacon - once per round, the flagship may stamp one enemy; your ships gain +10 aim against the stamped enemy || Standard Borne Forward - ships that move toward the flagship this round gain +1 movement
- Changes: none.
