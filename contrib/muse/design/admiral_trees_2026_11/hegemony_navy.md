# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: Hegemony Navy

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Hegemony Navy

#### First Admiral Ilsa Kassar - Command: Synchronized broadside

- Branch A name: Synchrony
- T1: Wide Net - synchronized fire radius +1 hex || Second Volley - the first synced target each round may be fired on by one additional synced ship out of arc
- T2: Shared Solution - synced ships share their best aim modifier || Cold Drill - sync forms at round start even if the flagship has not moved
- T3: Rolling Fire - a kill by a synced ship lets another synced ship fire immediately, once per round || Iron Tempo - sync survives the flagship's first crippling hit each battle
- Capstone: Living Relay - the flagship projects sync from its position at double radius, and sync no longer requires line of sight to the flagship || One Voice - once per battle, every synced ship fires at one target simultaneously, resolving as a single combined strike
- Changes: none.

#### Commodore Dace Vorn - Command: Interdiction strike

- Branch A name: Filed Interdiction
- T1: Target Filed in Triplicate - when the strike begins, file one enemy as the interdicted target; your hits on it gain +10 crit chance || Strike by the Book - during the strike round, ships that fire in ascending speed order gain +15% damage
- T2: Cross-Referenced Fire - during the strike round, the second and later hits on the same target gain +15% damage || Filed Before Movement - the strike may be declared before movement, and ships that move into range during the round keep the full bonus
- T3: Total Interdiction - during the strike round, your ships may split their fire freely, and any target hit by three or more ships takes +30% damage from every hit that round || Standard Seven Breach - during the strike round, a critical hit also shuts down the struck section's weapons for one round
- Capstone: Interdiction, Permanent File - any target hit during Interdiction strike takes the strike damage bonus again from all your fire next round || One Order, Every Gun - Interdiction strike may be declared at the start of a round, and your ships fire before the fastest enemy ship that round
- Changes: none.

#### Fleet Marshal Corin Reyes - Command: Launch the strike group

- Branch A name: Strike Group Procedure
- T1: Cruisers Filed as Carriers - during the launch, choose one cruiser; it launches a wing as if it were a carrier, in addition to the carriers extra wings || Launch Order Published - publish the strike order before launching; the first wing to strike gains +20% damage
- T2: Second Wave on Schedule - you may hold all extra wings and launch them at the start of next round instead, striking then at full effect || Procedure Rehearsed - after the first strike resolves, each extra wing may be reassigned to a new target for its strike again
- T3: Full Group Assembled - if every carrier launches an extra wing, each carrier launches one more, but all extra wings must strike the single filed target || Registry of Escorts - extra wings may instead form a screen over your fleet, cutting enemy wing damage by 25% this round
- Capstone: One Target, Every Wing Filed - designate one enemy before the launch; all extra wings must strike it, and it takes +35% damage from all wing hits this round || Standing Strike Group - after the Command, one extra wing per carrier stays on patrol and strikes the first enemy that enters its carrier range each round
- Changes: Capstone first slot: the draft returned all wings for a second strike at half damage, a second use chassis the Wings family already carries elsewhere; the replacement is a concentration decision, all extra wings on one filed target at +35% damage (Qwen leg fix, adopted by the chair).


#### Line Admiral Oskar Maren - Command: Shield wall order

- Branch A name: Column Standard
- T1: Column Filed Straight - during the wall, ships in a line of three or more adjacent friendly ships refill +25% more shield || Overflow by Regulation - during the wall, shield refill beyond a ship's maximum flows to the adjacent friendly ship with the lowest shields
- T2: Wall at the Bell - the wall may be declared at the very start of the round, before any ship moves or fires || Standard Rotation - during the wall, a ship may swap hexes with an adjacent friendly ship without losing the damage reduction
- T3: The Column Endures - while the wall is active, the first ship each round to have its shields emptied immediately refills to 25% || Filed in Armor - during the wall, a ship that does not fire this round reduces hull damage by a further 15%
- Capstone: The Standard Covers All - Shield wall order also refills the shields of each ship adjacent to a ship in the column at half strength, even if that ship is not in the line || The Flagship Holds the Standard - while the wall is active, the flagship counts as part of every friendly line for column effects, and ships in a line with the flagship refill +10% more shield
- Changes: Capstone first slot: the draft two round duration and refill chassis repeated Thale and Brin; the replacement extends refill coverage to ships adjacent to the column. Capstone second slot: the draft transferred half the flagship hull damage to another ship, a cross ship damage allocation the pack vocabulary does not contain, and its name used a sea word; the replacement makes the flagship join every friendly line for column effects.

- Branch B T1 (shared): Formation Filed Before Battle - choose one standard formation at battle start; ships that end their movement inside it gain +10 aim that round || Drill Revision One - once per battle, after the enemy has moved, reposition one of your ships up to 2 hexes
- Branch C T1 (shared): Flagship Registry Beacon - once per round, the flagship may stamp one enemy; your ships gain +10 aim against the stamped enemy || Standard Borne Forward - ships that move toward the flagship this round gain +1 movement
- Changes: none.
