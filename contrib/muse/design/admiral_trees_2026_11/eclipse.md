# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: Eclipse

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Eclipse

#### Nadir Aurex, Ninth Frame - Command: Release the repair swarms

- Branch A name: Iteration of the Whole Hull
- T1: Restore from the Ninth Frame - restore one ship from its archived state for the full repair amount, and every other ship receives half the normal swarm repair || Copies Triage the Damage - assign the repair section by section in any order you choose; a section restored to full also clears all of its damage effects
- T2: Iteration Runs During Fire - a ship being rebuilt by the swarms may act normally this round; the rebuild never delays its fire || The Archive Answers First - the swarms may be released at the very start of the round, before any ship moves or fires
- T3: Restored Beyond the Original - a section rebuilt by the swarms carries the next iteration plating, gaining +20% section hull for the rest of the battle || Every Copy Contributes - the flagship repair share may be divided among any number of friendly ships
- Capstone: Tenth Frame - choose one ship when the Command is declared; that ship's repair is tripled, and one destroyed section on it returns to full function || The Earlier Copy Returns - choose one damaged ship; it is repaired for the full repair amount on every section, all its damaged sections are patched, and its shields refill
- Changes: Capstone first slot: the draft rebuilt every ship a second time at half strength, the same chassis as the Mending Throng capstone; the replacement is one focused triple repair. Capstone second slot: the draft restored a ship to an exact archived state at battle start, a snapshot mechanic the pack vocabulary does not contain; the replacement is a full repair, section patch, and shield refill in existing terms.

#### Umbra Vessane, Third-Housed - Command: Noon volley

- Branch A name: Corona Iteration
- T1: Arcs Written Warm - a volley hit from a corona arc splashes 25% of its damage onto one enemy adjacent to the target || Noon Is a Choice - you may fire the volley at the start of the round, before movement, at +15% damage
- T2: The Copy Fires Again - a ship whose volley destroys its target may fire one weapon at a new target || Target Archived Mid-Flight - before the volley resolves, each ship may switch its target once
- T3: Corona Without Shadow - the volley ignores evasion granted by terrain and cover || Every Frame at Noon - a ship that did not move fires its volley at +25% damage and +10 aim
- Capstone: Noon That Does Not Pass - Noon volley fires again next round, at half damage, from every ship || One Sun, Copied - every ship's volley is also copied onto the enemy with the least remaining hull, at half damage
- Changes: T1 first slot and T2 second slot: the draft names used words owned by the Glasswrights; renamed in warmth and archive terms, mechanics unchanged (T1 fix from the Argon leg, adopted by the chair). T3 second slot: the draft fired the volley twice at half damage for an unmoved ship, restating the first capstone at lower power; the replacement is a damage and aim bonus for the unmoved ship, with no repeat fire.

#### Ostrel of the Long Copy - Command: Out of the shadow

- Branch A name: The Long Copy Hunt
- T1: Copied from Behind - during the Command round, rear arc hits gain +20% damage on top of the Command bonus || Unseen in the Archive - during the Command round, a ship that was not targeted last round gains a further +15% damage
- T2: The Shadow Is a Position - the Command round may be declared after movement, once you can see where your ships stand || Strike the Uncopied Target - during the Command round, hits on an enemy that has not yet been hit this battle gain +15% damage
- T3: Iteration of the Kill - during the Command round, when one of your ships lands a rear arc hit, the next hit on that target this round gains +20% damage || Silent in the Archive - during the Command round, any ship that ends its activation outside enemy front arcs gains +15 evasion until next round
- Capstone: Out of Every Shadow - the Command bonus covers two consecutive rounds for any ship that ends each round in a rear arc or not targeted that round || The Long Copy Strikes - when the Command is declared, each ship may first move 2 hexes toward an enemy rear arc
- Changes: Branch name: the apostrophe in the draft branch name was removed for the naming lint; mechanic unchanged. T1 second slot: the draft name used a word owned by Hegemony; renamed Unseen in the Archive, mechanic unchanged. T3 second slot: the draft was a flat accuracy penalty with no piloting decision and under the floor; the replacement pays +15 evasion for ending outside enemy front arcs (both fixes from the Argon leg, adopted by the chair). Capstone first slot: the draft condition used an unobserved state the pack vocabulary does not define; replaced with not targeted that round, which the T1 second slot already tracks.

#### Seveth, Twice-Restored - Command: Sunshade wall

- Branch A name: Layered Iterations
- T1: Plates over Shadow - during the wall, you may stack the wall on one ship: that ship takes 75% less hull damage this round, and every other ship takes 25% less instead of 50% || The Twice-Restored Order - a ship that has already been repaired earlier in this battle takes a further 15% less hull damage during the wall
- T2: Wall Archived in Advance - the wall may be declared at the start of the round, and its reduction applies from the first enemy shot of the round || Layers Shift - during the wall, at round end each ship may shift 1 hex toward the flagship without losing the wall damage reduction
- T3: No Layer Falls Alone - while the wall is active, the first time each ship shields reach zero, its layered plates absorb the hit that emptied them || Stillness Counted - during the wall, a ship that does not move gains +20 aim on its first shot next round
- Capstone: Wall of Ten Thousand Copies - while the wall is active, hull damage each ship takes in a round cannot exceed 25% of its maximum hull || The Restored Fleet - while the wall is active, a ship repaired this round keeps the wall hull damage reduction into next round after the wall ends
- Changes: T1 first slot: the draft name used a word owned by the Glasswrights, and its effect converted a shield refill this Command does not grant; the replacement is a stacked reduction choice on one ship. T1 second slot: the draft bonus was a shield refill bonus this Command does not grant; the replacement is a further reduction for a ship repaired earlier in the battle. T2 first slot: the draft name used a word owned by Hegemony; renamed Wall Archived in Advance, mechanic unchanged (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair). T2 second slot: the draft passed shields between ships, a transfer the pack vocabulary does not contain; the replacement is a 1 hex shift at round end that keeps the reduction. T3 second slot: the draft kept the reduction for one extra round, restating a duration capstone at lower power; the replacement is +20 aim on the first shot next round for a ship that does not move. Capstone both slots: the draft capstones were a two round duration with a shield refill and a shield overflow, both built on a refill this Command does not grant; the replacements are a per round hull damage cap and a repair linked extension of the reduction for one ship.

- Branch B T1 (shared): Formation Loaded from Archive - choose one archived formation at battle start; ships that end their movement inside it gain +10 evasion that round || Iteration Swap - once per battle, swap the positions of two of your ships, as the Archive substitutes a corrected version of the plan
- Branch C T1 (shared): The Flagship Hosts the Index - once per round, the flagship may copy another friendly ship's target and fire at it at +15% damage || Frame Zero - the flagship's first missed shot each battle is recorded and fired again at half damage
- Changes: none.
