## Audits

- Numbers audit (rules 1 and 2): Pure numeric skills in the final set: none. Every percentage or point effect in the final set is gated by a named target, a position, a facing, a movement choice, a timing choice, or a stated trigger, so no skill is a standalone pure numeric effect. Count per tree: 0 pure numeric out of 8 picks per admiral (Branch A tiers 1 to 3 plus both capstones), and 0 out of 4 shared picks per faction, so the one in three limit passes in every tree and rule 2 placement passes because no pure numeric sits on any branch. Conditional percentage effects are all at or above 15%; the smaller values in the set are point effects under the pack section 5 point caps (+10 aim, +10 evasion, +10 crit chance, and 10 or 20 point accuracy penalties), not percentage effects. Draft numerics removed: the Seveth T1 +25% shield refill bonus and the Seveth capstone half strength shield refill were removed in the Seveth rework because his Command grants no shield refill, not for a floor failure; no other draft numeric was removed for failing the floor.
- Rule 4 audit: No final skill duplicates any of the 12 LEGACY effects. No skill grants starting credits, fleet command points, shipyard hull availability, a dreadnought allowance, shipyard price changes, run start salvage, starting captain rank or perks, or credit gains from battles. Reserve entry skills deploy ships already in the fleet and grant none of those effects.
- Rule 6 audit: Four restating pairs were found and separated. Matriarch T3 first slot and capstone first slot both added extra swarms; T3 now pays +25% damage for an unmoved brood instead. Tamsa T3 second slot and capstone second slot both sent a recorded strike into next round; T3 now launches and strikes this round. Salvo family: no second Command use survives; Draven Salvo Without End reloads torpedoes a second time, a component reload, and the other salvo capstones extend or retarget the bonus.
