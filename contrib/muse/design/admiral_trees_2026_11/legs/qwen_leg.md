Audits

Numbers audit (rules 1 and 2): No skill in the final set is a pure numeric effect. Every skill on Branch A and Branch B changes a decision (timing, targeting, positioning, allocation, or activation order). The shared Branch C tier 1 sets likewise attach their numeric components to once-per-round or once-per-battle timing decisions, positional conditions, or conditional triggers. Zero pure numerics in 216 slots; the one-in-three cap is not approached. No draft numeric was removed because the Oct 29 assembly had already replaced all sub-floor and Branch A numerics; this verification confirms none survived.

Rule 4 audit: No final skill duplicates any of the 12 LEGACY doctrine effects. No skill touches shipyard availability, starting credits, fleet command points, starting captain rank, salvage generation, shipyard pricing, or post-battle credit recovery. Clean.

Rule 6 audit: All 24 admirals checked. No tier 3 skill restates either of its admiral's capstones at lower power. Closest pair examined: Reyes T3 "Full Group Assembled" (conditional extra wings, all at one filed target) versus replacement capstone 1 "One Target, Every Wing Filed" (all existing extra wings concentrate for +35% damage). T3 grants additional wings conditional on full carrier commitment; the capstone adds a damage bonus to wings already launched without requiring full commitment. Different purchases. No other pair required separation.
