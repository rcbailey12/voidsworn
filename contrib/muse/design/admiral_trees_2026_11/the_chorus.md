# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: The Chorus

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

The Chorus

#### The Matriarch of Nine Throats - Command: Spawning surge

- Branch A name: Too Many Mouths
- T1: Choose the Brood That Swells - one brood releases two extra swarms instead of one, and the other broods release none extra || Spores on the Tide - extra swarms may be placed up to 2 hexes from their brood instead of adjacent to it
- T2: The Brood Splits - each extra swarm may strike a different target than the rest of its brood's swarms || Spawned Before the Strike - spawning happens before your ships move, so the new swarms can screen your movement this round
- T3: Every Throat Opens - a brood that did not move this round has its extra swarms strike at +25% damage || The Second Throat Bites - a swarm striking a target already hit by another swarm this round gains +25% damage
- Capstone: Nine Throats, One Hunger - Spawning surge releases an extra swarm from every ship that carries swarms, not only from broods || The Brood Does Not Stop - the extra swarms remain after the Command and strike once more at the start of next round before fading
- Changes: Branch name, T2 both slots, T3 second slot, and both capstone slots: draft names used retired vocabulary barred by pack section 6; renamed in throat, brood, and hunger terms, mechanics unchanged except T3 first slot. T3 first slot: the draft released one more extra swarm, restating the first capstone count expansion at lower power; the replacement gives the unmoved brood extra swarms +25% damage instead.

#### Razormaw - Command: Blood frenzy

- Branch A name: The Hunting Throat
- T1: Run Down the Slowest - during the frenzy, your ships gain +20% damage against targets slower than themselves || Teeth at the Throat - a ship that ends its frenzy movement behind an enemy strikes that enemy rear arc with its first shot this round
- T2: One Prey in Both Throats - name one prey when the frenzy begins; ships moving toward the prey gain +1 movement || Scent in the Dark - during the frenzy, a ship may change targets freely after each kill without losing the frenzy accuracy protection
- T3: Frenzy Feeds the Throat - a ship that lands a rear arc hit during the frenzy may immediately move 2 more hexes || Breath Before the Bite - during the frenzy, an enemy adjacent to two or more of your ships loses a further 20 accuracy
- Capstone: The Throat Turns Red - ships that end the frenzy round adjacent to an enemy gain +25% damage on the first shot they fire next round || One Prey, Every Mouth - when the frenzy is declared, every ship may move directly toward the named prey, ignoring its turn limit once
- Changes: Branch name, T2 first slot, T3 first slot, and capstone first slot name: draft names used retired vocabulary; renamed in throat and prey terms. T2 second slot: the draft name used a sea word; renamed Scent in the Dark, mechanic unchanged (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair). Capstone first slot effect: the draft carried the frenzy movement and accuracy protection into next round, the same chassis as the Grax Ironmaw capstone; the replacement pays out as +25% damage on the first shot next round for ships that end this round adjacent to an enemy.

#### Mending Throng - Command: Regrowth tide

- Branch A name: The Mending Throat
- T1: Grow Where It Hurts - you may assign one ship's entire regrowth to a single damaged section || Skin Grown Thick - a ship may take its regrowth as temporary extra hull, up to +15%, instead of repairing damage
- T2: The Tide Comes Early - Regrowth tide may be used at the start of the round, before any fire is taken || Growth Holds the Wound - a section healed by the tide regrows at double rate again next round
- T3: Flesh Knit for War - a ship the tide brings above three quarters hull gains +20% damage next round || The Throat Refuses Holes - while any of your ships is below half hull, the most damaged ship regrows 10% at each round start until it passes half
- Capstone: Tide Without End - Regrowth tide heals a second time at the end of the round, at half strength || What Was Torn Grows Back - the tide restores destroyed sections to full function, and a section restored this way cannot be destroyed again this battle
- Changes: Branch name, T1 second slot, T3 second slot, and capstone second slot: draft names used retired vocabulary; renamed in throat, skin, and flesh terms, mechanics unchanged. Capstone first slot: the draft name used a sea word; renamed Tide Without End, mechanic unchanged (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair). The admiral header in all four legs read The Mending Throng; the shipped name is Mending Throng and the header above uses it. T2 second slot: the draft name used a memory word owned by Eclipse; renamed, mechanic unchanged.

#### Voice of the Deep - Command: Spitting volley

- Branch A name: The Acid Throat
- T1: Acid Before Aim - each ship may spit its volley at the nearest enemy instead of the best target || Every Throat Spits - a ship whose guns cannot bear may still spit once at half damage at the nearest enemy in range
- T2: The Burn Stays - an enemy hit by the volley takes acid damage at the start of next round equal to 15% of the volley damage it took || Spat in Two Rounds - you may fire the volley in two halves, the second half after your ships move
- T3: Acid Finds the Seams - volley hits ignore armor on targets below half hull || Many Mouths at Once - before the volley resolves, each ship may switch its target once; if four or more ships spit at one target, that target loses 20 accuracy next round as acid eats at its scanners
- Capstone: The Volley That Grows - after the volley, every ship that hit with its first spit spits again at half damage || Mouth of the Deep - choose one ship; it spits with every weapon it carries, twice, at any single target in range
- Changes: Branch name, T2 second slot, and T3 second slot: draft names used retired vocabulary; renamed in throat, mouth, and acid terms, mechanics unchanged.

- Branch B T1 (shared): Roles Assigned at Hatching - at battle start, give each ship a role (strike, screen, or mend); a ship may change its role once per battle || Broods in Reserve - hold one brood off the field; it enters, grown and ready, at the start of round 2
- Branch C T1 (shared): The First Throat Opens - once per battle, one other ship may act immediately after the flagship finishes its activation || Throat of the Deep - ships within 2 hexes of the flagship gain +10 aim
- Changes: Branch B first slot and Branch C both slots: draft names and the role wording used retired vocabulary; renamed in role, throat, and hatching terms, mechanics unchanged.
