# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: Ashfall Hullbreakers

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Ashfall Hullbreakers

#### Warlord Grax Ironmaw - Command: Full burn

- Branch A name: The Scar Run
- T1: Run at the Biggest Thing - ships that end their burn movement closer to the largest enemy than they started gain +15% damage this round || Ram First - a ship that moves at least 4 hexes during the burn may ram with no accuracy penalty
- T2: The Pack Closes - during the burn, your ships may pass through hexes held by friendly ships || Ram Rush - a ship that ends its burn movement adjacent to an enemy may ram it immediately instead of firing this round
- T3: All Scars Forward - during the burn, a ship gains +1 movement for each enemy ship it passes adjacent to, up to +2 || The Maw Opens - during the burn, the first ram by each ship deals +30% damage
- Capstone: Burn Until the Hulls Meet - Full burn movement and accuracy protection carry into next round movement phase || Run of the Warlord - when Full burn is declared, the flagship may move twice, and may ram after each move
- Changes: T1 second slot: the draft name used a word owned by the Chorus; renamed, mechanic unchanged. T2 second slot: the draft proposed a boarding action the battle rules do not have; restated as an immediate ram. T3 second slot: the boarding reference was removed; ram only. Capstone first slot: the draft name used a word owned by the Chorus; renamed, mechanic unchanged. Capstone second slot: the boarding reference was removed and the apostrophe in the draft name was removed for the naming lint; ram only.

#### Sable the Smiling Knife - Command: Knife in the dark

- Branch A name: Scars Behind the Prey
- T1: Knife Finds the Back - during the Command round, rear arc hits gain +15% damage on top of the Command bonus || Torpedoes in the Dark - torpedoes fired this round are screened at -20 effect, as if the firing ships were never seen
- T2: Cut and Turn - a ship that lands a rear arc hit this round may turn once for free after firing || Second Knife - torpedoes that miss this round reload one turn sooner than normal
- T3: The Smile Before the Cut - during the Command round, ships that begin their activation behind an enemy act before that enemy || Every Knife at Once - if three or more of your ships hit the same target rear arc this round, that target's weapons lose 20 accuracy next round
- Capstone: Dark Full of Knives - the Command damage bonus also applies to the first shot each ship fires next round, if that shot comes from a rear arc || The Cut That Feeds the Pack - when the Command is declared, every ship may immediately move 2 hexes toward the nearest enemy rear arc
- Changes: none.

#### Old Mott - Command: Release the swarm

- Branch A name: Junkyard Wings
- T1: Patched Wing - choose one carrier; its extra wing is patched together from scrap, strikes at +25% damage, and is removed after its strike || Hulk Holds More - one of your largest ships may launch an extra wing during the Command even though it is not a carrier, using its held wing space
- T2: Re-Tasked Mid-Flight - after the first strike resolves, each extra wing may switch to a new target for its strike again || Launched from the Wreck Line - carriers that did not move this round launch their extra wings at +15% damage
- T3: The Whole Yard Flies - every ship with any hangar space launches an extra wing during the Command, not only carriers || Wings Come Back Screaming - the strike again keeps the full +50% bonus even when it hits a new target across the field
- Capstone: Swarm Over the Scrap Field - Release the swarm launches the extra wings twice, once at the start of your turn and once at its end || Old Mott Private Wing - choose one carrier; its extra wing stays launched for the rest of the battle and strikes at half damage at the start of each round
- Changes: Capstone second slot: the apostrophe in the draft name was removed for the naming lint; mechanic unchanged.

#### Vesh the Collector - Command: Patch and weld

- Branch A name: Scar Welding
- T1: Weld What Matters - instead of spreading the repair evenly, you may put one ship's entire repair share onto its single most damaged section || Patch Over the Guns - each repaired ship chooses where its repair lands first: weapon sections or engine sections
- T2: Scrap to the Front - you may take the flagship's repair share and give it to any one other ship, doubled || Welded While Fighting - a section patched by the Command works at full effect in the same round it is patched
- T3: Double-Bolted Plate - a section patched by the Command gains +20% section hull for the rest of the battle || The Pack Bites On - a ship that the Command brings back above half hull gains +15% damage next round
- Capstone: Welded Whole - Patch and weld restores one destroyed section on every ship to full strength, not merely patched function || Nothing Stays Broken - Patch and weld may be declared at the start of the enemy turn, landing before the first hit that would cripple one of your ships that turn
- Changes: T3 first slot: the draft name used a memory word owned by Eclipse; renamed Double-Bolted Plate, mechanic unchanged (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair).

- Branch B T1 (shared): Prey Chosen by Scent - at battle start, name the pack's prey; your ships gain +10 aim against it || Loose Pack, Wide Lanes - your ships never block each other's movement or firing lanes
- Branch C T1 (shared): The Flagship Takes the First Scar - once per battle, the flagship may take a hit aimed at an adjacent friendly ship onto itself || Trophy Standard - after the flagship lands a kill, every friendly ship may turn once for free
- Changes: Branch B second slot: the draft name used a word owned by the Chorus; renamed in lanes terms, mechanic unchanged (chair wording, following the Argon leg).
