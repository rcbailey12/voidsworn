# Admiral skill trees, Phase 1: Ashfall Hullbreakers

Final chair text for board issue 31. Per admiral: Branch A tiers 1 to 3 (two options each) and both capstones, authored against the admiral's primary Command only. Per faction: the shared Branch B tier 1 set and Branch C tier 1 set. Effects are stated in the game terms of the evidence pack (data.js and core.js vocabulary). Changes lines record where the final text departs from the Oct 29 assembled draft and which leg the fix came from. See index.md for the synthesis, the audits, the screening notes, and the flags carried to Opus.

Ashfall Hullbreakers

#### Warlord Grax Ironmaw - Command: Full burn

- Branch A name: The Scar Run
- T1: Run at the Biggest Thing - ships that end their burn movement closer to the largest enemy than they started gain +15% damage this round || First Over the Rail - a ship that moves at least 4 hexes during the burn boards at +25% strength
- T2: The Pack Closes - during the burn, a ship that ends its movement next to a friendly ship gains +10 aim this round || Rush the Rail - a ship that ends its burn movement adjacent to an enemy may board it even if it has already fired this round
- T3: All Scars Forward - during the burn, a ship gains +1 movement for each enemy ship it passes adjacent to, up to +2 || The Maw Opens - during the burn, the first ram by each ship deals +30% damage
- Capstone: Burn Until the Hulls Meet - Full burn movement, turns and accuracy protection carry into the next round || Run of the Warlord - when Full burn is declared, the flagship may move twice, and may board after each move
- Changes: T1 second slot: the draft name used a word owned by the Chorus; renamed, mechanic unchanged. T2 second slot: the draft proposed a boarding action the battle rules do not have; restated as an immediate ram. T3 second slot: the boarding reference was removed; ram only. Capstone first slot: the draft name used a word owned by the Chorus; renamed, mechanic unchanged. Capstone second slot: the boarding reference was removed and the apostrophe in the draft name was removed for the naming lint; ram only.

#### Sable the Smiling Knife - Command: Knife in the dark

- Branch A name: Scars Behind the Prey
- T1: Knife Finds the Back - during the Command round, rear arc hits gain +15% damage on top of the Command bonus || Torpedoes in the Dark - torpedoes fired this round are intercepted at half the usual chance: enemy flak and screen wings roll at half effect
- T2: Cut and Turn - a ship that lands a rear arc hit this round may turn once for free after firing || Second Knife - torpedoes that miss this round reload one turn sooner than normal
- T3: The Smile Before the Cut - each round during the Command, the first of your ships to activate gains +20% damage on its first shot || Every Knife at Once - if three or more of your ships hit the same target rear arc this round, that target's weapons lose 20 accuracy next round
- Capstone: Dark Full of Knives - the Command damage bonus also applies to the first shot each ship fires next round, if that shot comes from a rear arc || The Cut That Feeds the Pack - when the Command is declared, every ship may immediately move 2 hexes toward the nearest enemy rear arc
- Changes: none.

#### Old Mott - Command: Release the swarm

- Branch A name: Junkyard Wings
- T1: Patched Wing - choose one carrier; its extra wing is patched together from scrap, strikes at +25% damage, and is removed after its strike || Hulk Holds More - choose one of your largest ships that is not a carrier; it launches one extra wing during the Command, from the Hulk's spare racks
- T2: Re-Tasked Mid-Flight - an extra wing that strikes a target no other wing struck this round gains +20% damage || Launched from the Wreck Line - carriers that did not move this round launch their extra wings at +15% damage
- T3: The Whole Yard Flies - a launch ship that launches all its wings this Command launches one more extra wing || Wings Come Back Screaming - an extra wing's strike again gains +15% damage when it hits a target already hit this round
- Capstone: Swarm Over the Scrap Field - Release the swarm launches the extra wings twice: once when the Command is used, and once after your last activation this round || Old Mott Private Wing - choose one launch ship; its extra wing stays for the battle and gains +20% damage on its first strike each round
- Changes: Capstone second slot: the apostrophe in the draft name was removed for the naming lint; mechanic unchanged.

#### Vesh the Collector - Command: Patch and weld

- Branch A name: Scar Welding
- T1: Weld What Matters - instead of spreading the repair evenly, you may put one ship's entire repair share onto its single most damaged section || Patch Over the Guns - a ship whose weapons section is repaired by the Command gains +10 aim this round
- T2: Scrap to the Front - you may take the flagship's repair share and give it to any one other ship, doubled || Welded While Fighting - a ship repaired by the Command may fire one weapon at half damage at once
- T3: Double-Bolted Plate - a section patched by the Command gains +20% section hull for the rest of the battle || The Pack Bites On - a ship that the Command brings back above half hull gains +15% damage next round
- Capstone: Welded Whole - Patch and weld restores one destroyed section on every ship to full strength, not merely patched function || Nothing Stays Broken - the first time one of your ships would lose a section this battle, that section is not broken and is restored to full
- Changes: T3 first slot: the draft name used a memory word owned by Eclipse; renamed Double-Bolted Plate, mechanic unchanged (Argon leg fix, adopted by the chair).

- Branch B T1 (shared): Prey Chosen by Scent - at battle start, name the pack's prey; your ships gain +10 aim against it || Loose Pack, Wide Lanes - a ship gains +1 movement on a move that ends with no friendly ship within 2 hexes
- Branch C T1 (shared): The Flagship Takes the First Scar - once per battle, the flagship may take a hit aimed at an adjacent friendly ship onto itself || Trophy Standard - after the flagship lands a kill, every friendly ship may turn once for free
- Changes: Branch B second slot: the draft name used a word owned by the Chorus; renamed in lanes terms, mechanic unchanged (chair wording, following the Argon leg).
