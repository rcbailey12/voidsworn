# Prompts as used, concept round 4 (issue #467)

How these were chaired: DeepSeek wrote the writer leg (per-variation sheet and beauty prompts plus risk lines); the blind Rey leg was written first; Flash and Grok filed ideation (risk lines for all nine, full prompts for the Glasswright variations and C8). The chair built the generation prompts on DeepSeek's leg, folding in the guards from Grok and Flash where they agreed with the brief, with two chair rulings applied throughout:

1. Chorus interior glow is AMBER. All three outside legs reached for yellow-green or bile tones; the picked C4 base carries amber, so amber stands.
2. G8's bow is POINTED, per the brief (a quartz point, lenses on the collar behind it). DeepSeek's leg softened it to a blunt termination; not adopted. DeepSeek's guard that the lenses stay clear of the point was adopted.

Beauty prompts are the chair's, written from each accepted sheet in the house pattern (three-quarter view, black starfield, one escort defined by absences). Rejected tries are noted where their prompt differed in a way worth recording. Every generation was image-to-image from the named reference picture stated at the head of its prompt.

## G7 Long Prism, sheet (delivered try 1; reference ithar_B round 1)

Image reference: the round 1 Prism picture attached. Take from it only the clear glass and the warm white light moving inside it. Do not copy its layout, its rails, its scattered small guns, its slab body or its unclear bow and stern.

Design sheet for the Glasswrights capital ship, Long Prism (G7). Orthographic, flat on, no perspective: top view above, side view below, exactly the same scale, on a plain dark grey background, evenly lit, no text, no labels, no border, no watermark, no arrows. The top view is the plan and carries the identity; the side view confirms it.

The ship: ONE long three-sided prism of clear glass, one sharp ridge up, two lower long edges, about 5 : 1 : 1 (length : beam : height), long and slim, never a broad slab. Warm white light moves inside the glass along its whole length, brightest toward the bow; the glass is clear, not frosted, not grey, not faceted armour. A silver structural frame carries it: one silver beam along each of the three long edges, joined by three or four slim silver ribs that wrap the prism. No metal hull shaped like a crystal, no crystal bolted to a hull, no faceted grey armour.

Stern: a flat silver engine block caps the stern, with exactly three white drive rings side by side across the stern face, soft white, no flame, nothing else glowing at the stern.

Bow: the glass ends in a flat clear optical emitter face; on the silver bow collar just behind it sit exactly two forward pulsar lenses, one each side of the centre line, large and round, recessed in armoured silver iris mounts with visible overlapping iris blades, facing forward.

On the top silver beam, in this order from the bow: the top pulsar lens turret, one large lens in an armoured iris mount on a visible silver base; the gun turret, twin short thick dark barrels in a silver housing, barrels pointing forward; one small flak turret, a small silver cluster of four short barrels; then a low silver command block aft. That is five weapons and no others: pulsar forward, pulsar forward, pulsar turret, gun turret, flak turret. Nothing else anywhere may look like a gun: no extra lenses, no rails of small guns, no lit port strips, no second turret, no dome. Gun ports and muzzles are dark, never lit like windows. The ship must read as armed in the top view at thumbnail size.

Rainbow appears only as a thin fringe on the cut edges of the glass, never pooled, never as a gem. Nothing insect-like: no wings, no petals, no antennae, no legs. No masts, no fins, no portholes, no sea parts. Clear glass, warm white light, silver frame, thin rainbow edge fringe only.

Rejected try 2 added a constant-width guard ("the SAME width from bow to stern... NOT tapered, NOT pointed") and an explicit "NOT fans, NOT vents" lens guard. It held the width but produced two plan views and flank turrets, so try 1 stands.

## C7 The base, grown, sheet (delivered try 1; reference round 3 C4 sheet; amber per chair ruling 1)

Image reference: the round 3 Chorus C4 design sheet attached. Keep its body, its hide colours and its weapon organs; push everything organic further and make the weapon organs plainer.

Design sheet for the Chorus capital ship, the base grown (C7). Orthographic, flat on, no perspective: top view above, side view below, exactly the same scale, on a plain dark grey background, evenly lit, no text, no labels, no border, no watermark, no arrows.

## Beauty prompts (chair's, from the accepted sheets)

Pattern for all nine: "Image reference: the accepted <code> design sheet attached. Build this beauty view of exactly that ship/animal. Beauty view, three-quarter perspective from front-above against a black starfield." Then the variation's sheet description restated in beauty terms, then its escort, then the never list. The escort definitions, which are the part worth reusing:

- Glasswrights (all three): a smaller ship of the same family, a short silver-framed glass spar with ONE forward lens and NO turret row and NO command block, one drive ring at its stern. Not the hero shrunk.
- G9 beauty try 1 lacked an explicit colour guard and drew blue fan lenses; try 2 added "the lenses are warm white glass with silver iris blades, NO blue anywhere on the ship" and "No blue light or blue lenses anywhere", and held.
- A7: an escort made of ONE salvaged hull section only, a single slab-sided block with one small rack of tubes and one engine bell, not a stack of sections.
- A8: same one-section escort.
- A9: one salvaged section with one small rack, one bell and one short length of cargo chain with a single cage.
- C7: a younger, smaller animal with a CLOSED bloom, no tendril crown and no brood sacs, one small amber throat bud at its bow.
- C8: a younger animal with a closed bloom, no crown, no sacs, and only two or three SMALL bone spines just breaking its back.
- C9 try 1: escort as C7's. The hero's bloom grew a third opening and the try was rejected. Try 2 added "EXACTLY TWO bioplasma throats, two deep round openings glowing amber, side by side, and no third opening anywhere on the bloom" for the hero and "no sacs and no bulges" for the escort, and held.
