## Full reply

1. Trap list

- BBC Sound Effects: free downloads under RemArc are personal/educational/research only; commercial game use requires paid licence from Pro Sound Effects. https://sound-effects.bbcrewind.co.uk/faqs
- Freesound CC-BY-NC (and legacy Sampling+): commercial use forbidden; filter must exclude them or the ship is invalid. https://freesound.org/help/faq/
- ZapSplat free tier: commercial allowed but attribution to ZapSplat is required where reasonably possible; missing credits breaks the licence. https://zapsplat.com (Standard License)
- OpenGameArt mixed packs: CC-BY-SA forces share-alike on derivatives; closed-source commercial ship can fail if any SA asset is altered. Check per file. https://opengameart.org
- Pixabay Content License: commercial OK and no attribution, but standalone redistribution of the sound itself is forbidden; keep files inside the game binary only.
- Sonniss GDC bundles: commercial, no attribution, unlimited projects OK, but raw files may not be resold or used to train AI. https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-licence/
- Habit that produces thin/weak: single short sample with no low-mid body or layered decay; result lacks weight and dies instantly.
- Habit that produces arcade/cheesy: stock laser pews, rising whooshes, bright synthetic zaps, or stock cartoon explosions instead of mechanical/electrical sources.
- Habit that produces harsh/annoying: bright top-end, long bright tails, or repeated identical takes without variation; listener fatigue in long sessions.
