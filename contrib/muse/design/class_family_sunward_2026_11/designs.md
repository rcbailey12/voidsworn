# Sunward Crusade starter fleet: designs

The tables in this file outrank the pictures. Fit order per R236:
data.js first, then the faction guide and lore, the concept last.
Stations are percent of hull length from the prow tip. Lengths are
world units from the class table (hull_table.gd CLS_LEN). Beams are
concept targets for the builder, widest point of the hull envelope.

## Family law (from the faction sheet, binding on all five)

A black iron blade with burning spires. Long narrow dagger or
spearhead hull, widest at the stern third, symmetric. Tall radiator
spires along the spine on a forged spine block, tallest amidships,
ember fins between iron posts; the spires shed reactor heat, they are
not steeples. Broadside gun decks in long straight rows where the data
has broadsides. Two to four large engine bells, ember, deep-set.
Plate with rivet lines, never rust. Black iron, frost-white markings,
ember orange light only. Breaks run along the gun decks, the hull
splits lengthwise between deck rows, and the spire line snaps off as
one piece. Never: rust, blue light, a ram prow shaped like a ship's
stem, literal cathedral windows, crosses, statues.

## Weapon convention (from S8's accepted weapon match)

- A turret-arc slot is drawn as a PAIR of mounts, one at each deck
  edge at the same station. Shots distribute over the pair's barrels
  and every barrel fires each volley (S8: the flak slot's 3 shots run
  over two twin mounts; the sear turret slot's 2 shots run one per
  mount).
- A broadside battery slot is drawn as one deep round port per shot
  on that side, between armoured lips, with ember gallery slits
  between ports (S8: heavy battery 5 shots, 5 ports a side).
- A forward slot is a fixed bow mount: a gun is base, mantlet,
  breech, barrel, muzzle; a launch tube has depth, a lip and a dark
  bore, never a disc.

## Never change across the family

1. The spearhead bow (one point at mid height, diamond section) and
   the blade hull widest at the stern third, in black iron with
   frost-white markings and ember-only light.
2. The radiator spire line on a forged spine block, tallest
   amidships, ember fins between iron posts.
3. The broadside band on every hull: ports where the data has
   broadsides, plated and louvred where it does not, a hangar trench
   on the carrier. Breaks lengthwise, spire line snapping as one
   piece.

## Never copy from S8 literally

Seven spires, five ports a side as a default count, four deck
turrets, 2.3-unit bells, S8's proportions. Every class below carries
its own counts; family language without shape cloning.

## Warden, frigate (data key warden), 6.5

Concept: the knife. The smallest blade in the fleet, built around its
bow gun: the spearhead itself carries the autocannon in a forged
casemate at its root, and the hull behind it is smooth, fast and
nearly featureless except for the flak pair and a short spire row.

| Item | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Length / beam | 6.5 / 1.5, widest at about 72 percent |
| Autocannon, forward, 3 shots | Compact forged casemate at the spearhead root, station 14 percent; three-barrel rotary cluster, all barrels fire per volley |
| Flak, turret, 3 shots | One pair of small twin tubs at the deck edges, station 60 percent; 3 shots distributed over the pair per the family convention |
| Broadside band | Plated and louvred, no ports (no broadside slot in data) |
| Spire line | 3 low spires, stations 68 / 76 / 84 percent, tallest 0.7 (reduced per Ryan's approval note, Nov 19, 2026: approved with smaller spires) |
| Command | Ember slits in the spine block face, station 58 percent |
| Engines | 2 bells, 0.55 across, offsets plus/minus 0.4 |
| Breaks | Lengthwise along the band; spire line snaps as one piece |

Defining features: the bow casemate gun; the portless plated band;
the three low aft spires. No visible feature is invented for the
evasive ability (all three legs agreed; the read is the narrow hull).

Picture slips: the beauty draws four small deck tubs (the table's one
flak pair at 60 percent governs) and four spires (table: 3). The
sheet's side view renders the spire block as flat panels; its front
view is the reliable identity view.

## Harrow, destroyer (data key harrow), 9.5

Concept: the torpedo bow. The whole ship is the delivery system for
one heavy salvo: twin tubes in the bow cheeks, a widened magazine
section behind them venting reload heat, then a clean narrow hull.

| Item | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Length / beam | 9.5 / 2.1, widest at about 70 percent |
| Torpedo salvo, forward, 1 shot | TWIN tubes flanking the centreline in the bow cheeks, station 10 percent, offsets plus/minus 0.45; each tube has depth, a forged lip and a dark bore; the pair is the one salvo slot |
| Turret, turret arc, 2 shots | One pair of single-emitter tubs at the deck edges, station 46 percent, one shot per mount |
| Magazine section | Widened forward-mid hull, stations 20 to 45 percent, heavy rivet lines and ember heat-vent slits (the reload read; no external machinery is invented) |
| Broadside band | Plated, no ports |
| Spire line | 4 spires, stations 66 / 73 / 80 / 87 percent, tallest 1.2 |
| Engines | 2 bells, 0.8 across, offsets plus/minus 0.55 |
| Breaks | Lengthwise along the band; the bow tube block separates at the forward knuckle; spire line snaps as one piece |

Defining features: the twin bow tubes; the vented magazine bulge;
the clean portless flanks. Chair ruling: DeepSeek proposed a single
centreline tube; the twin tubes stand, because the game's own variant
text for this hull (vs_harrow_t) reads "Twin prow torpedo tubes",
and data outranks leg inference (R236). A single centreline tube
would also blunt the spearhead point.

Round 2 (Ryan's ruling, Nov 19, 2026): the round 1 pictures were
sent back because the riveted twin-hull read and the hazard
chevrons made the ship look like a different faction. Round 2 keeps
the table above and redraws the ship as ONE solid spearhead hull:
the twin tubes are recessed into the spearhead's cheek plates like
gun ports, the chevrons are gone (frost-white is one thin stripe
and a small sigil, as on S8), and the rivet work follows S8's fine
seam lines. The round 2 beauty, sheet and line-up replace the
round 1 pictures in this folder.

Picture slips (round 2): the beauty and the sheet's side view draw
five spires (table: 4). The sheet's third view is the stern, not
the bow; the bow identity is carried by the sheet's top view and
the beauty. The sheet's top view draws the spine block as a row of
lit blocks; the spires themselves are as tabled.

## Vigil, cruiser (data key penitent), 14.5

Concept: the lance prow. The first true broadside hull in the
family: a full gun deck under a clean crowned deck, and the fleet's
signature weapon, the prow lance, fed by an armoured power trunk
along the centre ridge.

| Item | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Length / beam | 14.5 / 3.0, widest at about 68 percent |
| Battery, port, 4 shots | Four deep round ports, stations 38 / 47 / 56 / 65 percent, armoured lips, ember gallery slits between |
| Battery, starboard, 4 shots | Mirrored, same stations |
| Lance, forward, 1 shot | Single emitter set in the spearhead face, station 4 percent; housing and dorsal power trunk along the centre ridge to station 30 percent |
| Deck turrets | None in data; the crowned deck stays clean |
| Spire line | 5 spires, stations 64 / 70 / 76 / 82 / 88 percent, tallest 1.3 (reduced per Ryan's approval note, Nov 19, 2026: approved with smaller spires) |
| Command | Ember slits in the spine block face, station 60 percent |
| Engines | 2 bells, 1.2 across, offsets plus/minus 0.8 |
| Breaks | Lengthwise between the deck rows; spire line snaps as one piece |

Defining features: the prow lance and its power trunk; the four-port
gun deck; the clean turretless deck. Chair ruling: the legs split on
three ports (Rey) versus four (DeepSeek, Gemini); four stands,
because the family's own battery pattern on S8 maps shots to ports
one for one. The faction law's "one heavy pair at the bow" is met in
form by the emitter and its paired cheek housings; the data's lance
is one shot and is drawn as one emitter.

Picture slips: the beauty's spearhead reads as a shallow fork around
the emitter (build one point, emitter in its face) and its flank
port count is muddled (table: four a side). The sheet's side view
draws three port columns and seven spires (table: four ports, five
spires); its front view is the reliable lance view.

## Hearth, carrier (data key reliquary), 16.5: TABLE ONLY

Pictures follow Ryan's pick of the trio. Concept: the wide one. The
broadside band becomes a hangar trench, and the spire line splits
into two ridges to keep the turret arcs clear.

| Item | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Length / beam | 16.5 / 4.2, widest at about 66 percent; the widest hull for its length in the family |
| Hangar, 2 wings | Two mouths per flank inside one long trench band, stations 38 and 54 percent; armoured lips, dark bores, ember deck light inside; rectangular forged openings, never arched |
| Turret, turret arc, 2 shots | One pair of single-emitter tubs at the deck edges, station 30 percent |
| Flak, turret, 3 shots | One pair of twin tubs at the deck edges, station 86 percent |
| Spire line | 6 spires in a split twin-ridge line, 3 plus 3 along the upper knuckles, stations 44 to 80 percent, tallest 1.6 |
| Engines | 4 bells, 1.0 across |
| Breaks | Lengthwise through the hangar band; each spire ridge snaps as one piece |

Chair notes: DeepSeek's dorsal flight deck was not taken (a flat
dorsal deck risks the sea-carrier read the concept standard fails);
the flank trench carries the carrier read instead. Gemini's split
twin-ridge spire line was taken over a centreline row because both
turret slots need clear arcs. Bay count is not wing count; the data
fixes two wings, the trench carries two mouths a side as openings,
not as a squadron count.

## Bastion, battleship (data key cathedral), 21.0: TABLE ONLY

Pictures follow Ryan's pick of the trio. Concept: the bruiser. S8's
broadside weight on a shorter, denser hull, differentiated by count
and packing, never by copying the flagship's layout.

| Item | Design |
| --- | --- |
| Length / beam | 21.0 / 4.5, widest at about 67 percent |
| Heavy battery, port, 5 shots | Five heavy deep round ports a side, packed tighter than S8's spread: stations 34 / 42 / 50 / 58 / 66 percent |
| Heavy battery, starboard, 5 shots | Mirrored, same stations |
| Lance, forward, 1 shot | Single emitter in the spearhead face, station 4 percent; power trunk to station 28 percent |
| Flak, turret, 3 shots | One pair of twin tubs at the deck edges, station 62 percent (the family convention, as on S8) |
| Spire line | 6 spires (not S8's seven), stations 64 to 84 percent, tallest 2.2 |
| Engines | 3 bells, 1.45 across |
| Breaks | Lengthwise between the deck rows; spire line snaps as one piece |

Chair notes: five ports a side matches S8's count because the data
says five shots; the anti-cloning work is done by the tighter
packing, six spires, three bells, one flak pair and no sear turret
pair. DeepSeek's single aft flak turret was not taken; the family's
turret convention is the deck-edge pair.
