# Sunward starter fleet: image prompts

All pictures were generated image-to-image. Beauties took the S8
three-quarter anchor as the material and style reference; sheets
were generated from the accepted beauty of the same ship; the
line-up took all four ships as references. The operative prompt text
for each delivered picture is below, lightly trimmed of repetition.

## warden_beauty.webp (second take, the one delivered)

First take drew the autocannon as a large twin-barrel turret block
amidships and two centreline dome turrets; it was corrected with:
"Using the attached picture as the exact ship reference, draw THE
SAME frigate with these corrections: (1) REMOVE the large
twin-barrel turret block from the forward deck entirely. In its
place, set a compact low casemate directly into the root of the
spearhead bow, carrying a tight cluster of three short autocannon
barrels pointing forward over the bow point. (2) REMOVE the two
small dome turrets from the centreline deck. In their place put ONE
pair of very small low flak tubs, one at each deck edge at the same
point along the hull, each with two tiny barrels. (3) Make the
glowing radiator stack toward the stern read as THREE separate
spires with dark gaps between them. Keep everything else identical:
the narrow black-iron blade hull, smooth flanks with no gun ports,
frost-white markings, two ember engine bells at the stern, the same
three-quarter view with the bow pointing left, painterly realistic
rendering, deep space. No text or lettering anywhere on the hull."

First-take base prompt (also the Harrow and Vigil pattern): "Using
the attached picture only as the reference for material, colour and
rendering style (forged black iron plate with rivet lines,
frost-white markings, glowing ember-orange heat light, massive
gothic space warship): draw a MUCH SMALLER frigate of the same
fleet, a single spaceship in deep space, three-quarter view from
the front and above, bow pointing left. The frigate is a narrow
black-iron blade: a sharp spearhead bow, smooth riveted flank
armour with NO gun ports along its sides, a forged casemate at the
spearhead's root carrying a three-barrel rotary autocannon cluster
pointing forward, one pair of small low flak turret tubs at the
deck edges amidships, three low radiator spires with ember-glowing
fins clustered toward the stern, and two small deep-set engine
bells glowing ember orange at the stern. Lean and fast-looking, the
smallest ship of its fleet. Painterly realistic rendering, deep
space background. No text, no other ships."

## harrow_beauty.webp

Base pattern as above, ship text: "a destroyer of the same fleet...
whose spearhead bow is split by TWO deep torpedo tubes, one in
each bow cheek flanking the central point, each tube a dark round
bore with a thick forged lip, clearly launch tubes and not gun
barrels. Behind the bow a widened armoured magazine section with
heavy rivet lines and thin ember heat-vent slits. Smooth riveted
flanks with NO broadside gun ports. One pair of low turret tubs at
the deck edges amidships, each with a single short emitter barrel.
Four radiator spires with ember-glowing fins along the spine
toward the stern, and two deep-set engine bells glowing ember
orange at the stern."

## vigil_beauty.webp

Base pattern as above, ship text: "a cruiser of the same fleet...
with a spearhead bow: set into the spearhead's face is a single
heavy lance emitter lens glowing ember, with a raised armoured
power trunk ridge running along the top centreline from the
emitter back to the spine. Along each flank, a straight row of
FOUR deep round broadside gun ports with thick armoured lips and
thin ember gallery slits between the ports. The top deck is clean,
no turrets. Five tall radiator spires with ember-glowing fins
stand along the spine toward the stern, tallest in the middle, and
two large deep-set engine bells glow ember orange at the stern."

## Sheets (warden_sheet.webp, vigil_sheet.webp, first takes)

"Using the attached picture as the exact design reference, draw a
three-view concept sheet of THE SAME [ship] on one page with a
flat neutral dark grey background: a true top view (bow pointing
up the page), a true side view (bow pointing left), and a front
view, the whole ship fully visible in every view, orthographic
with no perspective distortion, one soft light from forward and
above in all views. Features identical to the reference: [the
ship's feature list from designs.md]. Painterly realistic
rendering. No text, no labels, no other ships."

## harrow_sheet.webp (second take, the one delivered)

The first take split the hull into twin booms in the top view. The
delivered take added: "CRITICAL: the ship has ONE single hull, a
single narrow blade shape, NOT two hulls and NOT a catamaran. In
the front view the two torpedo tube bores sit side by side INSIDE
the one hull's bow, flanking its central spearhead point." (The
top view still splits; see the slip note in designs.md.)

## family_lineup.webp

"Using the four attached pictures as the exact design references,
draw a fleet line-up of the SAME four black-iron warships in
profile side view, all bows pointing left, floating in a row in
deep space at their TRUE relative sizes: the huge dreadnought from
the first picture is 27 units long and towers over the others; the
cruiser from the second picture is about half its length (14.5
units); the destroyer from the third picture is about a third of
its length (9.5 units); the frigate from the fourth picture is
about a quarter of its length (6.5 units). Same forged black iron,
frost-white markings, ember-orange radiator spires and engine glow
on all four. Painterly realistic rendering, deep space background.
No text, no labels."
