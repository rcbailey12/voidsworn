# Grok ideation leg, issue #270 (verbatim from the browser run, Nov 8, 2026; ideation seat, ungated Fast mode)

1. Glass Cockpit Diegetic Overlay. Thin hard-edged metallic bezels and etched viewport framing treating the full screen as one forward bridge window; UI as projected glass layers inside the 3D scene. Ultra-condensed monospaced technical type, all caps, status codes over words. Gold as thin glowing edge highlights for selection and primary actions; bone on dark glass; cyan/amber strictly bar fills; green/lavender side tags only. Crest tiny, etched, top-centre frame bar. Hides rounded cards, pill buttons, shadows, most explanatory labels. Weakness: sparse, hard to parse for new players on a busy field. Reference: Dead Space RIG diegetic HUD (thegamer.com article). Grok rank 2.

2. Analog Instrument Panel. Heavy bolted steel frames, rivets, recessed dials, physical switch plates. Embossed stencil lettering, single-weight numerals. Gold for primary controls and indicator lights; bars simplified to dial gauges. Crest a stamped metal plate, embossed. Hides detailed data tables; gauges give trend not precision. Weakness: exact values harder to read. Reference: analog cockpit design vendor page (cockpitsonic.com). Grok rank 4.

3. Holographic Tactical Table. Lightweight luminous translucent frames, glass-like horizontal panels floating over the hex grid. Clean futuristic sans, thin weights, uppercase headings. Gold for selected ships and actions; cyan/amber glowing bar values; green/lavender side markers as hologram projections. Crest a tiny floating holographic emblem above the top bar. Hides heavy borders, texture, traditional widgets. Weakness: intangible, readability in bright scenes. Reference: a Reddit holographic HUD concept post. Grok rank 3.

4. Cold Military Terminal. Brutalist monospaced terminal windows, hard rectangular borders, high-density grid, no bevels. Terminal monospace, hierarchy by spacing and capitals. Gold for player-owned and primary; cyan shield, amber hull as values; green/lavender minimal side indicators. Crest a tiny ASCII or monochrome emblem in the top bar. Hides icons and illustrative detail: everything is text, line and value. Weakness: austere, visually flat. Reference: BSG-inspired diegetic UI in Battlestar Galactica Deadlock (Reddit post). Grok rank 1.

5. Salvaged-Hardware Console. Patched plates, exposed wiring, duct-taped labels, mismatched panels bolted on over time. Mixed stamped and hand lettering, inconsistent sizes, scratched annotations. Gold accent; green/lavender as painted side markers. Crest a riveted, scuffed plate; identity by wear rather than colour. Hides polish and symmetry; panels occupy more space. Weakness: visual noise against the field and nameplates. Reference: Alien: Isolation diegetic UI (Reddit post). Grok rank 5.

6. Living Ship Membrane (wildcard). Organic rounded membrane panels with pulsing edges and soft internal glow, reined in by strict geometric grid divisions. Flowing irregular letterforms for headers, monospace numerals for data. Gold as bioluminescent marking; cyan/amber glowing fills; green/lavender membrane highlights. Crest a living emblem grown into the top bar. Hides mechanical framing and technical readouts. Weakness, in Grok's own words: conflicts most strongly with the hard-edged warship-console brief and could confuse the game's visual identity. Reference: Alien: Isolation diegetic UI (same Reddit post as direction 5). Grok rank 6.

Grok's ranking: 1 Cold Military Terminal, 2 Glass Cockpit, 3 Holographic Table, 4 Analog, 5 Salvaged, 6 Membrane.

Chair notes (added on filing, not part of the leg): Grok's five family directions track the example kinds named in its prompt, so they count as refinement and votes more than independent inventions; its one true invention is the Membrane wildcard. Its reference links are weak (two Reddit posts, a listicle, a vendor page); the reference study does not rely on them.
