# Flash ideation leg, issue #270 (from the browser run, Nov 8, 2026; gate verified: picker selected 3.8 Flash exactly, prompt bar read Flash before and after; ideation seat)

Note from the run: the account sidebar showed "Pro" under Ryan Bailey, and the picker offered 4 Argon as an option. Recorded for Ryan; the Flash gate for this leg was met regardless.

1. Vector C.I.C. (Combat Information Center Cathode Terminal). Bezel-bounded cathode display casing, 45-degree chamfered corners, rivet stampings, wireframe segment dividers; sub-panels simulate CRT phosphor bloom, raster sweep lines, faint barrel distortion. Fixed-width monospace (Share Tech Mono / VT323); numbers as segmented line glyphs; telemetry as formatted codes (TRK//04-CRUISER). Pitch black grounds (#0a0a0c); gold as glowing phosphor traces for selected routes, vector arrows, armed action buttons; hull and shield as stepped bar segments; enemy vectors lavender, friendlies green; bone at low luminance, muted red only at critical thresholds. Crest a monochrome vector wire-burn in the top bar, like an etched military stamp with serial numbering. Hides floating labels, portraits, persistent log (log becomes a single ticker line, expanding on toggle). Weakness: wireframe and raster clutter when ships cluster. Reference: NEBULOUS: Fleet Command (Steam). Flash rank 1 (least Godot cost, fits the locked colours, keeps the field uncluttered).

2. Physical Bridge Console (High-G Armored Helm). Stamped cold-rolled steel faceplates, matte black industrial bezels, screwheads, warning decals; the roster is a physical bank of recessed spring switches. Industrial stencilled DIN (DIN 1451, Chakra Petch), mechanical odometer numerals. Graphite panels (#1c1e22); backlit push-buttons glow gold for the player's own and primary; Heat is a physical needle-bar gauge tipping into red; shields and hull as backlit bargraph lamps. Crest a cast metal badge plate riveted into the top bulkhead, with serial number and a signal lamp. Hides floating hex highlights and world-space cards; the enemy phase is a large physical red lamp ring; log is a scrolling ticket strip. Weakness: heavy framing eats screen area at 1080p; dense panels harder to scan. Reference: HighFleet (Steam). Flash rank 2.

3. Tactical Holo-Plotter (Command Deck Volumetric Table). Zero physical frames; collimated light above a dark glass table: thin vector lines, scan rings, ghost images; the selected-ship panel is a rotating translucent carousel appearing only on selection. Geometric technical sans (Rajdhani, Eurostile Bold Extended), tight caps, tracked out, thin-stroke numerals. Transparent negative space; gold as concentrated glows for selection and the fire order; thin cyan and amber ring-bands around ships; lavender and green as faint light pylons. Crest a rotating micro-hologram in a glass bead emitter in the top bar, prism inverting its colour when the enemy phase starts. Hides solid containers and roster cards; reserve ships are grey wire ghosts; log a single-line holo strip. Weakness: legibility against bright nebulae and explosions, thin glow at 1080p. Reference: Homeworld Remastered / Deserts of Kharak Sensors Array (Steam). Flash rank 4.

4. Cold USCM Combat Terminal (1980s Retro-Milspec). Severe low-bandwidth military display; thick matte olive/charcoal border blocks, no radii, no glow; the hex field as a dashed wire grid; panels as low-res pixel blocks. Dot-matrix and bitmap fonts (MicroSquare, Px437 IBM VGA); abbreviated telemetry columns (HDG, VEL, PWR-SHLD). Phosphor bone on slate-black (#0f1112); gold only for armed keys and the targeting reticle (F1 FIRE, F2 BURN, F3 BRACE); bars as blocky character segments. Crest a 2-bit monochrome bitmap in a fixed 24x24 box, a degraded logo file. Hides nameplate health bars entirely; nameplates are call-sign text lines only, detail lives in the terminal module. Weakness: austerity, abbreviation learning curve. Reference: Aliens (1986), APC console and Hadley's Hope ops screens. Flash rank 3. (Chair note on filing: removing nameplate bars breaks a locked spec rule; this direction is screened out as a standalone and its texture is noted inside the Vector C.I.C. family.)

5. Stripped Scrapper Rig (Salvaged Cockpit Telemetry). Asymmetric spliced hardware: mismatched monitor brackets, weld seams, bolted auxiliary panels, every block a different bezel and tilt. Mixed type: condensed grotesque plus handwritten stencil and spray-painted numbers. Patinated metal and grease tones; gold as a hand-wired circuit trace or taped PRIORITY label; gauges mismatched and dented; green and lavender as cable ties, LEDs, stencil tags. Crest a weathered stencil plate welded into the top casing, scraped and heat-discoloured. Hides unified organisation; action buttons not uniform; roster a rack of mismatched CRT tiles; log a taped thermal-paper strip in handwriting. Weakness: fatigue and mess if Godot theme scaling is not rigorous. References: Battlestar Galactica (2004) CIC, Hardspace: Shipbreaker (Steam). Flash rank 5.

6. Wildcard: The Admiralty Astronavigation Parchment (Ironclad Starmap). Brass and blued-steel gimbals framing a dark drafting-slate table with a central star map; rivet patterns, knurled brass dials, a pivot-mounted ranging compass. Classical engineering serif (IM Fell English, Playfair Display), hand-engraved mono numerals. Indigo/slate table (#10141a); gold as hand-inlaid brass lines, compass arcs, the illuminated brass route-line of the player's own course; hull and shield as inked wax-seal bars; green and lavender only as wax seals and flag pins. Crest an embossed sovereign seal die-struck into a brass cap at top centre, with a chain that swings when the enemy phase begins. Hides every sci-fi UI trope: no scanlines, no glow shapes, no blinking; ships are brass markers with hand-drawn vectors; the log is a formal war diary in italic serif. Weakness: archaic, polarising, fine drafting letters may fail at 1080p. References: Sunless Skies, Battlefleet Gothic: Armada 2 (Steam). Flash rank 6.

Flash's ranking: 1 Vector C.I.C., 2 Physical Bridge Console, 3 Cold USCM, 4 Holo-Plotter, 5 Scrapper Rig, 6 Admiralty Parchment.
