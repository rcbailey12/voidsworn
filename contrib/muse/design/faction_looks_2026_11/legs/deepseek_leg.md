The Glasswrights

Species base / People

Species base: Threaded A. Tall, slender, long necked organic beings of pale half translucent flesh. Fine crystal threads are woven through the flesh like fibre optics under the skin, so the body reads as a person first. Glass and glass like matter are grown and woven into living tissue for the prismatic look, but they are not literally glass and not crystal constructs. One admiral may use Bloomed C: crystal clusters growing outward at joints, spine and crown like frost flowers. Do not assemble the being from separate substances.

Face: two forward grown lens eyes set side by side, deep green, flush in the flesh. Lids and a heavy segmented brow carry expression. A short reed grille sits flush low in the face as the speaking mouth. Speech is a normal voice. Colour in the glass is an involuntary emotion tell only, never speech. No faceless look and no colour pulse speech.

Age runs young to old: rooted, near Starwright faces with craft grown lens eyes in the young and deep lens eye infusion in the old. They are markedly less estranged from their Starwright roots than Eclipse. They live 300 to 400 years. They are the Starwrights who stayed flesh, the Lattice optical engineers who tried to stop the Upload and failed.

Body and craft: four arms, two working and two precision, with wrist spinnerets that draw and weave glass thread onto the workpiece. A little spider sits in the carriage: segmented thorax, low wide weaver stance, support limbs. A full spider body is too monstrous for the base and possible only as an ancient elder. Long tapering fingers. Layered crystal and mirror thread fabrics. Small light cores worn as jewellery.

Prism Sails: grown crystal sail vanes echo the ships clear crystal and silver vanes. They are practical, modest in size, jointed with tendon and cartilage, grown and with no hardware. They furl at the bench, and one edge focuses light onto the work. These are working glasswrights shown at their craft. Age and admiral variety live in the sail plan: larger, bloom edged and worn for elders.

Ships

Space ships only. Clear crystal and silver hulls with faceted edges that split light into rainbow fringes. Mirrors and lenses turn to catch light. Crystal vanes and stored light glowing deep inside. Hull form uses armoured flat bellies with chines, sensor housings, antenna arrays, equipment pods, recessed lights, main engine bells, thruster pods, docking hatches and heat radiators. Parts sit on visible mounts. Guns are in matched symmetric pairs at sensible hard points, aimed forward or out to the side, never backward. Shields keep faction colours.

The faction flies the caged core A-4 Astra to hunt the other five cores and shut them all down, Astra last. Fleet names: Glasswright sliver frigate, Glasswright lancet destroyer, Glasswright prism cruiser, Glasswright aperture carrier, and The Unfinished Ark flagship built around the largest surviving piece of the Lattice. Weapons: Prism lance, Prism shards, Needle beam, Star torpedo, Mirror veil. Effects: white core Prism lance beams with rainbow fringe, pale green white stored light, silver white shield flicker. The shipped style word sail means crystal vanes and grown jointed vanes, not literal naval sails on a mast and not rigging.

Ashfall Hullbreakers

Species base / People

Species base: Knuckle Stalker. Huge hunting beasts, wolf and bear inspired. Big shoulder hump, thick forearms, heavy claws. Coarse mane over hump, neck and shoulders only, in grey, black or rust brown. Everywhere else tough dark hide. The base is low and wide: knuckles down at rest, shoulder hump above the head.

Face: bare plated horn. Short, blunt, overlapping plates of dull horn. No fur on the face, no snout, no nose. The jaw splits down the middle of the chin into two halves that open sideways, lined with teeth. Four small eyes across the brow, shining in the dark, with heat sensing pits beside them. Ridged plates down the back and shoulders. R64 void hunter look: bear heavy, mane only, bare plated face, sideways jaw, four small eyes, heat pits, ridged back plates.

Posture by character: Grax upright in challenge, Old Mott low and wide, Sable low and skulking, Vesh upright over trophies. The Warlord is always upright. Direction 2 Den alpha Upright is allowed for Warlord portraits. One hero trophy per portrait, and trophies are machines and hull plates, never skulls or bodies. Scars are re-derived from each new base. Not bears with gear. Not the film Predator: no quill dreadlocks, no mouth tusks, no helmet look, no dreadlocks, no skull necklaces, no mask face.

Ships

Space ships, not naval ships. Movement is slow and deliberate. Matte soot black and rust hulls, lopsided, few windows, dim red running lights, red orange engine glow. Big listening dishes and wake sniffer scoops at the bow. Boarding claws. Chains between hull sections. Haphazard and junky is on brand. Parts sit on visible mounts, and guns are aimed forward or out to the side, never backward. No lances at all. Battle trophy plates on Hullbreaker ships in battle are flagship only.

Fleet names: Hullbreaker raider frigate, Hullbreaker gutship destroyer, Hullbreaker wrecker cruiser, Hullbreaker hulk carrier, and Warlords Pyre Barge Battle barge flagship, with the name Pyre Barge kept. Weapons: Autocannon, Scrap scatter gun, Junk turret, Rattle rocket pod, Heavy rocket pod, Chaff guns. Effects: red orange engine glow, dull red tracers, white red rocket exhaust. Shields are thin and keep faction colours. Do not write fast sleek ships.

The package says plates torn from every faction are bolted on in old colours. R78 Q5 says battle trophy plates on Hullbreaker ships in battle are flagship only, so non-flagship ship prompts must not show battle trophy plates.
The package directions include Long Skulker as direction 3, but the chair pick is Knuckle Stalker as the one species base, with Den alpha Upright allowed for Warlord portraits. Use Knuckle Stalker as the base and keep Long Skulker out of the locked base.
