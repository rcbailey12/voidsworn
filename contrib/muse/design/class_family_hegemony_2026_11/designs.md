# Hegemony Navy class family: chair designs (issue 1146)

Chair: Rey, from a blind Rey leg written first, then DeepSeek and Gemini
legs (the Gemini seat ran on Hydrogen, the disclosed picker deviation of
record). Qwen was blocked at its daily Qwen3.8-Max limit and Grok by the
cloud bot wall; both seats are recorded as blocked, and the delivery stands
without them. Fit order throughout: data.js first, then the faction sheet,
then the anchor builds, then the pictures. Where a picture and a table
disagree, the table governs. Picture slips are listed per ship.

## The family, from the anchors

Anchors: H10 Dominion (battleship, 21.0, data key h_dominion) and IM5 Iron
Mandate (super-dreadnought, 31.0), both built. The family is a pale wedge
with a line of blue light: a heavy machined wedge on twin rails, about 3:1,
the same machined parts repeated at different sizes across the fleet.
Sameness is the family law here, where other factions vary by class.

Never change across the family:
1. The blunt bow face and chamfered wedge: flat deck, vertical flank band
   carrying the implant-blue light line, flat worked belly. Never a pointed
   prow, never prongs.
2. The ion weapon, where data fits one, is a recessed emitter trench in the
   blunt bow face: blue lens, stepped capacitor rings, inset muzzle lip.
   Capacitor ring count scales with hull size: Cordon 3, Vanguard 4,
   Dominion 6.
3. Guns sit low and sunk: armoured mounts in shoulder or centreline
   terraces, roofs barely over deck, short thick barrels. One low sensor
   fin on the centreline, never a tower. Engines are a flat row of
   rectangular blue-white nozzle pods at the stern. Breaks fall at the
   bulkhead rings between modules; breach anchors sit on the module joints.

Never copy from the anchors:
1. Dominion's exact terrace station band (38 to 46 percent) and its
   shoulder-bay beam as a default for smaller hulls.
2. The six-ring trench and the four-nozzle row as fleet defaults; smaller
   hulls step both down (see the ring ladder above; Picket and Cordon carry
   two nozzle pods).
3. Dominion's exact rail gap and drone-block layout; the Vanguard's bay is
   deliberately longer, the escorts carry flush flank doors only.

Weapon conventions (from H10's weapon match):
- A turret slot is ONE low terrace mount. Where a hull carries two turret
  slots, the mounts mirror port and starboard at the same station (the
  Vanguard, as Dominion's two heavy slots sit one a side). Where a hull
  carries one turret slot, the mount sits on the centreline (the Cordon).
- A forward gun that is not the ion cannon is a low casemate in the bow
  face, barrels visible, no rings (the Picket).
- The flak slot on the smallest hull is one low dorsal barbette with three
  short emitters, one per shot (the Picket; chair took the dorsal mount
  both external legs proposed over a scaled belly pair).
- Shots are volleys distributed over the mount's barrels and emitters.

## PICKET (frigate, data key h_picket, old code H7_23)

Length 6.5, beam 2.2. hp 40, shields 10, speed 5, evasion 18, ability
evasive. The smallest machined wedge: a compressed heavy blade on two stub
rails, the fleet's screen, the first Navy hull a new player flies beside.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Compact wedge, blunt bow face, twin stub rails aft |
| Plasma cannon F (laser, 2 shots) | Low casemate in the bow face below the blue line, station 5 to 15 percent; two short thick barrels side by side; NOT the ion trench, no capacitor rings |
| Picket lasers T (flak, 3 shots) | One low faceted dorsal barbette at 52 percent, three short emitters, sunk so its roof barely clears the deck |
| Sensor fin | One, low, 1.2 long, at 40 percent |
| Engines | Two rectangular nozzle pods in one flat stern row, one per rail |
| Drone doors | Flush flank doors only (faction small drone bays; no hangar in data) |
| Break lines | Bulkhead rings at 35 and 70 percent |

Picture slips: the beauty seats the casemate barrels on the deck edge
rather than inside the bow face, and the stern glow reads as four plumes;
the table's two pods govern. The sheet's top view repeats the four-plume
stern. The fin and the barbette merge into one lump in the beauty; the
sheet separates them.

## CORDON (destroyer, data key h_interdictor, old code H8_23)

Length 9.5, beam 3.2. hp 74, shields 18, speed 4, evasion 10, ability
ionpulse. The interdictor: the ion trench is its face, and the twin-rail
silhouette starts here at escort scale.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Wedge, wide blunt bow face, twin rails aft with a clear gap |
| Ion cannon F (1 heavy shot) | Recessed emitter trench in the bow face, about 1.1 by 0.6, blue lens, THREE stepped capacitor rings, inset lip |
| Plasma lance T (turbo, 2 shots) | ONE low turret on a centreline terrace at 48 percent, twin short thick barrels (one slot, centreline; all three legs converged here) |
| Sensor fin | One, low, at 38 percent, forward of the turret so it clears the firing arc |
| Engines | Two rectangular nozzle pods, one at the end of each rail |
| Drone doors | Flush flank doors only |
| Break lines | Bulkhead rings at 32 and 66 percent |

Picture slips: the beauty's stern glow again reads as several plumes; the
sheet's side view draws a dark gap through the hull at the rail split. The
trench, fin and centreline turret all read as tabled.

## VANGUARD (cruiser, data key h_vanguard, old code H9_23)

Length 14.5, beam 4.8. hp 140, shields 24, speed 3, evasion 4, ability
launch, hangar 1. H10's architecture at cruiser scale, and the fleet's
working line ship: the drone bay is this hull's identity.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Broad wedge, twin rails aft, armoured bay block between the rail roots |
| Ion cannon F (1 shot) | Recessed bow trench, about 1.4 by 0.8, blue lens, FOUR stepped capacitor rings |
| Plasma lance T slot 1 (turbo, 2 shots) | Low turret in the port shoulder terrace at 42 percent, twin short barrels |
| Plasma lance T slot 2 (turbo, 2 shots) | Low turret in the starboard shoulder terrace at 42 percent, mirrored (two slots, one a side, as Dominion's heavy pair) |
| Hangar (1 wing) | Bay block between the rail roots, stations 58 to 76 percent, flush armoured doors in its roof |
| Sensor fin | One, low, 2.0 long, at 47 percent, between the terraces as on Dominion |
| Engines | Four rectangular nozzle pods in one flat row, two per rail |
| Break lines | Bulkhead rings at 30, 55 and 80 percent |

Chair note: the blind leg staggered the two turrets (40 and 58 percent);
both external legs and the Dominion precedent put them mirrored at one
station, and the chair took the mirrored pair. The faction sheet's small
flank drone bays give way here to the data's full hangar wing, carried in
the rail-root block exactly as Dominion carries its own.

Picture slips: the beauty's turrets read a touch tall and round against
the sunk ideal; the hangar roof doors are not legible in the beauty (they
read in the sheet's top view). The trench in both pictures is the stepped
ring emitter the family wants.

## CONVOY TRANSPORT (objective, old code O2_37)

Transport class, length 11.0, beam 3.7. No weapons in data. The thing being
escorted, readable as defenseless at a glance: the wedge and rails are
fleet parts, the mass is cargo.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Plain wedge bow, long twin rails extended aft |
| Bow face | Solid armoured bumper plate with docking clamps; NO trench, NO gun, no terrace |
| Cargo | FOUR identical container modules locked in a row between the rails, plain slab sides, mooring clamps; the blue line runs through the row |
| Sensor fin | One, low, at 25 percent, ahead of the cargo row |
| Engines | Four rectangular nozzle pods in one flat row, standard fleet part |
| Break lines | Module rings at 28, 50 and 72 percent; a loaded module is its own break piece |

Chair note: beam holds the family's 3:1 at 3.7; one leg argued 4.2 for
cargo readability and the chair kept the law, since the four repeated
modules already carry the read.

Picture slips: the beauty shows three container modules on deck, the sheet
four; the table's four govern. The sheet's third view is the stern, not the
bow; the bow's blank face reads in the beauty and the sheet's side view.

## RELAY BEACON (objective station, old code O3_11)

No class length exists in data; the size below is a PROPOSAL for the Board
to lock against the capture-zone radius. Not a ship: a fixed relay built
from fleet modules, readable as an objective because it has no propulsion
row and no prow.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Form | Open twin-rail cradle clamped around a central relay module (DeepSeek's form, chaired over a horizontal spine design and a vertical pylon alternative) |
| Relay lens | ONE oversized recessed blue lens in the module's forward face, stepped machined rings, NO muzzle lip and no capacitor trench; a thin blue ring at its base pulses in time with the fleet |
| Size (proposal) | Footprint 7.5 long, cradle span 4.8 |
| Sensor fin | One, low, on the module |
| Docking | Four mooring collars on the rails |
| Propulsion | None; small station-keeping thruster pads on the module's sides only |
| Break lines | The cradle joints and the relay-ring module joints; it breaks into modules, not a spine |

Picture slips: in the sheet's top view the rails read as bars passing the
module rather than a cradle under it; the beauty and the sheet's front view
carry the cradle read. The lens in both pictures is deliberately larger
than any ion trench in the fleet.
