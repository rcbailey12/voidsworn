# Hegemony Navy: image prompts (issue 1146)

Generation pattern: each beauty was generated image-to-image from the H10
Dominion three-quarter anchor render, then each sheet image-to-image from
its own beauty. Prompts recorded as sent; the sheet prompts all follow one
template with the ship's feature list swapped in.

## picket_beauty (reference: H10 threequarter)

Using the attached picture as the reference for design language, material
and rendering style (pale grey machined armour plate, flush panels and
straight seams, a thin glowing implant-blue light line along the flank, a
heavy wedge warship on twin aft rails): draw a much SMALLER frigate of the
same fleet, a single spaceship in deep space, three-quarter view from the
front and above, bow pointing left. A compact thick wedge hull with a
blunt bow face: set low in the bow face is a shallow casemate with two
short thick gun barrels side by side. One small low sensor fin blade on
the centreline deck. One low wide flat turret mount on the dorsal deck
amidships with three tiny short emitter barrels. The aft hull narrows into
two short rails, and the stern carries two rectangular engine nozzles with
blue-white glow in one flat row. The thin blue light line runs unbroken
along the flank from bow to stern. No tower, no mast, no ornament, no
rust. Painterly realistic rendering. No text, no other ships.

## cordon_beauty (reference: H10 threequarter)

Same style preamble. A destroyer of the same fleet, bigger than a frigate
and smaller than the flagship: a thick wedge hull with a wide blunt bow
face; recessed into the bow face is a rectangular emitter trench with a
glowing blue lens at its back and stepped rings around it. One small low
sensor fin blade on the centreline deck near the bow. One low wide turret
mount sunk into a flat terrace on the centreline deck amidships, with two
short thick barrels. The aft hull splits into two clear rails with a gap
between them, and the stern carries two rectangular engine nozzles with
blue-white glow, one at the end of each rail. The thin blue light line
runs unbroken along the flank. No tower, no mast, no ornament, no rust.

## vanguard_beauty (reference: H10 threequarter)

Same style preamble. A cruiser of the same fleet: a broad thick wedge hull
with a wide blunt bow face; recessed into the bow face is a rectangular
emitter trench with a glowing blue lens and stepped rings. Two low wide
turret mounts sit sunk in stepped terraces on the left and right shoulders
of the deck, each with two short thick barrels. One small low sensor fin
blade on the centreline between the turrets. The aft hull splits into two
rails with a long armoured block between their roots, its roof carrying
flush rectangular hangar doors. The stern carries four rectangular engine
nozzles with blue-white glow in one flat row. The thin blue light line
runs unbroken along the flank. No tower, no mast, no ornament, no rust.

## convoy_beauty (reference: H10 threequarter)

Same style preamble (modular blocks on twin rails): draw an UNARMED CARGO
TRANSPORT of the same fleet. A plain wedge bow with a completely blank
blunt bow face: no gun, no emitter, no trench. Behind the bow, four
identical large rectangular cargo container modules sit in a row locked
between two long rails, plain slab sides with mooring clamps, the thin
blue light line running through them. One small low sensor fin blade on
the bow deck. NO turrets anywhere. The stern carries four rectangular
engine nozzles with blue-white glow in one flat row. It must read at a
glance as a defenseless freighter, not a warship.

## relay_beauty (reference: H10 threequarter)

Same style preamble: draw a FIXED RELAY STATION of the same fleet, not a
ship, floating in deep space, three-quarter view from the front and above.
Two short parallel pale-grey rails form an open cradle, and clamped
between them sits a central module block whose front face carries ONE huge
recessed circular relay lens glowing bright blue, ringed by stepped
machined rings, much larger than any weapon. Four docking clamp collars
grip the rails. One small low sensor fin blade on the block. NO engines,
NO nozzle row, NO weapons, NO turrets: only small dark thruster pads on
the sides. A thin glowing blue ring pulses around the lens base. It must
read at a glance as a stationary beacon that ships fight over.

## family_lineup (reference: vanguard beauty)

Using the attached picture as the reference for design language, material
and rendering style: draw a FAMILY LINE-UP CHART of six vessels of this
fleet in strict side profile, all bows pointing left, floating in a row on
a flat dark background, drawn at TRUE RELATIVE SCALE, smallest at the top
and largest at the bottom: (1) a tiny frigate, compact wedge, small bow
gun casemate, one dorsal turret; (2) a small boxy relay station, a central
block with a huge glowing blue circular lens clamped between two long
rails, no engines; (3) a destroyer, wedge with a glowing blue emitter
trench in its bow face and one centreline turret; (4) an unarmed cargo
transport, plain wedge bow carrying four big container boxes, no weapons;
(5) a cruiser, broad wedge with a bow emitter trench and two shoulder
turrets; (6) a huge battleship, the same cruiser design grown much larger,
the flagship. The largest ship is more than three times as long as the
smallest. No text, no labels, no size numbers.
