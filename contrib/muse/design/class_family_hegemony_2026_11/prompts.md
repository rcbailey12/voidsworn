# Generation prompts (issue 1146, run 3)

The run 3 pictures were generated by editing the run 1 pictures
image-to-image where the details were right and changing the outline,
per the run 3 brief. The silhouette checks were drawn programmatically
from the tables in designs.md, not generated. The exact briefs given
to the image model, trimmed of repetition:

## picket_beauty.webp (edited from run 1 picket_beauty.webp, two passes)

Pass 1: Redraw with a completely new hull outline, keeping the pale
machined grey plate, flush panels, steel-blue trim, one glowing
implant-blue light line and the three-quarter bow view. A small
frigate, a single compact solid body shaped like a short dart: NO
twin rails, NO forked stern, NO gap anywhere. Blunt flat bow face,
widest just aft of middle, one flat stern block. In the bow face, a
laser casemate: two short thick barrels side by side INSIDE the bow
face. One low faceted dorsal barbette with three short emitters just
aft of middle. One low sensor fin forward of the barbette. Exactly
TWO rectangular engine nozzle pods side by side at the stern.

Pass 2 (the delivered picture): make the hull much shorter and more
compact, a stubby dart about three times as long as wide, widest just
aft of middle; shorten the bow barrels so they sit recessed INSIDE
the bow face as two short stubby muzzles; flatten the dorsal mount
into a low faceted barbette whose roof barely rises above the deck.

## cordon_beauty.webp (edited from run 1 cordon_beauty.webp)

Redraw with a completely new hull outline, same family finish. A
destroyer in two visibly joined modules: the forward third a deep,
wide bow block with parallel slab sides and a tall blunt bow face,
in its centre a recessed ion emitter trench, a glowing blue lens
framed by three stepped rectangular capacitor rings. At one third of
the length the hull steps sharply inward and downward in one clean
vertical step to a narrower, shallower single aft body (no rails, no
fork, no gap). ONE low centreline turret with two short thick barrels
on the aft body. One low sensor fin just behind the step. Exactly TWO
rectangular nozzle pods side by side at the stern.

## vanguard_beauty.webp (edited from run 1 vanguard_beauty.webp)

Redraw with a completely new hull outline, same family finish. A
cruiser built as a catamaran: a solid bow block spanning the full
width, in its blunt face a recessed ion trench, a glowing blue lens
framed by four stepped rectangular capacitor rings. Behind it two
long parallel rails with a wide open slot of empty space between
them. Broad shoulder terraces widen the rails just behind the bow
block, each with ONE low turret with two short thick barrels,
mirrored. Between the rails further aft, a solid armoured hangar
block with flush rectangular roof doors; the rails continue past it
to the stern. One low sensor fin between the turrets. Exactly FOUR
rectangular nozzle pods in one row at the stern, two per rail.

## The three sheets

Each sheet was generated from its delivered beauty with the same
instruction, ship description filled from the tables: a technical
three-view concept sheet on one page, flat neutral dark grey
background, three orthographic views of the SAME ship at the SAME
scale (three-quarter 45 degrees off the bow toward port, 30 degrees
above, bow left; true side, port, 0 degrees, bow left; true top,
directly overhead, bow up the page), a 5-unit scale bar on the side
and top views, and a small printed feature table from designs.md.

## family_lineup.webp (generated, second attempt kept)

Six spaceships in a row against a black space background at the same
relative scale, longest at the left, each described by outline only,
with an explicit ban on sea-ship features (the first attempt drew a
naval hull with a conning tower and was discarded). Drawn scale is
approximate; silhouette_check_scale.webp carries the true scale.

## Silhouette checks (drawn, not generated)

silhouette_check_top.webp, silhouette_check_side.webp and
silhouette_check_scale.webp were drawn with a small Python script
from the outline geometry tabled in designs.md (run 3 rows) and the
run 1 designs (comparison rows), black on white.
