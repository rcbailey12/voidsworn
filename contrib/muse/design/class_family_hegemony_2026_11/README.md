# Hegemony Navy class family (issue 1146)

Concept designs for the five remaining old Navy hulls, derived from the
built anchors H10 Dominion and IM5 Iron Mandate, so Ryan can pick a
three-view sheet and feature table for each ship. Pictures and design text
only; nothing here is a build sheet.

Files:
- designs.md: the chair's feature table for each ship, the family weapon
  conventions, the never-change and never-copy lists, picture slips, and
  what could not be verified. Start here.
- prompts.md: the image prompts used, for regeneration.
- picket_beauty.webp, picket_sheet.webp: the Picket frigate (h_picket).
- cordon_beauty.webp, cordon_sheet.webp: the Cordon destroyer
  (h_interdictor).
- vanguard_beauty.webp, vanguard_sheet.webp: the Vanguard cruiser
  (h_vanguard).
- convoy_beauty.webp, convoy_sheet.webp: the Convoy transport objective.
- relay_beauty.webp, relay_sheet.webp: the Relay beacon objective.
- family_lineup.webp: all six hulls in profile at relative scale, the
  Dominion included as the anchor. The chart compresses the true length
  spread (6.5 to 21.0); the tables carry the real lengths.

Council: blind Rey leg first, then DeepSeek and Gemini legs, Rey chairing
(R89, R231). The Gemini seat ran on Hydrogen; the picker offers no Flash
label, the same disclosed deviation as issues 1141 and 1145 and the Neon
audit council. Qwen was blocked at its daily Qwen3.8-Max limit (brief
parked for a one-message retry) and Grok by the cloud bot wall; both seats
are recorded as blocked, not empty, and the pack stands without them.

Chair headline calls: the Cordon's single turret sits on the centreline
(all legs converged); the Vanguard's two turret slots mirror port and
starboard at one station, as Dominion's heavy pair does; the ion trench
ring ladder steps 3, 4, 6 from destroyer to battleship; the Relay is an
open rail cradle with an oversized lens, not a ship and not a tower; the
Convoy keeps the family's 3:1 beam and lets its four cargo modules carry
the objective read.
