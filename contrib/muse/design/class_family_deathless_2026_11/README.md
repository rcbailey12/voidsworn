## How the picks were made

Legs ran first (blind Rey, DeepSeek, Grok, Flash; Qwen's seat empty on its daily cap, chat left ready). The chaired text designs (a scythe mote from DeepSeek's leg, a split-shade flare) went to pictures and Ryan ruled on the pictures:

- The scythe mote: rejected, "isn't really eclipse themed. It looks comical."
- The split-shade flare, four blade rounds: rejected, the last as not fitting the theme at all.
- Ryan named the round 1 Annulus direction (deathless_C) for the flare: "The flare is what I was thinking of for the annulus." Accepted as pictured (E14 v6).
- Annulus takes for the mote: rejected, the mote felt the same as the flare. Ryan: "The mote should go a different route entirely but within the eclipse theme."
- The mote took the round 1 Fan direction (deathless_B; Ryan in R172: "Fan is definitely good"), accepted as pictured (E13 v6) on 2026-11-12.

## Build notes

- Pictures guide, build sheets enforce (R207). Two generator slips in the accepted flare beauty are conformed in designs.md: the caged core is green-white in the build, not the teal the beauty drew (colour fixed in build per R204), and the forward gun is recessed in its housing, not the protruding tube the beauty drew.
- The mote beauty and sheet underplay its single gun; designs.md places it on the spine at the fan's root and the build follows the text.
- The mote sheet's first pass drew the front view as a pinwheel; it was regenerated so the front reads as an overlapping fan stack. The filed sheet is the corrected pass.
- The line-up's drawn scale is approximate (the flagship draws slightly small against the cruiser); designs.md carries the true relative game lengths (28.0 / 14.5 / 9.5 / 6.5).
