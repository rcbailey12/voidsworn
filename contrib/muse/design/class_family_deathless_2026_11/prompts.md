# Issue 627 image prompts (chaired by Rey)

The accepted pair came from Ryan's rulings on 2026-11-12, not from the chaired text designs: he rejected the scythe and the blade rounds, named the round 1 Annulus (deathless_C) for the flare, ruled the mote must take a different route entirely within the theme, and accepted the round 1 Fan (deathless_B) for the mote. Both beauties are image-to-image from their round 1 direction pictures so the plate, light and rendering stay locked to the family's origin art.

## E13 mote, the Fan (reference: deathless_B_fan.jpg, the round 1 Fan direction)

Using the attached picture's fan ship as the design and style reference (long overlapping dark violet-black shade plates fanned back over the body, a thin green-white glowing line on every plate rim, a bright core glowing in a gap between the plates, an eclipse ring of pale light at the stern, deep space, painterly realistic rendering), draw a NEW small frigate starship in that same fan design, alone, the whole ship in frame, three-quarter view from the front and above. The ship is a short dart-shaped body almost hidden under five long overlapping shade plates: the plates are rooted together at the bow and fan out and sweep back over the body like the blades of a hand fan, their tips reaching past the body's sides toward the stern, each plate a separate layered slab with its own thin green-white rim line. A small bright green-white point of trapped light glows in a gap between the plates near their root. Exactly one small low gun housing sits on the body's spine at the fan's root. At the stern a small thin eclipse ring of pale light encircles the tail. No gunfire, no sparks, no other ships in frame. The ship reads small, quick and sleek: a fan of dark plates with a bright heart, built for speed.

## E14 flare, the Annulus (reference: deathless_C_annulus.jpg, the round 1 Annulus direction)

Using the attached picture's ring ship as the design reference (a broken ring of large curved dark violet-black plates with clear gaps between them, thin green-white glowing lines on the plates' shade-facing rims, a bright core floating at the centre, a dark layered body behind the ring, an eclipse ring of pale light at the stern, deep space with a few far stars, painterly realistic rendering), draw a NEW destroyer starship, alone, the whole ship in frame, three-quarter view from the front and above. It is bigger and heavier than a frigate: a broad broken ring of fewer, larger curved shade plates, and at the centre of the ring a layered spar body carries the ship's heart: a bright core of trapped light held in a broken cage of small dark plates, and beside the cage a short dark gun housing whose round aperture faces forward with a thin green ring inside it. One low gun housing sits on top of the spar behind the ring. Two tiny repair drones with green lights hover in the ring's gaps. At the stern a broad eclipse ring of pale light encircles the tail. No gunfire, no orange light, no other ships in frame.

Build conforms noted in designs.md: the filed beauty drew the caged core teal and the forward gun as a protruding tube; the build draws the core green-white (R204) and the gun recessed in its housing.
