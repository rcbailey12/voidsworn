## IM4: twin hulls, the channel as the gap

Two full wedge hulls run parallel for their whole length, joined by three low armoured bridge decks (bow, midships, stern), flush with the hull tops. The gap between the hulls is IM2's channel: a deep recessed armoured trench with machinery blocks on its floor, its walls the hulls' inner faces. From above (IM4_top.jpg) the silhouette is two long rails bridged three times around a dark central trench.

Weapon callouts (h_boss slots only; the picture shows exactly these):

- hturbo P (Heavy pulse laser, battery, 3 shots): one low armoured sunk mount with three short thick barrels on the PORT hull's shoulder.
- hturbo S (Heavy pulse laser, battery, 3 shots): the matching mount on the STARBOARD hull's shoulder.
- ion F (Ion cannon, lance, 1 shot): the recessed emitter trench in the channel floor at the bow end; it fires forward out of the channel's mouth, framed by the two bow faces.
- turbo T (Pulse laser, battery, 2 shots): the low turret on the midships bridge deck, centreline.
- flak T (Flak battery, 3 shots): the short-barrel cluster on the stern bridge deck, centreline.
- Sensor fin: one low fin on the midships bridge, centreline (faction symmetry).
- Engines: each hull's stern ends in a flat row of rectangular blue-white nozzles, the two rows aligned on one transverse datum so the split stern reads as one array (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted).
- No hangar and no drone bays: the data has none. Ability nova has no mount.

## IM5: one hull, the bridged canyon

One broad wedge hull. Two raised armoured shoulder ridges run its full length and IM2's channel is cut deep between them as an armoured canyon, machinery visible on its floor, spanned by three low bridge decks flush with the ridge tops. From above (IM5_top.jpg) the ship is unmistakably one vessel: a single wedge outline with a dark central gorge crossed three times.

Weapon callouts (h_boss slots only; the picture shows exactly these):

- hturbo P and hturbo S (Heavy pulse laser, battery, 3 shots each): low armoured sunk mounts with three short thick barrels on the port and starboard outer shoulders, mirrored.
- ion F (Ion cannon, lance, 1 shot): the recessed emitter trench across the bow face's centre, at the canyon's mouth.
- turbo T (Pulse laser, battery, 2 shots): the low turret on the midships bridge, centreline.
- flak T (Flak battery, 3 shots): the short-barrel cluster on the stern bridge, centreline.
- Sensor fin: one low fin on the bow deck ahead of the canyon mouth, centreline (DeepSeek and Flash both placed it forward, independently; adopted).
- Engines: one flat row of rectangular blue-white nozzles across the single stern, between the ridge roots.
- No hangar and no drone bays: the data has none. Ability nova has no mount.

## The chair's pick: IM5

Unanimous among the seated legs, and the chair agrees. IM5 keeps IM2's channel as the dominant top-down feature while the silhouette stays singular from every angle; there is no formation read to mitigate, the stern is one array, and the canyon gives the Board a buildable interior structure (bridges, machinery floor, canyon walls) inside a hull whose outline stays pure Hegemony wedge. IM4 is the bolder picture and its top view is genuinely striking; it stays a full option if Ryan wants the Mandate to read as a split vessel. The risk register from the legs: IM4's formation read (mitigated by flush structural bridges, shared channel machinery and the aligned stern datum, but not eliminable in a still), and both readings' bridge decks drifting naval if they rise above the hull line (they are kept low and flush in both).

## Production notes (render drift the sheet must correct, not copy)

- In both top views the heavy batteries render on the hulls' outer FLANKS with barrels pointing sideways, not as dorsal shoulder mounts. The design intent is the faction's shoulder language (sunk mounts on the shoulders, as the three-quarter renders show); the sheet decides the final placement against the P and S arcs.
- IM4's flak cluster renders on the starboard hull's deck near the stern rather than on the stern bridge; the design places it on the stern bridge, centreline.
- IM4's ion emitter renders as a housed aperture in the bow bridge rather than a trench in the channel floor; the design intent is the trench.
- IM4's sensor fin sits midships in the three-quarter render and aft in the top view; the design fixes it on the midships bridge.
- IM5's bow reads rounder in the top view than the wedge the faction wants; the sheet sharpens it.
- Canyon and channel depths, bridge thicknesses and seam layouts are render-stage values; the blockout settles them (both design legs flagged this honestly).
