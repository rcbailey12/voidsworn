# Concepts round 8c: Sunward S10, Eclipse E11 and E12, Glasswright G18

Refs #522. Follow-on to rounds 8 (issue 515) and 8b (issue 517). The four ships Ryan did not pass in round 8 (FINDINGS R201), redrawn in the one concept style (R197: corsair_A.jpg, the round 1 Scrap Brick) by image-to-image editing under R192.

## What changed in the method, and why

Round 8 edited the whole picture at once and, on these four, the edit only polished: the smooth, flat-lit source anchored every call. This round ran the blind leg's method (leg_rey.md):

- M1, section first. A tight crop of each source was edited into the target surface on its own and gated before any full picture was attempted. A crop that only polishes is rejected early, where the failure is cheap.
- M2, style input first in every beauty call, then the shape picture, then the accepted crop or crops as same-ship surface references.
- M3, prompts written as inventories of physical parts, not adjectives.
- M4, colour locks stated per surface.
- M5, an escalation ladder, used twice (below).
- M6, for the Glasswright weapons, geometry language only.

Result: all five metal crops transformed on try 1, and three of the four beauties took their crop's surface at try 1. The method, not stronger adjectives, is what round 8 was missing.

## The gate

The twelve lines of docs/playbooks/concepts_round2.md, including line 11's naval pass (no boat profile, a worked belly, no deck house, no ship's prow, nothing hanging off, no battleship turrets), plus the style line: a photograph of a real, very large built object, not an illustration, a toy or a game icon; many separate small plates, panels and fittings giving the ship its size, with seams, wear and repairs; no large flat single-colour area; real materials under one hard light on plain black space with a few faint stars; weapons, engines and fittings as working machinery with mounts. This round adds: keep the shape reference's colours; no nebula.

## ithar_G18 (Glasswright flagship)

Shape reference: ithar_G15_beauty.jpg. Round 8 fault (R201): the lenses became clear glass bubbles and glowing globes that no longer point forward. The lens brief was changed for this round (weapons as solid grown forms with flat forward aperture faces, a grown turning collar on the turret, flak as skin apertures; no bubbles, domes or globes).

- Gate: all twelve lines pass. Inventory verified: two bow weapons, one spine turret weapon, one small spine gun, a rank of flak apertures along the spine, three stern engine cylinders, escort present carrying the same weapons.
- The weapons: solid opaque ivory-glass barrels with fine ribs and gold inlay bands, grown out of the hull plating with no mount, no bracket and no base plate, each ending in a flat dark aperture face, pointing forward. The turret weapon is the same form on a smooth grown collar. The flak rank is small dark aperture faces set flush in the skin. Nothing on the ship is a dome, a bubble or a glowing globe.
- Style line: photographic, pass. Realism from ribs, inlay, layered glass panels with real thickness at their frames and fine metalwork, pass; the ship stays clean and bright, never rusty. One hard light on black space, pass.
- Colour lock: held (pearl, amber, green and violet webbing as G15; the weapons' ivory sits brighter than G15's metal, recorded as a light mark).
- Drift: none in mass, silhouette or character. Sheet marks: in the sheet's profile the turret weapon tilts upward on a small pedestal instead of pointing forward on its collar; follow the beauty for the turret. The beauty is the view of record for all three weapons.
- Tries: the crop took two tries (try 1 returned the weapons as transparent ribbed cylinders on base plates; the escalation removed the lens vocabulary entirely and described only solid forms, and try 2 was accepted). The beauty took two tries: try 1 re-supplied the transparent tubes at full frame despite the chaired prompt, and was rejected; try 2 led the call with the accepted crop as the weapon design of record and was accepted. Lesson recorded for the playbook: at full frame this model re-supplies its template object unless an accepted crop of the actual target form leads the call.
