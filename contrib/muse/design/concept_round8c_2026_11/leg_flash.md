Ship-by-Ship Risks, Failure Modes, and Guard Checks

1. voidsworn_S10 (Sunward Flagship)

Risks in this brief:

Preserving exact counted hardpoints (seven organ pipes along the top edge, seven flank barrels, four dome turrets, roughly eleven vertical stern radiator slits, two engine bells, recessed prow lance, and escort) while applying dense surface plating.
The model treating dark iron plates under hard lighting as shiny polished chrome, or introducing ambient fill that washes out the dark palette.

Specific failure modes:

Colour drift: The base shifting to a lighter steel or mid-grey. Introducing cold blue glow into the prow notch, engines, or weapon ports.
Template shapes: Weapon mounts degenerating into generic smooth turrets; organ pipes becoming standard rocket tubes; the gothic stepped profile softening into a sleek wedge.
Gun port failure: The model lighting gun ports or weapon slits internally instead of keeping them unlit and dark.

Guard checks and sentences for the chair:

Prompt guard sentence: "Hull plating is rough, matte dark cast iron no lighter in tone than S8, with unlit dark gun ports and zero blue illumination across pincer prow, vents, weapon bays, or engine bells."
Chair gate check: "Tally the topside pipes (exactly 7), flank barrels (exactly 7), and dome turrets (exactly 4); verify background is pitch black and no blue pixel exists on the prow lance or engines."

2. deathless_E11 (Eclipse Flagship)

Risks in this brief:

Breaking the expansive, diaphanous crescent silhouette when applying deep cavities: the crescent must read as an expensive flagship, not an unfinished sketch.
Maintaining the precise battery count and placement from E4 despite the transformation to a crescent.

Specific failure modes:

Broad faces staying smooth: The model reading "slab-sided" as permission to leave broad dorsal/ventral crescent faces untextured, losing the intricate texture required for the flagship.
Template shapes: The crescent degrading into a generic smooth blade or banana; recessed bays becoming decorative dents rather than true cavities.
Thickness loss: The ship's cross-section reading as a thin sheet instead of a deep, heavy structure.

Guard checks and sentences for the chair:

Prompt guard sentence: "The broad dorsal and ventral crescent faces are constructed of thousands of overlapping, stepped rectangular plates with visible seams, expansion joins, and recessed fastener tracks, showing massive cross-sectional edge thickness."
Chair gate check: "Confirm the crescent faces display dense, faceted tectonic armor plating down to minute scale, edge rims reveal heavy layered plate thickness, and turret counts match E4 (two large twins, one small, one flak group)."

3. deathless_E12 (Eclipse Ring)

Risks in this brief:

The transformation to a ring introduces complex geometry where spatial depth must be maintained by linework alone.
Balancing the dark violet and green colour scheme against black space without losing the ring's structural framing to silhouette.

Specific failure modes:

Ring reading as a flat band: The seven-segment ring collapsing into a flat, featureless halo or torus lacking internal depth and framework.
Colour drift: The dark violet armour washing into slate grey or navy; green light bleeding across the entire ring instead of remaining a hard edge.
Backdrop contamination: The model adding nebula clouds, mist, or atmospheric blue glow to fill the black background.

Guard checks and sentences for the chair:

Prompt guard sentence: "The ring is an assembly of seven separate, thick, box-section curved arc segments joined by heavy structural framework, clad in deep dark violet armor plate, set against void-black starfield with zero nebula haze."
Chair gate check: "Inspect ring geometry for exactly seven discontinuous arc segments with visible internal depth on four struts, dark violet plating with no slate drift, green light confined strictly to the core and cutting edges, and deep black space seen through all open gaps."

4. ithar_G18 (Glasswright flagship)

Risks in this brief:

Conveying realism through strict geometric structure and predictable mechanical detail while avoiding any weathering or organic texture.
Achieving mechanical clarity for optics, weaponry, and sensor systems without the crystalline material reading as soft glass.

Specific failure modes:

Lenses losing their forward point: The bow optical weapons rendering as decorative spherical lamps or glass balls instead of directional optics aimed straight forward.
Texture drift: The model introducing dirt, rust, grease, or smudging onto the pristine surfaces, violating the clean material rule.
Weapon counts shifting: Flak point defence, turrets, and spine turret count drifting (its "5 flak points" figure is the leg's own invention, flagged in the chair header).

Guard checks and sentences for the chair:

Prompt guard sentence: "Bow optical weapons are solid crystalline hull extensions terminating in flat, forward-aimed directional aperture faces seated within stepped platinum collar bezels, pristine with no weathering, dirt, or spherical bubble forms."
Chair gate check: "Verify both primary bow weapon lenses and the spine turret lens exhibit flat or faceted directional apertures facing straight forward, flak points remain small flush hull apertures, and surfaces show micro-machined jewel-like precision without grease, grime, or rust."

Critique of the blind leg's method claims and its M7 addition: see the chair header; the critique argues against renamed methods that are not the blind leg's M1 to M6, and is recorded but not relied on.
