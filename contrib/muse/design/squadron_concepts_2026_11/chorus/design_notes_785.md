# Chorus spore swarm, round 1: two directions (issue 785)

Refs #785

Per-faction squadron craft (R253). The Chorus launches the spore swarm from the brood-sac (hangar 3) and the Ninefold Maw (hangar 3). The standing craft rules from the Sunward rounds govern both directions, translated for a living hull: no mechanical parts at all, no canopy or sensor hardware (eye-spots at most), nothing hung under the body, propulsion as muscle-ringed orifices, weapons exactly the data's and nothing else that reads as a weapon, bold simple forms that survive 32 px. Chorus language: the accepted class family (frigate, spitter, brood-sac); dark plum flesh (#57364c) grown over pale bone (#a4913a), ribbed muscle, bone ridges, translucent sacs, orifice openings with shaped rims, never flat discs. The acid sacs are translucent fluid, not emissive: a faint amber pulse at most, so they never consume the sparse-glow budget or drift yellow-green (DeepSeek's glow point, adopted). One dominant silhouette per craft, even for a swarm: the wing already shows four craft, so the swarm read comes from the wing, not from multiplying bodies inside one unit.

Files: CS1.jpg, CS2.jpg (each 720 px tall, name on the picture), plus silhouette_sheet.jpg with both at 32 px wide, their battle-camera size.

## The spore swarm (Spore swarm: sporeb T, "Acid sacs", 2 shots, pierce, acid trait; hp 26; speed 5; size 1.6, drawn 3.84)

Bomber-role numbers on a living organism. Every unit carries exactly that one weapon and nothing else that reads as a weapon.

### CS1, the Spore Mother

One rounded ribbed flesh pod, plum-dark, grown over a pale bone ridge that runs along its spine and forks at the bow. Weapon callout: the Acid sacs are two large translucent amber sacs grown into the flanks, one each side, visibly swollen with fluid, enlarged so they are the only translucent read on the body (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted); the small growths toward the stern are opaque matte flesh buds in the body's own plum, never amber, so the weapon count reads as exactly two. Propulsion: a muscle-ringed orifice cluster at the stern, puckered flesh. A row of small dark eye-spots along the bow ridge instead of any sensor slit. From above: a seed pod with two amber cheeks.

### CS2, the Drift Cluster

A swarm grown as one body: a central flesh mass wrapped in a partly fused frame of grown bone, with five smaller pods grown along the frame at uneven intervals and joined by muscle tendons, the whole one knotted organism (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted: the bone frame partly fused and continuous around a larger central hub, not a broken open ring, so the silhouette holds together). Weapon callout: the Acid sacs are the two largest pods, grown at the front of the frame, translucent amber and swollen; every other pod is opaque plain flesh. Propulsion: small orifice puckers on the rear pods. The named risk is reading as debris rather than a craft; the fused frame's order is the mitigation. Flash would DROP this direction outright on the silhouette test; the chair keeps it as a live option with the refinement above, since the brief asks Ryan for two options per faction.

## The chair's pick: CS1

Unanimous among the seated legs. CS1 reads instantly at 32 px and carries the Chorus family (flesh, bone, sacs) in a single body; it is the spore swarm's equivalent of the magazine block, a unit visibly built around the two sacs it fires. CS2 stays a full option and is the only true swarm read of the pair; its delivered render is the second generation, after the first drew the bone frame as separate smooth femur-like bones, which read as props rather than growth.

## Production notes (render drift the sheets must correct, not copy)

- CS1's bone ridge renders with the eye-spots set into the bone itself and a fork that flares like an antler at the bow; the sheet keeps the fork low and tight to the flesh, with the eye-spots along the bow ridge. Its two sacs render one forward and one aft rather than one per flank; either placement satisfies the callout, the sheet picks one and keeps exactly two.
- CS2's bone must stay irregular grown ridges fused into the flesh, as in the delivered second render, never smooth separate bone shapes (the first render's failure).
- Both: sacs are translucent, not glowing; any pulse stays faint and amber. No green or yellow-green anywhere.
