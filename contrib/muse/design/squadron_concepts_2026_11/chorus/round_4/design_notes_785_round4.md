# Issue 785, round 4: the Chorus sleekness pass (CS1B-A2, CS1B-C2)

Owner's ruling (Nov 15, 2026): the closed-hull Chorus direction is much better; CS1B-A and CS1B-C are very close to right, but the craft needs to be a bit sleeker. This round is a proportion pass on those two only; CS1B-B is out. Data unchanged: spore swarm, sporeb T ("Acid sacs", 2 shots, pierce, acid), hp 26, speed 5, evasion 25; size 1.6, drawn 3.84. Colours unchanged (flesh #57364c, bone #a4913a, amber sacs). The closed hull stays absolute: no open grooves, no exposed fluid, no round clear domes; amber is translucent fluid colour only, no glow, no bloom.

"Sleeker" as specified for this pass: the pod drawn out along its flight axis (longer against beam and depth), lower in profile, the bow tapering to a smoother point and the stern tapering behind the orifice cluster, the flank features stretched lengthwise and tightened against the body. Two proportion guards from the council are part of the sheet: the flank swell still breaks the silhouette after narrowing (the weapon source must stay legible), and the stern taper never reduces the orifice cluster; only the hull behind the cluster tapers.

## The two craft

### CS1B-A2, the Sealed Gills, sleek (CS1BA2.jpg)
CS1B-A elongated: the bone spine runs a longer, lower line to a drawn-out bow, the bow fork a blunt integrated split of the spine and hull taper, never forward prongs (DeepSeek). The gill bands are fewer and longer, following the taper; the valleys between them are continuous-skin surface contour only, raised-band shadows, never incised grooves or exposed fluid (DeepSeek). The bladder bulge is drawn out into a long low swell, enough of it carrying the matte amber translucence that one sac per flank stays legible at 32 px. The orifice cluster stays distinct at the tapered stern.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two sealed gill bladders, one per flank, the long low swell and band rhythm the weapon sign, the swarm's only weapon; (2) eye-spots along the bow ridge; (3) the muscle-ringed orifice cluster at the stern.

### CS1B-C2, the Shuttered Bladders, sleek (CS1BC2.jpg)
CS1B-C elongated the same way: the plate row stretched along a longer flank and consolidated into a few large, broad, overlapping plates (DeepSeek: a long row of thin stripes would read as gills, not shutters), each plate long and low at a shallow overlap angle, hugging the drawn-out bulge beneath. The hairline gaps stay shadow seams only, closed skin, no exposed fluid, and sit over the swell so the sac read survives. Bow and stern taper discipline matches A2.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two shuttered bladders, one per flank, amber in the plate seams, the swarm's only weapon; (2) eye-spots along the bow ridge; (3) the muscle-ringed orifice cluster at the stern.

## Chair's lean

CS1B-A2. DeepSeek, Flash and the blind leg all land there, and the reasoning is the same as round 3: it is unmistakably the sealed-gill craft, just faster. CS1B-C2 remains the bolder silhouette and the standing second if the owner weights the 32 px plate read over continuity with CS1B-A.

## Production notes (render drift; the written sheet governs)

- Both renders run the stern taper out to a sharp spike and extend the bone line into a point; the sheet tapers the hull behind a distinct, full-size orifice cluster and ends blunt.
- CS1B-A2's bow fork renders as two longish prongs; the sheet keeps the fork short, blunt and low, as in every Chorus sheet so far.
- CS1B-C2's plates render as segmented shell bands with full bone frames; the sheet keeps a few broad plates with bone as edging, overlapping like shutters.
- CS1B-A2's amber renders a touch stronger than the sheet's faint translucence; the build keeps it matte and muted.
