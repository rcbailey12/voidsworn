# Issue 785, round 3: CS1B variations with a closed hull (Chorus spore swarm)

Owner's commission (Nov 15, 2026): variations of CS1B, the Gill Bladders (round 2), with a CLOSED hull. The open amber grooves go away; the hull is a continuous closed skin. Exactly two acid sacs remain the weapon, one per flank, enclosed (sporeb T, "Acid sacs", 2 shots, pierce, acid; hp 26, speed 5, evasion 25; size 1.6, drawn 3.84). No variation presents two round clear domes. Wording carried through the whole set on Qwen's flag: wherever amber shows, it is translucent amber fluid colour, no glow, no bloom. Chorus colours as before (flesh #57364c, bone #a4913a, amber sacs).

## The three variations

### CS1B-A, the Sealed Gills (CS1BA.jpg)
CS1B's bladders skinned over completely: the gill ribs remain as raised surface bands across a smooth closed flank, the bladder's swollen outline still bulging the flank, and the amber shows only as a faint warm translucence through the thinnest skin in the shallow valleys between the bands, a muted blush, never an open groove and never emissive.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two sealed gill bladders, one per flank, their bulge and band rhythm the weapon sign, the swarm's only weapon; (2) eye-spots along the bow ridge; (3) the muscle-ringed orifice cluster at the stern.

### CS1B-B, the Smooth Pod (CS1BB.jpg)
The hull entirely smooth and closed: no bladders, no ribs. On the refinements adopted from DeepSeek and Qwen (the two legs agree): the two sac swellings are enlarged until each is a definite bulge that breaks the flank's outline, and the sealed seam along each bulge's crest is deepened and widened until it registers as a visible score at 32 px, with a narrow core of translucent amber fluid colour at its centre, no glow. The most minimal Chorus craft in the set.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two seam-marked sac bulges, one per flank, the swarm's only weapon; (2) eye-spots along the bow ridge; (3) the muscle-ringed orifice cluster at the stern.

### CS1B-C, the Shuttered Bladders (CS1BC.jpg)
The bladders covered by overlapping flesh-and-bone scale plates, like closed shutters lying along each flank, the bladder bulge preserved beneath them (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted), amber visible only in the hairline shadow gaps between plates, a few gaps widened just enough for the amber to register at 32 px (DeepSeek). Closed, armoured, and the plate row keeps CS1B's gill rhythm with no open fluid.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two shuttered bladders, one per flank, amber in the plate gaps, the swarm's only weapon; (2) eye-spots along the bow ridge; (3) the muscle-ringed orifice cluster at the stern.

- Qwen (design leg, Qwen3.8-Max verified both ends): KEEP A and C; REFINE B (adopted above). Leans CS1B-C, on pure 32 px boldness, with A the runner-up.
- Grok: seat empty (bot wall, 40th consecutive).

## Chair's lean

CS1B-A. DeepSeek and the blind leg land there, and it is the only variation that is unmistakably still CS1B with the hull closed: same bulge, same rhythm, nothing open. CS1B-C is the strong second and Qwen's pick; if the owner weights the 32 px read over identity, C is the defensible swap. CS1B-B is the set's minimal pole even refined, and its delivered render shows why the legs pushed on it: the seams read more like closed lips than weapon marks, so B stands as the comparison's low end unless the owner loves the smoothness itself.

## Production notes (render drift; the written sheet governs)

- CS1B-A's amber renders stronger between the bands than the sheet's faint blush; the build keeps it muted and transmitted, never a lit groove.
- CS1B-B's render understates even the refined bulge; the sheet's definite-bulge term governs.
- CS1B-C's plates render with pale bone edges all round; the sheet keeps the plates flesh-and-bone with the bone as edging, not full frames.
