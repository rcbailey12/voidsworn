## The craft

HF1D takes HF1C's body: the sleek spined wedge at HF1C's narrower proportions, the low broad flat-topped armoured ridge (squared cross-section, no taller than the stern block) running bow to stern, the panel grid breaking around it in ordered ranks. Onto that body it sets HF1B's bow: a distinct framed bow block, only slightly wider than the wedge behind it and stepped back into the hull in a clean single-stage bevel (Qwen's execution note, adopted), carrying the gun cassette centred in one framed recess with the three muzzles grouped tightly inside it so they read as a single cassette, not three separate guns (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted), and the sensor slit in its own slot directly above the recess. HF1B's stepped plate edging runs from the bow block's corners back along the hull's edges, kept flush and shallow so it never reads as wings or ledges (DeepSeek). The bow block does not overhang the spine line; the ridge runs from the block's top aft to the stern block. One recessed ion-blue engine slit in the stern block.

Weapon callouts: (1) the gun cassette, three muzzles grouped in the single framed recess in the bow block, the fighter's only weapon; (2) the sensor slit in its own slot above the recess; (3) the recessed ion-blue engine slit at the stern.
