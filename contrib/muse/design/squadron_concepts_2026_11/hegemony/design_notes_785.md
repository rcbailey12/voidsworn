# Hegemony fighter, round 1: two directions (issue 785)

Refs #785

Per-faction squadron craft (R253). The Hegemony Navy launches the fighter wing from the Vanguard cruiser and the Dominion dreadnought (hangar 1 each). The standing craft rules from the Sunward rounds govern both directions: no canopy glass (an armoured sensor slit at most), no aero wings or tail fins, nothing hung under the hull, thrusters recessed, weapons exactly the data's and nothing else that reads as a weapon, bold simple forms that survive 32 px. Hegemony language: the H10 Dominion and IM5 Iron Mandate sheets, rigid and clean; wedge hulls, even ordered panel grids, a seamline module band, pale steel (hull #c4ccd5, trim #3a78d0, glow #5fa0ff), show tones darker than the palette swatch at this size.

Files: HF1.jpg, HF2.jpg (each 720 px tall, name on the picture), plus silhouette_sheet.jpg with both at 32 px wide, their battle-camera size.

## The fighter (Fighter wing: guns T, "Interceptor guns", 3 shots; hp 18; speed 6; evasion 35; size 1.6, drawn 3.84)

Every fighter carries exactly that one weapon and nothing else that reads as a weapon.

### HF1, the Registry Wedgelet

One clean miniature wedge: a single faceted steel wedge hull with an even panel grid and a thin module band along each flank, the Dominion's discipline at the smallest size in the fleet. Weapon callout: the Interceptor guns are three muzzles recessed as one chunky cassette in an armoured recess at the bow, on a shallow traverse ring, with the thin sensor slit at the absolute forward edge of the wedge above them (Flash's stacking refinement, adopted, so the two bow elements never crowd at 32 px; DeepSeek's render note, keep the muzzles one chunky recessed cassette, not three fine holes, adopted). Stern: one recessed ion-blue engine slit across the stern block. One thin blue trim stripe along the wedge edge. From above: a perfect small arrowhead.

### HF2, the Forked Cordon

A wedge split into two thick parallel prongs joined at a single stern block, the channel between them a miniature of the Iron Mandate's bridged canyon, one slim bridge deck spanning the prongs aft of the gun cassette and kept low (DeepSeek's refinements, adopted: prongs thickened to survive 32 px, channel mouth widened, bridge moved aft and lowered so it never covers the guns, prong-tip sensor bands dark and non-glowing). Weapon callout: the Interceptor guns are three muzzles in one recessed cassette set flush into the front face of the stern block, firing down the channel's mouth, the shallow traverse ring visible from above. Recessed engine slits in the stern block, ion blue. From above: a tuning-fork wedge. The named risks: the channel must not read as a hangar mouth, and the fork must not read as damage; the prongs' thickness and the single shared stern block are the mitigations.

## The chair's pick: HF1

Two legs to one, and the renders bear it out. HF1 is the cleanest 32 px read of the round: one arrowhead, one gun recess, one engine slit, nothing to mistake. HF2 is the more distinctive picture and the IM5 canyon echo is genuinely Hegemony; it stays a full option, and if Ryan wants the fighter with a signature, HF2 is the one to argue for. Flash's dissent is recorded above, not smoothed over.

## Production notes (render drift the sheets must correct, not copy)

- HF1's gun recess renders in the dorsal bow face rather than the bow's front face; either placement satisfies the callout, the sheet picks one and keeps the sensor slit at the forward edge.
- HF1 renders very flat and pale; the sheet keeps the wedge's thickness visible at the edges and takes the show tones darker than #c4ccd5, per DeepSeek's value point.
- HF2 renders four muzzles in the cassette; the data is three shots and the sheet draws three. Its channel also renders a blue louvre glow under the cassette and on the flank; the sheet keeps ion glow to the stern engine slits only, so the channel reads as structure, not machinery light.
- Both: panel detail stays bold and simple at build; fine greeble at this scale reads as a shrunken capital ship.
