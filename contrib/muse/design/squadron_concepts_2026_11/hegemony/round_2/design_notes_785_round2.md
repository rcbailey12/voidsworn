# Issue 785, round 2: HF1 variations (Hegemony fighter)

Owner's commission (Nov 15, 2026): take HF1, the Registry Wedgelet (round 1), and spin off three variations to compare against each other and against HF1 itself. Same data for all four: fighter wing, guns T ("Interceptor guns", 3 shots), hp 18, speed 6, evasion 35; size 1.6, drawn 3.84. Hegemony colours as round 1 (hull #c4ccd5, trim #3a78d0, glow #5fa0ff, show tones darker than the swatch). One picture per variation, named, no taller than 720 px, plus the set's silhouette sheet.

## The three variations

### HF1A, the Long Dagger (HF1A.jpg)
HF1 stretched: a much longer, narrower wedge, the panel grid elongated with it, two longitudinal module rails running the hull's length. The gun cassette sits at the very tip of the bow, sensor slit directly above it; twin recessed engine slits at the stern; the stern block stays blunt and thick (both seated legs' condition, adopted).
Weapon callouts: (1) gun cassette at the bow tip, three muzzles in a row, the fighter's only weapon; (2) sensor slit across the bow face above the cassette; (3) twin ion-blue engine slits recessed in the stern block.

### HF1B, the Broad Shield (HF1B.jpg)
HF1 widened: a broad, flat arrowhead wedge, wider than HF2's prongs are apart, with stepped plate layers visible at the edges so the depth reads. The gun cassette is centred in the broad bow face under the sensor slit; the module band runs across the shoulders; engine slits at the stern corners.
Weapon callouts: (1) gun cassette centred in the bow face, three muzzles, the fighter's only weapon; (2) sensor slit across the bow face above the cassette; (3) recessed ion-blue engine slits at the two stern corners.

### HF1C, the Raised Spine (HF1C.jpg)
HF1 with a dorsal ridge: a low, broad, flat-topped armoured ridge with a squared cross-section runs from bow to stern, no taller than the stern block (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted: no raised bow cap, and the sensor slit sits in the bow face at the ridge's root, never a spine-tip pane, so the canopy read is closed off). The panel grid breaks around the ridge in ordered ranks; the module bands run along both flanks.
Weapon callouts: (1) gun cassette in the bow face below the ridge's root, three muzzles, the fighter's only weapon; (2) sensor slit across the bow face at the ridge's root; (3) one recessed ion-blue engine slit in the stern block.

## Council

- Blind Rey (filed before the pack went out): leans HF1C (the Dominion lineage mark), HF1A the safe option, HF1B's flat-plate risk named.
- DeepSeek (design leg, DeepThink on, Search on, verified both ends): KEEP on HF1A and HF1B, REFINE on HF1C (the ridge terms adopted above). Leans HF1B: at 32 px the broad blunt arrowhead reads cleanest and stays Hegemony-clean.
- Flash (design leg, Flash-Lite verified both ends): REFINE HF1A (stern thickness specified, adopted) and HF1C (spine tip bevels down, adopted in spirit through DeepSeek's stronger root-slit fix), KEEP HF1B. Leans HF1B.
- Qwen: seat empty (Qwen3.8-Max daily cap, still not reset). Grok: seat empty (bot wall, 39th consecutive).

## Chair's lean

HF1B. Both seated legs lean there, and the delivered render carries it: the stepped width is the most ownable fighter silhouette in the fleet at 32 px, and it separates cleanly from HF1, HF2 and the Sunward set alike. HF1C is the close second and the chair's own blind pick; its ridge is the better lineage story and the render keeps it safely armoured. HF1A is sound and the least distinct of the three.
