# Issue 785, round 3: KB1C variations (Hullbreaker bomber)

Owner's commission (Nov 15, 2026): more variations of KB1C, the Low Barge (round 2), under two standing briefs: differentiation against the whole bomber field (base KB1, KB1A, KB1B, KB2, SB3, SB4, SB5 and KB1C itself), and every variation must still read unmistakably as a SPACE BOMBER, never a boat, water barge, platform or ground vehicle. Nothing is hung under any hull in this set (restated on Qwen's flag). Data as KB1C: bomber wing, bombs T ("Bomb payload", 2 shots, pierce), hp 28, speed 5, evasion 25; size 1.6, drawn 3.84. Hullbreaker colours as before (hull #6a2c22, trim #b8402a, glow #e0402c; chains and cages off).

## The three variations

### KB1C-A, the Split Lid (KB1CA.jpg)
The lid split lengthwise into two deck plates with a recessed central channel running bow to stern between them; the sensor slit sits in the channel's bow end. The diagonal glacis mouths stay from KB1C. Flank machinery recesses deepen under each lid half; thrusters recessed in the flat stern face.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two bomb tube mouths set diagonally in the glacis, the bomber's only weapon; (2) the sensor slit in the channel's bow end; (3) the recessed stern thruster bank.

### KB1C-B, the Stepped Barge (KB1CB.jpg)
Two stacked shallow slabs: a wide lower hull and a narrower upper hull set back from the bow, the step kept shallow and tied to the tube face so the upper hull reads as a low superstructure, not a terraced platform (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted). The tube mouths sit side by side in the upper hull's sloped front face. The lower bow is a plain glacis with the sensor slit under a strengthened brow (DeepSeek). Thruster banks recessed in both stern faces, strongly legible, so the flight axis dominates (DeepSeek).
Weapon callouts: (1) the two bomb tube mouths side by side in the upper hull's sloped face, the bomber's only weapon; (2) the sensor slit under the brow on the lower glacis; (3) the recessed thruster banks in both stern faces.

### KB1C-C, the Wide Glacis (KB1CC.jpg)
Wider and lower: the whole bow one continuous shallow glacis slope, the lid's overhang nearly gone, a shallow armoured lip at the lid/glacis junction against the ramp read (DeepSeek's refinement, adopted). The two tube mouths sit at the glacis's extreme corners, each recessed in a dark armoured collar (DeepSeek), the widest-separated pair in the field. Flank machinery in recessed side bays with armoured doors, the doors carrying at least a two-tone value shift so they survive 32 px (Qwen's execution note, adopted). Proportional guard (Qwen, adopted): the beam stays well under the length, roughly 60 to 65 percent at most, so the silhouette stays a bomber and not a platform.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two bomb tube mouths at the glacis's extreme corners in their dark collars, the bomber's only weapon; (2) the recessed flank machinery bays; (3) the recessed stern thruster bank.

- Grok: seat empty (bot wall, 40th consecutive).

## Chair's lean

KB1C-A, the lean of DeepSeek, Qwen and the blind leg (Flash kept A and C and stated no lean): the channel is the clearest flight-axis structure any low barge in the field carries, and it separates the craft from KB1C itself at a glance. KB1C-C is the bold second. KB1C-B remains the risk the council named: its delivered render does lean toward a tracked vehicle from some angles, and if the owner keeps it, the strengthened brow and thruster banks in the sheet are load-bearing.

## Production notes (render drift; the written sheet governs)

- KB1C-A's mouths render as cylindrical tubes standing in triangular recesses; the sheet keeps them as mouths recessed into the glacis slope itself.
- KB1C-B's render shows the vehicle read plainly; see the chair's lean above.
- KB1C-C's render is taken from above and behind, which flatters the platform read; the sheet's proportion guard and collar terms govern the build.

## Owner's ruling (Nov 15, 2026)

KB1C-C, the Wide Glacis, is the pick of the KB1C variations, in the owner's words "the best by far". The council's lean for KB1C-A is recorded above and was not taken. KB1C-C's sheet terms (extreme-corner mouths in dark collars, the armoured lip, the beam guard) stand as written.

The owner has not ruled whether this winner replaces the base design or stands alongside it as a variant; that decision remains open.
