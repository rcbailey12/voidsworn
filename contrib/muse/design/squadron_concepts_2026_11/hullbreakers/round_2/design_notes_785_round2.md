# Issue 785, round 2: KB1 variations (Hullbreaker bomber)

Owner's commission (Nov 15, 2026): take KB1, the Flying Magazine (round 1), and spin off three variations, with MORE DIFFERENTIATION against the other bombers than round 1 achieved. Same data for all: bomber wing, bombs T ("Bomb payload", 2 shots, pierce), hp 28, speed 5, evasion 25; size 1.6, drawn 3.84. Hullbreaker colours as round 1 (hull #6a2c22, trim #b8402a, glow #e0402c; chains and cages off). The field each variation must separate from, in silhouette AND weapon face (DeepSeek's framing, adopted): base KB1 (tall brick, horizontal pair), KB2 (twin blocks, prow pair), SB3 (twin hulls), SB4 (black-iron block, horizontal pair), SB5 (dart core, one mouth per sponson).

## The three variations

### KB1A, the Twin Pipes (KB1A.jpg)
The magazine turned inside out: the hull is the two bomb tubes themselves, two huge parallel armoured cylinders fused side by side for the craft's whole length, scrap plate welded into the valleys. On DeepSeek's refinement (adopted), the tubes are STAGGERED: the port tube extends further forward, so the mouths sit at two depths and the weapon face is unique as well as the silhouette. A welded brow over the recessed mouth carries the sensor slit; the stern is capped by a blocky scrap block with slit vents and no round openings aft.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two bomb tube mouths, staggered at two depths in the bow, the bomber's only weapon; (2) sensor slit on the welded brow above the recessed mouth; (3) slit vents in the stern cap block, no round openings at the stern.

### KB1B, the Vertical Slab (KB1B.jpg)
One tall slab hull, strongly asymmetric: the port side a sheer flat armoured wall of layered plate, the starboard side stepped terraces of patch plate with shallow non-circular heat slits. The bomb tubes are stacked VERTICALLY in the bow face, one above the other, on a welded traverse cassette; no other bomber in the field carries its pair vertically. Sensor slit under a brow at the top bow corner; the bow foot slopes forward and the thrusters sit recessed low in the stern (the answers to the building read; no roof-like cap, per DeepSeek).
Weapon callouts: (1) the two bomb tube mouths stacked vertically in the bow face, the bomber's only weapon; (2) sensor slit under the brow at the top bow corner; (3) shallow heat slits across the terraced flank, kept non-circular so they never read as muzzles.

### KB1C, the Low Barge (KB1C.jpg)
A very low, wide, flat barge: a heavy lid-like top deck plate overhanging a shallow hull, the bow a sloped glacis. On DeepSeek's refinement (adopted), the two tube mouths sit DIAGONALLY across the glacis, one port-high, one starboard-low, both recessed under the lid's overhang, so the weapon face is not another horizontal pair. Shallow non-circular heat slits and machinery recesses along the flanks; thrusters recessed in the flat stern face.
Weapon callouts: (1) the two bomb tube mouths set diagonally in the glacis, the bomber's only weapon; (2) machinery recesses and shallow heat slits along the flanks under the lid; (3) thruster nozzles recessed in the stern face.

## Council

- Blind Rey (filed before the pack went out): leans KB1A (the silhouette nothing else owns), KB1B the close second, KB1C weakest on the lid read.
- DeepSeek (design leg, DeepThink on, Search on, verified both ends): REFINE KB1A (the stagger, adopted) and KB1C (the diagonal mouths, adopted), KEEP KB1B. Leans KB1B: the vertical stacked face is the strongest unique weapon-face separation in the field.
- Flash (design leg, Flash-Lite verified both ends): KEEP on all three, with a REFINE on KB1B (an asymmetric lower foot protrusion, adopted in the sheet's sloped bow foot). Leans KB1A.
- Qwen: seat empty (Qwen3.8-Max daily cap, still not reset). Grok: seat empty (bot wall, 39th consecutive).

## Chair's lean

KB1A, as refined. Two of the three leans land there, and the stagger answers the one gap DeepSeek found: the variation now differs from all six bombers in silhouette and in weapon face. KB1B is the strongest alternative and the council's split is honest: it wins on weapon face alone, but its body is the one that reads closest to a structure rather than a craft (its own render shows the tower risk the pack named). KB1C is the weakest of the three even refined.

## Production notes (render drift; the written sheet governs)

- KB1A renders partly as a pair of cannons; that is inherent to the concept, and the stern cap block plus the welded scrap mass are what carry the craft read in build.
- KB1B's render shows the tower read plainly; the sheet's sloped bow foot and low recessed thrusters are load-bearing, not decoration.
- KB1C's tubes render as protruding cylinders standing on the deck rather than mouths recessed into the glacis slope; the sheet keeps them recessed under the lid's overhang.
