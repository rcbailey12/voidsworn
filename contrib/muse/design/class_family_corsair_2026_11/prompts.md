# Hullbreakers class family: image prompts

One beauty prompt per new ship, for generation against the two anchor
pictures (corsair_A16_beauty.jpg, corsair_A17_beauty.jpg) as style
references. Shared style block first, then the ship-specific block. The
line-up is assembled from the beauties at game scale (see README), not
generated as one picture, so the lengths stay true.

## Shared style block (prepend to every prompt)

Concept art of a warship from the Ashfall Hullbreakers, a scavenger faction
in a space game, in the exact style of the two reference ships: a blunt,
lopsided brick hull welded from patch plates in soot black, rust and
dried-blood red, with a few trophy armour plates bolted flat in their old
colours (pale grey, violet). Mismatched guns bolted on wherever they fit,
none pointing backward. A rocket rack of mismatched tubes bolted on the top
deck. Engine bells at the stern glowing red-orange, mismatched sizes. Dim
red running lights, sparse. Painterly, gritty, heavy metal, dark space
background with faint stars. Three-quarter view from forward, port and
above, bow toward the lower left, the whole ship in frame. No chains or
anything hanging off the ship, no cages, no crystal, no masts, no funnels,
no porthole rows, no railings, nothing that reads as a sea ship. No clean
symmetry, no fresh paint, no bright light.

## A18 Frigate, "Sniffer" (length 6.5)

A small frigate, a short welded box hull with the mass pushed forward into
a deep sensor head at the bow. One oversized round listening dish, nearly
as wide as the hull, canted forward on the port bow shoulder. Two gun
barrels of different sizes set into the cheeks of the bow face. A pair of
small articulated grabber arms tucked under the bow. One pale grey trophy
plate bolted flat on the starboard flank. Two engine bells at the stern,
the starboard one much larger. The smallest, scrappiest ship of the
family: a little brick with one big ear.

## A19 Destroyer, "Mauler" (length 9.5)

A long, lean destroyer in three steps: a heavy bow block with a flat panel
face and two low intake tubes, a long low middle deck, and a raised stern
block. Two heavy guns of obviously different sizes side by side in the bow
block, one long barrel and one short fat barrel. Two rocket racks of
different tube counts on the middle deck. Two listening dishes, one large
on the port bow shoulder, one small on the starboard flank. Four
articulated grabber arms under the bow in two pairs. One big pale grey
trophy plate amidships on the port flank. Three engine bells in the stern
block, the middle one largest. A gun ship: long, lean and loud at the bow.

## A20 Carrier, "the Den" (length 16.5)

A long, wide carrier hulk. The bow has a flat panel face with two low
intake tubes and two listening dishes of uneven size on the shoulders. The
port flank is cut by two large, uneven open hangar mouths, dark rectangles
framed in torn plate with armoured doors slid open; the nose of a small
cobbled-together fighter pokes out of the forward mouth. The starboard
flank is a closed wall of patch plates. The top deck is a flat salvage
field with bolted-down trophy plates and a spare engine bell lying flat as
cargo, and one rocket rack aft. Four grabber arms under the bow. Five
engine bells of uneven sizes across the stern, one housed in violet
trophy plate. A dim red lamp glows inside each open hangar mouth. A den
ship: hollow, dark-mouthed, full of stolen craft.

## Line-up assembly (not a prompt)

One canvas, dark background, the five family ships in side profile at true
relative lengths (A16 26, A20 16.5, A17 14.5, A19 9.5, A18 6.5), longest at
the back or top, each labelled under its hull with its code and name:
A16 Battle-barge, A20 Carrier "Den", A17 Cruiser "Pack Hunter", A19
Destroyer "Mauler", A18 Frigate "Sniffer". Assembled from the accepted
beauties (A16, A17) and the new beauties (A18, A19, A20) scaled by length,
so the size read Ryan judges is honest.
