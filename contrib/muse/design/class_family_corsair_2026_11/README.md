# Hullbreakers class family round (Muse, Nov 12 2026)

The first class family round under the Board's plan (relayed by Ryan,
Nov 12 2026, 18:35 CST): every Muse leg designs each missing class of one
faction, Rey merges and writes the image prompts, images are made from the
accepted flagship and cruiser, and Ryan gets a line-up of the whole family
at game sizes to pick or pass each ship.

Faction: Ashfall Hullbreakers (Ryan's pick, first round). Anchors: A16
Battle-barge (R201) and A17 Cruiser (R201), the accepted build references.
Missing classes from the game's hull table: Frigate C13s_23 (6.5),
Destroyer C14s_23 (9.5), Carrier C16s_23 (16.5).

Contents:
- designs.md: the merged designs (chair), one per class, with the one open
  fork (the carrier's hangars) decided and the rejected forms named.
- prompts.md: one beauty prompt per ship against the anchors, plus the
  line-up assembly spec.
- ../pack/pack.md: the design pack every leg worked from (faction sheet,
  class data, breach anchor table, anchor descriptions).
- ../legs/: leg_rey.md (blind, written first), leg_deepseek.md,
  leg_grok.md, leg_flash.md (ideation seats), leg_qwen.md (seat empty:
  Qwen3.8-Max daily cap; the chat is left ready for a retry).

Chair decisions worth the Board's eye:
- Names: Sniffer (frigate) and the Den (carrier) were chosen independently
  by the blind Rey leg and by Grok; Mauler (destroyer) is the chair's.
- Carrier hangars: two large open mouths on the port flank, armoured doors
  slid open, a fighter nosing out of one. The flight-deck and hidden-bay
  forms were rejected (see designs.md).
- Working codes A18/A19/A20 continue the A16/A17 sequence; the Board
  renumbers if its ledger says otherwise.

Status Nov 12 2026: v1 beauties presented 18:57 CST; Ryan's verdict
19:01 CST: all three look like the exact same ship, almost no visible
variation. The chair owns the cause: the prompts led with the shared
style block and the generator anchored on A16's hull as a template, so
the class reads drowned. v2 redrawn the same evening with silhouette
first (materials only from the anchors, hull shape explicitly not to be
copied, proportions stated per ship): A18 a short tall cube with a dome
sensor head and an oversized dish; A19 a long low flat slab with bow-face
casemate guns and flat-lying tube racks; A20 a wide flat barge with two
huge flank hangar mouths and a fighter emerging. v2 files:
corsair_A18_beauty_v2.jpg, corsair_A19_beauty_v2.jpg,
corsair_A20_beauty_v2.jpg, family_lineup_v2.jpg. Chair's residual nits
(sent to Ryan): A18's dish rides on a front stalk and is very pale;
A19's deck batteries read as torpedo tubes and its stern flames are
rocket-like; A20's bell row sits along the deck edge.

Ryan's verdicts on v2 (Nov 12, 19:14 CST): Den close but proportions off
(the v2 fighter was drawn about a third of the ship's length against the
game's 1.6 vs 16.5, so the hull could not plausibly hold squadrons);
Sniffer cartoonish, concept not loved; Destroyer "just a long box".
v3 redrawn the same evening against those notes. Chair's read on v3:
Den now has a deep hull and an open hangar gallery with small craft
parked inside (the two designed mouths merged into one long gallery;
closer, still not exact). Mauler's three steps landed (tall bow block

v3 FAILED: the generator ignored the short tall brick brief and produced
another long family brick with a flat hatch where the flush dish should
be. Chair's proposed frigate fix, put to Ryan with v3: edit the v2
picture (the one genuinely distinct silhouette obtained) to swap the
smooth dome for a welded sensor block and the white dish for rusted

Ryan's verdicts on v3 (Nov 12, 19:18 CST): Frigate GOOD (the v3 picture
