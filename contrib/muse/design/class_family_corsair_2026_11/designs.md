# Hullbreakers class family: merged designs (chair)

The three missing Ashfall Hullbreakers classes, designed against the
accepted anchors A16 (Battle-barge flagship, length 26) and A17 (Cruiser,
class length 14.5). Merged by the chair from five legs: blind Rey,
DeepSeek, Qwen (seat empty, daily cap), Grok and Flash. Leg records are in
../legs/. Working codes continue the A16/A17 sequence.

Family language held across all three: blunt welded brick hulls of patch
plates in soot black, rust and dried-blood red with trophy plates bolted
flat in their old colours; listening dishes and scoops at the bow; grabber
arms under the bow; rocket racks bolted on the top deck; mismatched guns,
never facing backward; mismatched engine bells glowing red-orange, one
larger than the rest; sparse dim red running lights; lopsided massing;
nothing hanging off the ship; no crystal; no sea-ship parts.

## A18 Frigate (C13s_23, length 6.5): Sniffer

Outline. One welded box hull, short and tall for its length (about 3:1),
mass pushed forward: the bow third is a sensor head, deeper than the rest
of the hull, over a flat chined belly. From the top, a blunt wedge wider at
the bow than the stern, the port shoulder swollen out past the hull line by
the dish mount, the starboard flank flat. From the side, the deck steps
down once behind the sensor head to a low aft engine block.

Class read. One oversized listening dish, nearly the beam's width across,
canted forward on the port bow shoulder. A little brick with one big ear:
the finder of the pack. (All four legs converged on the single dominant
dish; Rey and Grok independently named the ship Sniffer.)

Fittings. Two bow casemate guns of different calibres in the sensor head's
cheeks, facing forward; one small deck gun aft, facing forward; one rocket
rack of four mismatched tubes on the top deck; the oversized dish and one
wake-sniffer scoop under the chin; one pair of small grabber arms under the
bow; two engine bells, the starboard one clearly larger, red-orange; one
pale Hegemony trophy plate bolted flat on the starboard flank; sparse dim
red lights (bow, midships, stern).

Breaks. 3 breach anchors, uneven: behind the sensor head, at the midships
weld, ahead of the engine block. One authored break line just aft of the
bow. Patch plates tear first; the sensor head comes away as one ragged
piece with the dish still on it.

Insides. One deck: a kennel nook, a loot hold the size of a closet, frames
and machinery scavenged from three different ships.

## A19 Destroyer (C14s_23, length 9.5): Mauler

Outline. A stretched slab, about 4:1, leaner than the cruiser, in three
steps: a heavy bow casemate block with A17's flat panel face and two low
intake tubes, a long low middle deck carrying the rocket battery, and a
raised stern block with the engines. From the top the port flank carries
one big pale Hegemony trophy plate amidships, widening the outline there;
from the side the stepped spine reads clearly, highest at the bow block.

Class read. The bow block's two heavy guns of obviously different calibres
side by side, one long-barrelled, one short and fat, on a long lean hull.
A17 is dish-led; A19 is gun-led. The pack's gun ship.

Fittings. Two heavy bow casemates as above; two flank guns of mixed calibre
facing out; one deck turret aft facing forward; two rocket racks on the
middle deck, six tubes and four; two listening dishes (large on the port
bow shoulder, small on the starboard flank); one chin scoop; four grabber
arms under the bow in two pairs, as A17; three engine bells in the stern
block, the centre one largest, red-orange.

Breaks. 4 breach anchors, uneven: the bow block seam, two along the middle
deck welds (not mirrored), one at the stern block. Two authored break
lines. The bow block tears away whole along its weld; patch plates first.

Insides. Two decks: gun crews' galleries along the casemates, a kennel deck
under the bow block, a loot hold aft, scavenged frames throughout.

Name: the chair's blind pick. The legs offered Rivet Biter (DeepSeek),
Hook (Grok) and Rust Viper (Flash).

## A20 Carrier (C16s_23, length 16.5): the Den

Outline. A long slab-sided hulk, the family's widest hull relative to its
length after A16, built around a hollow middle. The bow keeps the family
face: a flat panel with two low intake tubes and two listening dishes of
uneven size on the shoulders. The port flank is cut by two large, uneven
open hangar mouths, dark rectangles framed in torn plate with their
armoured doors slid open; a cobbled fighter noses out of the forward mouth.
The starboard flank stays a closed patchwork wall, so the ship is lopsided
in function as well as mass. The top deck is a mostly flat salvage field:
trophies and a spare engine bell bolted down flat, one rocket rack aft.
The stern is a row of mismatched engine bells.

Class read. The two dark open mouths along one flank, and the tiny fighter
silhouette at one of them giving the scale. No other Hullbreaker has
openings in its side.

Chair fork, decided. The legs split on the hangars: open flank mouths
(Rey), two armoured crib mouths with flush doors (DeepSeek), a stern
hangar block (Grok), an internal bulge with a flight deck (Flash). The
chair takes flank mouths in DeepSeek's armoured form, two mouths rather
than Rey's four: two big mouths read at the battle camera and stay clear
of the porthole-row ban, and an open mouth with a fighter showing is the
only carrier read that survives at 70 px. Flash's flight deck is rejected
(sea-ship read, and hidden bays give no class read); Grok's stern block is
rejected for the same invisibility.

Fittings. Two medium bow casemates, mismatched, facing forward; two flank
guns on the closed starboard wall facing out; one deck gun aft; one rocket
rack of six tubes aft on the top deck; two dishes as above; one chin
scoop; four grabber arms under the bow in two pairs, plus two heavy
grapple arms folded flat at the stern quarters for dragging prizes
alongside; four hangar bays behind the two mouths (two per mouth, upper
and lower) carrying cobbled fighters and bombers (length 1.6); five engine
bells across the stern, uneven, one in a violet Eclipse trophy housing,
all glowing red-orange; one dim red lamp inside each bay mouth.

Breaks. 7 breach anchors, uneven; three authored break lines. The bay side
tears open first (doors and the plate between the mouths go early, craft
spill), then the hull parts at the two main welds fore and aft of the
hangar block: bow, hangar sections and stern, never halves.

Insides. Two hangar deck levels with half-built fighters on scavenged
frames, kennel decks for boarding crews, loot holds in the bow, machinery
aft.

Name: Rey and Grok converged on Den independently; DeepSeek offered Den
Hulk, Flash Scrapper's Den.
