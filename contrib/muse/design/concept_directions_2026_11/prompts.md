# #417 prompts: the exact prompt used for each delivered picture

Model for every picture: Muse's image generation pipeline (media.generate_image), called by the chair. The pipeline does not expose an underlying model name; that is the whole model record. Prompts are the chair's, written blind-leg first and cross-checked against the DeepSeek and Qwen writer legs and the Flash and Grok ideation legs (see the leg files in this folder). Where a direction took more than one try, the prompt below is the one that produced the delivered picture, and README.md says what the earlier tries got wrong.

Every prompt shares one framing block, stated in full in each: landscape orientation, three-quarter view from the front and slightly above, one hero capital ship with one small escort of the same faction beside it for scale, plain near-black background with a few faint stars at most, lit from the upper left, whole ships in frame with a little space around them, no text, no logos, no borders, no planets.
