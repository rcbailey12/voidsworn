# #429 concept round 2: the prompts used

How these prompts were chaired: the blind Rey leg was written first (leg_rey.md). The chair built each variation's sheet prompt on the DeepSeek writer leg (the most disciplined of the round), folding in Flash's engine and stern-face specifics, Grok's guard phrases, and the Qwen leg's escort specifications and guards. Two chair rulings apply throughout: the Chorus carries exactly three brood sacs in total, and the Glasswright mount count follows the factions.md grammar text. Generation ran through Rey's image pipeline; the pipeline receives the prompt text below and expands it internally, so the text here is the chair's input, which is what this file records. Every sheet was drawn first; every beauty view was then generated image-to-image from the accepted sheet, with the accepted sheet supplied as the reference image, and the beauty prompt says so. Where a picture needed more than one try, the retry changed only the lines named in gate_notes.md (kept in the working record) and summarised in README.md under what the model kept getting wrong; the prompt below is the first-try form unless the delivered try is noted.
