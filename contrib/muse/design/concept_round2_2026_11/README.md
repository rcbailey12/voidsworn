# #429 concept round 2: design sheets and beauty views, chaired

Round 2 was drawn to the twelve-line gate in docs/playbooks/concepts_round2.md, answering Ryan's round 1 verdicts: guns are the game's own and counted, proportions are the grammar's, identity lives in the plan view, and every beauty view is made from its accepted sheet.

Method, as the issue required: the design sheet was drawn FIRST for every variation and judged against the gate before any beauty view was attempted. The pipeline can take a picture as a reference, and did: every beauty view was generated image-to-image from the accepted sheet for that variation, with the sheet supplied as the reference image. Up to three tries per picture; the delivered try is named per variation below, and failures are marked, not hidden. Prompts are in prompts.md. The council legs are filed alongside (leg_rey.md is the blind leg, written before any picture; leg_deepseek.md and leg_qwen.md are the writer legs; leg_grok.md and leg_flash.md are ideation). Two chair rulings ran through the round: the Chorus carries exactly three brood sacs in total, and the Glasswright mount count follows the factions.md grammar text.

The gate, for reference: 1 weapons counted, the game's own mounts only. 2 nothing blocks a gun, no gun aims backward or into the ship. 3 one thrust axis, engines at the stern in line, filling a quarter to a half of the stern face, nothing else glowing like an engine. 4 one spine. 5 one command block and one sensor cluster, low and protected. 6 a hangar is a door. 7 symmetric about the centre plane (Hullbreakers lopsided in plating only). 8 designed from above first. 9 fixed proportions per class, and the escort a smaller ship of the same grammar, never the hero shrunk. 10 one scale across a faction's ships. 11 space, not sea, not air. 12 the faction's sheet holds: colours, its one accent light, its Never list.

Marked failures in this round, up front: S1 line 9 (plan a needle at 7.2 : 1), H3 line 1 (four turret objects for two mounts), A1 lines 6 and 9 (four hangar-style pits; 4.4 : 1), C3 line 1 (eleven bow openings for nine). Everything else passed, much of it with notes, and the notes are in the tables.
