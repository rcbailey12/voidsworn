# Glasswright family: concept picks and build notes

Provenance: concept sketches generated by Muse in chat with Ryan on Nov 16, 2026, exploring the locked Glasswright language around CD5, the flagship of record (R298). Every pick below is Ryan's, made in chat on Nov 16, 2026. These are sketch-level settles and concept guides (R207, R236): the build sheet and game data govern the build, and where a sketch has gaps the builder builds differently and notes it. The Board record is the Board's to make.

## Locked language (all hulls)

- A long leaf of flowing, twisted blown glass lit from inside. Rigid, symmetric about the long axis, one continuous skin, lenses grown in, never attached parts.
- Clear and pearly glass with silver veins; panes of amber, violet and green glass lit from inside like stained blown glass; stored light reads seafoam.
- Data palette: hull #eef3f1, trim #d7c88a, glow #9fffd2.
- Guns sit on the optical axis. A prism lance fires through the bow optic: white core with rainbow fringe. Smaller lenses are secondaries.
- Engines are a soft white bloom at the stern optic. No flame.
- The hull breaks by shattering. Breach anchors: one fewer than class base (frigate 1, destroyer 2, confirmed in the family record).
- Never: wings, petals, sails, masts, anything insect-like, rust, smoke, flame, crystal facets, detached vanes, open frames. The crystal and prism routes are scrapped (R180, R183(5)); paired wings and petals are scrapped (Nov 10, 2026).

## Sketch habits to write against

The sketch generator has three recurring habits. Build sheets should specify against all three:

1. Bow optics render as open glass tubes. Always specify a grown, solid optic: a lens point or a lens grown flush, never a tube, ring or porthole.
2. Streamers and tails go wispy, toward smoke. Always specify rigid glass.
3. Small hulls lose their long axis and collapse into blobs. Always state one dominant axis and keep it.
