# Frigate: Glasswright sliver (i_wisp)

Data: Frigate, size .46, cp 1, weapon shard F, ability blink. Breach anchors 1.

Pick: R2a (Ryan, Nov 16, 2026): two ribbons fused at both ends, cleft closed along most of the length.

![Frigate R2a](img/frigate_sliver_r2a.webp)

Build notes:

- Form: two ribbons of blown glass twisting around each other, fused at the bow and at the stern, pressed together along most of their length so the hull reads as one solid twisted body with only a short glowing cleft amidships.
- Shard F: the shard cluster at the fused bow, on the centreline.
- Stern: soft white bloom at the fused stern optic.
- Watch the small-hull habit: at size .46 the form must keep one clear long axis or it collapses into a blob at battle scale.
