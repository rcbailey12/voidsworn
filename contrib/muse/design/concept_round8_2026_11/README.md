# Concepts round 8: every picked ship in the one realistic style

Issue #515. Method per FINDINGS R192: each picture is an image-to-image edit of its named reference; only the listed changes were made. Style reference for the whole round: corsair_A.jpg, the round 1 Scrap Brick (R197). Every result is scored below against the twelve-line gate (docs/playbooks/concepts_round2.md) and against the style line, point by point. Failures and partials are marked where they stand; nothing was quietly regenerated past a marked fault except the two tries recorded.

The style line, as scored: (1) a photograph of a real, very large built object, not an illustration, a toy or a game icon; (2) the surface made of many separate plates, panels and fittings small enough to give the ship its size, no large flat single-colour area; (3) real materials, the faction's own, under one hard light on black space with a few faint stars; (4) weapons, engines and fittings drawn as working machinery with mounts, not smooth symbols.

Council: blind Rey leg written first (leg_rey.md). DeepSeek wrote the prompt set the chair ran, with two chair corrections (leg_deepseek.md). Flash and Grok ideation legs converged and their guard sentences were folded in (leg_flash.md, leg_grok.md). The Qwen writer leg ran with its model gates verified, but the job pack never reached the model (the upload showed as attached after a retry; Qwen states the pack body never arrived, and its output confirms it: it redrew the ships with new cameras and listed the Hullbreaker crystal as a fitting to keep, the opposite of the brief). Its set was not used; its plate counts were used only as cross-checks where they agree with the sheets. Recorded in full in leg_qwen.md. Argon seat empty, blocked as standing.

Scope note, from Ryan's ruling while this job ran (Nov 11, 2026): the Hegemony flagship is H10, and H10 gets this same realistic treatment, as does the Chorus (C9, C8). Those redraws are NOT in this folder; this round's fixed list was already in generation. They are the follow-on job.

## corsair_A16, Hullbreaker flagship (the Scrap Brick itself)

Change made: the pale crystal shard on the upper flank removed and plated over with mismatched scrap plate. Nothing else.

Gate: lines 1 to 12 pass. The reference was already gate-clean and the edit touched nothing else; chains, two bow intakes, two dishes, boarding hooks, rocket tubes, flank batteries, the big stern engine and the escort all stand as drawn.

Style line: 1 pass (the reference set this style), 2 pass, 3 pass, 4 pass. The patch at the shard's site reads as torn, layered salvage in the hull's own language, not a clean plate.

Drift from the shape reference: none the chair can find beyond the one change.

Tries: beauty 1, sheet 1. Sheet marks: a small antenna cluster appears on the plan's centre line that the beauty only hints at (inside the one sensor cluster allowance), and the stern engine furniture reads slightly multiplied in plan. Neither moves a mount.

## voidsworn_S9, Sunward flagship (style pass on S8)

Change made: style only. Shape, mounts and counts are S8 exactly: forked prow, recessed lance, seven organ pipes, seven flank barrels, four dome turrets, two engine bells, radiator bank, escort.

Gate: lines 1 to 12 pass on the beauty. On the sheet, line 1 is PARTIAL: the profile renders the flank battery twice, seven barrels under the hull line and a row of seven orange-lit ports above them. Gun ports stay dark in this project; the builder should take the barrels as the battery and ignore the lit row. The plan view is clean and is the view of record.

Style line: 1 pass, 2 pass, 3 partial, 4 pass. On 3: the iron is now real riveted cast plate with seams, edge wear and heat bluing at the bells, a clear gain on S8's smoother read; the liberty is the prow notch, whose walls are tinted blue well beyond heat staining at the lance. It reads as heat-treated metal and sits inside the brief's thermal bluing language, but it is the strongest colour the pass added anywhere. Backgrounds on both S9 pictures carry more star dust than the reference's near-black; minor.

Drift: none in mass, silhouette or counts.

Tries: beauty 1, sheet 1. Chair note on counts: the writer leg's first draft carried S8's counts wrong (two rows of five barrels, three dome turrets). The sheet of record and the Flash leg agree on seven, seven and four, and those are the numbers the prompts ran with.

## deathless_E10, Eclipse ring cruiser (style pass on E8)

Change made: style only. The ring lies flat in broken arcs with the same gaps (seven arcs), four struts, the green core in its cage, spine turrets, three stern bells, escort.

Gate: lines 1 to 12 pass on both pictures. In the sheet's plan two stern arcs overlap the engine block slightly; presentation only, the counts hold. The profile shows the spine edge-on with the ring collapsed to its line, the honest projection of a flat ring.

Style line: 1 pass, 2 pass, 3 pass, 4 pass. The arcs read as tiled plate with thickness and seams now, no flat cutouts, and the green stayed confined to the core and the razor edges. One note carried from the beauty: the arc faces read closer to dark slate tile than to Eclipse shade metal, and the beauty's background carries more nebula dust than the reference. Both inside the brief.

Drift: none. The ring never closed, tilted or thickened, the failure this ship invites.

Tries: beauty 1, sheet 1.

## ithar_G17, Glasswright flagship (G15 with its lenses grown in)

Changes made, the two the brief lists: (a) every lens grown out of the hull in one continuous skin of glass and bright metal, no pod, bracket, ring mount, bolt or seam; (b) the style line, kept clean and bright. Lens inventory as drawn in G15: two large bow lenses, one large turret lens on the spine, one small spine gun, a rank of small flak lenses along the spine, three stern engine cylinders.

Gate: lines 1 to 12 pass on the beauty, with the lens change itself scored here: the accepted beauty (try 2) shows the lenses as solid swellings of the hull's own glass, lit from inside the glass, the flak rank as glass beads in the skin, the escort treated the same. It is 90 percent of seamless: the turret lens base still shows a fine machined ring and the bow lenses sit in shallow rims. On the sheet, the plan view carries the grown read correctly and is the view of record; the profiles seat the spine lens as a dome on a base, the cover read the brief bans. MARKED: in the sheet's profiles, follow the beauty and the plan for lens seating, not the profile. A second sheet try fixed neither this nor the layout (it put plan and profile side by side, against the sheet form) and was not delivered.

Style line: 1 pass, 2 pass, 3 pass, 4 pass on the accepted beauty. The hull stayed clean and bright; the depth comes from ribs, inlay and layered glass, not wear, as the brief requires for this faction.

Drift: none in mass, silhouette or lens stations across either try.

Tries: beauty 2 (try 1 put clear bubble domes over metal barrels, a pod by another name, rejected), sheet 2 (try 1 delivered with the profile fault marked above; try 2 rejected on layout and not delivered).

## What the Board should know

1. The round's one hard fault is the G17 sheet's profiles (lens seating). The beauty and the plan are right; a builder briefed by this README will not be misled.
2. The S9 sheet's lit port row is the other marked partial; the plan is clean.
3. E9's style gain is real but the smallest of the six. If any ship gets a second style pass later, it is this one.
4. Follow-on scope already ruled by Ryan: H10 (picked as the Hegemony flagship) redrawn in this style, and the Chorus pair (C9, C8) redrawn in this style. Neither is in this folder.
5. Sheet orientation is not uniform across the set (the Sunward and Eclipse sheets point the bow right, the Hullbreaker sheets left). Presentation only; each sheet is internally consistent.
