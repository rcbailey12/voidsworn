# Prompts as run, issue #515 (concepts round 8)

Chaired by Rey from the DeepSeek writer leg (the alternative set is preserved in leg_deepseek.md), with the Flash and Grok ideation guards folded in, and one chair correction and three chair amendments:

1. S9 counts corrected to the S8 sheet: DeepSeek wrote two rows of five barrels and three dome turrets; voidsworn_S8_sheet.jpg shows seven organ pipes in the radiator bank, seven flank barrels and four dome turrets (the Flash leg counted the same). The chaired prompts use the sheet's numbers.
2. E9's turrets are named as drawn (two large twin turrets, one smaller turret, one small flak cluster on the spine) rather than DeepSeek's bare "three spine turrets".
3. Sheets carry the escort for all six ships, not only the Hullbreakers: every reference beauty carries an escort and the sheet is a re-projection of the accepted beauty (Grok's guard). DeepSeek's S9, E9, E10 and G17 sheets said hero only.
4. The Qwen writer set was not used: the pack never reached that model (see leg_qwen.md). Its plate counts were used only as cross-checks where they agree with the sheets.

The style line spoken in every prompt: a photograph of a real, very large built object, not an illustration, a toy or a game icon; many separate plates, panels and fittings small enough to give the ship its size; real materials under one hard light on black space with a few faint stars; weapons, engines and fittings as working machinery with mounts, not smooth symbols.

## Retries and amendments as run

1. ithar_G17_beauty, try 2 (accepted). Try 1 seated the big lenses in clear glass bubble domes over metal barrels, a pod by another name. Try 2 redefined the lens and was run with this wording in place of the brief's first change: every lens is a solid swelling of the hull's own glass, the way a bottle wall bulges outward into a thick glass bump; no dome, no bubble cover, no transparent canopy over machinery, no pod, bracket, ring, collar, bolt or seam; the emitter glows from inside the solid glass bulge; a turning lens sits in a smooth socket where the hull's glass thickens around its base, still one continuous surface. The rest of the prompt was unchanged.

2. ithar_G17_sheet, try 2 (rejected, not delivered). Aimed only at try 1's profile fault: exactly two views of the hero (one plan, one profile) plus the escort in plan and profile; lenses solid glass swellings in both views, not transparent, nothing showing through, no base, ring or collar. The result put plan and profile side by side, against the sheet form in the brief, and still seated the spine lens on a rim. Try 1 stands as the delivered sheet with its fault marked in the README.

All other pictures were accepted on their first try.
