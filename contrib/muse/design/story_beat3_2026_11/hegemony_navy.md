### Flags

- Learning payoff: the source line ended at "Couriers are already carrying it" and never stated the lag. The settled Hegemony fiction and R62 require doctrine to travel at courier speed with no exceptions, so the line now states the speed, the late arrival one sector behind where the entry was needed, and that the entry lands after a loss as fully as after a win. All three legs that flagged the line agreed on the fix.
- Node, second option: "The links stay lit" became "The links stay on." Light vocabulary belongs to the Glasswrights under bible 1.3. Flagged by the DeepSeek leg; the chair applied the smallest change that clears it.
- Reyes spotlight: the source line used pronouns for the unnamed crews ("as they form up, so the wings hear the number they intend to embarrass"). The line now names its nouns: the wings, the squadrons, the strike group. Flagged by the Argon leg.
- Maren spotlight: "shield reading" became "deflector reading." Bible 1.2 gives the Hegemony deflectors, not the generic shield term. Flagged by the Argon and DeepSeek legs.
