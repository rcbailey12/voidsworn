## G10 sheet (writer leg, verbatim, try 1 accepted)

Orthographic design sheet of a single spaceship, two views stacked, top view above and side view below, flat on with no perspective, same scale in both views, on a plain dark grey background, lit evenly with soft neutral light, no text, no labels, no dimensions, no callouts, no reference picture used. The ship is a Glasswright warship: a precision optical instrument built as a warship, polished silver structure and clear glass, exact machined joints, nothing rough, nothing heavy. Overall proportions about 4.5 : 1 : 0.8 (length : beam : height). One continuous structural spine runs bow to stern. Symmetric about the centre plane.

Aft half, about half the length: a finished closed polished silver hull, smooth plates with exact seams, a real engine block at the stern whose three soft white drive rings fill about a third of the stern face, no flame, no other glow at the stern. A single low armoured command block sits on the spine just forward of the engine block. One small sensor cluster, low and protected, near the command block, never a mast or tower.

Forward half: open structure of slender polished silver ribs and longerons, machined and exact, evenly spaced, with no skin on them, clearly deliberate precision framework like a watch with its case open, never wreckage, never scaffolding, never construction junk. Two outer longerons run forward from the shoulders of the closed hull, joined by curved ribs over the top and around the sides, all polished and exact.

Held inside this open frame and plainly visible from above between the ribs: one long clear glass vessel full of soft white stored light, a smooth sealed glass cylinder with rounded domed ends running along the centre line from the front of the closed hull to just behind the bow. The stored light inside is soft white, even and calm, not flame, not lightning, not a star. The vessel is large enough to dominate the frame: the light is the ship, the frame is its mount.

Weapons, exactly five and no others: two large round lens domes in the shoulders of the bow, one each side of the centre line, mounted at the front ends of the two outer longerons. Each lens is a smooth dome of clear glass set in a plain silver ring, like a camera lens or a glass eye, no other detail on the glass. One lens dome turret on the upper centre line amidships, its smooth dome of clear glass in a plain silver ring facing up and outward so that from above it reads as a clean circle. One small gun turret on the upper centre line aft of the dome turret, with two plain heavy barrels pointing forward. One small flak turret on the upper centre line near the stern, a small matching cluster of short barrels.

No crystal blocks, no prisms, no shards, no gems, no faceted metal anywhere. Rainbow only as a thin fringe at the edges of glass where light catches it, and very little of it. Plain dark grey background, no text.

## G11 sheet (writer leg, with chair amendment on try 2)

Try 1 used the writer leg's prompt as written in leg_deepseek.md and was REJECTED: five glass domes (the bow pair duplicated at the stern), seven mounts. Try 2 added this chair amendment to the weapons paragraph, and it is the try delivered:

"Weapons, exactly five and no others, each set in a round cut-out in the mirror so it is plainly countable from above. THERE ARE EXACTLY THREE GLASS DOMES ON THE WHOLE SHIP: two large round lens domes in the shoulders of the bow, one each side of the centre line, each a smooth dome of clear glass in a plain silver ring like a camera lens or a glass eye; and one lens dome turret on the upper centre line amidships, its smooth dome of clear glass in a plain silver ring facing up and outward so it reads as a clean circle from above. NO DOMES AT THE STERN, no dome anywhere else. The remaining two weapons are NOT domes: one small gun turret on the upper centre line, set in a round cut-out with a dark mount that contrasts with the bright mirror, with two plain heavy barrels pointing forward; and one small flak turret on the upper centre line near the stern, also in a dark round mount, a small matching cluster of short barrels, small and dark, never a dome."

## G12 sheet, the Beam Line (writer leg's G12-alt, with chair amendment on try 2)

Try 1 used the writer leg's G12-alt prompt as written in leg_deepseek.md and was REJECTED on format: portrait sheet, top view rotated bow up, hull about 7:1. Try 2 added these chair amendments, and it is the try delivered: the sheet header specifies LANDSCAPE format with the bow pointing LEFT in both views; the proportions sentence adds "the hull is broad enough to read as a ship's hull, never a thin tube or rail"; the stations sentence describes the collars as "ring collars standing around the bench like hoops round a barrel" and the dome turret as "a silver collar standing around the bench with the dome inside it".
