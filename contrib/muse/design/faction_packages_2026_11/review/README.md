# Job 9: the faction packages, made reviewable (issue #3)

The six Job 2 faction packages, condensed into six one page briefings Ryan can read and rule on from his phone, plus one index of every question.

Files:
- glasswrights.md, hullbreakers.md, sunward_crusade.md, hegemony_navy.md, the_chorus.md, eclipse.md: the six one pagers, in that order (Glasswrights and Hullbreakers first, as the brief asked). Each page: who they are in three sentences, how they look, how they play, their admirals in a line each, and the three biggest open decisions in the package as multiple choice questions with the chair pick marked.
- question_index.md: all 18 questions, numbered continuously, one line each with the chair pick. Answer by number.
- legs/: the four legs the pages were built from (DeepSeek, Qwen, blind Rey, Argon).

How to use: read a page, answer its three questions by number (the index collects all 18). The chair picks are the defaults if a question does not matter to you.

## How the legs were used

DeepSeek and Qwen produced near identical pages and agreed on almost every pick; the blind Rey leg was the cleanest format and is the spine of these pages. Argon ran under the updated board rule (briefs and sources only) and is filed as a record: two of its finds were adopted because the packages back them (the Hegemony refusal question, also surfaced by the Rey leg, and its framing of the Chorus core eating question), and the rest was cut where it could not be backed.

Argon problems, for the record: it invents two core ids ("M-2 Meridian" for the Hegemony core, which is V-2 Vyre; "C-3 Crucible" for the Chorus core, which is H-3), it claims the Eclipse portrait question was settled by a ruling fixing one portrait per admiral (no such ruling exists; the question is live as Q17), it merges the Hegemony voice and overlay questions into one, and it garbles the Hullbreaker speed ruling. DeepSeek offered one settled question (the Hullbreaker species base, ruled Nov 1) as if it were open; the chair pages state the base as ruled instead. Qwen repeated the package's "fast ships" line for Hullbreakers before carrying the ruling correction; the chair page phrases it cleanly.

## Conflicts the legs found in the packages themselves

1. The cross faction appendix still calls the Glasswright look fix "faceless" under R66. The Nov 1 portrait ruling overturned that: real faces, normal speech, colour as an emotion tell only. The packages' head notes agree with the ruling; the appendix line is stale and should be struck when Opus next touches it.
2. The Hullbreakers package says "your ships are fast". R64 rules the opposite in fiction (about twice as slow, ships moving one at a time in pack order) and R67 keeps gameplay pace separate. The package never states the slow movement rule; flagged here so the package can be corrected.
3. The appendix separates Glasswrights from Eclipse by "a trapped light chest core" as if it were general. The Nov 1 ruling makes the core one Eclipse machine pattern only, forms varying by admiral. The Eclipse page follows the ruling.

## One structural note on the Nemesis drafts

The six drafted pairings do not form a rotation. Eclipse is drafted as the hunter twice (for the Crusade and for the Chorus), the Hegemony is never drafted as anyone's hunter, and only one pairing is mutual (the Crusade and Eclipse hunt each other). The questions keep each package's own draft, as the brief asked. If you want a clean rotation instead, say so when you answer and the set gets redrawn as one decision rather than six.

## Near miss

Memory's presence in Chorus play was asked by two legs and answered the same way by both (story and debrief only), but it is close to settled already: the bible keeps the Memory throat in story and dialogue. It is not counted among the 18. If you want it reopened, say so.
