# The Sunward Crusade

Who they are
The Vesper Reach made into a fleet: forty odd frontier systems the Hegemony wrote off when their stars began to dim, seceded and bound by one oath to stop the Siphoning before their worlds freeze.
They fly black iron ships crewed by unaugmented people who chalk every name on the hull, because the Registry deleted theirs.
They fight in line, they hold, and they pay for the dawn in names.

How they look
People: weathered, unaugmented humans, frost scarred skin, surgical scars where implants were cut out, oath brands, grey stubble, chalk dust on the gloves. High collared dark void coats, heavy shoulder plates.
Ships: black iron hulls, frost white markings, ember orange windows and engines, tall heat radiator spires glowing ember orange, heavy broadside decks, everything symmetric, no rust.
Colours: black iron, frost white, bright ember orange.

How they play
Commit early and hold the line: advance two or more hexes to power the broadsides, or brace and refuse the ground.
Torpedoes set targets burning, carriers are precious and few, and the fleet gets better the longer a straight fight lasts.
Bad at: rigidity. A flanked line turns slowly.

Admirals
Roderic Vex (Fire for effect): the line anchor; mass guns, name one target, hold the lane.
Malek Draven (Hunter's salvo): the hunter; torpedo first, set the board burning, call the debt on one marked ship.
Sera Ashgrove (Scramble all wings): the wing admiral; overwhelm with wings, keep the Hearth carrier alive, spend squadrons like the fleet can grow more.
Oryn Thale (Iron wall): the bulwark; screens up, damage refused, a slow advance the enemy can only delay.

Decisions

Question 7. Who hunts the Crusade as their Nemesis? (Drafted in the package: Eclipse.)
A. An Eclipse Archivist commanding the siphon defence. The enemy your whole war is about: patient, never retreats, returns once after death.
B. A Hegemony line admiral, the secession wound. The state that wrote your worlds off.
C. A Hullbreaker Warlord, the funeral scavenger. A raider picking over your dead.
Chair pick: A. The Siphoning is their war, and the Nemesis should be the war itself.

Question 8. Should the Roll of Vesper be a readable campaign screen?
A. Yes, a screen listing lost admirals, ships, and learned Nemesis weaknesses. The names your crusades paid in are always one tap away.
B. No, story text only. The Roll appears in scenes and is never a screen you browse.
C. Yes, but names only; intel stays on the debrief.
Chair pick: A. A crusade that pays in names should let the player read the bill, and the learned weaknesses make the screen useful, not just memorial.

Question 9. May the Crusade field captured hulls with their links ripped out?
A. Yes, captured Hegemony and Eclipse hulls appear in the Cinder Line yards from run 3, at Crusade prices. Strip and ransom rules stay unchanged for anything not kept.
B. No, prizes are stripped for salvage and credits only. The fleet stays purely Reach built.
C. Yes, but only as one flagship variant.
Chair pick: A. A written off frontier flying the enemy's own ships, links ripped out, is the Registry's arithmetic answered in kind, and it opens build variety without breaking the black iron identity. One leg (Argon) picked B on purity grounds; the other three picked A.
