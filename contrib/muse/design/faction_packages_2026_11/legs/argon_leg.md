Story Spotlight Beat — Umbra Vessane, Third-Housed
Beat Title: The Debt of the Third Seat
Narrative Setup: Born into the resource-starved Third House—long relegated to maintaining the outer-system Siphon Relays while the First and Second Houses monopolized the inner-orbit coronal harvesting—Umbra Vessane engineered a silent coup at the Keen-Array Drydocks. When a First-House inspection armada arrived to seize her experimental Null spires for "doctrinal overreach," Umbra did not power down the array; she inverted the Siphon Relays, draining the inspection fleet's reactor cores in ninety seconds to overcharge her own flagship's Corona arc coils.
Playable Spotlight Moment (Act II — The Cold Conduit): Umbra's strike group begins the battle surrounded in a dead-zone nebula with base capacitor generation cut by 50 percent. To survive, the player must anchor three Siphon Relays under fire, linking them to enemy capital ships to parasitic-charge her Null shredders and Corona arc batteries.
Character Payoff: Defeating the First-House Enforcer flagship without losing a single Siphon Relay unlocks Umbra's personal edict, Third-House Audit, permanently granting her fleet +15 percent Siphon transfer efficiency and forcing the High Conclave to recognize her seizure of the outer arrays as legitimate.

5. Nemesis
Primary External Nemesis: The Solar Mandate (Radiant Hegemony)
Ideological & Doctrinal Inversion: Where Eclipse survives through energy parasitism, sensor occlusion, and the cold patience of the dark, the Solar Mandate thrives on overwhelming radiant energy, total battlefield illumination, and the doctrine of absolute visibility. Mandate fleets are built around solar-lance batteries, flare-beacon networks, and reactor overcharge systems that turn their ships into miniature suns on the battlefield.
Mechanical Counter-Play Loop:
Illumination vs. Sunshade: Mandate flare-beacons and solar-lance sweeps strip standard cloak states in a radius, forcing Eclipse players to rely on Sunshade shrouds and Siphon Relay corridors rather than simple stealth.
Siphon Feast: Because Mandate ships carry oversized reactor pools to power their radiant lances, Eclipse Siphon Relays drain them at +50 percent efficiency, turning the Mandate's greatest strength into a liability if Eclipse can survive the initial illumination.
Vendetta Passive (The Blinding War): When fighting a sworn Nemesis fleet, Eclipse units gain +20 percent damage against illuminated targets and +15 percent resistance to radiant/thermal damage, representing centuries of adaptation to fighting in the light.
Internal / Crisis Nemesis: The Hollow Sun Conclave
Trigger Condition: Reached in the late campaign when the player's Siphon Network drains more than 60 percent of the sector's total stellar output, triggering a collapse in local star stability.
The Threat: A rogue coalition of disenfranchised Eclipse Houses and automated deep-space Siphon dreadnoughts that have embraced total energy consumption, seeking to drain entire stars to power a single, god-like Stellar Siphon.
Crisis Mechanics:
The Hollow Sun deploys Inverted Siphon Relays that drain energy from both friendly and enemy fleets unless destroyed within 30 seconds of deployment.
Their ships field overcharged Null spires that ignore 25 percent of standard Sunshade mitigation, forcing players to use active evasion and Siphon Relay positioning rather than passive shroud tanking.

6. Progression and Story
Faction Progression Loop: The Three Phases of Occlusion
Eclipse progression is governed by Siphon Capacity and House Ascendancy, evolving the fleet from parasitic raiders to a sector-spanning eclipse engine.
Phase I: Penumbra (Early Game) — Corona arc corvettes, Null shredder frigates, Basic Siphon Relays, local cloak emitters. — Parasitic Skirmishing: Low baseline resource generation supplemented by siphoning neutral relays and enemy wrecks.
Phase II: Antumbra (Mid Game) — Null cannon cruisers, Siphon Relay tethers, Sunshade projector modules, House Edict specialization. — Sector Blackout: Siphon Relays link across adjacent sectors to form persistent shroud corridors. Corona arc weapons chain across linked relays.
Phase III: Totality (Late Game) — Umbral siege dreadnoughts, Null spires, Fleet-wide Sunshade domes, Stellar Siphon super-arrays. — Total Occlusion: Entire battlefields are plunged into artificial eclipse. Enemy fleets fight with crippled sensors and drained reactors while Eclipse fleets operate at peak efficiency.

Act I — Shadows on the Periphery: Following the fracture of the Outer Relay Ring, the player assumes command of a scattered Eclipse flotilla, rebuilding the Siphon Network from derelict relays and recruiting disgraced House captains.
