# Status of the documents in this folder (read first)

Added after Artoo's review (finding 6) so no reader mistakes research history for settled direction.

- map_design.md is the pre-test design round (Nov 17, 2026, evening). Its builds 1-5 are proposals. Build 1 (plural sites) was prototyped the same evening and missed its pass numbers; its own sections 7, 7b and 7c record the sizing test, the objective follow-ups and the feel test, and builds 3-5 were not proceeded on the original argument. The recommendation language in its early sections is superseded by those results.
- mechanics_synthesis.md is the pre-test council synthesis. It is superseded wherever mechanics_results.md disagrees, notably the evasion framing: the bot already prices candidate movement evasion (stateView stamps the candidate path length), so the gap is the size of the incentive, not its absence; and stronger evasion values did not change movement shape in testing.
- consolidated_results.md and mechanics_results.md are the record of the first round of measurement. retest_results.md (added after Artoo's review) supersedes their two-pass and falloff verdicts: two-pass buys displacement at a measured combat price and fails the no-damage criterion at the tested budget, and the damage-band falloff shows no reliable effect in the paired retest. A second retest round (mult_retest_results.md) then put multiplicative scoring through the same per-controller treatment: its coverage and clustering gains replicate, each seat alone pays a combat tax, and with both seats scoring that way the taxes mostly cancel while the spread persists, at a pacing cost of about 1.5 rounds.
- Nothing in this package is an adopted rule change or a ruling by Ryan. Ryan's decisions so far cover running the tests and landing the package. The 1.25 speed and range feel setting, two-pass selection, multiplicative scoring, the damage-band falloff, and every other mechanic in these documents remain unadopted proposals with the test status the results record.
