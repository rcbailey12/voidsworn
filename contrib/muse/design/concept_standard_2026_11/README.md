## How the trial views were made

Generator: Muse's media pipeline (media.generate_image), image-to-image, one call per candidate, each call given the original concept (docs/ship_pipeline/concepts/C15s_23.webp) as the reference image plus one text prompt. No other model settings are exposed by the pipeline; the prompts below are the whole instruction each candidate got. A and B prompts are independent phrasings of the same request; C prompts are corrective, written after A and B were judged.

Side A prompt: "Draw the SAME spaceship from the attached reference image as a true orthographic side profile view: perfectly flat side elevation, no perspective, no three-quarter angle, port side facing the viewer, bow pointing left, stern at right. Keep the exact design: blunt rounded prow, rusty red-brown bow and foredeck with orange rust blotches, dark charcoal grey aft hull, olive green belly, two bow casemate turrets with twin barrels pointing forward, twin foredeck tubes with orange tips, three large horizontal banded cylinders on the deck, blocky bridge tower with a lattice mast, A-frame crane at the stern, dark keel fin under the bow, row of small round portholes along the aft hull. Single light from the upper left, neutral dark grey background, whole ship in frame, nothing cropped."

Side B prompt: "Technical concept sheet, side elevation of the exact spaceship in the attached reference image. Strictly flat orthographic profile, like a blueprint elevation: bow left, stern right, no angle, no perspective, no visible top deck. Identical design details: blunt rounded prow in rusty red-brown with orange rust blotches, dark charcoal aft hull, olive belly plating, two bow casemate turrets each with twin forward barrels, twin foredeck launch tubes, three big yellow-banded cylinders amidships, lattice mast above the bridge, A-frame crane at the stern, keel fin blade under the bow, small portholes in a row on the aft hull. One light from upper left, plain mid-grey background, ship fully in frame."

Side C prompt (corrective): "Draw the SAME spaceship from the attached reference image as a true orthographic side profile, bow left, stern right, perfectly flat, no perspective. Important: the two bow casemate turrets point FORWARD, so in side profile each turret must be drawn as a short horizontal cylinder seen from its side with two short barrel stubs protruding toward the bow (left). Do NOT draw the turrets as round faces looking at the viewer. Exactly these features and no others: blunt rounded prow, rusty red-brown bow with orange rust blotches, dark charcoal aft hull, olive green belly under the bow half only, exactly two bow casemates in profile, twin foredeck tubes, exactly three large banded cylinders amidships and exactly two smaller cylinders behind the bridge, lattice mast, one A-frame crane at the stern, dark keel fin under the bow, exactly one row of seven small portholes on the aft hull. Single light from upper left, neutral dark grey background, whole ship in frame."

Top A prompt: "Draw the SAME spaceship from the attached reference image as a true top-down plan view: camera directly overhead, perfectly flat orthographic plan, bow pointing up, stern down, no side of the hull visible at all, no keel fin (it is underneath and must not show). Keep the exact design from above: blunt rounded prow outline, rusty red-brown foredeck with orange rust blotches, dark charcoal aft deck, twin foredeck tubes on the centreline, two bow casemate turret housings, one on each flank, with twin barrels pointing up toward the bow, three large horizontal banded cylinders across the deck amidships, bridge tower top with lattice mast base, A-frame crane at the stern. Single light from the upper left, neutral dark grey background, whole ship in frame, nothing cropped."

Top B prompt: "Technical concept sheet, plan view of the exact spaceship in the attached reference image. Strictly flat orthographic top view like a blueprint: looking straight down, bow at the top of the page, stern at the bottom, zero perspective, no hull sides visible, the underside keel fin must NOT appear. Same ship: wide blunt prow, rust-red foredeck with orange rust blotches, charcoal grey aft deck, twin launch tubes on the centreline at the bow, a casemate turret housing on each bow flank with twin barrels aimed at the bow, three yellow-banded cylinders side by side amidships, bridge and mast base behind them, A-frame crane over the stern deck. One light from upper left, plain mid-grey background, ship fully in frame."

Top C prompt (corrective): "Draw the SAME spaceship from the attached reference image as a true top-down plan view, camera directly overhead, bow pointing up, perfectly flat, no hull sides visible, no keel fin. Strict feature count, nothing extra: exactly TWO casemate turret housings total, one on each bow flank, each with twin barrels pointing up toward the bow; exactly TWO foredeck tubes on the centreline; exactly THREE large banded cylinders in one row across the deck amidships; exactly TWO smaller cylinders behind the bridge position; one bridge and mast base; one A-frame crane at the stern; NO other turrets, NO extra cylinders, NO stern canisters. Colours from above: rusty red-brown foredeck with orange rust blotches, dark charcoal aft deck. Single light from upper left, neutral dark grey background, whole ship in frame."
