# Concept round 3: three armed variations per faction (issue #445)

Round 3 builds on Ryan's round 2 picks (FINDINGS R176 and R178). Each variation starts from the picked base sheet named in the issue, changes only what the variation says, and must read as an armed warship in its top view at thumbnail size (the round 3 amendment to gate line 1). The Chorus has no guns: its weapons are organs, held to the same plain-to-see standard. The Glasswrights have no base: the giant crystal direction was ruled out by Ryan, and the new grammar makes the ship itself the optic.

Files: for each variation, `<faction id>_<code>_sheet.jpg` (top view above, side view below, flat on, same scale, plain dark grey, no text) and `<faction id>_<code>_beauty.jpg` (three-quarter from the front and above, hero and one escort, near black, made image-to-image from the accepted sheet). `prompts.md` holds the chaired prompts. Council legs are filed as `leg_rey.md` (blind, written before any picture), `leg_deepseek.md`, `leg_qwen.md`, `leg_grok.md`, `leg_flash.md`.

Council note: all four legs landed. Qwen's leg derailed (it redesigned Hegemony, Eclipse and the Chorus wholesale against the brief) and was rejected as a drafting source; the chair prompts are built on DeepSeek's leg with Flash's and Grok's guards folded in where they agree with the brief. Nothing from Qwen is in the delivered prompts. The seat is recorded as landed but derailed, not empty.

## Marked failures, up front

No failure is hidden. The ones that matter most:

1. **S5, line 1 FAIL.** The delivered sheet has no lance at the bow and its guard carries four twin-barrel turrets per flank instead of barrel rows. Its sword plan is still the round's best Crusade plan, and the other two tries were worse (one mounted the lance on a tripod pointing backward, a line 2 hard fail). Delivered nearest, marked.
2. **A4, lines 1 and 6 FAIL.** The scatter racks doubled (two racks per flank, four comb groups in plan) with a second dome turret besides the flak, and the side view shows three hangar openings against the table's two. The alternate try fixed the counts but broke the palette outright with yellow-green camouflage, which all three Ashfall variations ban.
3. **Glasswright stern rings, all three variations.** Every Glasswright try drew six drive rings in two rows of three for the three asked; the model copies the G3 base sheet's stern and count wording never moved it.
4. **Barrel shape, all Crusade sheets.** The model will not draw short thick barrel stubs. Across nine Crusade tries the gallery barrels came out as long thin naval cannons, round turret discs, or lit port strips (the lit strips were banned successfully by try 3 on S4 and S6). Counts and placement are right on S4 and S6; the barrel proportions are not the brief's.
5. **Beauty escorts.** The round 2 fault is reduced, not cured. Seven of eighteen escorts are proper distinct frigates (H4, H5, A6, E4, E6, G5, and S4's hero-side read aside). The Crusade beauties drew the escort as a literal sea battleship with masts and funnels three times; E5, C4, C6 and G6 escorts are shrunk copies of their heroes; C5's escort has a visible eye and a fish face.

## How to read the tables

Each variation gets the twelve gate lines scored on the delivered sheet, in the chair's judgment: PASS, FAIL, or a pass with a note. Line 1 includes the round 3 armed read. Measured ratios are the chair's measure of the delivered plan view, length to beam. The beauty paragraph scores the beauty view separately; the sheet is the deliverable of record.

## What the model kept getting wrong, across the round

1. **Counts inflate or double, even stated twice.** Ashfall scatter racks doubled on every try of all three variations; hangar mouths became three or four pits; Glasswright stern rings were six for three on every try; A5 grew five engine bells for three. Naming each mount and its place helps placement, not number.
2. **Barrels will not be short and thick.** The Crusade gallery rows came out as long thin naval cannons in most tries. The two counters that worked at all were describing muzzles as cropped stubs and banning lit port strips explicitly; even then the delivered S4 and S6 barrels are long.
3. **Turrets multiply toward symmetry.** Hegemony tries 1 and 2 of H4 put turrets on the aft modules (and floated one pair detached). The guard that worked: exactly two turret objects in the whole picture, both named to the wedge, barrels outward.
4. **Lenses default to pods, eyeballs or blank discs.** Glasswright lenses needed three different wordings across three variations and still sit proud on G4 and G5. G6's ring mapping (rings as the mounts themselves) was the one clean solution.
5. **Things detach.** Eclipse engine cones floated off the stern (E5 try 1), a Hegemony turret pair floated beside the hull (H4 try 2), an S6 engine bell split off as a glow. Naming the attachment in the prompt fixed E5 and H4.
6. **The base picture's faults are inherited.** The A1 base's camouflage tint and the G3 base's six stern rings propagated into every child sheet. A base carries its errors as faithfully as its grammar.
7. **Escorts copy the hero shrunk,** in thirteen of eighteen round 2 beauties and still in five of eighteen here. Specifying the escort by what it lacks fixed Hegemony and A6; it did not fix the Crusade, whose beauties reached for a sea battleship instead, three times.
8. **Lit ports return unless banned by name.** S4 try 2 and S6 try 1 both lit the hull edges orange. The ban worked when stated as its own sentence.
