# Issue 631 image prompts: final accepted takes (chaired by Rey)

Each accepted beauty was generated image-to-image from an accepted anchor or from its own previous take, so the hide, glow and rendering stay locked across the family. Colour is not chased (R204): the anchors render grey-amber, the build sets bile yellow-green. The prompts below are the operative final-take prompts, with the reference each one started from; the full round-by-round chain is in designs.md's process record. Sheets were generated from the accepted beauty of the same ship with the standard three-view sheet prompt (top, side, front, whole ship in every view, features identical to the reference).

## C12 drone-spawn, final take v10 (references: chorus_C12_drone_spawn_v9.jpg, chorus_C13_spitter_v7.jpg for the stern)

Using the first picture as the ship reference and the second picture only as the reference for how this fleet's sterns look: draw the frigate from the first picture with its rear rebuilt to match the family. REMOVE the rounded drive bulb at the stern entirely. The hull now tapers at the back into layered, soft, translucent trailing membranes like the second picture's stern: thin veils of flesh streaming backward, lit faintly amber from within, soft and flowing, NOT stiff fins and NOT wings. Keep everything else from the first picture identical: the spine-cluster gun at the prow, the dorsal mound of boarding tendrils draping backward, the compact lumpy flesh hull, the two small sacs hanging below. Painterly realistic rendering, deep space, three-quarter view from the front and above. No text, no other ships.

(The take this edited, v8, established the weapon separation: the prow is a gun, a raised knoll of puckered flesh carrying a spine volley cluster, a dark central vent ringed by seven short thick bone spines angled forward; no tendrils anywhere near the front, every boarding tendril rooted in the dorsal mound and draping backward. v9 removed the stern fins for a vent bulb; v10 replaced the bulb with the family membranes.)

## C13 spitter, final take v7 (reference: chorus_C13_spitter_v6.jpg)

Using the attached picture as the exact design reference, draw THE SAME destroyer creature-ship with ONE cleanup: its prow is too sharp. Round the prow off into a blunt, soft curve of flesh; keep every other feature, proportion and marking of the ship exactly the same, in the same three-quarter view: the lumpy asymmetric hull, the spine-volley glands in the prow and dorsal knolls, the flank sacs, the trailing membranes at the stern. Painterly realistic rendering, deep space. No text, no other ships.

(The accepted v6 it cleaned up was drawn from scratch on the weapon data: no mouth, both guns drawn as spine glands, puckered vents in knolls of lumpy flesh, after the quill-fan and maw takes were rejected as artificial.)

## C14 brood-sac, final take v11 (reference: chorus_C14_brood_sac_v10.jpg)

Using the attached picture as the exact design reference, draw THE SAME vast carrier creature-ship with ONE removal: every small pod is GONE. No pods inside the openings, no pods drifting around the ship, nothing else in the frame but the ship itself. The five launch openings around the rim stay, but they are EMPTY, their insides dark with a faint warm glow deep within. Keep everything else identical: the giant central sac of folded scarred flesh, the ragged pore patch on top, the modest front throat, the teardrop sacs hanging below, the trailing membranes at the rear. Painterly realistic rendering, deep space. No text, no other ships, no small craft of any kind.

(The chain behind it: a fresh build on the lore, one giant sac with squadron openings around its outside; then a scale pass (pods at a tenth of the hull still read too big), a hive pass (tiny pods packed in the openings), an even-dispersal correction pulled back from a full ring to five openings, and finally the pods removed from the art at Ryan's direction.)

## Family line-up v2 (references: the five accepted beauties and anchors)

Using the five attached pictures as exact design references, one ship per picture, draw a family line-up of EXACTLY FIVE creature-ships in a single row in profile against deep space. NOTHING else in the frame: no pods, no small craft, no extra objects of any kind. The five ships descend strictly in size from left to right: the first (maw titan) largest by far; the second (sac carrier) clearly smaller, about 60 percent of its length; the third (bladeback cruiser) slightly smaller than the second; the fourth (lumpy destroyer) much smaller, about a third of the first ship's length; the fifth (compact frigate) smallest, under a quarter of the first ship's length. Every ship whole and fully in frame. Same painterly realistic rendering and flesh materials across all five. No text, no labels.
