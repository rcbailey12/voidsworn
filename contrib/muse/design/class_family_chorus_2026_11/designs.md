# Issue 631 final designs: Chorus class family (chaired by Rey; takes accepted by Ryan, Nov 13, 2026)

Council round: blind Rey leg first, then DeepSeek, Qwen, Grok and Flash, all gated (one early Gemini attempt ran on the wrong model and was discarded uncredited). The council designs were then worked through nine rounds of takes against Ryan's verdicts; what follows is the accepted end state, not the council's first pass. Anchors: C10 The Ninefold Maw (bio-titan b_boss, 28.0) and C11 bladeback cruiser (b_razor, 14.5), accepted earlier.

Standing rules applied throughout: creatures never machines; swelling tapering bodies, no flat faces; sacs hang below the body; weapons are organs set in grown hulls (factions.md: guns sit in throats, spines and sacs); engines are pulsing vents or trailing membranes; every pictured feature is one of the ship's in-game weapons or systems, listed per ship below; colour is set in build (R204), the anchors render grey-amber and the build sets bile yellow-green.

## Board flag: frigate kit change (Ryan's ruling, Nov 13, 2026)

data.js gives b_drone one weapon only, the boarding tendril turret. Ryan ruled the gunless frigate does not stand: "send the frigate back on guns." The accepted concept therefore carries a second weapon, and this delivery asks the Board to make the matching data change: b_drone gains bioplasma fixed forward, giving w: [['bioplasma','F'],['tendril','T']], the bladeback cruiser's kit at frigate scale. The concept below is drawn to that loadout.

## C12, drone-spawn (data id b_drone), frigate, game length 6.5, size 0.48

Final take: chorus_C12_drone_spawn_v10.jpg; sheet: chorus_C12_drone_spawn_sheet_v2.jpg.

A compact hull of lumpy grown mass, short and quick, nothing sleek about it: the fastest hull in the faction (speed 5, evasion 15, both best in faction) drawn as a dense little knot of flesh rather than a dart. The prow carries the gun: a raised knoll of puckered pink flesh with a dark central vent ringed by a crown of short thick bone spines angled forward, the spine volley (bioplasma F, per the Board flag above; Chorus name for bioplasma is spine volley, its material spines). Amidships on top sits the turret: a low mound of knotted muscle holding five heavy boarding tendrils (tendril T, range 1), cables of flesh with blunt rounded tips, no claws, draping backward over the hull and down the rear sides at rest. Two small sacs hang below the hull: the regrow reserve (regen 3). The stern tapers into the family's layered trailing membranes, soft veils lit faintly from within; no fins, no bulb, no nozzle. Ryan's edits across the rounds: guns restored (above), front protrusions shortened, tendrils moved fully aft so the prow reads as a gun and nothing else, fins removed, then the stern rebuilt into the family membrane language.

Top view: a compact knotted oval, gun knoll at the prow, tendril mound amidships, membranes streaming aft. Distinct from every sibling from above (R233): the only hull whose top reads as a knot with a crown.

## C13, spitter (data id b_spitter), destroyer, game length 9.5, size 0.6

Final take: chorus_C13_spitter_v7.jpg (accepted v6 with the prow rounded); sheet: chorus_C13_spitter_sheet_v1.jpg.

No mouth at all. Both of the spitter's guns are spine volleys (data: bioplasma F and bioplasma T), so both are drawn as spine glands: clusters of puckered vents and short spines sunk in knolls of flesh that are part of the hull's lumpy mass, one cluster low in the prow behind the rounded nose, one cluster on the dorsal knolls amidships. The hull is lumpy, asymmetric, scarred flesh over deep mass, broad in the middle and short at the tail; nothing tubular, squared or manufactured survives in it (the early takes' quill fan was rejected as manufactured, and its bow throat was removed once the weapon data showed both mounts are the same spine weapon). Round bile sacs bulge along the lower flanks, swelling before a volley; ragged regrown hide patches mark the shoulders (regen 4). Drive is the family's trailing membranes at the short stern over pulsing vents.

Top view: a broad lumpy shield, rounded prow, no ridge (that is C11), no ring (C14), no knot crown (C12).

## C14, brood-sac (data id b_brood), carrier, game length 16.5, size 0.9

Final take: chorus_C14_brood_sac_v11.jpg; sheet: chorus_C14_brood_sac_sheet_v5.jpg.

One central mass: a giant sac or hub of folded, scarred flesh, the second-largest hull in the family, slow (speed 2) and vast. Around the sac's rim sit five launch openings with thick fleshy rims, dispersed evenly with wide stretches of plain flesh between them: the hangar (hangar 3, spore squadrons). The openings are drawn empty, dark with a warm glow deep inside; squadron capacity is an internal, build-sheet fact, after Ryan first corrected the pod scale (pods at a tenth of the hull read too big to fit) and then removed the pods from the art entirely. On top of the sac is a ragged patch of pores: the spore cloud (spores T). Set in the front folds is one small glowing pore: the spine volley (bioplasma F). A row of teardrop sacs hangs below the hull: spore feed storage and regrowth flesh (regen 6). The rear trails the family's membranes, lit from within.

Top view: a vast disc of flesh, five rim openings at even intervals, the pore patch off-centre on top. Unmistakable from above, and unlike C10 in every particular: C10's front is the feature and its sacs are belly rows; C14 is all chamber, its front deliberately modest.

## Process record

Ryan's verdicts, Nov 12 to 13, 2026: the v1 beauties were rejected as giant creatures (the council legs' animal similes had carried into the prompts; Chorus ships are grown hulls with organs set in, per the C10 and C11 anchors). The spitter's spine fan was rejected as artificial; the frigate's sleek and then comical takes were rejected; the carrier went through stern-led, hub-and-spoke and hive readings before the giant-sac take landed, with the squadron scale corrected twice and the pods finally removed from the art. Every accepted take carries exactly its in-game weapons, with the one flagged exception of the frigate's ruled gun.
