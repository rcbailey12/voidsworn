# Design pack: the Vesper Reach station (station_T2)

Build reference: contrib/muse/design/station_concept_2026_11/station_T2_beauty.jpg and station_T2_scale.jpg (accepted, R206). Build path: issue 373, through the ship recipe, once the build sheet drawn from this pack passes (R203). Council: blind Rey leg first, then DeepSeek, Qwen, Grok and Flash (R231); chaired by Rey, Nov 13, 2026. Items marked [call] are council design calls adopted by the chair; section 9 belongs to the Board.

## 1. The shape of record

T2 is the Hegemony fleet station of the Vesper Reach after its write-off: the same station as the accepted in-service concept T1, dead and half dark. From above it is a wheel: a core stack of 9 deck drums, a habitat ring on a segmented bearing track with one open gap segment, four docking arms at crossed angles and two heights, five towers at five heights with five different caps, radiator banks under the core. In T2 the ring is seized off-angle with its gap segment moved off its T1 clock position, one spoke is buckled, one arm is torn to a stub, towers are dead with one leaning, radiators are cold, debris rests where it drifted to a stop against the arms and the ring, frost and dust coat the shadow side, and torn holes show deck frames inside. The silhouette that must survive the build, at the default battle camera (r 170) and from above: the ring with its moved gap, the drum core, the four arm angles including the stub, the five tower heights including the lean, the radiator fan below. The three reads that carry it at r 170: the dark mass, the one lit quarter (section 5), and the one slow moving part (section 5). Positions of the damage (gap clock position, buckled spoke, stub arm, leaning tower) are lifted from the accepted pictures, not invented as numbers [call]: gap in the lower-right quadrant of the top view, stub arm opposite the lit quarter, lean toward the ring. It must read without the orange blocker outlines explaining it.

## 2. Rulings that bind this build

- R109 (Ryan, Nov 5, 2026, on the old repaint): "we need WAY more surface detail, it looks super plain and just like regular shapes right now." Forces: one built set piece, parts at three scale bands (hex-scale core and ring, cruiser-scale arms, metre-scale fittings); no repaint logic, no smooth primitives.
- R206 (Ryan, Nov 11, 2026): T2 accepted ("T2 damaged"); forces: this concept and this damage state, build-sheet gate first (R203).
- R98 and R209 (Ryan, Nov 4 and Nov 11, 2026): broken pieces never visibly empty; insides are built (deck levels, bulkheads, frame stations, machinery spaces), lit or burning, never flat caps or hollow shells. Forces: the INSIDE work in sections 5 and 6.
- R113 (Ryan, Nov 7, 2026): no triangle caps; triangles are reported figures only. R116: the station is priced in ms on the scene gate like a ship (12.5 ms, 75 percent of the 16.7 ms frame; 1080p median and 4K GPU), the whole piece including debris and the moving part in one accounting [call].
- R151 (Ryan, Nov 9, 2026): kits are per faction; only invisible plumbing (mount sockets, scale rules, LOD and bake pipeline, export) is shared. Forces: a Hegemony kit. T1 and T2 are two states of one Hegemony station, so the kit carries both states' part variants; that is within-faction reuse, not a shared kit.
- R207 and R233: the concept guides, the builder designs; the Board makes the calls between concept and build. Forces: corrections to what would not work as a real built object are listed on the build sheet; this pack's open questions go to the Board, not to Ryan.
- R236: game data, the lore bible and the faction guide govern; the concept is a base. Forces: sizes from godot data (section 3), palette and break language from docs/playbooks/factions.md.
- R217: every part modelled as the thing it is (a berth collar has depth, a lip, a dark bore; a tower cap is its system), and the fit check applies, including the moving part's swing arc and the seized ring clearing the buckled spoke (a bend, not an overlap).
- Faction guide (Hegemony): machined, modular, horizontal; flush panels, straight seams, repeated modules; pale grey, steel-blue trim, implant-blue light; never rust, never warm light, no ornament; breaks in modules at bulkhead rings, rims torn and scorched, breach anchors on module joints; insides white corridors, blue emergency light, racks of identical equipment.
- The orange strips [call, unanimous across the council]: the few dull orange battery strips in the accepted pictures are a damage state, not the faction's lit language. They appear only in T2, only at torn frames, dull and desaturated, small total area. The T1 assembly from the same kit never carries them.

## 3. Shared build rules, as they apply to a station

Recipe: the ship recipe read at station scale (build sheet with INSIDE section, fit view and fit report, paint stack, scene gate), station parts kit under tools/blender/kits per issue 373. Scale from game data: blocker_visuals.gd gives each blocked station cell a piece 14.0 units across; the station is sized from the hexes it blocks, at least three hexes wide, taller than any ship. hull_table.gd SHIP_K 1.6, cruiser 14.5, so a cruiser is 23.2 units; the accepted scale view puts the station a little over twice that across. Build targets [call]: widest span tip to tip 52 units; ring outer diameter 34 units; core stack about 20 units tall (9 drums at about 2.2 units each); overall height about 32 units from lowest radiator tip to highest tower cap, to be verified at sheet time against the longest ship entry in hull_table. The exact hex footprint is game data the Board owns (section 9); the 52-unit span satisfies the three-hex minimum (3 x 14.0 = 42) and the scale view. Insides are built once per module type and instanced. Debris and the moving part are part of the station piece for gate accounting, not separate props [call]. Collision stays with the blocker hexes.

## 4. Lore and kit

Built by the Hegemony Navy as the Vesper Reach fleet station: berthing for cruisers and tenders, cargo pod staging at the arms, command and medical decks in the lower core, habitat decks in the ring, heat rejection from the radiator banks under the core. Written off after the single event in section 6: evacuated, never repaired, nobody aboard, nothing maintained since. Kit language is the Hegemony ship language at building scale: drum modules joined at bulkhead rings, repeated modules with batch variation, berth collars as deep docking sockets with guide-light rings, tower caps that are sensor and comms systems. The kit carries both states: T1 (turning ring, lit collars, about 650 lights, fresh panels among older) and T2 (everything in this pack). Fleet or refinery first: fleet. T2 is the accepted build reference and the fleet station is the game's primary blocker; the refinery (T3) is a different plan and a different kit and follows on its own.

## 5. Parts inventory

Counts are the record unless marked [call].
- Core: 9 deck drums stacked on a vertical axis; 8 drum-to-drum bulkhead rings (the break lines and breach anchors). Drum functions assigned on the sheet: command and medical low, machinery mid, storage and habitat support high [call]. Two pre-existing breaches: a top rim tear with peeled petals showing the top deck's frames, and one side hole showing two deck levels [call, from the pictures' torn holes].
- Ring and bearing track: habitat ring in 6 arc modules [call]; segmented bearing track in 12 segment slots, 11 closed plus the 1 open gap segment [call]; in T2 seized, gap moved per section 1. Ring window strips are the ring's light carriers.
- Spokes: 4, at the ring plane, offset from the arm angles [call]; the spoke on the break side (spoke 3 in the sheet's numbering [call]) is buckled, bent out of plane, attached at both ends, casing split at the bend.
- Docking arms and berth collars: 4 arms at crossed angles and two heights, in the 18 to 24 unit band; three whole, one torn to a stub at its root collar, rim scorched. One berth collar per arm, each with depth, lip and dark bore (R217); all dead in T2. Guide-light fittings exist at the collars but are dark in T2 except where counted in the light map below. Cargo pods [call]: two, one clamped dead at a surviving arm's collar, one loose in the lodged debris; scale cues only. There are no queued pods and no active berths in T2; anything livelier belongs to T1.
- Towers and caps: 5 towers at 5 heights, 5 caps, each cap its system: comm lattice array, twin-dish yoke, sensor dome with whip needles, crane pedestal with parked lattice boom, banded antenna drum [call, shapes from the pictures]. All dead; tower 1 (comm array) leans a few degrees off its mount, base ring cracked.
- Radiator banks: flat banks under the core, cold. Radiator language is built into the flat shape [call]: micro-groove flush panels, header manifolds and pump blocks at the roots, no cell gridlines and no glass read, so they do not read as solar arrays at r 170 (the concept round's mark, answered in modelling, not by changing the shape).
- Lights: about 90 implant-blue in T2, in 7 stubborn clusters [call]: three clusters of 12 on core drums 1 to 3, two clusters of 15 on the ring quarter opposite the break, one cluster of 10 on the equipment ring at the tower bases, one cluster of 14 on core drum 5 facing the lit quarter. Plus 5 dull orange battery strips [call], one each at: the stub break face, the buckled spoke root, the core side hole rim, the leaning tower's base joint, the ring gap's torn edge. The T1 map (about 650) stays in the kit as the in-service state; the maps are never blended.
- Debris: a lodged field where it drifted to a stop against the arms and ring: about 12 chunks of 0.6 to 4.0 units plus a baked scatter of small plates near the stub [call]; visual only.
- Frost and dust: a baked mask on the shadow-side hemisphere, fixed to a station-local axis matching the battle light [call].
- The moving part [call, the council's convergent answer, specified]: the torn outer section of the stub arm, about 9 units of arm box with its dead berth collar at the far end, still hanging from the stub's break ring on torn conduits and one bent tether, turning in a slow tumble (one full turn in about 240 seconds, with a slight swing of about 3 degrees on a roughly 40-second cycle). It needs no power, it is the only animated node on the asset, and it cannot be mistaken for the ring turning. At r 170 it reads as the one thing still settling, its collar catching the light once per turn. Fallback if the fit check fails on its arc: shorten it to 6 units and keep the collar; never substitute a second moving part, and the ring never turns.

## 6. How it breaks

The damage story [call, adopted as the design story; whether lore fixes the cause is section 9]: a fire in a docked cargo pod's cells blew the berth collar module and then the arm root module joint, on a breach anchor line. The plume and pressure ran down the spoke on that side and buckled it; the buckled spoke drove into the bearing race and seized the ring off-angle, shoving the gap segment off its clock position. The reactor scrammed and vented coolant, killing the radiator loops and the main power spine; the plume knocked tower 1 off true. The arm tore at its mid module joint, leaving the stub and the tethered section. Power survives only in module-local battery cells: core drums 1 to 3, the ring quarter opposite the break, and the equipment ring at the tower bases, feeding the 90 lights and back-feeding the battery strips. The dark half is the break half, the hemisphere centred on the stub arm, the buckled spoke and tower 1; the lit quarter is the opposite hemisphere plus the low core band. The crew evacuated; the Hegemony wrote the station off; debris lies where it stopped and frost holds on the shadow side because nobody ever came back.
Break lines in battle (Hegemony rule: at bulkhead rings and module joints; breach anchors on the joints):
- Core: primary lines at the drum 3/4 and drum 6/7 bulkhead rings, secondary at the other rings. Whole drum groups come away, rims torn and scorched; insides show the white corridor ring, radial bulkheads, racks of identical equipment, deck frames at the rim.
- Ring: at the gap segment's two ends and at the spoke root joints; arc modules come away showing the ring corridor tube, window strips, frame stations, cross bulkheads.
- Spokes: at core and ring joints; the buckled spoke is pre-weakened and goes first; insides are cable trays and pipe runs over a corridor.
- Arms: at the root joint (further hits shorten the stub), the mid-arm joint, and the collar joint; segments and collars come away as whole rings showing the corridor tube and rack rows; a collar break shows lip and bore, never a disc.
- Towers: at cap and mast joints; caps come away whole as their systems. Tower 1 is pre-weakened but sheds only its cap and top mast segment, never a full fall across the ring [call].
- Radiators: at the header manifolds; banks shear in halves showing header channels and pump blocks; no fire from radiator hits (the coolant vented long ago).
- Fire state [call]: hits on the dead station show small insulation and dust fires seated in torn frames, self-limiting in look; no fuel flames, pending the Board's call in section 9.
Every break face shows the insides above; no flat caps, no hollow shells (R98, R209).

## 7. Must-keep and must-avoid

Must-keep: the wheel silhouette (ring with moved gap, drum core, four arm angles including the stub, five tower heights including the lean, radiator fan below); the counts (9 drums, 4 arms, 5 towers, 1 gap segment, about 650 lights in T1, about 90 in T2); the damage positions from the pictures; the tethered arm section as the single moving part; the light map and the 5 battery strips at torn frames only; radiator language on the banks; frost on the shadow side; debris lodged against structure; modelled insides at every break; the Hegemony palette and flush modular panelling; family resemblance to T1 carried by ring, core and arms.
Must-avoid: any weapon or mount (the station carries none; do not invent any); triangle caps and flat break caps; hollow shells; rust; warm light as a design language (the orange strips are a damage state only); solar-array gridlines or glass on the radiators; sea language (no hull, no prow, no engine bells; the stack ends in collars and platforms); a turning ring or any second moving part; fresh panels anywhere on T2; live berths, queued pods or lit guide rings outside the light map; count drift from the record.

## 9. Open questions for the Board

1. Hex footprint: the exact blocked cells for this station (three or four) and the centre offset. Game data owns the cell list; it can move the 52-unit span target in section 3.
2. Burning insides: may battle hits on this vented, uncrewed station show burning spaces (R98 and R209 allow lit or burning), or only the dust and insulation fires of section 6?
3. Armament in canon: does the lore bible give Hegemony fleet stations any self-defence weapons? This pack carries none per the job's ground rule; if canon says otherwise, the Board reconciles it before the sheet is cut.
4. T1 timing: T2 builds first from the shared kit. Does the Board want the T1 in-service assembly built in the same job as a second state of the sheet, or later?
5. The write-off cause: section 6 adopts the pod-cell fire chain as the design story. Does lore fix that cause in game text, or does it stay ambiguous to the player?
6. Battery strips: static, or a faint pulse? (Flash leg's question; an FX call with a dead-station mood on one side and readability on the other.)
