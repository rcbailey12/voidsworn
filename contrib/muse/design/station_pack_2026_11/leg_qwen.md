4. LORE AND KIT
Who built it: the Hegemony. What it was: a fleet station of the Vesper Reach, a patrol and resupply wheel: berth collars for cruisers and tenders, cargo pod staging at the arms, command and medical decks in the lower core, habitat decks in the ring, comms, sensor, crane and antenna towers at five heights, heat rejection from radiator banks under the core. It was written off after a single event (section 6): evacuated, not repaired, because frontier repair cost exceeded replacement. Nobody is aboard; nothing has been maintained; frost and dust have settled on the shadow side.
Kit language (faction guide): machined, modular, horizontal. Flush panels, straight seams, repeated modules; drum modules repeat with batch variation (fresh replacement panels among older ones, inherited from T1). Colours pale grey with steel-blue trim; light is implant-blue; never rust, never warm light in service states; no ornament. Break language: modules separate at bulkhead rings between modules, rims torn and scorched; breach anchors sit on module joints; insides are white corridors, blue emergency light, racks of identical equipment. The kit is the Hegemony station kit; it carries part states for both assemblies (lit collars, turning ring, 650-light map for T1; dead collars, seized ring, 90-light map, battery strips, debris for T2), because per-faction kits may carry state variants of one station.

6. HOW IT BREAKS
[CALL C14, the damage story, one event] The write-off was a single chain beginning at arm 3's berth: a cargo pod cell fire blew the berth collar module and then the arm root module joint, exactly where Hegemony breach anchors sit; the plume and pressure wave ran down spoke 3 and buckled it, and the buckled spoke drove into the bearing race, seizing the ring off-angle and shoving the gap segment off its clock position; the reactor scrammed and vented coolant, killing the radiator loops and the main power spine; the arm root plume knocked tower 1 off true, leaving it leaning; the arm itself tore at its mid module joint, leaving the stub and the tethered sleeve. Power now exists only where module-local battery cells sit: core drums 1 to 3 (lower command and medical), the ring quarter opposite the break (habitat arcs), and the equipment ring at the tower bases. Those cells feed the 90 stubborn implant-blue lights and back-feed the dull orange emergency strips at torn frames. The crew evacuated; the Hegemony wrote the station off; nobody repaired it and nobody is aboard, which is why debris still lies where it stopped and frost holds on the shadow side. The dark half is the break half: the hemisphere centered on the arm 3 stub, spoke 3 and tower 1; the lit quarter is the opposite hemisphere plus the low core band.
Break lines in battle (Hegemony rule: modules separate at bulkhead rings and module joints; breach anchors on module joints):
Core stack: primary break lines at the drum 3/4 and drum 6/7 bulkhead rings (module batch joints), secondary at the remaining drum rings. What comes away: whole drum groups, rims torn and scorched. Insides at each break: white corridor ring, radial bulkheads, racks of identical equipment, deck frames at the rim, blue emergency strips at the break face (damage state).
Core top and core side tears: pre-broken; further hits peel petals and shed deck frames, showing the machinery space under the top drum and two deck levels at the side hole. Never a flat cap.
Ring: break lines at the two gap-segment ends and at the four spoke root joints. What comes away: arc modules of ring box. Insides: ring corridor tube with window strips, frame stations, cross bulkheads at module ends.
Spokes: break at core joint and ring joint; buckled spoke 3 is pre-weakened and goes first. Insides: cable trays and pipe runs over a white corridor.
Arms: break at root module joint (further hits shorten the stub), mid-arm bearing joint, and collar joint. What comes away: arm segments and collars as whole rings. Insides: corridor tube with rack rows; collar breaks show the lip and dark bore, never a disc.
Towers: break at cap joint and mast module joints; caps come away whole as their systems. Tower 1 is pre-weakened: [CALL C15] it may shed its cap and top mast segment only, not a full fall across the ring, to protect the silhouette and the gate.
Radiators: break at header manifold joints; banks shear in halves showing header channels and pump blocks; no fire from radiator hits (coolant vented long ago).
Fire state default: [CALL C16] hits on the dead station show insulation and dust fires only, small, seated in torn frames, self-limiting in look; no fuel flames, pending Board ruling in section 9.
