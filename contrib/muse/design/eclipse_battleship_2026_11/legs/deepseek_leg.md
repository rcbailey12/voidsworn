1. Headline

The Eclipse battleship is Eclipse wane, a cp 6, no-shield, no-hangar line anchor built around two forward Corona arcs, two Null cannon turrets, and a Null spire. Its defining strength is sustained fleet anchoring: high hull, armor, regen, reanimate, and inherited Repair swarm let it hold a mid-to-late Eclipse fleet together while the Zenith repairs and Jackals screen. Its defining cost is that it is slow, expensive, and cannot be a shield tank or carrier; at 590 on main / 630 on rules15 it is deliberately dearer than the Zenith cruiser, and if it is the first Eclipse ship to fall it returns under Archive restore unable to fire that round.

2. Role in the fleet

The Zenith cruiser is a cp 4, 145 hp, armor .3 support hull with Repair swarm and one Corona arc. It can repair and contribute, but it cannot survive battleship-grade focus, cannot carry two heavy forward lances, and cannot be the fleet's single "hold here" piece. Eclipse wane does that: it is the armoured centre that lets a Zenith stay behind it and repair, while Jackals and Motes use Fold drive to pass rocks, wrecks, and enemy ships and take flanks. A fleet with one Wane looks like this: Wane as the slow centre, Zenith behind at repair range, one or two Jackals as screen, and Motes as fast objective or escort ships. It is a shipyard purchase and mid-to-late run piece, not a starting hull, per the pack's starting fleets.

It serves best the Harvester + Jackal admiral style, because the cruiser can keep the battleship repaired while the Jackal adds damage, and the Harvester + two Motes style, because the battleship gives that defensive escort fleet a real anchor. It is worse with three Jackals, which want speed and tempo the Wane cannot provide. It should never be used as a solo raider, a carrier, a squadron platform, or a chaser of fast evasive ships; it has no hangar, no squadrons, no shields, speed 2, turn 1, evasion 0. It also should not be the flagship if the owner wants Archive restore to apply, because the restore never affects the flagship.

4. Weapons and ability

The loadout is two Null cannon turrets for turret coverage, two Corona arcs on the prow for the battleship's defining forward punch, and one Null spire turret for point defence. It uses only shipped Eclipse weapons. I do not put Null shredder on it: that is boss-only today, and putting it on a player battleship would be a boss-only expansion not needed for the role. No new weapon is proposed.

Rough expected damage per firing turn, accuracy applied and cooldown-2 weapons averaged as half contribution per turn, ignoring crit, armor, and pierce: Null cannon each = 3 × 6 × .74 = 13.32; two = 26.64. Corona arc each = 40 × .78 = 31.2 per firing, ÷2 = 15.6 per turn; two = 31.2. Null spire = 2 × 4 × .80 = 6.4. Eclipse wane total ≈ 64.24 per turn. Zenith: two Null cannons 26.64 + one Corona arc 15.6 + Null spire 6.4 = 48.64 per turn. This is labelled rough. Bastion and Dominion cannot be numerically compared from this pack because their weapon damage, accuracy, and cooldowns are not listed; any number would be invented. By mount count only, Bastion carries two heavy broadsides, one Oath lance, one Spark flak; Dominion carries two heavy lances, one ion cannon, one picket laser plus hangar. Wane carries five Eclipse mounts, but two are the same forward lance pair. I mark the Bastion/Dominion numeric comparison as not checkable from the pack.

Ability: Repair swarm is the right inheritance. It is shipped, it restores 20 percent hull to the most damaged ship within 3 hexes including itself, cooldown 3. On 260 hp that is 52 hull, meaningfully larger in absolute terms than on a frigate or destroyer; it fits this hull and no smaller one because the battleship is the fleet anchor and the repair scales with its hull. It also avoids giving a player hull the boss/Crusade-only Spinal cannon. If the owner wants Spinal cannon instead, I flag it as an alternative only: it is today boss and Crusade dreadnought only, so putting it on a player Eclipse battleship is a ruling-level escalation and would need a balance pass and likely a price or CP increase; it is not in the main design.

5. Name

Display name: Eclipse wane. Data id: d_wane. Class label: Battleship. "Eclipse" plus lowercase noun from light and its absence follows mote, flare, zenith; "wane" is the diminishing of light, fitting the lost-sun and memory motif without using glass or reflection, which belong to the Glasswrights. The id is in the free d_ family and is stated. Runner-up: Eclipse dusk. It lost because "dusk" is a generic time of day and less tied to the waning-light idea than "wane"; also "wane" better suggests the Archive's memory of a sun being reduced.

6. The look

The silhouette is a broad, layered sunshade fan: a dark violet-black armoured flat belly, with two or three forward-swept heat-shield fans layered over the central hull, the ship sheltering behind them. The forward hull carries two Corona arc lance housings in a matched symmetric pair, aimed forward, recessed behind a white-gold eclipse corona ring. Two Null cannon turrets sit on dorsal saddle mounts, one left and one right, aimed forward or outward, never backward into the ship. One Null spire turret sits on a ventral ball mount for point defence. The engines are main engine bells surrounded by eclipse rings, with no flaming engine bells. Heat radiators are layered fan-edge panels, not funnels. The sensor array is a forward antenna cluster of short rods and plates, not a mast. The Archive core, if it shows, is a recessed circular violet-white housing at the centre of the hull under the forward superstructure tier. Palette: dark violet-black plates, white-gold eclipse corona, violet-white inner glow; effects are white-gold corona light, violet-white Null fire, cold white repair swarms. No green glow anywhere. The shipped code line data.js:126 still says hull dark grey-green, trim green, glow green; that line is stale against the lore bible and faction package and the build must not copy it.
