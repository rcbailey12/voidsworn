# Concept sheet prompts (issue #48)

The four prompts used to generate the concept sheet from the chaired design, recorded as sent. Images live in Rey's local workspace (see README.md).

## 1. Bench worker

Concept art of an alien species design, full body at work: a Glasswright, a low wide weaver creature with a broad segmented thorax of pale half-translucent flesh that grades into living glass, four support limbs planted wide beneath the body, and four arms (an upper strong pair holding a crystal workpiece, a lower finer pair weaving fine glowing glass thread drawn from small pores at the wrists). Its head is low and forward, broad and shallow, with two deep green lens eyes set flush side by side in the face, a heavy segmented brow, and a tiny flush reed grille mouth. From its upper back grow five thin crystal sail vanes like a ship's crystal vanes, mostly folded back along the body, one vane angled forward with a bright edge focusing a thin line of light onto the workpiece. Clear crystal and silver tones, faint rainbow fringes at the vane edges, a soft pale green-white glow of stored light deep in the chest. Dark glass forge interior background. The anatomy itself is alien, not a human or elf in costume. No metal, no brass, no steampunk, no hardware, no fabric sails, no masts, no wings.

## 2. Face study

Concept art portrait close-up of an alien face: a Glasswright head, broad and shallow, wider than it is tall, made of pale half-translucent living flesh that grades smoothly into clear living glass toward the front of the face. Two large deep green lens eyes set side by side, flush with the surface of the face, smooth and slightly convex like grown lenses, no eye whites, no sockets, no rims. Above them a heavy brow made of five to seven segmented plates of denser glassy flesh in a shallow arc. Low in the face a very small mouth: a short row of thin vertical crystal reeds set completely flush into the flesh, subtle and still. No nose, no ears, no hair. A faint pale green-white glow of stored light deep under the skin, subtle frost-like lattice texture inside the glassy regions. Dark background, soft forge light. The face is expressive and alive, alien in its anatomy, not a human face, not an elf, not a grey alien, no mask, no jewellery, no metal, no steampunk.

## 3. Admiral (Tamsa)

Concept art of an alien admiral in a command posture: a Glasswright, a low wide creature with a broad segmented thorax of pale half-translucent flesh grading into living glass, four support limbs planted wide, four arms held ready, head low and forward with two deep green flush lens eyes and a heavy segmented brow. Six large crystal sail vanes spread wide in a layered fan from its upper back, clear crystal with silver edges and faint rainbow fringes where the light splits, glowing softly with pale green-white stored light, vanes echoing the crystal vanes of a starship. Bright steady glow in the chest. Starship command deck background with a crystal and silver starship visible through a viewport. Regal and calm without standing upright. Anatomy fully alien, not human, not an elf. No metal armour, no brass, no steampunk, no hardware, no fabric sails, no masts, no wings, no throne.

## 4. Ancient elder

Concept art of an ancient alien elder: a very old Glasswright, the same species as a low wide glass weaver but at the far end of age. Elongated segmented thorax carried very low in a full wide eight-point stance on four strong support limbs plus four smaller arms, a broad heavy head with two forest-deep green flush lens eyes and a brow of many heavy segments. Eight or more enormous crystal sail vanes, their edges frosted and bloomed like frost flowers on glass, chipped and worn, furled down the long back like layered glass. Pale flesh clarified almost to glass, a deep saturated slow green glow of stored light in the trunk, rainbow fringes at the frosted vane edges. Venerable, slow, revered, not monstrous. Dark archive hall background with soft light. Anatomy alien and organic, grown glass not crystal statue. No metal, no brass, no steampunk, no hardware, no fabric, no masts, no wings.
