# Glasswright class family: chair designs (issue 1146)

Chair: Rey, from a blind Rey leg written first, then DeepSeek and Gemini
legs (the Gemini seat ran on Hydrogen, the disclosed picker deviation of
record). Qwen was blocked at its daily Qwen3.8-Max limit and Grok by the
cloud bot wall; both seats are recorded as blocked, and the delivery stands
without them. Fit order throughout: data.js first, then the faction sheet,
then the CD5 anchor, then the pictures. Where a picture and a table
disagree, the table governs. Picture slips are listed per ship.

SHAPE AND SILHOUETTE ONLY. The glass finish is still being worked (issues
1058 and 1141), so every picture in this pack is drawn in a plain neutral
clay material. Nothing here rules on colour, transparency or glow.

## The family, from the anchor

Anchor: CD5 (Ark, 26.0 by 8.47, data key not in the escort roster; feature
table r3 and R298 settle it). One flowing leaf of twisted pulled strands in
a single continuous skin, rigid and symmetric about the long axis, lit from
inside in the finished ship. Weapons are grown optics and centrepieces:
lenses grown into the skin with no pod, bracket, ring or seam, forward
lenses pointing forward. The prism lance is stored light out through a bow
optic. Engines are stern drive optics with a soft white bloom, never flame.
Breaks are ragged shatter paths at the lens bays; each large optic is its
own break piece; one fewer breach anchor than the class base.

Never change across the family:
1. The single rigid leaf in one continuous skin, with the low centreline
   ridge in silhouette where the braided core runs.
2. Grown bow optics as the primary weapons; secondary lenses grown into
   hull swells; no visible mounts; stern drive optics, no flame.
3. Shatter break logic: paths bracket the lens bays, optics shear as whole
   pieces, never straight module rings.

Never copy from the anchor:
1. CD5's ark scale and its exact proportions.
2. Its exact two-lance spacing and its three evenly spaced secondary
   optics as a template; each hull carries only the optics its data slots
   name.
3. Its exact shatter layout; paths follow each hull's own lens bays. (The
   Aperture keeps the three-optic stern count by role, as the fleet's
   second hull, not by cloning the arrangement.)

Optic conventions:
- One forward slot, one grown optic or optic cluster at the bow. The
  Sliver's three shard shots leave through three small beads grown in one
  bow swell: one feature, three exits.
- A turret slot on a grown hull is one lens in a centreline swell; the arc
  is emission steering, no moving mount (flagged by DeepSeek, adopted).
- Stern drive optics step by role: Sliver 1, Lancet 2, Prism 2, Aperture 3.

## SLIVER (frigate, data key i_wisp, old code I14_23)

Length 6.5, beam 1.9. hp 30, shields 12, speed 7 (fleet-fastest), evasion
28, ability blink. The dart: the narrowest leaf, nothing on it that does
not make it faster.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Narrow rigid leaf, pinched waist, low centreline ridge, one continuous skin |
| Prism shards F (shard, 3 shots) | Three small grown optic beads in a tight row along the axis inside one bow swell at the leaf's point, stations 0 to 14 percent |
| Drive | ONE stern drive optic blended into the taper |
| Break lines | One ragged shatter path at 55 percent, bracketing no bay: the hull snaps fore and aft |

Variant note: the lancer variant (i_wisp_l) swaps the shard cluster for
one short forward lance; recorded here, not drawn.

Picture slips: the beauty's stern optic reads as a small knob rather than
a blended bulb, and the sheet's side view adds a small ventral fin that is
not in the design. The three beads read correctly in the sheet's top and
front views.

## LANCET (destroyer, data key i_blade, old code I11_37)

Length 9.5, beam 2.8. hp 60, shields 15, speed 6, evasion 24, ability
blink. The duellist: a longer, narrower leaf built around one heavy light.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Elongated rigid leaf, low centreline ridge |
| Star torpedo F (startorp, 1 heavy shot, dmg 38, cd 2) | ONE large grown optic in the centre of the bow face, the hull's centrepiece; the torpedo is light, so no tube, no bore, no lip |
| Prism shards F (shard, 3 shots) | Three small grown beads in a crown around the star optic's rim (DeepSeek's nesting, chaired: it solves the two forward slots competing for one bow) |
| Drive | TWO stern drive optics side by side |
| Break lines | Ragged shatter paths bracketing the bow lens bay and at 62 percent; the star optic is its own break piece |

Chair note: both external legs gave the Lancet one wide stern optic; the
chair kept the blind leg's two, so the drive count steps 1, 2, 2, 3 across
the four hulls instead of flattening at the destroyer.

Picture slips: the beauty seats the star optic in a visible collar ring;
the faction law is grown seamless, and the sheet's front view (ring much
reduced) is closer. The sheet's front view grows beads all around the rim;
the table's three, in a crown on the upper rim arc, govern.

## PRISM (cruiser, data key i_song, old code I15_11)

Length 14.5, beam 4.4. hp 90, shields 26, speed 5, evasion 16, ability
surge. The CD5 pattern at cruiser scale, and the only hull besides the
anchor that carries two forward lances.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Broad rigid leaf, pronounced strand twist, low centreline ridge |
| Prism lance F slot 1 (pulsar, 2 shots) | One long pulled-glass forward lance at the bow, on the optical axis |
| Prism lance F slot 2 (pulsar, 2 shots) | The second long forward lance, the pair exactly the CD5 pattern (chair over DeepSeek's stacked throat apertures, which invent a bow form the faction sheet does not carry) |
| Needle beam T (turret, 1 shot) | One grown optic in a centreline swell at 54 percent |
| Drive | TWO stern drive optics |
| Break lines | Ragged shatter paths bracketing both lance bays and the needle swell, at 45, 65 and 82 percent |

Variant note: the Warded prism (i_song_w) puts one lance on a turret and
adds shields; recorded, not drawn.

Picture slips: the drawings stack the two lances vertically on the
centreline (the beauty's first take spread them to the flanks like tusks
and was redrawn); the table fixes the pair and their stations, and the
Board picks the final arrangement against CD5 when it builds. The sheet's
top view adds two small stern fins that are not in the design.

## APERTURE (carrier, data key i_carrier, old code I16_11)

Length 16.5, beam 5.6. hp 140, shields 28, speed 4, evasion 12, ability
launch, hangar 2. The broad leaf: the fleet's second hull, and the one
whose silhouette has to say carrier without pods or wings.

| Feature | Design |
|---|---|
| Hull | Very wide rigid leaf, thick centreline spine, one continuous perimeter |
| Prism lance F (pulsar, 2 shots) | ONE long pulled-glass forward lance at the centre of the bow |
| Needle beam T (turret, 1 shot) | One grown optic in a swell on the spine at 34 percent, ahead of the bays |
| Hangar (2 wings) | TWO smooth oval apertures piercing the leaf, one on each side of the spine, stations 42 to 78 percent; closed loops inside the single skin, rims annealed smooth (Gemini's through-apertures, chaired over belly mouths and internal vaults: the silhouette read is the strongest and the one-skin law survives) |
| Drive | THREE stern drive optics in a row |
| Break lines | Ragged shatter paths across the outer bay arches at 40 and 80 percent and bracketing the needle swell; each aperture rim is a break edge |

Picture slips: the beauty draws the apertures round rather than oval and
gives them a slight raised rim; read the rim as the annealed frame, and
the table's oval govern. The stern three read as a knob cluster in the
beauty; the sheet's top view separates them.
