# Glasswright class family (issue 1146)

Concept designs for the four remaining old Glasswright hulls, derived from
the built anchor CD5, so Ryan can pick a three-view sheet and feature
table for each ship. Pictures and design text only; nothing here is a
build sheet.

SHAPE AND SILHOUETTE ONLY: the glass finish is still being worked (issues
1058 and 1141), so all four new hulls are drawn in a plain neutral clay
material. Nothing in this pack rules on colour, transparency or glow.

Files:
- designs.md: the chair's feature table for each ship, the optic
  conventions, the never-change and never-copy lists, picture slips, and
  what could not be verified. Start here.
- prompts.md: the image prompts used, for regeneration.
- sliver_beauty.webp, sliver_sheet.webp: the Sliver frigate (i_wisp).
- lancet_beauty.webp, lancet_sheet.webp: the Lancet destroyer (i_blade).
- prism_beauty.webp, prism_sheet.webp: the Prism cruiser (i_song).
- aperture_beauty.webp, aperture_sheet.webp: the Aperture carrier
  (i_carrier).
- family_lineup.png: a scale composite of the four sheets' side views
  plus CD5's own settled side render, at true game lengths (6.5 to 26.0).
  See designs.md for why it is a composite and not a generated picture.

Council: blind Rey leg first, then DeepSeek and Gemini legs, Rey chairing
(R89, R231). The Gemini seat ran on Hydrogen; the picker offers no Flash
label, the same disclosed deviation as issues 1141 and 1145 and the Neon
audit council. Qwen was blocked at its daily Qwen3.8-Max limit (brief
parked for a one-message retry) and Grok by the cloud bot wall; both seats
are recorded as blocked, not empty, and the pack stands without them.

Chair headline calls: the Lancet's two forward slots nest, shard beads in
a crown around the star optic (DeepSeek's arrangement); the Prism keeps
CD5's two-lance pattern over a stacked-throat alternative; the Aperture's
hangar is two oval apertures piercing the leaf inside one continuous skin
(Gemini's arrangement), not belly mouths or internal vaults; stern drive
optics step 1, 2, 2, 3 across the four hulls.
