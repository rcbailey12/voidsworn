# Pyre-Barge paint calibration, issue 163

Double-click **RUN_PYRE_ROUND5.bat**. It queues GPU lane A through the PC watchdog, which waits while Ryan plays. It uses installed free tools and the retained round 4 cache. It never generates a mesh, starts an AI model, downloads anything, builds V3 or replaces shipping assets.

The default input is `%TEMP%\voidsworn_issue146`. Its stage 5 `.blend`, native low-resolution V2 GLB, donor normal, reference selection and original captures must exist. A missing cache stops the job. This runner cannot recreate it. `--cache` can select another retained round 4 work folder. Scratch files, logs, private game imports and saves go to `%TEMP%\voidsworn_issue163`; `--work` must remain inside TEMP. Prior captures and measurements are archived there before a new bake.

Results go to `docs/shots/ship_pipeline/pyre_round5/` and `Downloads\voidsworn_test\board\issue-163\shots`. The issue folder also gets the full PNGs, silhouette masks, colour-class images, final GLBs, parity transcript and selected draft evidence. The reference master and its presentation transform are copied unchanged from round 4.

Phases are `build`, `capture`, `game`, `bench`, `checks` and `sheets`, or `all`. For example, `RUN_PYRE_ROUND5.bat --phase capture`. Build accepts `--route V1` or `--route V2`. Capture always compares today, V1 and V2 together, and rejects stale material/build hashes. The same round 4 studio, real game scene, battle radius 170, close radius 49.92, front/side/quarter cameras and material surface stations are retained. A white unlit silhouette pass excludes sky and stars from colour measurement and is restored before the benchmark.

`round5_build.py` calls the retained round 4 build functions with a new region-based assignment and material graph. Region growth measures actual connected surface patches; its flattened vertex output is discarded. Soot shades vary by region. Only bounded small regions become red trim plates; lamps and engine throats retain explicit emissive roles. Rust is a thresholded irregular mask within the soot coat, with additional rust on real edges. Short-radius bevel-normal differences bound convex wear, geometry AO darkens recesses, and a separate low-frequency field adds scorch. Grayscale CC0 PaintedMetal004 photo scratches and displacement provide surface detail. V1's smart-UV margin falls from 0.0015 to 0.00015 to give the visible panels more texels at the same 4K resolution. V2 retains its native UVs and cached donor normal. Photo colour is never projected onto the hull, and no concept or AI albedo enters the bake. Roughness stays between 0.61 and 0.88; coated metalness starts at 0.07 and curvature wear approaches 0.76.

`round5_probe.gd` extends the unchanged round 4 probe and adds silhouette images. Separate black/white masks show actual emitting texels on V1 and V2, using a linear emission threshold of 0.01. Their projected area is reported separately from the art's estimated bright-red bin. `round5_bench.gd` runs three alternating pairs of the cached round 4 and final round 5 materials on both unchanged meshes. It uses the existing 100-frame warm-up, 360-frame measurement, 20 shared ships, 1920x1080 Forward+ and 4x MSAA. These are isolated rendering measurements, not full-battle frame rates. The main capture timings and every paired measurement are retained, including runs above the nominal 3.9 ms ceiling.

`round5_sheets.py` reports nearest-colour shares using one fixed set of sRGB anchors: soot `(40,39,38)`, rust `#6a2c22`, trim `#b8402a` and bright red `#e0402c`. One common warm-hue gate prevents neutral steel highlights from being counted as rust just because they are bright. It uses the exact battle and close crop rectangles on ship pixels only. Reference art is rigidly rolled using the retained round 4 transform, then resized to V1's projected width with its aspect preserved. A colour match means every bin falls within 10 percentage points of the reference in both crops. These are rendered appearance bins: neutral steel and trophy colours join the dark bin, and bright red is a proxy rather than an emissive-channel area measurement. Reference lighting and camera direction remain approximate. Classified PNGs and masks expose the measurement for review. Archived drafts are measured again with this same classifier.

The verifier compares triangle-coordinate fingerprints with round 4 independently of UV splits, vertex order and material slots. It also checks export/reimport counts, source mesh and recipe hashes, the GPU, camera resolution, complete captures, PR checks and unchanged shipping scene/map hashes. `verification.json` keeps colour and performance results separate from evidence integrity. `blob_sweeps.json` holds fresh human judgements on the new captures; paint cannot repair fused geometry, so a colour match does not grant a clean construction cell or shipping approval.
