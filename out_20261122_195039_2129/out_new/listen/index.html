Quietly triumphant instrumental reward-screen theme for a dark space strategy game, 80 BPM, 4/4, D Dorian, 16 bars. Warm brass and strings, calm and dignified, not cheesy. A solo cello states a warm four-note motif, the notes D, A, F, E; muted French horns and full low strings answer and swell to hard-won relief, then settle on a held open fifth that can lead back to the start. Real acoustic orchestra, mature and restrained, no vocals, no choir.

mus_battle_sunward_stemloop
battle music prototype, STEM-READY LOOP (source for the first stem-separation test), 40 s
Dark orchestral space fleet battle score built as one continuous loop, instrumental, 96 BPM, 4/4, D minor, 16 bars. Four parts that all play in every bar from the first to the last: deep measured war drums; cellos and double basses on a driving low ostinato; low brass carrying a grave four-note motif, the notes D, A, F, E, varied every four bars; a sustained pad of strings and forge-metal resonance. Even energy and density from start to finish; the interest comes from a chord progression that moves every two bars and from the motif's variations. Starts at full texture and the final bar leads straight back into the first. No vocals, no choir.
