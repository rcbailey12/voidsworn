Starting point `5c90ed933`. The Wounds V2 switch, fleet timing code and earlier performance work are unchanged.

- **Glasswright mouth (`I15_11`).** The saved DEFAULT_B and PROOF_A `w2w_I15_11__c26` cells show the flat dark glass disc described in `1741_DETAIL` and `1741_HITS_I15_11`. I found the matching close-up in the PC relay, not the two sheets under those names. `fx_breach.gd` now builds two offset layers along the real polygon cut, with narrow seam strips and a splintered lip.
