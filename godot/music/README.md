# godot/music: adaptive music (owner: music and portraits helper)

Direction (R37): brass and tactical percussion in battle, a synth drone on the map, organ and machine sounds only as faction flavour. Tracks (R40): free licensed tracks first, CC0 or CC BY only, every one listed in `CREDITS.md` with source, author and licence.

## Files

- `music_player.gd`: the player. One node for the whole game.
- `music.json`: which track plays where (title, map per sector, battle calm and intense layers, stings, faction flavour) and per-track gain. Generated by `tools/music/process.py build` from `tools/music/selection.json`.
- `tracks/*.ogg`: the edited tracks (cut, loop seam crossfaded, loudness levelled to about -18 LUFS for loops and -16 for stings, Ogg Vorbis q4).
- `CREDITS.md`: per track the title, author, source page, licence and licence URL, and the exact attribution text. The same attribution line sits in `music.json` as `credit.attribution`, ready for a credits screen. Regenerate with `python3 tools/music/process.py credits` (no audio needed) after editing `tools/music/selection.json`.
- `test/music_check.gd`: headless check (`godot --headless --path godot --script res://music/test/music_check.gd`, prints MUSIC CHECK PASS).

## For G2 (the hooks I need from you)

```gdscript
var music = preload("res://music/music_player.gd").new()
add_child(music)                                   # under Main, once
music.set_volume(settings.get("music_volume", 0.7)) # new setting: a "Music" slider beside "Volume" on the settings screen
music.set_on(settings.get("music", true))           # new setting: music on or off
music.play_title()                                  # title screen
music.play_map(sector)                              # campaign map, sector 1..3
music.play_battle(player_faction, enemy_faction)    # battle start
music.play_battle(player_faction, enemy_faction, true)  # sector flagship or finale fight: the boss theme becomes the intense layer
music.feed({"close": n, "shots": n, "hull": f, "flagship": f, "mine": n, "enemy": n, "boss": b})  # once per activation or round
# or music.set_intensity(x) with your own 0..1 value
music.victory() / music.defeat()                    # battle end; signal sting_finished(kind) when the sting ends
                                                    # (a battle started with boss = true wins with the champion fanfare)
```

- The player makes its own `music` bus sending to Master, so your master volume and compressor still apply on top.
- `feed()` keys are all optional: `close` enemy ships within 3 hexes of yours, `shots` hits this round, `hull` your fleet's hull left (0..1), `flagship` your flagship's hull left (0..1), `mine` and `enemy` ships alive, `boss` a boss fight.
- The intensity follower rises in about 2 s and falls in about 4 s, with the hand-over between 0.38 and 0.62, so one big hit does not flip the music back and forth.
- Missing track files are skipped quietly: the game runs with no music if `tracks/` is empty.
- Free statics on quit: none (the player holds its streams as instance data).

Organ and machine sounds appear only as Voidsworn and Deathless flavour (R37). Loops: six tracks use the author's own loop (whole file, 20 ms click guard); the rest are cut where the music after the loop point best matches the loop start (`process.py loopfind`, score in `music.json` per track, all 0.55 or better, most above 0.75) and crossfaded 0.5 to 4 s. Levels: loops about -18 LUFS (intense -17, boss -16), stings -16, true peak under -1 dBFS.

Rebuild: copy the raw pool from the PC (`Downloads\voidsworn_test\music\raw\`), then `python3 tools/music/process.py prep <raw>` and `python3 tools/music/process.py build <raw>`. Change picks in `tools/music/selection.json`. `tools/music/seamcheck.py` measures the jump at a loop's wrap.

## How the layers work

A battle starts the calm layer and the intense layer together (intense silent) plus an optional faction bed. Intensity crossfades them; the calm layer never drops below about -16 dB so the pulse stays under the brass. The enemy faction's flavour wins (you hear who you are fighting), with your own faction as the fallback. Flavour can replace the calm layer, the intense layer, or add a bed under both (`factions` in `music.json`).
