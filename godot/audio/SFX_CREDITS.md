# Voidsworn sound effect credits

Hand-filed at the issue 101 audio intake (Nov 3, 2026). Every recorded clip in
`godot/audio/clips/` comes from a free, open licence: CC0, or CC BY with the
attribution line filed below (owner ruling, FINDINGS R82; house licence rule
R40: no NC, ND or SA). Per-set licence lines for sets added at intake are in
the table at the bottom of this file.

## CC BY sources (attribution required)

### Little Robot Sound Factory, Sci-Fi Sound Effects Library (CC BY 3.0)

Source: OpenGameArt, "Sci-Fi Sound Effects Library" by Little Robot Sound
Factory. Downloaded Nov 3, 2026 (zip 42,262,851 bytes).

Required attribution line, as specified by the author:

"Attribute Little Robot Sound Factory, and provide this link where possible:
www.littlerobotsoundfactory.com"

The author also asks that they be contacted if the library is used in a
project, and names the licence as Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

## Clips that predate the intake

The 52 clips in 27 sets that shipped before this intake are Kenney CC0 and
Sonniss GDC bundle material, royalty free with no attribution required, as
recorded at the top of `sfx_data.js`. The Sonniss clips stay under the
owner's Oct 25, 2026 ruling (FINDINGS D22): existing clips only, no new
Sonniss material was pulled at this intake.

## Intake set licences

Sets added or extended by the issue 101 intake, with the licence of every
take in the set. Conversion at intake: mono Ogg Vorbis, from the source
files above, unmodified apart from channel mixdown, format, and one noted
two noted edits (torpLaunch: the 5.0 s thruster takes are cut to 1.4 s
with fades, the only way a launch one-shot can use them; deathRun: the
5.0 s engine loops carry a fade out over their second half so the cue
falls away instead of cutting off).
