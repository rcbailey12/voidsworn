# Portraits: Chorus and Eclipse in their own settings (R68)

What to look at:
- `chorus.webp`, `eclipse.webp`: every admiral and Nemesis of the faction as the game frames them. Each one now has its own setting, pose and light (Ryan, Nov 1: "so the admirals and nemesis don't all look the same").
- `chorus_scars.webp`, `eclipse_scars.webp`: each Nemesis, then the same Nemesis with each scar it can earn, painted onto the new picture (not pasted on).

What changed:
- The Chorus and Eclipse admirals were moved into new settings earlier today (Matriarch over a planet it is eating, the Mending nested in a gutted warship, Nekhet in a siphon hall draining a star).
- Their twelve Nemesis now carry scars painted on the new pictures. Two tries were made for every scar; the pick is the one where the scar reads and the background stayed clean.
