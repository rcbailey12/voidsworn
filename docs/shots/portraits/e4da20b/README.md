# Portraits: Ashfall Hullbreakers in their own settings (R68)

What to look at:
- `hullbreakers.webp`: the four Hullbreaker admirals and six Nemesis as the game frames them, each in its own place and light.
- `hullbreakers_scars.webp`: each Nemesis, then the same Nemesis with each scar it can earn (a burn through the mane and scales, a small iron eye ring with a red lens, and a claw scar through a blinded eye), painted onto the new picture.
- `hullbreakers_phone.webp`: the phone sheet with each admiral big and the earlier paintings as alternates.

What changed:
- Built on Ryan's A1 pick (the void hunter with the mane), with the Predator look taken out: the head is covered in dull, matte, grown scales (like a pangolin), not a metal mask or helmet; no quill dreadlocks, no tusks or horns round the mouth.
- Grax slumps on a scrap throne under hanging trophies, Sable stalks a red-lit airlock corridor with a long knife, Mott prowls a salvage hangar, Vesh welds at a forge bench.
- The Nemesis: a launch bay, a cockpit over a burning wreck, a radar room, a forge, an open airlock under a pale moon and a gutship cockpit.
- It took four rounds: the first heads came out as bare skulls, then rusty riveted caps, then the word "horn" grew bull horns on most of them, so the horns were taken off by edit. The implant scar first grew into a riveted face mask; it is now one small iron ring round one eye.

Calls Ryan can veto (Claude's picks):
- The heads read as scaled lizard or pangolin heads with two eyes; the four small eyes from the design do not show at this size.
- Sable and Nemesis 6 have small spikes at the cheek behind the jaw (not tusks round the mouth); Nemesis 3's head has round knobs grown into its scales.
- Nemesis 4 stands before a pale moon rather than the dark Hollow Sun disc: the try that kept the dark disc kept the metal discs on its cheek.
- The harness, belts and shoulder armour stay iron and leather; only the face is grown hide.
- Vesh's scales catch a bright highlight in the forge light and look a little shinier than the others.
