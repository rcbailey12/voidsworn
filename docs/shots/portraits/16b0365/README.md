# Portraits: Glasswrights in their own settings (R68)

What to look at:
- `glasswrights.webp`: the four Glasswright admirals and six Nemesis as the game frames them, each in its own place and light.
- `glasswrights_scars.webp`: each Nemesis, then the same Nemesis with each scar it can earn (a burn that clouds the crystal and blackens the neck, and a web of cracks across the crystal), painted onto the new picture.
- `glasswrights_phone.webp`: the phone sheet with each admiral big and the earlier paintings as alternates.

What changed:
- Every Glasswright is faceless: the head is a faceted crystal growing out of a long neck of milky, see-through flesh with pink sinew and glowing glass threads (Ryan's pick A: organic, with glass grown into living tissue, not a gem helmet).
- Tamsa launches glass starlings in a carrier hangar, Vell looks back over its shoulder in a hall of mirrors, Brin sits before the Hollow Sun, and Saro (painted for the first time) focuses a beam of light through a lens in an observatory.
- The Nemesis stand in a workshop, by a window over a crystal shore, before a star chart, under a spotlight, beside a pillar of light and in a hall of glowing cells.
- Saro and Nemesis 2 kept growing a human face under the crystal whenever they faced the viewer; an edit that removes the face fixed both.
- Nemesis 1's burn scar painted a face on twice; it was redone with "no face" spelled out.

Calls Ryan can veto (Claude's picks):
- The oil-paint pass is light (0.30 to 0.40) on the Glasswrights, because a stronger pass painted faces into the crystal; they look a little smoother than the other factions.
- The burn scar shows as a smoky cloud inside the crystal plus a scorched patch on the neck.
