# E33 hitstop and shake, with CM8's shipped values as the third candidate

For the Ryan review round. This is a values sheet, not a capture: hitstop and death slow motion are temporal and a
still cannot show them. The round needs one short recording (or a playtest hit) under each set.

## The three candidate sets

| | A: E33 low end | B: E33 high end | C: shipped now (CM8) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hitstop, normal hit | 2 frames (0.033 s) | 6 frames (0.100 s) | 8 percent of the punch duration, clock at 0.08 speed, 0.6 lerp |
| Hitstop, kill or crit | 7 frames (0.117 s) | 12 frames (0.200 s) | same window and depth as a normal hit |
| Camera shake, normal hit | 0.15 s positional | 0.30 s positional | trauma decays in about 0.3 s from a normal hit |
| Camera shake, capital destruction | 0.35 s positional | 0.45 s positional | about 0.45 s from the heaviest hit |
| Death slow motion | not in E33 | not in E33 | 0.48 s at 0.35 speed (A17 proposes 0.50 to 0.80) |

## Where the numbers live

- E33 ranges: `contrib/muse/reviews/fx_audit_2026_11/PACK.md`, item E33 (A5 sheets synthesis section 2.3, still open).
- CM8 shipped: `godot/fx/fx.gd`, `func _update_pauses` (`now + dur * 0.08`, `_pause_depth = 0.08`,
  `_trauma = maxf(0.0, _trauma - delta * 2.4)`), `func death_slowmo` (`_death_slow_left = 0.48`,
  `spd = minf(spd, 0.35)`), and the lerp at `_pause_depth` in `_process`.

## What to judge

1. Normal hit: does A read as a tap and B as a punch, or does B feel like a stutter on a 20-ship board? C's 0.08 depth
   is heavier than both E33 ends because it slows the whole effect clock to 8 percent for the pause.
2. Kill: E33 gives 7 to 12 frames; C uses the same window as a normal hit, so a kill has no extra weight from hitstop.
   The death slow (C) is the kill's accent instead.
3. Capital death: C's 0.48 s at 0.35 is the slowest candidate and sits outside A17's 0.50 to 0.80 speed range (0.35 is
   slower than 0.50). The review should add a 0.35 s at 0.6 speed candidate if C is close.
4. Shake: A and B differ by 2x in duration. On capital deaths the 0.45 s end is the one to watch against the 0.48 s
   slow motion, so the two accents do not stack into a double kick.

## How to run the review recording

The shipped values need no build change. To record an alternate, set the same fields in a probe:

```
# in a scene probe, before the hit
gfx._pause_depth = 0.08        # A/B: use a higher depth (closer to 1.0 = less stop)
gfx.timing  # not used here; hitstop is in fx.gd
gfx.death_slowmo()             # starts the 0.48 s at 0.35 slow
```

No value was changed in this pass. The three sets are ready for the single owner review round the FX settlement asks
for (E33: one battle scene with shake and hitstop at the range ends, played by the owner).
