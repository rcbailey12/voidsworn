# Pyre-Barge round 2: nine-cell sweep

These are route prototypes at the requested review stage, not production sign-off. **Clean** means the named form reads cleanly in this view. **Rebuild by hand** identifies the remaining authoring or bake cleanup. A clean cell does not certify every bolt or hidden face.

The reference is today's approved assembled-salvage Pyre-Barge, retained in the first column of the [matched sweep](sweep_C19_51.webp). The earlier single-hull concept does not describe this later four-mass donor. Bow is right. Top and side use the original battle lighting. The below inspection row adds the same upward fill to every route; the original unfilled captures remain in the issue-111 shots folder. Benchmarks never use that fill.

Inspect the full [A+ grid](cells_Aplus.webp), [baked-detail grid](cells_Bake.webp), and [reused-parts grid](cells_Reuse.webp). Each has bow, middle and stern from top, side and below. The [battle and close comparison](comparison_C19_51.webp) and [actual game captures](ingame_C19_51.webp) establish the appearance at player distances.

The A+ route is my pick. It gives the most controllable clean forms and the densest authored surface. It is still more regular and blocky than today's donor. Its remaining work is deliberate shape and fitting design, rather than recovering usable geometry from AI lumps. The copied-detail route proves that broad waves can be suppressed, but the [head-on and grazing proof](wavy_panel_proof.webp) also shows that useful detail is softened. Medium-scale ghost shapes elsewhere prevent calling the whole baked ship wave-free. The reused-parts route preserves some irregular machinery, but a flat mounting face alone does not make a soft donor chunk crisp.

No backward guns, raked stems, keels, mast ladders, rigging or smokestack funnels were found in the reviewed captures. New roots use visible raycast mounting plates. The automated contact check covers those root anchors, not every pipe union or child fitting. No shipping queue status was changed.

Not checked: hidden interior faces, all possible orbit angles, damage and wreck states, rotating weapons, full combat effects, 4K performance, or owner approval. The current task is a visual route comparison; these are not ready to replace live game assets.
