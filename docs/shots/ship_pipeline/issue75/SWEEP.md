# Shape sweep: prototype verdicts

These are visual verdicts for the retained route comparison, not production sign-off. `Clean` means the named form reads cleanly in that view. `Rebuild by hand` names what a finished hull still needs. No shipping queue status was changed. Automatic export, finite-position, contact and gun checks are separate and passed.

Reference paintings: [C19_51](../../../ship_pipeline/concepts/C19_51.webp), [D21_23](../../../ship_pipeline/concepts/D21_23.webp), [B16_23](../../../ship_pipeline/concepts/B16_23.webp). The current Pyre-Barge follows Ryan's later assembled-salvage ruling, rather than the earlier single-hull painting. Current approved faction palettes also supersede the paintings' green Eclipse and red/white Chorus paint. Naval tells in the old paintings are not copied.

## The Ninefold Maw

[Top, side and below comparison](sweep_B16_23.webp). B uses living tissue, veins and chitin with no panel grid, mechanical decals or metallic response. Broad organic bulges are appropriate here. However, voxel remeshing erases some narrow appendages and merges the crest. Source-point proximity is 95.3% within 0.025 ship units, versus 97.8% for the Pyre and 99.6% for the zenith. This is a distance check, not proof of silhouette fidelity.

The dark underside shots preserve the battle lighting. They establish silhouettes but leave some surface detail hard to judge. Neither prototype is presented as having passed a final production blob sweep. No new naval fittings, backward weapons, floating authored parts, missing hull bodies or shader error blocks were found in the final capture set.

Not checked: full-battle effects, damage states, 4K rendering, concept-camera fits, or sustained performance over a run. The job requested an isolated route bake-off; those checks belong to a later integration pass.
