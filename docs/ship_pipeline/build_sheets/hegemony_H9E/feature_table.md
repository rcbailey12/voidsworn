## INSIDE

The bow contains the ion feed and capacitors beneath the armoured jacket, a crew corridor and a main deck. The middle contains the reactor, power distribution and the two lances' magazine. The stern has the drone handling deck, equipment racks and engine room. Each region carries its own deck and framing, machinery and cool emergency light.

| Break line | Station % | What tears |
|---|---|---|
| B-1 | 46.2% | Bow module joint, kinks around the jacket fittings |
| B-2 | 73.1% | Stern module joint, ahead of the drone doors and transom equipment |

Four breach anchors flank the two joints. Paired forward braking and mid-flank boarding points sit in clear skin. The forward-most face is the collar, with zero overhang ahead of it. The aft engine lips set the full length.
