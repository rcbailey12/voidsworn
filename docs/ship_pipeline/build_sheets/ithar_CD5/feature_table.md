## Changed from the picture

- Three round secondary optics at x=-6, 0, +6 on the centreline, set into gentle glass swells. R298: even, balanced spacing and all-direction arcs; amber = pulsar T, violet = turret T, green = flak T. The stern cluster is propulsion.
- Two separate long drawn-glass lances, one per forward pulsar slot. Keep Ryan's loved long forward lances; the model uses the game's two F slots and visible optical exits.
- Deep rounded leaf with broad twisting ridges, smooth internal panes and full glass insides. The single three-quarter concept cannot settle the underside or depths; faction symmetry and R215 supply them.
- Breaks bracket the lens bays; O3 is an upper peel clear of both lances. Glasswrights shatter; each large optic remains a whole part. The forward lances cannot be cut by a new transverse seam.
