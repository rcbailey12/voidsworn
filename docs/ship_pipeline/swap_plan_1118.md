### Checks that matter for the swap

- **Mapping.** The recipe files' `game_code` and `game_ship` agree with the [Godot hull table](../../godot/render/hull_table.gd), including E13 as Mote/Frigate and E14 as Flare/Destroyer. R201 anchors A16/A17, R204 anchors C10/C11, and R206 anchors E11/E12. Later picks R254/R255, R269, R298, R302, R306 and R310 account for S8 and its craft, the upright E14, CD5, the cage and flesh changes, and KB1CC. [Rulings](../../contrib/opus/FINDINGS.md) · [Recipe definitions](../../tools/recipe/ships/)
- **Ship finish.** The Nov 18 satin approval applies to Navy, Hullbreakers, Eclipse and Sunward. The four Eclipse and six Sunward finish bundles in `board/issue-1035/finish_eclipse_exports/` and `board_b/issue-1036/finish_sunward_exports/` match the original A4 geometry. They provide a look reference for the new bakes. The Navy small hulls, transport, beacon and shared fighter/bomber manifest models got the same A4 treatment in `board_b/issue-1016/finish_1118_fleet_exports/`. R320 calls for nearly reflective glass and flesh or hard shell, both without wear. [Navy and Hullbreakers, PR 1040](../shots/ship_finish_1118/fleet/README.md) · [Eclipse, PR 1043](../shots/ship_finish_eclipse/README.md) · [Sunward, PR 1041](../shots/ship_finish_sunward/README.md)
- **Damage and wreck.** The seven installed recipe bundles and the located Eclipse and Chorus candidate exports have no separate `_damage.glb` or `_wreck.glb`. Stage 7 retires the old A4 files and the game uses the new hull's authored rooms and seams for runtime wounds and breaks. Before each install, prove that route on the final export. Check a wound, each authored break, no severed machines or stray interior triangles, and no old A4 scene left in the refreshed cache. The A4 hulls keep their existing damage and wreck files until replaced. [Stage 7 and authored damage](RECIPE.md#authored-insides-and-weak-seams-issue-557)
- **Eclipse variants.** Checked unpainted test GLBs for `d_scythe_s`, `d_jackal_h` and `d_harvester_t` are in `%TEMP%/voidsworn877/variant_final_proof/ships/`, with earlier issue 812 proofs in `board_b/issue-812/shots/glbs/`. They use separate template IDs and have no manifest rows today. They need matching finished exports and their own Godot readback. Verify the base and every template that draws it, especially E14's null lance and guard spire. [Variant route](../playbooks/ships.md) · [E12 armoured-core variant](build_sheets/deathless_E12/variants/d_harvester_t/README.md)
- **Squadrons and station.** [SquadronWing](../../godot/render/squadron_wing.gd) makes procedural craft by faction and role. A raw overwrite of the generic fighter or bomber code would leave that path unchanged. The Navy/Sunward fighters and Sunward/Hullbreaker bombers share manifest codes. [The station loader](../../godot/assets/ships/a4_props.gd) uses T2 for the large cluster when cached and O1_37 for smaller clusters or fallback. [Station proof](../shots/look_bench/station/README.md)

## Install order

1. **E11 and E13 first, about 2 runs.** One satin bake/export batch, then one isolated install and scene check. Both already have comparable PC finish, hard-point and LOD evidence. Use their passing geometry as the baseline. Keep the current A4 finish on screen until the replacement passes.
2. **E12 and E14, about 2 to 3 runs.** Rebuild and bake the armoured zenith and the lance flare. Pass stages 6 and 7 with current weapons, then verify the flare's lift and the breaks in-game. Include variant exports and a separate verification run if taking all three variants in this wave.
3. **C11, C10, S8 and CD5, about 6 to 9 runs.** Use C11 to prove the no-wear Chorus stack, then take C10 v7 through it (2 to 3 runs). In parallel with review, clear S8's seam and run the current Sunward bake/export (2 to 3). Give CD5 its own recipe, glass finish and same-scene cost check (2 to 3).
4. **Wing models, about 3 to 5 runs.** Prove one faction-and-role model load end to end, then install HF1D, SF3, SB4, KB1CC and CS1BA2 in batches. Check launches, strike and intercept views, trail origins, wing spacing and existing faction fallbacks.
5. **Classes without a replacement, about 15 to 25 runs.** Start with the five Sunward and three Navy combat classes, then the four Glasswright and three Chorus classes. Estimate about one design/build run per combat hull, with packed export and review runs as in the earlier fleet passes. Budget a further 2 to 4 runs for the Navy bomber and the transport/beacon objective work once their designs are set. The local T2 and O1_37 split needs no swap run.

These are agent runs, based on the project's batch bakes in 642 and 1016 and sheet revisions in 877, 912 and 921. Each install run backs up the current files, uses the stage 7 manifest and cache refresh, and verifies the exact installed ship in Godot before moving to the shared build.
